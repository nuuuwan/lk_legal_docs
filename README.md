A legal act is a law passed by Parliament that governs rights, duties, economy, and society, shaping daily life and national policy.

- [**3,925** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_acts) (**959.2 MB**), from **1981-01-22** to **2025-09-10**, scraped from **[https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html](https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html)**

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (9%), **TXT** (9%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (9%)

- In **සිංහල**, **தமிழ்** & **English**
