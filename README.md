# ⚖️#SriLanka 🇱🇰 Act `Dataset`

![LastUpdated](https://img.shields.io/badge/last_updated-2025--09--19_16:25:56-green)

[https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_act](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_act)

A legal act in Sri Lanka is a law passed by Parliament that governs rights, duties, economy, and society, shaping daily life and national policy.

- [**4,017** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_act) (**53.3 MB**), from **1980-01-10** to **2025-09-10**, scraped from **[https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html](https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html)**

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (2%), **TXT** (2%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (2%)

- In **සිංහල**, **தமிழ்** & **English**
