# ⚖️#SriLanka 🇱🇰 Acts `Dataset`

![LastUpdated](https://img.shields.io/badge/last_updated-2025--09--20_01:24:04-green)

[https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_acts](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_acts)

A legal act is a law passed by Parliament that governs rights, duties, economy, and society, shaping daily life and national policy.

- [**3,925** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data/data/lk_acts) (**2.6 GB**), from **1981-01-22** to **2025-09-10**, scraped from **[https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html](https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/acts_2025.html)**

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (37%), **TXT** (37%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (37%)

- In **සිංහල**, **தமிழ்** & **English**
