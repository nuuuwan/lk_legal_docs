An Extraordinary Gazette is an official government publication used to announce urgent laws, regulations, or public notices with immediate effect.

- [**56,379** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data_lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2010s/data/lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2010s) (**17.4 GB**), from **2010-01-01** to **2019-12-31**, scraped from [https://documents.gov.lk](https://documents.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (69%), **TXT** (69%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (69%)

- In **සිංහල**, **தமிழ்** & **English**
