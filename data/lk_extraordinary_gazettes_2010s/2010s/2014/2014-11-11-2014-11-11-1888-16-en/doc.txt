NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(A) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Nuwara Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Hettiyakandage Arunapushpa Saumyasiri Fernando returning officer of the Nuwara Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha do herebydeclare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Rajaratnam Ravikumar ofthe Nuwara Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has ceased to be amember of the Up-Country People's Front.

H. A. S. FERNANDO,

Returning Officer,Nuwara Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Nuwara Eliya,11th November, 2014.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

PANADURA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of failing to make and subscribean oath or affirmation in terms of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Mohan Lal Gunaransi belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of that PanaduraPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;
