FINANCE ACT, No. 11 OF 1963

Order under Section 72

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 72 read with Section 71 of the Finance Act, No. 11 of 1963 as amended fromtime to time, read with Article 44(2) of the Constitution, I, Mahinda Rajapaksa, President, do by this Order, vest in the People'sBank established under the People's Bank Act, No. 29 of 1961, with effect from 17th December Two Thousand and Fourteen,the properties described in the Schedule hereto.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,

President.

The Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,17th December 2014.
