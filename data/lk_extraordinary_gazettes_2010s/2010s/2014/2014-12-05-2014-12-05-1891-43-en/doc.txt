THE LAND REFORM LAW, No. 1 OF 1972

Order under Subsection (4) of Section 27A

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by subsection (4) of Section 27A of the land Reform Law, No. 1 of 1972 as amendedby the Land Reform (Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981, read with paragraph (2) of Article 44 of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Mahinda Rajapaksa, President, do hereby -

(a)amend the Order, made under subsection (1) of Section 27A of the Land Reform Law, No. 1 of 1972 and publishedin Gazette Extraordinary No. 183/10 of March 12, 1982 relating to the land called and known as Hantane Estatemorefully described in the First Schedule to this Order and which had vested in the Janatha Estate DevelopmentBoard established by the State Agricultural Corporation Act, No. 11 of 1972, by revoking, on grounds ofnon-compliance with such terms and conditions relating to consideration for the vesting in such Board, suchportion of the said Order as relates to the extent of land called Hantane Estate specified in the Second Schedule; and

(b)State that upon revocation, such extent of land as specified in the Second Schedule to this order, shall re-vest inthe Land Reform Commission.
