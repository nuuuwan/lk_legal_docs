TRANSFER OF STAMP DUTY STATUTE OF THE NORTHERN PROVINCE, NO. 02 OF 2014

A Statute to provide for the transfer of Stamp Duty collected under the Finance Statute of the Northern Province,No. 1 of 2014 of the Northern Province to the Local Authorities in the Northern Province, remittance of administrative cost tothe Provincial Fund and incidental thereto.

Be it enacted by the Provincial Council of the Northern Province as follows :

01. This Statute may be cited as the Transfer of Stamp Duty Statute of the Northern Province,No. 02 of 2014.

02 (1) Where the Provincial Council has charged and collected, in terms of the Finance Statute ofthe Northern Province, No. 1 of 2014, any stamp duty (hereinafter referred to as “the duty”) on anyinstrument relating to a transfer of any immovable property situated within the administrative limits of anyLocal Authority in the Northern Province and paid to the Provincial Fund of the Northern Province, theProvincial Council may, on a written claim made in the form as provided in Annex 1 of this Statute by theAdministrative Officer of that Local Authority to the Minister in charge of the subject of Finance of theNorthern Province within a period of two years from the year in which the duty was collected as aforesaid,transfer such amount of the duty, subject to the provisions of Sub-Section (3), to the Local Authoritywithin which administrative limits the immovable property in respect of whose transfer the duty wascollected is situated.

(2) Any duty not claimed by any Local Authority within the period as stipulated under SubSection (1) shall not be claimable thereafter by the said Local Authority. Provided however a Local Authority,which had failed to make a claim within the stipulated period, may submit a late claim to the Minister and ifthe Minister after having satisfied with the reasons for the delay in submitting the claim, direct the authorityto release such funds as provided under Sub Section (1).

(3) A sum equivalent to one per centum (01%) of the sum of duty to be transferred to any LocalAuthority under Sub Section (1) shall be deducted by the Provincial Council and retained in the ProvincialFund as being the expenditure of every description incurred by the Provincial Council in connection withthe collection and transfer of the duty.

03. No claim under Sub Sections (1) and (2) of Section 2 shall be valid unless accompanied by aSchedule prepared in the manner as prescribed under Annex II of this Statute for the transfer of the dutyspecified in that claim and certified by the Registrar General or any officer authorized by him in that behalf.

04. In this Statute, unless the context otherwise requires —

“Minister” means the Provincial Minister in charge for the subject of Finance in the NorthernProvince ;

“Local Authorities” means any Municipal Council, Urban Council and Pradeshiya Sabhaand includes any Authority created and established by or under any law to exercise, performand discharge powers, duties and functions corresponding to or similar to the powers,duties and functions exercised, performed and discharged by any such Councillor Sabha ;

“Instrument” has the same meaning as provided in the Finance Statute of the NorthernProvince, No. 1 of 2014 ;

“Duty” means the stamp duty provided under Section 35(a) of Chapter XI of Part C of theFinance Statute of the Northern Province, No. 1 of 2014 ;

“Administrative Officer” means :-

(a) In relation to a Municipal Council, the Municipal Commissioner of the Council ;

and(b) In relation to an Urban Council or a Pradeshiya Sabha, the Secretary of the

respective Council or Sabha ;
