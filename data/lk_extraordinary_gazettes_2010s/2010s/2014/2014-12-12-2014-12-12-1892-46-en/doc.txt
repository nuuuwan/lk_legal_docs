PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

L.D.B. 4/ 2009.

THE ANIMALS ACT, No. 29 OF 1958

Order under Section 11 (C)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 11 (c) of the Animals Act, No. 29 of 1958, I Arumugan Thondaman, Ministerof Livestock and Rural Community Development do by this order suspend the implementation of the regulation 22 in partof the Regulations made under the aforesaid Act, and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1629/17 ofNovember 26, 2009, for a period of two years.

ARUMUGAN THONDAMAN,Minister of Livestock and Rural

Community Development.

Colombo,08th December, 2014.
