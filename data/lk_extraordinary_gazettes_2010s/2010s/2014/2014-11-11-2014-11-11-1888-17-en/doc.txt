FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

VALVETITHURAI URBAN COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Valvetithurai Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of being Ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance, to whichthe former Member, Mr. Ponnuthurai Theiventhiran belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of thatValvetithurai Urban Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. SivananthaiyarSelvalingam being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Ponnuthurai Kuganathan, Returning Officer of Valvetithurai Urban Council acting in termsof Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Sivananthaiyar Selvalingam elected as a memberof the Valvetithurai Urban Council.

P. KUGANATHAN,Returning Officer,Valvetithurai Urban Council.Elections Office,Jaffna District,07th November, 2014.
