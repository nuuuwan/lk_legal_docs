FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

MADURAWALA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Madurawala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the party:

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the Formermember Amarasingha Kahatapitiya belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that MadurawalaPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. GurusinghedapanadurageAmal Nishantha Samarasinghe being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Hannedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Madurawala Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Gurusinghedapanadurage AmalNishantha Samarasinghe elected as a member of the Madurawala Pradeshiya Sabha.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer,Madurawala Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Kalutara,21st November, 2014.
