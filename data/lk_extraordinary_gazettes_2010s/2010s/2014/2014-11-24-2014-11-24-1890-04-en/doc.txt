FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

HORANA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Horana Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending more than threeconsecutive meetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance, to whichthe former member, Ashok Krishanthan Nikalas belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of thatHorana Urban Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Donmanuwelge NalinChinthaka being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hannedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Horana Urban Council acting in termsof Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Donmanuwelge Nalin Chinthaka elected as amember of the Horana Urban Council.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer, Horana Urban Council.District Elections Office,Kalutara,21st November, 2014.
