NOTICE UNDER THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS ACT, No. 44 OF 1980 AS AMENDED BY THE

REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 27 OF 2013

CERTAIN persons have been internally displaced as a result of any actions of a terrorist militant or other group during therecent past :

02.According to the Section 2 of the Registration of Electors (Special Provisions) Act, No. 27 of 2013. anypersons,

2.1 who is or had been an internally displaced person ;2.2 whose name appeared in the register of electors for any electoral district in the Northern Province or

Eastern Province for any year, until the end of the year 2009 and ;2.3 whose name has not been entered in any register in operation subsequent to the year 2009 ;

shall on proof of the matters specified in above paragraphs and on production of a certificate issued by the GramaNiladhari of the area in which the applicant is presently residing, to the effect that the applicant is or the applicant had beenan internally displaced person, be entitled to apply to the registering officer of the electoral district within which theapplicant was permanently resident prior to May 18, 2009, to be registered in the register of electors of such electoral district,

03.According to the Section 3 of the Special Provisions Act, a child of Sri Lankan citizen mentioned in the 2ndparagraph,

3.1 who had not attained the age of eighteen years on the date on which such citizen became an internally

displaced person and have attained the age of eighteen years or more on the date on which the revisioncommenced in respect of the register in operation, at the time in which the application is made,3.2 who were born after such citizen became an internally displaced person and have attained the age of

eighteen years or more on the date on which the revision commenced in respect of the register in operation,at the time in which the application is made,3.3 whose name has not been entered in any register in operation subsequent to the year 2009,
