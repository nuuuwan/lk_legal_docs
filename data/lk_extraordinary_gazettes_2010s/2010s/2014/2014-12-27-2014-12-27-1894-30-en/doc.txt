FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE

PANADURA URBAN COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Chairman in Panadura Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance the recognized political party, to whichM. D. Nandana Gunathilaka the former Chairman, belonged was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon the member of that Panadura Urban Council who belongs to such party, who shall be declared elected asChairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Chakrawarthige Jeradi Riyensi Fernando fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Hannedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Panadura Urban Council, acting interms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Chakrawarthige Jeradi Riyensi Fernando electedas a Chairman of the Panadura Urban Council.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer,Panadura Urban Council.

District Elections Office,Kalutara,26th December, 2014.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

PANADURA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Panadura Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance, to whichthe former member, M. D. Nandana Gunathilaka, belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that PanaduraUrban Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Waduge Vijith VishwanathFernando being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Hannedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Panadura Urban Council, acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Waduge Vijith Vishwanath Fernando elected as amember of the Panadura Urban Council.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer,Panadura Urban Council.District Elections Office,Kalutara,26th December, 2014.
