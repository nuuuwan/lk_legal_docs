CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notice by the Governor of the Central Province

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk

KNOW ye that by virtue of powers vested by the article 154(b) : (8):(b) of the thirteenth amendment to the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Tikiri Banda Kobbekaduwa, Governor of the Central Province, do hereby thisnotice prorogue the Second Session of the Sixth Council of the Central Province with effect from 17th December, TwoThousand Fourteen.

By virtue of powers vested in me by paragraph 8(a) of the said Article, I do hereby summon the said Council to meetat the Central Province Provincial Council Meeting Hall at Pallekelle, Kundasale on Thursday the 15th January, Two ThousandFifteen at 10.00 a. m.

TIKIRI BANDA KOBBEKADUWA,Governor of the Central Province.

Given at Kandy,this 14th day of November, Two Thousand Fourteen.
