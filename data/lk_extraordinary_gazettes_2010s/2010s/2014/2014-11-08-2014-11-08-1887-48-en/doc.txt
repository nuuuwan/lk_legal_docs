NOTICE UNDER SECTION 3(1) OF THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)

ACT, No. 27 OF 2013

Supplementary list to insert the names of Sri Lankan citizens whose names have not been registered as electorselsewhere in 2013 and who were internally displaced as result of actions of militant groups in Northern/Eastern

Province

ELECTORAL DISTRICT No. 10 - JAFFNA

IN terms of the Section 3(1) of the Registration of Elections (Special Provisions) Act, No. 27 of 2013, it is hereby notified thatthe preparation of the supplementary list for displaced persons relevant to the afore mentioned electoral district has beencompleted and it is now open for inspection by the general public during office hours at the District Elections Office Jaffna/Kilinochchi

02. Any person who is eligible to be registered in the supplementary list of afore mentioned electoral district but

whose name does not appear in the list may appeal to the Registering Officer using the form set out in the aforementioned Registration of Elections Act, in order to enter or retain his/her name in the register. Every applicationregarding this matter should be submitted to the Registering Officer of District Elections Office, Jaffna/Killinochchiwithin one week from the date on which this notice is published in the Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka.

03. Any person whose name is included into the supplementary list of the afore mentioned electoral district, could

object to retain his/her own name or the name of any other person. These objections shall be in duplicatesubstantially on Form “D” set out in the afore mentioned Act and be submitted to the Registering Officer of theElections Office, Jaffna/Kilinochchi every objection relating to the entering of a name in the register should besubmitted to the Registering Officer of District Elections Office, Jaffna/Kilinochchi within the one week from thedate on which this notice is published in the Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

This notice will be exhibited at the Elections Office, Jaffna/Kilinochchi from 08th November 2014 to 14th November2014 (including the said date as well)
