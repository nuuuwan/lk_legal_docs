FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

THIRUKKOVIL PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending meetingsof the Council for a Period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely IIankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi to which theFormer member Meharasa Chandirakumar, belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that ThirukkovilPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. KandhaiyaAmirthalingam being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Kandhaiya Amirthalingam electedas a member of the Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Ampara,31st October, 2014.
