NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 31/0133 Matale District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 310045 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Kiralagolla (Block 02) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Divi-sion of E440B - Kiralagolla in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Dambulla in the District of Matale in the Province of Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 10th January, 2015 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determi-nation in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 310045 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. GAMINI ILANGARATHNA, Commissioner of Title Settlement.
