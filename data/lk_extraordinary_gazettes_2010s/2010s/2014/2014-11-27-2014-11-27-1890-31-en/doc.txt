THE FINANCE STATUTE OF THE NORTHERN PROVINCE

A Statute to provide for the Imposition and Collection of Taxes and Fees and for matters connected therewith orincidental thereto in Terms of Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987

This Statute is inconsistent with certain provisions of the Turnover Tax Act, No. 69 of 1981 as amended, the StampDuty Act No. 43 of 1982 as amended, the Excise Ordinance, No. 08 of 1912 as amended, the Prize Competition Act No. 37 of1957 as amended, the Municipal Councils Ordinance, No. 29 of 1947 as amended, the Urban Councils Ordinance, No. 61 of1939 as amended, the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987 as amended. This is brought into focus in terms of the relevantprovisions of the Constitution of Sri Lanka.

Whereas the people of the Northern Province have suffered due to man-made destruction and natural disasters inthe past three decades;

Whereas the civil administration of the Northern Province had been dysfunctional during this period and even theprovision and maintenance of the minimum services to the People: of the Northern Province had been seriously affected anddisrupted;

Whereas it has become the primary duty of the Provincial Council to address the pressing needs of the people of theNorthern Province with a view to helping them recover from the ravages and the miseries they suffered in the past;

Whereas development and maintenance of basic infrastructure facilities and the provision of essential services areimperative to enable the people of the Northern Province to satisfactory standard of livingWhereas the carrying out of the task of such development, maintenance of infrastructure facilities and provision ofservices require substantial sums of money for the Provincial Administration;

Whereas the Provincial Council while being empowered to impose and collect taxes, duties and fees for stipulatedpurposes, is deeply conscious of the hardship any unnecessary or unjustified taxation may bring upon the people of theNorthern Province;

AND Whereas the Provincial Council is constrained to raise some revenue for the provision of essential services toits People:

Be it enacted by the Provincial Council of the Northern Province as follows :

1.(1) This Statute may be cited as the Finance Statute of the Northern Province No. 01 of2014 and its provisions and Parts shall come into operation on such date or dates asmay be appointed by the Minister by Order published in the Gazette

(2) The Minister may appoint different dates in respect of different provisions and parts tobring it or them into operation.

CHAPTER I

2.(1) There shall be established a Department known as the Northern Provincial RevenueDepartment (hereinafter referred to as the Revenue Department) which shall administerthe provisions of this Statute with due diligence and efficiency.

PART B

CHAPTER II

IMPOSITION OF TURNOVER TAX

3.(1) Subject to such limits and exemptions as have been and may be prescribed by lawmade by Parliament and other provisions of this Statute and any other Statute, thereshall be charged for every quarter from every person who carries on any business inthe Province, a tax (hereinafter referred to as the “turnover tax”) in respect of theturnover made by that person in that quarter from that business the rate as shall bespecified by law.

(2) For the purpose of this Part “business” shall mean and include selling by wholesale orretail of any article as shall be specified by law.

4.(1) A person shall, in respect of any business carried on by him in the Province, bechargeable with turnover tax for each quarter.

(2) Where a person is chargeable with turnover tax in respect of any business carried onby him and change of ownership of that business occurs, and if the tax cannot berecovered and if the business has been in the hands of the previous owner thennotwithstanding the provisions of Subsection (1), the new owner of that businessshall be chargeable with turnover tax as though he had been the owner of that businessimmediately before the occurrence of the change of ownership, if the tax cannot berecovered from the previous owner.

CHAPTER III

EXEMPTIONS

5.(1) The Minister may, if he is of the opinion that it is essential for the economic progress ofthe Province exempt by Order published in the Gazette, any business or such businessas may be specified which is carried on by any person, from the turnover tax.

(2) Every Order under Subsection (1) shall come into force on date of its publication in theGazette or on such later date as may be specified in such Order and shall be broughtbefore the Provincial Council within a period of three months from the date of thepublication of such Order in the Gazette or, if no meeting of Provincial Council is heldwithin such period, at the first meeting of Provincial Council held after the expiry ofsuch period, by a motion that such Order shall be approved.

(3) Any Order which the Provincial Council refuses to approve shall, with effect from thedate of such refusal, be deemed to be revoked but without prejudice to the validity ofsuch Order until the date of such refusal, and the notification of the date on whichsuch Order is deemed to be revoked shall be published in the Gazette.

CHAPTER IV

TURNOVER

6.(1) For the purpose of this Statute "turnover" in relation to any business of selling anyarticle means the total amount received or receivable from transactions entered into inrespect of that business;

(2) For the purpose of Subsection (1) above "turnover" shall not include any amountreceived or receivable by the sale of capital assets.

(3) Where in respect of any quarter turnover tax has been paid in respect of any sumreceivable during that quarter no such tax in respect of that sum need be paid in respectof that quarter in which such sum is actually received.

(4) In ascertaining the turnover of a business for a quarter there shall be deducted anamount equal to any bad debt incurred by that business which has become a bad debtduring that quarter, being an amount which has been included in the turnover of thatbusiness for a previous quarter and in respect of which turnover tax has been paid:

Provided that any sum received in any quarter by that business on account of anamount previously deducted under this Subsection in respect of a bad debt shall beincluded in the turnover of that business for the quarter in which that sum is received.

CHAPTER V

RATES OF TAX

7.(1) The rate of turnover tax in respect of any business may be determined by reference tothe nature of such business and accordingly different rates as shall be specified by lawin respect of different classes or description of business by Regulations published inthe Gazette.
