NORTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointments made by the Governor of the Northern Province

GAMMANPILA Arachchige Chandrasiri, Governor of the Northern Province by virtue of Power vested in me under theSection 154 F (1), (4) and (5) of the 13th Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,do hereby announce that I have made the following appointments.

Hon. Canagasabapathy Viswalingam Wigneswaran- Minister of Finance and Planning, Law and Order,

Lands,Electricity, Housing and Construction, Industriesand Enterprise Promotion, Tourism, Local Government, andProvincial Administration of the Northern Province witheffect from 10th of December 2014.

Hon. Ponnudurai Ayngaranesan- Minister of Agriculture and Agrarian Services, Animal

Husbandry, Irrigation, Water Supply, Food Supply andDistribution, Environment and Co-operative Developmentof the Northern Province with effect from 10th of December2014.

Hon. Pathmanathan Sathiyalingam- Minister of Health and Indigenous Medicine, Social

Services and Rahabilitation, Probation and ChildcareServices and Women's Affairs of the Northern Provincewith effect from 10 th of December 2014.

Hon. Balasubramaniam Deniswaran- Minsiter of Fisheries, Transport, Trade and Commerce,

Rural Development, Road Development and Motor Trafficof the Northern Province with effect from 10th of December2014.

G.. A. CHANDRASIRI,

Governor,Northern Province.10th December, 2014
