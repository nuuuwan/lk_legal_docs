FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

LUNUGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Lunugala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance to whichthe former Member, Arumuham Ganeshamoorthy belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Lunugala Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Manamendra AcharigePalitha Ruwan Kithsiri Manamendra being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Rathnayaka Ralalage Asanka Sathis Kumara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of LunugalaPradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. ManamendraAcharige Palitha Ruwan Kithsiri Manamendra elected as a member of the Lunugala Pradeshiya Sabha.

ASANKA RATHNAYAKA,

Returning Officer,Lunugala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Badulla,09th December, 2014.
