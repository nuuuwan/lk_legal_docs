UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointments made by the Governor

BY, virtue of powers vested to me by Section 32(1) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, I, C. Nanda Mathew, Governorof Uva Province do hereby notify that the following appointments have been approved by me with immediate effect.

01.Mrs. R. M. S. D. Rathnayake, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, As Deputy Chief Secretary(Administration) for the Uva Provincial Council.

02.Mrs. A. A. U. Sakunthala Adhikari, Class III officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, As Commissioner ofProbation and Childcare service of Uva Province.

03.Mr. A. A. P. S. K. Karunarathne, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service, As Director to the Buildingof Uva Province.

04.Mr. K. A. B. Senevirathne, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service, As Director to the Road of UvaProvince.

05.Mrs. J. W. C. S. Priyabashini, Class III officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, to perform the duties of theDeputy Chief Secretary (Personnel and Training) for the Uva Provincial Council in full time basis.

06.Mr. W. M. N. Wickramasinghe, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Accountancy Service, As Director (Finance) of theDepartment of Finance Management of Uva Province.

07.Mr. V. C. Hettiaratchi, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Planning Service, As Director (Planning) to the Ministry ofFinance and Planning, Law and Peace, Education, Local Government, Land, Cultural Affairs, Social Welfare andRural Infrastructure Development and Construction of Uva Province.

C. NANDA MATHEW, Governor of Uva Province.10th October 2014,Office of the Uva Governor.
