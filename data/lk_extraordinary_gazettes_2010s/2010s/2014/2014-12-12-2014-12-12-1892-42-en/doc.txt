FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE

VALIKAMAM WEST PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attendingmeetings of the Council for a period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognised political party to which NavaratnamSivaranjan the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, todecide upon the member of that Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared electedas a Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognised political party has communicated his decision to Mr.Navaratnam Sivaranjan fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Ponnuththurai Kuganathan, Returning Officer of Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Navaratnam Sivaranjan elected as Vice Chairmanof the Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. KUGANATHAN,Returning Officer,Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,9th December, 2014.
