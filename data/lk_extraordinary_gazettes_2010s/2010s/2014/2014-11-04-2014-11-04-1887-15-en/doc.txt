THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dissanayake MudiyanselageSumanaweera Banda Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, do by this order, on the recommendation of the UniversityGrants Commission, amend the Order made under Section 21 of the aforesaid Act, establishing the South Eastern Universityof Sri Lanka and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 916/7 of March 27, 1996, as last amended by Order published inGazette Extraordinary, No. 1787/27 of December 05, 2012 by the addition immediately after item (b) in Column II under theheading “Department of Study” which correspond to the entry “Faculty of Management and Commerce” in Column I underthe heading “Faculty” in the Schedule II to such order, of the following new item :-

“(c) Department of Management and Information Technology”

DISSANAYAKE MUDIYANSELAGE SUMANAWEERA BANDA DISSANAYAKE,

Minister of Higher Education.
