1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

RIDIGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Ridigama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation:

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance, to whichthe Former member Mr. S. D. Saman Bandula Weerarathna Malmeewala belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days. a person eligible under the Ordinance for Electionas a member of that Ridigama Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. RathnayakeMudiyanselage Bandula Rathnayake being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Thalangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Thalangama, Returning Officer of RidigamaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling ofvacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. RathnayakeMudiyanselage Bandula Rathnayake elected as a member of the Ridigama Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. THALANGAMA,

Returning Officer,Ridigama Pradeshiya Sabha.

Distric Elections Office,Kurunegala,13th November, 2014.
