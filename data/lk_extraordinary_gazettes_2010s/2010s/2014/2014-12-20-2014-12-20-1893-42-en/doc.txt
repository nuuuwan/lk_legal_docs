FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEKATARAGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES

ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES

(SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

And whereas the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember Ashoka Wickramasinghe Withanage, belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of thatKataragama Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Dandeniya GamageChandana Sugath being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

Now therefore, I, Suranga Ambagahatenne, Returning Officer of Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms ofSection 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Dandeniya Gamage Chandana Sugath elected as a memberof the Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATENNE,

Returning Officer,Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Moneragala,16th December, 2014.
