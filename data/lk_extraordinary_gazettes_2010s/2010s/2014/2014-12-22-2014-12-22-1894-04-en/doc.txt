CONTROL OF PESTICIDES ACT, No. 33 OF 1980

Order under Section 11

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section II of the Control of Pesticides Act, No. 33 of 1980, I, Gamage AnuraWickramasiri Wijesekara, Registrar of Pesticides, do by this Order, in the interest of the public and on the advice of theTechnical and Advisory Committee, modify every licence issued in respect of pesticides containing the active ingredientsbearing the Abstract Service Registry number specified in Column I of the schedule hereto by prohibiting the use, offer forsale or sale in the areas specified in Column II.

DR. G. A. W. WIJESEKARA,

Registrar of Pesticides.Peradeniya,22nd December 2014.
