PART III - LANDS NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998 (SECTION 55) Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Primsiri Kordon who resides in the village of Lakshapathiya, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Moratuwa, in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Primsiri Kordon described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 18th December 2014 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
