CF 1/64.THE PUBLIC SECURITY ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 40)

Order

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 12 of the Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40), I, Mahinda Rajapaksa,do by this order call out all the members of the Armed Forces specified in the First Schedule hereto, for the maintenanceof public order in the areas specified in the Second Schedule hereto.
