Government Notifications

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, Jagath Premalal Wijeweera, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 17th November, 2014, all duties ofCustoms as well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall bepaid and received at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1888/1 of10th November, 2014 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similarnotification to be published on or before 23rd November, 2014.

JAGATH P. WIJEWEERA,Director General of Customs.

Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,14th November 2014.
