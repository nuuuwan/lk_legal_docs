NORTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Supplementary Provision for Appropriation Statute No. 03 of 2014

A statute to provide Supplementary Provisions to previously approved expenditure of the Northern Provincial Council forthe financial year 2014 by the Honourable Governor on the 21st day of December 2013 in accordance to First Schedule to thisstatute and to make payments by way of advance out of the Northern Provincial Council funds or monies to provide for therefund of such monies to the Northern Provincial Council funds and to make provisions for matters connected therewith orincidental thereto :

Be it enacted by the Northern Provincial Council as follows

1.This Statute may be cited as the Supplementary Provision for Appropriation StatuteNo. 03 of 2014.

2.Without prejudice to any other law which authorizes for any such expenditure, the expenditureof the Northern Provincial Council during the financial year beginning from 1st January2014 and ending on 31st December 2014 referred to as the “Financial year 2014” in thisStatute the allocation of Rupees One thousand seven hundred and twenty five million sixhundred and three thousand five hundred and twenty two only (Rs. 1,725,603,522) fromthe expenditure the Honourable Governor approved on the 21st December 2013 and to thelimits stated in the First Schedule of this Statute shall be met ;

(A)From the Northern Provincial Council funds or monies, or

(B)From the allocation of funds allocated in accordance to Article 154(R) of theConstitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and any other fundsallotted or granted by the Government of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

3.The debit balance outstanding at the end of the financial year 2014 of any activities specifiedin the Second Schedule of this statute is revised from the maximum and minimum limits theHonourable Governor approved on 21st December 2013 in the Second Schedule of theStatute.
