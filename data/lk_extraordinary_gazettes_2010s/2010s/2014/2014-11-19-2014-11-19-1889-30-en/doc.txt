FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

ADDALACHENAI PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Addalachenai Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation:

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Sri Lanka Muslim Congress, to which theformer member Ahamed Lebbe Zubaideen belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of thatAddalachenai Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Abdul CareemIbralebbe Mohamed Nazeer being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Don Thilina Ishan Wikramarathna, Returning Officer of Addalachenai Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Abdul Careem Ibralebbe MohamedNazeer elected as a member of the Addalachenai Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WIKRAMARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Addalachenai Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Ampara,18th November, 2014.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

KATUWANA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending meetingsof the Council for a period of Three Months without permisson :

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which theFormer member Jasinge Roshan Karunarathne belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days. a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member ofthat Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Omalpage ChandanaThilak being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Udaha Dasilige Deepal Darshana Chandrarathna, Returning Officer of Katuwana PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies inLocal Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Omalpage Chandana Thilakelected as a member of the Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha.

DEEPAL CHANDRARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Hambantota,19th November, 2014.
