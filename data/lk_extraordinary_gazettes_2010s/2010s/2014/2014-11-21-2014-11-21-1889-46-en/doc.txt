FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

DOMPE PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending meetings of thecouncil for a period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party to which the formermember, Liyana Arachchige Thushara Hemachandra Liyanaarachchi belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) ofthe aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election asa member of that Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Ranasinghe ArachchigeSarath Kumara Ranasinghe being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Dompe Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Ranasinghe Arachchige SarathKumara Ranasinghe elected as a member of the Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. A. S. SENARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Gampaha,19th November, 2014.
