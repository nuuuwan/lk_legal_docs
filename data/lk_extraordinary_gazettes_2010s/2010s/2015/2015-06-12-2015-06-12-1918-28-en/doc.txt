PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

DECLARATION AS PENSIONABLE POSTS

‘‘ Home Guard Force’’ which was established under Section 52 of Manpower Mobilization and Supplementary Forces Act,No. 40 of 1985, has been established as an new department under the name of ‘‘Department of Civil Security’’ as per theGazette Extraordinary of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka dated 13.09.2006 and it is hereby declared that thefollowing posts of the said Department shall be pensionable posts as per Section 2 of the Pensions Minute.

PostDepartment

(a)District OfficerDepartment of Civil Security

(b)WardenDepartment of Civil Security

(c)Sub WardenDepartment of Civil Security

(d)Civil SecurityDepartment of Civil Security

J. DADALLAGE,

Secretary,Ministry of Public Administration,Local Government and Democratic Governance.
