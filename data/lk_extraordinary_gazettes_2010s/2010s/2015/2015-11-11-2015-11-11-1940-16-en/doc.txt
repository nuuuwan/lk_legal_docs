PART III - LANDS FINAL ORDERS LAND SETTLEMENT ORDINANCE Settlement Orders No. 4906 (Mannar) Whereas a settlement notice under Section 4 of the Land Settlement Ordinance was duly published in the Gazette No. 40 of 28th March 1980 and as otherwise required by the said Section, in respect of the lands situated in the village of Pandaraweli in Musali D.R.O’s Division of the Mannar District in the Northern Province and Described as Lot nos. And whereas all claims received in pursuance of the said notice have been duly dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the said Ordinance: I, the undersigned, in pursuance of the powers conferred on me by Section 5(5) of the said Ordinance do hereby make order that the lands or portions thereof be settled as specified in the Schedule to this order. P.M.H.PRIYADHARSHANI, Assistant Settlement Officer Commissioner of - Land Title Settlement Given at Land Title Settlement Department, Battaramulla this 02nd October, 2015.
