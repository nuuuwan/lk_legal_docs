FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

MAHAWA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be a memberof the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party to which the formermember, Anura Bandara Imihami Mudiyanselage belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of that MahawaPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. DissanayakaMudiyanselage Sarath Dissanayaka being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Karunasinghe Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of Mahawa PradeshiyaSabha acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies inLocal Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Dissanayaka MudiyanselageSarath Dissanayaka elected as a member of the Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,

Returning Officer,Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kurunegala,12th May, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE

PALINDANUWARA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance the recognized political party to whichRanaweera Patikirige Bandupema the former vice chairman belonged was required by me under Section 65A(1) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party whoshall be declared elected as a Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to communicate his decisionwithin the period specified ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hannedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Athula Priyantha Bellana who hadsecured the next highest number of preferences at the election held on 17th March 2011 to Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabhaelected as Vice Chairman of the Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha in respect of which such vacancy occured.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer,Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kalutara,12th May, 2015.
