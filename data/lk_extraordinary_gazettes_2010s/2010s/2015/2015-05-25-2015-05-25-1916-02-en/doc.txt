FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF A MEMBER OF THE

VALIKAMAM EAST PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the IIankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi the recognized political party to which KandiahThurairasa the member belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominatea person, eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha who belongsto such party who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Seeniyar Kunarathnambeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Seeniyar Kunarathnam elected asa member of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,

Returning Officer,Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Jaffna,22nd May 2015.
