SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake,Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of the commodity itemsspecified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries inColumn II of that Schedule.
