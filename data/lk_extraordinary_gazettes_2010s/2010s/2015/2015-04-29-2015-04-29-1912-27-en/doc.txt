FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

KUNDASALE PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember, Mr. Anura Senavirathne Kumburegedara belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr.WeerasekaraMudiyanselage Harsha Kumara Weerasekara being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of KundasalePradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling ofvacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. WeerasekaraMudiyanselage Harsha Kumara Weerasekara elected as a member of the Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,

Returning Officer,Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kandy,27th April, 2015.
