THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20(4)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20(4) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, Ministerof Higher Education and Highways, on being satisfied that the administration of the affairs of the Eastern University, SriLanka have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said University have failed to restore normal conditions,do by this Order, appoint Professor Uma Coomaraswamy to be the Competent Authority with effect from January 1, 2016, forthe purpose of exercising, performing or discharging in lieu of Vice-Chancellor any power, duty or function respectivelywhich has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to, the said Vice-Chancellor of the Eastern University, Sri Lanka underthe aforesaid Act or any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By Law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Educationand Highways.

Colombo,23rd December 2015.
