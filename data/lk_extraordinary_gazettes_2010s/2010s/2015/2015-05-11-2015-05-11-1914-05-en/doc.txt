THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

REGULATION made by the Minister of Labour under Section 63 of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136), and approvedby Parliament.

S. B. NAWINNE,Minister of Labour.Colombo,06th May 2015.

Regulation

The Wages Boards Regulations 1971, published in the Ceylon Government Gazette No. 14961 of June 4, 1971 as lastamended by regulations published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1382/23 of March 4, 2005 is hereby further amended inregulation 31 thereof as follows:-

1. in sub-paragraph (a) of that regulation by the substitution for the words "rupees four hundred" of the words

"rupees one thousand and five hundred"; and

2. in sub-paragraph (b) of that regulation by the substitution for the words "rupees five hundred" of the words

"rupees two thousand".
