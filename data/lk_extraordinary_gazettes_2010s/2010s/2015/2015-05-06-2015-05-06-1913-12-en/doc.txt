FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

DOMPE PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Abesinghe Mudiyanselage Upali Ranjith Siriwardana belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for electionas a member of that Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Balasooriyage SamanthaIndika Dayasundara being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Dompe Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Balasooriyage Samantha IndikaDayasundara elected as a member of the Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. A. S. SENARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Dompe Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Gampaha,05th May, 2015.
