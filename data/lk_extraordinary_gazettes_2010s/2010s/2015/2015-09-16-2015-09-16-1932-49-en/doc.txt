PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THEEASTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Eastern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of the ProvincialCouncils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Eastern Province ProvincialCouncil by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Muslim Congress, the recongized political party, to which themember belonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fillsuch vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Ibra Lebbe MohamedMahir to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 02 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Ibra Lebbe Mohamed Mahir, elected as amember of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Ampara in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,15th September, 2015.
