BIRTH AND DEATH REGISTRATION ORDINANCE

Notice under section 05

I, M. Joseph Michael Perera Minister of Home Affairs by virtue of powers vested by me under Section 5 of Birth and Deathregistration ordinance (Chapter 110 ) do hereby amend the Notice of Puttalam District in North western Province whichpublished in the supplementary Gazette No. 8146 on 20th September 1935.

By inserting list of Grama Niladhari Divisions in schedule “A” and “B” instead of the Grama Niladhari Divisionsnotified in connection with Othara North and Othara South Birth and Registration Division in Dankotuwa Divisional SecretaryDivision under heading No. 13 and,

By inserting list of Grama Niladhari Divisions in schedule “C” and “D” instead of the Grama Niladhari Divisionsnotified in connection with Naththandiya and Mudukatuwa Birth and Death Registration Division in Naththandiya DivisionalSecretary Division under heading No. 11 and 12,

By inserting list of Grama Niladhari Divisions in schedule “E” and “F” instead of the Grama Niladhari Divisionsnotified in connection with Kammal Pattuwa Birth and Death Registration Division in Wennappuwa Divisional SecretaryDivision under heading No. 14,

I will be amended this reformation by this notice with effect from on the day of 08th, May of 2015

M. JOSEPH MICHAEL PERERA,Minister of Home Affairs.07th April, 2015,Colombo.
