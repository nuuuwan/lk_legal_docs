UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

By virtue of powers vested in me by section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 I, Dissanayake MudiyanselageSumanaweera Banda Dissanayake, the Minister of Higher Education, having obtained a report under section 70 C of theaforesaid Act, in respect of the KAATSU- Highly Advanced Medical Technology Training Centre (Pvt) Limited (KIU) aCompany incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No.7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 66705, do bythis Order and Subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto, recognize the KAATSU-Highly Advanced MedicalTechnology Training Centre (Pvt) Limited (KIU) as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing HigherEducation therein, leading to the award of the degrees specified in Column I of the Schedule II hereto, of which the abbreviationsare set out in the corresponding entries respectively in Column II of the Schedule II.

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

I. KAATSU- Highly Advanced Medical Technology Training Centre (Pvt) Limited (KIU) (hereinafter referred to

as the “Institute”) shall before expiry of two months from the date of granting the degree awarding status,submit its Articles of Association duly amended as set out below to the Specified Authority :-

(a) to specify higher education as an object in the Articles of Association ;

(b) to undertake to inform the Specified Authority of voluntary winding up of the Company prior to such

winding up.

2. The Institute shall before the expiry of six months from the date of granting the degree awarding status,

submit the following details to Specified Authority:-
