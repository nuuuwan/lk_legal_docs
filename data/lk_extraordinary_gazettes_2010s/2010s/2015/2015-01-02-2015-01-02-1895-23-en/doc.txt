DFCC BANK (REPEAL AND CONSEQUENTIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 39 OF 2014

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 2 of the DFCC Bank (Repeal and Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 39 of2014, read with Article 44(2) of the Constitution, I, Mahinda Rajapaksa, President/Minister of Finance and Planning, do bythis Order specify January 6, 2015, as the date from which the DFCC Bank Act (Chapter 165), be repealed.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,President/Minister of Finance and Planning.
