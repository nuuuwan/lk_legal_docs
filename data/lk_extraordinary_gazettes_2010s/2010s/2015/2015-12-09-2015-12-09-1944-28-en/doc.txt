WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Provincial Road Development Authority Statute No. 04 of 1989 (Western Province)

REMOVAL FROM OFFICE - CHAIRMAN, PROVINCIAL ROAD DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (WESTERN PROVINCE)

ACCORDING to the powers entrusted in me by Section 3(8) of the Part 01 in the Provincial Road Development AuthorityStatute No. 04 of 1989 (Western Province), I, Kejitan Lalith Wanigarathne, Minister of Provincial Road, Transport,Cooperative Development and Trade, Fisheries, Housing and Constructions, Urban and Estate Sector InfrastructureFacilities, Industries and Rural Development of the Western Province, do by this notice announce that Mohamed MulapparMohamed Amjad, who held the Chirman's Office in Western Province Provincial Road Development Authority was removedwith immediate effect from the Chairmanship and membership of the said Authority.

KEJITAN LALITH WANIGARATHNE,Minister of Provincial Roads, Transport, Cooperative Developmentand Trade, Fisheries, Housing and Constructions, Urban andEstate Sector Infrastructure Facilities, Industries andRural Development of the Western Province.
