Government Notifications

ORDER UNDER SECTION 10 (1) (a) AND 11 (1) (a)

OF THE HOLIDAYS ACT, No. 29 OF 1971

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 10 (1) (a) and 11 (1) (a) of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971, I, M. JosephMichael Perera, Minister of Home Affairs and Fisheries, do by this order declare Wednesday, 14th January, 2015 a specialPublic and Bank Holiday of the State visit of H. H. Pope Francis to Sri Lanka.

M. JOSEPH MICHAEL PERERA,Minister of Home Affairs and Fisheries.
