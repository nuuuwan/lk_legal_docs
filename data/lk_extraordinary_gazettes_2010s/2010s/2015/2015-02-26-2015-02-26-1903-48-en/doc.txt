FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

LUNUGAMWEHERA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Lunugamwehera Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attendingmeetings of the Council for a period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party to which the formerMember, Nawimana Urapolage Samitha Chathuranga belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Lunugamwehera Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Wije KankanamPathiranage Upul Nishantha being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Udaha Dasilige Deepal Darshana Chandrarathna, Returning Officer of LunugamweheraPradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Wije KankanamPathiranage Upul Nishantha elected as a member of the Lunugamwehera Pradeshiya Sabha.

DEEPAL CHANDRARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Lunugamwehera Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Hambantota,23rd February, 2015.
