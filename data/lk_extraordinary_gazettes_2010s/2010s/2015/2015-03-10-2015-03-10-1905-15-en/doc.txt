FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

BUTTALA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being elected as a memberof Uva Province Provincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party to which the formerMember, Wijesingha Herath Mudiyanselage Dharmasena belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. SandanayakaMudiyanselage Pemadasa being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Suranga Ambagahatenne, Returning Officer of Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms ofSection 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Sandanayaka Mudiyanselage Pemadasa elected as amember of the Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATENNE,

Returning Officer,Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Moneragala,07th March, 2015.
