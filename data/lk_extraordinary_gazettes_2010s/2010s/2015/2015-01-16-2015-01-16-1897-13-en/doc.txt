NORTH WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Municipal Council Regulations

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of Provincial Council (Incidental Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 to beread with Section 289 Chapter 252 of Municipal Council Ordinance, I, J. P. Dayasiri Pathmakumara Jayasekara, the ChiefMinister of the North Western Province and the Minister in Charge of the subject of Lcoal Government do hereby impose theregulations relating to the payment of allowances for the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Members of Municipal Councils. Theseregulations shall be effective from 01.11.2014.

J. P. DAYASIRI PATHMAKUMARA JAYASEKARA, Chief Minister of the North Westren Provinceand the Minister of Finance and Planning, Law andOrder, Local Government and Provincial Administration,

Human Resources, Education and Cultural Affairs,Lands Environment and Food Supply and Distribution.

Chief Minister in the North Western Province,Kurunegala,22nd December 2014.

REGULATIONS

1. These regulations are cited as the regulations relating to the disbursement of allowances for the Mayor, Deputy

Mayor and Members of Municipal Councils.

2. A monthly allowances per the below mentioned rates should be paid for the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and

Members of Municipal Councils.

Mayor30,000.00Deputy Mayor25,000.00Members20,000.00

3.(a) At the following instances the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Members of Municipal Councils shall not be

entitled for allowances.

i. Being absent at the General meeting and Committee meetings conducted during the month relevant

to the payment of allowances without obtaining the approval from the General Council.

ii. Being remanded or imprisoned on conviction of guilty for any criminal offence.

iii. Suspension of duties of the Members of any Municipal Council in terms of the Statute of Local

Government Authorities (powers of administration and supervision) of the North Western ProvinceNo. 01 of 1991 of the North Western Provincial Council or Section 277 of Municipal CouncilOrdinance.

Provided that in case the period of remand custody, imprisonment, on leave or suspension ofservice is a part of a month these allowances may be paid for the rest of the month on proportionalcalculation.

(b) Whatsoever is mentioned in Sub Paragraph (1) of Paragraph (b) of this order a Members' allowances shall

not be deprived in case a Member is absent at a Council meeting on a formal approval to perform anofficial duty.
