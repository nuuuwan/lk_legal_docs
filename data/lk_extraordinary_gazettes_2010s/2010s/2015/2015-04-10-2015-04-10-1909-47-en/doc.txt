CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES LAW, No. 5 OF 1972 READ WITH THE CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 32 OF 1983 AND THE CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT) ACT,No. 11 OF 1992

Conferment of Powers — Order

IN pursuance of the powers vested in me under the Section 2(2) of the Co-operative Societies Law, No. 5 of 1972 read with Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 1983 and the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 11 of 1992,I, Mallawaarachchige Gamini Jayawickrama Perera, Minister of Food Security do hereby confer on the persons appointed toassist the Commissioner of Co-operative Development and the Registrar of Co-operative Societies and specified in theColumn I of the Schedule hereto, from the date specified in Column II under Co-operative Societies Law, No. 5 of 1972 readwith Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 1983 and the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 11 of1992 and under the rules made there under, the powers specified in Column III of the said schedule.

MALLAWAARACHCHIGE GAMINI JAYAWICKRAMA PERERA,Minister of Food Security.02nd April, 2015,Colombo.
