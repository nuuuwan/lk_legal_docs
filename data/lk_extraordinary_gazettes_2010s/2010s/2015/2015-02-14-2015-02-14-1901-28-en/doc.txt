SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake,Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of the commodity itemsspecified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries inColumn II of that Schedule. This order shall be valid for a period of 04 months commencing from 15th February, 2015, subjectto provisions of Sub-section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1871/35 of 17.07.2014 in respect of Mackerel Fish (HS. code No. 0303.54) and Gazette Extraordinary No. 1892/39 of12.12.2014 in repect of Potatoes (HS Code No. 0701.90) are rescinded with effect from 15th February 2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,

Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,14th February, 2015.
