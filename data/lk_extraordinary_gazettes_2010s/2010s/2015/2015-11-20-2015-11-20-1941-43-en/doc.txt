SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof the commodity items specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified inthe corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 21st November 2015, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1931/7 of 07.09.2015 in respect of Potatoes (HS Code No. 0701.90), Gazette Extraordinary No.1935/45 of 09.10.2015 in respect of B’Onions (HS Code No. 0703.10.20), Gazette Extraordinary No. 1934/32 of01.10.2015 in respect of Fish (HS Heading No. 03.02 and 03.03), Gazette Extraordinary No. 1919/33 of 16.06.2015and Gazette Extraordinary No. 1931/7 of 07.09.2015 in respect of Lentils (HS Code Nos. 0713.40.11, 0713.40.12,0713.40.21, 0713.40.22) are rescinded with effect from 21st November 2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,20th November, 2015.
