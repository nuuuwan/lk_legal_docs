The matter in dispute between the parties is : -

“Whether Mr. U. S. S. K. Rudrigo working as LeaderFireman - Grade C-l, at the Fire and BewareDivision of the Ceylon Petroleum StorageTerminals Ltd. has been caused injustice by beinggiven No pay leave when he applied for leave on14.09.2011 and if so, to what relief he is entitled.”

Applicant was represented by Mr. Ananda Palitha, BranchSecretary of the Jathika Sevaka Sangamaya. The respondentorganization was represented by Attorney at Law Miss

Melani Kudaliyanage. After brief discussion both partiesagree to reslove the matter in dispute on the following basis

a) The day in question, 14.09.2011, which day theapplicant requested casual leave, is approved ; andb) Salary and other dues, arising out of the day inquestion, will be restored.

Both parties sign the notes in my presence confirmingthe agreement.

This is just and fair award.
