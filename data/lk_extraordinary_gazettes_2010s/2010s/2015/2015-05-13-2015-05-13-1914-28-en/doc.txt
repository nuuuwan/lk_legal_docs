NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Ella Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of the Ella Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) ofthe (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance, that Mr. Basnayake Mudiyanselage Udayajeewa Bandara, of theElla Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has ceased to be a member of theUnited National Party.
