Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)Notice Under Section 38(3)

I, Nagalingam Vethanayagan, the Returning Officer, do by this Notice ;

Extend the date of poll of the under-mentioned Local Authorities fixed for 24.03.2012 by notice published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1744/8 of 08.02.2012 under Section 38(3) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262), asamended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 01 of 2002, and appoint 28.02.2015 as the date for takingof the poll of the said Local Authorities.

N. VETHANAYAGAN,Returning Officer,Administrative District of Mullaitivu.District Secretariat,Mullaitivu District,02 nd February, 2015.
