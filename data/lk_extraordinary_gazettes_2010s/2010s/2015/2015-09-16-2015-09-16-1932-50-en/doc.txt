FILLING OF A VACANCY UNDER PARAGRAPH 13(b) OF ARTICLE 99 OF THE CONSTITUTION OFTHE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS the Secretary-General of Parliament has informed me, under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act,No. 1 of 1981, as amended by Act, No. 15 of 1988, that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Eighth Parliament byreason of Resignation of Mr. Don Arther Chamara Sampath Dasanayake, who was declared elected as a Member of Parliamentfor the Electoral District No. 19-Badulla under Section 62 of the aforsaid Act ;

AND WHEREAS the Returning Officer, for the electoral district which returned the said member was directedby me under Section 64(1) of the aforesaid Act to fill this vacancy as provided for under paragraph 13(b) of Article 99 ofthe Constitution ;

AND WHEREAS the said Returning Officer acting under Section 64(2) of the aforesaid Act has made a return inForm “M” set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. Lakshman Pinthu Jayathilake Senevirathnewas declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said electoral district ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, in the exercise of the powers vested inme by Section 64(2) of the aforesaid Act, do hereby notify the general public that Mr. Lakshman Pinthu JayathilakeSenevirathne has been declared elected as a member of the Eighth Parliament for the Electoral District No. 19-Badulla tofill the vacancy caused by reason of Resignation of Mr. Don Arther Chamara Sampath Dasanayake.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Sarana Mawatha,Rajagiriya,16th September, 2015.
