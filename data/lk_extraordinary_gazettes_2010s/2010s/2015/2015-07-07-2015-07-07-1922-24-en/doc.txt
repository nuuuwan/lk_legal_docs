FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEVADAMARADCHI SOUTH WEST PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi to which theformer member, Aalvaapillai Mathiyarasan belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of thatVadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. RamachandranSivachchanthiran being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of Vadamaradchi South West PradeshiyaSabha acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. RamachandranSivachchanthiran elected as a member of the Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,06th July, 2015.
