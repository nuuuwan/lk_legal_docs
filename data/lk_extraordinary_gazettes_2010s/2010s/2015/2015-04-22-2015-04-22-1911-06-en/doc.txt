FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE

MEDIRIGIRIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of removal of Chairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognised political party to whichThebuwaththe Gamaralalage Ananda Sri Dharmasena the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty who shall be declared elected as Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognised political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Widana Arachchilage Padmasiri Hemachandra to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Indika Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in termsof Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Widana Arachchilage Padmasiri Hemachandraelected as Chairman of the Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA HATHURUSINGHA,

Returning Officer,Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Polonnaruwa,21st April, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

UDAPALATHA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Udapalatha Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Up - Country Peoples Front, to which theformer member Mr. Periyasamy Mayilvaganam belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of thatUdapalatha Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Balakrishnan Sridarbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thalangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Thalangama, Returning Officer of UdapalathaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. BalakrishnanSridar elected as a member of the Udapalatha Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. THALANGAMA,

Returning Officer,Udapalatha Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kandy,17th of April, 2015.
