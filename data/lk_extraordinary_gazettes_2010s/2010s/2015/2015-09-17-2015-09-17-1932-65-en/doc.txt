PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Southern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Southern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the SouthernProvince Provincial Council by reason of being elected as a member of the Parliament ;

And whereas the Secretary of the People's Liberation Front, the recognised political party to which the memberbelonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Wahalathanthrige SisiraKumara to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Wahalathanthrige Sisira Kumara, elected as a memberof the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Hambantota in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,17th September, 2015.
