PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Northern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Northern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Northern ProvinceProvincial Council by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recongized political party, to which themember belonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fillsuch vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. KanapathippillaiTharumalingam to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Council Elections Act, No. 02 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Kanapathippillai Tharumalingam, elected as amember of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Jaffna in respect of the which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,14th September, 2015.
