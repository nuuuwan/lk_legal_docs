FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DEPUTY MAYOROF THE MATARA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Deputy Mayor in Matara Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Deputy Mayor hasbeen appointed as Mayor ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party towhich Deegoda Gamage Yasarathna the former Deputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Matara Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shallbe declared elected as Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Kodippili Yakdehiyage Hareendra Ruwan Chinthaka, to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of Matara MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Kodippili YakdehiyageHareendra Ruwan Chinthaka elected as Deputy Mayor of the Matara Municipal Council.

W. H. R. WIJAYA KUMARA,Returning Officer,Matara Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Matara,21st December, 2015.
