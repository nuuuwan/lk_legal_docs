FINANCE BUSINESS ACT, No. 42 OF 2011

CANCELLATION of a licence to carry on finance business under Section 37 of the Finance Business Act, No. 42 of2011.

ARJUNA MAHENDRAN,Chairman,Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The public is hereby informed that the Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka cancelled the licencegranted to Orient Finance PLC to carry on finance business under the Finance Business Act, No. 42 of 2011 with effectfrom 16.07.2015, subsequent to the amalgamation with Bartleet Finance PLC. Further, please be informed that BartleetFinance PLC has changed its name to Orient Finance PLC with effect from 14.08.2015.
