PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a vacancy in the Eastern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Eastern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of the ProvincialCouncils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Eastern Province ProvincialCouncil by reason of Expulsion from the Party ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Muslim Congress, the recognized political party, to which the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Abdhul Razzak KulanthaiMaraikkar to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Abdhul Razzak Kulanthai Maraikkar, elected as amember of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Ampara in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,16th October, 2015.
