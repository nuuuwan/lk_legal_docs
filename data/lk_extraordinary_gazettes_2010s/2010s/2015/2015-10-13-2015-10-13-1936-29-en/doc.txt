FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEKALMUNAI MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Kalmunai Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, to which theformer member, Arulnayagam Amirthalingam belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as amember of that Kalmunai Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. KunjuththampiEhamparam being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Kalmunai Municipal Council,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies inLocal authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Kunjuththampi Ehamparamelected as a member of the Kalmunai Municipal Council.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Kalmunai Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Ampara,08th October, 2015.
