FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

NUWARA - ELIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Up - Country Peoples Front, to which theformer member Mr. Ambaladevan Nagarajah belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of thatNuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Ponniah Ravichanthiranbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hettiyakandage Arunapushpa Saumyasiri Fernando, Returning Officer of Nuwara - EliyaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. PonniahRavichanthiran elected as a member of the Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha.

H. A. S. FERNANDO,

Returning Officer,Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Nuwara - Eliya,03rd March, 2015.
