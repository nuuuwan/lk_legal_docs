WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Provincial Road Development Authority Statute No. 04 of 1989 (Western Province)

REMOVAL FROM OFFICE - CHAIRMAN, DIRECTOR BOARD MEMBER OF PROVINCIALROAD DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (WESTERN PROVINCE)

ACCORDING to the powers entrusted in me by Section 3(8) in Part 01 of the Provincial Road Development Authority StatuteNo. 04 of 1989 (Western Province), I, Kejitan Lalith Wanigarathne, Minister of Provincial Roads, Transport, CooperativeDevelopment and Trade, Fisheries, Housing and Constructions, Urban and Estate Sector Infrastructure Facilities, Industriesand Rural Development of the Western Province, do by this notice announce that Balage Don Vipula Rohana Kulasiri whoheld office of Chairman in the Western Provincial Road Development Authority was removed with immediate effect from theChairmanship and membership of Western Provincial Road Development Authority, whereas, Don Mahinda ChithrasenaRanaweera and Kahavitage Raja Sooriyakumara who held Director Board memberships in the said Authority were removedwith immediate effect from the Director Board membership of the said Authority.

KEJITAN LALITH WANIGARATHNE,Minister of Provincial Roads, Transport,Cooperative Development and Trade,Fisheries, Housing and Construction,Urban and Estate Sector Infrastructure,Industries and Rural Development,Western Province.
