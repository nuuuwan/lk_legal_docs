Government Notifications

MINUTE OF THE GOVERNMENT LIBRARIAN SERVICE - REVISION No. 02

THE Public Service Commission has ordered to revise the Minute of the Government Librarian Service published in theGazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1620/22 dated 24.09.2009 in thefollowing manner.

I.To remove the provision mentioned within the bracket in Para. (a) (ii), Para. 10.3.1.1. of Minute of theGovernment Librarian Service No. 1620/22 dated 24.09.2009 (It is sufficient to have the Service as aLibrarian in Grade III even though Serving as a Librarian in Grade II has been specified).

K.V. P. M. J. GAMAGE,Director General of Combined Services.
