THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No : WEE/LND/AQUIR/44/(WEERA/2014/33). Land Ministry Ref. No : 4-3/6/2014/HW/294.

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends toacquire the following lands which are required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

Lot No. 740 - 741 (Lots 02) allotments in total extent ofabout 0.5172 Hectare, of land in FVP No. 261, SupplementNo. 56 and Sheet No. 56 and dated 26.12.2014 prepared bythe Sinior - Superintendent of Survey of HambanthotaDistrict on behalf of Survey General, situated in theMinor Division of Giruwa Paththuwa North in GramaNiladhari Division of Badigama South in the village ofBadigama in Divisional Secretary’s Division of Weerakatiyain Hambanthota District in the Southern Province.

01. Lot No. 740Name of land: Raththagearahena, Gonhitiyawalawatta;Description: Contains garden, 39 coconut plant trees about1 - 3 years old, 57 coconut trees about 60 - 65 years old and04 palmyra trees; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: D. S. P.

Jagath, No. 385, Mahaweediya, Ambalangoda (Part of the Plotof Land No. 10S); Extent: 0.4023 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 741Name of land: Raththagearahena, Gonhitiyawalawatta;Description: Paddy field; Claimant: State; Original Claimant:D. S. P. Jagath, No. 385, Mahaweediya, Ambalangoda (Part ofthe Plot of Land No. 10S); Extent: 0.1149 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agent duly authorized inwriting, before me at Divisional Secretariat office ofWeerakatiya on the from 07.10.2015 at 9.30 a.m and arehereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on orbefore the 30.09.2015 the nature of their interests in the land,the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said lands, the amount of compensationand the details of the computation of such amount.

C. P. W. WANNIARACHCHI, Divisional Secretary & Acquiring Officer Weeraketiya.
