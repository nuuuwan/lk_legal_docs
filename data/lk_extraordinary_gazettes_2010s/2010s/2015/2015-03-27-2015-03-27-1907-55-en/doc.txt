NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

KIRINDA PUHULWELLA PRADESHIYA SABHA

I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Kirinda Puhulwella Pradeshiya Sabha do herebydeclare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Wellappuli ArachchigeShanaka Janapriya of the Kirinda Puhulwella Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha ashe has ceased to be a member of the United National Party.

W. H. R. WIJAYA KUMARA,

Returning Officer,Kirinda Puhulwella Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Matara,23rd March, 2015.
