NATIONAL ZOOLOGICAL GARDENS ACT

ZOO Development and Welfare Fund was established under Section 8 of National Zoological Gardens Act, No. 41 of 1982and the entrance ticket fees of National Zoological Gardens are being credited to that fund. The rates for the entrance ticketsof Pinnawala Zoo newly opened for the public will be commenced from 01.05.2015 onwards as given below.

M. I. M. RAFEEK,

Secretary,Ministry of Tourism and Sports.23rd of April 2015.
