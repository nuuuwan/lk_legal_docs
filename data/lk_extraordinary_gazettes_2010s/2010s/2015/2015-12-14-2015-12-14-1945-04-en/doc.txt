FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MAYOR OF THEKANDY MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Mayor in Kandy Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichMr. Thushantha Maheendra Rathwaththe of the former Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Kandy Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shall bedeclared elected as Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Sena Dissanayaka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of KandyMunicipal Council acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by fillingof vacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. SenaDissanayaka elected as Mayor of the Kandy Municipal Council.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Kandy Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Kandy,14th December, 2015.
