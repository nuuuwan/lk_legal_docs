DEBT RECOVERY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT

Notification under Section 30

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of the powers vested in the Monetary Board established under the Monetary Law Act(Chapter 422), in terms of Section 30 of the Debt Recovery (Special Provisions) Act, No. 02 of 1990, the MonetaryBoard has, having regard to the current rates of bank interest, determined that with effect from 01st January 2016, theMarket Rate of Interest for the purpose of this Act, shall be five point nine eight per cent (5.98%) per annum.

LAKSHMAN ARJUNA MAHENDRAN,Chairman of the Monetary Board andGovernorCentral Bank of Sri Lanka.

Central Bank of Sri Lanka,Colombo,18th December, 2015.
