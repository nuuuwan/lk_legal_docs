EXTRAORDINARY

1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

RATNAPURA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Ratnapura Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance, to whichthe former member, Abeyratna Victor Kapil Wickramanath belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election asa member of that Ratnapura Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to make a nomination within theperiod specified ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Matara Achchige Padmasiri Chandrawansha Perera, Returning Officer of Ratnapura MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of Vacancies inLocal authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Gangulwitiya Gamage HemanthaSudesh Gamage who has secured the highest number of preferences next to the last of the member declared elected at theelection held on 08th of October 2011 to Ratnapura Municipal Council elected as a member of the Ratnapura MunicipalCouncil in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

M. A. P. C. PERERA,

Returning Officer,Ratnapura Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Ratnapura27th March, 2015.
