NORTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Supplementary Provision for Appropriation Statute No. 04 of 2015

Be it enacted by the Northern Provincial Council as follows :

1. This Statute may be cited as the Supplementary Provision for Appropriation StatuteNo. 04 of 2015.

2. Without prejudice to any other Law which authorizes for any such expenditure, theexpenditure of the Northern Provincial Council during the financial year beginingfrom 1st January 2015 and ending on 31st December 2015 referred to as the “Financialyear 2015” in this Statute re-allocation of Rupees One Hundred and Four MillionNine Hundred and Fifty Seven Thousand Four Hundred and Eighty Eight and SeventyCents only (Rs. 104,957,488.70) from the expenditure the Honourable Governorapproved on the 22nd December 2014 and to the limits stated in the First Scheduleof this Statute shall be met :

A) From the Northern Provincial Council funds or monies, or

B) From the allocation of funds allocated in accordance to Article 154 (R) of theConstitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and any otherfunds allotted or granted by the Government of Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka.

C. V. KANDIYA SIVAGNANAM,M. JEGU,Chairman,Secretary,Northern Provincial Council.Council Secretariat,Northern Provincial Council.10-330/1
