UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Announcement made under the Sub section 2(3)(A)(1) of 2011 No. 02, statute about powers to administrate and monitor LocalGovernment Institutions in Uva Provincial Council

02. According the powers assigned to me under the Sub Section 2(3)(A)(1) of 2011 No. 02 statute about powers toadministrate and monitor Local Government Institutions in Uva Provincial Council, Harin Fernando the Chief Minister of UvaProvince and the Minister of Finance and Planning, Law and Peace, Education, Local Government, Social Welfare and RuralInfrastructure Development and Construction, suspending the duties assigned to Mr. Rajapakse Mudiyansalage Abeysekaraas the Chief Executive (Chairman) of Welimada Pradeshiya Sabha in Badulla District with effect from 22.04.2015.

03. As the duties of the Chief Executive of Welimada Pradeshiya Sabha in Badulla District has been suspended underthe Sub Section 2(3)(A)(1) of 2011 No. 02 Statute about powers to administrate and monitor Local Government Institution inUva Provincial Council the authority will be command to Mr. Herath Mudiyansalage Jayathilaka, the Deputy Chairman ofWelimada Pradeshiya Sabha in Badulla District to implement this authority and to fulfill the duties as per the powers assignedin the above mentioned Statute.

HARIN FERNANDO,Chief Minister of Uva Provinceand the Minister of Finance and Planning, Law and Peace,

Education, Local Government, Social Welfare andRural Infrastructure Development and Construction
