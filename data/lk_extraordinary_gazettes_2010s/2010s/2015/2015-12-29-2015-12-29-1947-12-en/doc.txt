THE INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT, CHAPTER 131 OF THE LEGISLATIVE ENACTMENTS OF CEYLON( 1956 REVISED EDITION)

Revocation of Order under Section 4(1)

WHEREAS by Order made under Section 4(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments ofCeylon (1956 Revised Edition), as amended by Act Nos. 14 and 62 of 1957, 14 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 read with Industrial Disputes(Special Provisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968 dated 24.06.2015 and published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri LankaExtraordinary No. 1921/56 dated 02.07.2015 the Industrial Dispute in respect of the matters specified in the statement of the Commissioner ofLabour dated 18.06.2015.

01. Mr. T. H. Dharmasena, 'Hewawasa', Mahagama (via Kalutara)02. Mr. W. A. Wickramasinghe, No. 56, Talwatta, Gonawala (W.P.)03. Mr. P. M. G. B. Senanayaka, Napana, Gunnepana04. Mrs. A. W. W. Abesiriwardana, Athauda Gardens, Gadaladeniya Road, Pilimathalawa of the one part

and

National Savings Bank, No. 255, Galle Road, Colombo 03 of the other part,

was referred to Mr. T. E. Santharajan for settlement by arbitration.

AND whereas it is now deemed expedient that the said Order be revoked, I, W. D. J. Seneviratne, Minister of Labour and Trade UnionRelations do hereby revoke the said Order and further make Order that no proceedings be taken upon the said Order dated 24.06.2015.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

16th December, 2015.Colombo.

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is
