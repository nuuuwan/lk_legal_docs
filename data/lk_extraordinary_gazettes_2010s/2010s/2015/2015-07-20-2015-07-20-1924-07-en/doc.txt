UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointments made by the Hon. Governor of Uva Province

BY virtue of the powers vested to me by Section 32(1) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, I, M. P. Jayasinghe,Governor of Uva Province do hereby notify that the following appointments have been approved by me.
