FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THEPACHCHILAIPALLI PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichMr. Parnandu Dominic Anton the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall bedeclared elected as a Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Mailvaganam Kumaravelu to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, R. C. Amalraaj, Returning Officer of Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms ofSection 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Mailvaganam Kumaravelu elected as Chairman of thePachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kilinochchi,01 st July, 2015.
