FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THEVALIKAMAM SOUTH WEST PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which ShanmugamSivakumaaran the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decideupon the member of that Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared electedas a Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Sinnathamby Mahenthiran to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of Valikamam South West PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies inLocal Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Sinnathamby Mahenthiranelected as Chairman of the Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,17th June, 2015.
