FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE

SORANATHOTA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THE

LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY

LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance the recognized political party, to whichManamendra Patabendige Leelasena the former Chairman, belonged was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party, who shall bedeclared elected as Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to communicate his decisionwithin the period specified ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rathnayaka Ralalage Asanka Sathis Kumara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of SoranathotaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling ofvacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Wijerathna GamageNeel Premathilaka who had secured the next highest number of preferences at the election held on 17th March, 2011 toSoranathota Pradeshiya Sabha elected as Chairman of the Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha, in respect of which such vacancyoccured.

ASANKA RATHNAYAKA,

Returning Officer,Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Badulla,20th of January, 2015.
