Order under Section 39 (1) of the Sports Act, No 25 of 1973 and Section 54 (3) of the National

Sports Associations Regulations

I, Navin Eranjan Dissanayeke, Minister of Tourism and Sports, in terms of Order No.1907/22 dated 25th March 2015 underSection 39 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1907/ 22 dated 25th March 2015 shouldhave called nominations from all National Sports Association for elections prior to 15 th of May 2015.

But, it was not possible to hold elections. Therefore I, do hereby, under Section 39 (1) of the Sports Act, No. 25 of1973 inform all the under mentioned Sports Associations that I have appointed Mr. K. D. S. Ruwanchandra, Director Generalof the Department of Sports Development as Competent Authorty for the smooth functioning of Sports Associations thatfailed to hold elections in lawful manner during the specified periods of time till such elections are held.

Accordingly in pursuance of power vested in me in respect of the Sports Association mentioned below, by orderpublished in Gazette Extraordinary No.1825/38 dated 30th August 2013 under Section 41 of the Sports Act, No.25 of 1973.I, do hereby direct Mr. K. D. S. Ruwanchandra to convene and hold elections with respect to the following NationalAssociations to elect office bearers.

1. Badminton2. Hockey3. Rugby Football4. Golf5. Tennis6. Bridge7. Boxing8. Volleyball9. Rowing

NAVIN ERANJAN DISSANAYEKE,Minister of Tourism and Sports.

On this 16th day of May 2015,At Colombo.
