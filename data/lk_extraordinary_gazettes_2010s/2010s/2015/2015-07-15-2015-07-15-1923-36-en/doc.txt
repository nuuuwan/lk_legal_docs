NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 83/0213 Hambantota District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 830114 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Hambantota (Block 04) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Divi-sion of 94 - Hambantota West in the Divisional Secretary’s Divi-sion of Hambantota in the District of Hambantota in the Province of Southern should submit his claim to the undersigned before 10th August, 2015 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly pub-lish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 830114 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretary’s, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. G.VIJITHA NANDA KUMAR, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No.1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla 03rd July, 2015
