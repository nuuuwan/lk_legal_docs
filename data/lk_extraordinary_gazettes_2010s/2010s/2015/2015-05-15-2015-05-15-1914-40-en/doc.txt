CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Announcement

I, Sarath Ekanayake, as the Chief Minister of the Central Province, and as the Minister in charge of the subject of LocalGovernment, do hereby enforce following Regulations in respect of allowances payable to the Mayors, Deputy Mayors andMembers of the Municipal Councils, according powers vested in me under Section 289 of the Municipal Councils OrdinanceChapter 252 to be read with the Section 02 of the Provincial Councils Act No. 12 of 1989 (Consequential Provisions). TheseRegulations shall be effective from the 01st of November 2014 and the Regulations published in the Gazette of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, bearing No. 1391/25 and dated 06th May 2005, in respect of this subject shall hereby replacedeffective from the date preceding the said date.

SARATH EKANAYAKE,The Chief Minister and the Minister in charge of the

subject of Local Government,

Central Province

The Chief Minister’s Office,

RULES

01.These Regulations shall be called and known as Regulations in respect of payment of allowances to Mayors,Deputy Mayors and Members of the Municipal Councils in Central Province.

02.An allowance as mentioned below may be payable to the Mayors, Deputy Mayors and Members elected to theMunicipal Councils of the Central Province, effective from 01st of November, 2014.

Mayor– Rs. 30,000.00Deputy Mayor– Rs. 25,000.00Member– Rs. 20,000.00

03. 1.The Mayors, Deputy Mayors and Members of the Municipal Councils shall not entitle for obtaining theseallowances under following instances.

i. Failure to attend Council meetings and committee meetings without the approval of the Council in respect of

the relevant month for which allowances are payable.

ii. Going abroad for private purpose, and outside the functions of the Councils.

iii. Being in custody or imprisonment in respect of any criminal charges.

03. 2.A Mayor, Deputy Mayor or a Member shall not lose his right to claim allowances for failure to attend Councilmeetings or committee meetings, when they are engaged on any offficial function with the formal approval,irrespective of whatever mentioned in Sub section (i) of Section 1 of this Regulation.
