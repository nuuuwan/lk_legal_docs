PROMOTION AND APPOINTMENT APPROVED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

IT is hereby notified that HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT has been pleased to promote Air Marshal Kolitha AravindaGunatilleke, RWP, RSP, VSV, USP, ndc, psc, qfi to the rank of Air Chief Marshal with effect from June 16, 2015 and toappoint him as the Chief of Defence Staff in terms of Section 2 of the Chief of Defence Staff Act, No. 35 of 2009, with effectfrom that date.

By order of His Excellency,

P. B. ABEYKOON,Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01.16th July, 2015.

P.S. No.: CSA/7/6/4.No. 294/2 of 2015

PROMOTION AND APPOINTMENT APPROVED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

IT is hereby notified that HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT has been pleased to promote Air Vice Marshal Gagan PulasthiBulathsinghala, RWP, RSP, USP, ndc, psc, to the rank of Air Marshal with effect from June 15, 2015 and to appoint himunder Article 61E(a) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as the Head of the Sri LankaAir Force, with effect from that date.

By order of His Excellency,

P. B. ABEYKOON,Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01.16th July, 2015.
