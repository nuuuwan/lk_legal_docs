SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect ofthe commodity items specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified inthe corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 08th September 2015, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1924/9 of 20.07.2015 in respect of Sugar (HS Heading Nos. 17.01) are rescinded with effect from08th September 2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,07th September, 2015.
