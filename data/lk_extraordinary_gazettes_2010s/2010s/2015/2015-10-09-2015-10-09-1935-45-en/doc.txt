SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, LakshmanYapa Abeywardana, Acting Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order imposein respect of the commodity items specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the ratesspecified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 04 monthscommencing from 9th October 2015, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special CommodityLevy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1931/7 of 07.09.2015 in respect B’Onions (HS Code No. 0703.10.20) are rescinded with effectfrom 9th October 2015.

LAKSHMAN YAPA ABEYWARDANA,Acting Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,09th October, 2015.
