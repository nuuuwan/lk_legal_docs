FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEDELFT PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Delft Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of failing to make and subscribean oath or affirmation in terms of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance towhich the former member, Vayithilingam Sangarappillai belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a memberof that Delft Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to communicate his decisionwithin the period specified ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of Delft Pradeshiya Sabha acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Santhiyagappu Castona elected as amember of the Delft Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Delft Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,05th July, 2015.
