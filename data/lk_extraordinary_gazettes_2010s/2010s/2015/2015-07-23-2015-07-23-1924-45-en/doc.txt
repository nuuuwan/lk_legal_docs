IT is hereby announced to the public that following Council Convention No. M.C 5(1) was adopted at the Meeting of theCouncil which was held on 14.07.2015 by the Municipal Council, Kurunegala as per the powers assigned by the Sub-section (1) of Section 3 of Local Government Institutions (Standard By -laws) Act No.6 of 1952 , being the 261st authority.

ANURADHA GAMINEE PERAMUNAGE, Mayor, Municipal Council, Kurunegala.

17th July 2015,At the office of the Municipal Council,Kurunegala.

Proposal

PROPOSAL TO EMBRACE OF STANDARD BY-LAWS OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT INSTITUTIONSTHAT WERE ESTABLISHED IN THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTE No. 541/17 AND 20.01.1989

I, propose to this Council to implement Standard By-laws depicted in the below mentioned annex mentioned inthe Standard By-laws series which were occurred to be published in the Extraordinary Gazette Paper No.541/17 and20.01.1989 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka made by Minister in charge of the Local Government asper the authority assigned under Sub section (1) of Section 2 of Local Government Institutions (Standard By-laws) No. 6of 1952 which is the 261st authority to accept as Standard By-laws relevant to the Municipal Council Kurunegala from theday that this proposal is published in the Gazette paper under the section 3 of (1).

Mayor,Municipal Council, Kurunegala.
