FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE

SORANATHOTA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Vice Chairmanhas been appointed as Chairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance the recognized political party, to whichWijerathna Gamage Neel Premathilaka the Former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shallbe declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Balraman Vedasalam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Rathnayaka Ralalage Asanka Sathis Kumara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of SoranathotaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling ofvacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr.BalramanVedasalam elected as Vice Chairman of the Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha.

ASANKA RATHNAYAKA,

Returning Officer,Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Badulla,01st April, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF A MEMBER OF THE

CHAVAKACHCHERI PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS

ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely IIankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi to which theFormer member Vijayaratnam Edwin Daniel , belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of thatChavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to make a nomination within theperiod specified ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Ponnuththurai Kuganathan, Returning Officer of Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Thankarasa Srivengadesvaran who had securedthe highest number of preferences next to the last of the member declared at the election held on 23rd July, 2011 toChavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha elected as a member of the Chavakachcheri elected Pradeshiya Sabha in respect of wichsuch vacancy occured.

P. KUGANATHAN,Returning Officer,Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,31st March 2015.
