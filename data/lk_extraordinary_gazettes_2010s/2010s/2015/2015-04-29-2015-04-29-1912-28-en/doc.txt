AWARD

The Honourable Minister of Labour Relations Gamini Lokuge,do by virtue of the powers vested in him by Section 4(1) ofthe Industrial Disputes Act, Chapter131, of the LegislativeEnactments of Ceylon (1956 Revised Edition), as amendedby Act, Nos. 14 of 1957, 4 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 (read withIndustrial Disputes – Special Provisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968hereby appointed me on 10.06.2014 to be the Arbitrator andreferred the above said dispute to me for settlement byArbitration.

Statement of matter in dispute:

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is:

"Whether the employees of the Cinnamon Grand Hotelhave been caused injustice by being punished in accordancewith the disciplinary practice implemented with effect from01.01.2010, and if so, to what reliefs they are entitled".

Appearances:

Party of the First Part:

Mr. Joseph Leon Arulanandan Arulvasagam-Representative.

Mr. Majuwan Kankanamlagea Hemapala represents for theJathika Sevaka Sangamaya - (Parent Union)

Mr. Honnanthara Arachchige Roshana Sarath Philip, thePresident of the Cinnamon Grand Hotel - Jathika SevakaSangamaya - (Branch Union).

Party of the Second Part:

Mr. Chameel Perera Attorney-at-Law appears for the Party ofthe Second Part.

Mr. Sujeeva Cooray - Director Human Resource and VicePresident John Keels Hodings PLC represents CinnamonGrand Hotel.

Both Parties stated, above have submitted their respectivestatement that has lead to the dispute in terms of theRegulation 21(1) of the Industrial Dispute Regulation, 1958.Thereafter, Written Submissions were ordered on thePreliminary Objection made by the Party of the Second Partregarding jurisdiction of Court.

After, having analayed red both the Statements and WrittenSubmissions thereto, it is clearly discerned that there is nodispute between the parties. This fact was explained in detailand the end result was that the party of the First Partconsented to settle the dispute reserving the right, if any, tomake a fresh complaint.

There is, therefore, no dispute in existence now.

I make no Award.

I consider this Award as just and equitable in thecircumstances.

T. EDMUND SANTHARAJAN,Arbitrator.

At Colombo,Dated 16th March, 2015.
