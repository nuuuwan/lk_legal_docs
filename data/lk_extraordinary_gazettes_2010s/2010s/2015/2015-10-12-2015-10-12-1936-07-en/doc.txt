Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE TEA GROWING AND MANUFACTURING TRADE

IT is hereby notified under regulation 30 of the Wages Boards Regulations, that Mr. U. C. Perera and Mr. R. G. Walpola havebeen appointed as Workers' Representative and Employer's Representative, respectively of the Wages Board for the TeaGrowing and Manufacturing Trade by the Hon.
