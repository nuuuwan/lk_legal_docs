WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Pawnbrokers’ Statute of the Western ProvinceProvincial Council No. 01 of 2015

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 53 of the Pawnbrokers’ Statute No. 01 of 2015 of the Western ProvincialCouncil, I Edirisingha Arachchige Isura Dewapriya Dharmasena Perera, Chief Minister and Minister of Finance and PlanImplementation, Engineering Services, Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration, Economic Promotion,Power and Energy, Environmental Affairs, Water Supply and Drainage and Tourism of the Western Province ProvincialCouncil, do hereby declare that the following regulations and read with relevant Section of the above Statute, shall come toeffect from Gazette date.

EDIRISINGHA ARACHCHIGE ISURA DEWAPRIYA DHARMASENA PERERA,Chief Minister and Minister of Finance and Plan Implementation,Engineering Services, Law and Order, Local Government andProvincial Administration, Economic Promotion, Power and Energy,Environmental Affairs, Water Supply and Drainage and Tourism of theWestern Province Provincial Council.Office of the Chief Minister,‘‘Shrawasthi Mandiraya’’,Colombo 07.16th October 2015REGULATIONS

1. These regulations may be cited as the regulations under Pawnbroker’s Statue of the Western Provincial Council No.01 of 2015

2. The following fees and deposit should be charged on Pawn Broking Business under Sub section 7(3) and 9(2) ofthis statute,

DescriptionFee Rs.

a. Registration of Pawn Broking Business25,000/-b. Annual License fee payable for Pawn Broking Business5,000/-c. Amount of security payable by Pawn Broking Business holder100,000/-

3. Security deposit shall be credited only to a deposit account of People Bank, Bank of Ceylon and National SvingsBank.
