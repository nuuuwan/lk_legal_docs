CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointment made by the Hon. Governor of the Eastern Provins Provincial Council

I, Kalupage Austin Fernando, Governor of the Eastern Province by virtue of power vested in me under Section 154 F (1)and (5) of the Thirteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka do herebyOrder publish the resignation tendered on 21.08.2015 by Hon. Ibrahim Mohamed Mohamed Mansoor, Member ofProvincial Council of the Eastern Provincial Council and declare that he is no more a Minister of the Board of Ministersof the Eastern Provincial Council as appointed by order published by me in the Extra Ordinary Gazette of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1905/ 05 dated 09.03.2015 and Hon. Ahamed Nazeer Zainulabdeen, Member and theChief Minister of the Eastern Provincial Council shall temporarily cover the powers, functions and duties of that vacantMinistry until a permanent arrangement is made.

AUSTIN FERNANDO, Governor - Eastern Province.

Governor's Secretariat,Eastern Province,Orr's Hill,Trincomalee,25th August 2015.
