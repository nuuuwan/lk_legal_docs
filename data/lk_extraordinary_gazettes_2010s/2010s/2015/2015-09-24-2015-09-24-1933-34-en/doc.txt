PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Northern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Northern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Northern ProvinceProvincial Council by reason of being elected as a Member of the Parliament ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recongized political party, towhich the member belonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible personto fill such vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. AgilathasSivakkolunthu to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Agilathas Sivakkolunthu, elected as a member ofthe above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Jaffna in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,22nd September, 2015.
