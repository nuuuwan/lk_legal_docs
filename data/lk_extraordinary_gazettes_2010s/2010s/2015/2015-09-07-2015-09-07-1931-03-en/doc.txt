PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Western Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Western Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the WesternProvince Provincial Council by reason of Resignation ;

And whereas the Secretary of the People's Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Rambukana VidanapathiranaSarath to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Rambukana Vidanapathirana , elected as a member ofthe above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Colombo in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya.07th September, 2015.
