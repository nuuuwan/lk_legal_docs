FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

KEGALLE PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People’s Freedom Alliance to whichthe former Member, Kudagoda Bopage Sarath Rupasena belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. KaluarachchilageJanaranjana Saman Kumara Kaluarchchi being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha acting in termsof Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Kaluarachchilage Janaranjana Saman KumaraKaluarchchi elected as a member of the Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,

Returning Officer,Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kegalle,17th March, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE

DIMBULAGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of removal ofChairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognised political party to whichSinhalage Jagath Samarawickrama the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall bedeclared elected as Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognised political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Pushpa Kumara Ranasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Indika Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in termsof Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Pushpa Kumara Ranasinghe elected asChairman of the Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA HATHURUSINGHE,

Returning Officer,District Elections Office,Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha.Polonnaruwa,16th March, 2015.04 - 88
