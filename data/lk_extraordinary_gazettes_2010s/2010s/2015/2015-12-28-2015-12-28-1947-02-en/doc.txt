(Published by Authority)PART IV (A) - PROVINCIAL COUNCILS

1A

CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notice by the Governor of the Central Province Provincial Council

KNOW ye that by virtue of powers vested by the Articles 154(b:(8):(a) of he Thirteenth Amendment to the Constitutionof the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Surangani Vishaka Ellawala, Governor of the Central Province, do bythis notice prorogue the Third Session of the Sixth Provincial Council of the Central Province with effect from 18thDecember Two Thousand Fifteen.

(02) By virtue of powers vested in me by paragraph 8(a) of the said Article, I do hereby summon the said Council tomeet at the Chamber of the Central Province Provincial Council Complex at Pallekelle, Kundasale on Tuesday the 16thFebruary, Two Thousand Sixteen at 10.00 a. m.

SURANGANI VISHAKA ELLAWALA,Governor of the Central Province.

Given at Kandy, this 15th day of December, Two Thousand Fifteen.
