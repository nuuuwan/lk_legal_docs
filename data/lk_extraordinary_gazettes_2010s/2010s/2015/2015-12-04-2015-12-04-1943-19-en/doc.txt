UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointments made by the Governor of the Uva Provincial Council

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 32(1) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1947, I, M. P. Jayasinghe,Governor of Uva Province, do hereby notify that the following appointments have been approved by me.

01. Mr. A. B. M. Wijeyathunga, Officer of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Service, to perform the duties of the Director ofProvincial Agriculture (Uva), from the date 22.08.2015 to 29.08.2015, till the return of Mr. R. M. Nandasiri, to theIsland who is the Director of Provincial Agriculture (Uva).

02. Mr. G. A. Ariyasena Gurusinghe, Officer of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Service, to act as Deputy Director ofAgriculture (Training) of Bindunuwewa in-service Training Institute, on full time basis with effect from the date01.08.2015.

03. Mr. A. B. M. Wijeyathunga, Class I Officer of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Service, to act as Director to Agricultureof Uva Province, in addition to his permanent post, from the date 01.10.2015.

04. Dr. H. G. G. L. P. Ariyadasa, to perform the Duties of the Regional Director to the Health Service of Monaragala, inaddition to his permanent post, from the date 07.11.2015 to 22.11.2015, till the return of Dr. P. D. K. Adhikari, to theIsland who is the Regional Director to the Health Service of Monaragala.

05. Mr. R. M. Ananda, Class I officer of the Sri Lanka Educational Administrative Service, to perform the duties ofthe Director to Education of Uva Province, in addition to his permanent post, from the date 06.11.2015 to18.11.2015, till the return of Mr. R. M. P. Rathnayake, to the Island who is the Director to Education of UvaProvince.

06. Mr. J. A. S. P. Jayasinghe, Class III officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, to act as Secretary to the Co-operative Service Commission of Uva Province, in addition to his permanent post, with effect from the date18.11.2015.
