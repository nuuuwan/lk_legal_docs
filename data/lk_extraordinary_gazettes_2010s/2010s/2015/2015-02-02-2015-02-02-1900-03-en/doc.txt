Government Notifications

L. D. B. 2/ 2015.

CODE OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE ( SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 2013

Order under Section 7 (2)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 7 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2of 2013, I, Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, Minister of Justice and Labour Relations do by this Order extend for a further period notexceeding two years the operation of the provisions of the aforesaid Act, with effect from 2nd February, 2015.

DR. WIJEYADASA RAJAPAKSHE, P. C.Minister of Justice and Labour Relations.

Colombo,2nd February, 2015.
