PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THENORTH WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

WHEREAS the Secretary of the North Western Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the North WesternProvince Provincial Council by reason of being elected as a member of the Parliament ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recongized political party, to which themember belonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fillsuch vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Al. M. S. Sahabdeento fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Council Elections Act, No. 02 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Al. M. S. Sahabdeen, elected as a member of theabove Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Kurunegala in respect of the which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,10th September, 2015.
