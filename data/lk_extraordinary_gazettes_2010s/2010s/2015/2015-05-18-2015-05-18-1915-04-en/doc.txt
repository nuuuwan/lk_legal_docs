PART III — LANDS

Land Reform Commission Notices

LAND REFORMS ACT, No. 71 OF 1972

Order under Sub Section (4) of Section 27 a

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Sub section (4) of Section 27 a of the Land Reform Act, No. 01 of 1972 of theParliament amended by Land Reform (special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981, which should be read with Sub Aricle (2) ofArticle 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Munasinghe Kariyawasam AppuhamilageDon Somadasa Gunawardana, Minister of Lands do hereby:-

(a) Amend the Order made under sub section (2) of section 27A of the aforesaid Act, and published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 815/10 dated 21 April 1994 relating to the land called and known as Walahanduwa Waththadepicted in detailed in First Schedule of this order and alienated to Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporationestablished under Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation Act, No. 04 of 1958, by revoking on grounds of non-compliance with such terms of conditions relating to consideration for the alienation to Sri Lanka StatePlantation Corporation, such portion of the said order as relates to the extent of lands depicted in secondschedule hereto which is part of the land called and known as Walahanduwa Waththa.

(b) Further I do hereby declare that upon revocation, the extent of lands shall be re-alienated to Land ReformCommission.

M. K. A. D. S. GUNAWARDANA,Minister of Lands.

Ministry of Lands,Mihikatha Medura,1200/6, Rajamalwaththa Road,Battaramulla.14 th May, 2015.
