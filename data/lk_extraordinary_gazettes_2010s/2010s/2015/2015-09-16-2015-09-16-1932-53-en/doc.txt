PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988Filling of a vacancy in theCentral Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Central Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of the ProvincialCouncils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Central Province ProvincialCouncil by reason of being elected as a Member of the Parliament ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recongized political party, towhich the member belonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible personto fill such vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Kamal Ajith KumaraBeligolla to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Kamal Ajith Kumara Beligolla, elected as amember of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Kandy in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,16th September, 2015.
