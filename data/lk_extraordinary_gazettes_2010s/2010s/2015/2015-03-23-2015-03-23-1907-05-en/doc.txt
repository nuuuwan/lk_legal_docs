EXTRAORDINARY

1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

MORATUWA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Moratuwa Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember, Yon Meregngnage Chaminda Kumara Silva belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a memberof that Moratuwa Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Athula Kernal Peirisbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Moratuwa Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of Vacancies in Local authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Athula Kernal Peiris elected as a member of theMoratuwa Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Moratuwa Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo.23rd March, 2015.
