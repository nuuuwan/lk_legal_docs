NOTIFICATION

IT is hereby notified that His Excellency the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka andCommander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces has been graciously pleased to confer the Vishista Sewa Vibhushanaya at anInvestiture to be held at the Presidential Secretariat on the eight day of October in the year Two Thousand and Fifteen onthe Officers named in the Schedule hereto in terms of Clause Four of the Presidential Warrant instituting the VishistaSewa Vibhushanaya published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 156/5 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankaissued on the first day of September in the year One Thousand Nine Hundred and Eighty one.

By His Excellency’s Command.
