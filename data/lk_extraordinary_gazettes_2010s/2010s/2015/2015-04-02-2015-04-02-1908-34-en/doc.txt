SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

01. Notification under the section 184 of the Urban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255) and the Provincial Council (ConsequentialProvisions) Act No.12 of 1989 .

02. I, Maheepala Herath, Chief Minister and Minister of Law and Order, Finance and Planning, Local Government,Health and indigenous Medicine, Women Affairs, Co-operatives, Trade and Food, Transport and Estate Welfare, Environment,News, Housing and Construction of the Sabaragamuwa Province, do hereby appoint a retired Judicial Officer, Hon. SamanWickrama Wickramarachchi (Retired High Court Judge), as a single person tribunal of inquiry as per the powers vested in meby the provisions of Section 184 (1A) of the Urban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255) to inquire and submit a report based onthe matters under section 184 (1) of the Urban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255), regarding the functions of the Chairman ofthe Embilipitiya Urban Council, Ven. Vewelduwe Gnanaprabha Thero.

03. I, Maheepala Herath, Chief Minister and Minister of Law and Order, Finance and Planning, Local Government,Health and indigenous Medicine, Women Affairs, Co-operatives, Trade and Food, Transport and Estate Welfare, Environment,News, Housing and Construction of the Sabaragamuwa Province, do hereby temporally suspend with immediate effect, Ven.Vewelduwe Gnanaprabha Thero, Chairman of the Embilipitiya Urban Council of the Ratnapura District, functioning as theChairman of the aforesaid Urban Council, as per the powers vested in me by the provisions of Section 184 (1B) (A) (i) of theUrban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255)

04. As the functions of Ven. Vewelduwe Gnanaprabha Thero as the Chairman of the Embilipitiya Urban Council hasbeen temporally suspended with immediate effect under the provisions of section 184 of the Urban Council Ordinance(Chapter 255) and the Provincial Council (Consequential Provisions) Act No.12 of 1989, I do hereby order Mr. W.G. LalithDharmakeerthi, Deputy Chairman of the Embilipitiya Urban Council to execute the powers and carry out the tasks thereof,under the section 184 (1B) (A) (i) of the Urban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255) and the Provincial Council (ConsequentialProvisions) Act No.12 of 1989 .

.

MAHEEPALA HERATHChief Minister and Minister of Law and Order, Finance and Planning,Local Government, Health and indigenous Medicine, Women Affairs,

Co-operatives, Trade and Food, Transport and Estate Welfare,

Environment, News, Housing and Construction

of the Sabaragamuwa Province.

On this 02nd of April, 2015,Chief Ministry of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council,New Town, Ratnapura.
