NORTH WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notice made under the 5(I) of Paragraph II relevant to the General and Private Cemeteries Act, No. 57 of 1946

IT is hereby published that I, Jayasekara Pathirennehelage Dayasiri Pathmakumara Jayasekara, as the Chief Minister andThe Local Government Minister of the North Western Provincial Council, with accordance to the powers entrusted to mefrom the provisions given by Section 5(I) of Paragraph (A) of the Sub section II of Section II of Section 2 of the ProvincialCouncil Act, No. 12 of 1989 (Consequential Regulations), the cemeteries which does cremations or inhumations, asunder mention Schedule I and II situated within the Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha Limits of Kurunegala District of NorthWestern Province, as general cemeteries.

J. P. DAYASIRI PATHMAKUMARA JAYASEKARA, Attorny-at-LawChief Minister of the North Western Provincial Counciland The Finance and Planning, Law and Peace, LocalGovernment and Provincial Administration, Human Resources,Educational and Cultural Affairs, Lands, Environment, Tourism,Investments Coordinating, Co-operative Developmentand Food Supply and Distribution Minister,North Western Province.

Chief Minister's Office,Kurunegala,15th July 2015.
