L.D. B. 21/78(XII).THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27 (1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 27 (1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Kabir Hashim, Ministerof Higher Education, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, amend the Scheduleto the Order made under section 21 of the said Act in relation to the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka published inGazette Extraordinary, No. 896/2 of November 7, 1995 as last amended by order published in Gazette Extraordinary,No. 1691/13 of February 1, 2011, by the repeal of the item “Faculty of Social Studies and Languages ” appearing in ColumnI of such Schedule and its corresponding entries appearing in Column II thereto, and the substitution therefore of thefollowing :-
