FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

COLOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Democratic Peoples Front to which theformer member, Thiagaraja Nirosh belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominatewithin a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of that Colombo MunicipalCouncil who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Aananthan MurugaiahThirubagara Kumar being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Colombo Municipal Council acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Aananthan Murugaiah Thirubagara Kumarelected as a member of the Colombo Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo,26th March, 2015.
