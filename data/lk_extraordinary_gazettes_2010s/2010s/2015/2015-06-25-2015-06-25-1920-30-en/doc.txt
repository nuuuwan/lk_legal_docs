FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEHOMAGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the Party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely People's Liberation Front, to whichthe Former member W. M. Ajith Premakumara belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a memberof that Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. SusithSamarawickrama Liyanage being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy :

NOW THEREFORE I, Rajapakshage Vajira Nishantha Gunawardana , Returning Officer of Homagama PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Susith SamarawickramaLiyanage elected as a member of the Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha.

VAJIRA GUNAWARDANA,Returning Officer,Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Colombo,24th June 2015.
