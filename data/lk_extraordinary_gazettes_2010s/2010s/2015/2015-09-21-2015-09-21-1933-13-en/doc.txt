THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

WHEREAS I have in the exercise of powers vested in me in terms of paragraph (1) of Article 43 of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, determined the number of Ministers of the Cabinet of Ministers and the Ministriesand the assignment of subjects and functions and Departments, Public Corporations & Statutory Institutions to the saidMinisters ;

It is now hereby notified that the subjects and functions and Departments, Public Corporations & StatutoryInstitutions in the charge of various Ministers shall be as set out in the Schedule given below, from the date of thisNotification.

All such subjects and functions and Departments, Public Corporations & Statutory Institutions that are not specificallyassigned to any Minister will continue to remain in my charge as the President.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,September 21, 2015.
