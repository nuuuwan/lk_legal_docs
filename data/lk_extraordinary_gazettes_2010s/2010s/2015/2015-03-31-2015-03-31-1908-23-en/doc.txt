THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Sarath Amunugama,Minister of Higher Education and Research, do by this order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act in relation to the Rajarata University of Sri Lankapublished in Gazette Extraordinary No. 896/2 of November 7, 1995 and as last amended by Order published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1856/5 of March 31, 2014, by the subsitution for the item “Facultry of Social Sciences and Humanities”appearing in Column I of such Schedule and its corresponding entries appearing in Column II thereto, and the substitutiontherefore of the following :-

“Column IColumn IIFacultyDepartments

Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities1. Department of Social Studies2. Department of Humanities3. Department of Archeology and Heritage Management4. Department of Environmental Management5. Department of Languages.”.

DR. SARATH AMUNUGAMA,Minister of Higher Education and Research.

Colombo,30th March 2015.
