FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

PALINDANUWARA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Ranaweera Patikirige Bandupema belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member ofthat Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Galpayage Don TrimalSampath being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hennedige Rasika Priyanga Peiris, Returning Officer of Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Galpayage Don Trimal Sampathelected as a member of the Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha.

RASIKA PEIRIS,Returning Officer,Palindanuwara Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kalutara,29th March, 2015.
