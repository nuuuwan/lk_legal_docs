PART III — LANDS

Land Reform Commission Notices

ORDER UNDER SUB SECTION (4), SECTION 27‘a’ OF THE LAND REFORM ACT, No. 01 OF 1972 OF THEPARLIAMENT

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27a(4) of the Land Reform Act, No. 01 of 1972 amended by the Land Reform(Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981 of the Parliament, I, Munasingha Kariyawasam Appuhamilage Don SomadasaGunawardana, Minister of Lands, do hereby,

(a)Amend the order by way of revocation of the order made on the portion of land in relation to the extent of landof the land called Nalanda Waththa depicted in the Second Schedule of the order made under Section 27a(1)of the Land Reform Act, No. 01 of 1972 in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 181/12 dated 27th February 1982in respect of the land called and mentioned as Nalanda Waththa, which has been alienated to Sri Lanka StatePlantation Corporation established under Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation Act, No. 4 of 1958 anddepicted comprehensively in first Schedule of this order, due to not following the order or conditions of theAgreement made of alienation of the land to Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation.

(b)It is hereby declared that the extent of land depicted sepcifically in Second Schedule of this order is alienatedagain to the Land Refrom Commission as a result of this revocation.

M. K. A. D. S. GUNAWARDANA,Minister of Lands.

Ministry of Lands,Land Secretariat,Mihikatha Medura,No. 1200/6, Rajamalwaththa Avenue,Battaramulla05th October, 2015.
