NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 72/0140 Monaragala District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 720029 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Pahatha Arawa (Block 07) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Nilad-hari Division of 126B - Pahatha Arawa in the Divisional Secre-tariat Division of Siyambalanduwa in the District of Monaragala in the Province of Uwa should submit his claim to the under-signed before 15th October, 2015 In the event of failure to sub-mit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Ga-zette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 720029 referred to above may be persued at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Title Settlement. G. VIJITHA NANDA KUMAR, Commissioner of Title Settlement.
