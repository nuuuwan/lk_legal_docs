PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Sabaragamuwa Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sabaragamuwa Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the SabaragamuwaProvince Provincial Council by reason of died ;

And whereas the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party, has nominated Mr. Ajith Kumara Meddagama,to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Ajith Kumara Meddagama, elected as a member of theabove Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Ratnapura in respect of such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Rajagiriya,10th March, 2015.
