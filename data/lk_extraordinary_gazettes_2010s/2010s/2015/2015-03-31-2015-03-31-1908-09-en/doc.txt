Order No. 33

CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Order under Section 20(5)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 20(5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, the ConsumerAffairs Authority orders that no manufacturer, importer, packer, distributor or trader shall sell, expose or offer for sale, displayfor sale any 50 kg bag of Cement bearing the following generic names and sold under any Brand name, above the maximumretail prices given below.

Maximum Retail Price

(per 50 kg bag)

Ordinary Portland Cement870.00Portland Limestone Cement870.00Masonry Cement870.00

This Order shall come into effect from 31st March 2015.

The Consumer Affairs Authority do by this Order rescind Order No. 30 Published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1902/ 47dated 20th February 2015.
