PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Southern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Southern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the SouthernProvince Provincial Council by reason of being elected as a member of the Parliament ;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognised political party to which themember belonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Kariyawasam PethangodageSisira Kumararathne to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Kariyawasam Pethangodage Sisira Kumararathne,elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Galle in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,16th September, 2015.
