THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20 (4)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20 (4) of Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, on being satisfied that the administration of the affairs of the GampahaWickramarachchi Ayurveda Institute, Sri Lanka have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said Institutehave failed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Professor Jayantha Walihinda to be the CompetentAuthority with effect from November 26, 2015, for the purpose of exercising, performing or discharging in lieu ofDirector of the Gampaha Wickramrachchi Ayurveda Institute, Sri Lanka any power, duty or function respectively whichhas been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to, the said Director under the aforesaid Act or any Order, Oridnance,Statute, By-law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education andHighways.

Colombo,24th November, 2015.
