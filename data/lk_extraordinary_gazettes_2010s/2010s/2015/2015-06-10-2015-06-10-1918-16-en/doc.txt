1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

COLOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember, Nadil Dhushantha Malagoda belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of thatColombo Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Aluth GedaraChaminda being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Colombo Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of Vacancies in Local authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Aluth Gedara Chaminda elected as a member ofthe Colombo Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo,10th June, 2015.
