UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the Nagananda International Institute for Buddhist Studies (pvt) Ltd. a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under theCompanies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 94125, do by this Order, recognize the NaganandaInternational Institute for Buddhist Studies (pvt) Ltd. as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing HigherEducation therein, leading to the award of the Degrees specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, of which theabbreviations are specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of the Schedule hereto.

SCHEDULE
