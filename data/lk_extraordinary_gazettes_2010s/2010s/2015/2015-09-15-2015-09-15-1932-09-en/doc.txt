UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointment made by the Governor of Uva Province

BY virtue of powers vested to me under Section 154 F. (4) of the 13th Amendment to the Constitution of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, M. P. Jayasinghe, Governor of the Uva Province, do hereby announce that with effectfrom the date 15th September, 2015, Hon. Arthur Chamara Sampath Dassanayake Don was appointed as the Chief Ministerof the Provincial Council of Uva Province.

M. P. JAYASINGHE,Governor of Uva Province.Office of the Governor - Uva,Badulla,

On this 15th day of September 2015.
