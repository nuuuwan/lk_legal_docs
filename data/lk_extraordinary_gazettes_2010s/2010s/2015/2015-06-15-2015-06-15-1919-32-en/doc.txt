CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Pawn Brokers Statute No. 04 of 2014 of the Central Province

ORDER UNDER SECTION 50(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 50(1) of the Pawn Brokers Statute No. 04 of 2014 of the Central ProvinceProvincial Council I, Sarath Ekanayake, the Chief Minister and Minister of Finance and Planning, Law and Order, LocalGovernment and Provincial Administration, Manpower, Education, Cultural Affairs, Tourism, Lands, Co-operativeDevelopment, Trade and Commerce, Food Supplies and Distributional Affairs and Investment Co-ordination of the CentralProvince, do hereby declare that the following orders pertaining to the business of Pawn broking shall come into affect from10th June 2015.

ORDERS

A. 8(3) Sub section --Rs. 25,000.00i. Registration fee applicable for a Pawn Broking Business

B. 10(2) Sub section -i. Annual License fee for Pawn Broking Business-Rs. 5,000.00ii. Security to be deposit to be made by a license holder- Rs. 100,000.00of Pawn Broking Business

C. 10(3) Sub section -i. State Bank in which the security deposit be made- National Saving Bankby the Licence holder

D. Whenever, the transactions of pawning is taken place the Pawn Broker should ensure that the copy of NationalIdentity Card, Valid Passport or Driving Licence of the person concern, should be obtain and kept under safecustody.

SARATH EKANAYAKE,Chief Minister and Minister of Finance and Planning,Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration,Manpower, Education, Cultural Affairs, Tourism, Lands,Co-operative Development, Trade and Commerce, Food Supplies andDistributional Affairs and Investment Co-ordination of the Central ProvinceProvincial Council.Office of the Chief Minister,Central Province Provincial Council,10th June 2015.
