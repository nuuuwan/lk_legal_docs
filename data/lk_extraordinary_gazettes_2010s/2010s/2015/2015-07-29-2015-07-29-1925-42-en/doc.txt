FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THEVALIKAMAM SOUTH WEST PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of ViceChairman has been appointed as Chairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to whichSinnathamby Mahenthiran the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shallbe declared elected as a Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Arumugam Sinniah Kanesavel to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, Returning Officer of Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Arumugam Sinniah Kanesavelelected as Vice Chairman of the Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,27th July, 2015.
