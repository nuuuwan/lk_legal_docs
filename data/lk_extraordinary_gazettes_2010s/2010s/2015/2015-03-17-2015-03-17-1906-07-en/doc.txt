EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake,Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of the commodity itemsspecified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries inColumn II of that Schedule. This order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from 18th March 2015, subject toprovisions of Sub-section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1901/28 of 14.02.2015 in respect of Mackerel Fish (HS. Code No. 0303.54) is rescinded with effect from 18th March 2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,

Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,17th March, 2015.
