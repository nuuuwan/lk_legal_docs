FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THENEGOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Negombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being elected as amember of the Western Province Provincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance towhich the former member, M. S. M. Sakawulla belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a memberof that Negombo Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. JayasinghaArachchige Don Sanath Anil Prasanna being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Negombo MunicipalCouncil acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Jayasingha ArachchigeDon Sanath Anil Prasanna elected as a member of the Negombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATHNA,Returning Officer,Negombo Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Gampaha,18th November, 2015.
