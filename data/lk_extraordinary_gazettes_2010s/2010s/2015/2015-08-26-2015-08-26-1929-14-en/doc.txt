PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Central Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Central Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the CentralProvince Provincial Council by reason of Nullification of the office of a member of the said Provincial Council;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. AN. D. Nimal Premawanshato fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. AN. D. Nimal Premawansha, elected as a memberof the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Kandy in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya.24th August, 2015.
