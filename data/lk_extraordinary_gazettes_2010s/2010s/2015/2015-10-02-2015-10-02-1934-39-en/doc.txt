PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a vacancy in theSabaragamuwa Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sabaragamuwa Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the SabaragamuwaProvince Provincial Council by reason of Nullification of the office of a member of the said Provincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party, to which the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. BasuriyageAriyawansha to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Basuriyage Ariyawansha, elected as a member ofthe above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Ratnapura in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,01st October, 2015.

PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a vacancy in theNorth Central Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the North Central Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the North CentralProvince Provincial Council by reason of being elected as Member of the Parliament ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recongized political party, to which the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Anuradhanayaka K.to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Anuradhanayaka K., elected as a member of theabove Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Anuradhapura in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,01st October, 2015.
