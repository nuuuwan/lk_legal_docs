1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

GAMPAHA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Gampaha Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember, Guruge Susantha Perera belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of that GampahaMunicipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Mudali MaheepalaAppuhamillage Waruna Pradeep Maheepala being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathne, Returning Officer of Gampaha Municipal Council,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of Vacancies in Localauthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Mudali Maheepala AppuhamillageWaruna Pradeep Maheepala elected as a member of the Gampaha Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATHNE,

Returning Officer,Gampaha Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Gampaha.25th May, 2015.
