ORDERS

01. The name of these orders in No. 01 of 2014 of orders of Wayamba Human Resource Development & Managementof North Western Province.

1ST PART

TUITION CLASSES HELD IN “SUNDAYS” & “FULL MOON POYA DAYS”

02. No one can conduct Tuition Classes from 6.00 am to 1.30 pm in Sundays for the school students from grade 1 to 11(up to G.C.E. Ordinary Level) and no one can allow to do any type of tuition on “Full Moon Poya Days” within North WesternProvince.

2ND PART

MONITORING OF INSTITUTIONS

03. Chairman or any person who has given authority by the Chairman of the Wayamba Human Resources DevelopmentAuthority can examine any Institution or collect information about it.

04. Chairman or any person who has given authority by the Chairman of the Human Resource Development Authoritywith supportive staff has powers to enter any institution within office hours or collect information about it.

05. The responsibility of the owner’s /manager’s party is to provide necessary information and other support.

06. Chairman can transfer the powers related to orders of Human Resource Development & Management of NorthWestern Province to the General Manager.

3RD PART

CANCELLING THE LICENSES

07. When the opinion of Chairman is violated the conditions by of this notification’s 2nd sentence by an Institute, thenotice to give reasons within 3 weeks by registered post for not to order cancellation of the License under the section 18 –2 (C) of the statute of Human Resources Development Authority of North Western Province.

08. If reply not received within 3 weeks or the reply cannot be satisfied, the chairman has powers to order to cancel thelicense under section 07.

4TH PART

APPEALS

09. Any unsatisfied person can appeal to the Minister about cancellation of License or rejection of renewal.

10. The minister has to appoint consultation committee with no more than 3 members to investigate such appeal anddecisions have to be made by Committee recommendations.

11. Any applicant by himself or by a representative should have representation about the matter in his Appeal.

12. Minister’s decision of the Appeal is final and decisive.

5TH PART

ADVISORY COMMITTEE

13. Advisory Committee which is appointed by the order of the minister under the section 18 – 2 (C) of the StatuteNo. 1 of 1994 of North Western Province, appeared in the notices section IV (A) Provincial Councils of extra ordinary Gazettenotification numbered 1780/4 dated Monday 15th October 2012 of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

6TH PART

ADMINISTRATION

14. Special Administrative Unit which is appointed by the order of the minister by the Authority to Register Institutes,Administration & Consultative Services under the section 18 – 2 (C) of the Statute No. 1 of 1994 of North Western Province,appeared in the notices section IV (A) Provincial Councils of extra ordinary Gazette notification numbered 1780/4 datedMonday 15th October 2012 of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.
