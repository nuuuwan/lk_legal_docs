EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 973

MINIMUM MONTHLY EXCISE DUTY PAYABLE BY ARRACK AND BEER MANUFACTURERS

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 28(a) read with Section 16(c) of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52) as amendedfrom time to time, I, Ravi Karunanayake, Minister of Finance, do by this Notification order that all arrack and beer manufacturersshould pay a minimum excise duty of Rs. 200 million per month.

02. Liquor Manufacturing Licenses of all arrack and beer manufacturers who shall not fulfill the above condition withrespect to a certain month shall be cancelled with effect from the 01st of the subsequent month. This procedure shall be in effectfrom 01.04.2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,

Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo,13th February, 2015.
