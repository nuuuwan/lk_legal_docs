CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Removal of Board Members of the Central Provincial Road Development Authority under the Central Provincial Road

Development Authority Charter No. 2 of 2003 of the Provincial Council of the Central Province

I, the Minister of Road Development, Transport, Power and Energy and Housing Construction of the Central ProvincialCouncil, hereby announce that the Board Members of the Central Provincial Road Development Authority appointed by methrough the Gazette No. 1847/4 of 27th January 2014, as per the Section No. 3(1) and (2) in the part I of the Central ProvincialRoad Development Authority Charter No. 2 of 2003 of the Central Provincial Council, are removed from the Director Board ofthe Central Provincial Road Development Authority with effect from the date mentioned before their names, as per the powersbestowed on me by the Statement No. 3(8) in part I of the same Charter.

EDIRIWEERA WEERAWARDENA,Minister of Road Development, Transport,Power and Energy and Housing Construction,
