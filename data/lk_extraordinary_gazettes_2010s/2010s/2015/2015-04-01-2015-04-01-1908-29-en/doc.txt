PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007,I, Ravi Karunanayake, Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order imposein respect of the commodity items specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the ratesspecified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This order shall be valid for a period of06 months commencing from 2nd April 2015, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the SpecialCommodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,

Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,1st April, 2015.
