SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MahindaSamarasinghe, Acting Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose inrespect of the commodity items specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specifiedin the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This order shall be valid for a period of 04 months commencingfrom 29th June, 2015, subject to provisions of Subsection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MAHINDA SAMARASINGHE,Acting Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,29th June 2015.
