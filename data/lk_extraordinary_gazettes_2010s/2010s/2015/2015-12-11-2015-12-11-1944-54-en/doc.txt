SRI LANKA EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT, No. 40 OF 1979

Order under Section 14

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 14(1) of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No. 40 of 1979, I, MalikSamarawickrama, Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade with the concurrence of the Minister ofFinance, do by this order declare that, a Cess shall be charged, levied and paid on import of any article specified in theColumn II and III of the Schedule hereto at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column IV in the Schedulehereto, with effect from 14.12.2015, Accordingly, for every article specified under the H.
