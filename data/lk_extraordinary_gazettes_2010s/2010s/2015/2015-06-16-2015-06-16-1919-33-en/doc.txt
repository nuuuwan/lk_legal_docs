EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake,Finance Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of the commodity itemsspecified in “Column I” of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entriesin Column II of that Schedule. This order shall be valid for a period of 04 months commencing from 17th June 2015, subject toprovisions of Sub-section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1908/29 of 01.04.2015 in respect of vegetable oil (HS Heding Nos. 15.07, 15.11, 15.12, 15.13) and Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1903/4 of 24.02.2015 in respect of Massor dhal (red lentils) - Split (HS Cord Nos. 0713.40.12) and Yellow lentils - Split(0713.40.22) are rescinded with effect from 17th June 2015.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,15th June, 2015.
