FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE

NAVITHANVELI PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Vice Chairman in Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Illankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi the recognized political party to which AmaradashaAnanda the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decideupon the member of that Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared elected as ViceChairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Arumugam Pillaiyanthamby to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Arumugam Pillaiyanthamby electedas a Vice Chairman of the Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Ampara,07th January, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

NAVITHANVELI PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi to which theformer member, Mr. Amaradasha Ananda belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of that NavithanveliPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr.Thiyagarasa Puwasanthanbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Thiyagarasa Puwasanthan electedas a member of the Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Navithanveli Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Ampara,07th January, 2015.
