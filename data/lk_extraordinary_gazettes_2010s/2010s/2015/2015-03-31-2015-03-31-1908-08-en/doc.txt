THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 of 1978

Order under Sub Section (4) of Section 20

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub Section (4) of Section 20 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, SarathAmunugama, Minister of Higher Education and Research, on being satisfied that the administration of the affairs of theEastern University, Sri Lanka have been seriously disrupted and that the authorities of the said University have failed torestore normal conditions, do by this Order appoint Professor Uma Coomaraswamy to be the Competent Authority for thepurpose of exercising, performing or discharging in lieu of Vice-Chancellor the powers, duties and functions respectivelywhich have been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the Vice-Chancellor of the Eastern University, Sri Lanka, under thesaid Act or any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By Law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

SARATH AMUNUGAMA,Minister of Higher Education and Research.

Colombo,30th March, 2015.
