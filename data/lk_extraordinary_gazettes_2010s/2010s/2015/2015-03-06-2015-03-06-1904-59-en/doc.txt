FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE

NUWARA - ELIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased tobe a member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Up - Country Peoples Front, the recognised political party to whichMr. Kathiravel Sivapragasam Sachithananthan the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such partywho shall be declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognised political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Periyasamy Sivasamy Pathmanathan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hettiyakandage Arunapushpa Saumyasiri Fernando, Returning Officer of Nuwara - EliyaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. PeriyasamySivasamy Pathmanathan elected as Vice Chairman of the Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha.

H. A. S. Fernando,

Returning Officer,Nuwara - Eliya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Nuwara - Eliya,03rd March, 2015.
