PROVINCIAL COUNCILS NOTIFICATIONS

NORTH CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification under Section 185 of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 that should be read with Section 2 ofProvincial Council Act (Incidental Provision) of Number 12 of 1989 of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

I have received the report prepared by retired judicial officer, K. Sunil Perera Appointed, in terms of the Section 185 (2) ofPradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 to satisfy that Sinhalage Jagath Samarawickrama Chairman of Dimbulagala PradeshiyaSabha has committed one, more or all the offences as per the Section 185(1) of Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 bySamarakoon Mudiyanselage Ranjith Samarakoon Former Chief Minister, and Minister of Provincial Law and Order, Financeand Planning, Local Government, Provincial Irrigation, Rural Infrastructure Facilities, Sport and Youth Affairs. In terms of thesaid report it has been authenticated that Sinhalage Jagath Samarawickrama Chairman of Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha hascommitted the offences as per the Section 185 (1) i and ii and after the confirmation of such offences, I do here by notify asper the powers vested to me as The Minister of Portfolio of Local Governmnent, Sinhalage Jagath Samarawikrama hasremoved from the post of Chairman and the Membership of Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha with immediate effect.

SUDASINGHE MUDIYANSELAGE PESHALA JAYARATHNA BANDARA,Chief Minister and Minister of Provincial Law and Order,Finance and Planning, Local Government, ProvincialRoad Development, Rural Infrastructure Facilities and SpecialProject, Tourism, Education, Information Technology and Cultural Affairs.

On 18th February 2015,Chief Ministry,Office of the Chief Ministry.
