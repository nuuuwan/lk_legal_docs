PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in The Northern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Northern Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of the ProvincialCouncils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Northern Province ProvincialCouncil by reason of Death ;

And whereas the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party, fails to make a nomination within the specifiedperiod, to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare elected, Mr. Kandiah Sivanesan, who has secured thehighest number of preferences next to the last of the Members declared elected at the Northern Province Provincial CouncilsElection which was held on 21st September 2013, as a member of the above Provincial Council from the AdministrativeDistrict of Mullaitivu in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,29th April, 2015.
