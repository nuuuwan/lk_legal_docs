FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN

OF THE ERAVUR URBAN COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Eravur Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of being elected as a member ofthe Eastern Province Provincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which AliZahir Moulana Seyed the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon the member of that Eravur Urban Council who belongs to such party who shall be declared elected asChairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Mohamed Ismail Mohamed Thasleem, to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Abdul Majeed Mahammadu Kabeer, Returning Officer of Eravur Urban Council, acting interms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Mohamed Ismail Mohamed Thasleem elected asChairman of the Eravur Urban Council.

A.M. M. KABEER,Returning Officer,Eravur Urban Council.District Elections Office,Batticaloa,27th March, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF A MEMBER OF THE

PUTTALAM URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY

LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Puttalam Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Group leader of the Independent Group No. 08 to which the Member, MR. S. R. M. M. Muhusibelonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act,No. 16 of 2007, to nominate a person eligible under the Ordinance to appoint as a member of that Puttalam Urban Councilwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Group leader of the Independent Group has nominated Mr. Mohamed Rizwan Sabar beingan eligble person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pakkiyarajah Ragunathan Returning Officer of the Puttalam Urban Council, acting in termsof Section 65A(2) (b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling of Vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Mohamed Rizwan Sabar elected as a member ofthe Puttalam Urban Council.

PAKKIYARAJAH RAGUNATHAN,

Returning Officer,Puttalam Urban Council.District Elections Office,Puttalam,27th March 2015.
