NATIONAL WATER SUPPLY AND DRAINAGE BOARD ACT, No. 02 OF 1974

Order under Section 64(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 64(1) of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board Act, No. 02 of 1974,I, Rauf Hakeem, the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage, do by this order, embody in the Schedulehereto the provisions of the voluntary transfer scheme approved by me under Section 63(1) of the aforesaid Low, inrespect ofthe Hakmana Water Supply undertakings on by the Pradeshiya Sabha Hakmana.

RAUFF HAKEEM, Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage.

Ministry of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage,No. 35, ‘Lakdiya Medura’New Parliament Road,Pelawatta, Battaramulla,26th June, 2015.

SCHEDULE

Contents of Voluntary Transfer Scheme Section 61 (1)

(a) The following movable and immovable property to be transferred to and vested in the National Water Supplyand Drainage Board to wit,

(i) All lands, buildings pipelines, all mechanical and electrical machinery and equipment fixtures and all sparesaccessories and stores items required for the implementation and maintenance of all intakes refineries,pressure and balancing tank and pumping station of the Hakmana Water Supply Scheme listed in schedule I.

(ii) Servitude over pipelines, spares accessories and stores items along with main pipelines and distributionpipeline required for the operation and maintenance of water distribution network at the Hakmana WaterSupply Scheme within the area of authority of the Pradeshiya Sabha Hakmana.

(b) The rights, obligations and liabilities of the Pradeshiya Sabha Hakmana together with any contract the councilhas entered into in connection with design,supply, installation, constructions, operation and maintenance withrespect of the aforesaid property will also be transferred to the Board;

(d) The assets of these facilities valued at Rs. 3,831,730/- (Rupees Three million Eight Hundred and Thirty OneThousand and Seven Hundred Thirty) will be provided on grant to the Board by the Government.

(e) All agreements entered into by the Pradeshiya Sabha Hakmana in connection with water supply will also becontinued and honored by the Board. The above transfer will come into effect from the date of publication ofthis notice.
