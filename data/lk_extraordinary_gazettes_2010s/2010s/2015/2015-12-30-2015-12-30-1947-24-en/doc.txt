THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order establishing the University of Kelaniya, Sri Lanka, which order is deemed, by virtue ofthe provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be an Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, (as last amendedby Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1908/24 of March 31, 2015) by the addition immediately after theentry “Faculty of Commerce and Management”appearing in Column I and its corresponding entries in Column II thereof,of the following :-

“Column IColumn II

Faculty of Computing and Technology1.Department of Software Engineering2.Department of Computer Systems Engineering3.Department of Applied Computing”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Educationand Highways.
