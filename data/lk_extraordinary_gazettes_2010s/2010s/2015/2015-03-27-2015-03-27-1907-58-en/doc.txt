SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Order under Sections 32, 33 and 34

IN terms of the powers vested in me under Sections 32, 33 and 34 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973, I, Navin EranjanDissanayake, Minister of Tourism and Sports, do hereby inform that the appointments of the office bearers of the Sri LankaCricket are cancelled from 01st of April 2015 and the names of persons given below are appointed from 01st April 2015 to carryout the functions of Sri Lanka Cricket until an election is held or until further notice.

1. Sidath Wettamuny-President2. Prakash Schafter-Secretary3. Lucille Wijewardena-Treasurer4. Kushil Gunasekare-Vice President5. Kapila Wijegunawardene-Vice President

Members

1. Nuski Mohomed2. Prassanna Jayawardene (PC)3. Jayananda Warnaweera4. Duminda Hulangamuwa.

NAVIN ERANJAN DISSANAYAKE,Minister of Tourism and Sports.
