Government Notifications

SRI LANKA ELECTRICITY ACT, No. 20 OF 2009

Order under Section 52

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 52 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009 and upon the recommen-dations made by the Minister in charge of the subject of Power and Energy, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka being of the opinion that :-

(a)the land specified in the Schedule hereto is urgently required for the construction of a Power TransmissionProject ; and(b)the said Power Transmission Project being a public purpose would serve for the general welfare and benefit ofthe public.

do by this order declare that the said land is required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,

President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,20th February 2015.
