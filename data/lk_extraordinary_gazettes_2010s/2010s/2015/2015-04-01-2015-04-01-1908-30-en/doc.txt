FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF A MEMBER

OF THE PACHCHILAIPALLI PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to bea member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which theMember Subramaniam Suren belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) as amended by the Local AuthoritiesElections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2007, to nominate a person eligible under the ordinance to appoint as a member of thatPachchilaipalli of Pradeshiya Sabha within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Mariyampillai Jesuthasanbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, P. Kuganathan, Returning Officer of Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha , acting in terms ofSection 65A(2) (b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Mariyampillai Jesuthasan elected as memberof the Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. KUGANATHAN,Returning Officer,Pachchilaipalli Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,30th March, 2015.
