THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978 OF THE NATIONAL STATE

ASSEMBLY

Cancellation of the Vesting Order under Section 15

BY virtue of the powers vested with the Minister of Defence and Urban Development under Section 15(1) of the UrbanDevelopment Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 the land described in the Schedule hereto was vested with the Urban DevelopmentAuthority by the Order under Section 15 published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1884/57 dated 16.10.2014.

I, Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage as the subject Minister of UrbanDevelopment hereby cancel that aforesaid Vesting Order since the subject property is not required for the Urban DevelopmentAuthority.

RAUFF HAKEEM M. P.,Minister of Urban Development,

Water Supply and Drainage.Ministry of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage,No. 35, ‘Lakdiya Medura’,New Parliament Road,Pelawatta, Battaramulla,09th April, 2015.
