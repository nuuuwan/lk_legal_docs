Greetings !

WHEREAS, in the exercise of powers vested in me by Article 33 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka a National Research Council was established by the Presidential Directive dated July 24, 2007 ;

Whereas, amendments have been made to the said original directive from time to time ;

And whereas, Dr. Rohan Prasantha Perera Esquire, the Member of the said Council has resigned from the saidmembership ;

And Whereas, the need to fill the vacancy of the said post has been identified ;

Now therefore, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,reposing great trust and confidence in the prudence, ability and fidelity of Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe Esquire, DeputyGovernor of the Central Bank do hereby appoint the said Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe Esquire as a member of the NationalResearch Council established by the said Presidential Directive :

Given at Colombo, under the seal of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, on this 08th day of July, TwoThousand and Fifteen.

By order of His Excellency,

P. B. ABEYKOON,Secretary to the President.
