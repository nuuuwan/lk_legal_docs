PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Western Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Western Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the WesternProvince Provincial Council by reason of Death;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was required by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Mohammadu SaleemSakaulla to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Mohammadu Saleem Sakaulla , elected as amember of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Gampaha in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.

Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya.11th November, 2015.
