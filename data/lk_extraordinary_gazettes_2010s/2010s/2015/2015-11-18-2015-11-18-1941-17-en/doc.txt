FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DEPUTY MAYOR OF THENEGOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Deputy Mayor in Negombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being elected as amember of the Western Province Provincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichM. S. M. Sakawulla of the former Deputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Negombo Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shall bedeclared elected as Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Dayan Lansa Moses Warnakulasooriya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Negombo MunicipalCouncil acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Dayan Lansa MosesWarnakulasooriya elected as Deputy Mayor of the Negombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATHNA,Returning Officer,Negombo Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Gampaha,18th November, 2015.
