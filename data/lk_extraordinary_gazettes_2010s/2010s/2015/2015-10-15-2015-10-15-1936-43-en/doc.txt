NORTH WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

North Western Provincial Council Notification made under Co-operative Employees CommissionStatute Number 05 of 1995 of the North Western Province

I, Gunadasa Dehigama the Minister of the Provincial Ministry of Road, Transport, Co-operative Development, Trade, Housingand Constructions, Industry and Rural Development of North Western Province by virtue of the powers vested to me bySection 05 (01) of the Co-operative Employees Commission of North Western Province, Constituted under the Provisionsmade by Section 05 of North Western Provincial Council's Co-operative Employees Commission Statute No. 05 of 1995, dohereby appoint following Persons as members of the North Western Province Co-operative Employees Commission witheffect from 01.10.2015 to 01.10.2018.

Further it is hereby notified that under Section 07 of the Statute Mr. R. P. Champika Lasitha Kumara Attorney at Lawhas been appointed as the Chairman of this Commission with effect from 01.10.2015 to 01.10.2018.

GUNADASA DEHIGAMA, Minister of the Provincial Ministry of Road, Transport,Co-operative Development, Trade, Housing and Constructions, Industry andRural Development of North Western Province.

9th October 2015.
