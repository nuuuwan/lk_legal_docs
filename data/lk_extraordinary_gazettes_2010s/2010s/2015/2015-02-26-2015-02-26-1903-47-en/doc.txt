PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in The North Central Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the North Central Province Provincial Council has informed me under Section 65(1) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the North CentralProvince Provincial Council by reason of Appointing the Governor of North Central Province Provincial Council ;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was requested by me under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party, has nominated Mr. Abdul Rahim AbdulHusain, to fill such vacancy ;

I, Waduge Warunasiri Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of theProvincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988, do hereby declare Mr. Abdul Rahim Abdul Husain, elected as a member ofthe above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Anuradhapura in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,20th February, 2015.
