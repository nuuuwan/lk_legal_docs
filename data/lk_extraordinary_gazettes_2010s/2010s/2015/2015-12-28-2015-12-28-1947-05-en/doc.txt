THE FIRING RANGE AND MILITARY TRAINING ACT

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Defence under Section 17 of the Firing Range and Military Training Act, readwith Article 44(2) of the Constitution.

MAITHREEPALA SIRISENA,Minister of Defence.

Colombo,18th December 2015.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Firing Range Regulations 1983

2. 300 meters Small Arms Firing Range at Morawewa Air Force Camp (Hereinfater referenced to as “The 300meters Small Arms Firing Range, at Morawewa Air Force Camp) shall not be used for any firing other than Small Armsupto 7.62 mm caliber.

03. The Commander of the Air Force or an Officer authorized by him shall cause notices to be displayedconspicuously at suitable points along the boundary of the 300 meters Small Arms Firing Range at Morawewa Air ForceCamp. Such notices shall state that a firing range is situated within these points and that when firing is about to commenceor is in progress, red flags will be flown by day and all around red lights will be displayed by night at the following points.

Butts and stop ButtsFiring Points

At the turning point junction to Morawewa Air Force Camp along Anuradhapura main road at a point ahead of thesub-guard room located in the road leading from Wewapara, to Morawewa Air Force Camp.

04. The commander of the Air Force or an Officer authorized by him shall, during the period of half an hourbefore the commencement of firing at the 300 meters Small Arms Firing Range of Morawewa Air Force Camp, and at thetermination of firing, cause red flags to be flown by day and all around red lights to be displayed by night at the followingpoints.

Butts and Stop ButtsFiring Points

At the turning point turning point junction to Morawewa Air Force Camp along Anuradhapura main road and at apoint ahed of the sub-guard room located in the road leading from Wewapara to Morawewa Air Force Camp.

05. During the period when read flags are flown or all around red lights are displayed at the 300 meters SmallArms Firing Range at Morawewa Air Force Camp, in accordance with regulations 3, no person other than the personsparticipating in the firing at the 300 meters Small Arms Firing Range at Morawewa Air Force Camp shall enter or remainin the area within such range.

06. The Commander of the Air Force or an Officer authorized by him may issue such orders as may be necessaryfor the safety of persons engaged in the firing at the 300 meters Small Arms Firing Range at Morawewa Air Force Camp.
