FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

PANNALA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, S. A. Piyasena belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominatewithin a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of that Pannal PradeshiyaSabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Mohomed SamunMohomed Safwan being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thalangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Thalangama, Returning Officer of PannalaPradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling ofvacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. MohomedSamun Mohomed Safwan elected as a member of the Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. THALANGAMA,

Returning Officer,Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kurunegala,10th March, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

ERAVUR URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Eravur Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of appointed to Member of EasternProvincial Council ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Ali Zahir Moulana Seyed belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of that EravurUrban Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr.Meera Sahibu MohamedNasir being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Abdul Majeed Mahammadu Kabeer, Returning Officer of Eravur Urban Council, acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Meera Sahibu Mohamed Nasir elected as amember of the Eravur Urban Council.

A. M. M. KABEER,Returning Officer,Eravur Urban Council.District Elections Office,Batticaloa,10th March, 2015.
