FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

PASBAGE KORALE PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Pasbage Korale Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being Ceased to bea member of the Party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Up Country Peoples Front to which theformer Member, Mr. Dewamunige Asanka Prabath Silva belonged was required by me under Section 65A (2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Pasbage Korale Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Karuppiah Pathmanathanbeing an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Mudiyanselage Kumudu Saman Sri Rathnayake, Returning Officer of Pasbage KoralePradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 65A (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Filling ofVacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. KaruppiahPathmanathan elected as a member of the Pasbage Korale Pradeshiya Sabha.

SAMAN SRI RATHNAYAKE,

Returning Officer, Pasbage Korale Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Kandy,03rd of January, 2015.
