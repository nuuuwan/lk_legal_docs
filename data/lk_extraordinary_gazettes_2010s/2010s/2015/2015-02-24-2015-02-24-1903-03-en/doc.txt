PROVINCIAL COUNCILS NOTIFICATIONS

NORTH CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification under Section 185 of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 that should be read with Section 2 ofProvincial Council Act (Incidental Provision) of Number 12 of 1989 of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

I have received the report prepared by retired judicial officer, K. Sunil Perera Appointed, in terms of the Section 185 (2) ofPradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 to satisfy that Athmadale Aluthwatthe Gedara Manju Sri Aluthwattha Chairman ofLankapura Pradeshiya Sabha has committed one, more or all the offences as per the Section 185(1) of Pradeshiya Sabha ActNo. 15 of 1987 by Samarakoon Mudiyanselage Ranjith Samarakoon Former Chief Minister, and Minister of Provincial Law andOrder, Finance and Planning, Local Government, Provincial Irrigation, Rural Infrastructure Facilities, Sport and Youth Affairs.In terms of the said report it has been authenticated that Athmadale Aluthwatthe Gedara Manju Sri Aluthwattha Chairman ofLankapura Pradeshiya Sabha has committed the offences as per the Section 185 (1) i and after the confirmation of suchoffences, I do here by notify as per the powers vested to me as The Minister of Portfolio of Local Government, AthmadaleAluthwatthe Gedara Manju Sri Aluthwattha has removed from the post of Chairman of Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha withimmediate effect.

SUDASINGHE MUDIYANSELAGE PESHALA JAYARATHNA BANDARA,Chief Minister and Minister of Provincial Law and Order,Finance and Planning, Local Government, ProvincialRoad Development, Rural Infrastructure Facilities and SpecialProject, Tourism, Education, Information Technology and Cultural Affairs.

Chief North Central Province,Office of the Chief Ministry,18th February 2015.
