Rules

1. These rules may be cited as the Insurance Agents (other than Individuals) Register Rules, 2015.

2. The Register to be maintained by every insurer and broker, of all persons who are registered with them asinsurance agents (other than Individuals), shall be in such form as specified in the Schedule hereto.
