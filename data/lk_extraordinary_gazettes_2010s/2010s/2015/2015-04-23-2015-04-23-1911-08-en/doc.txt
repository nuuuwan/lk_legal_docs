FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

DIMBULAGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of removal of member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Sinhalage Jagath Samarawickrama belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member ofthat Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Wasala MudiyanselageWala Gedara Janaka Bandara being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Indika Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of Dimbulagaala Pradeshiya Sabha acting in termsof Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Wasala Mudiyanselage Wala Gedara JanakaBandara elected as a member of the Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA HATHURUSINGHA,

Returning Officer,Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Polonnaruwa,22nd April, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE

LANKAPURA PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of removal of chairman;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance the recognized political party to whichAthmadale Aluthwaththe Gedara Manju Sri Aluthwaththa the former chairman belonged was required by me under Section65A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty who shall be declared elected as Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Suraweera Arachchilage Bandula Samansiri to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Indika Hathurusinghe, Returning Officer of Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms ofSection 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Suraweera Arachchilage Bandula Samansiri elected asa Chairman of the Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA HATHURUSINGHA,

Returning Officer,Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Polonnaruwa,22nd April, 2015.
