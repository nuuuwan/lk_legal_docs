PART III — LANDS

Land Reform Commission Notices

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 29 OF THE LAND REFORM LAW, No. 01 OF 1972 AS AMENDED BY LANDREFORM (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 39 OF 1981 AND LAND REFORM (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT,No. 14 OF 1986

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 29 of the Law, I hereby direct every person who is interested in anyagricultural land or lands described in the Schedule hereto immediately before the date on which such land or lands havebeen vested in the Land Reform Commission to make, within one month from 25.07.2015 a written claim in triplicate to thewhole any part of the compensation payable under the law in respect of such land or lands and to specify in the claim :-

(a) His name and address,

(b) The nature of his interest in such land/lands,

(c) Particulars of his claim ; and

(d) How much of such compensation is claimed by him.

All claims should be sent by registered post, addressed to the Chairman, Land Reform Commission.

T. A. SUMANATISSA THAMBUGALA,Chairman,Land Reform Commission.

Office of the Land Reform Commission,C 82, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha,Colombo 07,17th June, 2015.
