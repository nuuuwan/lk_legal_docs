SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification under the Section 184 of the Urban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255) and the Provincial Council(Consequential Provisions) Act, No.12 of 1989

I, Maheepala Herath, Chief Minister and Minister of Law and Order, Finance and Planning, Local Government, Health andindigenous Medicine, Women Affairs, Co-operatives, Trade and Food, Transport and Estate Welfare, Environment, News,Housing and Construction of the Sabaragamuwa Province, In terms of the powers vested in me under section 184 (1A) of theUrban Council Ordinance (Chapter 255), do hereby notify that the duration of the Commission of Inquiry comprising retiredHigh Court Judge, Saman Wickrama Wickramaarachchi Esquire, appointed by the Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 1908/34 datedthe Thursday, Second April, 2015 in the Part IV (A) – Provincial Council Notifications of the Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka, to inquire whether the matters mentioned under section 184 (1) of the Urban Council Ordinance, were committedby the Chairman, Ven. Vewelduwe Gnanaprabha Thero, while holding his office as the Chairman of the Embilipitiya UrbanCouncil and to submit a report , is extended further 03 months.

I also notify that the provisions mentioned in the section 2 and 3 of the said gazette will be operated further.

MAHEEPALA HERATHChief Minister and Minister of Law and Order,Finance and Planning, Local Government,Health and indigenous Medicine, Women Affairs,Co-operatives, Trade and Food, Transport andEstate Welfare, Environment, News, Housing andConstruction of the Sabaragamuwa Province.

Chief Ministry of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council,New Town, Ratnapura,On this 30th of June, 2015.
