1A

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEKANDY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Kandy Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party, to which the formermember, Sinniah Sivagnanam belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member of that KandyMunicipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. ThumbaneThribhuwana Keerthisinghe Dunuweera being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of KandyMunicipal Council, acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by fillingof Vacancies in Local authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. ThumbaneThribhuwana Keerthisinghe Dunuweera elected as a member of the Kandy Municipal Council.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Kandy Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Kandy,15th December, 2015.
