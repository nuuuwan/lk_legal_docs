FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

WATTALA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Wattala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending meetings of thecouncil for a period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance to whichthe former member, Arachchige Nilantha Leshan Sanjeewa Senasingha Senarathna belonged was required by me underSection 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under theordinance for election as a member of that Wattala Pradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Bope ArachchilageDon Susil Nishantha being an eligible person under the aforesaid ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Wattala Pradeshiya Sabhaacting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Bope Arachchilage Don SusilNishantha elected as a member of the Wattala Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. A. S. SENARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Wattala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Gampaha,18th February, 2015.
