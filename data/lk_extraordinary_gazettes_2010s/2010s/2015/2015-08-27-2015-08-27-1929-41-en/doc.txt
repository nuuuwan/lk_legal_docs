(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

1A

THE SOCIETIES ORDINANCE

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 and 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), I, RishadBathiudeen Minister of Industry and Commerce, do by this notification :-

1.Authorize the purpose for which the Society known as “ Sri Saddhananda Daham Pasal Adi Shishya Sangamaya’’situated at Sri Saddhananda Daham Pasal Adi Shishya Sangamaya Paramadhamma Visuddharamaya, Katuwawala,Boralesgamuwa as a purpose to which the powers and facilities of that Ordinance ought to be extended ; and

2.Limit the application of that Ordinance to aforesaid society by exempting it from the provision ofSection 5 (4) of that Ordinance.

RISHAD BATHIUDEEN,Minister of Industry and Commerce.

Ministry of Industry and Commerce,No. 73/ 1, Galle Road,Colombo 03,14th August, 2015.
