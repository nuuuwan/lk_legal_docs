CONTROL OF PESTICIDES ACT, No. 33 OF 1980

Order under Section 11

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 11 of the Control of Pesticides Act, No. 33 of 1980, I, Gamage AnuraWickramasiri Wijesekara, Registrar of Pesticides, do by this Order, in the interest of the public and on the advice of thePesticides Technical and Advisory Committee, cancel every licence issued in respect of Pesticides containing the activeingredient bearing the Chemical Abstract Service Registry number specified in the Schedule hereto.

DR. G. A. W. WIJESEKARA,Registrar of Pesticides.

Peradeniya,01st October, 2015.
