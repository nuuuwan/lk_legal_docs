Government Notifications

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

ORDINANCE made by the University Grants Commission under Section 23(2) read with Section 18 of the Universities Act,No. 16 of 1978.

Secretary,University Grants Commission.

Colombo ,09th June, 2015.

Ordinance

1.This Ordinance may be cited as Open University of Sri Lanka (Amendment) Ordinance, No. 02 of 2015 and shallcome into force upon publication in the Gazette.

2.The Open University of Sri Lanka Ordinance, No. 1 of 1990 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 591/18 ofJanuary 05, 1990 as last amended by Ordinance published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1839/41 of December 05, 2013 ishereby further amended in paragraph 16, of the Ordinance as follows :-

(1) by the repeal of sub-paragraph (1) of that paragraph and the substitution there for, of the following :–

‘‘(1) The Open University shall have the following Faculties :–

(a) Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences ;(b) Faculty of Natural Sciences ;(c) Faculty of Engineering Technology ;(d) Faculty of Education ; and

(e) Faculty of Health Sciences. ’’;

L.D.B 21/78 (xiii).
