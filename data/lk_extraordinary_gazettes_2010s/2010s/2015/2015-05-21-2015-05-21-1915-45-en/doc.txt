SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Order under Sections 32, 33 and 34

IN terms of the powers vested in me under Sections 32, 33 and 34 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973, I, Navin EranjanDissanayake, Minister of Tourism and Sports, do hereby inform that the appointments of the office bearers of the Sri LankaJudo Association are cancelled from 15th of May 2015 and the names of persons given below are appointed from 15th ofMay 2015 to 15 th of July 2015 in order to carry out the functions of Sri Lanka Judo Association until an an election is heldor until further notice.

1. E. S. Kithsiri De Silva-President2. Dr. Sampath P. Weerasinghe-Secretary3. M. A. S. Nandana Wijesinghe-Treasurer4. Lakshman Joshep Gunasekara-Vice President

1. K. B. Ranjith Dayananda2. P. C. Pushpakumara3. P. W. M. R. Lalith Bandara Angunawala
