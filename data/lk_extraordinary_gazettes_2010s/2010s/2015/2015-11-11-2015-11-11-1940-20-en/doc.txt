FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MAYOR OF THEMATARA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of Mayor in Matara Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance the recognized political party to whichHadunge Sosindra Nandana the former Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Matara Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shall be declaredelected as Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Deegoda Gamage Yasarathna to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of Matara MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Deegoda GamageYasarathna elected as a Mayor of the Matara Municipal Council.

W. H. R. WIJAYA KUMARA,Returning Officer,Matara Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Matara,11th November, 2015.
