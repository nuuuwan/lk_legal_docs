URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, I, RauffHakeem, Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage being of opinion that the area specified in ColumnI of Schedule II hereto and having the metes and bounds specified in corresponding entity in Column II of Schedule IIhereto are suitable for development and do hereby this order declare such areas for the purpose of the said section.

I do hereby declare that, the already declared areas set out in Schedule I together with the other areas declared asaforesaid, shall be included within the limits of the expanded Kurunegala Urban Development Area set out in underSchedule II hereto.

RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of Urban Development,Water Supply and Drainage,

Ministry of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage,“Lakdiya Medura” ,New Parliament Road,Pelawatta,Battaramulla,26th June 2015.
