TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 269)

Order under Section 6(2)(b) and Section 21(2)(b)

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 6(2)(b) and Section 21(2)(b) of the Town and Country PlanningOrdinance (Chapter 269), I, Ranil Wickramasinghe, Minister of Policy Planning, Economic Affairs, Child, Youth andCultural Affairs do by this order :-

(a)declare that the area of land specified in the Schedule hereto shall be an Urban Development Area for thepurpose of the aforesaid Ordinance and shall be called the “Battaramulla Batapotha Sri Sudarshanarama PuranaViharaya Sacred Area” and

(b)direct that an outline scheme shall be prepared for the whole of the area declared to be “Battaramulla BatapothaSri Sudarshanarama Purana Viharaya Sacred Area”.

RANIL WICKRAMASINGHE,Minister of Policy Planning, Economic Affairs,Child, Youth and Cultural Affairs.

Ministry of Policy Planning, Economic Affairs,Child, Youth and Cultural Affairs,Colombo 01,17th July, 2015.
