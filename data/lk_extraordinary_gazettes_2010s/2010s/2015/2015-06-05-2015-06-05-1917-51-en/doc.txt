FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

NALLUR PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, to which theFormer member Thambimuthu Thuraimani belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that NallurPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has failed to make a nomination withinthe period specified ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in termsof Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mrs. Amirthanathan Niranchanathevi who hadsecured the highest number of perferences next to the last of the member declared elected at the election held on 23rd July2011 to Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha elected as a member of the Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha in respect of which such vacancyoccurred.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,

Returning Officer,Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Jaffna.03rd June 2015.
