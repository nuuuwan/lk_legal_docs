PILGRIMAGE ORDINANCE

Annual Festival - Mother’s National Church, Matara

It is hereby notified under Section 02 of regulations framed under Pilgrimage Ordinance (Chapter 175) published in theGazette No. 687 of 01.11.1991 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, that the Annual Festival of Mother’s Nationalchurch of Matara in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Matara in the Matara District, will be conducted 03rd of Septemberto 13th of September 2015.

RANJITH YAPA,Divisional Secretary,Matara.At Divisional Secretariat,Matara.17th June, 2015
