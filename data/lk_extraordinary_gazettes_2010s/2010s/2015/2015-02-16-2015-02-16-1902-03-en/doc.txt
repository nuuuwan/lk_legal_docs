LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

I, Mahinda Deshapriya, Commissioner of Elections by virtue of powers vested in me by Section 27 (1) of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Act, No. 24 of 1987, read with Section 4 (1) of the said Act, do hereby appointthe officer specified in Column III of Schedule hereto as Returning Officer of each Local Authority described in the corre-sponding entry in Column II of the said Schedule, situated in the Administrative District described in the corresponding entryin Column I of the said Schedule, for the purpose of election of members of the prescribed Local Authorities in the MullaitivuAdministrative District.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,Commissioner of Elections.
