FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

THIRUKKOVIL PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 65A (2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS, the office of a member in Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, to which theFormer member Veluppillei Mahenthiran belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, tonominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for Election as a member of that ThirukkovilPradeshiya Sabha who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. SomasundaramPakkiyarasa being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in LocalAuthorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Somasundaram Pakkiyarasaelected as a member of the Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,

Returning Officer,Thirukkovil Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Ampara.10th June 2015.
