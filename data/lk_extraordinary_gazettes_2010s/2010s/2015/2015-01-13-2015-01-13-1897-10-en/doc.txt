APPOINTMENT MADE BY THE GOVERNOR OF UVA PROVINCE

BY virtue of powers vested to me under section 154F. (4) of the 13th Amendment to the Constitution of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, C. Nanda Mathew, Governor of the Uva Province, do hereby announce that with effect fromthe date 14th January, 2015. Hon. Harin Fernando was appointed as the Chief Minister of the Provincial Council of UvaProvince.

C. NANDA MATHEW,Governor of Uva Province.
