FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DEPUTY MAYOR OF THEKANDY MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Deputy Mayor in Kandy Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Deputy Mayor hasbeen appointed as Mayor ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichSena Dissanayaka of the former Deputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that Kandy Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shall be declaredelected as Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Asanka Punyajith Bandara Dodamwala to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of KandyMunicipal Council acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by fillingof vacancies in Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. AsankaPunyajith Bandara Dodamwala elected as Deputy Mayor of the Kandy Municipal Council.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Kandy Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Kandy,29th December, 2015.
