FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DEPUTY MAYOR OF THE

RATNAPURA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(1) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Deputy Mayor in Ratnapura Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Appointed asMayor ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People's Freedom Alliance the recognized political party to whichGodahena Kattadige Nilantha Roshan the former Deputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 65A(1) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ratnapura Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shallbe declared elected as Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMr. Jayasinghe Mudiyanselage Chandradasa Jayasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Matara Achchige Padmasiri Chandrawansha Perera, Returning Officer of Ratnapura MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 65A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies inLocal Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Jayasinghe MudiyanselageChandradasa Jayasinghe elected as a Deputy Mayor of the Ratnapura Municipal Council.

M. A. P. C. PERERA,

Returning Officer,Ratnapura Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Ratnapura,29th April, 2015.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE

MORATUWA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Moratuwa Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance, to whichthe former member, Thennakoon Pathiranage Dilip Kumara belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as amember of that Moratuwa Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr.Wedige Nimal YasasiriPeiris being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Moratuwa Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Wedige Nimal Yasasiri Peiris elected as amember of the Moratuwa Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Moratuwa Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo,29th April, 2015.
