These regulations shall be effective from 01st September 2017.

Regulations

01.These regulations are introduced as “Passenger Transport Regulation for taxi vans, No. 01, 2017” of Eastern Province Road PassengerTransport Authority and will be effective from ................... day of 01st Month September 2017.

02.On hired basis with fees charged, separately or in groups without the permission of Eastern Province Road Passenger TransportAuthority or registration from 01.03.2017 should not be engaged in Passenger Transport.

03.A permit shall not be issued to individual other than a registered owner of the taxi van.

04.When issuing a permit the priority should be given to resident of the Local Administrative Division of the relevant authorty.

05.The Authority reserves the right to issue a permit, to renew or to transfer a taxi van from one vehicle part to another or to reject suchreques.05.1. When using the discretion the following matters shall be considered.

(a)The taxi van executed from the relevant vehicle park how long the requirements of the passenger transport is fulfilled.

(b)If requirements of passenger transport is fulfilled from the vehicle park executed presently. is it appropriate to issuea new permit for the said place.

(c)The authorized area for a taxi van park should be decided and un operated taxi van from that parks sholud be includedto the quantity of hired van relevant to that place.

11.The permit included the following terms and conditions.

11.1. The permit is not transferable.

11.2. if otherwise stated in the permit, the permit will be in force for twelve months period from the date of issued.

11.4. The permit label issued by the authority should be displayed on the left side of the windscreen of the van.

11.5. Permit holder and his employee should maintain the van used for rendering the relevant service in good mechnicalconditions and with clean condition.

11.6. In addition to these regulations the responsibility of the permit holder and the responsibilities of the driver of the hiredvan are considered the terms and conditions of the permit.

(d)Things should be carried under Motor Traffic Ordinance.

13.The responsibilities of permit holder.

13.1. The permit holder should run his van subject to the conditions of the taxi van society / company or organization acceptedby the authority

13.2. When the taxi van subjected to an accident it should be informed to the authority within seven days through hired vansociety company.

13.3. Regarding a serious or fatal accidents the hired van should not engaged in the service until new appropriate certificate isobtained.

13.4 A driver who perpetrated a serious or fatal vehicle accidents should not engaged service until he is certified to be in proper physical condition (by serious accident meant that as the research of the accident a injured person under treatment in hospital exceeding seven days or a damage to the property exceeding Rupees Ten Thousand. The accidents where four Wheels went out of the road are considered serious accident. By fatal accident it is meant that the subjected person died by the result of it.,

14. Responsibility of the driver:-

14.1Taxi van should be driven as to certify the safety and convenience of all passengers travelling in the van.

14.2The van should not be driven with exceeding the approved quantity of passengers.

14.3Should be in good conduct and good character and should be courteous to the passengers, government and other officers.

14.4Should not be under the influence of liquor and drugs when engaged in service and within the service time should not take liquoror drugs.

14.5Should not drive uncaringly, in disregard and dangerously the taxi van.

14.6The driver should be registered in authority.

14.7The taxi van should not be parked obstructing the vehicle park expect within the due place in the taxi van park.

14.8Always engaged in the service taxi van should be dressed an uniform of official identity card defined by the authority.

14.9In an occasion when the taxi van could not travel to the destination of the passenger or into any reason of accident/ mechanicaldefect or unavoidable circumstances the fees could be charged only to the place that accidence/ mechanical defect occured. Insuch instance every effort should be taken to give a optional way of transport to the passengers.

14.10Should not charge more or less amount than the approved fees of the authority from any passenger.

14.11Anybody should not be allowed to smoke in the van.

15. The responsibility of the passengers :-

15.1Within the taxi van, should not smoke or take liquor.

15.2Should conduct politely within the hired van.
