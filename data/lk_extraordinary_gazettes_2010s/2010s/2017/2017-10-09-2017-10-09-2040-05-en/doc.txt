WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Wages Board for the Textile Manufacturing Trade

IT is hereby notified under Regulation 30 of the Wages Boards Regulations published in Gazette No. 14961 dated04.06.1971 that the Hon. Minister of Labour, Trade Union Relations and Sabaragamuwa Development has been pleased toappoint the following persons to be members of the Wages Board for the Textile Manufacturing Trade, by virtue of thepowers vested in the Hon. Minister of Labour under Section 09 of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136) No. 27 of1941, for a period of three years commencing on 01st July 2017.
