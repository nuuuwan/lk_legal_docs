CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Order under Section 20(5)

Order No. 41

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 20(5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, the ConsumerAffairs Authority Orders that no producer, importer, distributor or trader shall sell, expose or offer for sale or display for salethe following varieties of rice above the Maximum Retail Price given hereunder ;

Samba Rice (Excluding Keeri Samba and Suduru Samba)Rs. 80 per kg.Nadu RiceRs. 72 per kg.Raw RiceRs. 70 per kg.

This Order shall come into effect from 08th February, 2017.

By Order of the Consumer Affairs Authority,

HASITHA TILLEKERATNE,Chairman.Colombo,08th February, 2017.
