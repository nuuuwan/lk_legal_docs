THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: UDU/2/2/10/42.

Land Ministry No.: L/03/J/95/FR/189.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as Amended by the landAcquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land/servitudefor public purpose (the conservation of Dumbara mountainrange.)

Description of the land / servitude to be acquired:

An allotment of land called ''Karapilahena Gatehena"bearing an extent of about 1.1410 Hectare mentioned in F.V.P426 Sup. 1 Sheet 1 (lot number 269), Supplement of theSurveyor General, situated in the Grama Niladhari Division ofPallekanda, in the Divisional Secretary's Division ofUdadumbara of Kandy District, Central Province.

Name and Address of Claimants : Mr. H. M. G. G.Ranaweera Banda, No. 221, Welankatiya, Kalugala.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude onwhich is to be acquired are hereby required to appearpersonally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before meat the Divisional Secretariat, Udadumbara, on the 16th August2017 at 10.00 a.m. and hereby requested to notify to me inwriting (in duplicate) on or before the 9th August 2017 natureof their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amountof compensation and the details of the computation of suchamount.

SUJANI R. WIJETUNGA,

Divisional Secretary,

Udadumbara.
