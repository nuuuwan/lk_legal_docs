L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the aforesaid Act, establishing the South EasternUniversity of Sri Lanka, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 916/7 of March 27, 1996 (as last amended by Orderpublished in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1973/28 of June 29, 2016) by the addition immediately after item (e) appearingin Column II under the heading “Department of Study” which corresponds to the entry “Factulty of Arts and Culture”appearing in Column I under the heading “Faculty” of the Schedule II to the such Order, of the following new item :-

“(f)Department of English Language Teaching”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,12th June, 2017.
