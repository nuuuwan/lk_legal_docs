Provincial Councils Notifications

CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Patha Dumbara Pradeshiya Sabha

IMPOSITION OF ENTERTAINMENT TAX

BY virtue of the power vested in, under Sub section (1) of Section 2 of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance (Chapter 267), itis hereby published the proposal resolved by the Secretary to the Patha Dumbara Pradeshiya Sabha is hereby approved bythe Minister in charge of the subject Local Government of the Central Provincial Council in the Central Province, by virtueof power vested by the Sub section (2) of the said Section.

P. B. WIJAYARATNA,Secretary,Chief Ministry, Education and Local Government Ministry,Central Province.

Pallekele,02nd December 2016.

PROPOSAL

By virtue of power vested in under Section 9(3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987, it has been decidedto charge 10% of Entertainment Tax (except Entertainment Tax) on entry ticket charges for the imposition of EntertainmentTax within the authority areas of Patha Dumbara Pradeshiya Sabha under Section 2(1) of the Entertainment Tax OrdinanceNo. 12 of 1946 and amended Entertainment Tax Act, No. 37 of 1984.
