UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect ofthe Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt) Ltd, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act,No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 62877, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made undersaid Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1975/47 of July 14, 2016 by the addition immediately after theentry " Bachelor of Science Honours in Information Technology" appearing in Column I and it's corresponding entry inColumn II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to theconditions specified in Schedule I hereto:

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt.) Ltd. shall, when admitting students for the degree programmespecified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Information Technology Honours in Networking and Mobilecomputing, adhere to the minimum entry qualifications of 3 "S" passes in any subject stream in one and the same sitting atGCE Advanced Level Examination or equivalent, as per UGC circular No. 995.
