LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 27F

IT is hereby notified under Section 27F of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance read with 19th amendment to theconstitution that the number of women candidates to be nominated in respect of each Local Authority specified in ColumnII of the Schedule hereto situated within the administrative district specified in Column I thereof for the purpose of theelection held under the said Ordinance, is indicated in the corresponding note of the Column II of the said Schedule and thatthe number of 25% women members to be returned is indicated in column IV of the said Schedule.
