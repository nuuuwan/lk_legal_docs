L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, Ministerof Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, amendthe Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1603/35 of May 28, 2009 and Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1711/24 of June 24, 2011 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1995/24 of December 01,2016) which specifies the Faculties and the Departments of the University of Sri Jayawardenepura, Sri Lanka, which isdeemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of thesaid Act, by the substitution for the item “Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences” appearing in Column I of the Scheduleto the aforesaid Order, under the heading “Faculty” and in its corresponding entries appearing in Column II of such Scheduleunder the heading “Department” of the following :-

“Column IColumn IIFacultyDepartment

Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences1.Department of Economics2.Department of Social Statistics3.Department of Geography4.Department of Sinhala and Mass Communication5.Department of History and Archeology6.Department of Pali and Buddhist Studies7.Department of Languages and Cultural Studies8.Department of English and Linguistics9.Department of Political Science10.Department of Criminology and Criminal Justice11.Department of Sociology12.Department of Anthropology”

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,14th July, 2017.
