PENSION MINUTE

Notification under the Section 51 of the Pension Minute dated 05th February, 1934, amended by the PensionMinute (Amendment) Act, No. 13 of 1948

NOTIFICATION

THE interim Section 2 and 17 of the Pension Minute as amended from time to time is hereby further amended by theinsertion immediately after aforesaid section of the following Sub section.

“Compulsory age limit of retirement of the officers in the Sri Lanka Engineering Service shall be extended up to61 years.”

The amendment made to the Pension Minute by this Notification deemed as coming into force with effect from07.02.2017.

R. M. RANJITH MADDUMA BANDARA,Minister of Public Administration and Management.
