PILGRIMAGE ORDINANCE (CHAPTER No. 175)

The Pageant In Which The Statute Of Deity Sri Sumana Saman And TheEnclouser Casket With Relics Will Be Carried To The Sri-Pada

EIGHT years after the entering in to the world great Bddha-hood, place the respectful foot emblem on the top of the Samantha-Kuta Summitin view of the invitation of the deity Sri Sumana Saman and made Sri Lanka purified. On that day onwards it was respected and worshipedand reverenced by the Kings and high esteems.

The statute of the deity Sri Sumana Saman is deposited in the Deity’s Palace in the Sri Pada Raja Maha Vihara Temple situated inGalpoththawala of Kuttapitiya Village in the Pelmadulla of Nawadun Korale of Ratnapura District in the Sabaragamu Province during theoff season.

1.On the day before full moon Poya of Unduwap of the year 2017, the ceremonial pageant in which the statute of the deity SriSumana Saman and the Enclosure Casket with Relics will be carried from Galpoththawala Sri Rajamaha Vihara to mountSamantha-kuta as follows:–

* The statute of the deity will be open to devotees for offerings and worshipping from 01.12.2017 at 5.00 p.m. to02.12.2017 5.00 a.m.

* Early in the morning of 02.12.2017, the pageant will start from Sri Pada Raja Maha Vihara.

1. At 05.02 am the vehicle pageant will be conducted from Sri Pada Raja Maha Vihara to Official Arama at Nall-thannithrough Hatton Road.

2. At 06.01 am the vehicle pageant will be conducted from Sri Pada Raja Maha Vihara to Palabaddala Temple throughRathnapura Road.

3. At 07.54 am from Sri Pada Raja Maha Vihara to Official Arama at Nall-thanni through Balangoda Road.
