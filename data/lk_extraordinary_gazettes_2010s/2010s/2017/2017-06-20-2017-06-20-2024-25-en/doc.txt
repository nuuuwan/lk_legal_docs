EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Proclamations & C., by the President

L.D.B. 6/77(ii)

EXTRADITION LAW, No. 8 OF 1977

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3(1) of the Extradition Law, No. 8 of 1977, I, Maithreepala Sirisena,President do by this Order declare that, since a Treaty was signed at Beijing on the 07th day of April 2016 between theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the People's Republic of China, a foreign state, in order to establish a bilaterallegal mechanism governing the transfer of persons subject to prosecution and sentenced persons and to improve the efficiencyof justice which would develop co-operation between the State Parties to the Treaty in the prevention of offences and inflictof punishment in respect of such offences and that the parties to the Treaty undertake to extradite to each other, upon requestand subject to the provisions of the Treaty, the persons who are found within the territories of the respective State Parties forprosecution or execution of sentences ; and that the provisions of such Treaty is in conformity with the provisions of Section4 of the Extradition Law No. 8 of 1977 ; the provisions of the said Law No. 8 of 1977 shall apply in respect of such foreign State,subject to such modification, limitation or condition as specified in the Treaty.

President.

Colombo,15th June 2017.
