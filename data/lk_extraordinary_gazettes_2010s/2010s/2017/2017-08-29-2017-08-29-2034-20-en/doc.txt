REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Kudavithanage Pemarathne Gunasiri who resided in the village of Udugama, in the Divi-sional Secretary’s Division of Meerigama, in the District of Gampaha.
