PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 17 OF 2017

Order under Section 3A(1)

I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka in terms of powers vested in meunder Sub-section 3A (1) of the Provincial Councils Elections (Amendment) Act No. 17 of 2017 do hereby appoint thefollowing as members of the Delimitation Committee for purposes of the aforesaid Act-
