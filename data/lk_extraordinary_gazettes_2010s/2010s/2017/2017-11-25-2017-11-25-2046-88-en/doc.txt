PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS - 2018

Exhibition of Electoral Registers

THE public is hereby notified that the valid electoral register to be used for the forthcoming elections to Local Authoritieshave been prepared by the relevant Returning Officers for each of the relevant Local Authorities based on the ElectoralRegister certified on the 12th of October, 2017 for the year 2017 for the purpose of Parliamentary Elections. Suchregisters have been made available for inspection by the relevant Returning Officers at the offices of the respective LocalAuthorities. It is also further notified that stets have been taken to include the names of all electors whose names are inthe Electoral Register for 2017 in these registers.

If an elector finds that his/her name has not been included in the register of the relevant Local Authority or thatthe name of an unqualified elector has been included in such a register, such information can be brought to the notice ofthe Returning Officer of the relevant District Election Office, in writing, on or before 05th December, 2017. It is alsofurther notified that, the Returning Officers have taken measures to exhibit all relevant notices at the offices of all LocalAuthorities.
