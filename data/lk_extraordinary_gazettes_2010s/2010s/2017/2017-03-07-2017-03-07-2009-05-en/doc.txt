PART 1: SECTION (1) - GENERAL

.SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake,Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of list of the commoditiesspecified in Column 1 of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries incolumn 11 of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from 08th March 2017, subject toprovisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1984/26 of 15.09.2016 in respect of Sugar (Other/Raw, HS Code Nos. 1701.12, 1701.13, 1701.14, 1701.91.10, 1701.91.90,1701.99.30,1701.99.90) are rescinded with effect from 8th March, 2017.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,

Minister of Finance.
