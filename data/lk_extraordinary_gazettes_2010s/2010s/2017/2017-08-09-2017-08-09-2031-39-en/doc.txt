NOTIFICATION UNDER THE CUSTOMS ORDINANCE

(Revenue Protection Order No. 02/2017)

IT is hereby notified that following resolution under Section 10 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235) was passed byParliament at its Public Session on July 26, 2017.

R. H. S. SAMARATUNGA,

Secretary,Ministry of Finance and Mass Media.

Resolution
