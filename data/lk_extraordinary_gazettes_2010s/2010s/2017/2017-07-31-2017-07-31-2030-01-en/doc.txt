CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, W. A. Chulananda Perera, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 31st July, 2017, all duties of Customsas well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid andreceived at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2029/1 of24th July, 2017 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notificationto be published on or before 06th August, 2017.

W. A. CHULANANDA PERERA,Director General of Customs.
