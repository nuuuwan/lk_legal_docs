THE NATIONAL MEDICINES REGULATORY AUTHORITY ACT, No. 5 OF 2015

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine under Section 142 read with Section 118 of the National Medicines Regulatory Authority Act, No. 5 of 2015.

Dr. Rajitha Senaratne, Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine.Colombo,12th December, 2017.

REGULATIONS

The National Medicines Regulatory Authority (Ceiling on Prices) Regulations No. 2 of 2016 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1989/61 of October 21, 2016 is hereby amended as follows :-

(1) by the substitution for regulation 1 thereof, of the following :-

“1. These regulations may be cited as the Ceiling on Prices Regulations, No. 6 of 2017 and shall come into operation on 14.12.2017 ”;

(2) by the substitution for regulation 4 thereof, of the following :-

“4. On and after the relevant date, no manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of any Scheduled drug set out in column II of the Schedule hereto, for the purpose of sale shall sell such drug above the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) stipulated in the corresponding entry in column V thereof.”;

(3) by the substitution for regulation 5 thereof, of the following :-

“5. A manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of a Scheduled drug for the purpose of sale currently sells such drug at a price less than the MRP stipulated in the Schedule may increase the existing price by only five per centum (5%) and shall maintain at such revised price.”;

(4) by the substitution for regulation 6 thereof, of the following :-

“6. The relevant MRP as set out in the Schedule hereto or the current retail price, whichever is less, of each unit of item in the stock of Scheduled drugs manufactured or available in the market, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of such drug for the purpose of sale on the relevant date shall be printed or marked on its commercial package or label as expeditiously as practicable, so however, before the expiry of a period of Forty-five days from the relevant date.”;

(5) by the substitution for regulation 7 thereof, of the following :-

“7. Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 6, the MRP of each unit shall be as set out in the Schedule on and after the relevant date. The MRP set out in the Schedule shall continue to be valid and effectual from the relevant date until revised.”;

(6) by the substitution for regulation 9 thereof, of the following :-

“9. Any manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of a Scheduled drug for the purpose of sale and contravenes the provisions of these regulations commits an offence and shall be triable under part IV of the National Medicines Regulatory Authority Act, No. 5 of 2015.”;

(7) by the substitution for regulation 10 thereof, of the following :-

“10. It shall be the duty of every manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of Scheduled drugs for the purpose of sale, to display at every retail outlet the MRP of the Scheduled drugs set out in the Schedule hereto.”; and
