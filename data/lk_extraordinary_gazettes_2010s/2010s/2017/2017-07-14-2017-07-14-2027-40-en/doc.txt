Northern Province Health Service (Amendment) Statute No. 10/2016

A STATUTE TO AMEND THE HEALTH SERVICE STATUTE OF

NORTHERN PROVINCE, No. 01 OF 2015

BE it enacted by the Northern Province Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Statute may be cited as the Finance (Amendment) Statute of Northern Province,

No. 10 of 2016 and it shall come into operation on such date as the Governor of the NorthernProvince may give his Assent thereto.

2. Section 8 of the Health Service Statute of the Northern Province No 01 of 2015 is hereby

deleted, and the following new subsection is inserted.

‘‘The Provincial Director shall in the exercise, discharge and performance of the powers,duties and functions by this Statute in matters relating to public health, act in consultationwith the Head of Local Authority Commissioner/ Secretary within its limits and the Head ofLocal Authority Commissioner/Secretary shall assist and furnish the required informationto the Provincial Director''

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Tamil and Sinhala or English texts of this

Statute, the Tamil texts shall prevail.
