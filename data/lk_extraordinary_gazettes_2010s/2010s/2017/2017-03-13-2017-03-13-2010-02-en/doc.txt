ORDER UNDER SUB SECTION (4), OF SECTION 27 ‘A’ OF 1972 OF THELAND REFORM ACT, NO. 01 OF 1972 OF THE NATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 27 a (4) of the Land Reform Act, 01 of 1972 amended by the Land Reform(Special Provisions) Act No. 39 of 1981 of the National State Assembly I, John Anthony Emmanuel Amaratunga, Ministerof Lands do hereby,

a. Amend the Order by way of revocation of the Order made on the portion of land in relation to the extent of landcalled Hutvil Waththa alias Haitiville Waththa speaifically depicted in the Second Schedule of the Order madeunder Section 27 a (1) of the Land Reform Act, No. 1 of 1972 published in the Gazette Extraordinary no195/6 dated 31st May 1982 in respect of the land called Hutvil Waththa alias Haitiville Waththa, which has beenvested in Janatha Estates Development Board established under State Agricultural Corporation Act, No. 11 of1972 and depicted comprehensively in first schedule of this Order, due to not following the order or conditionsof the Agreement made with regard to the vesting of the land to Janatha Estates Development Board.

b. It is hereby declared that the extent of land depicted specifically in Second Schedule of this Order is vestedagain in the Land Reform Commission as a result of this revocation.
