Appointments made by the Hon. Governor of North Central Province

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 32(a) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, I, Punchi BandaDissanayaka, the Governor of the North Central Province do hereby notify that the names of the officers, mention belowhave been appointed as the relevant posts for North Central Provincial Services.

1.Mr. R. A. S. Jayathilaka, Officer in Special Grade of Sri Lanka Administrative Services the Secretary of theNorth Central Province from 2016.04.11 to 2017.01.19
