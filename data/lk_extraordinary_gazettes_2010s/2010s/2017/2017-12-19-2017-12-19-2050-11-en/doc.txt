EXCISE DUTY ON IMPORT OF NON - POTABLE SPIRITS

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by sections 2, 12, 22 and 25, read with section 32 of the Excise Ordinance(Chapter 52), as amended from time to time, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media do bythis order amend the Rules set out in the Excise Notification No. 999 of the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2044/33dated 09 November 2017 by inserting the following paragraph immediately after paragraph 5 and renumberingparagraph 6 as paragraph 7.

“ 6. Any export oriented manufacturing company, which has entered into an agreement with the Board ofInvestment of Sri Lanka and exports not less than 80% of total products manufactured using, as rawmaterials the items in the schedule I of the Excise Notification No. 999 of the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2044/33 dated 09 November 2017 and such export is certified by the Director General of the Boardof Investment of Sri Lanka, the rate of excise duty for import of such raw materials is 10% of thepayable duty specified in the said Excise Notification No. 999.”

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,19 December 2017.
