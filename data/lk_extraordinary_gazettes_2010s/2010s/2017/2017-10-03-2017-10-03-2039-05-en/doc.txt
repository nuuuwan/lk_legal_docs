THE FAUNA AND FLORA PROTECTION ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 469)

Order under Subsection (2) of Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub-section (2) of Section 2 of the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance(Chapter 469), I, Gamini Jayawickrama Perera, the Minister of Sustainable Development and Wildlife, do by this Orderdeclare the area of land described in the Schedule hereto to be Sanctuary for the purposes of the aforesaid Ordinance andfurther declare that the said area of land shall be called and known as “Kodigahakanda Sanctuary”.

GAMINI JAYAWICKRAMA PERERA,Minister of Sustainable Development and Wildlife.
