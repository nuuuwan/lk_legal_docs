SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from November 09, 2017, subject to provisionsof Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2028/44 of 19.07.2017 in respect of Sprats classifiable under the HS Code0305.59.20, Gazette Notification No. 2039/2 of 02.10.2017 in respect of Dried Fish classifiable under the HS Code0305.59.90, Gazette Notification No. 2032/24 of 16.08.2017 in respect of Potatoes, B’Onion and Lentils classifiableunder the HS Code 0701.90, 0703.10.20, 0713.40.11, 0713.40.12, 0713.40.21, 0713.40.22 and Gazette NotificationNo. 2033/31 of 23.08.2017 in respect of Vegetable oils classifiable under the HS Headings 15.07, 15.11, 15.12 and15.13 are rescinded with effect from November 09, 2017.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,08th November, 2017.
