SRI LANKA INSTITUTE OF ARCHITECTS

The Correction of the Publication in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2026/ 22 of 03rd July 2017 of theRevision of the Regulations Gazetted in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka (Extraordinary) No. 1416/ 10 of Tuesday, 25th October 2005

THE notification relating to Regulations of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2026/ 22 of 03rd July 2017 is hereby corrected as follows :

As per the powers vested in the Council of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects by Section 6 (1) and 6(2) readwith Sections 3 and 4 of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects Law No. 1 of 1976 as amended by the Sri Lanka Institute ofArchitects (Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 1996, a decision has been taken to repeal clauses 4.2.2.3 (4) and 4. 2. 2. 4 (4) ofthe Regulations of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1416/ 10 of 25thOctober 2005 and to replace the said clauses with the following new clauses. The General Membership of the Sri LankaInstitute of Architects at the Quarterly General Meeting held on 26th August 2016 has approved the said decision asrequired under Section 6 (3) of Sri Lanka Institute of Architects Law No. 1 of 1976 as amended by the Sri Lanka Instituteof Architects (Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 1996.

4.2.2.3. (4)A Graduate Member upon the expiration of five years from the date of admission, shall cease to bea Graduate Member unless on application, the Council extends such Membership.

4.2.2.4. (4)A Student Member upon the expiration of eight years from the date of admission, shall cease to bea Student Member unless on application, the Council extends such Membership.

Archt. ROHANA BANDARA HERATH,Honorary Secretary.Sri Lanka Institute of Architects,No. 120/7, Vidya Mawatha,Colombo 07.15th September, 2017.
