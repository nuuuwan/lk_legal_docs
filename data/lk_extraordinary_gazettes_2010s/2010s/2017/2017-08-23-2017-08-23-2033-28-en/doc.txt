APPOINTMENT OF DISTRICT OPIUM BOARDS UNDER THE OPIUM ORDINANCE IN 1910,AMENDED BY THE POISONS, OPIUM AND DANGEROUS DRUGS ORDINANCE No. 17 OF 1929

IN terms of the power vested on me under the Para 42(2) of the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance No. 17of 1929 (Chapter 549), I, Rajitha Senarathna, Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine hereby amended theOpium Board for Kalutara District as follows.

Accordingly, Mr. D. Senanayake, who was appointed for the membership of the District Opium Board inKalutara District, in terms of the special Gazette Notification No. 1978/26, dated 03.08.2016 is hereby replaced byRev. Dr. Elapatha Samitha Thero of Malewana Laboratory, Cinnamon Garden, Beruwala as a member of the DistrictOpium Board.

DR. RAJITHA SENARATHNA,Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine.

13th August 2017.
