Appropriation Statute

2016

A Statute to amend the Appropriation Statute No. 04 of 2015, of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

1. This Statute may be cited as the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Appropriation (Amendment) Statute No. 05of the 2016, and shall come into operation of the date of receiving the assent of the Governor of theSabaragamuwa Province.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Twenty Nine Billion, SixHundred and Fifty Million Two Hundred and Nineteen Thousand Five Hundred and Seventy One Rupees andThirty Cents only (Rs. 29,650,219,571.30) for the service of the period beginning on 1st of January, 2016 andending 31st of December, 2016 in this Statute referred to as the “Financial Year 2016” , which is specified in2nd paragraph of the Appropriation Statute No. 04 of 2015 as amended by Appropriation (Amendment) StatuteNo. 02 of 2016, the first Schedule is hereby amended as the expenditure of the Sabaragramuwa ProvincialCouncil which is estimated Rupees Thirty Two Billion Two Hundred and Five Million, Six Hundred and NinetyThousand Nine Hundred and Ten Rupees and Thirty Cents only (Rs. 32,205,690,910.30) for the service of theperiod beginning on 01st January 2016 and ending 31st of December 2016 in this statute referred to as the“Financial Year 2016” as the First Schedule hereof.

3. The limits mentioned in the Second Schedule of the Appropriation Statute No. 04 of 2015 as amended byAppropriation (Amendment) Statute No. 02 of 2016, relevant to the limits of the Advanced Accounts is herebyamended as per the second schedule hereof.
