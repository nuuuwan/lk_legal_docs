No. 2036/40 – FRIDAY SEPTEMBER 15, 2017

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No.: KDS/HAR/4/2/2/21. Land Ministry’s Reference No.: 4/3/8/2013/CC/54.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460), as amended by the Land Acquisition Act No. 28 of 1964 was given by me that the Government intends to acquire the following land for the purpose of K/Katu/Kurundugolla Central College:- Description of the land or servitude to be acquired :

The following land lots 03 in total extent of Hectare 0.0758 depicted in the Preliminary Plan No. Maha 6709 prepared by the Senior Superintendent Surveyor of Kandy District situated at Kurundugolla Village of Kulugammana Siyapathuwa Minor Division in the Harispathuwa Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kandy District in the Central Province.

01. Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Paragahamulakumbura; Claimant: A. R. M. Nawas, 37/4, Kurundugolla, Weralagama; Extent: 0.0654 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 2 Name of the Land: Paragahayata Pela; Claimant: Not Known; Extent: 0.0092 Hectare.

03. Lot No. 3 Name of the Land: Paragahamulakumbura; Claimant: A. R. M. Nawas, 37/4, Kurundugolla, Weralagama; Extent: 0.0012 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are required to appear before me personally, or by agent duly authorized in writing, at Divisional Secretary’s Office Harispathuwa on the 11th day of December 2017 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 11th day of December 2017 the nature of their interest in the land, and the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the aforesaid land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

J. M. J. K. JAYASUNDARA, Divisional Secretary, Harispathuwa.

Divisional Secretary’s Office, Harispathuwa, 11th day of December, 2017.
