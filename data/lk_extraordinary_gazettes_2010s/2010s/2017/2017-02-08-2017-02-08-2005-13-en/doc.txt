THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1454/37 of July 22, 2006 (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1991/43 of November 03, 2016), which specifies the Facultiesand the Departments of the University of Kelaniya, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the addition immediately afterentry “9. Department of Western Classical Culture and Christian Culture” appearing in Column II of the Schedule whichcorresponds to the item “Faculty of Humanities” appearing in Column I of the Schedule of the following entry :-

“10. Department of English Language Teaching”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,25th January 2017.
