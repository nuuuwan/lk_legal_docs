1A G 25948— 08 (07/2017)

SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Orders under Sections 32 and 33

I, Dayasiri Jayasekera, the Minister of Sports by virtue of powers vested in me under Sections 32(b) and 33 of the SportsAct, No. 25 of 1973 do hereby suspend with immediate effect, the registration of the Sri Lanka Boxing Association andthe Bridge Federation of Sri Lanka of the National Association/Federation of Sri Lanka Boxing Association and theBridge Federation of Sri Lanka and as a interim arrangement, appoint Mr. Samasundara Ekanayake Rajawardene TennakoonMudiyanselage Saman Priyantha Bandara, Director General of the Department of Sports Development as competentauthority with effect from 20.07.2017 to continue its functions and further order that the election be convened and heldto elect new office - bearers to the Sri Lanka Boxing Association and the Bridge Federation of Sri Lanka for 2017.

DAYASIRI JAYASEKERA (Attorney-at-Law),Minister of Sports.

Colombo,20th July, 2017.
