AMENDMENT TO SECTION 3(1)(a)(i) OF THE MINUTE ON THE SRI LANKA AUDIT SERVICE

THE Section 3(1)(a)(i) of the Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 588/5of 11th December 1989 and amended by the Gazettes Extraordinary No. 993/17 of 19th September 1997 andNo. 1056/23 of 04th December 1998 and No. 1904/31 of 04th March 2015 has been amended by the Public ServiceCommission and the amended Section is published for information.

This amendment will take effect from the date on which it is published in the Gazette.

H. M. GAMINI SENEVIRATHNE,The Secretary,Public Service Commission.Public Service Commission,No. 177, Nawala Road,Narahenpita,Colombo 05,11th July 2017.

Amendment to Section 3(1)(a)(i) of Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Service

The Section 3(1)(a)(i) of the Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Service published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 588/5 of 11th December 1989 and amended by the Gazettes Extraordinary No. 993/17 of 19th September 1997 andNo. 1056/23 of 04th December 1998 and No. 1904/31 of 04th March 2015 has been amended by the Public ServiceCommission and the amended Section is published for information.

3(1) Promotion of the Deputy Auditors General in Class I Grade I of the Sri Lanka Audit Service to SpecialGrade.(a) Qualifications to be fulfilled :

(i) A postgraduate degree in Accounting or Auditing (with a subject field or a subject) obtained from auniversity/institution recognized by the University Grants Commission and a fellow membership of theInstitute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka or in England and Wales or the Chartered Institute ofManagement Accountants, United Kingdom or the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants, UnitedKingdom.
