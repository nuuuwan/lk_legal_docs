THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978 OF THE NATIONAL STATEASSEMBLY

Order under Section 15(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 15(1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978enacted by the National State Assembly, I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Developmentdo by this Order vest with the Urban Development Authority with effect from today, the land situated at Dematagoda inWanathamulla Ward within the limits of the Municipal Council of Colombo described morefully in the Schedule hereto,being the land called Slaughter House belonging to the Colombo Municipal Council.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA (M. P.),Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th and 18th Floor,“Suhurupaya”,Subuthipura Road,Battaramulla,22nd February 2017.
