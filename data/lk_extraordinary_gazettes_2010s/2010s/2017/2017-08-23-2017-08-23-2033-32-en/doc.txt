APPROPRIATION STATUTE - 2017

A Statute to amend the Appropriation Statute No. 04 of 2016 as amended by Statute No. 01 of 2017 of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

1.This statute may be cited as the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Appropriation (Amendment) Statute No.02 of 2017 and shall come into operation of the date of receiving the assent of the Governor of theSabaragamuwa Province.
