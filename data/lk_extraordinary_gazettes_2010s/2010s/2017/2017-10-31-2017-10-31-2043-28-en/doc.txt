THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978 ENACTED BY THE NATIONALSTATE ASSEMBLY

Order under Section 15(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 15(1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978enacted by the National State Assembly, I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Developmentdo hereby this Order, vest with the Urban Development Authority with effect from today the lands morefully described inthe Schedule hereto being lands vested in the Municipal Council of Moratuwa.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th and 18th Floor,Suhurupaya,Battaramulla,27th October 2017.

SCHEDULE

One allotment of land called Madangahawatta depicted as Lot 164 in the Cadastral Map No. 520214 dated 2010July to 2010 September prepared by Senior Superintendent of Surveys situated in Moratuwa village within the MunicipalCouncil Limits of Mortatuwa in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Moratuwa in the Colombo District, Western Provinceand bounded as North by Lot 165 Cadastral Map No. 520214, East by Lot No. 165 of Cadastral Map No. 520214, Southby Lot No. 165 Cadastral Map No. 520214 and West by Lot No. 165 of Cadastral Map No. 520214 containing in extentof Zero point Zero Eight Zero Six (0.0806 Ha.)
