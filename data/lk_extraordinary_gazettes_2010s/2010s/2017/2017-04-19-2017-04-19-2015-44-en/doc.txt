THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the aforesaid Act establishing the Uva Wellassa Universityof Sri Lanka and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1395/15 of June 01, 2005 by the addition immediately afteritem “3.
