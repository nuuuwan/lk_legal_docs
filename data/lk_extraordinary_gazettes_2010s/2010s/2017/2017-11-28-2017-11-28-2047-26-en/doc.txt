UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the South Asian Institute of Technology and Medicine Ltd, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the CompaniesAct, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 64641, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order madeunder the said Act, and published in Gazatte Extraordinary, No. 1960/4 of March 28, 2016 by the addition immediatelyafter the entry “Bachelor of Science in Engineering in Civil Engineering” appearing in Column I and it’s correspondingentry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and Column II respectively of the Schedule II hereto,subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

South Asian Institute of Technology and Medicine Ltd shall, when admitting students for the degree programmespecified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Honours Bio Systems, adhere to the minimum entryqualifications of three (03) ‘S’ passes in the Bio Science stream or Bio System Technology stream in one and the samesitting at the GCE Advanced Level Examination or equivalent.

SCHEDULE II
