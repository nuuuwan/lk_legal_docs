SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from December 14, 2017, subject to provisionsof Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2044/6 of 06.11.2017 in respect of Sail Fish (Istiophorus platypterus) andMarlin Fish (Makaira spp) classifiable under the HS Code 0303.89.90 and Extraordinary Gazette NotificationNo. 2044/25 of 07.11.2017 in respect of Thora Fish (Narrow - barred Spanish mackerel/Scomberomorus commerson)classifiable under the HS Code 0303.89.90 are hereby resinded with effect from December 14, 2017.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,13th December, 2017.
