EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (A) — PROVINCIAL COUNCILS

Proclamations & C., by the Governors

NORTH CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Declaration Made by the Governor of the North Central Province

BY virtue of powers vested in me under paragraph 8(b) of Section 154(b) of the constitution of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka, I, P. B. Disanayake, Governor of the North Central Province declare hereby that the fifth session ofthe six Provincial Council of the North Central Province came to end on mid-night of December 09th 2016, and

By virtue of powers vested in me under Paragraph 8(a) of Section 154(b) of the aforesaid constitution, declarehereby that the six session of the sixth Provincial Council of the North Central Province shall meet at 10.00 a. m. onJanuary 31st 2017, at conference hall of the North Central Provincial Council.

P. B. DISANAYAKE,Governor,North Central Province.

Governor's Office,North Central Province,23rd January 2017.
