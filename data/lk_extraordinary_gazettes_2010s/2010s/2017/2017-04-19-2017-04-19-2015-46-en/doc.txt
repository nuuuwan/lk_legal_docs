L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1482/26 of February 02, 2007 (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2002/33 of January 18, 2017), which specifies the Facultiesand the Departments of the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the addition immediately afterentry “15. Department of Surgery” appearing in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item “Faculty ofMedicine” appearing in Column I of the Schedule of the following entry :-

“16. Department of Radiology”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,05th April 2017.
