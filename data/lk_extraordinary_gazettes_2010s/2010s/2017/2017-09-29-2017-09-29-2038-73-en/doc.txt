HEREBY publish the regulations enacted under sub clause (G), (H) and (I)(i) of clause 08. 1 of second part of Eastern ProvinceRoad Passengers Transport Authority Audience of No. 02, 2009 of Eastern Provincial Council.

These regulations shall be effective from 01st September, 2017.

Z. A. NAZEER AHAMED (Engineer),Chief Minister, Minister for Financial Planning, Law and Peace,

Provincial and Divisional Administration, Rural Development,

Tourism and Environment, Buildings and Constructions,

Transport and Rural industries and housing,

Eastern Provincial Council.

01.These regulations are introduced as "Regulations of certifying the safety and convenience of passengers No. 2, 2017"of Eastern Province Road Passenger Transport Authority.

I Part - Driver/Conductor Uniforms

02.Every driver or conductor engaged in service of every bus under the permit of Eastern Province Road PassengerTransport Authority or another Authority, shall be dressed in an uniform defined by the Authority when engaged inthe service of that bus.

03.The uniform which shall be used by drivers and conductors is the uniform described in the schedule one below.

04.The official identity card shall be dressed by Drivers and conductors shall be compulsorily prepared according toschedule No. 2 below.

05.In every occasion as defined in the orders the shirt specified for upper part of the body, and trouser or sarom droppeddown to the ankle.

06.The period of engage in service is from the first turn of the first day concerned to the bus to the end of the turnincluding the entire time.

official identity card from time to time by the authority.

12. (1) Drivers and conductors who break the orders enacted on uniform and official identity card make offenses and

subject to the discipline Procedure of clause 9 schedule 3.

(2) The registration of drivers and conductors who breaks the orders after three times will be cancelled and withinseven days of informing the driver and conductor the said cancellation, the identity of driver and conductor shouldreturn to the authority.

13. (1) Those drivers and conductors who break the orders regarding uniform and official identity card after fourth time

will be black listed and listing more than two times will make way for suspending or revocation permit of the bus.

(2) Any bus which is blacklisted for breaking orders of uniform and official badges without instruction of DirectorGeneral of the Authority shall not be granted travel turns, issue of monthly log sheet by any officer.

17. The details of ticket machine shall be entered by forwarding the permit book issued from permit holder to Eastern

Province Road Passenger Transport Authority regarding use of passenger ticket issuing machine.

18. The ticket issuing machine which is used for issuing tickets to passengers of the relevant bus should be referred to the

main office of the authority and get endorsed. It shall be submitted to authority and get updated every six months anytime ordered by the authority.

19. The ticket machine used for issuing tickets to passengers shall be forwarded to flying squad officers or any authorized

officers of the authority to examine it on their request by the conductor of the concerned bus.

20. In every ticket issued by the passenger ticket machine the following details should be printed in the order correctly

and clearly:

1. The registered number of the bus,2. The passenger permit of the bus,3. Date,4. Time,5. Serial number of the passenger ticket,6. Start of journey,7. Destination,8. Fees,9. Registered number of the conductor,10. Registered number of the driver,11. The insurance company where the bus is insured.

21. The permit holder should keep in security the reports regarding daily issuance of tickets, date, months, years to

produce in any occasion when requested by the authority.

22. The conductor should not in any occasion issue a passenger any ticket which is altered or deformed or printed

illegibly or which is not included the details defined by the authority.

23. Any conductor should not use a ticket issuing machine which is with defects of issuing tickets or defects in printing.

24. When a ticket issuing machine is suddenly damaged or subjected to any breakdown should be informed to the

officer-in-charge at the nearby bus stand and should be endorsed monthly running charge with his signature.

25. When a ticket issuing machine subjected unavoidable damage, a ticket book issued from the authority for issuing

tickets should be kept in the bus and tickets should be issued in connection to the route permit ticket.

26. The responsibility of the conductor of the relevant ticket machine is to maintain properly. The ticket issuing machine

and should not do anything to make changes of order the ticket issuing machine or to be printed with defect to printedtickets.

27. An appropriate certificate issued by the Road Passengers Transport Authority for the standard should be obtained

for every ticket issuing machine for issuing tickets to every passenger.

28. (i) Any conductor issuing tickets by a machine not certified by the authority will be a culprit under Clause 5 of

Schedule 3 and subject to the relevant procedure.

(ii) Any conductor will be a culprit for issuing a ticket which does not include the details defined by the authority oraltered or deformed and shall be subject to Clause 6 of Schedule 3 discipline procedure.
