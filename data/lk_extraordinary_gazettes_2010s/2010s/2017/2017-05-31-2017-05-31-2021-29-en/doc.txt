ORDER UNDER SECTION 3

THE JUDICATURE ACT, No. 2 OF 1978

By virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978, I Wijedasa Rajapaksha, Minister ofJustice in consultation with the chief Justice and the president of the Court of Appeal, do by this Order, amend the Orderpublished in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1679/40 of November 10, 2010 as last amended Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1890/17 of November 26, 2014 as follows;

(1)by the substitution for the words "Thirty Three Judicial Zones" of the words "Thirty Four Judicial Zones";

(2)in the Schedule thereto :-

(i)by the substitution for the entry "Avissawella" appearing in Column I under the heading "Zone" and its correspondingentry in Column IV under the heading of "Area of Jurisdiction" of the following entries respectively :-

"11.AvissawellaThe Judicial Zone of Avissawella shall comprise the Judicial Districts ofAvissawella and Ruwanwella.";

(ii)by the deletion of all the entries relating to the Judicial District, Judicial Division and Area of Jurisdiction "Homagama"appearing in Column II, III and IV recpectively, appearing against corresponding Judicial Zone of "Avissawella" underColumn I;

(iii)by the substitution for the entry "Panadura" appearing in column I under the heading "Zone" and its correspondingentry in Column IV under the heading of "Area of Jurisdiction" of the following entries respectively:-

"4.PanaduraThe Judicial Zone of Panadura shall comprise the Judicial Districts of Panadura,Horana and Moratuwa.";

(iv)by the deletion of all the entries relating to the Judicial District, Judicial Division and Area of Jurisdiction "Kesbewa"appearing in Column II, III and IV recpectively, appearing against corresponding Judicial Zone of "Panadura" underColumn I;
