THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1482/26 of February 02, 2007 andGazette Extraordinary No. 1885/2 of October 20, 2014, which specifies the Faculties and the Departments of theUniversity of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to bea University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the substitution for the item “Faculty of Arts” appearing inColumn I of Schedule to the aforesaid Order, under the heading “Faculty” and in its corresponding entries appearing inColumn II of such Schedule, under the heading “Department” of the following.
