THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 07

I hereby give notice, under sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, that Government intends to acquirethe following lands which are required for a public purpose:

Description of the land to be acquired:

Three (03) allotments of land in extent of 0.0757 Hectare,as Lot No. 1- 3 depicted in preliminary Plan No. CO 9689 anddated 31.03.2017 prepared by the Surveyor-General, situatedin the village of Raththanapitiya in Divisional Secretary’sDivision of Kesbewa in the District of Colombo in WesternProvince more fully described as follows:

1. Lot No. 1Name of land: A part of Assmt. No. 014 of Colombo Road;Description: Garden, Contains a permanent building;Claimant: State; Original Claimant: K.D.S. Fernando, No. 01,G.H. Perera Road, Raththanapitiya, Boralesgamuwa; Extent:0.0035 Hectare.

2. Lot No. 2Name of land: Dawatagaha Kumbura; Description: Garden,Contains a mixed cultivation; Claimant: State; OriginalClaimant: Encroached by Weresganga Rainwater Drainage &

Environment Improvement Project (Part of Lot 13 in PP CO.9468); Extent: 0.0366 Hectare.

3. Lot No. 3Name of land: Dawatagaha Kumbura; Description: PaddyField; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Encroached byWeresganga Rainwater Drainage & EnvironmentImprovement Project (Parts of Lots 15, 16, 17 & 18 in PP CO.9468); Extent: 0.0356 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land hereby requiredto appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing,before me at Kesbewa Divisional Secretariat on the 05th May,2017 at 9.30 a.m. and hereby requested to notify to me inwriting (in duplicate) on or before 28th April, 2017 the natureof their interests in the land, the particulars or their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land the amountof compensation and the details of the computation of suchamount.

G. S. K. KANNANGARA,

Divisional Secretary,

Divisional Secretary's Office,Kesbewa,04th April 2017.
