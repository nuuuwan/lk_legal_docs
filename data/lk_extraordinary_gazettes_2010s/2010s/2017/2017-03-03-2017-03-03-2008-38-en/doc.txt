THE SOCIETIES ORDINANCE

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 and 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), I, Rishad BathiudeenMinister of Industry and Commerce, do by this Notification.

01.Authorize the purpose for which the Society known as “Women’s Association of Sri Lanka Malays” situatedat No. 96, De Mel Watta, Koswatta, Nawala, Rajagiriya as a purpose to which the powers and facilities of thatOrdinance ought to be extended ; and
