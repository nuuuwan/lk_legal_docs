Government Notifications

DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY OF THE DIRECTOR GENERAL OF DEPARTMENT OFDIVINEGUMA DEVELOPMENT TO DIVISIONAL SECRETARIES

I, Neel Bandara Hapuhinna, who has been appointed as the Director General of the Department of Divineguma Developmentby the Section 3(1) of the Divineguma Act, No. 1 of 2013, do hereby deligate powers, functions and duties of the Departmentof Divineguma to all the Divisional Secretaries of the Island according to the Section 3(2) of the said Act for the purposeof implementing the provisions of Divineguma Act within your Divisional Secretariat Division.

When implementing the duties of the Department of Divineguma Development as per this deligation of power,you will be subjected to the general administration of the Additional Director General (District Secretary/GovernmentAgent) who has been appointed as per the Section 38 of the Divineguma Act of your Divisional Secretariat Division inaddition to my advices and orders.

NEEL BANDARA HAPUHINNA,Director General,Department of Divineguma Development.
