THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) AS AMENDED BY THE LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDEMENT)

ACT No. 28 OF 1964

Notice under Section 7

My No.: KEG/LAN/AQUIS/A3/99.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act as Amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment Act), No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the lands described the schedule given below forthe reconstruction of the bridge No. 11/4, Bulathkohupitiya-Karawanella Road in Kegalle.

Description of the Lands to be acquired:

Four allotments of land described in the Scheduled attached below depicted in preliminary Plan No: P. P. K. 3529 and TwentyEight Allotments of land described in the Scheduled attached below depicted in Preliminary Plan No: P. P.K. 3530 prepared bythe Surveyor Genaral situated in Hungampola and Godigamuwa Grama Niladhari Divisions (within the Kegalle PradeshiyaSabha boundaries) in the Divisional Secterary's Division in Kegalle District of Sabaragamuwa Province.

Dates for Inquires:

Lot No: 01 - 04 of P. P. K. 3529on the date of 15.05.2017at 9.00 a.m.Lot No: 01 - 07 of P. P. K. 3530on the date of 15.05.2017at 10.00 a.m.Lot No: 08 - 15 of P. P. K. 3530on the date of 16.05.2017at 9.00 a.m.Lot No: 16 - 28 of P. P. K. 3529on the date of 17.05.2017at 9.00 a.m.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally or by agents dulyauthorized in written before me at Divisional Secretariat, Kegalle on the above mentioned days and on the scheduled time.

And are hereby requested to notify me in writing with duplicate of on or before 28h April 2017 the nature of their interest inthe land the Particulars of their claims for compensation for acquisition of the Said Land the amount of Compensation and thedetails of the Computation of such amount.

H. T. R. NALIKA PIYASENA,
