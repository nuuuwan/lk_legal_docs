SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION OF SRI LANKA ACT, No. 36 OF 1987

Order under Section 14A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Subsection (1) of Section 14A of the Securities and Exchange Commissionof Sri Lanka Act, No. 36 of 1987, I Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, doby this Order, specify in the Schedule hereto, the Rate of Cess to be charged and levied under Subsection (1) ofSection 14A of the above Act.

The Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1680/26 of November 19, 2010 pertaining to the Rateof Cess to be charged and levied, is hereby rescinded.

RANIL WICKREMESINGHE,Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs.Colombo,14th June, 2017.
