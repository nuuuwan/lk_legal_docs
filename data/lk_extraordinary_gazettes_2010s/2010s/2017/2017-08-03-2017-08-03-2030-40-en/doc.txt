I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as Amended by Land Acquisition Act,that the Government intends to acquire the following landswhich are required for a public purpose.

Following allotments of land called Falmgardenwaththa,Jambugahaarawa, Galgodedeniya, Pedikumbura, AgalawaththaDiganahenewaththa and Thennepitawaththa depicted as LotNo. 353 in Extent 5.2633 Hectares in F.V.P.347 situated inWeralupa Village of 153A New Town Grama Niladari Divisionof Uda Paththuwa Sub Division in Kuruwita Koralaya,Divisional Secretary's Division Rathnapura in the District ofRathnapura in Sabaragamuwa Province.

Moreover, they are here by requested to notify to me inwriting with two duplicates on or before 16th September, 2017the nature of their interest in land, the particulars of theirclaims for compensation for the acquisition of the said lands,the amount of compensation and details of the computationof such amount.
