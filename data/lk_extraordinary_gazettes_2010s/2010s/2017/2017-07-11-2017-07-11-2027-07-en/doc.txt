EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order amend item No. 1(d) of the Schedule II of the Ordermade under that Section and published by the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1992/29 dated 10.11.2016, bysubstitution of the following Schedule hereto.

This Order shall come into effect from 12.07.2017.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,10th July, 2017.

SCHEDULEDescriptionExcise Duty

1(d)Purchase of a locally manufactured motor vehicle or import of a30% of themotor vehicle by a Sri Lankan Diplomatic Officer who has served inpayable dutymissions abroad, using a permit issued by the Ministry of ForeignAffairs under the Circular No. 210(III) dated 31.10.2016 andsubsequent amendments.
