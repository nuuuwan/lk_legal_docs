SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Lakshman YapaAbeywardana, Acting Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in thecorresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from19th January 2017, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1996/50 of 09.12.2016 in respect of Vegetable Oils (H.S Heading Nos. 15.07, 15.11, 15.12, 15.13) are rescinded with effectfrom 19th January 2017.

LAKSHMAN YAPA ABEYWARDANA,

Acting Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,18th January, 2017.
