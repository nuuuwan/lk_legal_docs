THE FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES ACT, No. 2 OF 1996

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development under Section 61 read withSection 29 and Section 61(1)(u) of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.

MAHINDA AMARAWEERA,Minister of Fisheries and AquaticResources Development.

Colombo,05th April, 2017.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as Prohibition of Catching Fish Species Thambuwa (cephalopholis sonnerati)Regulations 2017.

2. No person shall catch, possess, transport, purchase, exhibit for sale, sell or export the fish species Thambuwa,cephalopholis sonnerati within Sri Lanka or Sri Lanka Waters.
