PART III — LANDS

Land Reform Commission Notices

Order No. 33Unique No. Ka/14

ORDER UNDER SECTION 22(1)(f) OF THE LAND REFORM ACT, NO. 1 OF 1972 OF THE NATIONAL STATEASSEMBLY

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under para (f) of Sub section (1) of Section 22, of land Reform Act, No. 1 of 1972of the National State Assembly, which has been amended by Act No. 39 of 1975, Act No. 14 of 1981, Act No. 39 of 1981,Act No. 14 of 1986 and Act No. 18 of 1986, I, John Anthony Emmanuel Amaratunge, the Minister of Lands do hereby thisOrder declare that,

(a) the agricultural land indicated specifically in Column II of the Schedule, under this Order shall be alienated tothe persons whose names are indicated specifically in Column I of the Schedule ;(b) The agricultural lands indicated specifically in the corresponding note of Column II of the Schedule, under thisOrder were belonged to the persons, whose names appeared specifically in Column I of the Schedule, and thesaid agricultural lands were included in the extent of lands which were considered to be belonged to the head ofthe family, in which the above persons were also the members.(c) The persons indicated in Column I of the Schedule, under this Order were below 18 years of their age at thetime of imposing the ceiling, on agricultural lands in terms of the above Act.(d) The agricultural lands indicated specifically in Column II of the Schedule, have not been alienated to any personunder Section 22 of the aforesaid Act.(e) No compensation has been paid under Section 28 of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the above agricultural lands.

JOHN ANTHONY EMMANUEL AMARATHUNGA,Minister of Lands.

Ministry of Lands,Land Secretariat,“Mihikatha Medura”,No. 1200/6, Rajamalwaththa Avenue,Battaramulla,02nd December 2016.
