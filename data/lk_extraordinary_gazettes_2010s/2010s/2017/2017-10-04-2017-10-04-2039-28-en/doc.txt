THE NATIONAL THOROUGHFARES ACT, No. 40 OF 2008

Order under Section 86

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 86 of the National Thoroughfares Act, No. 40 of 2008, I, LakshmanKiriella, Minister of Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, restrict access by the public to or from thePiliyandala Bypass which was declared as a national highway or any part of such highway other than from the access roadsspecified in the Schedule to the Order made under Section 8 of the aforesaid Act and published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1888/48 of November 14, 2014, for the reasons stated below :

1)to prevent the reclamation of paddy fields and low lying lands alongside the Piliyandala Bypass ; and

2)to prevent the reduction of productivity of the Piliyandala Bypass due to unauthorized access connections.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Battaramulla,02nd October 2017.
