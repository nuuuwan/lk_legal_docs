THE FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS REPORTING ACT, No. 6 OF 2006

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Finance under Section 29 read with Section 7 of the Financial TransactionsReporting Act, No. 6 of 2006.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

30th March 2017,Colombo.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Suspicious Transactions (Format) Regulations of 2017.

2. These regulations shall apply to every Institution within the meaning of Section 33 of the Act.

3. (1) Every suspicious transaction report under Section 7(1) of the Act, in respect of Institutions specified inColumn I shall, substantially be in the forms specified in the respective Schedules specified in the Column II of thisregulations.

(2) Every suspicious transactions report referred to above, in respect of Institutions other than those specifiedin Column I shall, substantially be in the form specified in Schedule V of this regulation.
