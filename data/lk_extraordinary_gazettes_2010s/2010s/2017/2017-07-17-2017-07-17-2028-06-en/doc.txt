THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE FLOWERS, ORNAMENTAL PLANTS, VEGETABLES AND FRUIT GROWINGAND EXPORT TRADE

IT is hereby notified under Section 29(3) read with Section 33(3) of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136) thedetermination relating to the minimum rate of wages in respect of workers engaged in Flowers, Ornamental Plants, Vegetablesand Fruits Growing and Export Trade specified in the Schedule hereto made by the Commissioner of Labour under Section33(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance in pursuance of the authority conferred on him by an order made by Hon. Minister ofLabour, under Section 33(1)(a) of the Ordinance and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1916/12 of May 27, 2015has been approved by the Hon. Minister of Labour, Trade Union Relations and Sabaragamuwa Development.

The determination shall come into force on the 01st August, 2017.

S. A. N. SARANATISSA,Secretary,Ministry of Labour, Trade Union Relations andSabaragamuwa Development.

Labour Secretariat,Colombo 05,13th July 2017.
