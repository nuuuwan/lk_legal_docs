FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES ACT No. 2 OF 1996

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development and State Minister of MahaweliDevelopment under Section 61(1)(u) of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.

MAHINDA AAMARAWEERA,Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development,and State Minister of Mahaweli Development.

Colombo,14th June, 2017.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Fish and Fishery Products, Export, Import and Re-export ManagementRegulations 2017.

2. Subject to the provisions of the Food Act, No. 26 of 1980 and any regulation made thereunder relating to fishspecies or fishery product imported for local consumption, any person engaged in, exporting, importing, and re-exportingof any fish species or fishery product for food, for human consumption or to be used as a bait in fresh, frozen or cannedform or any type of processed or live form shall be subject to the terms and conditions of these regulations.

3. Export, import or re-export of fish and fishery products shall be subject to the terms and conditions imposedby the Director-General in accordance with international obligations and to the provisions of the Customs Ordinance(Chapter 235) and the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969.

4. Exporter, importer or re-exporter of any Fish or Fishery product shall fulfill the requirements stipulated inaccordance with the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES).

PART I

REGISTRATION FOR EXPORT, IMPORT AND RE-EXPORT OF ANY FISH OR FISHERY PRODUCT

5. Any person engaged in Export, import and re-export of any Fish or Fishery products shall register with theDepartment of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources for that purpose.

6. Any person who intends to register an establishment to export, import and re-export of fish or fishery productsshall submit an application in Form I, Form 2 and Form 3 respectively specified in Schedule I of these regulations to theDirector General subject to terms and conditions issued by him.

7. Annual registration fee specified in Schedule 2 hereto shall be paid by the applicant together with theapplication for registration of any establishment.

8. Any authorized officer shall conduct an on site inspection at the relevant establishment or company whereapplicable according to the guidelines on export, import and re-export of any Fish or Fishery product.

PART II

REQUIREMENTS FOR EXPORT, IMPORT AND RE-EXPORT OF FISH AND FISHERY PRODUCTS

9. These regulations shall be applicable to any fish or fishery product entering Sri Lanka with a valid bill of ladingor airway bill.

10. Any fish or fishery product entering Sri Lanka without a valid bill of laden or airway bill or engaged intransshipment shall be subject to the Port State Measures to prevent, deter and eliminate illegal, unreported andunregulated Fishing Regulations 2015.

11. Any person carrying out on-port transactions of fish or fishery products shall furnish a bill of lading and othertraceability documents as specified by the Director-General.

12. Prior to coming into operation of these regulations no person shall engage in export or re-export of fisheryproducts in an establishment unless such establishment is registered with the Department of Fisheries and AquaticResources under Fish Processing Establishment Regulations No. 1 of 2003.

13. Any person who intends to export, import and re-export of any fish or fishery product shall declare thespecies, type and form of product according to the applicable HS codes which are in accordance with the internationalcommodity coding system.

14. No person shall export, import and re-export of any fish or fishery products prohibited under Fisheries andAquatic Resources Act, No. 02 of 1996.

15. No person shall export, import and re-export any fish or fishery product categorized or declared asoriginated from illegal, unreported or unregulated fishing by any Regional Fisheries Management Organization (RFMO).

16. No person shall export fish or fishery products which are not clearly traceable in the accompanying certifiedlog sheet it applicable.

17. No person shall export, import or re-export any fish or fishery products without a valid catch certificatewhere appliable or a validating document as legally caught fish or fishery product made out of such fish in terms of theconditions specified in the Fish Catch Date Collection Regulation 2014.

18. Details of all fish species shall be provided under the statistical document programmes of relevant RegionalFisheries Management Organization as required by the Director-General.

19. Statistical documents mentioned in regulation 18 shall be validated by an authorized government official ofthe flag state of the vessel that harvested the fish, or if the vessel is operating under a charter arragement, by an authorizedofficer of the exporting state.

20. The re-export certificate of such statistical document shall be validated by an officer authorized by theDirector-General in writing.

21. The Director-General with the approval of the Minister assigned the subjects of Fisheries and AquaticResources Development may impose precautionary management measures such as closure periods, limitations for sizes,volume, quantities, types, forms and duration for export or re-export for conservation purpose.

PART III

INSPECTION OF IMPORT AND FISH PROCESSING ESTABLISHMENT FOR EXPORT OR RE-EXPORT OF FISH AND FISHERY PRODUCTS

22. Every importer, owner or license of the fish or fishery product processing establishment shall permit andfacilitate any officer authorized by the Director-General to enter, inspect, collect samples and data and documents at thesaid establishment or at the point of import without prior notice.

23. Such officer authorized by the Director-General shall provide a details inspection report within seven daysof the inspection specified in regulation 22 carried out in terms of the said regulation.

PART IV

Suspension or cancellation of the Registration and Licenses

24. Where any person contravenes any provisions of these regulations, the Director-General shall suspend orcancel the registration for a period not exceeding six months, taking into consideration the magnitude of suchcontravention.

25. An applicant or any person who is aggrieved by the decision of the Director-General refusing to grant acertificate or the renewal of a certificate issued, as the case may be, shall be, entitled to appeal against such decision tothe Secretary of the Ministry of the Minister assigned the subject of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development,within thirty days of such decision.

26. The Secretary may upon receipt of such appeal, make such inquiries as he may consider necessary orapproprirate and thereafter.

(a) allow the appeal and direct of the Director-General to issue a certificate or renew a certificate issued, asthe case may be ; or

(b) disallow the appeal and confirm the decision made by the Director-General.

27. In these regulations :

“Air freight” means import, export or re-export by air ;

“Authorized Officer” means any officer authorized under subsection (1) of Section 46 of the Fisheriesand Aquatic Resources Act, No. 02 of 1996 ;

“Baits” means Food placed on a fishing gear to entice fish or other animals ;
