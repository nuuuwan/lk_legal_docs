THE NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL ACT, No. 47 OF 1980

Order under Section 23 W

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 23 W of the National Environmental Act, No. 47 of 1980 read withSection 51of the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, MaithripalaSirisena, President, do by this Order prohibit the use of all forms of polyethylene, polypropylene, polyethylene products orpolypropylene products as decoration in political, social,religious, national, cultural or any other event or occasion.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Colombo,30th August, 2017.
