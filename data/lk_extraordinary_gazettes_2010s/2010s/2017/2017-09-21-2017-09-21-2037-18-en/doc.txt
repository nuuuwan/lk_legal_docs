THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1469/38 of November 03, 2006 (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2005/12 of February 08, 2017), which specifies the Facultiesand the Departments of the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the substitution for the item“Faculty of Engineering’’ appearing in Column I of the Schedule and its corresponding entries appearing in Column IIthereto, of the following :-
