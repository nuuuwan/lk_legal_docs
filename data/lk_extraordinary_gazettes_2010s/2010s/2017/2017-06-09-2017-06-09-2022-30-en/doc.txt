EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order amend item No. 1(c) of the Schedule II of the Ordermade under that Section and published by the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1992/29 dated 10.11.2016, bysubstitution of the following Schedule hereto.

This Order shall come into effect from 10.06.2017.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,07th June 2017.

SCHEDULE

DescriptionExcise Duty
