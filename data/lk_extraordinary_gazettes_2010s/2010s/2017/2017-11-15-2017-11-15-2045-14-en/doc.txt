THE INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT, CHAPTER 131 OF THE LEGISLATIVE ENACTMENTS OF CEYLON( 1956 REVISED EDITION)

Revocation of Order under Section 4(1)

WHEREAS by Order made under Section 4(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments ofCeylon (Revised Edition 1956) as amended by Acts., Nos. 14 and 62 of 1957, 14 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 read with IndustrialDisputes (Special Provisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968 dated 14.03.2017 and published in the Gazette of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka Extraordinary No. 2012/14 dated 28.03.2017 the Industrial Dispute in respect of the matter specified inthe statement of the Commissioner of Labour dated 22.02.2017.

Group of Employees including Mrs. S. P. Anoma Shyamali Kumari,Balagollagama, Balalla. of the one part

AND

Central Cultural Fund, No. 212/1, Bauddhaloka Mawatha, Colombo 07. of the other part

was referred to Mr. T.E. Santharajan for settlement by arbitration.

AND whereas it is now deemed expedient that the said order be revoked, I, W. D. J. Seneviratne, Minister of Labour, TradeUnion Relations and Sabaragamu Development do hereby revoke the said Order and further make Order that no proceedingsbe taken upon the said Order dated 14.03.2017.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations,and Sabaragamu Development.

Colombo.09th November, 2017.

STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether the group of employees including Mrs.S.P.Anoma Shyamali Kumari, who had worked in the post of ExcavationConservation Assistant, in the places of worship at Yapahuwa, Dambadeniya, Ridee Viharaya (Rideegama) and Nathagane(Panduwasnuwara) of the Vayamba Square of the Central Cultural Fund have been caused injustice by termination of theirservices from 02.11.2015 and if so to what reliefs they are entitled.

A. WIMALAWEERA,Commissioner General of Labour.

Dated at the Office of the Commissioner General of Labour,Colombo, this 03rd day of October, 2017.
