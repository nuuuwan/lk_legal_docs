PART 1: SECTION (1) - GENERAL

CIVIL AVIATION ACT, No. 14 OF 2010

ORDER issued by me, as the Minister in charge of Civil Aviation by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 23Sub-section 1 (a) of the Civil Aviation Act No. 14 of 2010.

NIMAL SIRIPALA DE SILVA (M.P.),Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation.

Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation,Sethsiripaya - Stage II,Battaramulla.

03rd February, 2017.

Order

(1) This order shall be cited as Batticaloa Airport Protected Area Order No. 01 of 2017.

(2) Each area of land within the meets and bounds specified in the schedule hereto is declared to be Protected Area undersection 23 sub-section 1(a) of the aforementioned Act.

(3) Any construction of a building or erection of any structure or making of any alternation to any existing, building orother structure within the Protected Area shall conform to the requirements to be specified by the Director General ofCivil Aviation in terms of Section 23 (4)
