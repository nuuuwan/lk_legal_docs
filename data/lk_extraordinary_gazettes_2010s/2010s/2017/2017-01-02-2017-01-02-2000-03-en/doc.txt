SRI LANKA SOUTHERN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY ACT, No. 18 OF 1996

Cancellation of the Declaration issued within the powers of 17th Clause

THERE have been a declaration issued within the powers vested under the Clause 17th of Sri Lanka Southern DevelopmentAuthority Act, No. 18 of 1996 of Sri Lanka Democratic Socialist Republic Parliament. The lands said below annex hasbeen declared vested in the Gazette Notification of 17th June 1997.

Since that there is no necessity by now to be vested those lands the cancellation of the Gazette Notification isannounced.

S. B. DISSANAYAKE,Member of Parliament,Minister of Social Empowerment and Welfare.
