THE LAND REFORM LAW, No. 1 OF 1972

Order under Subsection (4) of Section 27A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by subsection (4) of the Section 27A of the Land Reform Law, No. 1 of 1972 asamended by the Land Reform (Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981, I, John Anthony Emmanuel Amarathunga, Ministerof Land, Tourism and Christian Affairs, do by this Order -

(a) amend the Order, made under subsection (I) of Section 27A of the Land Reform Law, No. 1 of 1972, andpublished in Gazette Extraordinary No. 456/4 of July 1, 1987 relating to the land called and known as HantaneEstate morefully described in the First Schedule to this Order and which had vested in the National HousingDevelopment Authority established by the National Housing Development Authority Act, No. 17 of 1979 aslast amended by National Housing Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 30 of 1999, by revoking, thesaid Order as relates to the extent of land called Hantane Estate specified in the Second Schedule ; and

(b) State that upon revocation, such extent of land as specified in the Second Schedule to this Order, shall re-vest inthe Land Reform Commission.
