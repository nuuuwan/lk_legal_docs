PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Uva Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Uva Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission under Section 65(1)of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Uva ProvinceProvincial Council by reason of Death;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligibleperson to fill such vacancy;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political party, has nominated Mr. Kuda Bandara Medawelato fill such vacancy;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Kuda Bandara Medawela, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the AdministrativeDistrict of Moneragala in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

Mahinda Deshapriya-Chairman-Election Commission

N. J. Abeysekere-Member-Election Commission

S. Ratnajeevan H. Hoole-Member-Election Commission

12th September, 2017,Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya.
