Direction No. 63

CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Direction under Section 12(2)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 12(2) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, theConsumer Affairs Authority directs all manufacturers, importers, distributors and traders that they shall not manufacture,import, distribute, store or sell or display for sale, expose for sale or offer for sale, wholesale or retail, products/items ofoperating voltage not exceeding 250V and listed below, unless the said products/items bear the SLS Product CertificationMark issued by the Sri Lanka Standards Institution.

Product/ItemSLS

(i)Rewirable and non-rewirable plugsSLS 734 : Part 1(ii)Switched and un switched Socket outlets and extensionSLS 734 : Part 2cords/portable socket outlets(iii)Adaptors and one to one conversion adaptorsSLS 734 : Part 3(iv)Fused connection units (switched and un switched)SLS 734 : Part 4(v)Fused conversion plugsSLS 734 : Part 5(vi)Cable reels fitted with plugs and socket outletsSLS 1532

This Direction shall come into effect from 01st January, 2019

By Order of the Consumer Affairs Authority

HASITHA TILLEKERATNE,

Chairman.Colombo,07th December, 2017.

Direction No. 64

CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Direction under Section 12(2)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 12(2) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, theConsumer Affairs Authority directs all manufacturers, importers, distributors and traders that they shall not manufacture,import, distribute, store or sell or display for sale, expose for sale or offer for sale, wholesale or retail any electricalappliance of operating voltage not exceeding 250V, unless it carries an electrical plug conforming to SLS : 734 Part 1.

This Direction shall come into effect from 01st January, 2019

By Order of the Consumer Affairs Authority

HASITHA TILLEKERATNE,

Chairman.Colombo,07th December, 2017.
