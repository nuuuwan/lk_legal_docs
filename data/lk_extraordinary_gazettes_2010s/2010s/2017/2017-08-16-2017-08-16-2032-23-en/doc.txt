THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act in respect ofthe Sri Lanka Institute of Nanotechnology (Private) Limited, a Company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the CompaniesAct, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 64697, do by this Order and subject to the conditions specified inthe Schedule I hereto, recognize the Sri Lanka Institute of Nanotechnology (Private) Limited as a Degree AwardingInstitution for the purpose of developing Higher Education therein, leading to the award of the Degrees specified inColumn I of the Schedule II hereto, of which the abbreviations are set out in the corresponding entries respectively inColumn II of the Schedule II hereto :-

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

Sri Lanka Institute of Nanotechnology (Private) Limited (hereinafter referred to as the “Institute”) shall adhere to theminimum entry qualifications as prescribed in the Sri Lanka Qualification Framework (SLQF) as follows :-

i. A Bachelor’s Honours/Special degree of SLQF Level 6, in the relevant field ; or

ii. A Bachelor’s Honours degree of SLQF Level 6 in a related field and successful completion of a qualifyingexamination ; or

iii. A qualification of SLQF level 7 or above in the relevant field.
