THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: NYGM/LAN/7-41.Land Ministry's Ref. No.: 13/99/VE/33.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the LandAcquisition Act as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment)Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire thefollowing Land/Servitude for a public purpose.

Description of the land/Servitude to be acquired:

An allotment of Land described below and depicted in Plan No.FVP 592 Supplementary sheet No. 26 and 27 situated in No. 30 B,Pitigala North Grama Niladhari Division of Niyagama DivisionalSecretary's Division in Bentara Walallavita Korale South in GalleDistrict Southern Province.

01. Lot No. 1028Name of Land: Miriswathu Mookalana; Present Occupation:Crown; Name and address of the Claiment: W. B. ThilakarathnaPeris (deceased) (spouse and two Children); Extent: 2.0462Hectares.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude are hereby requested to appear personally, orby agents duly authorised in writing, before me atNiyagama Divisional Secretariat, on 06th day ofNovember, 2017 at 10.30 a.m. and further requested tonotify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before09.10.2017 the nature of their interest in the land, theparticulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the land/servitude, the amount ofcompensation and the details of computation of suchamount.

H. B. G. PRAGEETH RASIKA KUMARA,

Niyagama, Niyagama Division.
