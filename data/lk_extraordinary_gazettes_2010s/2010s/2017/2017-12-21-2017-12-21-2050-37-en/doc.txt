L.D.B. 21/78 (XIV)UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20 (4) (b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 20(4) (b) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higer Education and Highways, on being satisfied that the administration of the affairs of the PostgraduateInstitute of Archealogy have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said Institute have failed to restorenormal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Professor Prishantha Gunawardhana to be the Competent Authority with effectfrom October 25, 2017 for the purpose of exercising, performing and discharging in lieu of Director of Postgraduate Institute ofArchaelogy, any power, duty or function which has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Director under thesaid Act, or any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By-Law, Regulation or Rule made there under.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Colombo,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.19th December, 2017.
