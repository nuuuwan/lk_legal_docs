SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of listof commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in thecorresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 60 days from 01st April 2017, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) ofSection 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 2000/85 of 06.01.2017 in respect of Rice classifiable under H.S Code Nos. 1006.30.10, 1006.30.20 and1006.30.30 is rescinded with effect from April 01st, 2017.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,28th March, 2017.
