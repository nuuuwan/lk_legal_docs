KAMBURUPITIYA SAPUGODA ANNUAL ASALA MAHA PERAHARA - 2017

UNDER the Pilgrimage Ordinance (Chapter 175) published in the Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka No. 308 dated 27th June1984, it is hereby notified that annual Asala Maha Perehara of Sapugoda IlangamgodaPurana Raja Maha Vihara (Sapugoda Vidyaniketha Pirivena) of Kamburupitiya, Matara district, Southern Province forthe year 2017 will be held from 06th July 2017 to 13th July 2017.

N. H. M. W. W. N. HERATH,Divisional Secretary,Kamburupitiya.

At Divisional Secretariat of Kamburupitiya,22nd June 2017.
