THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Gayantha Karunatilake, Minister of Lands and Parliamentary

Reforms, hereby declare under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the

Land Acquisition Act, that the land described below is needed for a

public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

An Allotment of land in extent of 3.4644 Hectares depicted as

Lot 1 in Advance Tracing Plan No. TRI/TWG/2016/145 and dated

30.09.2016 prepared by Surveyor General situated in the Village of

Sollaiyadi in the No. 243 in the Uppuveli Grama Niladhari Division,

in Divisional Secreatry's Division of Town and Gravets, in

Trincomalee District in Eastern Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot No. 1 – Kumarasingamtharai and Kuthiraikundu –

Extent: 3.4644 Hectares

North: Kumarasingamtharai; East: Kumarasingamtharai Path and

Kuthiraikundu; South: Kuthiraikundu, Lot Nos. 34 and 32; West:

Road (R.D.A.), Lot Nos. 31, 28, 27, 22, 23, 26, 24, 17, 16, 18 and

Kumarasingamtharai.
