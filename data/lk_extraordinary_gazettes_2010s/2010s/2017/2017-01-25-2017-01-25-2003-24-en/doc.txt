THE COLOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCIL

IT is hereby notified that the road described in the Schedule hereunder has been named as “ Sri Wachissara NahimiMawatha” by the Honourable Chief Minister of Western Province Provincial Council and Honourable Minister in chargeof the Subject of Local Government under Section 71 (1) of the Municipal Council Ordinance No. 29 of 1947 (Chapter252) which shall read with the Section 02 of the Provincial Council’s Act (Consequential Provisions) bearing No. 12 of1989.

V. K. A. ANURA,Municipal Commissioner,Colombo Municipal Council.Municipal Council,Town Hall,Colombo 07.

Schedule

1.Name of the Local Authority-Colombo Municipal Council

2.District-Colombo

3.Present Name of the Street-The road has not been named

4.New Name of the Street-Sri Wachissara Nahimi Mawatha

5.Road Description-This road is starting from in between the Assessment No. 64and Assessment No. 68 of Sri Siddhartha Path belonging tothe Kirulapone Municipal Ward, ends from in between theAssessment No. 66/ 5 and to Assessment No. 68/ 51.
