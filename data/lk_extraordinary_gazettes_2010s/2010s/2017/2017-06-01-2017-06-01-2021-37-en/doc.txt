THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: Ma/San/03/229.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends toacquire the following lands which are required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the lands to be acquired:

Allotments of land in extent of about 0.0270 Hectare,depicted as lot Nos. 01-03 in the dated 30.01.2017 in thePreliminary Plan No. Gam. 3985 was prepared by the Surveyor-General, situated in the village of Ihala Karagahamuna, in theGrama Niladari Division Ihala Karagahamuna North (248), inthe Minor Division of Adikari Paththuwa, in the DivisionalSecretary's Division of Mahara, in the Gampaha District inWestern Province.

01. Lot No. 1

Name of Land: Millagahawatta; Description: Garden, 3Coconut trees about 10 years old; Claimant: State; OriginalClaimant: P. V. J. Perera, No. 110, Mangala Mawatha, IhalaKaragahamuna, Kadawatha; Extent: 0.0081 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 2

Name of Land: –; Description: Road; Claimant: State; OriginalClaimant: Not known; Extent: 0.0099 Hectare.

03. Lot No. 3

Name of Land: Millagahawatta; Description: Garden, 3Coconut trees about 10 years old and One Mango tree;Claimant: State; Original Claimant: P. V. S. J. Perera, No. 106,Mangala Mawatha, Ihala Karagahamuna, Kadawatha; Extent:0.0090 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land hereby requiredto appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing,before me at Divisional Secretary's Office of Mahara on the05.07.2017 at 9.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify tome in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 28.06.2017 thenature of their interests in the land, the particulars or theirclaims for compensation for the acquisition of the said landthe amount of compensation and the details of the computationof such amount.
