PROMOTION AND APPOINTMENT APPROVED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

IT is hereby notified that His Excellency the President has been pleased to promote Major General Nanediri UpendraMahesh Magiliyan Weeratunga Senanayake, RWP, RSP, USP, psc to the rank of Lieutenant General with effect fromJune 27, 2017 and to appoint him under Article 61E(a) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka as the Head of the Sri Lanka Army, with effect from that date.

By His Excellency’s Command,

AUSTIN FERNANDO,Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,09th August 2017.
