THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 136)

Notification under Section 6 (2)

I, W.D.J. Seneviratne, the Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations, acting under the provisions of Section 6 (1) of the Wages Board

Ordinance (chapter 136) read with Section 6 (2) of the said Ordinance, do hereby make order applying the provisions of part II of the saidordinance to the Trade of Marine Transport and Operational Services, set out in the Schedule hereto.

The requirement under Section 7 of the said ordinance for the publication of the intention to make such order under Section 6(1), by

notification published in the Gazette, together with a notice calling for objections, have been met with by publishing the same in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 1987/37 dated 06th October 2016.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,

Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

Ministry of Labour and Trade Union Relations,Labour Secretariat,

Colombo 05.04th May, 2017.
