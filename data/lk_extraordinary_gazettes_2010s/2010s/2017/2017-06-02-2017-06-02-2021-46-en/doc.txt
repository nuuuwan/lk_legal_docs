AMENDMENT TO PARAGRAPH (a) OF APPENDIX I OF MINUTE ON THE SRI LANKA AUDITEXAMINERS’ SERVICE

The last paragraph under Appendix I (a) of Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Examiners’ Service published in the GazetteExtraordinary No.380/61 of 20th December 1985 has been amended by the Public Service Commission and the amendedparagraph is published for information.

Amendment to Paragraph (a) of Appendix I of Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Examiners’ Service

The last paragraph under Appendix I (a) of Minute on the Sri Lanka Audit Examiners’ Service published in theGazette Extraordinary No.380/61 of 20th December 1985 has been amended by the Public Service Commission andthe amended paragraph is published for information.

“Marks for the Accounting and Auditing question papers will be 150 each. Marks for other two question papers willbe 100 each. Applicants should obtain at least 40 per cent of the total marks for each question paper to beconsidered for passing in the Efficiency Bar Examination.”
