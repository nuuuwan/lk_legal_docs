PILGRIMAGES ORDINANCE

SRI SITHIRA VELAYUTHA SWAMI TEMPLE ANNUAL FESTIVAL - 2017

DIVISIONAL SECRETARIAT OF THIRUKKOVIL DIVISION IN AMPARA DISTRICT

IT is hereby notified for the information of the pilgrims who attend the above festival and other concerned that theabove festival commences on 06.07.2017 with the schedule of execution of duties to the manes on 23.07.2017. Alsothe festivals terminate on 25.07.2017 with the "Vairavar Pooja".

The attention of the pilgrims who attend the festival and others concerned is drawn to standing regulationpublished in the Government Gazette No. 10247 dated 11th May 1951 which will be force during the above festival.

The camp area of the above festival has been enlarged as follows,

North: Mahavidyalaya Road, Thambiluvil;East: Fisheries Road, Thirukkovil;South: Beach Road, Thirukkovil;West: Main Road, Thirukkovil.

S. JAGARAJAN,Divisional Secretary.

Divisional Secretariat,Thirukkovil.
