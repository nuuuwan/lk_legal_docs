NATIONAL WATER SUPPLY AND DRAINAGE BOARD ACT, No. 02 OF 1974

Order under Section 92

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, under Section 92 of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board Act, No.02 of1974, I, Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, hereby approve the acquisition of servituderights of the land, described in the following Schedule as it is required by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board,for the construction of Access Road of Syston Service Reservoir of Kandy North Pathadumbara Water Supply Project.

RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning andWater Supply.Ministry of City Planning and Water Supply,No. 35, “Lakdiya Medura”,New Parliament Road,Pelawatta, Battaramulla,30th October, 2017.5/2/1/1316SCHEDULE

An allotment of land called Syston Estate in total extent of about 14 perches situated at village of Syston Estatein Alawathugoda Grama Niladhari Division in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Akurana in Kandy District and boundedas follows :—

North by:Remaining portion of the same land claimed by Mr. G. Welagedara ;East by:Remaining portion of the same land claimed by Mr. G. Welagedara ;South by:Remaining portion of the same land claimed by Mr. G. Welagedara and Road fromAlawathugoda to Malgammandeniya ;West by:Remaining portion of the same land claimed by Mr. G. Name and address of the claimant :Mr. H. M. G. Welagedara,Syston Bunglow,Alawathugoda.
