CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, H. P. Jayananda, Additional Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 25th September, 2017, all dutiesof Customs as well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall bepaid and received at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2037/1 of18th September, 2017 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similarnotification to be published on or before 01St October, 2017.

H. P. Jayananda,Additional Director General of Customs.
