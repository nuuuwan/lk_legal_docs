THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

IT is hereby notified that in terms of Section 4 of the Commission of Inquiry Act (Chapter 393), His Excellency MaithripalaSirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, further enlarged the time by 23 days up to 31stDecember, 2017, having enlarged the said period by 06 weeks up to 08th December, 2017 by Notification published inthe Gazette Extraordinary No. 2042/9 dated 24th October 2017, and by Notification published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 2029/15 dated 25th July, 2017 enlarged the period ending 27th April, 2017 by three months up to27th July, 2017 and thereafter by another three months up to 27th October, 2017 for rendering of the report of theCommission established under Section 2 of the said Act, on 27th January 2017 by Notification published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 2003/41 dated 27th January, 2017 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka to investigate,inquire into and report on the Issuance of Treasury Bonds during the period 01st February 2015 to 31st March, 2016 oncompletion of the aforesaid tasks.

By Order of His Excellency,

AUSTIN FERNANDO,Secretary to the President.
