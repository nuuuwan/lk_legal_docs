L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1454/37 of July 22, 2006 (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2005/13 of February 08, 2017) which specifies the Facultiesand the Departments of the University of Kelaniya, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the addition immediately afteritem “9. Department of Sport Science and Physical Education” appearing in Column II of the Schedule which correspondsto the entry “Faculty of Social Sciences” appearing in Column I of the Schedule, of the following new item :-

“10.Department of International Studies”.11.Department of Social Statistics12.Department of Political Sciences”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,12th June, 2017.
