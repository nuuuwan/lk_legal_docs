THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Order made under Section 21 of the aforesaid Act in relation to the establishing of the WayambaUniversity of Sri Lanka, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1093/8 of August 17, 1999 (as last amended by Orderpublished in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1995/25 of December 01, 2016), in the Schedule thereto, as follows :-

(1) by the substitution for the entry ‘‘Faculty of Applied Sciences’’ appearing in Column 1 of the Schedule tothe aforesaid Order, and its corresponding entries in Column 2 thereto, of the following :-

“Column 1Column 2

Faculty of Applied SciencesDepartment of Industrial Management;Dapartment of Electronics ;Dapartment of Mathematical Sciences ;Dapartment of Computing and Information Systems.’’
