Notice under Section 7

My No. : UDU/2/2/10/71.Land Ministry No. : 13/97/FR/65.

I hereby give Notice, under sub section (1) of section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No.28 of 1964, that the Government intendsto acquire the following land/ servitude for public purpose(the conservation of Dumbara mountain range.)

Description of the land servitude to be acquired:

An allotment of land called ''Koboneela Watta"bearing anextent of about 21.2508 Hectares mentioned in F. V. P. 504 Sup.9 Sheet 9 (lot number 192, 193,194,197), Supplement of thesurveyor Genaral, situated in the Grama Niladhari Division ofKobonilla, in the Divisional Secretary's Division ofUdadumbara of Kandy District, Central Province.

Name and address of Claimants

Mr. Niyomal Rajkumar,No. 272,Madawala,Katugastota.

PRINTED AT THE DEPARTMENT OF GOVERNMENT PRINTING, SRI LANKA.
