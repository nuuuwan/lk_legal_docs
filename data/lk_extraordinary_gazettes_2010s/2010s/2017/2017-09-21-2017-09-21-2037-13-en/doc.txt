NORTH WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification made by the Governor of North Western Province

AMARA Piyaseeli Rathnayaka being the Governor of the North Western Province in terms of power vested in me underSub-section (5) of Section 154(f) of Amendment Act, No. 13 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka, appointed the new cabinet of the North Western Province as follows. Accordingly I do hereby proclaim thatHon. Dasanayake Gunarathne Mudiyanselage Dharmasiri Dasanayake and Hon. Piyasiri Ramanayake were sworn in/affirmedbefore me at Colombo on this 15th September 2017 and Hon. R. P. D. Gunadasa Dehigama and Hon. W. J. Sumal Thiserawere sworn in/affirmed before me at Kurunegala on this 19th September, 2017 on the following posts in terms of Section165 and Section 157 (a) 7 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

01.Hon. Minister Dasanayake GunarathneBasnayaka MudiyanselageDharmasiri Dasanayake

02.Hon. Minister Piyasiri Ramanayake

03.Hon. Minister R. P. D. Gunadasa Dehigama

04.Hon. Minister W. J. Sumal Thisera
