SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose, in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06months commencing from 19th July 2017, subject to provisions of Sub-Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special CommodityLevy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Mangala Samaraweera,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,18th July, 2017.
