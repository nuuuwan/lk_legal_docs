UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt) Ltd, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the CompaniesAct, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 62877, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order madeunder said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1975/47 of July 14, 2016 by the addition immediately afterthe entry ‘‘Bachelor of Science Honours in Information Technology’’ appearing in Column I and it’s corresponding entryin Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and Column II respectively of the Schedule II hereto,subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt.) Ltd. shall, when admitting students for the degree programmespecified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology in Networking andMobile Computing, adhere to the minimum entry qualifications of 3 “S” passes in any subject stream in one and the samesitting at GCE Advanced Level Examination or equivalent, as per UGC circular No. 995.
