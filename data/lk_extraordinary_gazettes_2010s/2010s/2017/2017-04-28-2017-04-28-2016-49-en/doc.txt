ANNUAL PERAHARA OF BADULLA MUTHIYANGANA RAJAMAHA VIHARAYA

IN accordance with the powers vested in me by Gazette Notifications No. 9902 of 10.09.1948, No. 10283 of 17.08.1951and No. 14778 of 25.03.1955 published under Chapter 362 of the Pilgrimage Act, and under the powers conferred onDivisional Secretaries by Act, No. 58 of 1992, and according to the constituion published in No. 1710 Gazette Notificationof 10.06.2011 on Annual Perahara of Badulla Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya, I, P. S. P. Abeywardhana, the DivisionalSecretary of Badulla do hereby make the following orders in connection with the Annual Perahara of Badulla MuthiyanganaRajamaha Viharaya, 2017.

01. I do hereby notice that, the Annual Perahara of Badulla Muthiyangana shall begin with Inner Perahara on 09th ofMay 2017 and proceed along the streets on 10th and 11th of May 2017, and end after having returned to theMuthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya.

02. Route of the PeraharaInner Perahara ceremony on 9th May 2017

The Perahara shall start from Badulla Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya premises on 10 th May 2017 and proceedalong Station Road, Anagarika Dharmapala Mawatha, Lower Street, King’s Street, and downwards from LibraryMawatha and back to Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya.

The Perahara shall start from Badulla Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya premises on 11th May 2017 and proceedalong Station Road, Anagarika Dharmapala Mawatha, Cocowatte Road, Udayaraja Mawatha, King’s Street, andfrom Bazaar Street to Lower Street and back to Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya.

03. Cooking and selling articles of trade are prohibited in and around Badulla Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharayacompound.

04. Private vehicles are prohibited to park in the main roads during the Perahara Season. All types of vehicles,including bicycles and carts are prohibited to enter the Vihara area without a permit issued by me or the TrusteeChief incumbent of Muthiyangana Rajamaha Viharaya.

05. The permanent buildings and the buildings currently being constructed along the Perahara route under a formalapproval given by Badulla Municipal Council must conform to the instructions of the Health Department. At theend of the Perahara, after having dismantled the temporary sheds, the lesses shall dispose of all polythene andother garbage lying along the route of Perahara and Viharaya premises as instructed by the Public HealthInspectors.

06. Construction of any temporary hut or building in and around the sacred area shall not be done without a permitobtained from me thereto.

07. No person shall construct any temporary hut or building of any form, or effect any extensions to any existingbuilding on a street or on a land reserved for a street or in the sacred area without a permit obtained from me orfrom the Badulla Municipal Council.

08. Burning of crackers within the area is prohibited.

09. No person shall engage in selling tickets, flags or engage in any other task meant for collecting subscriptions,within the camping site indicated in clause No. 03, without the prior approval of an Authorized Officer ofDivisional Secretary.
