THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 281/7 of January 24, 1984 establishing the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka (as last amended by Order publishedin Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1995/ 26 of December 01, 2016) by the addition immediately after the entry “Facultyof Technology’’ appearing in Column I and its corresponding entries in Column II thereof, of the following :

“Column IColumn IIFacultyDepartment

Faculty of Allied Health Sciences1.Department of Nursing2.Dapartment of Pharmacy3.Department of Medical Laboratory Sciences’’.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.
