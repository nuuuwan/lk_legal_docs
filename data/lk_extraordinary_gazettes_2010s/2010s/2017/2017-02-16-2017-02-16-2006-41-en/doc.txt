SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarnanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect oflist of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified inthe corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 17th February 2017, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1981/46 of 24.08.2016 in respect of Potatoes (HS Code No. 0701.90), Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1980/37 of 19.08.2016 in respect of B’Onions (HS Code No. 0703.10.20) and Gazette Extraordinary No. 1986/55of 29.09.2016 in respect of Lentils (HS Code No. 0713.40.11, 0713.40.12, 0713.40.21 and 0713.40.22) are rescindedwith effect from 17th February 2017.

RAVI KARNANAYAKE, Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,16th February, 2017.
