THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

I, hereby give notice by virtue of Article 44(5) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka thatthe subjects and functions and departments, statutory institutes and state corporations vested in the State Minister ofFisheries and Aquatic Resources Development appointed in terms of Article 44(1) of the Constitution of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka shall be discharged in conformity with the Schedule hereto with effect from 22.02.2017.

MAHINDA AMARAWEERA,Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development.

Colombo,22nd February 2017.
