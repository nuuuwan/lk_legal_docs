CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

IT is hereby notified that in the exercise of powers vested in me in terms of paragraph (1) of Article 43 of the Constitution ofthe Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology Gazetted under theMinistry of Higher Education and Highways in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1933/13 dated September 21, 2015 whichwas amended from time to time, will be removed from the Gazette Notification relating to Subjects and Functions ofMinistries and it will be deemed as not coming under the purview of any Ministry with effect from May 16, 2017.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,28th June 2017.
