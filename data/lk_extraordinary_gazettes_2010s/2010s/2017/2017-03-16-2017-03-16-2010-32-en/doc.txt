THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Repubic of Sri Lanka upon the recommendation ofthe Minister-in-Charge of the subject of Urban Development being of the opinion that the land specified in the Schedulebelow is urgently required for the construction of the commercial complex under the Town Development Project ofMalabe, do by this order, declare that the said land is required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,13th March, 2017.

SCHEDULE

All allotments of land called Dombagahawatta depicted as Lot B1 of Plan No. 2058 made by Anil Nawagamuwa,Licensed Surveyor, in respect of land situated in Talangama North in Pallepattuwa Hewagam Korale within the area ofauthority of Kaduwela Municipal Council in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kaduwela in the District of Colomboin Western Province containing in extent of about 03 roods and 17.50 perches (0.3478 Hectares) of which boundaries areas follows :

North:Bus stand premises of Malabe ;East:Bus stand premises of Malabe and land belonging to S and G Property Developers ;South:Udawatte Road ;West:Kaduwela Divisional Secretariat Building.
