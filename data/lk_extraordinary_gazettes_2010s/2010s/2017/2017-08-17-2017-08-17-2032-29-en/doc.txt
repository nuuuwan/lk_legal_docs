LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) AMENDED

BY LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDMENT)

ACT, No. 28 OF 1964

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No.: 4/1/b/w;am;a/79.Reference No. of the Ministry of Lands:

4-3/9/2015/WS/30.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquirethe following land which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the Land to be acquired :

The allotment of land called "Kongahamulawatta" in totalextent of about 0.0334 Hectares, depicted as Lot No. 1 inPreliminary Plan No. Ku 2374, dated 06.07.2017, Surveyed andprepared by the Surveyor General, situated at the village ofTheliyagonna, in Mallawapitiya Grama Niladhari Division ofNo. 711, in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Mallawapitiyain Kurunegala District, North Western Province and boundedas follows.

Lot No. 01 – Name of the Land: Kongahamulawatta; Namesand Address of the claimants: 1. Mrs. Rishana Maharoof,No. 59, Udayar Mawatha, Theliyagonna; Extent of the Land:0.0334 Hectare.

Boundaries:

North by: Kongahamulawatta claimed by N. H. Halaldeen;East by: Ela; South by: Hijra Mawatha; West by:Kongahamulawatta claimed by Noor Sadeeka andKongahamulawatta claimed by Ummu Saleema.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized inwriting, before me at the Divisional Secretary's Office, atMallawapitiya on the 16th day of October, 2017 at 10.00 a.m.and are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (induplicate) on or before the 9th day of October, 2017, the natureof their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amountof compensation and the details of the computation of suchamount.

R. M. P. RATHNAYAKA,

Divisional Secretary,

Mallawapitiya.
