CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCILNOTICE BY THE GOVERNOR OF THE CENTRAL PROVINCE

KNOW Ye that by virtue of powers vested by the Article 154 (b): 8 (b) of the Thirteenth Amendment to the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Niluka Ekanayake, Governor of the Central Province, do by this Notice proroguethe fifth Session of the Sixth Provincial Council of the Central Province with effect from 08th December Two ThousandSeventeen.

By virtue of powers vested in me by paragrph 8(a) of the said Article, I do hereby summon the said Council to meet at theChamber of the Central Province Provincial Council at Pallekelle, Kundasale on Wednesday the 07th February, Two ThousandEighteen at 10.00 a.m.

NILUKA EKANAYAKE,Governor,Central Province.
