LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Order under Section 76A

BY virtue of the powers vested in Section 76A of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by the LocalAuthorities Elections (amended) Act, No. 22 of 2012 the Election Commission hereby directs that every reference in thatordinance to an “Election Officer” or “Assistant Election Officer” of an administrative district, shall be deemed to be areference respectively to the “Returning Officer” or “Assistant Returning Officer” of that administrative district.
