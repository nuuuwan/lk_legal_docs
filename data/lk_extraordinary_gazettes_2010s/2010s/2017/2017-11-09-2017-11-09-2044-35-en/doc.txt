EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 1001

EXCISABLE ARTICLES

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 2 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), I, Mangala Samaraweera,Minister of Finance and Mass Media, declare that the substances specified in the Schedule hereto are declared to be“liquor” for the purposes of the aforesaid Ordinance.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,09 November 2017.

SCHEDULE

1) Molasses ;2) Rice pulp used for the production of spirits by distillation ;3) Maize pulp used for the production of spirits by distillation ; and4) Fruit pulp used for the production of spirits by distillation.
