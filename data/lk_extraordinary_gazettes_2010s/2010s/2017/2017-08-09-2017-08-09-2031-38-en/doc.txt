THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 24A(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub-section (1) of Section 24A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I,Lakshman Kiriella, Minister of Higher Education and Highways, in consultation with the University Grants Commission,do by this Order -

(1) establish with effect from 01.08.2017 the Postgraduate Institute of Indigenous Medicine as an Institute forHigher Learning, for the purpose of providing, promoting and developing higher education ; and

(2) assign to such Institute the name and style of “the Postgraduate Institute of Indigenous Medicine, University ofColombo”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,03rd August 2017.
