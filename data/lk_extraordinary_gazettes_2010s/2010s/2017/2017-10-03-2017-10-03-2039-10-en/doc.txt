THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka upon the recommendation ofthe Minister-in-Charge of the subject of urban development being of the opinion that the land specified in the Schedulebelow is urgently required for science, technology and associated urban development purposes in Stage II of Mahenawatte,do by this order, declare that the said land is required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,

President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,29th September 2017.
