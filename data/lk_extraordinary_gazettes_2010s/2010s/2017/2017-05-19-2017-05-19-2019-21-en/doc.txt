SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, NO. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of list of the commodities speciﬁ ed in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates speciﬁ ed in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months eommencing from 21st May, 2017, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE, Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,19th May, 2017.
