NOTICE UNDER SECTION 3(1) OF THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)

ACT, No. 10 OF 2017

Supplementary Register of Electors - 2017

FOR THE SRI LANKAN CITIZENS WHO HAVE BEEN INTERNALLY DISPLACED AS A RESULT OFANY ACTIONS OF A TERRORIST MILITANT OR OTHER GROUP DURING THE RECENT PAST AND

WHOSE NAMES HAVE NOT BEEN REGISTERED AS ELECTORS ELSEWHERE IN THE ISLAND

THE calling for applications in respect of the preparation of the Supplementary Register on the above matter has expired on19.10.2017. Any person who is eligible under the Registration of Electors (Special Provisions) Act to be registered in thesupplementary register but whose name does not appear in the said register, may appeal in writing to the respective RegisteringOfficer on the format set out in the aforesaid Registration of Electors Act, in order to have his/her name entered or retainedas the case may be in the register. Every appeal in this regard should be made so as to reach the Registering Officer of therespective District Election Office within two weeks (02nd November 2017) from the date of this notice published in theGazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Any person whose name is included in the aforesaid supplementary register for the aforementioned electoraldistrict may object to the entry or retention of his/her own name or that of any other person in the register. Every objectionshould be made in writing in duplicate and should be substantially in Form “C” set out in the aforesaid Act and be submittedto the Registering Officer of the respective District Election Office within two weeks (02nd November 2017) from thedate of this notice published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Forms to make a claim, to enter or retain a name in the register or to submit objections to any claim to enter orretain any name in the register may be obtained from the Election Office of the respective administrative district.

By Order of the Election Commission.

H. M. T. D. HERATH,

Secretary,Election Commission.At the Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,On this 20th day of October 2017.

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 3(1) OF THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)

ACT, No. 10 OF 2017

Supplementary Register of Electors - 2017

FOR THE SRI LANKAN CITIZENS WHO HAVE BEEN INTERNALLY DISPLACED AS A RESULT OFANY ACTIONS OF A TERRORIST MILITANT OR OTHER GROUP DURING THE RECENT PAST AND

WHOSE NAMES HAVE NOT BEEN REGISTERED AS ELECTORS ELSEWHERE IN THE ISLAND

NUMBER AND NAME OF THE ELECTORAL DISTRICT : 10 - JAFFNA

IN terms of Section 3(1) of the Registration of Electors (Special Provisions) Act, No. 10 of 2017, it is hereby notified thatthe prepartion of the supplementary register for displced persons of the aforementioned electoral district has now beencompleted and it is now open for inspection by the general public during office hours at Jaffna/Killinochchi DistrictElection Office.

2. Any person who is eligible under the aforesaid Special Provisions Act to be registered in the supplementaryregister for the aforementioned electoral district but whose name does not appear in the said register, may appeal in writingto the registering officer of Jaffna/Killinochchi District Election Office on the format set out in the aforesaid Registrationof Electors Act, in order to have his/her name entered or retained as the case may be in the register. Every appeal in thisregard should be made so as to reach the registering officer of Jaffna/Killinochchi District Election Office within twoweeks from the date of this notice published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

03. Any person whose name is included in the aforesaid supplementary register for the aforementioned electoraldistrict may object to the entry or retention of his/her own name or that of any other person in the register. Every objectionshould be made in writing in duplicate and should be substatially in Form “C” set out in the aforesaid Act and be submittedto the registering officer of Jaffna/Kilinochchi District Election Office within two weeks from the date of this noticepublished in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

This notice will be exhibited at the Jaffna/Killinochchi District Election Office from 20th October 2017 to02nd November 2017 (including both days).

04. Forms to make a claim, to enter or retain a name in the register or to submit objections to any claim to enteror retain any name in the register may be obtained from the aforesaid Election Office of the administrative district ofJaffna/Kilinochchi.

T. AKILAN,Assistant Registering Officer,Electoral District No. 10 - Jaffna.At the Jaffna District Election Office,On this 20th day of October 2017.
