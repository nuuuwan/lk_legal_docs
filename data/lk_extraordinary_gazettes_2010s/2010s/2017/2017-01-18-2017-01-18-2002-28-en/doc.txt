CODE OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 2013

Order under Section 7(2)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 7(2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure (Special Provisions)Act, No. 2 of 2013, I, Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, Minister of Justice do by this Order extend for a further period not exceedingtwo years the operation of the provisions of the aforesaid Act, with effect from 2nd February, 2017.
