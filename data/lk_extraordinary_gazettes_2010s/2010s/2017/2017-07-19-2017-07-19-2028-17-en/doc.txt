ORDER UNDER SECTION 22(a) OFGRATER COLOMBO ECONOMIC COMMISSION LAW (BOARD OF INVESTMENT LAW)

No. 04 OF 1978 AS AMENDED BY ACT No. 43 OF 1980

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 22 (a) of Grater Colombo Economic Commission Law (Board of InvestmentLaw) No. 04 of 1978 as amended by Act, No. 43 of 1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka, upon the recommendation of the Minister - in - Charge of the subject of development strategies and internationaltrade hereby declare areas described in the Schedule below to be a licensed zones for the purpose of this Act.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,

President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo,17th of July 2017.
