UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification made by the Governor of Uva Province

IT is hereby notified that I, M. P. Jayasinghe, Governor of Uva Province, do hereby announce that Hon. RathnayakeMudiyanselage Kumarasiri Rathnayake has been removed from the post of Minister of Health and Indigenous Medicine,Probation and Childcare, Women’s Affairs and Social Welfare of Uva Province, with effect from the date 10th August2017 who was appointed by me as a Minister of Health and Indigenous Medicine, Probation and Childcare, Women’sAffairs and Social Welfare of Uva Province with effect from 22nd October 2015 by virtue of powers vested to me underthe Section 154F. (5) of the 13th Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

M. P. JAYASINGHE,Governor of Uva Province.
