Provincial Councils Notifications

WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification published under Section 3 of Local Authorities (Standard by-laws) Act, No. 06 of 1952 which has to

be read with Section 2 of Provincial Council (Incidental Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

IT is hereby informed that, the draft by laws of Pradeshiya Sabha made by the Chief Minister and Minister in charge ofLocal Government subject of Western Province under Section 2 of Local Authorities (Standard by Laws) Act, No. 06 of1952 which has to be read with Section 2 of Provincial Council (Incidental Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 and publishedin Extraordinary Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1976/21 dated 20th of July 2016 have beenapproved by the Provincial Council of Western Province on 17th of January 2017 in terms of the provision of Section 3 ofLocal Authorities (Standard by Laws) Act, No. 06 of 1952 which has to be read with Section 2 of Provincial Council(Incidental Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 as mentioned in the following Schedule.

ISURA DEVAPRIYA,Chief Minister of Western Province andMinister of Finance and Planning, EngineeringServices, Law and Order, Local Government andProvincial Administration, Economic Development,Power and Energy, Environmental Affairs, Water Supply

and Drainage and Tourism.Chief Ministry (Western Province),Shrawasthi Mandiraya,Colombo 07,26th April, 2017.
