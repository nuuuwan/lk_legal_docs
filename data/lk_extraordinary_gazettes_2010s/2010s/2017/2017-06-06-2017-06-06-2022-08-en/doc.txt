SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 5

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 5 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby make anorder to waive off Rs. 25 per kg. from the applied Special Commodity Levy of 10% or Rs. 75 per kg. on the importation of fish,classifiable under the HS Headings of 03.02 and 03.03, listed out in the Schedule of the Order published in the ExtraordinayGazette Notification No. 2018/2 of 08th May 2017.

In terms of this Order, a Special Commodity Levy of 10% or Rs. 50 per kg. on importation of fish shall becollected for a period of 30 days effective from 07th June 2017.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,05th June, 2017.
