REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mrs. Mallika Arachchige Sumanawathi who resided in the village of Homagama, in the Divi-sional Secretary’s Division of Homagama, in the District of Colombo.
