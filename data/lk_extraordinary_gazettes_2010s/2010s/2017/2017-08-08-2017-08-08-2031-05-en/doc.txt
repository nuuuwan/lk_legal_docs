STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether injustice had been caused to Mr. C. J. Ranjith who was an Employee of the Bank of Ceylon,

01. By being terminated from the said bank on 08.09.1987 on the ground of vacation of post,02. Being reinstated in his post on 19.06.2003 unconditionally but disputed by the said Bank, and03. Non granting of arrears of salary, Promotions and other dues upon reinstatement from 08.09.1987 to 19.06.2003

and if so, to what reliefs he is entitled.
