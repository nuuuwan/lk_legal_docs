THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978 ENACTED BY THE NATIONALSTATE ASSEMBLY

Order under Section 15(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 15(1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978enacted by the National State Assembly I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Developmentdo hereby this Order vest with the Urban Development Authority with effect from today the lands morefully described inthe Schedule hereto being lands vested in the Municipal Council of Dehiwala Mount Lavinia.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA (M.P.),Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th and 18th Floor,“Suhurupaya”,Battaramulla,12th June 2017.
