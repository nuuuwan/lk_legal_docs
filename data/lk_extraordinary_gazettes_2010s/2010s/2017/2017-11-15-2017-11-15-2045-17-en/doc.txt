L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1603/35 of May 28, 2009 (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2028/34 of July 19, 2017) which specifies the Facultiesand the Departments of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka, which is deemed, by virtue of the provisoins ofSection 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act as follows :

(1) by the addition immediately after the entry “12. Department of Anthropology” appearing in Column II of theSchedule which corresponds to the entry “Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences” appearing in Column I ofthe Schedule, of the following entry :-

“13. Department of Philosophy and Psychology” ;

(2) by the addition immediately after the entry “9. Department of Statistics” appearing in Column II of the Schedulewhich corresponds to the entry “Faculty of Applied Sciences” appearing in Column I of the Schedule, of thefollowing entry :-

“10. Department of Sports Science”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,10th November 2017.
