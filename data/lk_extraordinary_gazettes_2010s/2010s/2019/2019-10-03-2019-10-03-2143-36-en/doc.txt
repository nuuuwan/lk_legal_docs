REGULATIONS made by the Minister under Section 20 read with Subsection (3) of Section 4 and Section 14 of theImports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 as amended by Act, No.48 of 1985 and No.28 of 1987.

MALIK SAMARAWICKRAMA,Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade.Colombo.

Regulations

01. These Regulations may be cited as the Imports and Exports Control Regulation No. 02 of 2019.
