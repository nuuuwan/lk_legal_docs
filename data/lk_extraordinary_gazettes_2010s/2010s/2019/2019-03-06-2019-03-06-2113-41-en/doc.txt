FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PORATIVPATTUPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Porativpattu Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofresignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent group 2, to which V. Ayushman the Member belonged, wasrequired by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if Independentgroup 2 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateN. Indranathan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Porativpattu Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that N. Indranathanwas selected as the member of the Porativpattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Porativpattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Batticaloa,06th March, 2019.
