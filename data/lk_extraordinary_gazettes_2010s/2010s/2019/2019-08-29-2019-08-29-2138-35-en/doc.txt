THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of1980, I, do by this Order declare that the term : Municipal Council where the allotment of land described in the ScheduleII of the Notification No. 2046/11 dated 21st November 2017 which is urgently required for construction of flats forresettlement of underserved settlers on the recommendation of the Minister in charge of the subject of Megapolis andWestern Development is situated, should be amended to read “within the Municipal Council Limits of Moratuwa ” inplace of “within the Municipal Council Limits of Dehiwela - Mount Lavinia”.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,August 24, 2019.
