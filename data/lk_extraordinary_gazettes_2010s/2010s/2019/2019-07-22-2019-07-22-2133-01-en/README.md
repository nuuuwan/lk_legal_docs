WHEREAS, I am of the opinion that by the reason of a public emergency in Sri Lanka, it is expedient, so to do, in the interestof public security, the preservation of public order and the maintenance of supplies and services essential to the life of thecommunity;

Know ye that, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President, by virtue of the powers vested in me by section 2 of the PublicSecurity Ordinance (Chapter 40) as amended by Act, No. 8 of 1959, Law No. 6 of 1978 and Act, No. 28 of 1988, do by thisProclamation declare that the provisions of Part II of that Ordinance, shall come into operation throughout Sri Lanka on andafter July 22, Two Thousand and Nineteen.

Declared at Colombo on the July, 22, Two Thousand and Nineteen.

By His Excellency’s Command,

UDAYA R. SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.
