FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE KAMBURUPITIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUHORITIES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT)ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWeerasuriya Mudiyanselage Sanjeewa Priyanath the former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66 B(2) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty who shall be declared selected as Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid Political Party has communicated his decision to nominateWeerasinghe Kankanamage Ananda Weerasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the KamburupitiyaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) asamended by Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Weerasinghe KankanamageAnanda Weerasinghe elected as Chairman of the Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Matara,02nd October, 2019.

FILLING OF A VACANCY THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE KAMBURUPITIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUHORITIES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT)ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Appointmentas Chairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWeerasinghe Kankanamge Ananda Weerasinghe the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section66 B (2) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party who shall be declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Lalith Alwis Samarasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of Kamburupitiya PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended byLocal Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017, do hereby declare Lalith Alwis Samarasinghe elected asVice Chairman of the Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Matara,02nd October, 2019.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KAMBURUPITIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWeerasuriya Mudiyanselage Sanjeewa Priyanath the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) ofthe aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 1 - Gathara of Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongsto such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Rejee Namel Wellappili to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the KamburupitiyaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Rejee Namel Wellappili was elected as the member of the 1- Gathara Kamburupitiya PradeshiyaSabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Matara,02nd October, 2019.
