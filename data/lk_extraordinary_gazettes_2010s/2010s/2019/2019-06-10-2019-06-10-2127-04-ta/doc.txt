FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KORALAI PATTUPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a elected member of Koralai Pattu pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Thamil Makkal Viduthalai Pulikal, the recognized political party to whichNagalingam Thiraviyam the member elected belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward 06-Kalkudha of Koralai Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Ponnaiyah Pushpenthiran to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Koralai Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that PonnaiyahPushpenthiran was elected as the member of the Ward 06-Kalkudha of Koralai Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. Saseelan,Returning Officer,Koralai Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Batticaloa,07th June, 2019.
