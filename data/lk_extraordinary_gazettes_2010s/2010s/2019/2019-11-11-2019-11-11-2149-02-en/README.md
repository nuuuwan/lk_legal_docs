NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 16 SUB-SECTIONS (2D) OF THE COAST CONSERVATION ANDCOASTAL RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT, No. 57 OF 1981

Proposed Rehabilitation of Port of KankasanthuraiJaffna District

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT REPORT

IT is hereby notified that the Report of Environmental Impact Assessment study on proposed Rehabilitation of Port ofKankasanthurai in Jaffan District submitted by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority, No. 19, Chaithya Road, Colombo 01 asrequired by the Section 16(1) of the Coast Conversation and Coastal Resource Managment Act, No. 57 of 1981 asamended by the Act, No. 64 of 1988 and No. 49 of 2011 will be available for inspection by the public at the followinginstitutions from 8.30 a. m. to 4.15 p. m. for a period of 30 days from 11.11.2019 (Except weekends and public holidays)

In addition, this report with 03 languages can be used in the official wedsite (www.coastal.gov.lk) of theDepartment of Coast Conservation & Coastal Resource Management.
