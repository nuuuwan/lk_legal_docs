FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Chamidu Lakshan Ranasinghe, the memberappointed to Ward No. 02, Mirihana North of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 02, Mirihana North of MaharagamaUrban Council who belongs to such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified undersection 8 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateWanni Arachchige Don Amarapala to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama UrbanCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Wanni Arachchige Don Amarapala was elected as the member of the Ward No. 02 Mirihana North of MaharagamaUrban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.District Election Office,Colombo,15th March, 2019.
