SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MahindaRajapaksa, Minister of Finance, Economic and Policy Development of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,do by this order impose in respect of the list of commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a SpecialCommodity Levy, at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 31 days commencing from December 14, 2019, subject to provisions ofSub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Finance, Economicand Policy Development.
