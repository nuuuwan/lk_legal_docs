THE INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT, CHAPTER 131 OF THE LEGISLATIVE ENACTMENTS OF CEYLON( 1956 REVISED EDITION)

Revocation of Order under Section 4(1)

WHEREAS by Order made under Section 4 (1) of the Industrial Disputes Act Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments of Ceylon (RevisedEdition 1956) as amended by Acts, No. 14 and 62 of 1957, 14 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 read with Industrial Disputes (Special Provisions) ActNo. 37 of 1968 dated 02.09.2016 and published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ExtraordinaryNo. 1983/38 dated 09.09.2016 the Industrial Dispute in respect of the matter specified in the statement of the Commissioner of Labourdated 18.07.2016.

Mr. D.H. Pathirane, No. 207, Valley Road, Digana Nilagama, Digana.

and

Taprobane Studios Ranch (Private) Ltd, No. 50, Raja Mawatha, Ethul Kotte Road, Nugegoda.

was referred to Mr. S. Virithamulla for settlement by arbitration.

AND whereas it is now deemed expedient that the said Order be revoked, I, Ravindra Samaraweera, Minister of Labour and Trade UnionRelations do hereby revoke the said Order and further make Order that no proceedings be taken upon the said Order dated 02.09.2016.

RAVINDRA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.Colombo,28th February,2019.

STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is

Whether injustice has been caused to Mr. D.H. Pathirana who had worked as a Director of Taprobane Studios Ranch (Private) Ltd by thenon-payment of salaries and allowances from October 2007 to September 2009 and termination of employment by closing down the officehe had worked in and if such injustice has been caused to what reliefs he is entitled.
