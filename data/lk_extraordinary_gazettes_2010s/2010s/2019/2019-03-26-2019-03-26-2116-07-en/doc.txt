NOTIFICATION UNDER THE CUSTOMS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 235)

NOTIFICATION under Sub-section (1) and (2) of the Section 22A of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235) as amendedby Act, No. 83 of 1988 made by the Minister of Finance is hereby published for general information.

R. H. S. SAMARATUNGA,Secretary,Ministry of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,26th March, 2019.

Notification under Sub-sections (1) and (2) of the Section 22A of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

By virtue of the powers vested in me under Sub-sections (1) and (2) of the Section 22A of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter235) as amended by Act, No. 83 of 1988, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance of Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka, do by this Notification amend the Rule No. 4 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1053/11 of 11.11.1998by insertion of the following new section immediately after the Section 4 of the Gazette.

4.1 The importation of Black Pepper, Areca-nut, Nutmeg/Mace, Tamarind and Cinnamon shall not be permitted underthis Scheme.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,26th March, 2019.
