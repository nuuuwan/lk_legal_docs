FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which BamunuAarachchige Ranil Chithrananda the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Hewapalatuwage Rohana Samantha Weerasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchilage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Colombo MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Hewapalatuwage Rohana Samantha Weerasinghe was selected as the member of the Colombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.District Election Office,Colombo City,13th November, 2019.
