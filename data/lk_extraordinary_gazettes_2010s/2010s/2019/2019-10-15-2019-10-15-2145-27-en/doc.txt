FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATUWANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which MunasingeSirisena the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Hewa Kuda Anthonige Ananda Kulasundara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Ravindra Subhashana Wedage, Returning Officer of the Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatHewa Kuda Anthonige Ananda Kulasundara was selected as the member of the Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha.

RAVINDRA SUBHASHANA WEDAGE,Returning Officer,Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Hambantota,14th October, 2019.
