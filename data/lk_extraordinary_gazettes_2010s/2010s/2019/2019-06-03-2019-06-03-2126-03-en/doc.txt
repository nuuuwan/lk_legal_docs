FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE CHAVAKACHCHERI PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSivakunasothy Vallipuram the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateKanthaiya Thurkka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that KanthaiyaThurkka was selected as the member of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,31st May, 2019.
