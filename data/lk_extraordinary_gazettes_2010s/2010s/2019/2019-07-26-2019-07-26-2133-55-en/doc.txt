FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NATTANDIYA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Nattandiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending forCouncil meetings period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which WarnakulaAdiththa Arshaneela Iththa Thimasha Subhodini Loach the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if Nattandiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selectedas a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political Party has communicated his decision to nominatePoruthotage Elanrose Fernando to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Nagalingam Regan , Returning Officer of the Nattandiya Pradeshiya Sabha , acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that PoruthotageElanrose Fernando was selected as the member of the Nattandiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Nattandiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Puttalam,25th July, 2019.
