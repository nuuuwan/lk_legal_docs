FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attendingthree consecutive meeting without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilkankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSomalingam Jeevananthan the Member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that ward 04-Kuppilan if Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party andis not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Rasaraththinam Ushanth to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Rasaraththinam Ushanth was elected as the member of the Ward 04 - Kuppilan of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,22nd March, 2019.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilkankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichArulnathan Kirusnaveni the Member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Kanthasami Sothimalar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Kanthasami Sothimalar was selected as the member of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.
