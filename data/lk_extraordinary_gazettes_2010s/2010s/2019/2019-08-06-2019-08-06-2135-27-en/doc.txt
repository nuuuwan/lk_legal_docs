NOTICE OF APPROVAL OF THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN FOR THE NUWARAELIYA URBANDEVELOPMENT AREA COMPRISING OF NUWARAELIYA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL ANDNUWARAELIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA (PART) AREAS

NOTICE is given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8G of the UrbanDevelopment Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended from time to time that I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Ministerin charge of the subject of Megapolis & Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8F ofthe said Law, have approved the Development Plan for Nuwaraeliya Urban Development Area comprising of NuwaraeliyaMunicipal Council and Nuwaraeliya Pradeshiya Sabha (Part) Areas on the 01st day of August, 2019.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th and 18th Floors,“Suhurupaya”,Subhuthipura Road,Battaramulla.01st August, 2019.

APPROVAL OF THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN FOR THE NUWARAELIYA URBAN DEVELOPMENTAREA COMPRISING OF NUWARAELIYA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL AND NUWARAELIYA PRADESHIYASABHA (PART) AREAS

PUBLIC are hereby informed that the Development Plan prepared under Section 8A of the Urban Development AuthorityLaw, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 4 of 1982, for the Nuwaraeliya Urban Development area comprising ofNuwaraeliya Municipal Council and Nuwaraeliya Pradeshiya Sahba (Part) areas has been approved on 01st August2019, by Hon.
