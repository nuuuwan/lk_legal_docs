FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHAWA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Andarawad Patabedige Abeyrathna Gunawardhanathe member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember if Independent Group who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateAthapaththu Konara Mudiyanselage Sujeewa Sampath Athapaththu to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha , actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AthapaththuKonara Mudiyanselage Sujeewa Sampath Athapaththu was selected as the member of the Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha .

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Mahawa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,12th July, 2019.
