THE NOTICE TO RECTIFY THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALISTREPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA BEARING No. 2129/95 DATED 28TH JUNE, 2019

I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me underthe Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended, do hereby rectify the Extraordinary Gazette Notificationbearing No. 2129/95 dated 28th June, 2019 (07-4554/10).

In the caption of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification the words “MUNICIPAL COUNCIL AREA”Shall be substituted with “URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREA COMPRISING OF THE POLONNARUWA MUNICIPALCOUNCIL AND POLONNARUWA PRADESHIYA SABHA (PART) AREAS.”

The content of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by establishing therelevance of the said Development Paln to, POLONNARUWA URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREA COMPRISING OF THEPOLONNARUWA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL AND POLONNARUWA PRADESHIYA SABHA (PART) AREAS.

The word “had” in the fourth line of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be substituted with “have”.

The rectifications introduced to the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be read as follows;
