SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose, in respectof the list of commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rate specified inthe corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from May 22, 2019, subject to provisions ofSub-Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2112/48 of 02.03.2019 in respect of B’ Onions is rescinded with effect fromMay 22, 2019.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,21st May, 2019.
