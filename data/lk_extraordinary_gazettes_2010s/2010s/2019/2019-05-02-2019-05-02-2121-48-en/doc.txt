EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KARACHCHI PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Karachchi pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignations ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to which IyampillaiAshokkumar the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturned member of Sri Lanka Freedom Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Velayutham Gajan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of the Karachchi pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that VelayuthamGajan was selected as the member of the Karachchi pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Karachchi pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kilinochchi,30th April, 2019.
