TRANSFER OF POWERS (DIVISIONAL SECRETARIATS) ACT, No. 58 OF 1992

Order under Section 2

ESTABLISHMENT OF DIVISIONAL SECRETARY’S DIVISIONS IN NUWARAELIYA, GALLE ANDRATHNAPURA DISTRICTS

I, Wajira Abeywardana, Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government hereby establish10 new Divisional Secretary’s Divisions in Nuwaraeliya Administrative District, revising the 05 existing DivisionalSecretary’s Divisions, 02 new Divisional Secretary’s Divisions revising Divisional Secretary’s Division Baddegama, and03 new Divisional Secretary’s Division revising Divisional Secretary Division Hikkaduwa in Galle District, 02 new DivisionalSecretary’s Divisions in Rathnapura District revising Divisional Secretary’s Division Balangoda, on the power vested onme by Section 2 of the Transfer of Powers (Divisional Secretaries) Act, No. 58 of 1992. Further the names of DivisionalSecretary’s Division are mentioned in Column I of the Schedule and each Divisionial Secretary’s Division is comprised ofGrama Niladhari Divisions indicated in Column II. The boundaries of the Divisional Secretary’s Divisions are mentionedagainst each name in Column III and the date of implementing the provisions of Section 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th of the Actfor each Divisional Secretary’s Division are indicated in Column IV.

WAJIRA ABEYWARDANA (M. P.),Minister of Internal & Home Affairs andProvincial Councils & Local Government.

Ministry of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils,& Local Government,“Nila Medura”,Narahenpita,Colombo 05,23rd Ocrober, 2019.
