NOTICE OF APPROVAL OF THE AMENDMENT OF PLANNING & BUILDING REGULATIONS2008-2020 FOR SRI JAYEWARDENEPURA KOTTE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL PREPARED UNDER THEURBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978

NOTICE is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8G of theUrban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 as amended from time to time that I, Patali Champika Ranawaka,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8F of the UrbanDevelopment Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, have approved the proposed amendment to be included to the Planning &Building Regulations 2008 -2020 for Sri Jayawardenapura Kotte Municipal Council Area.

I, hereby approve the amendment applicable to Section 37 (1) of the Planning & Building Regulations for SriJayawardenapura Kotte Municipal Council by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8H of the Urban DevelopmentAuthority Law No. 41 of 1978.

01 Section 37 (1) of the Planning & Building Regulations for Sri JayawardenapuraKotteMunicipal Council Area is hereby further amended by the insertion of clause “f” as follows;

“ The number of floors for a given development is decided on the width of the roadgiven under the guidelines in Schedule 6 - Form C in Planning & Building Regulationfor Sri Jayawardenapura Kotte Municipal Council Area therein the corresponding accessroad can have a minimum road width of 12 meters (40 feet),or two or more alternativeand separate access roads, each of which can have a minimum width of 6 meters, subjectto such discretion vested in the Authority to grant necessary approval.”

Amendment ofPlanning &BuildingRegulations2008-2020 for SriJayewardenepuraKotte MunicipalCouncil Area.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKAMinister of Megapolis and Western Development

14th July, 2017

Note: This regulation herein shall henceforth be operative and enforceable from 14th July, 2017.
