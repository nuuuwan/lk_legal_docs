URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, I, PataliChampika Ranawaka, Minister of Magapolis & Western Development being of opinion that the area specified in ColumnI of Schedule II hereto and having the meet’s and bounds specified in corresponding entry in Column II of Schedule IIexcept the area already declared as Urban Development Area and set out in Schedule I hereto are suitable for developmentand do hereby this Order declare such areas for the purpose of the said Section.

I do hereby declare that, the already declared area set out in Schedule I together with the other areas declared asaforesaid, shall be included within the limits of the expanded Ella Urban Development Area set out in Column II underSchedule II hereto.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA (M. P.),Minister of Megapolis & Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis & Western Development,17th & 18th Floors, “Suhurupaya”,Sri Subhuthipura Road,Battaramulla,21st April, 2019.
