SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

2019

Third Supplementary Financial Statement

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OFPROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT, No. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SIXTH SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIALCOUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26 TH SEPTEMBER, 2017

2019

1.This permission may be cited as ''Permission to disburse under third financial statement - 2019 of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council'' and come in to operation from 09.10.2019

2.The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Four BillionTwenty Three Million, Four Hundred and Six Thousand One Hundred and Ninety Seven and Cents TwentyFour only (Rs. 34,023,406,197.24) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January 2019 andending on 31st December, 2019, which is specified as ”Financial Year 2019” in the Budget Estimate - 2019,that was approved on 10.04.2019 and amended by First and Second Supplementary Financial Statement -2019, is hereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Four Billion Fifty Seven Million Eight Hundred and Six ThousandOne Hundred Ninety Seven and Cents Twenty Four Only (Rs. 34,057,806,197.24) and grant permission tothe Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same from theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

3.Accordingly the first Schedule of the said Gazette Notification is amended as the first schedule hereof andgrant permission to disburse the same.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilto disburse as per the Third Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2019, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 27(a) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as term of the 6th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Four Billion TwentyThree Million, Four Hundred and Six Thousand One Hundred and Ninety Seven and Cents Twenty Four only(Rs. 34,023,406,197.24) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2019 and ending 31st December,2019, which is specified in annual financial statement - 2019 and amended by first supplementary estaimate - 2019 ishereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Four Billion Fifty Seven Million Eight Hundred and Six Thousand One Hundred andNinety Seven and Cents Twenty Four Only (Rs. 34,057,806,197.24) and grant permission to disburse the same from theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2019 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date ofsummoning the 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil which occurs first.

DHAMMA DISSANAYAKE,Hon. Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province.

09th October , 2019.
