SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Regulations under Sections 32 and 33

I, Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports in pursuance of powers vested inme under Sections 32(b) and 33 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973, do hereby suspend the registration of the Sri LankaNetball Federation, the National Association for netball with immediate effect and appoint the following interim committeeto continue the activities of the above Netball Federation, as a provisional arrangement.

1.Mitimanage Yamuna Perera-President2.Ranjith Wimalasooriya-Secretary3.Edirisinghe Dewage Asha Lakmali Edirisinghe-Treasurer

Moreover, the Director General of the Department of Sports Development is delegated with power, in terms ofthe Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and amended regulations thereto, for convening a special general meeting and thereafterappoint an Election Committee and conduct the election of Sri Lanka Netball Federation, under the supervision of theDirector General.

HARIN FERNANDO (M. P.),Minister of Telecommunication, ForeignEmployment and Sports.03rd April, 2019.
