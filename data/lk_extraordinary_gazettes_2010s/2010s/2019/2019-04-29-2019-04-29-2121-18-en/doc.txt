SRI LANKA ELECTRICITY ACT, No. 20 OF 2009

Order under Sub section (1) of the Section 46

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested under Sub section (1) of Section 46 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009(as amended), the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka do hereby impose an annual levy in such amounts as set out inColumn II of the Schedule hereto, to be recovered from those licensees holding such licenses as specified in the correspondingentry in Column I of that schedule. The previous Gazette Notification published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1896/10 on Tuesday 06th January, 2015 is hereby revoked.

Chairman,Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka.Level 06,Bank of Ceylon Merchant Tower,No. 28, St. Michael’s Road,Colombo 03,29th April, 2019.
