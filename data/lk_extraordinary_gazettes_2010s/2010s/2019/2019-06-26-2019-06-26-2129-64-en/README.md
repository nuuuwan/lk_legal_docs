FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ARANAYAKA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Aranayaka pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be amember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichAmarasingha Arachchilage Ishara Madushani Amarasingha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 7 - Debathgama of Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha whobelongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Ethugal Pedige Wimalawathi to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Aranayaka pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that EthugalPedige Wimalawathi was elected as the member of the 7 - Debathgama of Aranayaka pradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Aranayaka pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kegalle,25th June, 2019.
