Government Notifications

L.D.B 21/78(xiv)

UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20(4)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20(4)(b) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on being satisfied that the administration of theaffairs of the Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities ofthe said Institute have failed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Major General (retired) ShanthaKumar Thirunavukarasu to be the Competent Authority with effect from August 15, 2019 for the purpose of exercising,performing and discharging in lieu of the Director, Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa, any power, duty orfunction respectively which has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Director under the said Act, orunder any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By-law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,23rd August, 2019.
