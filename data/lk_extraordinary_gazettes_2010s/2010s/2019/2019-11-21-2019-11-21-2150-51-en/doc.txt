MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA

AMENDMENT

KARIYAKSHAMA SEVA VIBHUSHANAYA

Orders

REGULATIONS mentioned in the following paragraphs, numbered as Fourthly, Seventhly, Eighthly, Ninthly, Eleventhly,Twelfthly, Thirteenthly, Fourteenthly and Fifteenthly : published in the Government Gazette Extraordinary Number412/20 dated 01.08.1986, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka are hereby amended by His Excellency thePresident :

Fourth Paragraph:It is ordained that the period of service requisite to qualify for the Decoration shall be anaggregate of twenty five years Efficient Commissioned Service on the Active Strength of theSri Lanka Army Volunteer Force, and the ranks shall be Brigadier and above, subject to suchconditions as may be laid down in the regulations hereinafter mentioned, provided that theperiod of service so tendered previously to reward any other medal for Efficient Service in theSri Lanka Army Volunteer Force, may be reckoned to reward the Kariyakshama SevaVibhushanaya.

Seventh Paragraph:It is ordained that service shall be reckoned as qualifying service for the Decoration if suchservice has already been reckoned for the Efficiency Medal, or, the Kariyakshama SevaPadakkama.

Eigth Paragraph:It is ordained that Officers who have the necessary qualifying service shall not be awarded theDecoration unless they are recommended by the Commandant of the Sri Lanka Army VolunteerForce in which the period of service requisite to qualify for the Decoration is completed ; and,finally approved by the Commander of the Sri Lanka Army, as efficient and thoroughly capableOfficers deserving in every way for the Decoration.

Ninth Paragraph:It is ordained that a recipient of this medal shall not be eligible for a second conferment orconferment of clasp during his lifetime.

Eleventh Paragraph:It is ordained that the reproductions of the Decoration in miniature, which may be worn oncertain occasions by those upon whom the Decoration is conferred, shall be half the size of theKariyakshama Seva Vibhushanaya.

Twelfth Paragraph:It is ordained that sealed patterns of the Kariyakshama Seva Vibhushana, and its miniatures, areheld by the appropriate authority at the Sri Lanka Army Headquarters.

Thirteenth Paragraph:It is ordained that it shall be competent for me, my elected successors, or by and order undermy Seal and Signature, or on a recommendation to that effect by, or, through the SecretaryDefence to cancel, and annul the award of the Decoration in respect of any person ; and thatthereupon his name is struck off the register where such awards are recorded ; but it shall becompetent for me or one of my elected successors to restore the Decoration when suchrecommendation has been withdrawn.

Fourteenth Paragraph :It is ordained that the Decoration shall be conferred under such regulation as to grant forfeiture,restoration, and other matters in amplification of this my Proclamation as may with my approvalsignified through the Secretary Defence be issued from time to time by the Commander of theSri Lanka Army.

Fifteenth Paragraph:I reserve to myself and my elected Successors the full power of annulling, abrogating,augmenting, interpreting or dispensing with these rules and regulations, or any part thereof bya Notification under my Seal and Signature, or under my Command.

By His Excellency’s Command,

UDAYA R. SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.

Office of Secretary to the President,Colombo,11th November, 2019.

AMENDMENT

KARIYAKSHAMA SEVA PADAKKAMA

Orders

REGULATIONS mentioned in the following paragraphs, numbered as Fourthly, Fifthly, Sixthly, Seventhly, Eighthly, Ninthly,Tenthly, Eleventhly, Twelfthly and Thirteenthly published in the Government Gazette Extraordinary Number 412/20dated 01.08.1986, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka pertaining to Kariyakshama Seva Padakkama arehereby amended.

Whereas I am desirous of rewarding long, efficient loyal service, and exemplary conduct of proven capacity byCommissioned Officers, Warrant Officers, Non-Commissioned Officers and Men of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force.

Fourth Paragraph:It is ordained that the Medal shall be awarded to Commissioned Officers, Warrant Officers,Non-Commissioned Officers and Men of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force who havecompleted an aggregate of eighteen years of Efficient Service on the Active list of the SriLanka Army Volunteer Force subject to such conditions as may be laid down in the regulationshereinafter mentioned and the medal shall be awarded to those who do not receive any othermedal for the efficient service on active list of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force for theperiod of service concerned and such service is not counted to award any other medal.

Fifth Paragraph:It is ordained that the award of the Medal to such Commissioned Officer, Warrant Officers,Non-Commissioned Officers and Men of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force should berecommended by the Commanding Officer of the Unit in which the period of service requisiteto qualify for the Medal is completed ; and such recommendation is certified by the Colonel ofthe Regiment and the Commandant of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force and finally approvedby Commander of Sri Lanka Army as efficient and thoroughly capable Commissioned Officers,Warrant Officers, Non-Commissioned Officers or Men deserving in every way to award theMedal.

Sixth Paragraph:It is ordained that Commissioned Officers, Warrant Officers, Non-Commissioned Officersand Men on the Reserve of the Sri Lanka Army Volunteer Force are entitled to claim for thisMedal, if at, the time they were placed on the Reserve, they had such qualifying service requisitefor the Medal.

Seventh Paragraph:It is ordained that a recipient of this medal shall not be eligible for a second conferment orconferment of clasp during his lifetime.

Eighth Paragraph:It is ordained that the Eighth regulation ordained previously to be null.

Ninthly Paragraph:It is ordained that sealed patterns of the Kariyakshama Seva Padakkama in miniature, which maybe worn on certain occasions by Commissioned Officers and Warrant Officers on whom theMedal is conferred, shall be half the size of the Kariyakshama Seva Padakkama.

BY HIS EXCELLENCY MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA, PRESIDENT OF THE DEMOCRATICSOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
