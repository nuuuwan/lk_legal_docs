01) This Statute may be cited as the Statute (Amendment) of Uva Provincial Road of No. ............. of2019 of the Uva Provincial Council and unless stated otherwise, it shall come into operation forall functions, with effect from the decided or approved date by the Governor of the Uva Province.

02) Section 3 of the Statute of Uva Provincial Road No. 02 of 2010 (hereinafter referred as “themain Statute”) shall be replaced with following new subsection and, it shall come into operationas subsection 3 of the main Statute.

3.(1) A certain road, in accordance with the powers vested by subsection 6 of Section 6 can bedeclared as belonging to the Uva Provincial Council by the Minister in charge of UvaProvincial Road subject (hereinafter referred to as “the Minister”) through orders publishedin the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. Every such road shouldbe graded as a road of “C”, “D” or “R”class, also all the roads known as “E” in the mainStatute should be hereinafter referred as “R” class roads.

(2) Criteria for grading provincial roads can be enacted by the Minister by means of orders.

03) (a), (b) and (c) subsections of subsection (1) of section 26 of the main Statute is replaced withthe following new subsection and, it shall be enforced as (a) and (b) under 26(1) of the mainStatute.

1. By replacing (a), (b) and (c) paragraph of subsection (1) of that section with followingsubsection :-

(1) (a) in respect of a road declared by the Minister of Road as a provincial road, thelimit of constructing buildings in accordance with the distance decided by theProvincial Road Director, towards both sides from the the road center line ofthat road and the weight limit of the vehicle with total weight loaded, includingmaterials, people and animals should be published in the Gazette by theProvincial Road Director.

(b) Limits ordered by the paragraph (a) above, can be ordered in respect of a certainroad or even separately on a certain part of it or parts.

2. Subsection 26(4) of the main Statute is replaced with the following new subsection and, itshall come into operation as subsection 26(4) of the main Statute.

(4) The limits ordered by the above subsection (1) can be amended by the ProvincialRoad Director after having considered the recommendation received by a committeeappointed by the Provincial Road Director including two chief engineers of the UvaProvincial Department of Road Development and, the aforesaid amendment can beamended by an announcement published in the Gazette.
