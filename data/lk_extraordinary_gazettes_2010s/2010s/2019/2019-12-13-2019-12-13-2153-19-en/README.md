THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20 (4)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 20(4) (b) of the Universities Act, No. 6 of 1978, I , Bandula Gunawardana,Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, do by this Order -

(a)On being satisfied that the administration of the affairs of the University of the Visual and Performing Arts, SriLanka have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said University have failed to restorenormal conditions, appoint Emeritus Professor Weerakoon Mudiyanselage Abeyrathna Bandara to be theCompetent Authority with effect from December, 13, 2019 for the purpose of exercising, performing anddischarging in lieu of the Vice-Chancellor, University of the Visual and Performing Arts, Sri Lanka, any power,duty or function respectively which has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Vice-Chancellorunder the said Act, or under any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By-law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder ; and

(b)rescind the Order made under Section 20(4) (b) and published in the Governmet Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2142/25 of September 24, 2019.

BANDULA GUNAWARDANA,Minister of Higher Education, Technology and InnovationColombo,13th December, 2019.
