EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM WESTPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to whichThiruganasampanthan Subaruban the Member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member if Sri Lanka Freedom Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thavarasa Satheswaran to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatThavarasa Satheswaran was selected as the member of the Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Jaffna,25th March, 2019.
