FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BUTTALA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Not attending threeconsecutive meetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichHerathmudiyanselage Chinthaka Sampath Bandara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) ofthe aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that No. 01 Pelwaththa of Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongsto such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Indrasena Liyana Arachchi to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Indika Gayan Pathirana, Returning Officer of the Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that IndrasenaLiyana Arachchi was elected as the member of the No. 01 Pelwaththa of Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA GAYAN PATHIRANA,Returning Officer,Buttala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Moneragala,23rd December, 2019.
