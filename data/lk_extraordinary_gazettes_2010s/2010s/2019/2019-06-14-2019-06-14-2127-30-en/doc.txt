FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KALMUNAI MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kalmunai Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front of Good Governance, the recognized political party to whichAbdul Gafoor Mohammathu Natheer the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member if National Front of Good Governance who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateThajutheen Mohammathu Mufaris to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Kalmunai Municipal Council,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ThajutheenMohammathu Mufaris was selected as the member of the Kalmunai Municipal Council.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Kalmunai Municipal Council.District Election Office,Ampara,13th June, 2019.
