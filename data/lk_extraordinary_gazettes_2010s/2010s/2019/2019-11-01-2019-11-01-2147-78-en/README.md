EASTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Provincial Councils Notification of Eastern province

CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES LAW No. 5 OF 1972 AS AMENDED BY CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 32 OF 1983 AND THE CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 11 OF1992 READ IN CONJUNCTION WITH PROVINCIAL COUNCIL (CONSEQUENTIAL PROVISIONS)ACT No. 12 OF 1989

CONFERMENT OF POWERS

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by section 2(2) of the Co-operative societies Law No.5 of 1972 as amended byCo-operative societies (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 1983 and the Co-operative societies (Amendment) Act, No. 11 of1992 read in conjunction with provincial council (Consequential Provisions) Act No. 12 of 1989, I, Shan Wijayalal DeSilva Governor of Eastern Province do hereby confer on each of the persons named in column I in the schedule hereto,who have been appointed to assist the Commissioner of Co-operative Development and Registrar of Co-operative societiesof the Eastern province, such powers of the Registrar under the Co-operative societies Law, No. 5 of 1972 as amended byCo-operative societies (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 1983 and the Co-operative societies (Amendment) Act, No. 11 of1992 and the rules there under and which are more fully described in Column III of the said schedule in respect of theirarea with effect from the dates shown in Column II of the said schedule, to be exercised by the aforesaid during theirtenure of office.

SHAN WIJAYALAL DE SILVA,Governor,Eastern Province.Trincomalee,25th October 2019.
