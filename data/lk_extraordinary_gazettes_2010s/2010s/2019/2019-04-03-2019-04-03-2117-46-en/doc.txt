SRI LANKA EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT, No. 40 OF 1979

Order under Section 14

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 14(1) of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No. 40 of 1979, I, MalikSamarawickrama, Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade, with the concurrence of the Minister ofFinance, do hereby this Order amend the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2113/4 dated March 05, 2019, by theaddition of the following sub paragraph immediately after sub paragraph (3) of paragraph A.

“(4) Any goods imported on the basis of Letter of Credit (LC), established on or before March 05, 2019 and for which, theBill of Entry to be delivered to the Sri Lanka Customs on or before June 30, 2019, shall be paid at the rate specified in theColumn IV of the Schedule in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2047/2 dated November 27, 2017.”

MALIK SAMARAWICKRAMA,Minister of Development Strategiesand International Trade.Ministry of Development Strategies and International Trade,Colombo 01,April 04, 2019.
