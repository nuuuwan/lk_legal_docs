FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PANDUWASNUWARAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which YapaMudiyanselage Somarathne the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Panambara Mudiyanselage Kumarasena Jayalath to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Panduwasnuwara PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Panambara Mudiyanselage Kumarasena Jayalath was selected as the member of the Panduwasnuwara PradeshiyaSabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,18th September, 2019.
