FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KUCHCHAVELIPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kuchchaveli Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSubramaniyam Paramesvaran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision to nominateSoosaipillai Mohanadas to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sangarapillai Suthakaran, Returning Officer of the Kuchchaveli Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that SoosaipillaiMohanadas was selected as the member of the Kuchchaveli Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. SUTHAKARAN,Returning Officer,Kuchchaveli Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Trincomalee,26th August, 2019.
