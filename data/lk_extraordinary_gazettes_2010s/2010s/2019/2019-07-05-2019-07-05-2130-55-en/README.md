FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DEPUTY MAYOR OF THE AKKARAIPATTUMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66B(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION (AMENDMENT)ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Deputy Mayor in Akkaraipattu Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Congress, the recognized political party to which Abdul GafoorAzmy the former Deputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 66 B (2) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon me member of that Akkaraipattu Municipal Council who belongs to such party who shall be declared electedas a Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid Recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Muhammed Caseem Muhammed Yaseer to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of the Akkaraipattu MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended byLocal Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare that Muhammed Caseem MuhammedYaseer elected as the Deputy Mayor of the Akkaraipattu Municipal Council .

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Akkaraipattu Municipal Council.District Election Office,Ampara,04th July, 2019.
