FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KANTHALE PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kanthale pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group 02 which Dehiwala Gurunnanselage George CamilusCosta, the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturn member of Independent Group 02 who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group 02 has communicated his decision to nominateHitihamu Mudiyanselage Muthubanda to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sangarapillai Suthakaran, Returning Officer of the Kanthale pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that HitihamuMudiyanselage Muthubanda was selected as the member of the Kanthale pradeshiya Sabha.

S. SUTHAKARAN,Returning Officer,Kanthale pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Trincomalee,10th June, 2019.
