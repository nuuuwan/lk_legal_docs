Guidelines issued by the Election Commission in addition to the Media Guidelines published in the Gazette No.2141/87 of 21.09.2019.

1.Photographs or declarations of public officers who do not have political rights or of members of Armed forcesand police officers who have are in active service or photographs taken and declarations made by retired officerswhile they were in active service and in uniform, should not be published through electronic, print, social or anyother media to promote or prejudice a party or a candidate.

2.Photographs should not be taken and videoing inside polling stations should not be done without the writtenapproval of the Election Commission. Photographs or videos taken inside a polling station or a postal votingcentre taken without authority should not be published through electronic, print or social media.

3.Unofficial results of the poll should not be published untill they are officially declared by the ElectionCommission.
