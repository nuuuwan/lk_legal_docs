THE MEDIATION (SPECIAL CATEGORIES OF DISPUTES) ACT, No. 21 OF 2003

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Mediation (Special Categories of Disputes) Act, No. 21 of 2003,I, Thalatha Athukorale, Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms, do by this Order, specify the area set out in the Schedulehereto, as a Special Mediation Board Area within which the settlement of disputes relating to grievances of migrantworkers and members of their families residing in Sri Lanka as determined by Order published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2078/36 dated July 04, 2018 shall be carried out, under the provisions of the said Act.

THALATHA ATHUKORALE,Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms.

Colombo,16th May, 2019.
