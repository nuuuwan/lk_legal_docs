RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE SOUTHERN PROVINCE AGRICULTURAL PRECEPT

Directives

REGISTRATION OF VOLUNTARY AGRICULTURAL DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION UNDER THE SOUTHERN PROVINCIAL DEPARTMENT

OF AGRICULTURE

Conditions :–

1. It should be an organization formed within the Southern Province for a specific agricultural purpose (agriculturalproductions that are acceptable within the Southern Province Agricultural Department such as organicproductions, Value added products, produce made under Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), Production ofAgricultural Seeds and Planting, Bee Keeping, Mushroom Cultivation or Flowericulture).

2. Such Agricultural organizations should have been permanently formed and have been active within the six monthspreceding.

3. The voluntary agricultural organization should agree to work under the instructions of the Agricultural Instructorof the area.

4. Should bide by the conditions imposed currently and in the future on Voluntary Agricultural Organization by theSouthern Provincial Council.

5. Should conduct its activities within an annual plan of the organization.

6. All activities of the Voluntary Agricultural Organization should be conducted under the supervision of theAgricultural Instructor of the area.

7. The voluntary agricultural organization should hold its meetings of the membership within a specific timeinterval and should maintain its records.

8. While the time interval above should be a minimum of 2 months the members should meet at least once everymonth.

9. To register, the Voluntary Agricultural Organization should submit six reports of such meetings.

10. The Voluntary Agricultural Organization should adopt the constitutions made available by the Southern ProvincialCouncil.

11. While the minimum number of members of the Voluntary Agricultural Organization should be 10 members themaximum number of members should not exceed 25.

12. Such Voluntary Agricultural Organization are permitted to work, either within, the Grama Niladhari Division,Agricultural Department’s area of purview, Divisional Secretariat area or the area of purview of the office ofAssistant Director of Agriculture.

Registration

1. Should apply for registration with a formal application form.

2. The registration applications can be obtained on the payment of a specific fee from, the extension officeslocated in the Agrarian Service Centres, offices of the Assistant Directors of Agriculture and from the officesof the Deputy Director of Agriculture.

REGISTRATION OF ORGANIC FERTILIZER MANUFACTURERS UNDER THE SOUTHERNPROVINCE AGRICULTURE DEPARTMENT

Conditions :

1. Should be a permanent resident of the Southern Province.

2. The organic manufacturing plant should be located within the Southern Province.

3. The organic fertilzer manufacturing process should be subject to the supervision of the Provincial Director ofAgriculture or an officer of the Southern Province Department of Agriculture empowered by the ProvincialDirector of Agriculture, in periodic intervals.

4. The manufacturer should provide samples of the organic fertilizer produced to the relevant authorized officerappointed to inspect the quality of the fertilizer whenever such requirement to inspect arises.

5. The manufacturer should ensure that the quality standards mandatory for the fertilizer produced should bemaintained at all times.

6. In the event where different organic fertilizer manufacturers have been subject to a categorization by the SouthernProvince Department of Agriculutre, the manufacturer should submit to such categorization.

7. If the organic fertilizer manufactured is being sold in the market, the fertilizer should be in a regularised contianer(bag) approved by the Southern Province Agricultural Department.

8. An organic fertilizer manufacturer who manufacturers fertilizer as above will be entitled to sell his fertilizer atthe Ruhunu Rasara Trade Organization after having obtained the necessary registration.

Registration :

a. It is possible to register or to renew the existing registration by duly completing an application form providedby the Department of Agriculture of the Southern Province and handing over such application to the Extentionoffice of the area.

b. The manufacturer should agree to pay the registration fee at the time of registration.

c. The manufacturer should be in agreement with the full registration process of organic fertilizer manufacturersof the Department of Agriculture of the Southern Provincial Council.

d. The Southern Province Department of Agriculture will issue a registration number at the point of registration toevery manufacturer and that number, thereafter, should be mentioned by every manufacturer in theircorrespondence with the department.

e. The registration, so obtained, is valid only for one calendar year and at the end of that year the registration hasto be renewed by paying the required fee.

f. The year, as described above, should mean an year commencing on the 1st January to the 31st of Decemberevery year. In each year the registration of organic fertilizer manufacturers or the renewal of their agreementwill take place betweeen the 1st January to 31st March of the relevant year.

g. A manufacturer seeking a new registration should have been engaged in the manufaturing process for a period ofat least 6 months and accordingly, he should be prepared to provide proof of such engagement when required.
