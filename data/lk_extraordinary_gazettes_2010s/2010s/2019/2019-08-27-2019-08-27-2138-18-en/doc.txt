FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ATTANAGALLAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWepola Pathirannehelage Gihan Chinthaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that No. - 07 Magalegoda of Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha whobelongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Rupasinghe Arachchillage Neel Pradeep Rupasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the AttanagallaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Rupasinghe Arachchillage Neel Pradeep Rupasinghe was elected as the member of the No. - 07Magalegoda of Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,26th August, 2019.
