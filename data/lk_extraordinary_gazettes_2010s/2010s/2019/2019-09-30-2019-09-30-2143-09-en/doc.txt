I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby, in the exercise of powersvested in me by Section 4 of the Commission Inquiry Act (Chapter 393) once again enlarge the period of time up to 31stOctober 2019 for rendition of the final report on completion of the tasks assigned to the Commission established underSection 2 of the said Commission of Inquiry Act on 14th January, 2019 by Notification published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 2106/1 dated 16th January 2019 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka to investigate andinquire into acts of corruption, fraud, criminal breach of trust, criminal misappropriation of property, cheating an abuseor misuse of power or authority, state resources and privilages which offences and acts of wrong doing had allegedlyoccurred during the period of 15th January 2015 to 31st December 2018 having enlarged the period of six months endingon 14th July 2019 by two and a half months from 14th July 2019 up to 30th September 2019 by Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2132/4 dated 15th July 2019 in the exercise of the said powers vested in me.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.
