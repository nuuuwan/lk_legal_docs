FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ADDALACHCHENAIPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Addalachchenai pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Congress, the recognized political party to which MeerasaivuMuhammadu Jahfer the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of National Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Meerasaivu Muhammadu Niyaal to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of Addalachchenai pradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Meerasaivu Muhammadu Niyaal was selected as the member of the Addalachchenai pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Addalachchenai pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ampara,23rd May, 2019.
