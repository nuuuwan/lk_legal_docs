THE STATE INDUSTRIAL CORPORATION ACT, No. 49 OF 1957

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the State Industrial Corporation Act, No. 49 of 1957, I, RajithaSenaratne, Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine with the approval of the Government by the CabinetDecision of 18/0769/718/043 of dated 10th April 2018, do, by this Order repeal the First Schedule to the Order madeunder Subsection (1) of the Section 2 of the aforesaid Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 456/21 dated 4thJune 1987 and substitute the following :

“First Schedule

The objects of the Corporation shall be :

(1) either by itselft or by entering into such Joint Venture, Public-Private Partnership or other such agreement tomanufacture, process, stock, pack or repack medicine ;

(2) to create subsidiary companies ;

(3) to enter into International Agreements with potential investors ;

(4) to provide technical assistance for the manufacturing and processing of medicine ;

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk1A PG 4621— 517 (08/2019)
