FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KAHAWATTA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member in Kahawatta Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichGasperalalage Susil Sisira Kumara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Manathunga Arachchige Jagath Shantha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of the Kahawatta Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ManathungaArachchige Jagath Shantha was selected as the member of the Kahawatta Pradeshiya Sabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATHENNE,Returning Officer,Kahawatta Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ratnapura,04th April, 2019.
