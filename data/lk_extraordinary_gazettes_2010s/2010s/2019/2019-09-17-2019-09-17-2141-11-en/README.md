FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE DEHIWALA - MOUNTLAVINIA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIESELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which S. W.N. Danuska Silva the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of People’s Liberation Front, who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate K. Premasiri to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Dehiwala - MountLavinia Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter262) do hereby declare that K. Premasiri was selected as the member of the Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council.District Election Office,Colombo City,16th September, 2019.
