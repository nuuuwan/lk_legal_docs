FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Katana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group No. 1, to which Warnakulasooriya Sanath Rohan Fernandothe member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember of Independent Group No. 1 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group No. 1 has communicated his decision to nominateMelani Rose Ferreira to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the Katana PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Melani Rose Ferreira was selected as the member of the Katana Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Katana Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,24th September, 2019.
