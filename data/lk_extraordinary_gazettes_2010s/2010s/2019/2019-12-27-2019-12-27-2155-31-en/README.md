FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HOMAGAMA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichJayamuni Thilan Randima Gunarathne the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 05 Nawalamulla of Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Kuruppu Arachchige Chaminda Priyadarshana Kuruppu Arachchi to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of the of HomagamaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Kuruppu Arachchige Chaminda Priyadarshana Kuruppu Arachchi was elected as the member of theWard No. 05 Nawalamulla of Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Colombo,27th December, 2019.
