L.D.B 21/1978(X)(A)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Bandula Gunawardana,the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaidAct, in respect of the National School of Business Management Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under theCompanies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PB 4833, do by this Order, amend the Order made underthe said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2053/40 of January 11, 2018, by the repeal of the Schedule Iand II to that Order and Substitution therefor of the following new Schedule I and II as follows :–

“SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

National School of Business Management Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programmerelating to the degree specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Engineering Honours inComputer System Engineering, adhere to the minimum entry qualifications of three (3) simple passes (‘S’) in PhysicalScience subject stream in one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advance Level) Examination or equivalent.

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Bandula Gunawardana,the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaidAct, in respect of the National School of Business Management Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under theCompanies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PB 4833, do by this Order, amend the schedule II to theOrder made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2121/43 of May 2, 2019, by the additionimmediately after the entry “Bachelor of Management Honours in International Business” appearing in Column I and it’scorresponding entry in Column II thereto, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule IIhereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :–

“SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

National School of Business Management Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programmesrelating to the degrees specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Engineering Honours inElectrical and Electronic Engineering and Bachelor of Science Engineering Honours in Mechatronic Engineering, adhereto the minimum entry qualifications of three (3) simple passes (‘S’) in Physical Science subject stream in one and thesame sitting at the GCE (Advance Level) Examination or equivalent.
