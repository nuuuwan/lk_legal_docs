EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HARISPATTUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Harispattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which K. A. JanakaNayanananda the Member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate D. A. Bandulasiri Nimal Santha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of theHarispattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter262) do hereby declare that D. A. Bandulasiri Nimal Santha was elected as the member of the Harispattuwa PradeshiyaSabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Harispattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,19th March, 2019.
