NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO

LAND PARCELS

Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998

(Section 12)

NOTICE No : 32/0681 Kandy District

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 320487 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Thiththapajjala (Block 04) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Nilad-hari Division of 462 - Thiththapajjala North in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Harispaththuwa in the District of Kandy in the Province of Central should submit his claim to the under-signed before 17th April, 2019 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and ac-cordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 320487 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - Generals’ office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. K. A. K. RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Land Title Settlement (Actg.). Land Title Settlement Department, No.1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla 07th March, 2019.
