THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT, (CHAPTER 460)

Order under Section 49A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 49A of the Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460) read with Article 44 of theConstitution, I, Malik Samarawickrama, Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this Order, declare that the land specified in the Schedule hereto is required by theBoard of Investment of Sri Lanka being a public corporation established by the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka Law,No. 4 of 1978.

MALIK SAMARAWICKRAMA,Minister of Development Strategies & International Trade.

Colombo,07th May, 2019.
