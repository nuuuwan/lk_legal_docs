FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE CHILAW PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Chilaw pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichBalapuwaduge Nuwan Sanjeeva Mendis the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon member of that 13 Pothuvila of Chilaw Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected asa member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Ranasinghe Arachchige Amila Rangana Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Chilaw pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms ofSection 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that RanasingheArachchige Amila Rangana Kumara was elected as the member of the 13 Pothuvila of Chilaw pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Chilaw pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Puttalam,31st May, 2019.
