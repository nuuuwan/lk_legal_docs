INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ACT, No. 36 OF 2003

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Science, Technology and Research under Section 204 of the Intellectual PropertyAct, No. 36 of 2003.

SUJEEWA SENASINGHE (M. P.),Minister of Science, Technology and Research.

Ministry of Science, Technology and Research,Battaramulla,23rd October, 2019.

Fees Regulations of National Intellectual Property Office of Sri Lanka

The notification relating to the fees published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1445/10 of 17th May 2006 ishereby rescinded and the fees specified in the Second Schedule of the said notification are hereby amended as follows interms of the proposal 261 of Budget Proposals 2018 to increase fees charged for services provided by the governmentagencies which have not been revised during last 03 years, by 15%.

These regulations may be cited as the Intellectual Property Regulations No. 02 of 2019 and shall come intooperation on 30.10.2019.
