FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BERUWALA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Beruwala Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending forcouncil meetings period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which MohamedNalim Fathima Shiyama the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mohamed Hasan Fathima Rinusa to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Wannakuwatta Waduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the BeruwalaUrban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Mohamed Hasan Fathima Rinusa was selected as the member of the Beruwala Urban Council.

KALINDRA JAYAWEERA FERNANDO,Returning Officer,Beruwala Urban Council.District Election Office,Kalutara,11th June, 2019.
