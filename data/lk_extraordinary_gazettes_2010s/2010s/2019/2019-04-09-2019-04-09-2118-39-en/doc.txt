EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KURUNEGALAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Kurunegala Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of loss of seator office ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party towhich Krishnapalan Thiyagarajah the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Brahakmana Arachchige Susantha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Kurunegala Municipal Council,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatBrahakmana Arachchige Susantha was selected as the member of the Kurunegala Municipal Council.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Kurunegala Municipal Council.District Election Office,Kurunegala,08th April, 2019.
