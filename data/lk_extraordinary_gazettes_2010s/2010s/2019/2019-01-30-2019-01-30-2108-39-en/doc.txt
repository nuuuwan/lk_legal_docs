FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE SRI JAYAWARDANAPURA KOTTEMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Sri Jayawardanapura Kotte Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason ofresignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichRanawaka Aarachchige Don Janaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Vidyapathi Tharakaratne Gorakgoda Namage Prabath to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchilage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Sri JayawardanapuraKotte Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)do hereby declare that Vidyapathi Tharakaratne Gorakgoda Namage Prabath was selected as the member of the SriJayawardanapura Kotte Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Sri Jayawardanapura Kotte Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Colombo City,30th January, 2019.
