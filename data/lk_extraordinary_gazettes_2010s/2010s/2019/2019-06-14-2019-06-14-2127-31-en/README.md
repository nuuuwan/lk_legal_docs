FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ATTANAGALLAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resigning ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front of Good Governance, the recognized political party towhich Mohamed Raheem Mohamed Rassem the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of National Front for Good Governance who belongs to suchparty and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredselected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMuhammadu Mansoor Shiras Mohamed to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the AttanagallaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Muhammadu Mansoor Shiras Mohamed was selected as the member of the Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,13th June, 2019.
