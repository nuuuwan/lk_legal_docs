FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM EASTPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group to which Thevan Sasinanthini the member belonged, wasrequired by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if IndependentGroup who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateSelvakumary Selvaruban to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatSelvakumary Selvaruban was selected as the member of the Valikamam East Pradeshisya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,29th April, 2019.
