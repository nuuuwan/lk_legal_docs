Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Statue No. 1 of 2019 of the Provincial Council of Uva Province

This is a statute impose to amend the Co-operative Societies Statute No. 03 of 2011 of the ProvincialCouncil of Uva Province

Imposed as such by the Provincial Council of Uva Province in accordance with the powers vested to theprovincial councils by the 13th Amendment to the constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka.

01. This statute is identified as the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) statute No. 1/2019 of UvaProvincial Council and except as otherwise stated, it shall be deemed to have been effective for all purposesfrom the date it was approved by the Governor of Uva Province or from the date on which it was decided.

02. (Hereinafter referred to as the Main Statute). It is hereby amended by removing the subsection(5) of Section 5 and Paragraph (a) and (b) of subsection (5) of the Co-operative Societies Statute No. 3 of2011 of the Provincial Council of Uva Province and incorporating the paragraphs shown below.

(a) All the powers or some power out of them of the registrar, under any regulation made underthis statute or considered to be made, should be able to transferred to any Deputy Registrar,Assistant Registrar, Deputy Commissioner, Assistant Commissioner by the Registrar througha document.

(b) The authorized Deputy Registrar, Assistant Registrar, Deputy Commissioner, AssistantCommissioner should able to exercise any power vested on the above paragraph (a).

03. It is hereby amended by removing the Subseciton (4) of the Section (69) of the main statute andincorporating the new subsection shown below:

When a certificate is issued to a magistrate under the Paragraph (c) of the subsection (4)(1), themagistrate should summon the defaulter to give reasons why further actions shouldn’t be taken against himto recover the money even though he could not be punished by imprisonment, if he did not give adequatereasons that amount should be considered as the fine imposed in the penal order by the magistrate regardingthe defaulter for a offense only punishable with a fine. Then the provisions of section 291 of the code ofcriminal procedure act No. 15 of 1979 (other than the paragraphs (a) and (d) of that section) should beapplicable, when impose the penal order, the magistrate can made any command, that can be made by himaccording to the provisions of that section. Any offender who has been penalized for the period ofimprisonment in default of the fine imposed under section 291 of the code of criminal procedure act No. 15of 1979, must not be released for paying any amount mentioned in the specified certificate referred toparagraph (c) of sub section stated above.

04. If there is any inconsistency between the Sinhala and English text of this statute, the Sinhala texthas to be enforced.
