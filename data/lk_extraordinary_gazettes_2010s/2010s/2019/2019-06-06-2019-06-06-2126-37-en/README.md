FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE JAFFNA MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Jaffna Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichAjantha Thanabalasingam the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Deisy Philip Jeyaranjan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Jaffna Municipal Council, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that DeisyPhilip Jeyaranjan was selected as the member of the Jaffna Municipal Council.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Jaffna Municipal Council.District Election Office,Jaffna,04th June, 2019.
