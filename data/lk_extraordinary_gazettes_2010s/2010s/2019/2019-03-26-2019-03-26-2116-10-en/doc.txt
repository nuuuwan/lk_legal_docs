FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PATHAHEWAHETA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Pathahewaheta Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWeerasinghe Mudiyanselage Upual Bandara Weerasinghe the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 16-Hewawissa of Pathahewaheta Pradeshiya Sabhawho belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateWagaha Mulagedara Majee Sulbika Marikkar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of the PathahewahetaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Wagaha Mulagedara Majee Sulbika Marikkar was elected as the member of the 16-Hewawissa of PathahewahetaPradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Pathahewaheta Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,26th March, 2019.
