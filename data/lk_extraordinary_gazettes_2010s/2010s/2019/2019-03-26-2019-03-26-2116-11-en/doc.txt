THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul Rauff Hakeem,the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of theaforesaid Act, in respect of the Aquinas College of Higher Studies, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Ordermade under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2005/11 of February 07, 2017 by the additionimmediately after the entry “(5) Bachelor or Science Honours in Agro Industry Management” appearing in Column Iand it’s corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of theSchedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

Aquinas College of Higher Studies shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified inSchedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Education Honours in English, adhere to the minimum entry qualificationsof three (03) ‘S’ passes in any subject stream (‘English’ shall be one of main three subjects) in one and the same sitting atthe GCE Advanced Level Examination or equivalent.
