Government Notifications

THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

IT is hereby notified by me, as the Cabinet Minister in Charge of the Subject of Transport and Civil Aviation, interms of the Article 44 (5) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka that assignment ofsubjects and functions for State Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, appointed under the Article 44(1) of theConstitution, shall come into effect immediately under my supervision, control and guidance as listed in the followingSchedule.

ARJUNA RANATHUNGA,Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation.
