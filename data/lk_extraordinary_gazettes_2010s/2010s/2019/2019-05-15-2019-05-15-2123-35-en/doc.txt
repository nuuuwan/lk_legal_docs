THE NATIONAL MEDICINES REGULATORY AUTHORITY ACT, No .5 OF 2015

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine under section 142 read togetherwith section 118 of the National Medicines Regulatory Authority Act, No. 5 of 2015.

DR. RAJITHA SENARATNE,Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine.

Colombo,11th May, 2019.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Medicines (Ceiling on Prices) Regulations, 2019 and shall come intooperation on the date of publication of these regulations in the Gazette (hereinafter referred to as the “relevant date”).

2. There shall be a ceiling on prices of the medicines set out in the Schedule hereto (hereinafter referred to as“scheduled medicines”) from and after the relevant date.

3. The Maximum Retail Price limits in respect of the scheduled medicines shall be applicable to all brand namesand generic names of the scheduled medicines.

4. On and after the relevant date, no manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor,pharmacist, medical practitioner,dentist, veterinary surgeon,medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which isin possession of any scheduled medicine set out in column II of the Schedule hereto,for the purpose of sale shall sell suchmedicine above the Maximum Retail Price stipulated in the corresponding entry in column V thereof.

5.Every person or institution referred to in regulation 4, who sells or offer for sale any medicine set out in theSchedule hereto, shall upon such sale issue a receipt clearly indicating the appropriate name and the price of such medicine.

6. Any manufacturer or importer who is in possession of a scheduled medicine for the purpose of sale whocurrently sells such medicine at a price less than the Maximum Retail Price stipulated in the Schedule may increase theexisting price by only Fourteen Decimal Four per centum (14.4%) and any trader, distributor, pharmacist, medicalpractitioner, dentist, veterinary surgeon, medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or personwho or which is in possession of such scheduled medicine for the purpose of sale shall maintain such medicine at suchrevised price.

7. Where any dosage form or strength of the scheduled medicine is not specified in such schedule, the maximumretail price limit for the dosage form or strength not so specified shall be the maximum retail price limit as shall be fixedby the National Medicines Regulatory Authority.

8. The Maximum Retail Price as set out in the Schedule hereto or the current retail price, whichever is less, ofeach unit of item in the stock of the scheduled medicines manufactured or available in the market, medical institutionincluding a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession of such medicine for thepurpose of sale on the relevant date shall be printed or marked on its commercial package or label as expeditiously aspracticable, so however, before the expiry of a period of Forty- five days from the relevant date.

9. Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 8, the Maximum Retail Price of each unit shall be as set out inthe Schedule on and after the relevant date. The Maximum Retail Price set out in the Schedule shall continue to be validand effectual from the relevant date.

10. Any manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner, dentist, veterinarysurgeon,medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which is in possession ofa scheduled medicine for the purpose of sale, who contravenes the provisions of these regulations commits an offenceand shall be triable under section 131 of the National Medicines Regulatory Authority Act, No.5 of 2015.

11. It shall be the duty of every manufacturer, importer, trader, distributor, pharmacist, medical practitioner,dentist, veterinary surgeon,medical institution including a private medical institution, pharmacy or person who or which isin possession of scheduled medicines for the purpose of sale, to display at every retail outlet the Maximum Retail Priceof the scheduled medicines set out in the Schedule hereto.

12. The (Ceiling on Prices) Regulations, No. 2 of 2016 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1989/61 ofOctober 21, 2016 as amended by regulations published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2049/31 of December 14, 2017 andby regulations published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2086/37 of August 31, 2018 are hereby rescinded.

13. For the purpose of these regulations –

“dentist” shall have the same meaning as in the Medical Ordinance (Chapter 105);

“medical practitioner” shall have the same meaning as in the Medical Ordinance (Chapter 105);

“private medical institution” shall have the same meaning assigned to it by the Private Medical Institutions(Registration) Act, No. 21 of 2006;

“veterinary surgeon” means a person registered as a Veterinary Surgeon or a Veterinary Practitionerunder the Veterinary Surgeons and Practitioners Act, No. 46 of 1956.
