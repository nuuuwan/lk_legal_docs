FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NALLUR PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Nagan Sivaloganathan the member belonged,was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if IndependentGroup who belongs to such Independent Group, and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateMervin Roshan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that MervinRoshan was selected as the member of the Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Nallur Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,03rd May, 2019.
