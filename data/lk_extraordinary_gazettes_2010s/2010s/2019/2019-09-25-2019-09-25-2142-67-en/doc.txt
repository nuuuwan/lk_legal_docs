THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE LIQUOR AND VINEGAR TRADE

It is hereby notified under section 29(3) of the Wages Board Ordinance (Chapter 136), that the decisions of the Wages Boardfor the Liquor and Vinegar Trade made Section 30 of that Ordinance and specified in the Schedule here to have beenapproved by the Hon.Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

The decision shall came into force on the 01st of October 2019.

R.P.R. Rajapaksa,Secretary,Ministry of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

Department of Labour,Colombo 05.12th September, 2019.
