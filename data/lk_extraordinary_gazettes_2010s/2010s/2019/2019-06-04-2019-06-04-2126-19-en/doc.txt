FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NINTHAVUR PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the All Ceylon Makkal Congress, the recognized political party to whichAliyar Basheera Umma the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if All Ceylon Makkal Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Athamwawa Rifana to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of the Ninthavur PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Athamwawa Rifana was selected as the member of the Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ampara,03rd June, 2019.
