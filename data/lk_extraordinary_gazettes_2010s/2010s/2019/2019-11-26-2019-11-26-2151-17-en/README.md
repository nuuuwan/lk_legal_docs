NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A (1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIEIS ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Arachchikattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Arachchikattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a)of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mrs. Adhikari Mudiyanselage Iranganie Pushpalatha ofthe Arachchikattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated her office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as she has ceased to bea member of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party.

NAGALINGAM REGAN,Returning Officer,Arachchikattuwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Puttalam,25th November, 2019.
