EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MATARA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Matara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the group leader of the Independent group, the recognized political party to which HewamirissageJagath Prasantha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member if Independent group who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the group leader of the Independent group has communicated his decision to nominate MakawitaLiyanage Pethum Priyantha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the Matara PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Makawita Liyanage Pethum Priyantha was selected as the member of the Matara Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Matara Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Matara District,26th February, 2019.
