FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE EMBILIPITIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION (AMENDED)ACT, NO. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Chairman in Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of loss of seat or office;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichMeegasdeniya Kankanamge Amil the Former Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66B(2) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of Embilitpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall bedeclared elected as chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Weerasinghage Surantha Jayalath to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of the Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local AuthoritiesElection (amended) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Weerasinghage Surantha Jayalath elected as chairman of theEmbilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATHENNE,Returning Officer,Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ratnapura,20th June, 2019.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE EMBILIPITIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B(2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION (AMENDED)ACT, NO. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Vice Chairmanhas been appointed as Chairman ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichWeerasinghage Surantha Jayalath the former vice chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66B(2) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party whoshall be declared elected as vice chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mathara Arachchige Jayaweera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local AuthoritiesElection (amended) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Mathara Arachchige Jayaweera elected as vice chairman ofthe Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE EMBILIPITIYA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichKekanadura Vidana Arachchige Chandrasiri the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 5 Gangeyaya of Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Paththini Wickramarachchige Nimal Kumarasingha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of the Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that PaththiniWickramarachchige Nimal Kumarasingha was elected as the member of the 5 Gangeyaya of Embilitpitiya PradeshiyaSabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATHENNE,Returning Officer,Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ratnapura,20th June, 2019.
