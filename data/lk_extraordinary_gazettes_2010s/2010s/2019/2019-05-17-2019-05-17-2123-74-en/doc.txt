ADMINISTRATION OF LOCAL AUTHORITIES STATUTE No. .... OF 2019 OF WESTERNPROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

A Statute to provide provisions for the administration and supervision of affairs relevant for theduties and functions of the Local Authorities of Western Province for obtaining information anddocuments relevant for it including the incidentals thereto.

This Statute is inconsistent to the Section 278 of Municipal Council Ordinance, Section 197of the Urban Council Ordinance and Section 196 of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987.

Be it enacted by the Western Province Provincial Council of Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka.

1.This Statute is referred to as the Administration of Local Authorities Statute No. ..........of 2019 of Western Province Provincial Council and shall come into operation on thedate of receiving the assent granted by the Governor of Western Province.

2.An Investigation Officer who engages in an investigation task relevant thereto or anyofficer for whom the power has been specifically delegated for the same by the Ministeror the Commissioner, may request the Administrative Officer of the Local Authorityor any other officer for whom the power has been delegated by him for that task ; fora document or a book which is under the custody of Local Authorities and with regardto the execution of powers or to perform the duties thereof or its administration, acertain task that is being carried out or was being carried out under the order of theCouncil or a document, file relevant thereto, Minutes of a committee of the Councilor a committee appointed by the Council, a piece of information regarding and income,expense relevant to the fund of the Local Authority or reports on accounts or copiesof such documents or a piece of information relevant to a property which is under thecontrol of the Local Authority and may take them into custody for the function ofinspecting.

3.The Commissioner or the Investigation Officer for whom the power is delegated forthe ad hoc, shall have the power and authority to enter a Local Authority or any premiseswhich is under the control of the Local Authority, to perform the function of inspectingwhich is mentioned under the above Section 2.

4.Where a document, file, book or any other information is requested, deliberatelyneglecting or obstructing to provide such information shall be an offence and shall beliable to a fine not exceeding Rs. 5,000 after having been convicted by a MagistrateCourt.

5.In this Statute, unless the context otherwise requires :

“Minister” means the Minister of Western Province, who is in-charge of theSubject.

“Local Authorities” means Municipal Councils, Urban Councils and PradeshiyaSabha of Western Province.

“Commissioner of Local Government” means the Western ProvincialCommissioner of Local Government.

“Investigating Officer” means a Senior Investigating Officers that bears a post inProvincial Council Public Service having been attached to the Department ofLocal Government of Western Province or an Investigation Officer.

6.In the event of any inconsistency between Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Statute, theSinhala text shall prevail.
