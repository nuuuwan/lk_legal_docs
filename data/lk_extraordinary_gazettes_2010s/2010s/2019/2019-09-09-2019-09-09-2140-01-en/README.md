FILLING OF A VACANCY UNDER PARAGRAPH 13(b) OF ARTICLE 99 OF THE CONSTITUTION OFTHE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64(1) of theParliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, as amended by the Act, No. 15 of 1988, that a Vacancy has occured in theMembership of the Eighth Parliament by reason of death of Mr. Salinda Dissanayake, who was declared elected as aMember of Parliament for the Electoral District No. 15 - Kurunegala under Section 62 of the aforesaid Act ;

And Whereas the Returning Officer, for the Electoral District which returned the said member was directedby the Election Commission under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No.1 of 1981 to fill this vacancyas provided for under Paragraph 13(b) of Article 99 of the Constitution;

And Whereas the said Returning Officer acting under Section 64(2)of the aforesaid Act has made a return inForm “M” setout in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. H. M. D. B. Herath was declaredelected as a Member of Parliament for the said Electoral District;

Now therefore, we, the Election Commission, in the exercise of the powers vested in the ElectionCommission by Section 64(2) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 which shall be read with theProvisions of the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby notify the General Public that Mr. H. M. D. B. Herath has been declared elected as a Member of the EighthParliament for the Electoral District No. 15 - Kurunegala to fill the vacancy caused by reason of death ofMr. Salinda Dissanayake.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,N. J. ABEYSEKERE,S. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLE,Chairman,Member,Member,Election Commission.Election Commission.Election Commission.
