CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, H. P. Jayananda, Additional Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 04th February, 2019, all duties ofCustoms as well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paidand received at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2108/3 of 28th January,2019 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notification to bepublished on or before 10th February, 2019.

H. P. JAYANANDA,Additional Director General of CustomsFor Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,01st February, 2019.
