STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether the 49 employees inclusive of R. A. S. Lionel Premasiri, mentioned in the schedule had been caused injustice by theVoluntary Retirement Compensation Scheme not being followed in the termination of employees from Mihin Lanka (Pvt) Ltd inaccordance with Public Enterprise Circular No. 02/2016 dated 08.03.2016 introduced by the Ministry of Finance for surplusworkers of public enterprises and a less favourable method being followed and if such injustice had been caused, to what reliefeach of them is entitled.
