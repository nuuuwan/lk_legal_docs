FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM WEST PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group to which Selvarasa Krishnarasa the member belonged, wasrequired by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if Independent Groupwho belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateKunabalakrishnan Kirubakaran to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam West PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Kunabalakrishnan Kirubakaran was selected as the member of the Valikamam West Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,26th August, 2019.
