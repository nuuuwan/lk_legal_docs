Proclamation

P.S. No: PS/CSA/1/1/10.

WHEREAS HIS EXCELLENCY GOTABAYA RAJAPAKSA was declared elected President of the Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka on the Seventeenth day of November, Two Thousand and Nineteen at an Election held under Article 31(3) ofthe Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,

And Whereas, the Term of Office of the said President commenced, on the Seventeenth day of November TwoThousand and Nineteen in terms of Article 31(4) of the said Constitution,

It is hereby proclaimed that HIS EXCELLENCY GOTABAYA RAJAPAKSA has, by making and subscribing the affirmationsset out in the Fourth and Seventh Schedules to the said Constitution in the manner provided for in Article 32 read withArticle 157 A(7) thereof, on this Eighteenth day of November Two Thousand and Nineteen, assumed the Office ofPresident of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,

Whereof all persons are hereby required to take due notice.

Given at Anuradhapura on the Eighteenth day of November, Two Thousand and Nineteen.

By Order of His Excellency,

P. B. JAYASUNDERA, Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo-01,November 19, 2019.
