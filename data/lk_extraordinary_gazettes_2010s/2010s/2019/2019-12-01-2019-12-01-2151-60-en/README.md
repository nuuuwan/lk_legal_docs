EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, as amdended,I, Mahinda Rajapaksa, Minister of Finance, Economic and Policy Development, do by this Order declare that, the rate ofExcise duty specified in the Column IV applicable on corresponding articles specified in Column I, II and III of theSchedule hereto, shall replace the rate specified in Column IV on the corresponding articles of the Schedule I of theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2113/9 dated March 05, 2019 as amended by the Extraordinary GazetteNotification Nos. 2123/5 dated May 13, 2019, 2131/29 dated July 10, 2019 and 2145/12 dated October 14, 2019 issuedunder the Section 3 of this Act with effect from December 01, 2019.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Finance, Economic and Policy Development.
