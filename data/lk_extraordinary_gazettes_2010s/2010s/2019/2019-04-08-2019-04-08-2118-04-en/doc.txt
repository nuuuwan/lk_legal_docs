NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Batticaloa Municipal Council

I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Batticaloa Municipal Council, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) ofthe (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Sivalingam Somasuntharam of the Batticaloa MunicipalCouncil has vacated his office of member of that Municipal Council as he has ceased to be a member of the Tamil UnitedLiberation Front.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Batticaloa Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Batticaloa,05th April, 2019.
