FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VAVUNIYA SOUTH (TAMIL)PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which Nahur PichchaiJawahir the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon themember of that ward 06 Salambeikulam if Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Subramaniyam Ganesan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Jesuthasan Jeniton, Returning Officer of the Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatSubramaniyam Ganesan was elected as the member of the Ward 06 Salambeikulam of Vavuniya South (Tamil) PradeshiyaSabha.

J. JENITON,Returning Officer,Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Vavuniya,07th August, 2019.
