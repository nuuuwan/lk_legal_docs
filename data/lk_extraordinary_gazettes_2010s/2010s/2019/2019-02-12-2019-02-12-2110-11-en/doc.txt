FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATTANKUDY URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kattankudy Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front For Good Governance, the recognized political party towhich Muhammathu Buhari Muhammathu Firdawsi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if National Front For Good Governance who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected asa member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Abdullatheef Mohammadu Sabeel to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Kattankudy Urban Council, acting in terms ofSelection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AbdullatheefMohammadu Sabeel was selected as the member of the Kattankudy Urban Council.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Kattankudy Urban Council.

District Election Office,Batticaloa,11th February, 2019,
