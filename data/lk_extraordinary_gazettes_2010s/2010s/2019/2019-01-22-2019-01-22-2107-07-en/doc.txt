MINUTE OF THE CENTRAL PROVINCIAL PUBLIC MANAGEMENT ASSISTANT’S SERVICE

AMENDMENT No. 02

THE Hon. Governor of Central Province has approved to amend the Minute of the Central Provincial Public ManagementAssistant’s Service revised by the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 2047/43dated 30.11.2017 and published in the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No.1869/4 dated 30.06.2014, as follows:

1. The Section 7.3.2.1 of Para. 07 is revised as follows :

7.3.2.1 Educational Qualifications

Shall have passed the G. C. E. (O/L) Examination in six (06) subjects including Sinhala/Tamil/English andMathematics/ Arithmetic/ Pure Mathematics/ Elementary Mathematics/ Commercial Arithmetic with at least twocredit passes at not more than two sittings.

The Section 7.3.3.1 of Para. 07 is revised as follows :

7.3.3.1 Written Examination

06. Officers shall be exempted from the requirement of passing the Efficiency Bar Examination prescribed for theofficers in Grade I of Central Provincial Public management Assistant Service on successful completion of the 10day course including the basic principles of which a Senior Officer in public service should be aware, within 05years from the date of promotion to Grade I.

i.Conducting authority of the course:Central Provincial Chief Secretary andDirector General of Combined Services

ii.Duration of the course:The notification for calling applications relevant to the trainingcourse prescribed for Grade I shall be published in the firstquarter of every year and the number of training courseconducted shall be determined in considering the number ofapplications received.

iii.Syllabus of the course
