Government Notifications

PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Southern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Southern Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership ofthe Southern Province Provincial Council by reason of Resignation of Mr. Arachchige Krishantha PushpakumaraWeerasinghe ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which theformer member Arachchige Krishantha Pushpakumara Weerasinghe belonged was required by the Election Commissionunder Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has nominated Mr. Gardiyes Deneththi tofill such vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Gardiyes Deneththi, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the AdministrativeDistrict of Galle in respect of which such vacancy occured.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYAN. J. ABEYSEKERES. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLEChairmanMemberMemberElection CommissionElection CommissionElection Commission
