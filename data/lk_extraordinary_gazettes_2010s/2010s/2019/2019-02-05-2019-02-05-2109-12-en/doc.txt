Notice under Section 7

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section 7 of the LandAcquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intendsto acquire the following land/servitude which is required for apublic purpose:

Description of the Land or Servitude to be acquired:

Allotment of land called in extent 0.4079 of Hectare shownas Lot 1 in P.P.Tri 560 dated 23.10.2018 prepared by theSurveyor General Situated in within the Urban Council Limitsof Trincomalee No. 244P Orr's Hill Grama Niladhari Division ,Divisional Secretariat Division of Town & Gravets, TrincomaleeDistrict and bounded as follows.

Lot No. 1North Lots: Lot 4 in PPA 893, Lots 2 and 1 in PP tri 276; EastLots: Lot 2, 1 in PP Tri 276 and Lots 4 in PPA 839; South: Lot 4in PPA 839; West Lots: Lot 4 in PPA 839; Extent: 0.4079 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/lands on whicha servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appearpersonally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me atDivisional Secretariat Trincomalee on the 11th day of March2019, at 10.00 a.m. and you are hereby requested to notify to mein writing (in duplicate) on or before the 25th day of February,2019 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars oftheir claims for compensation and the details of the computationof such amount.
