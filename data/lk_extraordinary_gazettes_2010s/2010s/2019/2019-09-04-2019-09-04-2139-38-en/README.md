FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GODAKAWELAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member in Godakawela Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to bemember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United Progressive Alliance (Democratic People’s Front at Present), therecognized political party to which Rajamanikkam Asok Kumar the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of United Progressive Alliance (DemocraticPeople’s Front at Present) who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Annamalai Bothimathavan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Suranga Ambagahathenne, Returning Officer of the Godakawela Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatAnnamalai Bothimathavan was selected as the member of the Godakawela Pradeshiya Sabha.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATHENNE,Returning Officer,Godakawela Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Ratnapura,02nd September, 2019.
