THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No.: Kadu /Land /01 /134 /CEB.Land Ministry Reference No: 4-3/2/2016/CEB/23.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the LandAcquisition (Ammendment) Act No.28 of 1964, that theGovernment has intends to acquire the undermentioned land/Servitude for a public purpose.

Description of the Land or Servitude to be Acquired:

Two allotments of Lands as depicted in the names and extentsin the Preliminary Plan No. fld 9811 prepared by theSurveyor General, and situated in the village of Malabe inthe Grama Niladhari Division No. 476/A, Malabe West in theDivision of the Divisional Secretary of Kaduwela in ColomboDistrict of the Western Province in extent of 0.4199 hectaresas describe in below.

Preliminary Plan No. fld 9811 - Lot description

1. Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Ataheraliyagahakumbura; Description ofLand: Paddy field; Claimant: Government; Original Claimant:Mrs. H. Lilawathi Silva and others, No. 148/4, '' Sethsiri",Thalahena, Malabe.

2. Lot No. 02Name of the Land: Ataheraliyagahakumbura; Description ofLand: Paddy field ( Fixed in two high voltage towers); Claimant:Government; Original Claimant: Mr. S.L. Suriyaarachchi,Mrs. A.A.R.Swarna Perera, Mrs. A.A.R. Indra Kanthi, ''Ramya'', Pore, Athurugiriya.

All persons interested, possessed the right or with anyother relation in the aforesaid lands/servitude are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at Kaduwela Divisional Secretariat, onthe day of the 26th June 2019 at 10.00 a.m. Further, they arehereby requested to notify to me in writing with two copieson or before 19.06.2019 the nature of their interests in theland /servitude, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said lands, the amountof compensation and the details of the computation of suchamount.
