THE NOTICE TO RECTIFY THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALISTREPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA BEARING NO. 2129/94 DATED 28TH JUNE, 2019

I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me underthe Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended, do hereby rectify the Extraordinary Gazette Notificationbearing No. 2129/94 dated 28th June, 2019 (07-4553/2).

In the caption of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification “NOTICE OF” Shall be inserted at thebeginning of the caption and the word “(PART)” shall be included immediately after “WATTALA PRADESHIYA SABHA”and “KELANIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA” and “URBAN DEVELOPMENT” shall be included after “COLOMBOCOMMERCIAL CITY.”

The content of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting the word “(Part)”immediately after “Wattala Pradeshiya Sabha” and “Kelaniya Pradeshiya Sabha” and “Urban Development” shall be includedafter “Colombo Commercial City.”

The word “had” in the fourth line of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be substituted with “have”.

The rectifications introduced to the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be read as follows;
