5th AMENDMENT

Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Architects Service

THE Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Architects Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1877/28 dated 28th ofAugust, 2014 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as amended by the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1882/18 dated01st of October 2014, No. 1952/20 dated 02nd of February 2016, No.1994/68 dated 24th of November 2016 and No. 2085/46dated 24th of August 2018 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka is amended as follows and these amendedprovisions will be made effective from 26th of February 2019, the date on which the Cabinet of Ministers made the policydecision.

By order of the Public Service Commission,

M. A. B. DAYA SENARATH,Secretary,Public Service Commission.

07th August, 2019.
