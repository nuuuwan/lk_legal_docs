FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KARANDENIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which IlandariDewa Samantha Kumar the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mohomad Kiyas Mohomad Siras to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanda Uda Chandralal, Returning Officer of the Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that MohomadKiyas Mohomad Siras was selected as the member of the Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. U. CHANDRALAL,Returning Officer,Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Galle,17th June, 2019.
