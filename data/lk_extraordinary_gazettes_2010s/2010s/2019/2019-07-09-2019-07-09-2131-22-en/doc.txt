THE JUDICATURE ACT, No. 2 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978, I, Thalatha Atukorale, Minister ofJustice and Prison Reforms in consultation with the Chief Justice and the President of the Court of Appeal, do bythis Order, amend the Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1679/40 of November 10, 2010 by thesubstitution for the words and figures “No. 43/4 of July 2, 1979” appearing in item (g) of that Order of the words andfigures “No. 43/3 of July 2, 1979”.

THALATHA ATUKORALE,Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms.

03rd July, 2019,Colombo.
