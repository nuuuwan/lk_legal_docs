Ordinance

1. This Ordinance may be cited as the Postgraduate Institute of Medicine (Amendment) Ordinance No. 01 of 2019 andshall come into operation on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

2. (1) by the repeal of paragraph 6 thereof and the substitution therefor of the following -

“6. The officers of the institute shall be the following :-

(a) the Director of the Institute (hereinafter referred to as the “Director”) ;

“10. (1) The Board of Management (hereinafter referred to as the “Board”) shall be theacademic and executive body of the Institute, and shall consist of :-

(b)the following other members :-

(i)one member each nominated by the Faculty Board of the Faculties of Medicine,Medical Sciences, Medical and Allied Sciences, Health Care Sciences of theUniversities established under the Act, as elected by the Faculty Board of each suchFaculty, from among the Senior Professors, Professors, and Associate Professorsin the permanent cadre of each Faculty ;
