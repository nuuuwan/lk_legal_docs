UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on the recommendation of the UniversityGrants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, publishedin Gazette Extraordinary No. 1400/24 of July 08, 2005 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1686/22 of December 29, 2010), establishing the University of the Visual and Performing Arts, by the additonimmediately after the entry “6. Department of Musicology” in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item“Faculty of Music” in Column I of that Schedule, of the following entry :-

“7. Department of English Language Teaching”.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,03rd June, 2019.
