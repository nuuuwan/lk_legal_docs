SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order imposein respect of the commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy at the ratespecified in the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from February 09, 2019, subject to provisionsof Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.
