FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

The Order under Section 3 that has to be read with Section 64(1) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451), asNo. 13 of 1966, as amended by Acts, No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of2009 that have to be read with Section 51 of the 19th amendment of the Constitution of Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka.

The Order

By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) as amendedby Acts, No. 13 of 1966, No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, that haveto be read with Section 51 of the 19th Amendment of the Constitution of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, IMaithripala Sirisena, President do by this Order declare the Katagamuwa Demeda Mukalana Forest of 62.583 hectaresbounded by the limit set forth in the Schedule hereto as Katagamuwa Demeda Mukalana Reserved Forest from 18th day ofMarch 2019.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Colombo,18th March, 2019.
