L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on the recommendation of the UniversityGrants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act andpublished in Gazette Extraordinary No. 896/2 of November 07, 1995 (as last amended by Order published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 2079/13 of July 09, 2018), which specifies the Faculties and Departments of the Raja Rata Universityof Sri Lanka, by the addition immediately after the entry ‘‘6. Department of English Language Teaching’’ appearing inColumn II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item “Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities” appearing inColumn I of that Schedule of the following entry :-

“7. Department of Economics’’.A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supplyand Higher Education.

Colombo,06th March, 2019.
