FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KARANDENIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Karandeniya pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichBodiya Baduge Lalith Rathnakumara Gamini Perera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) ofthe aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selectedas a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thommaya Hakuru Nirosh Sanjeewa Ranasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanda Uda Chandralal, Returning Officer of the Karandeniya pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ThommayaHakuru Nirosh Sanjeewa Ranasinghe was selected as the member of the Karandeniya pradeshiya Sabha.

K. U. CHANDRALAL,Returning Officer,Karandeniya pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Galle,30th May, 2019.
