Government Notifications

PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS ACT, No. 1 OF 1981

Filling of a Vacancy under Section 64(5)

WHEREAS the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission under Section 64(5) of the ParliamentaryElections Act, No. 1 of 1981, as amended by Section 6 of the Elections (Special Provisions) Act, No. 35 of 1988, that a vacancyhas occurred in the membership of the Eighth Parliament by reason of the resignation of Mr. Alim Muhammad HizbullahMuhammad Lebbe being a member nominated by the United People’s Freedom Alliance Party, a recognized political party,and declared elected as a Member of Parliament under Article 99A of the Constitution ;

And whereas the Secretary of that Political Party was required by the Election Commission under the aforesaidSection to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid Political Party, has nominated Mr. Kuda Vidanalage Shantha Bandara tofill such vacancy ;

Now therefore, the Election Commission, in the exercise of the powers conferred on the Commission by Section 64(5)of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 to be read together with the provisions of the Nineteenth Amendment to theConstitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby declare Mr. Kuda Vidanalage Shantha Bandara,elected as a member of the Eighth Parliament to fill the vacancy caused by the resignation of aforesaid Mr. Alim MuhammadHizbullah Muhammad Lebbe.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYAN. J. ABEYSEKERES. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLEChairmanMemberMemberElection CommissionElection CommissionElection Commission
