SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose inrespect of the commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy at the rate specifiedin the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 3 Months commencing from March 03, 2019, subject to provisions ofSub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.
