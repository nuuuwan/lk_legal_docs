TOURISM ACT, No. 38 OF 2005

Order under Section 26(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Sub section (1) of Section 26 of the Tourism Act, No. 38 of 2005, I, JohnAnthony Emmanual Amarathunga, Minister of Tourism Development, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs, do by thisOrder declare the area specified in the Schedule hereto to be a Tourist Development Area for the purpose of the aforesaidAct.

JOHN ANTHONY EMMANUAL AMARATHUNGA,Minister of Tourism Development,Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs.Colombo,22nd October, 2019.

SCHEDULE

BOUNDARIES OF THE IRANAVILA TOURISM DEVELOPMENT ZONE

All that area of land depicted in the Preliminary Plan. mQ' 2937 and mQ' 3021 of the Survey General containing138.125 ha. and 27.913 ha. respectively in the villages of Iranavila, Galahitiyawa and Thuduwawa North of the IranavilaWestern and East Grama Niladhari divisions in the Mahawewa Divisional Secretariat Division of the Puttalam District inthe North Western Province.

North:Iranavila Estate belongs to the Land Reform Commission ;East:Galahitiyawa village and Lunu Oya ;South:Thoduwawa North ;West:This land belongs to Private Parties distributed by Land Reform Commission.
