THE NOTICE TO RECTIFY THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST

REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA BEARING No. 2129/95 DATED 28TH JUNE, 2019

I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me underthe Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended, do hereby rectify the Extraordinary Gazette Notificationbearing No. 2129/95 dated 28th June, 2019 (07-4554/8).

In the caption of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification the word “(PART)” Shall be includedimmediately after “EMBILIPITIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA” and the word “AUTHORITY” (given in the second line)shall be deleted.

The content of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting the word “(Part)”immediately after “Embilipitiya Pradeshiya Sabha” given in the fifth line thereof.

The word “had” in the fourth line of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be substituted with “have”.

The rectifications introduced to the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be read as follows;

“NOTICE OF APPROVAL OF THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN FOR THE EMBILIPITIYA URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREACOMPRISING OF EMBILIPITIYA URBAN COUNCIL AND EMBILIPITIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA (PART) AREAS.”

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.
