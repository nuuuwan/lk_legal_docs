FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NEGOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Negombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group 03, the Independent Group to which Rodriguge SunilWilfred Silva the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturned member of Independent Group 03 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateWarnakulasuriya Shamal Harshana Fernando to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the NegomboMunicipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Warnakulasuriya Shamal Harshana Fernando was selected as the member of the Negombo MunicipalCouncil.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Negombo Municipal Council.District Election Office,Gampaha,29th of November, 2019.
