FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALVETTITHURAI URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Valvettithurai Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichKandaiya Perananthan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thurairasa Sritharan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valvettithurai Urban Council, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ThurairasaSritharan was selected as the member of the Valvettithurai Urban Council.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valvettithurai Urban Council.District Election Office,Jaffna,25th June, 2019.
