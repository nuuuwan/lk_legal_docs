The minimum rates of wages for piece work

The minimum rates of the wages for piece work for Beedi Rolling (Inclusive of the preparation of wrappers for rolling,cutting, wrapping, leaves, filling wrappers with "Beedi Tobacco", Beedi Rolling and Tying rolled Beedies) shall be:
