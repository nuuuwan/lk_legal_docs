SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy at the rate specifiedin the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from September11th, 2019, subject to provisionsof Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2130/20 of 03.07.2019 in respect of Black gram (HS Codes 0713.31.29) isrescinded with effect from September 11, 2019.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,September 10, 2019.
