THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

REVOCATION UNDER SECTION 39(1)

THe allotment of land described in detail in the Order No. 189 of2002 dated 11.11.2002 made under the Proviso (a) to Section 38 of theLand Acquisition Act (Chapter 460) and published in the Gazette ExtraOrdinary No. 1262/26 dated 15.11.2002 is vested in the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

But the possession of the land described below has not yet beentaken for and on behalf of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka. Therefore, I Gayantha Karunatilleke, Minister of Lands andParliamentary Reforms, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 39 (1) of the Land Acquisition Act, do hereby revokeallotments described below, which are included in the aforesaid OrderNo. 189 of 2002 dated 11.11.2002 published in the Gazette ExtraOrdinary No. 1262/26 dated 15.11.2002.

Gayantha Karunatilleke,Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms.
