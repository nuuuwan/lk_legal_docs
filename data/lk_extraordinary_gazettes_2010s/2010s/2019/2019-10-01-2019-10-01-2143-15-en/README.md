FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AKURANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Akurana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party towhich Abdul Majeed Mohomed Simsan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Pahala Walauwe Abdul Azeez Hajju Mohideen to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thalangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of the AkuranaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Pahala Walauwe Abdul Azeez Hajju Mohideen was selected as the member of the Akurana PradeshiyaSabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Akurana Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,30th September, 2019.
