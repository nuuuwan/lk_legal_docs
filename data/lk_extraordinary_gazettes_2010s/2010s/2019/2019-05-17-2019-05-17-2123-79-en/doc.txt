EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE POINT PEDRO URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Point Pedro Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichAynkarathas Ilavarasi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Vasanthy Sivakadadsam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Point Pedro Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatVasanthy Sivakadadsam was selected as the member of the Point Pedro Urban Council.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Point Pedro Urban Council.District Election Office,Jaffna,16th May, 2019.
