NOTICE OF APPROVAL FOR RECTIFICATION OF THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTENOTIFICATION

NOTICE is given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka that I, Patali ChampikaRanawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8H(1) ofthe Urban Development Authority Law No. 4 of 1982 as amended, hereby approve the rectification in the Sinhala versionof the Extraordinary Gazette Notification bearing No. 1396/7 dated 7th June, 2005.

Primary Residential zone under the specified Land uses zones category set forth in Section 10.4.1.1(xv) of theDevelopment Plan Regulation for Kalutara shall be rectified to provide for a floor area not exceeding 250 sq. Meters.

Mixed Residential zone under the specified Land uses zones category set forth in Section 10.4.2.1(ix) of theDevelopment Plan Regulation for Kalutara shall be rectified to provide for a floor area not exceeding 500 Sq. Meters.

This rectification shall be deemed to have come into operation on 7th June, 2005 and accordingly, every actdone or omitted to done and every order made in pursuance of any such provision of regulation shall be deemed to havebeen valid and effectual as if the provisions of the said regulation had been in operation from the dates hereinbeforementioned.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17 & 18 Floors,‘Suhurupaya’,Battaramulla,25th June 2019.
