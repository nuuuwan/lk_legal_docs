EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HANGURANKETHAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Hanguranketha Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofDeath ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which KoswaththeMankada Gedara Muthubanda the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Alawathupitiye Gedara Tikiri Banda to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pulasthi Pathirana, Returning Officer of the Hanguranketha Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AlawathupitiyeGedara Tikiri Banda was selected as the member of the Hanguranketha Pradeshiya Sabha.

PULASTHI PATHIRANA,Returning Officer,Hanguranketha Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Nuwara Eliya,11th February, 2019.
