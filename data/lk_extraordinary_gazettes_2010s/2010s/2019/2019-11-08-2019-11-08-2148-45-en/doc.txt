EXTRAORDINARY

THE NATIONAL THOROUGHFARES ACT, No. 40 OF 2008

Order under Section 11

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 11 of the National Thoroughfares Act, No. 40 of 2008, I, Kabir Hashim, Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, do by this Order designate the National Highway which was designated as an "Expressway" by Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2148/ 42 of 08.11.2019 and speciﬁ ed in the Schedule to this Order to be an "User Fee National Highway".

KABIR HASHIM, Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development.Battaramulla,08th November, 2019. SCHEDULE

AREA COMPRISING THE USER FEE NATIONAL HIGHWAY

The stretch of road being 16.558 kilometers long and 70.000 metres broad, situated between the Andarawewa System Interchange (0.000km) and Hambantota (16.558km) of the Expressway, designated as the Andarawewa - Hambantota National Highway.
