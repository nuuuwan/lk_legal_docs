FILLING OF A VACANCY UNDER PARAGRAPH 13(b) OF ARTICLE 99 OF THE CONSTITUTIONOF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64(1) of theParliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, as amended by the Act, No. 15 of 1988, that a vacancy has occurred in themembership of the Eighth Parliament by reason of death of Mr. Gajadeera Vidhanearachchige Bans Chandrasiri, who wasdeclared elected as a Member of Parliament for the Electoral District No. 8 - Matara under Section 62 of the aforesaidAct.

AND WHEREAS the Returning Officer, for the electoral district which returned the said member was directedby the Election Commission under Section 64(1) of the aforesaid Act to fill this vacancy as provided for under paragraph13(b) of Article 99 of the Constitution.

AND WHEREAS the said Returning Officer acting under Section 64(2) of the aforesaid Act has made a return inForm “M” set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. Manoj Prasanga Hewagampalage wasdeclared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said electoral district.
