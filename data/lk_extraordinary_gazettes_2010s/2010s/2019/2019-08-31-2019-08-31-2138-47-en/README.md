FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATTANKUDY URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kattankudy Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front For Good Governance, the recognized political party towhich Haneefa Rakeefa the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of National Front For Good Governance who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Abdul Hameedu Jemhooth Niza to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Kattankudy Urban Council, acting in termsof Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Abdul HameeduJemhooth Niza was selected as the member of the Kattankudy Urban Council.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Kattankudy Urban Council.District Election Office,Batticaloa,30th August, 2019.
