FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WARAKAPOLAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Kotuwegoda Palliyaguruge Danusha NawodhanaKumara Kotuwegoda the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Independent Group who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateGamlath Arachchillage Wasantha Sisira Kumara Samaratunga to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that GamlathArachchillage Wasantha Sisira Kumara Samaratunga was selected as the member of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kegalle,27th May, 2019.
