FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KINNIYA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kinniya Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front For Good Governance, the recognized political party towhich Abdul Majeedu Anees the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of National Front For Good Governance who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Umer Ali Ranees to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sangarapillai Suthakaran, Returning Officer of the Kinniya Urban Council, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Umer AliRanees was selected as the member of the Kinniya Urban Council.

S. SUTHAKARAN,Returning Officer,Kinniya Urban Council.District Election Office,Trincomalee,26th August, 2019.
