CIVIL AVIATION ACT, No. 14 OF 2010

Order under Section 6

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Subsection (2) of section 6 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010, I, ArjunaRanatunga, Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, do by this Order, amend the Order made under section 6 of theaforesaid Act, and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1922/4 of July 7th, 2015, by the substitution for thewords, “Mattala Rajapakse International Airport, Mattala, Hambantota” of the words “the Mattala Rajapakse InternationalAirport, Mattala, Hambantota, the Colombo International Airport, Ratmalana, the Jaffna International Airport and theBatticaloa International Airport”.

ARJUNA RANATUNGA,Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation.

Colombo,11th October, 2019.
