THE SPORTS LAW No. 25 OF 1973

ORDER made by the Minister of Sports under Sections 32 and 33 of the Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973.

HARIN FERNANDO,Minister of Telecommunication,Foreign Employment and Sports.

Colombo,31st December, 2018.

Order under Sections 32 and 33

I, Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports by virtue of powersvested in me under Sections 32 (b) and 33 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and it’s Amendments do hereby temporarysuspend the registration of the Sri Lanka Cricket which is the National Association for Cricket and as an interimarrangement, appoint Mr. Wedikkara Arachchige Chulananda Perera, Secretary to the Ministry of Telecommunication,Foreign Employment and Sports as Competent Authority with effect from 31st of December 2018 to continue withadministration and perform any such other functions necessary for the due administration of Sri Lanka Cricket until theoffice bearers are elected from the election.

Gazette Notification bearing No. 2094/ 37 dated 26.10.2018 is hereby rescinded with effect from 31st of December2018.
