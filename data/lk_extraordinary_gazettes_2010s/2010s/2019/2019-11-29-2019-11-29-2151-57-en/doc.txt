FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE POLONNARUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichD. Lahiru Ashen the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate H. M. Sunil Kulathilaka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Leelanath Wickrama Arachchi, Returning Officer of the Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatH. M. Sunil Kulathilaka was selected as the member of the Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

LEELANATH WICKRAMA ARACHCHI,Returning Officer,Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Polonnaruwa,27th of November, 2019.
