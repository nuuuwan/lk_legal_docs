FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MATALE PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Matale Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending threeconsecutive meeting of the council without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which KonaraMudiyanselage Gewaththe Gedara Chaminda Kumara Konara the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to suchparty and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredselected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Palle Kankanamage Don Chandana to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Prathapasingha Arachchige Kumarini Dasanthi, Returning Officer of the Matale PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Palle Kankanamage Don Chandana was selected as the member of the Matale Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. A. K. DASANTHI,Returning Officer,Matale Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Matale,08th October, 2019.
