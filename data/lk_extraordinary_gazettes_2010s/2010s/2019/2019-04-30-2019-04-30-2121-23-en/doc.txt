FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM NORTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of notattending three consecutive meetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichPakkiyaraja Gobinath the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Tamil United Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Selvarathinarasa Ajeethkumar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendran Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam North PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Selvarathinarasa Ajeethkumar was selected as the member of the Valikamam North Pradeshisya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,29th April, 2019.
