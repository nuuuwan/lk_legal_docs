REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Dodampahalage Antony Nimal Fernando who resided in the village of Moratuwa, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Moratuwa, in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Dodampahalage Antony Nimal Fernando described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 08th February, 2019 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
