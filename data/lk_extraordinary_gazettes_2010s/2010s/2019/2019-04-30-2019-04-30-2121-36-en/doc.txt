AMENDMENT OF CHAPTER 7.3 OF THE SERVICE MINUTE OF THESRI LANKA SURVEYORS’ SERVICE

THE Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Surveyors’ Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1434/5 dated 27.02.2006 as amended by the Gazettes Extraordinary of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1482/17 dated 31.01.2007, No. 1509/15 dated 10.08.2007, No. 1623/13 dated15.10.2009, No. 1654/13 dated 20.05.2010, No. 1675/20 dated 12.10.2010 and No. 2042/12 dated 24.10.2017 shouldbe amended as follows.

By order of the Public Service Commission,

M.A.B. DAYA SENARATH,Secretary,Public Service Commission.Public Service Commission,No.1200/9, Rajamalwatta Road,Battaramulla,08th of April 2019.

7.3 “The officers serving as Assistant Superintendents of Survey/Surveyors in Class II/II will be promoted toClass II/I without any prejudice to the seniority of other Assistant Superintendents of Survey and with no further right toappointment in Class I if they have not passed the 2nd Efficiency Bar and have completed a satisfactory service during aperiod of immediately preceding 5 years at the time such officers complete a service of 10 years. This concession isapplicable only for the officers serving as Assistant Superintendents of Survey/Surveyors as at 01.08.2005, the date onwhich this Service Minute takes effect.”

The amendment will come into force only up to 24.12.2018, the date on which the Service Minute of the SriLanka Surveyors’ Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri LankaNo.2103/2 dated 24.12.2018 takes effect.
