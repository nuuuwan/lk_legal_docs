FILLING OF A VACANCY UNDER PARAGRAPH (13)(b) OF ARTILCE 99 OF THECONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPULIC OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission under Section 64 (1) of theParliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, as amended by the Act, No. 15 of 1988, that a vacancy has occurred in themembership of the Eighth Parliament by reason of death of Mr. Thirimadura Ranjith De Zoysa, who was declaredelected as a Member of Parliament for the Electoral District No. 21 - Rathnapura under Section 62 of the aforesaid Act ;

AND WHEREAS the Returning Officer, for the electoral district which returned the said member was directed bythe Electioin Commission under Section 64(1) of the aforesaid Act to fill this vacancy as provided for under Pararaph(13)(b) of Article 99 of the Constitution ;

AND WHEREAS the said Returning Officer acting under Section 64(2) of the aforesaid Act has made a return inForm “M” set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. Udagama Liyanage Waruna PriyanthaLiyanage was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said electoral distict ;

NOW THEREFORE , we the Election Commission, in the exercise of the powers vested in the Election Commissionby Section 64 (2) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, which shall be read with the provisions of theNineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby notify thegeneral public that Mr. Udagama Liyanage Waruna Priyantha Liyanage, has been declared elected as a Member of theEighth Parliament for the Electoral District No. 21 - Rathnapura to fill the vacancy caused by reason of death ofMr. Thirimadura Ranjith De Zoysa.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYAN. J. ABEYSEKERES. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLEChairmanMemberMemberElection CommissionElection CommissionElection Commission

At the Election Commission,Rajagiriya,12th December, 2019.
