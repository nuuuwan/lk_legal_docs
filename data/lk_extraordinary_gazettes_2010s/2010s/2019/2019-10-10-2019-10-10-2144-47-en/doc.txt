Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) Ltd., has been appointed as the Service Provider for provisions ofaeronautical services specified under paragraph (a), (c), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h), (j), (k) and (I) of Section 31 of the CivilAviation Act, No. 14 of 2010 at those airports.

Complete information relation to physical characteristics of the abovementioned airports and the facilities, andservices available at those airports including the operational hours of the airports has been provided in the Sri LankaAeronautical Information Publication (AIP), which is published in the official website of the Civil Aviation Authority ofSri Lanka (www.caa.lk).

Aircraft arriving in Sri Lanka or departing to overseas destinations may land firstly at or depart lastly from theabove-mentioned Airports and such aircraft may disembark and embark, passengers, cargo and mail as applicable on suchflights at those airport.

Every aircraft operating into and out of the abovementioned airports shall conform to the applicable nationallaws, regulations, rules and implementing standards relating to border controls and the established operating proceduresrelating to entry and departure of aircraft, which are published in the AIP.

Every departing and arriving passenger, in and out of the abovementioned airports shall conform to the applicablenational laws, regulations, rules and implementing standards and also the established operating procedures relating toentry and departure of persons and baggage and they may be subject to the applicable screening processes that will beperformed by the respective government authorities in charge of customs, immigration and emigration, aviation security,public health, plant and animal quarantine and narcotics control etc.

All inbound or outbound cargo includng mail and other articles of the abovementioned airports shall be inconformity with the applicable national laws, regulations, rules and implementing standards, and also the establishedoperating procedures relating to entry and departure of cargo, mail and other articles which are published in Sri LankaAeronautical Information Publication and such cargo, mail and other articles may be subject to the applicable screeningprocesses that will be performed by the respective government authorities in charge of customs, aviation security, publichealth, plant and animal quarantine and narcotics control etc.

No person inclusive of any item carried by that person or vehicle is permitted enter or remain in any area whichis designated as Security Restricted Area of the aforementioned airports unless such person or vehicle has been issuedwith a valid airpot access permit by the Airport Operator and the access permit is displayed conspicuously for inspection.The foregoing condition is not applicable to a passenger holding valid travel documents and a current boarding pass for aninbound flight terminating at or outbound flight originating from those airports, if the passengers complies with thesecurity screening procedures.

Operation of unmanned aerial vehicles including drones within and in the vicinity of the airport premises iscompletely prohibited
