(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, P. S. M. Charles, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 15th April, 2019, all duties of Customs as wellas other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid and received atthe Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2118/3 of 08th April, 2019is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notification to be publishedon or before 21st April, 2019.

P. S. M. CHARLES,Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,12th April, 2019.
