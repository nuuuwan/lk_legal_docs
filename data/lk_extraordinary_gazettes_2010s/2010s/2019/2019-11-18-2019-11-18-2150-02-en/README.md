THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No.: Kadu/Land/03/146/UDA.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the LandAcquisition (Amendment) Act No.28 of 1964, that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land/servitudewhich is required for a public purpose.

Description of the Land/servitude to be Acquired:

Two (02) allotments of land in extent of 0.3221 Hectaresdescribed below and depicted as Lot No.1 and 2 in PreliminaryPlan Co.9830 dated 03.10.2019 prepared by Surveyor Generalsituated in the village of Battaramulla (within the MunicipalCouncil limits of Kaduwela) in 492, Subuthipura GramaNiladhari Division, Divisional Secretariat Division ofKaduwela, Colombo District, Western Province.

Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Godellawatta alias Millagahawatta andKottanuwe Kumbura; Description: Open waste land;Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Urban DevelopmentAuthority;Extent: 0.0060 Hec.

Lot No. 02Name of the Land: Godellawatta alias Millagahawatta andKottanuwe Kumbura; Description: Open waste land;Claimant: State; Original Claimant: N.S. Senewirathne, St.DoraPrivate Hospital, Kottawa, Battaramulla;Extent: 0.3161 Hec.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude arehereby required to appear personally or by agents dulyauthorized in writing, before me at the Divisional Secretariat,Kaduwela on 27th November, 2019 at 10.00 a.m. and arehereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on orbefore the 20th November, 2019 the nature of their interestsin the land/servitude, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude,the amount of compensation and the details of thecomputation of such amount. All deeds and other documentsin suport of the claims should be produced at the inquiry.

A.D.Y.ANANDANI, Divisional Secretary,
