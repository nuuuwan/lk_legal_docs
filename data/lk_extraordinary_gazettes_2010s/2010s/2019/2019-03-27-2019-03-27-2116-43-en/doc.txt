L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on the recommendation of the University GrantsCommission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1518/15 ofOctober 09, 2007 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2023/32 of June 14, 2017) whichspecifies the Faculties and Departments of the University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisionsof Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the additionimmediately after the entry “16. Department of Law” appearing in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to theitem “Faculty of Arts” appearing in Column I of that Schedule, of the following entries :-

“17.Department of English Language Teaching18.Department of Media Studies.”.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,21st March, 2019.
