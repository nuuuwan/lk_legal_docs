WELFARE BENEFIT ACT, No. 24 OF 2002

BY virtue of the power vested in me under Section 09 of the Welfare Benefit Act, No. 24 of 2002, I, Mangala Samaraweera,Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby amend the effective date ofimplementation of methodology to identify low-income families for welfare benefit payments schemes published in theExtraordinary Gazette No. 2128/24 dated 20th June 2019 as 01st January 2020.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.

18th September, 2019.
