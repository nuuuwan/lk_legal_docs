PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS ACT No. 1 OF 1981

Filling of a vacancy under Section 64 (5)

WHEREAS the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64(5) of theParliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, as amended by Section 6 of the Elections (Special Provisions) Act,No. 35 of 1988, that a vacancy has occured in the Membership of the Eighth Parliament by reason of the resignationof Mr. Kuda Vidanalage Shantha Bandara being a member nominated by the United People’s Freedom Alliance Party,a recognized political party, and declared elected as a Member of Parliament under Article 99A of the Constitution;

And Whereas the Secretary of that Political Party was required by the Election Commission under theaforesaid Section to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid Political Party has nominated Mr. Kuda Vidanalage ShanthaBandara to fill such vacancy;

Now therefore, the Election Commission, in the exercise of the powers conferred on the Commission bySection 64(5) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 to be read together with the Provisions of theNineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby declareMr. Kuda Vidanalage Shantha Bandara, elected as a member of the Eighth Parliament to fill the vacancy caused bythe resignation of aforesaid Mr. Kuda Vidanalage Shantha Bandara.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,N. J. ABEYSEKERE,S. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLE,Chairman,Member,Member,Election Commission.Election Commission.Election Commission.

At the Election Commission,Rajagiriya,14th September, 2019.
