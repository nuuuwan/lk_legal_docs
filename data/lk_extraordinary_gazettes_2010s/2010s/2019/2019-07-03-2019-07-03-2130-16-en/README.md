IMPLEMENTATION OF ALL THE POWERS IN THE DISTRIBUTION OF FILMS BY NATIONALFILM CORPORATION OF SRI LANKA, WHICH HAVE BEEN VESTED IN NATIONAL FILMCORPORATION OF SRI LANKA BY NATIONAL FILM CORPORATION OF SRI LANKA ACT,No. 47 OF 1971 AMENDED BY ACT, No. 45 OF 1980

NOTIFICATION published by the Chairman of National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka by virtue of the powers vested inthe Chairman under Section 57 of National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka Act, No. 47 of 1971 amended by Act, No. 45 of1980

In relation to the case No. DSP 99/2018 filed by private film distribution circuits against National Film Corporation,the order of suspension, which has been issued by Court suspending the notification made by the Chairman of NationalFilm Corporation of Sri Lanka to film distribution circuits on 19.06.2018 making the agreement/understanding which hasbeen made between Corporation and distribution circuits entrusting film distribution to private circuits, null and void, hasbeen dissolved and further interim injunction requested from the Court against National Film Corporation by privatedistribution circuits has also been rejected.

By virtue of the powers vested in me as the Chairman in charge of the subject under Section 57 of National FilmCorporation of Sri Lanka Act, No. 47 of 1971 amended by Act, No. 45 of 1980, I do hereby notify that the powersimplemented so far by private film distribution circuits in the distribution of local/foreign films are cancelled from 18June 2019 based on the above litigation in order to formalize the distribution of local/foreign flim within Sri Lanka, andfurther it is hereby declared that all tasks pertaining to distribution of flims including the scheduling of films for CinemaHalls, releasing films for screening and collection of income on distribution will be performed, from that date, totally byNational Film Corporation of Sri Lanka.

ANUSHA GOKULA FERNANDO,Chairperson,The National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka.18th June, 2019.

Methodology for distribution and screening of filmsNational Film Corporation of Sri Lanka

1. Introduction

By virtue of the powers vested in the Chairman under Section 57 of National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka Act No 47 of1971 amended by Act No 45 of 1980, powers which have been entertained so far by private distribution circuits, have beencancelled from 18 June 2019 and from that date National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka performs all the functionsincluding scheduling films for cinema halls, releasing films for screening and collecting income from the distribution offilms totally as per the powers of the aforesaid acts.

2. Methodology

Distribution of films within Sri Lanka shall be performed by National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka (Hereinafter referredto as “Corporation”).

By virtue of the powers vested in the National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka under National Film Corporation ofSri Lanka Act No 47 of 1971 amended by Act No 45 of 1980, following methodologies should be in the distribution andscreening films.

2.1 By virtue of the powers vested in the Corporation under the provisions of the Act these regulations arehereby stipulated and all relevant parties should be bound to adhere to all these regulations.

2.2 Method for registration of films

2.2.1 Registration of local films

Every local film, which is expected to be screened at cinema halls, should be registered properly atthe Corporation.

The document containing the method for registration of local films is attached herewith. (Annex 01)

2.2.2 Importation of films

Registered Importers should obtain the written approval of the Chairman of the Corporation(Hereinafter referred to as “Chairman”) fulfilling all conditions as per the importation policy issuedby the Corporation to be followed before importation of films.

2.3 Local films registered in the proper manner or films imported will be scheduled by the Corporation for thepurpose of screening.

2.4 Following requirements should be satisfied for the purpose of categorisation and scheduling films.

2.4.1 Local films

2.4.1.1 Local films, which are expected to be screened, should be submitted for categorising to a localfilms evaluation board (Hereinafter referred to as “Evaluation Board”) appointed properly bythe Board of Directors of the National Film Corporation.

The document containing the composition of the Evaluation Board is attached herewith.(Annex 02)

2.4.1.2 The aforesaid Evaluation Board will categorise films in the following manner after watchingthe films submitted to the board.

* Films with classical - attractive characteristics* Films with commercial-attractive characteristics* Films with the combination of classical - attractive characteristics and commercial-attractivecharacteristics

The document containing the criteria prepared by the aforesaid Evaluation Board and applicableto the categorisation of films is attached herewith. (Annex 03)

Films which are not included in the above categorization will not be scheduled for screening atCinema Halls. A Board of Appeal for the evaluation of local films (Hereinafter referred to as“Board of Appeal”) has been appointed giving opportunity to producers for making an appeal.

The document containing the composition of the aforesaid Board of Appeal is attached herewith(Annex 04)

2.4.1.3 Local films, which are categorised by the aforesaid Evaluation Board, should be submitted tothe Public Performance Board by the producer of film.

2.4.1.4 Producer should hand over the films, to which the certificate of Public Performance Board isissued, to the Corporation for scheduling and distribution.

2.4.1.5 The films handed over by the producer (Hereinafter referred to as “Film Reserve”) will beincluded in the Films Reserve to be screened as per the order of dates in the certificate issuedby Public Performance Board.

2.4.1.6 For the purpose of obtaining recommendations for releasing films to Cinema Halls a SchedulingCommittee for local films will be appointed (Hereinafter referred to as “Scheduling Committee”).

The document containing the composition of the Scheduling Committee is attached herewith(Annex 05)

2.4.2 Imported films

2.4.2.1 Films, which are imported in terms of the importation policy of National Film Corporation,should be handed over to the National Film Corporation by the respective importer.

2.4.2.2 The films, which are handed over in the above manner, will be submitted by Corporation for theapproval of the Public Performance Board.

2.4.2.3 The films, to which the certificate of the Public Performance Board is issued, will be distributedby the Corporation.

10. The films, which are scheduled for screening through films reserve, will be maintained under each such channeland action should be taken to schedule at least 05 films under one channel. (Accordingly a waiting list of scheduled15 films should be maintained under three channels at every time.) This section will be implemented subject tothe provisions in Para 04.

11. If it is required to release, at a specific occasion, a film with limited number of copies to a certain channel for ashort period, such film can be released in a manner not to cause hindrance to the main release of the respectivechannel on the written approval of the Chairman based on the recommendations of scheduling committee andevaluation board.

12. The schedule made for screening under channel system will not be revised except on reasonable grounds andwritten approval of the Chairman of the Corporation should be obtained, if such revision is to be made. (The listcontaining such reasonable grounds is attached herewith-Annex - 09)

14. A new film will be scheduled in each channel from the film reserve within two weeks from the release of one filmout of the films scheduled under each channel. This order will be applied continually.

15. Action will be taken to screen the trailer of films scheduled under each channel at least with two films screenedpreviously and no payment should be made to a cinema hall or any other party for screening trailers.

22. Even though this methodology is implemented with a view to maintain film industry without interruption, thismethodology will be revised from time to time considering the recommendations and observations made inscientific and research aspects by the Investigation and Research Division of the Corporation on practical issuesarisen in Distribution, Marketing, Production, Research and Investigation divisions.

23. However the Corporation always considers the suggestions and views submitted logically, scientifically and withcommon agreement by all parties relevant to the field including producers, importers, directors, cinema hallowners, managers, artists and any other individual or organization , who wishes progress and enhancement of thecinema industry.

24. The Chairman of the Corporation retains all powers for revision, cancellation and determination of matters includingcirculars/guidelines issued for the purpose of implementation of the film screening and distribution methodology.

25. Any decision taken by the Chairman of the Corporation regarding any matter in relation to the methodology issuedto ensure proper distribution of films should be the final.

26. In case of any inconsistency observed among Sinhala, Tamil and English texts, Sinhala text should prevail.

13. It is required to be bound by the decisions of the Film Categorizing Committee.

14. National Film Corporation is not bound necessarily to screen every film registered at the Corporation.

SPECIFIC FILMS

Films, which contain one or several of the following features, are treated as specific films.

1. The films recommended by Evaluation Board of Local Films and Scheduling Committee of Local Films ashaving capacity for attracting larger audience.

2. Films, for which requests are made by majority of Cinema Halls for scheduling.

3. Films with timely themes.

4. Films, which won awards recognized at national or international level\

*“Specific” film - This should be a film certified by Public Performance Board for unlimited release.

General Manager,National Film Corporation.
