CIVIL PROCEDURE CODE

Notification under Section 192

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of the powers vested in the Monetary Board established under the Monetary Law Act(Chapter 422), in terms of Section 192 of the Civil Procedure Code, as amended by the Civil Procdeure Code (Amendment)Act, No. 06 of 1990, the Monetary Board has, having regard to the current rates of bank interest, determined that witheffect from 01st January 2020, the Legal Rate of Interest shall be eleven point six four per cent (11.64%) per annum.

INDRAJIT COOMARASWAMY,Chairman of the Monetary Board and Governor,Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

Central Bank of Sri Lanka,Colombo,18th December, 2019.
