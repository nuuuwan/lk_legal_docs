(Published by Authority)PART IV (A) - PROVINCIAL COUNCILS

UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Article 154E of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

I, Maithri Gunaratne, the Governor of the Uva Province, do hereby announce that, the Uva Province Provincial Councilhaving completed its five year Term, shall stand dissolved from the midnight of 08th October 2019, according to theArticle 154E of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

MAITHRI GUNARATNE,Governor of the Uva Province.

At office of the Governor of the Uva Province,07th October, 2019.
