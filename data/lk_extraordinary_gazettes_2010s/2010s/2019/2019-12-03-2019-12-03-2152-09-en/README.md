FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VAVUNIYA (TAMIL) SOUTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Vavuniya (Tamil) South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Eelam People’s Democratic Party, the recognized political party to whichSelvarasa Reegan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of Eelam People’s Democratic Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Tharmakunasingam Sujivan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Malawenna Godage Lalith Ananda, Returning Officer of the Vavuniya (Tamil) SouthPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Tharmakunasingam Sujivan was selected as the member of the Vavuniya (Tamil) South PradeshiyaSabha.

M. G. LALITH ANANDA,Returning Officer,Vavuniya (Tamil) South Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Vavuniya,02nd December, 2019.
