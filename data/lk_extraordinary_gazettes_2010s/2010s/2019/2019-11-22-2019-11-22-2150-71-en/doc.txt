SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council - 2019

SECOND SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OF PROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT, NO. 42

OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SIXTH SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OFSRI LANKA ENDED ON 26 TH SEPTEMBER, 2017.

2019

1.This permission may be cited as “Permission to disburse under second financial statement - 2019 of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council” and come in to operation from 16th, September 2018.

2.I do hereby grant premission to the Chief Secretary of this province and other accounting officers of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse the province and other accounting officers of the SabargamuwaProvincial Council to expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees ThirtyFour Billion Twenty Three Million, Four Hundred and Six Thousand One Hundred and Ninety Seven andCents Twenty Four Only (Rs. 34,023,406,197.24) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January2019 and ending 31st December, 2019, which is specified as “Financial Year 2019” in the Budget Estimate -2019, that was approved on 10.04.2019 and amended by First Supplementary Statement - 2019.

3.Accordingly the first Schedule of the said amended Gazette Notification is amended as the first schedulehereof and grant permission to disburse the same.

4.Advanced accounts limits which is specified in second schedule of Gazette Notification No. 2133/41 dated24.07.2019, is hereby amended as second schedule of this notification and grant permission to Chief Secretaryof this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil Fund.

5.This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2019 or upto the date that expires three months (03) fromthe date of summoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start thefunctions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which occurs first.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilto disburse as per the second supplementary financial statement for the year 2019, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council ofthe Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamua Provincial Council which is estimated for the service of the period beginningon 01st January, 2019 and ending 31st December, 2019, and specified in the annual financial statement - 2019 amended byFirst Supplementary Estaimate - 2019, Rupees Thirty Four Billion Twenty Three Million Four Hundred and Six ThousandOne Hundred and Ninety Seven and Cents Twenty Four only (Rs. 34,023,406,197.24) and grant permission to disburse thesame from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st December, 2019 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date ofsummoning the 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil which occurs first.

DHAMMA DISSANAYAKE,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province.

16th September, 2019.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby approve to amend the advance account limits stated in the Sixth Schedule of the Second Supplementary FinancialStatement for the year 2019, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka endedon 26th September, 2017.

Accordingly, I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council to bear the expenses subject to those limits.

This permission will be valid till 31st December, 2019 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date ofsummoning the 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil which occurs first.

DHAMMA DISSANAYAKE,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province.

16th September, 2019.
