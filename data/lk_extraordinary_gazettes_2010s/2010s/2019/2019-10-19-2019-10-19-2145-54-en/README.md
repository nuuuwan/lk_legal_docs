FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE LANKAPURA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which GarumuniGamini de Soysa the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Thewarapperuma Arachchige Indika Priyadharshana to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Leelanath Wickrama Arachchi, Returning Officer of the Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatThewarapperuma Arachchige Indika Priyadharshana was selected as the member of the Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha.

LEELANATH WICKRAMA ARACHCHI,Returning Officer,Lankapura Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Polonnaruwa,17th October, 2019.
