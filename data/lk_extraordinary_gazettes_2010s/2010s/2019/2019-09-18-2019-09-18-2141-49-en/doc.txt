LAND REFORMS ACT, No. 01 OF 1972 OF THE NATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

Order under Sub section (4) of Section 27a

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by the Sub Section (4) of Section 27a of the Land Reform Act, No. 01 of 1972 ofthe National State Assembly amended by Land Reform (Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981, which should be readwith Sub Article (2) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, GayanthaKarunatilleke, Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms do hereby :-

(a) Amend the Order made under Sub Section (2) of Section 27a of the aforesaid Act, and published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 815/10 dated 21st April, 1994 relating to the land called and known as Newchattlewatta depictedin detail in the First Schedule of this order and alienated to Janatha Estate Development Board established underState Agricultural Corporations Act, No. 11 of 1972 and alienated under the Sub Section (1) of Section 27(a) ofthe aforesaid Land Reform Act, a part of land called and known as Newchattelwatta and the aforesaid Orderspertaining to the extent of land depicted morefully in detail in the Second Schedule hereto as Newchattlewatta dueto the revocation for the establishment of an Industrial and Technological Development Supper Zone, such portionof the said order as relates to the extent of lands depicted in Second Schedule hereto which is part of the landcalled and known as Newchattlewatta.

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk1A - G 31003— 20 (09/2019)
