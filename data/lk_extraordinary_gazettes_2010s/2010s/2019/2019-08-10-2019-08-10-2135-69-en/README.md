SRI LANKA SUSTAINABLE ENERGY AUTHORITY ACT, No. 35 OF 2007

Order under Sub section (1) of Section 12

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub Section (1) of Section 12 of Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Act, No.35 of 2007, I, Ravi Karunanayake, Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development, in consultation with the Ministersin Ministry of Mahaweli Development and Environment, Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock Development, Irrigation andFisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, Ministry of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms, Ministry of PublicAdministraion, Disaster Management & Rural Economic Affairs, Ministry of Internal & Home Affairs and ProvincialCouncils & Local Government, Ministry of City Planning, Water Supply & Higher Education, Ministry of TourismDevelopment, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs, Ministry of Megapolis & Western Development and the relevantMinistries of Eastern Provincial Council to whom the subject of Environment is assigned, do by this Order declare theareas of lands described in the Schedule hereto as Energy Development Areas in the Batticaloa District for the purpose ofthe aforesaid Act.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development.

Ministry of Power, Energy and Business Development,No. 72, Ananda Kumaraswami Mawatha,Colombo 07,07th August, 2019.
