WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notification made under Section 2(2) of Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946

I, Isura Devapriya, the Chief Minister of Western Province and the Minister of Finance and Planning, Engineering Services,Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration, Economic Promotions, Power and Energy, EnvironmentalAffairs, Water Supply and Drainage and Tourism of Western Province do hereby notify that permission is granted underand by virtue of powers vested in me by provision of Section 2(2) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 thathas to be read with Section 2 of Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989, to impose andrecover the entertainment tax of 15% out of the entrance fee that is recovered for the entertainment activities as per termsof expression in Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946, that is amended by (Amendment) of Entertainment TaxAct, No. 37 of 1984, however as per provisions of Section 2(1) of Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946, theresolution that has been passed in the meeting held on 19.07.2018 in the Pradeshiya Sabha of Homagama, to impose andrecover 10% of the entertainment tax, for the displays those are displayed in the cinema halls, which fall within theadministrative limit of Homagama Pradeshiya Sabhawa.

ISURA DEVAPRIYA, Chief Minister of Western Provincial Council, Minister of Finance and Planning, Engineering Services,Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration,Economic Promotions, Power and Energy, Environmental Affairs,Water Supply and Drainage and Tourism of Western Province.

No. 204, Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha,Battaramulla,on this 18th day of April, 2019.

Notice published under Section 2(2) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946
