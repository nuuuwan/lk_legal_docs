WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Inquiry conducted under Section 184(1a) of the Urban Councils Ordinance read with Section 2 of theProvincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

I, Wijayapala Sri Narayana Wasala Bandaranayake Mohottalalage Seetha Kumari Arambepola, the Governor of WesternProvince do hereby appoint the retired Court of Appeal Judge Mr. Chandradasa Nanayakkara according to the powersvested in me as per provisions of Section 184(1a) of the Urban Councils Ordinance read with Section two of the ProvincialCouncils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 to inquire into the following matters/allegations and revealwhether the offences stipulated under Subsection 184(1) of the above said ordinance have been committed by Wattala-Mabola Urban Council and by Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council while functioning in his office, and submit mea report within a period of 03 months.

Matters/allegations to be inquired in relation to Wattala-Mabola Urban Council :-

I. Wattala-Mabola Urban Council in its functioning during the period of 21.05.2018 to 05.03.2019 or aperiod adjacent to it, committing an offence chargeable under Section 184(1)(b) of Urban CouncilsOrdinance by causing to sell, contrary to provisions of Section 36 of the Urban Councils Ordinance, theland in possession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing 79 Perches in extent or an extent equal tothat marked as Lot No. 1A2 in the Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January 2018 made by Licensed SurveyorD. A. Weerakkody.

II. Wattala-Mabola Urban Council in its functioning on 05.03.2019 or on a date adjacent to it, committing anoffence chargeable under Section 184(1)(d) of Urban Councils Ordinance by causing to sell, under anundervalued consideration and external to the prescribed tender procedure, the land in possession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing 79 Perches in extent or an extent equal to that marked as Lot No. 1A2 inthe Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January 2018 made by Licensed Surveyor D. A. Weerakkody, and therebycausing a loss to the Urban Councils Fund.

Matters/allegations to be inquired in relation to the Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council-

I. While functioning as the Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council, committing an offence chargeableunder Section 184(1)(e) of the Urban Councils Ordinance during the period of 21.05.2018 to 13.07.2018or during a period adjacent to it by taking action to obtain an undervalued consideration to the land inpossession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing 79 Perches in extent or an extent equivalent tothat marked as Lot No. 1A2 of the Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January 2018 made by Licensed SurveyorD. A. Weerakkody, stating that the said land will be sold on deed of transfer to Melco Oxygen Company Ltd.

II. While functioning as the Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council, committing an offence chargeableunder Section 184(1)(b) of the Urban Councils Ordinance on 01.03.2019 or on a date adjacemt to it bytaking action to sell the land in possession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing 79 Perches inextent or an extent equivalent to that marked as Lot No. 1A2 of the Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January2018 made by Licensed Surveyor D. A. Weerakkody, to Mohamed Subeir Fauzal Haq residing at No. 28,Bagathale Road, Colombo 03 external to proper tender procedure.

III. While functioning as the Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council, committing an offence chargeableunder Section 184(1)(b) of Urban Councils Ordinance on 01.03.2019 or on a date adjacent to it by takingaction to issue the letter of acceptance expressing consent to sell the land in possession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing an extent of 79 Perches or of an extent equivalent to such marked as LotNo. 1A2 of the Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January 2018 made by Licensed Surveyor D. A. Weerakkody, toMohamed Subeir Fauzal Haq residing at No. 28, Bagathale Road, Colombo 03 without a resolution passedby Wattala-Mabola Urban Council.

IV. While functioning as the Chairman of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council, committing an offence chargeableunder Section 184(1)(c) of the Urban Councils Ordinance during the period of 17.02.2019 to 01.03.2019or during a period adjacent to it by taking action without considering the instructions and/or directivesdated 07.02.2019 of the Minister in Charge of the subject of Local Government of Western Provincerelating to the sale of land in possession of Wattala-Mabola Urban Council containing 79 Perches inextent or an extent equivalent to that marked as Lot No. 1A2 of the Plan No. 1377C dated 17th January2018 made by Licensed Surveyor D. A. Weerakkody.

Furthermore, according to the provisions of Section 184(1a) of the Urban Councils Ordinance read with Section two ofthe Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989, powers entrusted to a Commissioner appointedunder the Commissions of Inquiry Act shall be delegated to this Single Person Board of Inquiry, and I, hereby informfurther that written and oral evidences identified as applicable in the process of inquiring into the above matters, havebeen included in the Schedule below.
