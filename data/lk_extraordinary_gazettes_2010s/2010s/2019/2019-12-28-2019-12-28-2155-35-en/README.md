FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE POLGAHAWELAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceasedto be member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political partyto which Nishshanka Dhanushka Rathnajeewa Pushpa Kumara the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongsto such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Balasooriya Mudiyanselage Anura Kumara Balasooriya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatBalasooriya Mudiyanselage Anura Kumara Balasooriya was selected as the member of the Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,27th December, 2019.
