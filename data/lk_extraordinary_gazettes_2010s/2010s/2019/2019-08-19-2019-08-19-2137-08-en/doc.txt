THE AWARD

The Honourable Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations by virtue of the powers vasted in him by Section 4(1) of theIndustrial Disputes Act Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments of Ceylon (1956 Revised Edition) as amended by ActsNos. 14 of 1957, 4 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 (read with Industrial Disputes - Special Provisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968 appointed meas Arbitrator by his order dated 03rd May 2019 and referred the following disputes to me for settlement by arbitration.

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties are :-

Whether the cancellation of salary revision given to Mr. A. M. Nishanthapriya, Supra Grade Clerk of the CeylonFishery Harbours Corporation on two occasions with effect from 01.08.2014 and 01.01.2016 is just and if not so, to what reliefhe is entitled.

Appearance

Mr. Gratian Silva, Representative appeared for the party of the First Part.

Mr.D. M. S. K. Wijetunga, Assistant Human Resources Manager appeared for the Party of the Second Part.

Party of the First Part filled the 1st statement under regulations 21(1) and 21(2) of the Industrial Disputes Regulationsof 1958 as amended.

On behalf of Party of the Second Part Mr. D. M. S. K. Wijetunga Assistant Human Resources Manager informed the Courtsalary revision was already granted to the Party of the First part. Futher the Party of the First Part also informed the Court thathe was granted the relief he sought for. Therefore the matter was settled. in the circumstances I make no award.

KAPILA M. SARATHCHANDRA,Arbitrator.22nd July, 2019
