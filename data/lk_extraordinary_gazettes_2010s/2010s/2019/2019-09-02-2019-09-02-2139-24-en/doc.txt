NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 72/0207 Monaragala District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands re-flected in the Cadastral Map No. 720033 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Dombagahawela (Block 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 128A - Dombagahawela in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Siyambalanduwa in the District of Monaragala in the Province of Uva should submit his claim to the undersigned before 25th September, 2019 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 720033 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Settle-ment. P.M.H. PRIYADARSHANI, Commissioner of Title Settlement (Covering up duties) Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 20th August, 2019 09 - 535/1
