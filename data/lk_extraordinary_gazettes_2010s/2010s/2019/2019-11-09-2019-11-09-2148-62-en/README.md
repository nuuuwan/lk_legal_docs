THE NATIONAL THOROUGHFARES ACT, No. 40 OF 2008

Order under Section 11

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 11 of the National Thoroughfares Act, No. 40 of 2008, I, KabirHashim, Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, do by this Order designatethe National Highway which was designated as an “Expressway” by Order published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2148/ 61 of 09.11.2019 and specified in the Schedule to this Order to be an “User Fee National Highway”.

KABIR HASHIM,Minister of Highways & Road Development andPetroleum Resources Development.Battaramulla,08th November, 2019.SCHEDULE

AREA COMPRISING THE USER FEE NATIONAL HIGHWAY

The stretch of road being 41.067 kilometers long and 80.000 - 100.000 metres broad, situated between the Godagama(122.389km) and Kasagala (163.456km) of the Expressway, designated as the Godagama- Kasagala National Highway (Sectionof Southern Expressway).
