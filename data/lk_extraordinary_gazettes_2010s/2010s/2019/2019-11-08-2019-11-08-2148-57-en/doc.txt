THE NOTICE TO RECTIFY THE EXTRAORDINARY GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALISTREPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA BEARING No. 2129/96 DATED 28TH JUNE, 2019

I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me underthe Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended, do hereby rectify the Extraordinary Gazette Notificationbearing No. 2129/96 dated 28th June, 2019 (07-4555/12).

The caption of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting “THEMAHO URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREA COMPRISING OF” Immediately after “FOR” and by inserting the word“(PART)” immediately after “MAHO PRADESHIYA SABHA”.

The content of the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting “the MahoUrban Development Area comprising of” immediately after “for” given in the fourth line thereof and by inserting the word“(Part)” immediately after “Maho Pradeshiya Sabha” given in the fifth line thereof.

In the content of the upper of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification, the word “had” in the fourth line shall besubstituted with “have”.

The rectifications introduced to the upper part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be read as follows:

“NOTICE OF APPROVAL OF THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN FOR THE MAHO URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREACOMPRISING OF THE MAHO PRADESHIYA SABHA (PART) AREAS.”

“NOTICE is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8G of theUrban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended from time to time that I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, theMinister in charge of the subject of Megapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in me underSection 8F of the said Law, have approved the Development Plan on the 28th day of June, 2019 for the Maho UrbanDevelopment Area comprising of the Maho Pradeshiya Sabha (Part) area, prepared under Section 8A of the saidLaw.”

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

07th November, 2019.

The caption of the latter part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting “the MahoUrban Development Area Comprising of” immediately after “for” and by inserting the word “(Part)” immediately after“Maho Pradeshiya Sabha”.

The content of the latter part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be rectified by inserting “theMaho Urban Development Area comprising of” Immediately after “for” given in the second line thereof and byinserting the word “(Part)” immediately after “Maho Pradeshiya Sabha” given in the second line thereof.

The word “(Amendment)” in the second line of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be substitutedwith “Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the”.

The word “Amendment Act” given in the last line of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be substituted with“Amended Law.”

The rectification introduced to the latter part of the said Extraordinary Gazette Notification shall be read as follows;

“Approval of the Development Plan for the Maho Urban Development Area comprising of Maho Pradeshiya Sabha(Part) Area”.

“Public are hereby informed that the Development Plan prepared under Section 8A of the Urban Development AuthorityLaw, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 4 of 1982, for the Maho Urban Development Area comprising of the MahoPradeshiya Sabha (Part) areas has been approved on 28th June, 2019, by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister ofMegapolis and Western Development, by virtue of the powers vested in him under Section 8F of the said amended Law.”

DR. JAGATH MUNASINGHE,Chairman,Urban Development Authority.07th November, 2019.

Notwithstanding the provisions contained in the antecedent Gazette Notification bearing No. 2129/96 dated 28th June,2019 (07-4555/12), the rectification hereinbefore mentioned shall have retrospective effect and shall be deemed to havecome into operation on the 28th day of June, 2019.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

07th November, 2019.
