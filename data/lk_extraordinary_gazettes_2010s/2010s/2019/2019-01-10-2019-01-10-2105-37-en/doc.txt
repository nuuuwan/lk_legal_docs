EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HAKMANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Hakmana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichLoku Hakuruge Shantha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that 4 - Narawelpita if Hakmana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Hewa Walimunige Ranil Gunathilaka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Thananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the Hakmana PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Selection 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Hewa Walimunige Ranil Gunathilaka was elected as the member of the 4 - Narawelpita of Hakmana PradeshiyaSabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Hakmana Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Matara,04th January 2019.
