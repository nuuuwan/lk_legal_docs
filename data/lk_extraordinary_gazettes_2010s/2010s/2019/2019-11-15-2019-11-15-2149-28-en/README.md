NATIONAL INNOVATION AGENCY ACT, No. 22 OF 2019

Order under Section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 1 of the National Innovation Agency Act, No. 22 of 2019, I, MaithripalaSirisena, the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this Order appoint November 15, 2019,as the date on which the aforesaid Act shall come into operation.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Colombo,15th November, 2019.
