LAND ACQUISITION ACT AS AMENDED BY LANDACQUISITION (AMENDMENT) ACT No. 28 OF 1964

(CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

Land Mini's Ref. No.: 4/3/9/2013/WS/461.

My Ref. No.: 9/3/5/7.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section (7) ofLand Acquisition Act, No. 28 of 1964 (Amendment) that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land which isrequired for a public purpose.

Description of the land to be acquired :

An allotment of land called Lewakandawaththa in totalextent of 0.1012 Hectare depicted in the Preliminary Plan No.Ma. 1716 prepared by Surveyor General situated in the villagecalled Punchilewakandawaththa in the Grama NiladhariDivision in Northern Matale Minor Division in the DivisionalSecretary's Divisiion of Yatawatta in Matale District in theCentral province and morefully described as follows.

Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Lewakandawaththa; Description: Forthe construction of Mahalewakanda water tank under the

project of Greater Matale Water Supply; Claimant:Hapukotuwe Gedara Gisban Hapukotuwa; Total Extent: 0.1012Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized inwriting, before me at the Yatawatta Divisional Secretariat on03.04.2019 at 10.30 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify tome in writing (in duplicate) on or before 21.03.2019 the natureof their interest in the land, the particulars of their claims forthe acquisition of the said lands, the amount of compensationand the details of the computation of such amount.

All deeds and documents in support of the claims shouldbe produced before me at the inquiry.

D. R. K. HENNAYAKE,Divisional Secretary,

Yatawatta.
