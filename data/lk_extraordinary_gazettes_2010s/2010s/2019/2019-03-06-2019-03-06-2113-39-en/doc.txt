FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GIRIBAWA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Giribawa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichVishnaka Mohotti Mudiyanselage Nayanananda Vishnaka the Member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 2- Perakumpura of Giribawa Pradeshiya Sabhawho belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and whoshall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Adhikari Mudiyanselage Ariyarathne to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Giribawa Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Selection 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatAdhikari Mudiyanselage Ariyarathne was elected as the member of the 2- Perakumpura of Giribawa Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Giribawa Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kurunegala,05th March, 2019.
