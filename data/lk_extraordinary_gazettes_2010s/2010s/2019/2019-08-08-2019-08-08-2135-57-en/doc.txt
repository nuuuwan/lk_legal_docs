FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MINUWANGODAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichLankathilake Adikari Mudiyanselage Roopasari Upachala Lankathilake the member belonged, was required by me underSection 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongsto such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Heenwella Arachchilage Anoma Kanthi Subhasingha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the MinuwangodaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Heenwella Arachchilage Anoma Kanthi Subhasingha was selected as the member of the MinuwangodaPradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,07th August, 2019.
