FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WANATHAWILLUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group 1, to which Pathirannehelage Rohini Mallika the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member ifIndependent Group No. 1 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under section 9 of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateKombala Thambiliya Godage Ruwani Nisansala to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that KombalaThambiliya Godage Ruwani Nisansala was selected as the member of the Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Puttalam,02nd November, 2019.
