THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Land Mini's. Reference No.:4-3/15/2013/VE/32.

Sec.Re.No.: 9/3/5/19.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act No. 28 of 1964(Amendment), thatthe Government intends to acquire the following land whichis required for a public purpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

An allotment of land called Yatawaththa Waththa in totalextent of 1.0136 hectare depicted in the Preliminary Plan No.MA 1712 situated in the village called Yatawaththa Waththain the Grama Niladhari Division No. of Maligathenna in theDivisional Secretary's Division of Yatawaththa in MathaleDistrict in the Central Province and morefully described asfollows.

1. Lot No. 1Name of the land: Yatawaththa Waththa; Description:Destribution under village expansion among the people who
