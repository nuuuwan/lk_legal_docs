Voting by Post

01. The following are the days of postal voting in terms of Section 23 of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981and the Second Schedule to that Act.

31 October 201901 November 201904 November 201905 November 201907 November 2019

The date or dates mentioned in the first paragraph above shall mean the poll date or dates of the officers andemployees who are deemed to be postal voters and ;
