(Published by Authority)PART IV (A) - PROVINCIAL COUNCILS

ARTICLE 154 B OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OFSRI LANKA

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Paragraph 8 of Article 154B of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republicof Sri Lanka, I, Tennakoon Mudiyanselage Rajith Keerthi Tennakoon, the Governor of Southern Province, do herebydissolve the Southern Provincial Council with effect from the midnight 10th April, 2019.

TENNAKOON MUDIYANSELAGE RAJITH KEERTHI TENNAKOON,Governor of the Southern Province.
