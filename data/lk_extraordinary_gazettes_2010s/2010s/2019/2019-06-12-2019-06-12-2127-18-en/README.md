FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GALIGAMUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Galigamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attendingfor council meeting for a period of three months ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to whichSenadinennehelage Champa Kumari Senadheera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Danansooriya Arachchillage Mallika Danansooriya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Galigamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that DanansooriyaArachchillage Mallika Danansooriya was selected as the member of the Galigamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Galigamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kegalle,11th June, 2019.
