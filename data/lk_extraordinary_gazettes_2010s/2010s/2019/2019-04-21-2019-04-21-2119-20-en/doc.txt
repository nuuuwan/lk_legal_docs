NOTIFICATION BY MR. SPEAKER

Convening of the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankaon Tuesday, 23rd April, 2019 at 1.00 p.m.

I, Karu Jayasuriya, Speaker of Parliament do hereby notify that in pursuance of Standing Order 16 of the Standing Orders ofParliament, I have summoned the Parliament to meet on Tuesday the 23rd day of April 2019 at 1.00 p.m. having beenrequested so to do by the Hon. Prime Minister.

KARU JAYASURIYA,Speaker.

Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte,21st April, 2019.
