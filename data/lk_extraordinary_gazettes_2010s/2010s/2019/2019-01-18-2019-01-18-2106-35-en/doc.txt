THE LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDMENT) ACT,

No. 28 OF 1964 (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No.: KDS/MDB/4/2/1/64/06.

Ministry of Lands & Parliamentary

Reforms Ref. No.: L13J/96/FR/178.

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofLand Acquisition Act, No. 28 of 1964 (Amendment) that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land which isrequired for a public purpose.

Purpose of acquisition: Knuckles Range Reservation:

Two allotments of land called Dothulukanda in total extentof about 2.6135 Hectares depicted as lots 02 and 03 in thePreliminary Plan No. P.P. Maha 6733 Dated 22.02.2018 preparedby the Senior Superintendent of Surveys - Kandy District,situated in the Village called Rambukpotha in the GramaNiladhari No. 807 of Rambukpotha in the UdasiyapattuwaSouth Korale Minor Division in the Divisional Secretary'sDivision of Medadumbara in Kandy District of the CentralProvince and more fully described as follows.

01. Lot No. 02Name of the land: Dothulukanda; Description: Jungle;Claimant: H. M. W. G. T. B. Herath Ekanayake, Rambukpotha,Medamahanuwara; Extent: 0.2835 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 03Name of the land: Dothulukanda; Description: Jungle;Claimant: H. M. W. G. T. B. Herath Ekanayake, Rambukpotha,Medamahanuwara; Extent: 2.3300 Hectares.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agent duly authorized inwriting before me at the Medadumbara Divisional Secretariaton the 03rd April, 2019 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requestednotify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 25th dayof March, 2019. The nature of their interest in the aforesaidland, the particulars of their claims of compensation and thedetails of computation of such amount.

A. M. WICKRAMARACHCHI,

Divisional Secretary,

Medadumbara.

At Divisional Secretariat,Medadumbara,14th January, 2019.
