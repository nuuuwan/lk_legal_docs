SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specifiedin the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from November 08, 2019 subject to provisionsof Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA, Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,07th November, 2019.
