ORDER UNDER SECTION 2A VALUE ADDED TAX ACT, No. 14 OF 2002 AS AMENDED BYACT, No. 06 OF 2005

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 2A of the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002, as amended by Act, No.06 of 2005, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance, do by this Order declare that, the Value Added Tax shall becharged on the supply of services specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, at the rate specified in the correspondingentry in Column II, of such Schedule, for a period of one year commencing from May 21, 2019.
