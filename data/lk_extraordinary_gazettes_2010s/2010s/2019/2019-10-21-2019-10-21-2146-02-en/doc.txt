I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act as Amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment Act), No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intendsto acquire the land of Undugoda, Yatideriyawaththa describedin the Schedule given below for re-settlement the people whowere displaced due to the earth slides occurred in 2016 in theDivisional Secretariat Division of Kegalle.

Description of the Lands to be acquired:

Five (05) allotments of land attached below depicted inPreliminary Plan No. 3699 prepared by Surveyor General,Kegalle situated in the Yatideriya village of 56B UndugodaGrama Niladhari Division in Kegalle Divisional Secretary'sDivision in Kegalle District of Sabaragamuwa Province.

Dates of inquiries for compensation-

Lot No. 01 – 05 of P.P.K. 3699. On the date of 5th December2019 from 9.30 a.m.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands to be requiredare hereby to appear personally or by agents duly authorizedin written before me at Divisional Secretariat, Kegalle on theabove mentioned date and on the scheduled time.

And are hereby requested to notify me in writing withduplicate of on or before 22.11.2019 the nature of their interestin the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation
