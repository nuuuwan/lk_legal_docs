FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NUWARAGAMPALATHACENTRAL PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIESELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member in Nuwaragampalatha Central Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Nuwaragampalatha Central Pradeshiya Sabha, the recognized politicalparty to which Rathnamalala Bandara Wanninayakage Chandraguptha Wanninayaka the member belonged, was required byme under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Frontwho belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and whoshall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Weliwaththa Gamlathlage Sumith Kulathunga to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Mohideen Bawa Mohamed Sufyan, Returning Officer of the Nuwaragampalatha CentralPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Weliwaththa Gamlathlage Sumith Kulathunga was selected as the member of the NuwaragampalathaCentral Pradeshiya Sabha.

M. B. M. SUFYAN,Returning Officer,Nuwaragampalatha Central Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Anuradhapura,15th July, 2019.
