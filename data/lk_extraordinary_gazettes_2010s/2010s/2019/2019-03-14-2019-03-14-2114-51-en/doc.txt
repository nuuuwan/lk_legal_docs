THE PILGRIMAGE ORDINANCE

The Annual Feast Pilgrimage of our Lady of Holy Rosary Shrine, Periyapullumalai - 2019

THE Annual feast and the Pilgrimage of our Lady of Holy Rosary Shrine, Periyapullumalai - 2019. It is hereby for theinformation of the Pilgrims who attend the above feast and the others concerned that the annual feast for the year 2019begins on the 26th of April (26.04.2019) with hoisting of flags, and ends on the 05th of May with the concelebratedEucharistic Celebration.

The special event of the feast would be a Pilgrimage which begins on 03rd of May at 3.30 p. m. from St. Mary’sCathedral, Puliyanthivu, Batticaloa and proceeds through Trinco Road, Chenkalady, Baddulla Road.

The attention of the Pilgrims who attend the above feast and others concerned is drawn to the regulation publishedin the Government Gazette No. 9886 of 16.07.1984 which will be inforce during the period of the feast.

N. VILVARETNAM,Divisional Secretary,Eravur Pattu.Divisional Secretariat,Eravur Pattu.
