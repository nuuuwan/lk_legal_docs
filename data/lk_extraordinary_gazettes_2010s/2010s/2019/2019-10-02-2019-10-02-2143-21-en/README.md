SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rate specifiedin the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 31 days commencing from October 03, 2019 subject to provisions ofSub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2124/4 of 21.05.2019 in respect of B’ Onions is rescinded with effect fromOctober 03, 2019.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,October 02, 2019.
