URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, I, PataliChampika Ranawaka, Minister of Magapolis & Western Development being a opinion that the areas specified in ColumnI excluding the already declared areas mentioned under Column I of Schedule II hereto and having the metes and boundsspecified in corresponding entry in Column II of Schedule II hereto are suitable for Development and do hereby this Orderdeclare such areas for the purpose of the said Section.

I do hereby declare that, the already declared areas set out in Schedule I together with the other areas declared asaforesaid, shall be included within the limits of the expanded Rideegama Urban Development Area set out in Column II underSchedule II hereto.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA (M. P.),Minister of Megapolis & Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis & Western Development,17th & 18th Floors, “Suhurupaya”,Sri Subhuthipura Road,Battaramulla,21st April, 2019.
