L.D-B. 11/1979(iii)

THE PREVENTION OF TERRORISM (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 48 OF 1979

Order under Section 9

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Subsection (1) of Section 9 of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions)Act, No. 48 of 1979, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President do, by this Order determine that the premises specified in theSchedule hereto, shall be a place of detention for the purposes of Subsection (1) of Section 9 of the Prevention ofTerrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act, No. 48 of 1979.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Colombo,24th May, 2019.

SCHEDULE

Sri Lanka Naval Ship-Gemenu, situated in Welisara, Ragama.
