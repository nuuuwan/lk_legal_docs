Statute No. 02 of 2018 Pawnbrokers (Amendments) of theSouthern Provincial Council

BE it passed by the Provincial Council of the Southern Province of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka as follows:

01. This statute may be cited as the Pawnbrokers Statute (Amendment) No. 02 of 2018 of the SouthernProvince.

02. The word ‘‘consistence’’ depicted as ‘‘This Statute is consistence with the provisions of pawnbrokersOrdinance No. 13 of 1942’’ in the first page of the pawnbrokers Statute No. 01 of 2013 of the SouthernProvince shall be repealed and amended as ‘‘Inconsistence’’.

03. The new section mentioned below is hereby included to the main statute and subsequent to thereofand the said section shall be come into force as II (I) of the main statute.

II (I) An interest incurred in respect of the deposit of pawnbroker business if any, should be creditedto the Provincial Council Fund.

04. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this statute, the Sinhala textshall prevail.
