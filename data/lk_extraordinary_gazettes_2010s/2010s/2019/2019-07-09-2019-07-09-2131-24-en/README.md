By Order of His Excellency,

UDAYA R. SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,04th July, 2019.

Schedule

The following new paragraph is inserted immediately after paragraph 9 thereof and shall be effective as paragraph 9A ofthe said Minute.

9A. However, when the services of a Secretary who is re-employed under para. 03of the schedule A. inserted underparagraph 1A of this Minute cease, his/her pension shall be revised based on the salary drawn by him at the timeof the cessation* of his service provided such cessation of service is due to a non-disciplinary reason.

* Here the salary drawn by him at the time of cessation of his services is considered as the salary drawn by thatofficer including the salary increments earned by him, salary increases granted by the Government from time totime, pensionable allowances and any other allowances which is pensionable.
