EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTHWEST PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichAmbikaipaham Kethusan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Tamil United Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thambipillai Thayaparan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South West PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Thambipillai Thayaparan was selected as the member of the Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,13th May, 2019.
