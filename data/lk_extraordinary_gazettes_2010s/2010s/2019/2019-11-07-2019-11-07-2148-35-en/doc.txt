Government Notifications

L.D.B 21/78(xiv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20(4)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20(4)(b) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on being satisfied that the administration of theaffairs of the University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said Universityhave failed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Emeritus Prof. Kathirkamanathan Kandasamy to be theCompetent Authority with effect from November 07, 2019 for the purpose of exercising, performing and discharging inlieu of the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka, any power, duty or function respectively which has beenconferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Vice-Chancellor under the said Act, or under any Order, Ordinance,Statute, By-Law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,07th November, 2019.
