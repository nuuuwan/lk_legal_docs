FILLING OF A VACANCY THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE BORALESGAMUWA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66B (2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman in Boralesgamuwa Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of death;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna the recognized political party to, whichSubaweerage Swarnalatha Dias the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66 B (2) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Boralesgamuwa Urban Council who belongs to such party whoshall be declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Liyanage Udaya Srilal Sigera to fill such vacancy ;
