FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM EASTPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to whichArumugam Nadesu Rajenthiran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward 01 - Valalai if Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Rasu Kugatheesan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Selection 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatRasu Kugatheesan was elected as the member of the Ward 01 - Valalai of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,11th April, 2019.
