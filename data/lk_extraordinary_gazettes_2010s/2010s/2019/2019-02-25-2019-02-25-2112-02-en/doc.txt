EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance the recognized political party to whichMohomed Asath Sanoon Sali the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Wathupiti Kandalage Shantha Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchilage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Colombo MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Wathupiti Kandalage Shantha Kumara was selected as the member of the Colombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Colombo City,25th February 2019.
