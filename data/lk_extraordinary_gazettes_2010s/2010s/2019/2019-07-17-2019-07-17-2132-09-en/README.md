FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ANURADHAPURAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Anuradhapura Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichAnanda Dahanayaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon the member of that 11- Samanpura of Anuradhapura Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid Recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Henda Weetharanage Indika Upul Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Mohideen Bawa Mohamed Sufyan, Returning Officer of the Anuradhapura MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Henda Weetharanage Indika Upul Kumara was elected as the member of the 11- Samanpura of AnuradhapuraMunicipal Council.

M. B. M. SUFYAN,Returning Officer,Anuradhapura Municipal Council.District Election Office,Anuradhapura,15th July, 2019.
