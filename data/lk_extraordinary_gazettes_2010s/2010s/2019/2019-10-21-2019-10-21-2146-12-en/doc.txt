AMENDMENT

MERITORIOUS SERVICE MEDAL

Orders

REGULATIONS mentioned in the following paragraphs, numbered as First, Second, Fourth, and Fifth published in theGovernment Gazette Extraordinary Number 2112/32 dated 28.02.2019, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka for the Meritorious Service Medal are hereby amended:

First Paragraph:Style and Designation - It is ordained that the medal shall be styled and designated the"SEVABHIMANI PADAKKAMA".

Second Paragraph:Description - It is ordained that the medal shall be of Gold colour metal and the flower of eightpetals in shape, with a diameter of 3.7 cm bearing in the centre of the surface a design comprisingan Eagle in flight head to the sinister superimposed on an Anchor with a chain twined around it, thewhole superimposed upon two crossed swords points uppermost surrounded by the legend"SEVABHIMANI PADAKKAMA" embossed in Sinhala, Tamil and English and in the relief on thereverse the Armorial Ensign of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka surrounded by a lotuspetal border within aears of paddy surrounded by the wheel of Doctrine with the filled Vessel atthe base and the depiction of Sun in splendour and the Moon on the right and left respectively andthe year of institution "2009" embossed below it.

Fourth Paragraph:Eligibility - It is ordained that the medal shall be granted to the Minister of Defence, the StateMinister of Defence, the Secretary of Defence of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankaand all Ranks of the Regular and Volunteer Forces of the Army, Navy and Air Force of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and members of the Sri Lanka Police, for the valuable contributionmade since 19.05.2009 for the post-war activities, count not less than Six years of aggregateservice excluding the basic training period with a good conduct and the permanent civil staffmembers with Six Years of aggregate service in Armed Services and Affiliates of the Sri LankaPolice subject to the regulations hereinafter made.

Fifth Paragraph:Registration - It is ordained that the names of all those to whom the Medal is awarded shall bepublished in the respective Service Orders and the register of such names shall be maintained underthe direction of the respective Service Commander. It is ordained that a register of the names ofthe Minister of Defence, the State Minister of Defence, the Secretary of Defence of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka shall be maintained under the Ministry of Defence.

Sixth Paragraph :Order of Wear - It is ordained that in the official list showing the order in which Orders, Decorationsand Medals are to be worn, the Sevabhimani Padakkama shall be worn immediately after the SriLanka Armed Service Long Service Medal.

Seventh Paragraph:Miniatures -It is ordained that reproductions in miniature known as Miniature Medal; which may beworn on certain occasions by those to whom the medal is awarded, shall be approximately half thesize of the medal and a sealed pattern of the miniature medal shall be kept in the Headquarters of theArmy, Navy, Air Force and Police.

By His Excellency's command,

UDAYA R SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.

SEVABHIMANI PADAKKAMA

1. The Medal shall be awarded to the Minister of Defence, the State Minister of Defence, the Secretary of De-fence of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka for the valuable contribution made since 19.05.2009 and all Ranksof the Regular and Volunteer Forces of the Army, Navy and Air Force and members of the Sri Lanka Police who have beenin active service on Nineteen day of May Two Thousand Nine or thereafter, and who have completed an aggregate period ofSix Years excluding the basic training period with a good conduct and the permanent civil staff members with Six years ofaggregate service in Armed Services and Affiliates of the Sri Lank Police.

4. A Board of not less than three Senior Officers nominated by the respective Service Commanders will examinethe recommendations made for the award of the SEVABHIMANI PADAKKAMA. The recommendations of the Board alongwith the recommendations of the respective Service Commanders will be forwarded to His Excellency the Presidentthrough the Secretary, Defence for His Excellency’s approval. The recommendations of the Minister of Defence, the StateMinister of Defence and the Secretary of Defence of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka should be sentdirectly by the Secretary of Defence for His Excellency’s approval.

6. The names of persons whom the Medal is conferred upon, shall be published in the respective Service Ordersand a register of all such recipients shall be maintained under the direction of the respective Service Commander.

8. Any Officer of the Sri Lanka Army, Navy, Air Force and Police who is cashiered, dismissed or has been calledupon to resign or retire his Commission, shall forfeit the Medal conferred on him unless His Excellency the Presidentshall direct otherwise.

9. Every Soldier, Seaman, Airman and Policemen who has been discharged from the Army, Navy and Air Forcewith disgrace or with ignominy, shall forfeit the Medal unless His Excellency the President shall direct otherwise.

13. In an event where a Medal is lost, and if it is desirous to replace it, such a loss should be reported to the Police.A declaration testified by a Justice of the Peace stating the circumstances under which the loss occurred should be submit-ted along with a copy of the complaint made to the Police. This declaration shall be forwarded to the Commanding Officerwho will forward it to the respective Service Commander through the usual channel of communication. The Medal losttherewith will be replaced on payment if the explanation as to the loss is considered satisfactory.

14. In those Regulations -

(a)"Services" means-The Sri Lanka Army,The Sri Lanka Navy,The Sri Lanka Air Force,

(b)"Police" meansThe Sri Lanka Police.

(c)"Service Commanders" means-The Commander of the Army,The Commander of the Navy,The Commander of the Air Force,

(d)"Inspector General" means-The Inspector General of Police.

(e)"Military Personnel" means-Army, Navy and Air Force personnel

(f)"Member of Police" means-Police Personnel

(g)"Member of the Civil Staff" means-Civilians working in the Army,Navy, Air Force and Police.
