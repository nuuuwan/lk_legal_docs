THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

I, hereby declare that out of the powers and functions vested in me in my capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economicand Policy Development of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, powers and functions specified in the followingSchedule I are delegated to the State Minister of Economic and Policy Development, Hon. John Senevirathna and powersand functions specified in the following Schedule II are delegated to the State Minister of Development Banks and Loans,Hon. Shehan Semasinghe in terms of the provisions of the Article 44(5) of the constitution and the both State Ministershave the opportunity of inquiring, mediating and coordinating all functions coming within the scope of work of the Ministry.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSE, Minister of Finance, Economicand Policy Development.

Ministry of Finance, Economic and Policy Development,The Secretariat,Colombo 01,30th December 2019.
