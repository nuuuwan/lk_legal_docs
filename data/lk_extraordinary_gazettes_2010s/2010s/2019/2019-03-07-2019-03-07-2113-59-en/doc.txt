SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Regulations under Sec. 32 and 33

BY virtue of powers vested in me, under Sections 32(b) and 33 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973, I, Harin Fernando,Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports suspend the registration, with immediate effect, of theSri Lanka Table Tennis Association, the National Association for the Sport of Table Tennis and appoint the followinginterim committee to continue the activities thereof as a Provisional arrangement.

1.Mr. Galla Liyanage Sajith Jayalal-President2.Dr. Dhammika Senanayake-Secretary3.Mrs. Kariyawasam Devarakottage Ayanthi Shalika Kariyawasam-Treasurer

And, I do hereby authorize the Director General of the Department of Sports Development to convene a SpecialGeneral Meeting of the Table Tennis Association and appoint an Election Committee in terms of the Sports Act, No. 25 of1973 and Subsequent amendments made thereto and convene and conduct the election under supervision of the DirectorGeneral.

HARIN FERNANDO,Minister of Telecommunication,Foreign Employment and Sports.Colombo,01st March, 2019.

SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Regulations under Sec. 32 and 33

BY virtue of powers vested in me, under Section 32(b) and 33 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973, I, Harin Fernando,Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports do hereby suspend the registration of with immediateeffect, of the Sri Lanka Body Building and Physical Fitness Federation and the Sri Lanka Powerlifting Federation andappoint the following interim committees to continue the activities thereof as a Provisional arrangement.

The Interim Committee to be appointed to the Sri Lanka Body Building and Physical Fitness Federation as follows :

1.Mr. Mahesh Gammanpila-President2.Mrs. Shiromi Ranawaka-Secretary3.Mr. P. B. Jayathilake-Treasurer

The Interim Committee to be appointed to the Sri Lanka Powerlifting Federation as follows :

1.Dr. Lal Ekanayake-President2.Mrs. Lakmini Samarasinghe-Secretary3.Mr. Ravindra Kumara-Treasurer

And, I do hereby authorize the Director General of the Department of Sports Development to convene a SpecialGeneral Meeting of the Sri Lanka Body Building and Physical Fitness Federation and Sri Lanka Powerlifting Federationin terms of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and Subsequent amendments made thereto and appoint election committee toconvene and conduct the election under supervision of the Director General.

HARIN FERNANDO,Minister of Telecommunication,Foreign Employment and Sports.Colombo,01st March, 2019.
