L.D.B 21/78(XV)(A)

UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on the recommendation of the UniversityGrants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, publishedin Gazette Extraordinary No. 281/7 of January 24, 1984 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2111/12 of February 18, 2019), establishing the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka, by the addition immediately afterthe entry “3. Department of Marketing” in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item ‘‘Faculty ofManagement and Finance’’ in Column I of that Schedule, of the following entry :-

“4. Department of Human Resource Management’’.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply andHigher Education.

Colombo,03rd June 2019.
