FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PANNALA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Adhikari Mudiyanselage Nalini Gunasena themember belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember of Independent Group who belongs to such Independent Group, and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateLiyana Pathirannehelage Chandrapala to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that LiyanaPathirannehelage Chandrapala was selected as the member of the Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Pannala Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,24th May, 2019.
