FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group No. 2 to which Krishnapillei Kirubanandan the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member ifIndependent Group No. 2 who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Rasaiya Ganeshan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchilage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Colombo MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Rasaiya Ganeshan was selected as the member of the Colombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Colombo City,29th January, 2019,
