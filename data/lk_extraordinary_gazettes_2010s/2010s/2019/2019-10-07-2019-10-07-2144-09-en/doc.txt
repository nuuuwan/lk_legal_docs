FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATTANKUDY URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kattankudy Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Muslim National Alliance, the recognized political party to whichMukammathu Farook Mukammathu Sibli the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of Muslim National Alliance who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Ahamathulebbe Ali Akbar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Kattankudy Urban Council, acting in termsof Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ahamathulebbe AliAkbar was selected as the member of the Kattankudy Urban Council.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Kattankudy Urban Council.District Election Office,Batticaloa,04th October, 2019.
