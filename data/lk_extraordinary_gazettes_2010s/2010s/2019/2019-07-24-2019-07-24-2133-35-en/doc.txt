FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE UDUBADDAWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Not Attendingfor council meetings period of three months without permisson ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which AlpenerisgeHasini Sandathaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member if United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thamma Hetti Mudalige Agnas Jasintha Peiris to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatThamma Hetti Mudalige Agnas Jasintha Peiris was selected as the member of the Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISANTHA,Returning Officer,Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,23rd July, 2019.
