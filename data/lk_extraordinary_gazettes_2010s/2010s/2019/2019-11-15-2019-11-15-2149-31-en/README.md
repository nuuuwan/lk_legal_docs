THE PILGRIMAGE ORDINANCE

Godapitiya Muslim Festival - 2019

IN terms of regulations "3" of the regulations formed under the pilgrimage Ordinance (Chapter 175) & Public in the GovernmentGazette No. 10630 of 8th January 1954. It is hereby notified that annual pilgrimage of Godapitiya Poruwai Mosque "MuhiyadeenMuslim Mosque" at Godapitiya, Akuressa in Athuraliya Division in Matara District. Will Commence on 11th December andterminate 22nd December 2019.

The regulations relating this pilgrimage Ordinance in Government Gazette No. 10630 of 8th January 1954. Shall in forcewithin the camp above referred to in the regulations & described in the scheduled there during the period of the Pilgrimagereferred to above.

NIROSHA S. GAMAGE,Divisional Secretary,Athuraliya.
