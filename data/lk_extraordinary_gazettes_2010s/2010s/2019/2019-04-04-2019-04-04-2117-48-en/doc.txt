EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, as amended, I,Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance, do by this Order declare that, the Excise Duty charged, levied and collected onmotor vehicles imported during the period of January 2015 to November 2017 specified in Column I of the Schedule I heretoshall be payable at the rate specified in Column II of the Schedule I wherever applicable with effect from April 05, 2019.

Any importer/individual who is fulfilling the criteria specified in the Schedule I to this Order and willing torelease his/her motor vehicles, on payment of such special duty rate should submit his/her application as specified in theSchedule II to this Order to the Director General, Department of Fiscal Policy of the General Treasury on or beforeAugust 05, 2019.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01.March 29, 2019.
