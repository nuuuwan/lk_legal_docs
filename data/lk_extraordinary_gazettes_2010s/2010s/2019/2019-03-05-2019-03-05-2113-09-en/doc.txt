EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance, do by this Order declare that :-

1. (a) The Excise Duty on every article specified in Column III of the Schedule I hereto shall be payable at the ratespecified in the corresponding entry in Column IV of the Schedule I;

(b) The concessionary Excise Duty on excisable articles specified in Column II of the Schedule II hereto shall bepayable at the rate specified in Column III of the Schedule II wherever applicable; and

(c) Wherever two rates are specified in Column IV of the Schedule I and Column III of the schedule II hereto, theapplicable rate for the purpose of calculating the Excise Duty payable shall be the rate which yields the higheramount payable as Excise Duty.

2. The orders made under Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No.13 of 1989 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notifications No.2044/32 of 09.11.2017, No.2045/33 of 15.11.2017, No.2046/14 of22.11.2017, No.2047/25 of 28.11.2017, No.2058/33 of 15.02.2018, No.2066/40 of 12.04.2018, No.2068/9 of24.04.2018, No.2074/39 of 08.06.2018, No. 2080/31 of 17.07.2018, No. 2082/10 of 31.07.2018, No. 2082/11of 31.07.2018, No. 2083/2 of 06.08.2018, No. 2089/18 of 17.09.2018, No. 2092/5 of 08.10.2018, No. 2095/18of 01.11.2018, No. 2100/3 of 03.12.2018 and No. 2105/48 of 11.01.2019 are hereby rescinded.

3. This Order shall come into effect from March 06, 2019.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01.March 05, 2019.
