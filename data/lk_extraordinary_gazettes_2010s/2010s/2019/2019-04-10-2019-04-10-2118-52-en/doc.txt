EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VAVUNIYA SOUTH (TAMIL)PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofresignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Sivakulanthai Nandaraj the member belonged,was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if IndependentGroup who belongs to such Independent Group, and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateManiyam Nagendhirarasa to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of the Vavuniya South (Tamil) PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Maniyam Nagendhirarasa was selected as the member of the Vauniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Vavuniya South (Tamil) Pradeshiya SabhaDistrict Election Office,Vavuniya,09th April, 2019.
