L.D.B. 104/44(v)

TRUSTS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 87)

Order under Section 114

WHEREAS the Trustees of “the Trustees of the Society of Jesus in Sri Lanka” namely -

1.Reverend Father Dexter Fredric Gray,2.Reverend Father Noel Felix Anton Pieris,3.Reverend Father George Anthony Fernando,4.Reverend Father Clement Ranjan Jayamanne.

have applied to the Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms for their incorporation under Section 114 of theTrusts Ordinance (Chapter 87) ;

Now therefore, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the aforesaid Ordinance, I, Thalatha Athukorala, Ministerof Justice and Prison Reforms, do by this Order authorize the incorporation of the said Trustees, and direct and declarethat-

(a)the Trustees of the said Trust named above and their successors for the time being duly appointed underthe Deed of Trust bearing No. 486 of June 25, 2019, attested by Tharini Prakarshi Dias SenerathGunasekera, Notary Public of Colombo, shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and acommon seal under the name and style of “the Trustees of the Society of Jesus in Sri Lanka” ;
