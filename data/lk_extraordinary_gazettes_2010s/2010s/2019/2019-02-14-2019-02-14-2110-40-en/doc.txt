SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Regulations under 32 and 33

I, Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports in pursuance of powers vested in mein terms of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 do hereby appoint the following three officers with effect from 14.02.2019.

1.Mr. Liyanaarachchi Appuhamilage Deepal Liyanaarchchi- Vice President2.Mr. Wanninayaka Mudiyanselage Wasantha Sisira Wanninayaka- Treasurer3.Mr. Mitimanaye Judith Priyani Perera- Secretary

as a provisional arrangement until such time a lawful election is held of the Sri Lanka Badminton, the NationalAssociation for the Sports of Badminton, of which registration has at present been suspended and consequent 03 positionshave fallen vacant whereas,

1. G. G. Mangala - Treasurer

who was a member of the first Interim Committee appointed by Gazette Notification No. 2053/06 published on08.01.2018 and whereas.

1.Kariyawasam Gonapinuwala Gamage Anjan Mohan Wijesinghe-Secretary2.Wasantha Kumara-Vice President

Who were appointed as members of that Committee by Gazette Notification No 2066/41 dated 12.04.2018 haveresigned.

Moreover, I do hereby authorize to Interim Committee to convene a special General Meeting of the Sri LankaBadminton in terms of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and the amended Regulations thereof and appoint an ElectionCommittee under the Supervision of the Director General of the Department of Sports Development.

HARIN FERNANDO (MP),Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports.
