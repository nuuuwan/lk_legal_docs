FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KOLONNAWA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Kolonnawa Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attendingthree consecutive meetings of the Council without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party towhich Batagoda Kankanamage Dona Champika the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected asa member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Hitihamu Mudiyanselage Podi Menike to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of the KolonnawaUrban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Hitihamu Mudiyanselage Podi Menike was selected as the member of the Kolonnawa Urban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Kolonnawa Urban Council.District Election Office,Colombo,29th March, 2019.
