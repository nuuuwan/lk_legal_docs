THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Ref. No. : THI/LND/L2/ACQVI/142.

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intendsto acquire the following lands which are required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

Allotments of Lot No. 01 and 02 in Total Extent of about0.0182 Hectare, depicted in Preliminary Plan No. Mara 2750,prepared by the Senior - Superintendent of Survey of MataraDistrict on behalf of the Surveyor General, situated in theVillage of Kapuduwa in the Grama Niladhari Division of 335 AKapuduwa in the Divisional Secretary’s Division ofThihagoda in Matara District.

01. Lot No. 01Name of land: 5/6th Lot of the land called Paluwatta;Description: Garden, contains a mixed cultivation; Claimant:State; Original Claimant: –; Extent: 0.0079 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agents duly authorized inwriting, before me at Divisional Secretary's office of Thihagodaon the 30th January, 2019 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requestedto notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 23rdJanuary, 2019 the nature of their interests in the land, theparticulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisitionof the said lands, the amount of compensation and the detailsof the computation of such amount.
