FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MIRIGAMA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resigning ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to whichHettiarachchige Santha Geethadeva the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of Sri Lanka Freedom Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Serasinghe Mudiyanselage Priyantha Lalith Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the MirigamaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Serasinghe Mudiyanselage Priyantha Lalith Kumara was selected as the member of the MirigamaPradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,03rd May, 2019.
