FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to bea member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United Peace Alliance, the recognized political party to which MohomadMasahir Mohomad Binkas the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of United Peace Alliance who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Cadar Mohideen Mohamed Nizamdeen to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Colombo MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Cadar Mohideen Mohamed Nizamdeen was selected as the member of the Colombo Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.District Election Office,Colombo City,10th September, 2019.
