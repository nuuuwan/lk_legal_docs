FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WANATHAWILLUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group 1, to which Katuwala Kande Gamladdalage PradeepAruna Kantha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member if Independent Group No. 1 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateKaleefa Marikkar Abdul Asees to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that KaleefaMarikkar Abdul Asees was selected as the member of the Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Wanathawilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Puttalam,20th September, 2019.
