FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PUTHUKKUDIYIRUPPUPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to whichSabareththinam Thiruchchelvam the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that 03 Udayarkaddu of Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiy Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredselected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Valarmathi Subakaran to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanthirasa Kantheepan, Returning Officer of the Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatValarmathi Subakaran was selected as the member of the 03 Udayarkaddu of Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. KANTHEEPAN,Returning Officer,Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Mullaitivu,24th September, 2019.
