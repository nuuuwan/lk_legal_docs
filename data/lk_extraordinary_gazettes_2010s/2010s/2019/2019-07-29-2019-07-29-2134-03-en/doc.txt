DEREQUISITION ORDER No. 2019/04/25/101/001

The Co-operative Societies (Special Provisions) Act, No. 35 of 1970

ORDER UNDER SECTION 10 (4)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, by the Section 10 (4) of the Co-operative Societies (Special Provisions) Act, No.35of 1970, I, Buddhika Pathirana, Minister of Industry and Commerce, Resettlement of Protracted Displaced Persons,Co-operative Development and Vocational Training and Skills Development (Acting) do hereby derequisition the propertythat had been requisitioned by the Gazette Notification No. 108/09 and dated on 26.04.1974 to conduct any businessactivity of the Yatiyanthota Multi- Purpose Co-operative Society Limited and Specified in the schedule below to theowners with effect from 17th day of July 2019.

BUDDHIKA PATHIRANA,Hon. Minister of Industry and Commerce,Resettlement of Protracted Displaced Persons,Co-operative Development and VocationalTraining and Skills Development (Acting).Colombo,17th July, 2019.
