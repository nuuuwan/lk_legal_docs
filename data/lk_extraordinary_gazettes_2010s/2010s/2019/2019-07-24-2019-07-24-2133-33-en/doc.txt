FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MORATUWA MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Moratuwa Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which BabaraGuwendaleen Pieris the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid Recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Ranhaluge Krishani Fernando to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Moratuwa MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Ranhaluge Krishani Fernando was selected as the member of the Moratuwa Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Moratuwa Municipal Council.District Election Office,Colombo City,23rd July, 2019.
