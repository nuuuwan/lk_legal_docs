LAND REFORMS ACT, No. 01 OF 1972 OF THE NATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 27 A (1) of the Land Reform Act, No. 01 of 1972 as amended and tobe read together with Sub Article (2) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankaand as it is requested by the Land Reform Commission, I, Gayantha Karunathilleke, Minister of Lands and ParliamentaryReforms of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby :-

1.Alienate the agricultural Lands specified in the Schedules hereto which had been vested in the LandReform Commission established under the Land Reform Act, No. 1 of 1972 of the National StateAssembly, in terms of the Section 3 of the said Act by way of sale, to the Board of Investment ofSri Lanka established by the Act, No. 4 of 1978 for the below mentioned purposes as per the Section22 (1) b of said Land Reform Act.

(a)To construct infrastucture and housing for employees required to run an export processing zone/industrial zone ;

(b)To sub lease the Land for investment projects approved by the Board of Investment of Sri Lankaand to divest upon the requirement of Board of Investment.

It has been agreed by the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka to pay a sum of Rs 112,000,000.00 (Hundred and TwelveMillion Rupees) as the total consideration for the said sale in respect of the Land scheduled hereto to the Land ReformCommission.
