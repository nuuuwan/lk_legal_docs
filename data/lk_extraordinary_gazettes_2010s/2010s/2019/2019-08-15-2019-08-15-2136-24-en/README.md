SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Granting Permission by Virtue of Powers Vested in me Under Section 27(A) of ProvincialCouncil Act, No. 42 of 1987 as the Term of the Sixth Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka Ended on 26th September, 2017

2019

1. This permission may be cited as ‘‘Permission to disburse under first financial statement - 2019 of the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council’’ and come in to operation from 19.07.2019.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty One Billion One HundredMillion only, (Rs. 31,100,000,000.00) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2019 and ending 31stDecember, 2019 is hereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Four Billion twenty Three Million Four Hundred and Six Thousandone Hundred and Ninety Seven and Cents Twenty Four only (Rs. 34,023,406,197.24) and grant permission to ChiefSecretary of this province and other Accounting officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial CouncilFund.

3. Accordingly the first schedule of the said gazette notification is amended as the first schedule hereof and grant permissionto disburse the same.

4. This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2019 or upto the date that expires three months (03) from the date ofsummoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council which occurs first.
