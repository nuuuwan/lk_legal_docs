THE INLAND REVENUE ACT, NO. 24 OF 2017

Notice under Section 1(a)(ii) of the Fifth Schedule

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 1(a)(ii) of the Fifth Schedule of the Inland Revenue Act, No. 24 of 2017, I,Mangala Samaraweera Minister of Finance, do hereby declare that the “Centre for Handicapped” organization referredto in the Schedule given below be an approved charity for the purpose of that section.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,09th October, 2019.
