PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION OF SRI LANKA

Notice under Section 20(2) of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009

INTENTION OF REVOKE LICENSES

THE Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (“the Commission”) hereby gives notice pursuant to Section 20(2) of theSri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009 that the Commission intends to revoke the electricity generation licensesgranted to below identified entities in order to generate electricity, due to the amalgamation of those entities with WKVHydro Technics (Pvt) Ltd with effect from 30th March 2018.

The Commission has already issued an electricity generation license for WKV Hydro Technics (Pvt)Ltd to operate Pathaha Oya Mini Hydro Power Plant (1.5 MW) under the Electricity Generation License Number EL/GS/10-056.
