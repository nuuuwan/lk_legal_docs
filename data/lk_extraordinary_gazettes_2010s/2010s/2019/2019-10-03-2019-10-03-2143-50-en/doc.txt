FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MEDIRIGIRIYAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichKudagamage Asoka Karunasena the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Disanayaka Mudiyanselage Amal Disanayaka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Leelanath Wickrama Arachchi, Returning Officer of the Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatDisanayaka Mudiyanselage Amal Disanayaka was selected as the member of the Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha.

LEELANATH WICKRAMA ARACHCHI,Returning Officer,Medirigiriya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Polonnaruwa,03rd October, 2019.
