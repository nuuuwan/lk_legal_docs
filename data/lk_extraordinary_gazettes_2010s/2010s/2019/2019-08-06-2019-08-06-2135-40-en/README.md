PROVINCIAL COUNCIL NOTIFICATIONS

PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF CENTRAL PROVINCE

I, Maithree Gunaratne, the Governor of Central Province, hereby make following regulations by virtue of the powers vested in me bySection 184 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987, which should be read with Section 2 (1) (a) of the Provincial Council(Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989, I hereby impose regulations in respect of following allowances for Members ofPradeshiya Sabha. These regulations shall be effective in respect of fuel and telephone allowances from 08th of May, 2019.

MAITHREE GUNARATNe,

Governor,Central Province.At the Governor’s Office.On this day of 08th of May, 2019.

REGULATIONS

01. These regulations may be cited as Regulations for payment of Fuel and Telephone Allowances for Members of Pradeshiya

Sabhas.

02. These allowances shall be in addition to the regulations published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 1914/40 of 15.05.2015 of

Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka Additionally, the other conditions imposed on furel allowance and telephone allow-ance under L. G. C. circular 01/2013 dated 05.04.2013 issued by me and Extraordinary Gazette No. 1900/08 dated 02.02.2015 willremain effective. Accordingly members of Pradeshiya Sabhas can be paid a fuel allowance and a telephone allowance as shownbelow from the Pradeshiya Sabha Fund of the respective Pradeshiya Sabha according to the given classification of PradeshiyaSabhas.
