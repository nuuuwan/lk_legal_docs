AMENDMENT

UTHTHAMA SEVA PADAKKAMA

Regulations

THE regulation for the Uththama Seva Padakkama published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 156/5 dated 01st September1981 and Extraordinary Gazette No. 1692/29 dated 11th February, 2011 are hereby amended by the substitution of thefollowing new paragraph 5 to be effective only for the year 2018, 2019 and year 2020. Therefore in place of Paragraph 5thereof.

Service Regulation 5 : “The grant of the award shall be restricted to 3% of the officers and 1% of the other ranksper annum from within the Tri Services as at the date of 01st January of the relevant yearshall be separately in numerical value”.

By His Excellency’s Command,

UDAYA R. SENAVIRATHNE,Secretary to the President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo,12th July 2019.
