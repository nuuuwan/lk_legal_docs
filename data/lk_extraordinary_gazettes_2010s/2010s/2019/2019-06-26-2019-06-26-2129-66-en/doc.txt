INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT (CHAPTER 131)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 22 of the Industrial Disputes Act, Chapter 131 of the LegislativeEnactments of Ceylon (Revised Edition 1956) as amended by the Industrial Disputes (Amendments) Act, Nos. 14 and 62of 1957 and No. 4 of 1962 [read with Industrial Disputes (Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1968], I, MaithripalaSirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby-

(1) Appoint the persons specified in the Schedule hereto be members of the panel of which Industrial Courts shallbe constituted, and

(2) Specify the period of three years from the effective date of appointment as the period of office of such members.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,24th June 2019.
