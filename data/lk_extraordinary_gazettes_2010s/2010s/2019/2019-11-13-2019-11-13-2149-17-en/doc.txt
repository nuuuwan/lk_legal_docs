PROCLAMATION BY THE PRESIDENT

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA

WHEREAS the premises of the Shrine of Our Lady of Madhu situated in the Divisional Secretary's Division in the Districtof Mannar in the Northern Province of Sri Lanka is highly venerated by Catholic devotees in Sri Lanka and the world over,And whereas the Shrine of our Lady Madhu has been proclaimed an Urban Development area by Gazette NotificationNo. 2145/55 dated 19.10.2019 under Section 6(2)(b) and 21(2)(b) of the Town and Country Planning Ordinance No. 13 of1946 aimed at providing the full range of facilities required to ensure the convenience of the devotees visiting to payhomage at the Shirne of Our Lady of Madhu.

And whereas Madhu pilgrimage regulations have been promulgated by Gazette Notification No. 185 dated 19thMarch 1982 under Pilgrimages Ordinance No. 13 of 1896 with the view of providing the opportunity for the hundreds ofthousand of people visiting the Madhu Pilgrims' area during the said Madhu Pilgrims' season to stay in without their havingto undergo difficulties.

And, in conformity with the aforesaidn regulations and emphazing the fact that the said extent of land should beutilized subject to the provisions enforced by the Acts : Conservation Ordinance No. 10 of 1885, Flora and Fauna ProtectionOrdinance No. 2 of 1937 and the National Environment Act, No. 47 of 1980. Since the aforesaid extent of land falls withinboundaries of the Madhu Road Forest Reservation proclaimed by Gazette Notification No. 1779/15 dated 10.10.2012 andthe Madhu Road National Park proclaimed by Gazette Notification No. 1920/3 dated 22.06.2015.
