SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof the list of commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy at the rate specifiedin the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from July 04th, 2019, subject to provisions of SubSection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,July 03, 2019.
