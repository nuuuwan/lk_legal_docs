FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Lakmini Thushari Gunawardhana the memberelected to Ward No. 11 Kottawa West of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 11 Kottawa West of MaharagamaUrban Council who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified underSection 8 of the Local authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has Communicated his decision to nominateGanegoda Haputhanthrige Sarojini to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of the MaharagamaUrban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) (1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Ganegoda Haputhanthrige Sarojini was elected as the member of the Ward No. 11 Kottawa West ofMaharagama Urban Council.

S.D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,25th February 2019.
