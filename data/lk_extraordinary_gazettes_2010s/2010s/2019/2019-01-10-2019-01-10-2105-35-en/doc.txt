FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BANDARAWELAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Bandarawela Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group No. 1 to which Naleen Priyantha Suriyage the memberbelonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of thatward No. 05, Bandarawela West of Bandarawela Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Indepdendent Group No. 1 has communicated his decision tonominate Heenkenda Mudiyanselage Mithrapala Heenkenda to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, H. I. R. Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of the Bandarawela Municipal Council, actingin terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that HeenkendaMudiyanselage Mithrapala Heenkanda was elected as the member of the Ward No. 05 Bandarawela West of BandarawelaMunicipal Council.

H. I. R. HATHURUSINGHA,Returning Officer,Bandarawela Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Badulla,09th January 2019.
