FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PORATIVU PATTUPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Porativu Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Thamil Makkal Viduthalai Pulikal, the recognized political party to whichKrishnapillai Sivanesan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Thamil Makkal Viduthalai Pulikal who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Gopalapillai Rasu to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Porativu Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that GopalapillaiRasu was selected as the member of the Porativu Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. SASEELAN,Returning Officer,Porativu Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Batticaloa,29th August, 2019.
