THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

REVOCATION UNDER SECTION 39(1)

By the Order made under the Proviso (a) to Section 38 of the LandAcquisition Act (Chapter 460) and published in the GazetteExtra Ordinary No. 2036/3 dated 11.09.2017, and the land specifiedmorefully in the Order No. 216 of 2017 dated 28.08.2017 was vestedin the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. But the possessionof the said land has not yet been taken for and on behalf of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. Therefore, I, GayanthaKarunatilleke, Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms, byvirtue of powers vested in me under section 39 (1) of the LandAcquisition Act, do hereby revoke the land sepcified morefully inthe aforesaid Order No. 216 of 2017 dated 28.08.2017 published inthe Gazette Extra Ordinary No. 2036/3 dated 11.09.2017.

Gayantha Karunatilleke,Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms.
