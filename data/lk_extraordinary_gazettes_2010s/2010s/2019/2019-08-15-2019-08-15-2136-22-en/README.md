FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VELANAI PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichPulenthiran Kajan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Aarumugam Kobalakirshnan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendran Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AarumugamKobalakirshnan was selected as the member of the Velani Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,15th August, 2019.
