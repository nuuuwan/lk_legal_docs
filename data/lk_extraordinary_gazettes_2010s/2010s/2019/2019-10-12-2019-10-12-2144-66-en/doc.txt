FIFTH SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OFPROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT, No. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SIXTH SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIALCOUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26TH SEPTEMBER, 2017.

2018

1. This permission may be cited as ‘‘ Permission to disburse under Fifth financial statement - 2018 of the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council’’ and come into operation from 26.12.2018.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Two Billion Eight Hundredand Thirty Four Million, Four Hundred and Eight Thousand One Hundred and Twelve and Cents Thirty Five only(Rs. 32,834,408,112.35) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2018 and ending 31st December, 2018,which is specified in 2nd paragraph of Gazette Notification No. 2052/15 dated 03.01.2018, which is amended by fourthsupplementary financial statement for the year 2018 and assented on 13.11.2018, is hereby amended as, Rupees ThirtyTwo Billion Nine Hundred and Thirty Four Million, Four Hundred and Eight Thousand One Hundred and Twelve andCents Thirty Five Only, (Rs. 32,934,408,112.35) and grant permission to Chief Secretary of this Province and otherAccounting Officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

NILUKA EKANAYAKE,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council.

26th day of December, 2018.

AUTHORIZATION

I, do hereby grant Permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council todisburse as per the Fourth supplementary financial statement for the year 2018, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 27 (a) of the provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Two Billion Eight Hundred andThirty Four Million, Four Hundred and Eight Thousand One Hundred and Twelve and Cents Thirty Five Only(Rs. 32,834,408,112.35) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2018 and ending 31st December, 2018, whichis specified in annual financial statement - 2018 (amended) is hereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Two Billion Nine Hundred andThirty Four Million, Four Hundred and Eight Thousand One Hundred and Twelve and Cents Thirty Five only(Rs. 32,934,408,112.35) and grant permission to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2018 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date of summoningthe 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilwhich occurs first.

NILUKA EKANAYAKE,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province.

26th day of December, 2018.
