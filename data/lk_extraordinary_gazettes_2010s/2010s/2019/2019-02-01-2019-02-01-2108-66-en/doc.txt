GLASSWARE MANUFACTURING TRADE

In pursuance of the authority conferred on me by the Minister of Labour by an order made by him under Section 33(1)(a) ofthe Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136) and published in the Gazzette Extraordinary No. 58 of 12th October, 1979,I, Ananda Wimalaweera, Commissioner of Labour, hereby notify under Section 28 of the aforesaid Ordinance, that I proposeto determine under Section 33(2) of that Ordinance the minimum rate of wages for time work set out in the schedule heretoin respect of the workers employed in the Glassware Manufacturing Trade.
