THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on the recommendation of the University GrantsCommission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1567/23 ofSeptember 19, 2008 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2079/10 of July 09, 2018)which specifies the Faculties and Departments of the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka, by the substitution for the entry “3.Department of Marketing” appearing in Column II thereof which corresponds to the item “Faculty of Management andFinance” appearing in Column I thereof of the entry “3. Department of Marketing”.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAMEEM,

Minister of City Planning,Water Supply and Higher Education.Colombo,11th February, 2019.
