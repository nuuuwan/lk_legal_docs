PROVINCIAL COUNCIL NOTIFICATIONS

CENTRAL PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Imposing of Entertainment Taxes by the Kandy Municipal Council

The following resolution passed by the Kandy Municipal Council under decision No. 8 (23) at the General Meeting held on 20.12.2018in terms of Sub - section (I) of Section 2 of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance (Chapter 267) has been approved by the Hon. Governor ofthe Central Province in terms of Sub - section (2) of that Section and is hereby published in terms of that Sub - Section.

MAITHREE GUNARATNE,

Hon. Governor,Central Province.At Governor’s Office,On this 22nd day of April, 2019.

RESOLUTION

In accordance with Section 2 (1) of the Entertainment Tax ordinance and Entertainment Tax (Amended) Act, No. 37 of 1984, the KandyMunicipal Council hereby resolves to impose and levy an entertainment Tax equivalent to 25% of the face value of entry tickets for everymagic show, circus, horse show, musical show, exhibition match, carnival and any other event where a fee is charged and an entertainmenttax equivalent to 7.5% of the face value of entry tickets to films in cinemas hed in the area within the administrative limits of the KandyMunicipal Council.
