FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NEGOMBO MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Negombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to bemember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Freedom Front, the recognized political party to whichPonnamperumage Fred Dexter the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member if United National Freedom Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Balasuriyage Nanda Sulochana Perera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasinghe Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the NegomboMunicipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Balasuriyage Nanda Sulochana Perera was selected as the member of the Negombo Municipal Council.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Negombo Municipal Council.District Election Office,Gampaha,01st July, 2019.
