FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHIYANGANAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a member of Mahiyangana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group to which Dissanayake Mudiyanselage Shayamalee KumariDissanayake the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon the member of that Ward No. 06 Dambana of Mahiyangana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such IndependentGroup and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateAkurugoda Kalu Arachchilage Manel Asokamala Kaluarachchi to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, H. I. R. Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of the Mahiyangana Pradeshiya Sabha , acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AkurugodaKalu Arachchilage Manel Asokamala Kaluarachchi was elected as the member of the Ward No. 06, Dambana of MahiyanganaPradeshiya Sabha .

H. I. R. HATHURUSINGHE,Returning Officer,Mahiyangana Pradeshiya Sabha .District Election Office,Badulla,16th May, 2019.
