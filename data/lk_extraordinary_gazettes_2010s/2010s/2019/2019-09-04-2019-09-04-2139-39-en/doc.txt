FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PITABEDDARAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party towhich Anil Chandranath Wijesiri Jayawardana Weeraman the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredselected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Gigummaduwa Kankanamge Ajith Wasantha Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Tananthiri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the Pitabeddara PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Gigummaduwa Kankanamge Ajith Wasantha Kumara was selected as the member of the Pitabeddara PradeshiyaSabha.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Matara District,02nd September, 2019.
