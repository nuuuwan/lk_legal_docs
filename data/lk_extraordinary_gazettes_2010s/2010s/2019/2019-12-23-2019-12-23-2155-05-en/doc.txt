EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

My Reference No.: J/DS/2/9/4/16-2016. Land and Parliamentary Ministry’s Ref. No.: 4-3/9/2017/

WS/70. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the land Acquisition Act, as amended by the land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the Land or Servitude to be Acquired: Preliminary Plan No. : YA/JFF/2017/114-PPYA 2947 Village : Vannarpannai West G. N. Division : J/88, New Moor Street D. S. Division : Jaffna District : Jaffna Province : Northern Province Purpose : To construct the Sewerage Pumping Station 25

Name of the land: Kuruthupidungi Vayal Assmt. No. 08 (Part) Araly Road; Land Use: Garden, Contains one

masonry foundation; Owner: Navamani Vasanthini wife of Nadarasa Navarajan; Extent: 0.0688 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/lands on which a servitude is to be acquired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat, Jaffna on the 14th day of February, 2020 at 10.00 a. m. and are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 20th day of January, 2020 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

gfg S. SUTHARASAN, Divisional Secretary, Divisional Secretariat, Jaffna.

Divisional Secretariat, Jaffna, 18th December 2019.
