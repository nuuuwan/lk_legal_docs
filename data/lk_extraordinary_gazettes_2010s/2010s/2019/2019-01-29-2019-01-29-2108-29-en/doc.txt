FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE JAFFNA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Jaffna Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to be a member of thecouncil ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Eelam People’s Democratic Party, the recognized political party to whichKarthigesu Velum Mayilum Kukenthiran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member if Eelam People’s Democratic Party who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Sebasthiyampillei Mariyadasan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, Returning Officer of the Jaffna Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that SebasthiyampilleiMariyadasan was selected as the member of the Jaffna Municipal Council.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Jaffna Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Jaffna,28th January, 2019.
