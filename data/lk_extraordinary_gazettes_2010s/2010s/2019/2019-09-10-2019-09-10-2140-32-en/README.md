FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VAVUNIYA NORTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichThirunavukkarasu Vijikaran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member if Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as member a within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Kanagar Kanapathippillai to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Malawenna Godage Lalith Ananda, Returning Officer of the Vavuniya North PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Kanagar Kanapathippillai was selected as the member of the Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha.

M. G. LALITH ANANDA,Returning Officer,Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Vavuniya,09th September, 2019.
