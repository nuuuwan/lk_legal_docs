CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, P. S. M. Charles, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 07th January, 2019, all duties of Customs aswell as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid andreceived at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2104/1 of 31st December,2018 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notification to bepublished on or before 13th January, 2019.

P. S. M. CHARLES,Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,04th January, 2019.
