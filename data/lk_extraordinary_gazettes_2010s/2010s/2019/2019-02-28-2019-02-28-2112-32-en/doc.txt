MERITORIOUS SERVICE MEDAL

REGULATIONS made by the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, to grant, forfeit, restore andother matters in amplification of the Provisions set out in the Presidential Warrant.

By His Excellency's Command,

UDAYA R. SENEVIRATNE, Secretary to the President.

Office of the Secretary to the President,Colombo,February 21, 2019.

Meritorious Service Medal

1. The Medal shall be awarded to the Minister of Defence, the State Minister of Defence, the Secretary of Defenceof the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka for the valuable contribution made since 19.05.2009 and all Ranks ofthe Regular and Volunteer Force of the Army, Navy and Air Force who have been in active service on Nineteen day of MayTwo Thousand Nine or thereafter, and who have completed an aggregate period of Six Years excluding the basic trainingperiod with a good conduct and the permanent civil staff members with Six years of aggregate service in Armed Services.

2. Those who have retired, cashiered, discharged or resigned before the completion of six years from the Nineteenthday of May Two Thousand Nine are not eligible for the Medal.

3. The award may be conferred posthumously to the lawful next-of-kin of the recipient or to a person nominatedby the next-of-kin.

4. A Board of not less than three Senior Officers nominated by the respective Service Commanders will examinethe recommendations made for the award of the Meritorious Service Medal. The recommendation of the Board along withthe recommendation of the respective Service Commanders will be transmitted to His Excellency the President throughthe Secretary, Defence for His Excellency's approval. The recommendations of the Minister of Defence, the State Ministerof Defence and the Secretary of Defence of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka should be sent directly by theSecretary of Defence for His Excellency's approval.

5. Every recommendation shall include the Number, Rank and Name in full, of the person recommended.

6. The names of persons upon whom the Medal is conferred upon, shall be published in the respective ServiceOrders and register of all such recipients shall be maintained under the direction of the respective Service Commander.

7. When a person who is a recipient of the Medal suffers death by sentence of a Court Martial, or any other Court,he shall forfeit the Medal conferred to him unless His Excellency the President shall direct otherwise. A register of thenames of the Minister of Defence, the State Minister of Defence, and the Secretary of Defence of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka shall be maintained under the Ministry of Defence.

8. Any Officer of the Sri Lanka Army, Navy or Air Force who is cashiered, dismissed or has been called upon toresign or retire his Commission, shall forfeit the Medal conferred on him unless His Excellency the President shall directotherwise.

9. Every soldier, seaman or airman who has been discharged from the Army, Navy and Air Force with disgrace orwith ignominy, shall forfeit the Medal unless His Excellency the President shall direct otherwise.

10. When a person who is a recipient of the Medal is convicted of an offence of the following nature, that is to saytreason, sedition, mutiny, cowardice, desertion or disgraceful conduct of an unnatural kind or is dismissed or discharged orwho is called upon to resign his Commission by His Excellency the President for misconduct or who whilst subject toMilitary, Naval and Air Force Law, is convicted by the civil power, shall forfeit the Medal conferred to him unless HisExcellency the President shall direct otherwise.

11. A Medal forfeited by any person may be restored by His Excellency the President at his discretion.

12. A notice of forfeiture or restoration shall in every case be published in the respective Service Orders.

13. When a Medal has been lost, and it is desirous to replace it, such a loss should be reported to the Police. Adeclaration testified by a Justice of the Peace stating the circumstances under which the loss occurred should be submittedwith a complaint made to the Police. This declaration shall be forwarded to the Commanding Officer who will transmit it tothe respective Service Commander through the usual channel of communication. The Medal lost therewith will be replacedon payment if the explanation as to the loss is considered satisfactory.

14. In those Regulations -

(a)“Service” means-The Sri Lanka Army,The Sri Lanka Navy andThe Sri Lanka Air Force

(b)“Service Commanders” means-The Commander of the Army,The Commander of the Navy andThe Commander of the Air Force.

(c)“Military Personnel” means-Army, Navy and Air Force personnel

(d)“Member of the Civil Staff” means-Civilians Working in the Army, Navy and Air Force

(e)“Army” means-The Sri Lanka Army

(f)“Navy” means-The Sri Lanka Navy

(g)“Air Force” means-The Sri Lanka Air Force

(h)“Soldier” means-Male/Female Soldier

(i)“Seaman”-Also includes a Woman Sailor

(j)“Airman”-Also includes an Air Woman
