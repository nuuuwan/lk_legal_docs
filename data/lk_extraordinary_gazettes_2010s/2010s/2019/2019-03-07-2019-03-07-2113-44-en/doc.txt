FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE SIYAMBALANDUWAPRADESHSIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Siyambalanduwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichDissanayaka Mudiyanselage Loku Banda the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon the member of that No. 01 Buddama of Siyambalanduwa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to suchparty and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared electedas a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Adikari Mudiyanselage Appuhami to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Indika Gayan Pathirana, returning officer of the Siyambalanduwa Pradeshiya Sabha acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that AdikariMudiyanselage Appuhami was elected as the member of the No. 01 Buddama of Siyambalanduwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

INDIKA GAYAN PATHIRANA,Returning Officer,Siyambalanduwa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Monaragala,06th March, 2019.
