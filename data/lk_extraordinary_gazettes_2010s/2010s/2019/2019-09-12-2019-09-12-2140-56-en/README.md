FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WELIVITIYA - DIVITHURAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Welivitiya - Divithura Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofresignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party the recognized political party to which SethuhewaPunsis Somawardhana the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Akuratiya Gamage Sriyani Renuka to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanda Uda Chandralal, Returning Officer of the Welivitiya - Divithura PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Akuratiya Gamage Sriyani Renuka was selected as the member of the Welivitiya - Divithura Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. U. CHANDRALAL,Returning Officer,Welivitiya - Divithura Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Galle,12th September, 2019.
