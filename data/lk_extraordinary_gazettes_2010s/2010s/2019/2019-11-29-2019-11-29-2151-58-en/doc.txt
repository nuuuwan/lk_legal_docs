CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Direction under Section 10(1)(a)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 10(1)(a) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, theConsumer Affairs Authority directs all Manufacturers and Traders of food wrappers (Lunch Sheets) that on each and everypacket or bundle of such food wrappers, a lable containing the following information in all three languages shall beincluded.

i.Brand Nameii.Maximum Retail Priceiii.Name and Address of the Manufactureriv.Date of manufacture and shelf lifev.Material used (Virgin PLA, Starch, PABT etc.)vi.Material Declaration (Food Grade and Biodegradable/Compostable)vii.Central Environment Authority Registration Numberviii.Size of a wrapper (in cm/inches)ix.Number of wrappers in a packet/bundle
