L.D.B 21/78(XVI)(A)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 24A(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Subsection (1) of Section 24A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I,A. Ibathul Rauff Hakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, in consultation with the UniversityGrants Commission, do by this Order -

(1) establish with effect from 09.09.2019 the Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences as an Institute for HigherLearning, for the purpose of providing, promoting and developing higher educaion ; and

(2) assign to such Institute the name and style of “the Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences, University ofPeradeniya”.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,09th September, 2019.
