THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) AS

Notice under Section 7

Ref. No.: 3/3/6/2/16.

I, hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Governmentintends to acquire the following land which are required for apublic purpose.

Land Acquisition for Katudeniya Clinical Center.

Lot No. 01 and 02 in total extent of above 0.0441 Hectare oflands in preminary plan mu/p/ma/1763 prepared by seniorsuperintendent of Matale, situated at Katudeniya Village inKatudeniya Grama Niladhari Division, of Minor Division inMatale Udasiyapaththu of Divisional Secretary DivisionUkuwela in the District of Matale Central Province.

Lot No. 01Claimant: Land Reforems Commission; Extent: 0.0017Hectare.

Lot No. 02Claimant: Land Reforems Commission; Extent: 0.0424Hectare.

All person interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agents duly authorized inwaiting before me 9.30 a.m. on 24.07.2019 at DivisionalSecretariat Ukuwela.

And are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (induplicate) before the 03rd day of July 2019, the nature of theirinterest in the lands, the particulars of their claims ofacquisition of the said lands, the amount of the compensationand the details of the compensation of such amount.
