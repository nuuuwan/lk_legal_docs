LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: Miri/Dev. Sec. Land/3/2016/05.

Land Ministry Ref.No.: 4-3/2/2016/CEB/24.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofLand Acquisition Act, that the Government has intended toacquire the undermentioned land/Servitude for a publicpurpose.

Description of the Land/servitude to be acquired :

Two allotments of lands describe below as depicted in LotNo. 928 to Lot 929 and total extents of 0.9291 Hectare inthe Plan in Zone No. 1 in the page No. 17 in the Cadaster MapNo. 510243 dated 05.10.2018 prepared by the SeniorSuperintendent of Gampaha District instead of the SurveyorGeneral, and situated in the village of Udugama in theMadapattuwa minor Division in the Division of the MirigamaDivisional Secretariat in Gampaha District of the WesternProvince.

01. Lot No. 928Name of the Land : Kadurugahalanda; Description: Gardenwith mixed crop; Claimed by: Government; Original Claimedby: Dharmawathi Piris, No. 25, Udugama, Veyangoda (Portionof the land Lot No. 330); Extent : 0.1334 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 929Name of the Land : Kadurugahalanda; Description: Gardenwith mixed crop; Claimed by: Government; Original Claimedby: Dharmawathi Piris, No. 25, Udugama, Veyangoda (Portionof the land Lot No. 330); Extent : 0.7957 Hectare.

All persons interested possessed the right or with anyother relation in the aforesaid lands/servitude are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agents duly authorized inwriting before me at the Dompe Divisional Secretariat on 6thMarch, 2019 at 9.30 a.m.

Further, they are hereby requested to notify to me in writingwith two copies on or before 25th February, 2019 the natureof their interest in the Land/servitude, the particulars of theirclaims for compensation for the acquisition of the said lands,the amount of compensation and the details of thecomputation of such amount.

Y. I. M. SILVA,Divisional Secretary and Acquisition Officer,Mirigama.

Divisional Secretary's Office,Mirigama,18th January, 2019.
