EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE DEHIWALA - MOUNT LAVINIAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reasonof resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichDilini Nelunika Antonet Weerasinghe the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Lathpandurage Diana Gayani Perera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senaratne, Returning Officer of the Dehiwala - MountLavinia Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter262) do hereby declare that Lathpandurage Diana Gayani Perera was selected as the member of the Dehiwala - MountLavinia Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATNE,Returning Officer,Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council.District Election Office,Colombo City,08th May, 2019.
