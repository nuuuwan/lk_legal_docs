FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VADAMARADCHI SOUTHWEST PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIESELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to whichYothiswarasarma Nagaraja Sarma the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward 17 - Thunnalai if Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha whobelongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mahendiran Pireman to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Vadamaradchi South WestPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Mahendiran Pireman was elected as the member of the Ward 17 - Thunnalai of Vadamaradchi SouthWest Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Vadamaradchi South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,23rd July, 2019.
