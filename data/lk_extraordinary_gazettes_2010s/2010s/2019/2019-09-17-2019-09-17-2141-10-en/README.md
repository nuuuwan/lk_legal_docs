FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PANVILA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of the return member of Panvila Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to bemember of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United Progressive Alliance (Democratic People’s Front at Present), therecognized political party to which Suppaiya Uma Saraswathi the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of United Progressive Alliance (DemocraticPeople’s Front at Present), who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Soosai Vijayamala to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of the PanvilaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Soosai Vijayamala was selected as the member of the Panvila Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Panvila Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,12th September, 2019.
