ORDER UNDER SECTION 68(A)(1) OF THE SRI LANKA TELECOMMUNICATION(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 27 OF 1996

I, Minister of Defence of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as the Minister in charge of the subjects of SriLanka Telecommunication Regulatory Commission in terms of powers vested in me by Section 68(A)(1) of the SriLanka Telecommunication (Amendment) Act, No. 27 of 1996, I, do hereby approve the acquisition of immovable propertydescribed below depicting the letter “A” in Clarification Advanced Tracing bearing No. CO/COL/2018/703 of SurveyorGeneral required for the development activites in second phase of the “Lotus Tower” project.
