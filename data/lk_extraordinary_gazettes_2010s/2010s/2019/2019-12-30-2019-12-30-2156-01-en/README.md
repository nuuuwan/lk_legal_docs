CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, A. V. Janadara, Director General of Customs (Covering up duties), determine that with effect from 30th December, 2019, allduties of Customs as well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235),shall be paid and received at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2155/1 of23rd December, 2019 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similarnotification to be published on or before 05th January, 2020.

A. V. JANADARA,Director General of Customs(Covering up duties).Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,27th December, 2019.
