FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AKKARAIPATTUMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a return member of Akkaraipattu Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Front for Good Governance, the recognized political party towhich Noorutheen Muhammath Siraj the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of National Front for Good Governance who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Muhammathu Muhaiyadeen Muhammath Thaiyar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Don Thilana Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of the Akkaraipattu MunicipalCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Muhammathu Muhaiyadeen Muhammath Thaiyar was selected as the member of the Akkaraipattu Municipal Council.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Akkaraipattu Municipal Council.District Election Office,Ampara,29th April, 2019.
