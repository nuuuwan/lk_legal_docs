FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KALUTARA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kalutara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which GardiHewage Chamila Kumari the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Govinnage Rammyamala Ranjani Perera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Wannakuwatta Waduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the KalutaraPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Govinnage Rammyamala Ranjani Perera was selected as the member of the Kalutara Pradeshiya Sabha.

KALINDRA JAYAWEERA FERNANDO,Returning Officer,Kalutara Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kalutara,20th August, 2019.
