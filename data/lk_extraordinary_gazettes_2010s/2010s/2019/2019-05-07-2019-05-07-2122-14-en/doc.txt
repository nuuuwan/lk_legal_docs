THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: YAK/LAN/01/03/16.

Land Ministry's Reference No.: 4-3/15/2017/VE/364.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Governmentintends to acquire the following land/servitude which isrequired for a public purpose.

Description of the Land or Servitude to be Acquired:

An allotment of land in extent of 4.9954 Hectares shown asLot No. 858 depicted in Sheet 25, supplementary 24 of FinalVillage Plan No. 638 described below prepared by SurveyorGeneral, situated in the Village of Nakiyadeniya in the GramaNiladhari Division of 179A Nakiyadeniya in the DivisionalSecretary's Division of Thalpe Pattuwa North, Yakkalamullain Galle District, Southern Province.

Lot No. 858Name of Land: Thalangahawatte; Usage of Land: Gardenwith mixed cultivation; Claimants: State; Original claimant:Watawala Plantation Company; Extent: 4.9954 Hectares.

All persons interested in the aforesaid Land/servitude arehereby requested to appear personally, or by agents dulyauthorized in writing, before me at Yakkalamulla DivisionalSecretariat on the 29th day of May 2019 at 9.00 a.m. and arerequested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or beforethe 22nd day of May 2019 the nature of their interests in theland, the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of he said land/servitude, the amount ofcompensation and the details of the computation of suchamount in duplicate.

CHANDIMA C. MUHANDIRAMGE,

Divisional Secretary,

Yakkalamulla.

Divisional Secretariat, Yakkalamulla,29th April, 2019.
