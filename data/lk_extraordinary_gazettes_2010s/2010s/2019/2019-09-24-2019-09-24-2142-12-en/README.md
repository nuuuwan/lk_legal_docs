L.D.B. 2/1995

ARBITRATION ACT, No. 11 OF 1995

Order

BY virtue of the powers vested in me in terms of the Arbitration Act, No. 11 of 1995, I, Thalatha Atukorale, Minister ofJustice and Prison Reforms do by this Order, with the concurrence of the Chief Justice, designate the High Court of SriLanka holden in the Judicial Zone of Jaffna and in the Judicial Zone of Kandy in addition to the High Court of Sri Lankaholden in the Judicial Zone of Colombo for the purpose of the implementation of the provisions of the Arbitration Act,No. 11 of 1995.

THALATHA ATHUKORALE,Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms.

Colombo,September 12, 2019.
