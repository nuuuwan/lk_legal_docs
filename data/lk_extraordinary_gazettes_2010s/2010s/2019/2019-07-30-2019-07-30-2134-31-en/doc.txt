FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PUTHUKKUDIYIRUPPUPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Anton Radnaraja Lavanja the member belonged,was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if IndependentGroup who belongs to such Independent Group, and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities ElectionsOrdinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateSelvarani Arulanantham to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanthirasa Kantheepan, Returning Officer of the Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatSelvarani Arulanantham was selected as the member of the Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. KANTHEEPAN,Returning Officer,Puthukkudiyiruppu Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Mullaitivu,29th July 2019.
