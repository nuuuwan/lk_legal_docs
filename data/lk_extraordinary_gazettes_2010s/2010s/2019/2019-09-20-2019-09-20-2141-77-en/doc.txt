THE FINANCE ACT, No. 11 OF 1963

Order under Section 72

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 72 read with Section 71 of the Finance Act, No. 11 of 1963, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance, do by this Order, vest in the People’s Bank established under the People’s Bank Act,No. 29 of 1961, with effect from 20th September, two thousand nineteen, the properties described in the Schedule hereto.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance.Colombo,20th September, 2019.

Amirtham and on the West by : V. C. road (ChurchRoad).
