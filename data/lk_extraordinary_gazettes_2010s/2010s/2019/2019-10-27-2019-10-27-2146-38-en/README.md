Directions issued by the Election Commission in addition to the Directions to prevent the misuse of movable orimmovable state property published in the Gazette Notification No. 2141/52 of 19.09.2019

1.All public servants and the members of security forces and police officers engaged in active service, shouldrefrain from taking photographs or videoing the marking of ballot papers, the marked ballot papers or the processin the polling stations or in postal voting centres, or aiding or providing opportunity to do so. Taking photographsand videoing or attempting to do so, is an offence. It is the responsibility of the officers engaged in pollingstations to prevent the commission or any attempt to commit such illegal activity by any voter or officer. It isalso an offence to publish photographs of polling stations, marked or unmarked ballot papers or videos throughelectronic, print or social media.

2.On the part of the public officers who do not have political rights and members of the Armed Forces and Policeofficers in active service, it is an offence to publish their photographs or declarations through electronic, printor any other media, for the purpose of promoting or prejudicing a political party or a candidate or aiding orconsenting any others to do so.
