APPOINTMENT OF RELEVANT OFFICERS FOR THE ADVISORY COMMITTEE UNDER URBANDEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY ACT, No. 41 OF 1978

I, do hereby appoint following officers for the Advisory Committee under Section 7(1), 7(2), 7(3) of the UrbanDevelopment Authority Act, No. 41 of 1978, to carry out a comprehensive inquiry on the demands for housing facilitieswith special needs and to provide appropriate recommendations, focusing special attention on the requisitions made bythe applicants who are resident within the areas of Urban Regeneration Project implemented by Urban DevelopmentAuthority under Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development.
