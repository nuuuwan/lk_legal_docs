ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT REPORT

IT is hereby notified that the Report of Environmental Impact Assessment study on proposed Liquefied Natural Gas(LNG) Infrastructure Development Project at Colombo Port, Kalanitissa, Kerawalapitiya submitted by the Sri LankaPorts Authority, No. 19, Chaithya Road, Colombo 01 on behalf of the Sri Lanka Gas Terminals Company Ltd as requiredby the Section 16(1) of the Coast Conversation and Coastal Resource Managment Act, No. 57 of 1981 as amended by theAct, No. 64 of 1988 and No. 49 of 2011 will be available for inspection by the public at the following institutions from8.30 a. m. to 4.15 p. m. for a period of 30 days from 25.11.2019 (Except weekends and public holidays)
