UNIVERSITY OF VOCATIONAL TECHNOLOGY ACT, No. 31 OF 2008

Order under Section 8(1)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 8(1)(b) of the University of Vocational Technology Act, No. 31 of 2008,I, Bandula Gunawardana, Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, on being satisfied that the administrationof the affairs of the University of Vocational Technology, Sri Lanka, have been seriously dislocated and that the authoritiesof the said University have failed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Senior Professor WachchaPatabendege Ranjith Premalal de Silva to be the Competent Authority with effect from December 18, 2019 for thepurpose of exercising, peforming and discharging in lieu of the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Vocational Technology,Sri Lanka, any power, duty or function respectively which has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Vice-Chancellor under the said Act, or under any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By-Law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

BANDULA GUNAWARDANA,Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation.

Colombo,December 18, 2019.
