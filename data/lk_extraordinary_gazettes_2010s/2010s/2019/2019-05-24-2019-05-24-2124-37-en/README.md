FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KARACHCHI PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Karachchi Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichKubendraselvan Puvidan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Tamil United Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Kandaiya Dharmalingam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Denicius Canute Aravintharaj, Returning Officer of the Karachchi Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatKandaiya Dharmalingam was selected as the member of the Karachchi Pradeshiya Sabha.

D. C. ARAVINTHARAJ,Returning Officer,Karachchi Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kilinochchi,23rd May, 2019.
