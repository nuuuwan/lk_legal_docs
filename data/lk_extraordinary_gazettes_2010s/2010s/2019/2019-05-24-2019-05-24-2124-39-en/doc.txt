FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GALLE MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Galle Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Group Leader of the Independent Group 02 to which Undugoda Pathirannehelage Kulathilakathe member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember if Independent Group 02 who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Group Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group 02 has communicated his decision tonominate Loku Yaddehige Anuradha Gayan Sanjeewa to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Kanda Uda Chandralal, Returning Officer of the Galle Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Loku YaddehigeAnuradha Gayan Sanjeewa was selected as the member of the Galle Municipal Council.

K. U. CHANDRALAL,Returning Officer,Galle Municipal Council.District Election Office,Galle,23rd May, 2019.
