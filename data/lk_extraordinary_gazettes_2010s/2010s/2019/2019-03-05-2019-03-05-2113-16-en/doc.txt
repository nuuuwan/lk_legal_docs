FOOD ACT, No. 26 OF 1980

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, in terms of Section 32 of the FoodAct, No. 26 of 1980 in consultation with the Food Advisory Committee.

DR. RAJITHA SENARATHNE,Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine.Colombo,12th February, 2019.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Preservatives) Regulation, 2019, and shall come into operationfrom 01.01.2020.

2. No person shall manufacture, import, store, transport, sell, offer for sale, distribute, use or advertise -

(a) any food preservative other than the preservatives specified in Schedule I hereto ;

(b) any food which has in or upon it any preservative, other than those specified in Schedule II hereto.

3. Any food specified in Column I of Schedule II (hereinafter referred to as a “specified food”) may have in orupon it, a preservative of the description specified in the corresponding entry of Column II therof (hereinafter referred toas a “permitted preservative”) and the use of such preservative shall be in compliance with the proportions set out in thecorresponding entry of Column III thereof.

4. Any specified food may have in or upon it, a mixture of two or more preservatives specified in Schedule IIhereto, only if the sum total of the preservatives does not exceed one hundred percentage when the quantity of each of suchpreservative present in such food is expressed as a percentage of the maximum quantity of that preservative appropriate tothe food as specified in that Schedule.

5. Any compounded food may contain any permitted preservative -

(a) introduced in the process of preparing such food using one or more specified foods, if the permittedpreservative :-

(i) is present in any specified food used in the compounded food ; and

(ii) is present in the specified food used, in no greater proportion than the proportion specified in relationto the proportion specified in Column III of Schedule II.

(b) if the compounded food is a specified food and does not contain any permitted preservatives in any greaterproportion than is specified in Column III thereto.

6. No person shall manufacture, import, store, transport, sell, offer for sale, distribute, use or advertise any permittedpreservative (including any permitted preservative mixed with any other substance) for use as an ingredient in the preparationof food-

(a) unless such preservative conforms to the standards of purity prescribed by the standards prescribed by theCombine Compendium of Food Additives Monographs by JECFA of the Food an Agricultural Organization- (FAO)

(b) except in a container bearing a label in accordance with the requirements contained in paragraphs (1) and(2) of Schdeule III hereto.

7. All proporations mentioned in these regulations are proportions calculated by the weight of the product unlessa contrary intention is expressed.

8. The label on every container or package of food, to which a permitted preservative as specified in these regulationshas been added, shall have a declaration as set out in the Food (Labelling and Advertising) Regulation 2005 published in theGazette Extraordinary No. 1376/9 of January 19, 2005 as amended from time to time or replaced.

9. The Food (Preservatives) Regulations 1989 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 615/11 of 19th June1990 are hereby rescinded.

10. In these Regulations -

“Preservative” means a food additive, which prolongs the shelf-life of a food by protecting againstdeterioration caused by microorganisms.

“Compounded food” means food containing two or more ingredients.

SCHEDULE III

1. Each container of package to which regulaiton 3 applies shall bear a label indicating a statement -

(a) of each permitted preservative, the common name and INS number ;(b) where more than one substance is present, the common Games and INS numbers ;(c) where a permitted preservative is admixed with a diluent the proportions of the diluent and the preservativepresent, in the diluted preparation.

2. Any statement required to be printed/attached in terms of paragraph 1-

(a) shall be clear and legible ;(b) shall be in a conspicuous position on the label which shall be securely attached/printed to the container orpackage in such a manner that the statement shall be easily read and understood by a prospective buyer ;(c) shall not be in any way hidden or obscured by any other matter.
