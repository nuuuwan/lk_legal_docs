THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: 3/3/6/2/24.

I hereby give notice, under sub-section (1) of section 7 ofLand acquisition Act, as Amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Governmentintends to acquire the following land/servitude* which isrequired for public purpose.

Acquisition for alternative land for the people displaceddue to heavy rain and landslides (Under village ExpansionProgramme).

Description of the Land or servitude to be Acquired:

An plot of land called Enasalmada Watta in extent of about1.6387 Hectares depicted as Lot 1 in the P.P.M. 1698 preparedby Senior Superintendent of Survey, Matale and situated atEnasalmada village in Enasalmada Grama Niladhari inKohonsiyapatthuwa minor division in Ukuwela DivisionalSecretary's division in Matale District in Central Province.

Name of the land: Enasalmada Watta.

Claimant: State Plantation Cooperation, No. 11, Duke Street,Colombo 01.

All persons interested in the aforesaid/lands* on which aservitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appearpersonally, or by agents duly authorized in writing before meat Ukuwela Divisional Secretariat on the 21st day of August2019 at 10.30 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify to me inwriting (in duplicate) on or before the 07th day of August2019 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars oftheir claims for compensationfor the acquisition of the saidland/servitude* the amount of compensation and the detailsof the computation of such amount.

E. W. N. A. EGODAWELA,

Divisional Secretary,

Ukuwela.

Divisional Secretary's Office,Ukuwela,14th May, 2019.
