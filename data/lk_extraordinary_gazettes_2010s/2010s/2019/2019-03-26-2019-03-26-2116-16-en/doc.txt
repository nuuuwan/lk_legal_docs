FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM EASTPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSivasubramaniyam Thanusha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member if Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Pushparajah Nisanthini to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatPushparajah Nisanthini was selected as the member of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Jaffna,25th March, 2019.
