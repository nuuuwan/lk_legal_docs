EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY THE OFFICE OF DUPUTY MAYOR OF THE BANDARAWELAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66B (2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Deputy Mayor in Bandarawela Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of DeputyMayor elected as Mayor ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group No. 1 to, which Janaka Nishantha Rathnayaka the formerDuputy Mayor belonged, was required by me under Section 66 B (2) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon themember of that Bandarawela Municipal Council who belongs to such Independent Group and who shall be declaredelected as Deputy Mayor within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group No. 1 has communicated his decision to nominateHeenkenda Mudiyanselage Mithrapala Heenkenda to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, H. I. R. Hathurusingha, Returning Officer of Bandarawela Municipal Council, acting interms of Section 66B(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local AuthoritiesElections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Heenkenda Mudiyanselage Mithrapala Heenkenda electedas Deputy Mayor of the Bandarawela Municipal Council.

H. I. R. HATHURUSINGHA,Returning Officer,Bandarawela Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Badulla,23rd January 2019.
