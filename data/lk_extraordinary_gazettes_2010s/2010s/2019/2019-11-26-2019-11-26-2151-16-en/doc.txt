EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Nishshanka Dhanushka Rathnajeewa PushpaKumara of the Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has ceasedto be a member of the United People’s Freedom Alliance.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,25th November, 2019.
