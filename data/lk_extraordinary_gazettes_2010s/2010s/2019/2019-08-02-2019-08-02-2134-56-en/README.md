SRI LANKA TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT, No. 25 OF 1991

REGULATIONS made by the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 71(2)(a) read withSection 5(f) of the Sri Lanka Telecommunications Act, No. 25 of 1991 as amended by Act, No. 27 of 1996.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Colombo,19th July 2019.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Subscriber SIM Cards (Subscriber Identification Modules - SIM)regulations No. 01 of 2019, and shall come into force upon publication in the Government Gazette of SriLanka.

2. These regulations shall apply to every operator licensed under Section 17(2) of the Act to provide digitalcellular mobile services.

3. Every operator shall comply with the regulatory measures as specified in Schedule I and II hereto for the purposeof registration of SIM Cards of retail subscribers and corporate subscribers respectively.

“retail subscriber” means any person under whose personal name, SIM Card is registered by theoperators ;

“SIM” means the Subscriber Identification Modules ;

“SIM Card” means the card or the chip issued to a subscriber containing the Subscriber IdentificationModules.

(Regulation 2)

SCHEDULE I

REGISTRATION OF RETAIL SUBSCRIBERS

Every operator shall comply with the following regulatory measures when registering retail subscriber SIM cards.

1. Obtain a duly filled and signed application form from the retail subscriber. (Mandatory details required : NationalIdentity Card Number, permanent address, new telephone number)

2. The application referred to in paragraph 1 shall accompany the following :–

(a) a photocopy of the National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport duly signed by thesubscriber ;

(b) a duly signed photograph of the retail subscriber with his or her signature where the photograph of theretail subscriber in the National Identity Card or Driving License or Passport is not clear ;

3.(a) the address in the National Identity Card shall be considered as the permanent address.

(b) where the address in the National Identity Card is different to the present address of the retail subscriberor where the Driving License or Passport is accepted in place of the National Identity Card, the retailsubscriber shall be required to submit billing information in proof of such person’s address.

4. National Identity Card number of the retail subscriber shall be entered in the operator’s database or networkprior to activation of the SIM.

5. Scanned copies of the documents listed in items 1, 2 and 3 shall be uploaded on the operator’s database ornetwork within one month of the SIM Card activation as and when required by an authorized officer.

6. Update the subscriber details referred to in paragraph 1, 2 and 3 in accordance with paragraph 5 from time totime and submit such details to an authorized officer when requests for such information.

(Regulation 2)

SCHEDULE II

REGISTRATION OF CORPORATE SUBSCRIBERS

Every operator shall comply with the following regulatory measures when registering corporate subscriber SIM Cards.

1. Obtain an application form or agreement seeking a corporate subscriber SIM Card to a new telephone corporateaccount, under a corporate package duly signed by an authorized officer of the corporate subscriber.

2. The application referred to in paragraph 1 shall accompany the following :-

(a) a true copy of the certificate of Business Registration ;

(b) list of details with Name, Number of National Identity Card or number of Driving License or number ofPassport, permanent address and present address of employees of the corporate subscriber obtainingconnections under the corporate package.

3. Business Registration Number of the corporate subscriber shall be entered in the operator’s database or networkprior to the activation of the corporate subscriber SIM Cards.

4. Scanned copies of the documents listed in paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 shall be uploaded on the operator’s database ornetwork within one month of the SIM Card activation as and when required by an authorized officer.

5. Where a corporate subscriber using a SIM Card leaves the registered business entity, the connection under thecorporate package shall be disconnected.

6. Details of a corporate subscriber under corporate package shall be updated in accordance with paragraph 4 fromtime to time and submit such details as and when required by an authorized officer.

7. Every operator is required to provide access to the database or network as and when required by TRCSL toobtain information and data of a subscriber.

8. Every operator is required to disconnect the telephone connection if the instructions set out in paragraph 4 arenot followed within one month from the date of SIM Card activation.
