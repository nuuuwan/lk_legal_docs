LAND REFORM COMMISSIOIN ACT, No. 1 OF 1972 OF THE NATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

Order under Sub-Section (4) of Section 27a

I, Gayantha Karunathilaka, the Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms, by this Order made in terms of powersvested in me by Sub-section (4) of Section 27A of Land Reform Act, No. 1 of 1972 of the National State Assembly asamended by Land Reforms (Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981.

(a)Do hereby amend that rule by revoking them on the basis of not adhering to the conditions and orders whenvesting the land called Walahanduwa Watta specified in Schedule two here to in Sri Lanka State PlantationCorporation, which had been vested in Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation established under Sri LankaState Plantation Corporation Act, No. 4 of 1958 and transferred under Sub-Section (1) of Section 27A of theLand Reform Commission Act, No. 1 of 1972 as published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 815/10 on 21stApril, 1994 relevant to the land called and mentioned as Walahanduwa Watta as detailed in the First Schedulehereto.

(b)Do hereby state that, following this revocation, the said extent of land explained in detail in the SecondSchedule hereto is conveyed back to Land Reform Commission.

GAYANTHA KARUNATHILAKA,Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms.Ministry of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms,Mihikatha Medura,Land Secretariat,1200/6, Rajamalwatta Lane,Battaramulla,23rd May, 2019
