SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, EranWickramaratne, Acting Minister of Finance of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose, inrespect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the ratesspecified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from July 19, 2019, subject to provisions of Sub-Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

ERAN WICKRAMARATNE,Acting Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,July 18, 2019.
