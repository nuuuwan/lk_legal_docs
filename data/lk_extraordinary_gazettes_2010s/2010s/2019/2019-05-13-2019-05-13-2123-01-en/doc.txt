EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, C. Jayasuriya, Acting Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 13th May, 2019, all duties of Customsas well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid andreceived at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2122/2 of 06th May, 2019is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notification to be publishedon or before 19th May, 2019.

C. JAYASURIYA,Acting Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,10th May, 2019.
