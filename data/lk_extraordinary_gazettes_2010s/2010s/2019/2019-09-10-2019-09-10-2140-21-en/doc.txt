SAMURDHI ACT, No. 1 OF 2013

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Primary Industries and Social Empowerment under the Section 42 of SamurdhiAct, No. 1, 2013.

DAYA GAMAGE,Minister of Primary Industries and Social Empowerment.

10th September, 2019,Colombo.

1. These Regulations are called Samurdhi Regulations No. 1 of 2019

2. Director General of Department of Samurdhi should take necessary actions to recruit Computer Assistants andpay appropriate remuneration to them by using fund of relevant Samurdhi Community Based Bank and SamurdhiCommunity Based Bank Society to implement the Samurdhi Banks and Bank Societies computerizing programmefor Samurdhi Community Based Banks established under Section 25(1) and Samurdhi Community Based BankSocieties established under Section No. 29(1) of Samurdhi Act, No. 1, 2013 and these actions should be takenunder the approval of the Board of Management of the Samurdhi Community Based Banks and Samurdhi CommunityBased Bank Society established under Section 33(1) of Samurdhi Act.
