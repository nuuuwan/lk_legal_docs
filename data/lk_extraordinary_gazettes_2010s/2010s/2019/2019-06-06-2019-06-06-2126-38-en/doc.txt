FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE THUMPANE PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending forCouncil meetings for a period of three months without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichKandanhena Yamanage Manjula Dinesh Yamanage the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) ofthe aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon member of that Number 05 Bananga if Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha who belongsto such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Weerakoon Mudiyanselage Dayarathna Banda to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of the ThumpanePradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Weerakoon Mudiyanselage Dayarathna Banda was elected as the member of the No. 5 Bananga ofThumpane Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. TALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,4th June, 2019.
