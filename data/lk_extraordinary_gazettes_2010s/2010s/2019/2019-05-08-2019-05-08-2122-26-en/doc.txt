PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

L.D.B 21/78(xiv)

UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20(4)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20(4)(b) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, A. Ibathul RauffHakeem, Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, on being satisfied that the administration of theaffairs of the Gampaha Wickramarachchi Ayurveda Institute, have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of thesaid Institute have failed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Major General (retired) HerathMudiyanselage Udaya Bandara Herath to be the Competent Authority with effect from May 01, 2019 for the purpose ofexercising, performing and discharging in lieu of Director of the Gamapah Wickramarachchi Ayurveda Institute, anypower, duty or function respectively which has been conferred or imposed on, or assigned to the said Director under thesaid Act, or under any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By-Law, Regulation or Rule made thereunder.

A. IBATHUL RAUFF HAKEEM,Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education.

Colombo,02nd May, 2019.
