LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Land Ministry's Reference No.: 4-3/15/2016/VE/233.

My Reference No.: WAR/LAN/ACQUS/A2/5-67.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the land acquisition(amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that Government intends toacquire the following land which is required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the Land in be acquired :

The following allotments of land from Lot No. 01 to 02depicted in the preliminary Plan No. K 3632 dated 29.12.2018prepared by the Senior Superintendent of Survey, KegalleDistrict for Surveyor General within the boundaries shownbelow situated in the Village of Bopitiya in the Grama NiladhariDivision No. 94, Bopitiya in the Divisional Secretary's Divisionof Warakapola in the District of Kegalle in SabaragamuwaProvince.

01. Lot No. 01Name of the Land : Pallegama Estate (Batuwana Division);Description of the Land: Estate; Claimant: Government; FirstClaimant: Unknown; Extent : 3.1986 Hectares.

02. Lot No. 02Name of the Land : Pallegama Estate (Batuwana Division);Description of the Land: Thicket; Claimant: Government;Extent : 0.0301 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agents duly authorized inwriting before me at Warakapola Divisional Secretariat on21.03.2019 at 10.00 a.m..

Further, you are hereby requested to notify to me in writing(in duplicate) on or before 07.03.2019 the nature of theirinterests in the land, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land the amountof compensation and details of computation of such amount.

LAXMENDRA DAMAYANTHKUMARA THENNAKOON,Divisional Secretary,Warakapola.
