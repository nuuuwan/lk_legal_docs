FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MINUWANGODAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which AbeysingheLiyanage Udara Rukmal Wijerathna the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Wijesuriya Arachchige Vishwantha Sadun Wijesuriya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasinghe Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the MinuwangodaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Wijesuriya Arachchige Vishwantha Sadun Wijesuriya was selected as the member of the MinuwangodaPradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Gampaha,23rd September, 2019.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VAVUNIYA NORTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSivaranshini Ragunesan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Thavarani Muththaiya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Malawenna Godage Lalith Ananda, Returning Officer of the Vavuniya North PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Thavarani Muththaiya was selected as the member of the Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha.

M. G. LALITH ANANDA,Returning Officer,Vavuniya North Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Vavuniya,23rd September, 2019.
