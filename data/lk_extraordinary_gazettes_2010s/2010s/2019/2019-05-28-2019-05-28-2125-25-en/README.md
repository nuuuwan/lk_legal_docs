FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WARIYAPOLAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Wariyapola Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichSubodha Guththika Weesingha Bandara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that No. 23, Demataluwa of Wariyapola Pradeshiya Sabha whobelongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shallbe declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Tennakoon Mudiyanselage Somawathie to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Wariyapola Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatTennakoon Mudiyanselage Somawathie was elected as the member of the No. 23 Demataluwa of Wariyapola pradeshiyaSabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Wariyapola Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kurunegala,24th May, 2019.
