FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE JA-ELA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Ja-Ela Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the Independent Group to which Jayakodi Arachchige Noel Deckster Perera themember belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember if Independent Group who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateDeekirikege Don Nuwan Sirikumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the Ja-Ela UrbanCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Deekirikege Don Nuwan Sirikumara was selected as the member of the Ja-Ela Urban Council.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Ja-Ela Urban CouncilDistrict Election Office,Gampaha,01st July, 2019.
