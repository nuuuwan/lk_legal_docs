FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PANADURA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which PethahandiNevil Chandrasoma the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Kumara Unnehelage Pradeep Nishshanka Dayas to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Wannakuwatta Waduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the PanaduraPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Kumara Unnehelage Pradeep Nishshanka Dayas was selected as the member of the Panadura PradeshiyaSabha.

KALINDRA JAYAWEERA FERNANDO,Returning Officer,Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kalutara,26th September, 2019.
