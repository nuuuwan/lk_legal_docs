Notice under Section 7

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section 07 of Land AcquisitionAct, No. 28 of 1964 (Amendment) that the Government intendsto acquire the following land which is required for a publicpurpose:

Description of the land to be Acquired:

The allotment of land in total Extent of about 1.0117 Hectaredepicted in PP/MADA/2793 Prepared by the Surveyor GeneralSituated in the Village of Karungalicholai in the DivisionalSecretary's Division of Koralaipattu in Batticaloa District,Eastern Province more fully described below:

Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Vellaiyadipooval; Description: The landcontain Permanent Buildings, 18 Palmyra trees, 10 Coconuttrees, 01 Mango tree and two Masonry wells; Claimant: N. V.Bede Johnpillai; Extent: 1.0117 Hectare.

Boundaries:North: Valaichenai Aru and Vellaiyadipooval Garden Claimedby N. V. B. Johnpillai; East: Vellaiyadipooval Garden Claimedby N. V. B. Johnpillai; South: Vellaiyadipooval Garden Claimedby N. V. B. Johnpillai Road (Pradeshiya Sabha); West: Road(Pradeshiya Sabha), Vellaiyadipooval Garden claimed by Notknown and Valaichcheni River.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at the Koralaipattu Secretary's Office on27th day of September, 2019 at 10.00 a.m., and are herebyrequested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or beforethe 20th day of September, 2019 the nature interests in theland, the particulars of their claims for the compensation andthe computation of such compensation and the details of thecompilation of such amount. All deeds and documents insupport of the claims should be produced before me at theinquiry.
