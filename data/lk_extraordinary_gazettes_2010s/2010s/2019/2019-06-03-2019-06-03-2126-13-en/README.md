AMENDMENT OF IMMIGRANTS AND EMIGRANTS REGULATIONS 1956 RELATED TOTHE IMMIGRANTS AND EMIGRANTS ACT, No. 20 OF 1948

BY virtue of powers vested in me under section 52 that should be read in conjunction with the section 23 of the Immigrantsand Emigrants Act pertaining to the Ministerial subject assigned to me, I Wajira Abeywardana as the Minister of Internal& Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government and as per the Cabinet Memorandum No. 8/IA/2019 dated31.01.2019 submitted by me on “Amendments to be made to the Immigrants and Emigrants Regulations for existing visacategories and to revise relevant fee in the visa Issuing system of the Department of Immigration and Emigration” , therevised Regulations pertaining to the Immigrants and Emigrants Act, No. 20 of 1948 (Chapter 351) attached to the aforesaidCabinet Memorandum are submitted herewith and I do hereby order to publish the amended regulations in the GovernmentGazette and subsequently to table the same in the Parliament for approval.

WAJIRA ABEYWARDANA,Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and ProvincialCouncils and Local Government.

Ministry of Internal & Home Affairs andProvincial Councils & Local Government,“Suhurupaya”,Sri Subhuthipura Road,Battaramulla.20th May, 2019.

Regulations

The Immigrants and Emigrants Regulations 1956, published in the Supplement to the Ceylon Government GazetteNo. 10,896 of February 24, 1956 are hereby amended as follows :-

1. in regulation 8 -

(1)in paragraph (1) by the substitution for the words ‘‘ furnish such Number of copies of the photograph orof the finger- prints or of both,” of the words ‘‘ furnish such Number of copies of the photograph or ofthe bio metrics or of both,’’;

(2)in paragraph (2), by the substitution for the words, “or of the finger-prints furnished” of the words, “ orof the bio metrics furnished”.

2. by the insertion immediately after regulation 10, of the following new regulation :-

“10A. (1) Any person who contravenes the condition which specifies the period of stay in any visagranted or issued to that person, by overstaying in Sri Lanka, shall be required to pay a penelty of UnitedStates Dollars 500, in addition to the applicable visa fee for the perdiod of over stay.

(2)Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (1), the prescribed Authority may grant a grace period -

(a)of two days at the port of depature to a person referred to in paragraph (1) ; or

(b)seven days when applying for an extension of the period of stay specifed in the visa from theDepartment of Immigration and Emigration.

3. by the substitution, for regulation 13 thereof, of the following regulation :-

“13. (1) The fees payable in respect of visas shall be as set out in the table below :-
