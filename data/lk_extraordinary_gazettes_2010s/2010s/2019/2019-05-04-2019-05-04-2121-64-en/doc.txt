FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BELIATTA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Beliatta Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which AmarakonManamperige Kapila Priyadarshana the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of United National Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Kotta Hewage Janaka Krishantha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Ravindra Subhashana Wedage, Returning Officer of the Beliatta Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that KottaHewage Janaka Krishantha was selected as the member of the Beliatta Pradeshiya Sabha.

RAVINDRA SUBHASHANA WEDAGE,Returning Officer,Beliatta Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Hambanthota,03rd May, 2019.
