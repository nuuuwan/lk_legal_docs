URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 2 of Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka upon the recommendation ofthe Miniser-in-Charge of the subject of Megapolis & Western Development being of the opinion that the land specifiedin the Schedule hereto is urgently required for Tec City Development Project, do by this Order declare that the said landis required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,June 03, 2019.
