FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTHPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofResignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichLogeswaran Laavanyan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member of Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Arumugam Thirunavukkarasu to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendram Christy Amalraaj, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Arumugam Thirunavukkarasu was selected as the member of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. C. AMALRAAJ,Returning Officer,Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Jaffna,13th May, 2019.
