NOTICE No : 52/1020 Colombo District

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands re-flected in the Cadastral Map No. 520040 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Kiriwaththuduwa (Block 19) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 603 - Kiriwaththuduwa North in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Homagama in the District of Colombo in the Province of Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 26th December, 2019 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 520040 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner General of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office.
