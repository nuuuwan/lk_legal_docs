FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AKURANA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Akurana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party the recognized political party to which NarayanasamyJayaseelan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponthe member of that Number 03 - Alawathugoda of Akurana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision to nominateGiddawa Gedara Niranjan Karunaratne to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Talangama Appuhamilage Chaminda Namal Talangama, Returning Officer of the AkuranaPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Giddawa Gedara Niranjan Karunaratne was elected as the member of the Number 03 - Alawathugoda of AkuranaPradeshiya Sabha.

T. A. C. N. THALANGAMA,Returning Officer,Akurana Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Kandy,28th August, 2019.
