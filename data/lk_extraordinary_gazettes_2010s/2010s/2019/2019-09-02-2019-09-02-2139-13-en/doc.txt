EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (chapter 262)

Notice under Section 38(3)

I, Somarathna Vidhanapathirana the Returning Officer of Galle District, do by this Notice :

Amend the date of poll of the under-mentioned Local Authority fixed for 10.02.2018 by notice published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2051/3 of 26.12.2017 under Section 38(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act of No. 16 of 2017 and appoint 11.10.2019 as the date for taking of the Poll of the said Local Authority in terms of the judgement in SC Writ Application No. 12/2018 of the Supreme Court. Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha

Somarathna Vidhanapathirana, Returning Officer and District Secretary/Government Agent, Galle Administrative District.

District Elections Office,Galle District,02nd September, 2019.
