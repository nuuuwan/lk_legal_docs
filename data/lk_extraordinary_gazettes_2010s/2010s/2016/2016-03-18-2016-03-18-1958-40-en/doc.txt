PART III - LANDS NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998 (SECTION 55) Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Samarasinghe Achchige Dharmasena who resides in the village of Kirigampamunuwa, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Homagama , in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Samarasinghe Achchige Dharmasena described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 29th April, 2016 stat-ing his/their right or interest to such intestate property. In case of failure to do so, expert action will be taken to register title under Sec-tion 55 (2) and (3) of the Registration of Title Act. No. 21 of 1998. K.A.K.RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 16th March, 2016.
