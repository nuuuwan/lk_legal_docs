FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEMATARA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Matara Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending threeconsecutive meetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United People's Freedom Alliance towhich the former member, Hadunge Sosindra Nandana belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a memberof that Matara Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Usman MohomadMohomad Safar being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Wewalwala Hewage Ravinda Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of Matara MunicipalCouncil acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Usman MohomadMohomad Safar elected as a member of the Matara Municipal Council.

W. H. R. WIJAYA KUMARA,Returning Officer,Matara Municipal Council.District Elections Office,Matara,13th January, 2016.
