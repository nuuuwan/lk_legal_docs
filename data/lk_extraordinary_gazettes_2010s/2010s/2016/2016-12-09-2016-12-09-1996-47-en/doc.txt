Government Notifications

PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Northern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Northern Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occured in the membership ofthe Northern Province Provincial Council by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate aneligible person to fill such vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recongnized political party, has nominated Mr. AndiyaiyaPuvaneswaran to fill such vacancy ;

THE ELECTION COMMISSION, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No.2 of 1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Andiyaiya Puvaneswaran, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the AdministrativeDistrict of Mullaitivu in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

Mahinda DeshapriyaChairmanElection Commission
