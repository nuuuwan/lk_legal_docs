L.D.B 21/78

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1469/38 of November 03, 2006 (as last amendedby Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1819/46 of July 19, 2013) which specified the Faculties and theDepartments of the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139 of theaforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the addition immediately after entry “12.Department of Transport and Logistics Management” appearing in Column II which corresponds to the item “Faculty ofEngineering ” appearing in Column I of the Schedule of the following entry :-

“13. Department of Languages”

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,01st November 2016.
