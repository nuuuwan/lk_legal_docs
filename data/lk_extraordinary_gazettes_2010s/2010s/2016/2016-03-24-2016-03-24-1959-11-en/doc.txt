FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a Member in Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

And whereas the Secretary of the Recognized Political Party namely Democratic Peoples Front to which the formermember Aananthan Murugaiah Thirubagara Kumar belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days a person eligible under the Ordinance for election as a member ofthat Colombo Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Lowrance Fernando beingan eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

Now therefore I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Colombo Municipal Council, acting in termsof Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacancies in Local Authorities(Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Lowrance Fernando elected as a member of theColombo Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Colombo Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo,23rd of March, 2016.
