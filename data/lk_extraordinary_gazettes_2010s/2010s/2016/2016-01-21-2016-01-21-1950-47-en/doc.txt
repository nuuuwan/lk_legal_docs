Western Province Provincial Council

Order Pertaining to the Delegation of powers of the Western Provincial Public Service under the section 32(2a) of theProvincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987 as amended by the Provincial Councils (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 1990

WHEREAS the Governor of the Western Province by his order published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette Notification No. 1859/32dated 24.04.2014 delegated to the Western Provincial Public Service Commission, the power of appointment, transfer, dismissalfrom service and disciplinary control of officers of the Western Provincial Public Service ;

From and out of the powers so delegated, the powers to the extent provided for in each Schedule, from Schedules01 to 07 hereto, are hereby delegated to the officers specifically mentioned in column 1 of each of the said Schedules, beingpowers pertaining to the appointment, transfer, dismissal and disciplinary control as are specified in corresponding columnIII of the Schedules, in respect of the categories of public officers as specified in column II of the Schedule thereto, inconformity with the provisions in clause 32 (2a) of the Provincial Coucils Act No. 42 of 1987 as amended by the ProvincialCouncils Act, No. 28 of 1990. Provided however, in the event of any question arising in respect of any delegation or as to theimplementation of the delegation hereof, made by the Western Provincial Public Service Commission, the decision of the saidCommission shall be final and conclusive. The Commission hereby directs that in the course of implementing the powershereby delegated, to the Provincial Chief Secretary or to any officer by the Western Pronvicial Public Service Commission, allthe decisions taken by such officer shall be implemented subject to the provisions in clause 32 (2a) and 32 (2b) of theProvincial Councils Act No. 42 of 1987 as amended by the Provincial Councils (Amended) Act, No. 28 of 1990.

The said Commission specifically directs hereby that whoever officer to whom the afore-mentioned powers havebeen delegated, they, at all times of implementing the powers so delegated to them, shall act in accordance with the ProceduralRules of the Western Provincial Public Service made by the Governor of the Western Province acting in terms of clause 32 (3)of the Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987 and also in conformity with the Establishment Code any conditions rules,regulations and with other procedural rules decided upon from time to time by the Governor of the western province and theWestern Provincial Public Service Commission.
