UVA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL (PAYMENTS OF SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES) AMENDED STATUTE

No. 02 OF 2016

A Statute to provide for amending the Sub Section No. 2(a) and 2(b) of amended Statute, No.09th of 2010 to make payments of Salaries and Allowances for the Chairman and members of the PublicService Commission of Uva Provincial Council.

Be it enacted by the Uva Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankaas follows :

01. This amended Statute may be cited as the Uva Provincial Council Public Service Commission

(Payments of Salaries and Allowances for the Chairman and the Members) amended StatuteNo. 02 of 2016 and shall come into operation on such day as approved by the Governor of UvaProvince.
