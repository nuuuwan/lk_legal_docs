PART III - LANDS NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998 (SECTION 55) Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Susew Hewage Tudor Darmapala who resided in the village of Pothupitiya, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kaluthara , in the District of Kaluthara. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Susew Hewage Tuder Dar-mapala described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 25th January, 2016 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
