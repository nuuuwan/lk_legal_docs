PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Direction under Section 54(3) of National Sports Association Regulations

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 54(3), Regulations of National Sports Associations, published inGazette Extraordinary No. 1825/38 of 30th August 2013, made under Section 41 of the Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973.

I, Dayasiri Jayasekara, Minister of Sports do by this order inform the Sri Lanka Judo Association that Mr.Samasundara Ekanayaka RajawardhanaTennakoon Mudiyanselage Saman Priyantha Bandara, Director General of theDepartment of Sports Development has been appointed as the Competent Authority, in terms of the Section 39(1) of theSports Act, No. 25 of 1973 for the smooth functioning of that Association and direct that Mr. Samasundara EkanayakaRajawardhana Tennakoon Mudiyanselage Saman Priyantha Bandara, Director General of the Department of SportsDevelopment, to convene the election of the Sri Lanka Judo Association to elect new office bearers until an election is heldlawfully, since the term of the interim Committee of Sri Lanka Judo Association has expired on 15.07.2015.

DAYASIRI JAYASEKARA (Attorney-at-Law)Minister of Sports.

Colombo,21st September 2016.
