“5. A non-refundable fee of Rupees Ten Thousand (Rs. 10,000) shall be paid when applying for a newlicense or renewal of license” ;

“6. Every license shall be valid for a period of one year. The License which is issued in the middle ofthe year shall be issued for the remaining period of the year” ;

c.By the repeal of Paragraph 7 and the substitution therefor, of the following :-

“7. Every license issued under the Excise Ordinance shall be subject to a license fee set out in ScheduleI and such fee shall be charged on a pro-rate basis in the case of a new license issued for a part of ayear. The payment shall be made at least seven working days prior to the date of commencement ofthe relevant financial year. However, if the fee is not received by the relevant Divisional Secretary atleast seven working days after the commencement of a financial year, an additional period of fourweeks shall be allowed to make payment subject to a penalty of 1% of the value of license fee. Afterthe period of four weeks, Penalty shall be 3% of the value of license fee.” ;
