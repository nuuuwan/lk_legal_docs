PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

MINISTRY OF WOMEN AND CHILD AFFAIRS

IT is hereby notified that from among the subjects and functions of Women and Child Affairs assigned to me throughGazette Extraordinary No. 1933/13 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka dated 21.09.2015, subjects andfunctions mentioned in the following Schedule are assigned to the State Minister of Child Affairs, exercising the powersvested in me under paragraph 5 of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

K. D. M. CHANDRANI BANDARA,Minister of Women and Child Affairs.

Ministry of Women and Child Affairs,6th Floor, 11nd Stage,Sethsiripaya,Battaramulla,23rd November 2016.

Schedule

01.Supervision of the Implementation of policies and programmes on early childhood care and developmentaimed at bringing up a physically and mentally healthy child, coordination and liaison with provincial levelcommittees, and monitoring of activities.

02.Supervision of the special programme on providing nutrition food with value of Rs. 20,000 to expecting mothers.

03.Supervision of the programme on glass of Fresh milk for children between 3-5 of age.
