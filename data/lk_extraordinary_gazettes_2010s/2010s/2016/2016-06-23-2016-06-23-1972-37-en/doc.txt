CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Minipe Pradeshiya Sabha

IMPOSING ENTERTAINMENT TAX

IT is hereby notified under Sub-section (1) of Section 2 of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance (Chapter 267) that the undermentioned Proposal was approved by the Minister in charge of the subject Local Government, adopted by the MinipePradeshiya Sabha, by virtue of power vested on it under Sub-section (1) of the said Ordinance.

P. B. WIJAYARATNA,Secretary,Chief, Education and Ministry of LocalGovernment,Central Province.Pallekele,04th June 2016.

PROPOSAL

In term of Entertainment Tax Ordinance, it has been resolved to impose and levy a tax (other than EntertainmentTax).equal to ten per centum (10%) of the entry payment on certain entertainment activity mentioned in the Entertainment TaxOrdinance, and it shall be effective from the first day of the following month, after publication of this Proposal notification inthe Gazette.

The tax shall be levied at the rate of seven point five (7.5%) per centum for a period of first two years of theimplementation of this tax, on the amount payable for a film show entry.
