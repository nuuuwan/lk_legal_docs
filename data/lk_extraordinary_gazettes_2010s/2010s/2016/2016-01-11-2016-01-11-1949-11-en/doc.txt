WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notice published under Section 268 of Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section 2 ofProvincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

IT is hereby announced that the by-law titled ; “By-law relating to Regularizing, Controlling and Levying Charges onParking of Three-wheeler Taxis” approved by Hon. Chief Minister and Minister in Charge of the subject of Local Governmentand published by way of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No.1788/8 dated 11th December 2012 under Section 268 of Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section02 of Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 ; having drafted by the Kaduwela MunicipalCouncil under the provisions of Sub-section 272(32) in Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) ; were adopted bythe Western Province Provincial Council on 03rd September 2013 as per provisions further stated in Section 268 of theMunicipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section 02 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions)Act, No. 12 of 1989.

ISURA DEVAPRIYA,Chief Minister and Minister of Finance andPlanning, Engineering Services, Law and Order,Local Government and Provincial Administration,Economic Development, Electricity and Energy,Environment Affairs, Water Supplies and Drainage andTourism Affairs of Western Province.Chief Ministry of the Western Provincial Council,Shrawasthi Mandiraya,Colombo 07,05th January 2016.
