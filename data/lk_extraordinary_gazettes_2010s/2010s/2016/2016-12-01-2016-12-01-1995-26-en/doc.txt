L.D.B 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 281/7 of January 24, 1984 (as last amended byOrder published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1964/10 of April 26, 2016), which specifies the Faculties and theDepartments of the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka, established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the additionimmediately after entry “14. Department of Surgery” appearing in Column II which corresponds to the item “Faculty ofMedicine” appearing in Column I of the Schedule of the following entry :-

“15. Department of Anaesthesiology”

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo,28th November 2016.
