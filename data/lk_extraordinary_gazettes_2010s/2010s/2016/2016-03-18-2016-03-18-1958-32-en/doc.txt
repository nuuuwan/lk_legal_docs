PART III - LANDS NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998 (SECTION 55) Reference the intestate property of late Mrs. Lilawathi Hiripitiyage who resided in the village of Pitipana North, in the Divisional Secre-tary’s Division of Homagama, in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mrs. Lilawathi Hiripitiyage described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 22nd April, 2016 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property. In case of failure to do so, experte action will be taken to register title under Section 55 (2) and (3) of the Registration of Title Act. No. 21 of 1998. K.A.K. RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 08th March, 2016.
