THE INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT, CHAPTER 131 OF THE LEGISLATIVE ENACTMENTS OF CEYLON( 1956 REVISED EDITION)

Revocation of Order under Section 4(1)

WHEREAS by Order made under Section 4 (1) of the Industrial Disputes Act Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments of Ceylon(1956 Revised Edition), as amended by Acts, Nos. 14 and 62 of 1957, 14 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 read with Industrial Disputes(Special Provisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968, dated 11.04.2014 and published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka Extraordinary No. 1862/7 dated 13.05.2014 the Industrial Dispute in respect of the matter specified in the statement of theCommissioner of Labour dated 03.04.2014.

Mr. W. M. Jayasinghe, ‘‘Sampath’’, Eranawela, Bandarawela of the one part

and

The Sri Lanka Transport Board, Head Office, No. 200, Kirula Road, Colombo 05 of the other part

was referred to Mr. K. R. M. N. Lawrence for settlement by arbitration.

AND whereas it is now deemed expedient that the said Order be revoked, I, W. D. J. Seneviratne, Minister of Labour and Trade UnionRelations do hereby revoke the said Order and further make Order that no proceedings be taken upon the said Order dated 11.04.2014.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

Colombo.24th February, 2016.

STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is

Whether Mr. W. M. Jayasinghe who worked at the Sri Lanka Transport Board had been caused injustice in receiving salaries and allowancesrelevant and in being placed in the respective grades at the time of being appointed to the post of Divisional Running Manager from15.07.2008 and at the time of being appointed to the permanent post of Chief Divisional Manager with effect from 21. 08.2010 and if so,to what reliefs he is entitled.
