SRI LANKA EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT, No. 40 OF 1979

Order under Section 14

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 14(1) of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No. 40 of 1979, I, SujeewaSenasinghe, Acting Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade with the concurrence of the Minister ofFinance, do by this Order declare that, the Cess shall be charged, levied and paid on import of every article specified inColumn II and III of the Schedule hereto, corresponding to the import of every article specified under the following H. S.Headings mentioned in the Column I of the Schedule of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1941/33 dated20.11.2015 issued under the Section 14(1) of this Act, shall be payable at the rate specified in the corresponding entry inColumn IV of the Schedule hereto, with effect from 29th June 2016.

SUJEEWA SENASINGHE,Acting Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade.

Ministry of Development Strategies and International Trade,Colombo 01.28th June 2016.
