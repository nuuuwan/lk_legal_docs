PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Southern Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Southern Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership ofthe Southern Province Provincial Council by a reason of Resignation ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligibleperson to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has nominated Mr. Baraniwala LiyanageLal Premanath to fill such vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Baraniwala Liyanage Lal Premanath, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from theAdministrative District of Matara in respect of which such vacancy occurred.
