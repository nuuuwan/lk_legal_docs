WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Announcement made under Section 268 of Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read withSection 2 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

IT is hereby announced that the By-laws relating to Regularization, Control, Administration and Levying Charges onPrivate Fairs conducted within Negombo Municipal Council Area of Authority ; By laws relating to Control and LevyingCharges on laying water pipes and drawing underground cables by various Government/Semi-government/Corporation/Boards, Companies and persons in streets controlled by the Municipal Council in Negombo Municipal Council Area ofAuthority ; By laws relating to Control and Levying Charges on the tasks of fixing pillars and towers constructed byvarious Government/Semi-government/Corporations/Boards, Companies and persons in streets controlled by the saidCouncil in Negombo Municipal Council Area of Authority ; drafted by the Negombo Municipal Council under Section272(32) of the Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252), approved by the Chief Minister and Minister in charge ofthe subject of Local Government under Section 268 of Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section 2of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 and published in the Extraordinary GazettePaper No. 1622/21 dated 07.10.2009 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, were adopted by the WesternProvince Provincial Council on 22nd September 2015 as per provisions further stated in Section 268 of MunicipalCouncils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section 2 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12of 1989.

ISURA DEVAPRIYA,Chief Minister and Minister of Finance andPlanning, Engineering Services, Law and Order,Local Government and Provincial Administration,Economic Development, Electricity and Energy,Environmental Affairs, Water Supplies and Drainage andTourism Affairs of Western Province.

Chief Ministry of the Western Provincial Council,Shrawasthi Mandiraya,Colombo 07,on the 05th day of January 2016.
