THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE RUBBER CULTIVATION AND RAW RUBBER PROCESSING TRADE

IN accordance with Section 28 of the wages Boards Ordinance (chapter 136), the Wages Board of the Rubber Cultivationand Raw Rubber Processing Trade hereby notifies that the board by virtue of powers vested in it by Section 30 of theaforesaid Ordinance propose to vary in the manner set out in the schedule here to such decisions of the Board as arespecified in that schedule.

Objections to the aforesaid proposal will be received by the Chairman of the Board until 12.00 noon on 31stMarch 2016.

Every such objection must be made in writing and must contain a statement of the grounds upon which suchobjection is taken.

M. D. C. AMARATHUNGA,

Chairman,Wages Board for the Rubber Cultivation and

Raw Rubber Processing Trade.

Labour Department,Colombo 05,29th February 2016.

SCHEDULE

The decision made by the Wages Board for the Rubber Growing Manufacturing Trade and set out in the scheduleto the notification published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 10047 dated 25 th November 1949 re-established as WagesBoard for the Rubber Cultivation and Raw Rubber Processing Trade in the notification published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1913/ 5 dated 05 th May 2015, with reference to the aforesaid decisions as varied from time to time and lastly variedby the notification published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1870/3 dated 07 th July 2014, shall be further varied bythe substitution of the following new part instead of the part III (a) and (b).

PART III - OVERTIME RATE

In respect of each hour of work in excess of normal working day, the minimum overtime rate shall be the minimumhourly rate (ascertained by dividing the daily minimum rate of wages of time rate by 8) increased by 50%.
