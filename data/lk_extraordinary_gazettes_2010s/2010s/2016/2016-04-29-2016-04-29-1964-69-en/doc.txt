RE-ESTABLISHMENT OF HEALTH MANAGEMENT ASSISTANT SERVICE

THE Health Management Assistant Service which was declared by the Gazette No. bearing 1355/15 dated 25.08.2004 andamalgamated to Public Management Assistant Service is hereby re-established with effect from 02.12.2015 with theapproval of the Cabinet of Ministers.

All the actions taken in respect of the officers of this service who were governed under the Public ManagementAssistant Service during 15.10.2009 to 02.12.2015 shall be valid and intact.

The rest of the clauses of the minute on Health Management Assistant Service dated 25.08.2004 shall be intactand valid.

ANURA JAYAWICKRAMA,Secretary,Ministry of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine.

Colombo,28th April 2016.
