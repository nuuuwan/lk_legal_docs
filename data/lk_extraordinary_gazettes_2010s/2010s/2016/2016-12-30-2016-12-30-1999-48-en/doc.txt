NATIONAL FILM CORPORATION OF SRI LANKA ACT No. 47 OF 1971

Orders made by the Minister of Parliamentary Reforms and Mass Media under Section 61 to be read with Section 5 ofNational Film Corporation of Sri Lanka Act No. 47 of 1971 as amended by Act No. 45 of 1980.

GAYANTHA KARUNATILLEKA,Minister of Parliamentary Reforms & Mass Media.

Colombo,15th December, 2016.

Orders

1. These Orders may be cited as National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka (The cinema Projector Specifications)Orders No. 1 of 2017 and shall be effective from 01stJanuary, 2017.

2. Horizontal and vertical pixel increased value 2048 × 1080 (pixel 2,211,840) or 2K or 2.2 Megapixel shall befunctional as the minimum value subject to terms and conditions issued for the relevant purpose by the NationalFilm Corporation of Sri Lanka (hereinafter referred to as “Corporation”) as digital projector specifications setout for all the cinemas operating in Sri Lanka.

3.(1) The minimum specifications contained in Order 2 above shall be applied in converting Projectormethodology in a currently operative cinema or newly constructed cinema into digital technology andwritten notice shall be given to the Corporation on the said methodology. The Corporation shall specifyterms and conditions pertaining to the relevant conversion or establishment and once such conversion or

“Films” means local or foreign films

The license fee specified at Orders No. 3(1), (2) and (3) should be Rs. Five thousand (Rs. 5,000/=) for the cinemascontaining upto 150 seats, Rs. Seven thousand five hundred (Rs.7,500/=) for the cinemas containing upto 350 seats andRs. Ten thousand (Rs. 10,000/=) for the cinemas containing over 350 seats.

NATIONAL FILM CORPORATION OF SRI LANKA ACT No. 47 OF 1971

Orders made by the Minister of Parliamentary Reforms and Mass Media under Section 61 to be read with Section 5 ofthe National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka Act No. 47 of 1971 as amended by the Act No. 45 of 1980.

GAYANTHA KARUNATILLEKA,Minister of Parliamentary Reforms & Mass Media.

Colombo,15th December, 2016.

Orders

1. These Orders may be cited as National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka (Miscellaneous Rates) Orders No. 2 of2017 and be effective from 01st March, 2017.

2.(1) Once a film hall operating in Sri Lanka is converted into the Digital Projector methodology approved asper National Film Corporation of Sri Lanka (The cinema Projector Specifications) Orders No. 1 of 2016,the rate (hereinafter referred to as “the increase”) charged by every cinema on approval of the NationalFilm Corporation of Sri Lanka (hereinafter referred to as “Corporation”) in addition to the entrance feefor a ticket and the entertainment levy,

(1.1) A rate of seven percent (7%) of value of the ticket should be charged for a local or foreign film.

(a) The said amount of seven percent (7%) should be divided in the portions of three rupees(Rs. 3/=) to the Producer if it is a local film and one rupee fifty cents (Rs. 1.50/=) each forthe National Film Corporation Trust for Film Artists and Technicians No. 3 of 1986 and to theNational Film Development Board established by the Gazette Extraordinary No. 799/1 of 27th

December, 1993,

(b) two rupees for the Digital Projector Daily Ticket Record,

(c) once the above charges are deducted, ninety percent (90%) of the outstanding amount amongthe cinema which converted the cinema Projector Methodology as specified in the NationalFilm Corporation of Sri Lanka (The cinema Projector Specifications) Orders No.1 of 2016or the investor and the other ten percent (10%) for the Corporation.

(1.2) Seven point five percent (7.5%) of the value of a ticket shall be charged for a locally orinternationally awarded film.

(d) The said amount of seven point five percent (7.5%) shall be divided in the portions of threerupees and fifty cents (Rs. 3.50/=) for the Producer if it is a local film and two rupees andfifty cents (Rs. 2.50/=) for the licensee if it is a foreign film

(e) two rupees (Rs. 2/=) to the cinema releasing it if it is a local film,

(f) one rupee and fifty cents (Rs. 1.50/=) to the cinema releasing it if it is a foreign film

(g) two rupees (Rs.2/=) for Digital Daily Ticketing Record

3. The outstanding amount after deduction of increase of the amount after selling tickets of a film and theentertainment levy is called “Film Rental”.

4. The total film rental received during the inaugural round of its screening and during its first round ofscreening –

(i) in case of a local film, should be divided as follows :-(i) Producer45%(ii) The cinema45%(iii) Licensed Distributor10%

7. Except at a film festival or a screening for aid or at an instance of a film with written permission of Chairman ofthe Corporation, no cinema should charge any fee above the total value of the increase contained in theseorders, entertainment levy and film rental for screening a film.

8. The charge rate contained in orders 2 (a), (b), (d), (e), (f), & (g) above shall remain unchanged for a period oftwo (2) years from the date of commencement of implementation of these orders by each such cinemas. Afterpassage of the initial term of two (2) years, such rates may be increased separately for a specific period of timeby circulars issued by Chairman of the Corporation.

9. If any cinema subject to these orders is in breach or violation of terms and conditions set out by Chairman ofthe Corporation under the above orders :

(b) the Chairman may make any rule of invalidation of the license or its suspension or imposing penalties.
