SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect oflist of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified inthe corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 08th March 2016, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1932/54 of 17.09.2015 in respect of Mackerel Fish (HS Code No. 0303.54) and Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1933/18 of 22.09.2015 in respect of Vegetable oils (HS Heading Nos. 15.07, 15.11, 15.12, 15.13) are rescindedwith effect from 08th March 2016.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,08th March, 2016.
