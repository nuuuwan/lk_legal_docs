PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2of 1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka upon the recommendation ofthe Minister-in-Charge of the subject of Urban Development being of the opinion that the lands specified in the Scheduleshereto are urgently required for the improvement of Koswatta Junction and Town Development Programme do by thisorder, declare that the said lands are required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,16th November 2016.

SCHEDULE

Boundaries of the land are as follows an allotment of land marked as Lot A and B of land called Koswatta Co-operative Filling Station and K. I. U. Campus depicted in sketch plan containing in extent of 18P (0.0455 Ha.) situated inat the village of Thalangama North, within the limits of Kaduwela Municipal Council area, Palle Pattu of Hewagam Korale,Kaduwela Divisional Secretary’s Division in Colombo District, Western Province :
