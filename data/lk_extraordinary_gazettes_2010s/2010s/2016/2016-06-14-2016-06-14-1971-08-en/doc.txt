THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Reference No. : 2/3/1/4/2.

Land Ministry's Ref. No.: LD/05/2001/IM/296.

I hereby give notice, under sub section (1) of section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intendsto acquire the following land which is required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

Public purpose means the Acquisition of land where theIndependence Television Network Service KaragahatennaTransmission centre is situated.

The allotments of land called Karagahatenna estate,containing in total extent 0.3417 hectares, depicted as lots 1and 2 in Preliminary Plan No. Ma 1655, dated 19.02.2016,prepared by Surveyor General, situated at KaragahatennaVillage in Gammaduwa Grama Niladhari Division, AmbangangaKorale minor Division of Ambanganga Korale DivisionalSecretary's Division, Matale District, Central province.

Lot No. 1 - Claimant: Land Reform Commission; Extent:0.3026 Hectares

North: Karagahatenna Estate; East: Karagahatenna Estate;South: Lot 1 in P. P. Ma. No. 1570 and lot 2 in this plan; West:Karagahatenna estate.

Lot No. 2 - Claimant: Land Reform Commission; Extent:0.0391 Hectares

North: Lot 1; East: Lot 1 and 2 in P. P. Ma No. 1570; South:Lot 2 in P. P. No. 1570 and lot 1 in P. P. No. 1247; West: Lot 1 inP. P. No. 1247 and Lot 1 in this plan.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land, on which aservitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appearpersonally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before meat Ambanganga Korale Divisional Secretariat, on the 09thday of August, 2016 at 10.00 a. m. and are hereby requested tonotify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 02nd dayof August 2016 the nature of their interests in the land, theparticulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisitionof the said land, the amount of compensation and the detailsof the computation of such amount.
