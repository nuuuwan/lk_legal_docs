CORRECTION

SERVICE MINUTE OF SRI LANKA SCIENTIFIC SERVICE

FOLLOWING revisions are made to the Minute of the Sri Lanka Scientific Service which was published on the GazetteExtraordinary of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1877/27 dated 28.08.2014 and revised by the GazetteExtraordinary of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1925/59 dated 29.07.2015 and the other matters andconditions remain unchanged.

1. Effective Date :

This Service Minute shall be effective from 01.01.2006

15. Interim Provisions

The Interim Period shall be effective till 31.12.2014

Note.- In addition, if special provisions prevail for each department, they should be included into the Scheme of Recruitementof relevant post.

15.1 Promotion to Grade II

1. An officer who joined the service before 01.01.2006 and absorbed into Grade III, shall be promoted to Grade IIduring the interim period to be effective from the date on which he/she satisfies all prerequisites andqualification required to be promoted into Grade I of Class II from Grade II of Class II, as mentioned in theMinute of Sri Lanka Scientific Service published on Gazette Extraordinary No. 509/07 dated 07.06.1988 andrelevant revisions.

II. Recommendations with regard to promotions to Grade II as above, shall be made by the Secretary of the Ministryin charge of Public Administration to the Public Service Commission.

15.2 Promotion to Grade I

I. Officers who joined the service before 01.01.2006 and absorbed into Grade II or promoted to Grade II duringthe interim period shall be promoted to Grade I if he/she completes the following qualifications during theinterim period, with effect from the date of completion.

(a) Shall have obtained all salary increments in the 05 years immediately preceding the date of promotion.

(b) Shall have not subjected to an disciplinary punishment in the 05 years immediately preceding the date ofpromotion.

II. Recommendation with regard to promotions to Grade I as above, shall be made by the Secretary of the Ministryin charge of Public Administration to the Public Service Commission.

15.3 Promotion to Special Grade

I. Officers who joined the service before 01.01.2006 and absorbed into Grade I or promoted to Grade I during theinterim period shall be promoted to Special Grade on seniority and merit depending on the vacancies after aninterview conducted by an interview board appointed by the Public Service Commission as per 10.3.2 of theService Minute given that they satisfy all the qualifications mentioned in 10.3.1 of the Service Minute in theinterim period.

Note.- In addition, if special provisions prevail for each department, they should be included into the Scheme of Recruitmentof relevant post.
