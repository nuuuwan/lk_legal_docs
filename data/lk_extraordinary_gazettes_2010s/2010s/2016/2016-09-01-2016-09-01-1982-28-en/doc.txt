REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS ACT, No. 44 OF 1980

Notice under Section 13(2)

REVISION OF ELECTORAL REGISTERS 2016

ELECTORAL DISTRICT - NO. : 1 - COLOMBO

NOTICE is hereby given under Section 13(2) of the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980, that–

(1) List “A” containing names of persons in the electoral register who are dead or have become disqualified ; and

(2) List “B” containing the names of persons whose name do not appear in the current electoral register but noweligible to be included in the register ;

have been completed in respect of the above Electoral District and that the register in operation and such lists or portionsthereof are open for inspection of the general public during office hours as follows :

Places of ExhibitionRegister and Lists or Portions thereof forExhibition

(i) District Elections OfficeComplete copy of the electoral register in force for the aboveColombo DistrictElectoral District and copies of the relevant “A” and “B” Lists.

(ii) Each Divisional SecretariatA copy of portion of the electoral register in force containing all PollingDistricts lying within the relevant Divisional Secretary’s area and copiesof the related “A” and “B” Lists.

(iii) Each Local AuthorityA copy of the electoral register in force containing all Polling Districts lyingwithin the area of authority of the relevant Local Authority and copies ofthe related “A” and “B” Lists.

(iv) Each Grama Niladhari’sA copy of the electoral register in force containing all Polling District lyingOfficewithin the relevant Grama Niladhari’s Division and copies of the related“A” and “B” Lists.
