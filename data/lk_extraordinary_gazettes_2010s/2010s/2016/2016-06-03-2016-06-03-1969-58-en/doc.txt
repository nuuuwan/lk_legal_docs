L.D.B- 172/36 III

REGISTERED STOCK AND SECURITIES ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 420)

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Finance under Section 55 of the Registered Stock and Securities Ordinance(Chapter 420).

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Colombo,03rd June 2016.

Regulations

The Registered Stock and Securities (Primary Dealers) Regulations No. 01 of 2009 published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 1607/19 of June 24, 2009 is hereby amended in regulation 9 as follows :-

(1) in Paragraph (1) thereof, by the substitution for the words and figures “The Registrar of Public Debt appointedunder the Registered Stock and Securities Ordinance (Chapter 420), on behalf of the Central Bank” of thewords and figures “The Director of the Department of Supervision of Non-Bank Financial Institutions, of theCentral Bank, appointed in terms of Monetary Law Act (Chapter 422)” ; and

(2) in paragraph (2) thereof, by the substitution for the words “the Registrar of Public Debt” of the words “theDirector of the Department of Supervision of Non-Bank Financial Institutions,”.
