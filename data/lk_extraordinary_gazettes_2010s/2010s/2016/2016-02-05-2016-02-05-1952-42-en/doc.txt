FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE MEMBER OF THEDEHIWALA - MOUNT LAVINIA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 65A(2) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BYLOCAL AUTHORITIES (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 30 OF 2013

WHEREAS the office of a member in Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of beingceased to be a member of the party ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the recognized political party namely United National Party to which theformer member, Krees Wilhelm Balthasar belonged was required by me under Section 65A(2) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to nominate within a period of thirty (30) days, a person eligible under the ordinance for election as a member of thatDehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council who shall be declared elected as a member ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. Munkapu ArachchigeNagasinha Rohitha being an eligible person under the aforesaid Ordinance to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sivasubramaniam Achchuthan, Returning Officer of Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia MunicipalCouncil acting in terms of Section 65A(2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by filling of vacanciesin Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 30 of 2013 (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Mr. Munkapu ArachchigeNagasinha Rohitha elected as a member of the Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council.

S. ACHCHUTHAN,Returning Officer,Dehiwala - Mount Lavinia Municipal Council.City Elections Office,Colombo,05th February, 2016.
