“Whether any injustice was caused to Mr. P. H. Party of he Second Part :

Mrs. T. M. N. S. Basnayaka - Senior Human ResourcesExecutive represents on behalf of the Janashakthi InsurancePLC.

Both Parties have been noticed and their respectivestatements were filed of record. Thereafter, the Party ofthe First Part commenced his Evidence in Chief and while,the Cross Examination was proceeding his services had beenterminated and he had filed an application in the LabourTribunal No. 02, Colombo - Case No. 2/167/2011.

In the circumstances the case was laid by.

In order to ascertain the present position of the saidLabour Tribunal Case, Colombo Parties were noticed to bepresent on 18th April, 2016. On the day in question the Partyof the First Part was absent and unrepresented. However,the Party of the Second Part informed the Court, that thematter had been settled between them in the Labour Tribunalon or about, 19th March 2012.

There is therefore, no dispute subsisting. This has beenconfirmed by the proceedings filed of record, of the saidLabour Tribunal dated 29.02.2012 and the order was signedby Honourable R. A. P. C. Ranatunga President and AdditionalMagistrate.

In the premises, this matter had been frustrated and it hasno force or avail in law.

And as such I make no Award.
