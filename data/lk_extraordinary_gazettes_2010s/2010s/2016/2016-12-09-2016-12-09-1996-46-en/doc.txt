AMENDMENT TO THE SRI LANKA MEDICAL SERVICE MINUTE

THE Sri Lanka Medical Services Minute published by the extra special Gazette No. 1883/17 dated 2014.10.11 of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka is hereby amended as following.

Date of Implementation :

This amendment shall have effect from the date of 01.11.2013.

By Order of Public Services Commission,

H. M. GAMINI SENEVIRATNE,Secretary,Public Services Commission.02nd, November 2016.

Post/Grade(a) Educational and other Qualifications

11.1.2.2I. A Medical Officer or Dental Surgeon who possess a MSc/MD (Medical admin)or MSc/MD (Com. Med.) or MSc/MD (Com. Den.) listed in the appendix 1 &11 of the Medical Service is eligible to be appointed to a post in the SeniorAdministrative Grade, subject to a structured interview based on a markingscheme approved by the Public Service Commission.

II. Should have completed a minimum of 3 years of satisfactory service in theDeputy Medical Administrative Grade.

III. The equal posts coming under the Senior Medical Administrative Grade areenvisaged in appendix IV and includes any posts created from time to time asper the service requirements.

Note 7.- Mandatory qualifications for each post falling under the senior medicaladministrative grade shall be the qualifications mentioned in appendix IV andthese posts shall be considered as equal posts.

Post/Grade(b) Selection Procedure

I. The vacancies in the Senior Medical Administrative Grade will be filled by wayof Annual Transfer among officers who hold the Senior Medical AdministrativeGrade posts. In case if a post could not be filled by the Annual Transfer, it willbe advertised and filled through an interview based on the marking schemeapproved by the Public Service Commission.

II. Until such time the vacancies are filled by the Annual Transfer, vacancies canbe filled by the due appointing authority by appointing on acting basis, anofficer who has complied with the required basic qualifications of the said postfrom among the Deputy Administrative Grade or the Senior MedicalAdministrative Grade officers.
