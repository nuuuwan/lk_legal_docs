THE JUDICATURE ACT, No. 2 OF 1978

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Justice under Section 61 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978 read with Section45 thereof.

WIJEYADASA RAJAPAKSHE,Minister of Justice.

Colombo,12th February 2016.

Regulation

1. This Regulation may be cited as the Justices of the Peace Regulation No. 1 of 2016.

2. Every Grama Niladhari who is in the permanent service, shall in his ex-officio capacity, be a Justice of thePeace for the Divisional Secretary’s Division within which his Grama Niladhari Division is situated.
