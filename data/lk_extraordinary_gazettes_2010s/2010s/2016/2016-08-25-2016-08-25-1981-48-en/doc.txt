NOTIFICATION

IT is hereby notified that following subjects and functions out of the subjects and functions assigned under the Ministryof Skills Development and Vocational Training by the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1933/13 of 24.05.2015 are assigned tothe Hon. Palitha Range Bandara, the State Minister of Skills Development and Vocational Training by exercising powersvested in me as per the sub-article 44(5) of Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

1.All functions pertaining to Sri Lanka Institute of Printing,2.Functions relating to giving vocational and technical education suitable for the job market pertaining to theDepartment of Technical Education and Training.3.Functions relating to offering training opportunities to the youth seeking overseas employments in an extentsufficient for the international demand, in collaboration with the Bureau of Foreign Employment.

MAHINDA SAMARASINGHE,Minister of Skills Development and Training.
