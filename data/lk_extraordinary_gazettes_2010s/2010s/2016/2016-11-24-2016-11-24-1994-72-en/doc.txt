THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW, NO. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978, I Patalee ChampikaRanawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, being of the opinion that the area specified in Column I, [excluding the alreadydeclared areas also mentioned under Schedule I and II in Column I] hereto, having specified the metes and bounds in the corrsponding entryin Column II of the Schedule hereto are suitable for development, do by this order declare such area to be an Urban Development Area.

PATALEE CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis & Western Development,17th and 18th Floor,"Suhurupaya",Subuthipura Road,Battaramulla.
