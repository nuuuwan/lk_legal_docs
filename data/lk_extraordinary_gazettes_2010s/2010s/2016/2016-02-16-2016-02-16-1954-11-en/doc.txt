APPROPRIATION STATUTE No. 04 OF 2015

A statute to provide for the services of the financial year 2016 , to authorize the raising of loans within theprovince of Sabaragamuwa for the purpose of such service, to make financial provisions in respect of certain activities ofthe Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council during that financial year, to enable the payment, by way of advances out of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council fund or any other fund or monies of, or at the disposal of, the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil fund, and to make provision for matters connected with or incidental to the aforesaid matter.
