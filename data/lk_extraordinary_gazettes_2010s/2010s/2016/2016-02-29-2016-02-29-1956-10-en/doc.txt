SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof list of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specifiedin the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 04 months commencingfrom 1st March 2016, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1941/43 of 20.11.2015 in respect of Potatoes and B’Onions (HS Code Nos. 0701.90, 0703.10.20)are rescinded on the 29th February 2016.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,29th February, 2016.
