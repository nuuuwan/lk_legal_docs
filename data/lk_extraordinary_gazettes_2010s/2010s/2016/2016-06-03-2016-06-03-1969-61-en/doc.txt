THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: EP/LD/AA/2008-2015/01.

Land Ministry No.: LD/05/2008/HW/STDP/JBIC/272.

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquirethe following lands which are required for a public purpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

Lot No. 1-2 (Lots 2) allotments in total extent of about 0.0638Hectare, of land in Preliminary Plan No. Ga 4166 and dated2016/03/30 prepared by the Sinior - Superintendent of Surveyof Galle Distric on behalf of Survey General in the village ofNawandagala in Grama Niladhari Divisions of 25A,Nawandagala in Divisional Secretary’s Division of Elpitiya inGalle District in the Southern Province.

01. Lot No. 1Name of land: Wattahenakotuwa; Description: Road (Part);Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Thilaka Iddamaldeniya,Atakohota, Nawandagala (Part of the plot of Land No. 2267 inP.P.9500); Extent: 0.0229 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 2Name of land: Wattahenakotuwa; Description: Open WasteLand; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: ThilakaIddamaldeniya, Atakohota, Nawandagala (Part of the plot ofLand No. 2267 in P.P.9500); Extent: 0.0409 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agent duly authorized inwriting, before me at Divisional Secretariat office of Elpitiyaon the from 13th July, 2016 at 10.00 a.m and are herebyrequested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or beforethe 05th July, 2016 the nature of their interests in the land, theparticulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisitionof the said lands, the amount of compensation and the detailsof the computation of such amount.

N. A. K. L. VIJENAYAKA,Divisional Secretary & Acquiring Officer,

Elpitiya.
