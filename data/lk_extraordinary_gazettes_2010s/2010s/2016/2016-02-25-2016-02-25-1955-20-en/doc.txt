UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Edcuation and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (Guarantee) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under theCompanies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number GL 24, do by this Order, recognize the Sri Lanka Instituteof Information Technology (Guarantee) Limited as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing HigherEducation therein, leading to the award of the Degrees specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, of which theabbreviations are specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of the Schedule hereto.
