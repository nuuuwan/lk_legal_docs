NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 83/0229 Hambanthota District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 830057 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Jayagama (Block 02) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Divi-sion of 72 - Jayagama in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Lunugamwehera in the District of Hambanthota in the Province of Southern should submit his claim to the undersigned before 09th June, 2016 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
