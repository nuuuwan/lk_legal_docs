SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of listof the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specified in thecorresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 11th November 2016, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1984/26 of 15.09.2016 in respect of White Sugar (H.S Code No. 1701.99.10 and 1701.99.20) arerescinded with effect from 11th November 2016.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,10th November, 2016.
