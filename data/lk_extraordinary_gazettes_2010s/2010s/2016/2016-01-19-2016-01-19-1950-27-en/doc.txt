L.D.B. 21/1978(x).THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25 A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, Ministerof Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act in respect of theNational School of Business Management Limited a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of2007 and bearing registration number PB 4833, do by this Order, recognize the National School of Business ManagementLimited as a degree awarding Institute for the purpose of developing higher education therein, leading to the award of theDegrees and Post Graduate Diplomas specified in Column I of Schedule I (subject to the conditions specified) and ScheduleII respectively, of which abbreviations are specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of Schedule I and Schedule IIrespectively.

SCHEDULE I

APPLICABLE CONDITIONS

1. National School of Business Management Limited shall adhere to the minimum university entry qualificationsstipulated by the University Grants Commission including a minimum of a simple pass in General English at GeneralCertificate of Education (GCE) Advance Level.

2. National School of Business Management Limited shall provide sufficient number of computers for the students,within 3 months from the date of publication of this Order and keep the Specified Authority informed of it.
