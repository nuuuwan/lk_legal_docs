Greetings :

WHEREAS the National Salaries and Cadre Commission was established by the Presidential Directive dated March03, 2016 in the exercise of powers vested in me by Article 33 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka.

AND WHEREAS representation of human resources in diverse fields is identified as a vital contributory factor in theeffective outcome of the Commission,

AND WHEREAS it is apparent that the diversity of membership composition of the Commission needs to be furtherwidened,

Now, therefore, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka reposing greattrust and confidence in the prudence, ability and fidelity of Suranga Naullage Esquire do by these presents appoint the saidSuranga Naullage Esquire as a member of the National Salaries and Cadre Commission in terms of the aforesaid PresidentialDirective.

Given at Colombo, under the seal of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on this 18 th day of March, in theyear Two Thousand and Sixteen.

By Order of His Excellency,

P. B. ABEYKOON,Secretary to the President.
