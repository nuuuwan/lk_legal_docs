Revocation of Order under Section 4(1)

WHEREAS by Order made under Section 4 (1) of the Industrial Disputes Act Chapter 131 of the Legislative Enactments of Ceylon(Revised Edition 1956), as amended by Act, Nos. 14 and 62 of 1957, 14 of 1962 and 39 of 1968 read with Industrial Disputes (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 37 of 1968 dated 15.07.2011 and published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri LankaExtraordinary No. 1715/11 dated 20.07.2011 the Industrial Dispute in respect of the matter specified in the statement of the Commissionerof Labour dated 24.05.2011.

Mr. H. A. D. Chandrasiri, No. 36, Atampitiya Road, Bandarawela, of the one part

and

Uva Development Bank, No. 26, Bank Road, Badulla, of the other part

Was referred to Mr. K. R. M. N. Lawrence for Settlement by arbitration.

AND whereas it is now deemed expedient that the said Order be revoked, I, W. D. J. Seneviratne, Minister of Labour and Trade UnionRelations do hereby revoke the said Order and further make Order that no proceedings be taken upon the said Order dated 15.07.2011.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.Colombo,23rd March, 2016.
