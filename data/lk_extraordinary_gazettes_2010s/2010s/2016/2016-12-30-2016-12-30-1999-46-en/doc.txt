PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the North Central Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the North Central Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership ofthe North Central Province Provincial Council by reason of Death ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which themember belonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate aneligible person to fill such vacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has nominated Mr. Nongomu VithanalageSamantha to fill such vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Nongomu Vithanalage Samantha, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from theAdministrative District of Polonnaruwa in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,N. J. ABEYSEKERE,S. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLE,Chairman,Member,Member,Election CommissionElection CommissionElection Commission
