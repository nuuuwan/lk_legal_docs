UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25 A

BY Virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the Sri Lanka International Buddhist Academy, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of2007 and bearing Registration Number GA 2232, do by this Order, recognize the Sri Lanka International Buddhist Academy,as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing higher education therein, leading to the award of the degreesspecified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, of which the abbreviations are specified in the corresponding entries inColumn II of the Schedule hereto.

SCHEDULE
