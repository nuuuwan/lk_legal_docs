Government Notifications

STRATEGIC DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS ACT, No. 14 OF 2008

Order under Sub Section (4) of Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub Section (4) of Section 3 of the Strategic Development Projects Act, No. 14 of2008 as amended, I, Malik Samarawickrama, Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade, being the Ministerin Charge of the subject of Investment Promotion, further to the Order published under Sub Section (4) of Section 3 underGazette Extraordinary No. 1714/3 dated 11th July 2011, do by this Order declare that,

Clause (4) of the Order published under Gazette Extraordinary No. 1714/3 dated 11th July 2011, is herebyamended to read as follows is order to extend the time granted to implement the Project :-

“(4) The aforesaid “project to construct, a luxury hotel as Mixed Use Complex Including High Quality Residenciesand a High - End Shopping Mall, in Fort, Colombo 01, in the Western Province’’ Shall commence commercial operationon 30.06.2019.’’

MALIK SAMARAWICKRAMA,Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade.

Colombo,24th June, 2016.
