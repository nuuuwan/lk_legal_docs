SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Southern Province Industrial Development Authority (Amendment) Statute

Be it, enacted by the Southern Province Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :-

1. This Statute may be cited as the Southern Province Industrial Development Authority(Amendment) Statute No. 01 of 2016 and shall come into operation on 03.06.2016 as the Governor hasassented to this Statute on 03.06.2016.

2. Section 4 of the Southern Province Industrial Development Authority Statute No. 01 of 2002(hereinafter in this Statute referred to as the “Statute”) is hereby amended by the insertion immediatelyafter paragraph (a) thereof, the following new paragraph :-

“(aa)to encourage the formation of societies and associations by the industrialistswithin the province for the promotion and advancement of the products of theirindustries ;”

3. Section 6 of the Statute is hereby amended by the insertion immediately after paragraph (17),the following new paragraph :-

“(18)register and issue a certificate or registration to any society or association establishedby the industrialists within the province for the promotion and advancement of theproducts of their industries.”

Short Title anddate of operation.

Amendment ofSection 4 of theSouthern ProvinceIndustrialDevelopmentAuthority StatuteNo. 1 of 2002.

Amendment ofSection 6 of theStatute.
