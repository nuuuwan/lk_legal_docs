EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, do by this Order declare that, the Excise duty on every article specified in the ColumnsI, II and III of the Schedule hereto, shall be payable at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column IV of theSchedule hereto, with effect from 07th December 2016.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,06th December 2016.
