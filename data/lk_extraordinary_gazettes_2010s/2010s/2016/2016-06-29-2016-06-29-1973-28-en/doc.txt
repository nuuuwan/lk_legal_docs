THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 916/7of March 27, 1996 (as last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1964/11 of April 26, 2016)which specifies the Faculties and the Departments of the South Eastern University of Sri Lanka, by the addition immediatelyafter the item “Faculty of Engineering” appearing in Column I of the Schedule and its corresponding entries in Column IIof the Schedule of the following item and entries :-

“Column IColumn II

Faculty of TechnologyDepartment of Bio Systems Technology

Department of Information andCommunication Technology”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,27th June, 2016.
