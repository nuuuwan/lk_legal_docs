EXCISE NOTIFICATION

IT is hereby notified that the following Order issued by the Honorable Minister of Finance, by virtue of the power vested inhim by Section 32 read with Section 22 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52) as amended from time to time, and published inthe Extraordinary Gazette No. 1956/19 of 02.03.2016 effective from 01.04.2016, was passed by Parliament on 19.05.2016.

R. H. S. SAMARATUNGA,Secretary,Ministry of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,16th June 2016.

The Excise Notification No. 972 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1885/44 of 24.10.2014 is herebyrescinded.

Rules

1. There shall be a imposed and charged excise duty on the articles specified in Column I and on the respectivequantities in the Column II at the rates specified in Column III of the Schedule I.

2. A person who wishes to import Ethyl alcohol shall submit an application form set out in accordance with theSchedule II to the Commissioner General of Excise.

3. If the Commissioner General of Excise is satisfied about the application forwarded by the applicant, the approvalshall be sent to the Controller of Imports and Exports with the form set out in accordance with the Schedule III.

4. If the Commissioner General of Excise is not satisfied about the application, the Commissioner shall infrom theapplicant, reason for rejection within five days of the receipt of the application.

5. After the receipt of Ethyl alcohol by the Department of Customs, the Director General of Customs shall issue aletter in accordance with Schedule IV for the purpose of clearance to the Commissioner General of Excise.

6. The Commissioner General of Excise shall issue a license for import and a pass for transport of Ethyl alcoholin the form set out in accordance with Schedule V upon payment of excise duty.

7. The import license issued by the Commissioner General of Excise shall be produced for inspection whenrequired by an Excise officer of the licensed premises.
