CIVIL AVIATION AUTHORITY OF SRI LANKA ACT, No. 34 OF 2002

Order under Section 36

ACQUISITION OF LAND FOR DEVELOPMENT OF DOMESTIC AIRPORTS

MEMBERS of the public are hereby informed that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 36 of the Civil AviationAuthority of Sri Lanka Act, No. 34 of 2002, I, Nimal Siripala De Silva, being the Minister in charge of the subject of CivilAviation has approved acquisition of lands specified in the following Schedule to the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lankafor the purpose of development of domestic airports as proposed in the National Budget - 2016 passed by Parliament.

NIMAL SIRIPALA DE SILVA,Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation.Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,01st March 2016.
