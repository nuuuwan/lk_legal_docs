NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 42/0419 Kurunegala District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 420202 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Mehiella North (Block 05) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Nilad-hari Division of 713 - Mehiella North in the Divisional Secre-tary’s Division of Mallawapitiya in the District of Kurunegala in the Province of North Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 29th April, 2016 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Ga-zette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 420202 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretary’s, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - Generals’ office.
