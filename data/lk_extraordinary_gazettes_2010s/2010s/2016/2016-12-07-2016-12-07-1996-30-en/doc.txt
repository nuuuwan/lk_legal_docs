THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Ref. No. : MCUDP/CMC/MD/CA/P4.

Reference No.: 4-3/8/2015/PC252.

I, hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Governmentintends to acquire the following lands for the widening anddevelopment of Norris Canal in Maradana under the MetroColombo Urban Development Project:

Description of the lands to be acquired:

Three (3) allotments of lands described in following nameand Total Extents of 0.0217 Hectare depicted as Lot Nos. 1, 2and 3 in the Preliminary Plan No. CO 9669 dated 02.12.2016prepared by the Surveyor General situated in the Village calledMaradana, in the Colombo Divisional Secretariat Division inColombo District, Western Province within the ColomboMunicipal Council limit, and morefully described as follows.

1. Lot No. 1Name of the Land: Asst. No. 338 (Part) T. B. Jayah Mawatha,Colombo 10; Land Use: Home Garden; Claimant: Cannot betraced; Extent: 0.0002 Hectare.

2. Lot No. 2Name of the Land: Asst. No. 338 (Part) T. B. Jayah Mawatha,Colombo 10; Land Use: Home Garden; Claimant: Cannot betraced; Extent: 0.0001 Hectare.

3. Lot No. 3Name of the Land: Asst. No. 338 (Part) T. B. Jayah Mawatha,Colombo 10; Land Use: Home Garden Parts of two permanentbuildings and a guard room; Claimant: Cannot be traced;Extent: 0.0214 Hectare.

Any person interested in the aforesaid lands, are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized inwriting, before me at the Municipal Commissioner's officeColombo, on the 28th December, 2016 at 9.30 a.m. and arehereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on orbefore 20th December, 2016 the nature of their interest in theland, the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said lands, the amount of compensationand the details of the computation of such amount.

V. K. A. ANURA,Municipal Commissioner andOfficer Implementing the Powers andFunctions of the Colombo MunicipalCouncil and Land Acquisition Officer,

Colombo Municipal Council.
