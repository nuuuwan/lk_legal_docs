THE MONETARY LAW ACT, REGISTERED STOCK AND SECURITIES ORDINANCE AND LOCALTREASURY BILLS ORDINANCE

It is hereby notified that by virtue of the powers vested in the Monetary Board under the provisions of the Monetary LawAct (Chapter 422), Registered Stock and Securities Ordinace (Chapter 420) and Local Treasury Bills Ordinance (Chapter417), the Monetary Board, in terms of Sections 5 and 86 of the Monetary Law Act, Regulation 11 of the Local TreasuryBills (Primary Dealers) Regulations No. 01 of 2009 dated 24.06.2009 and Regulation 11 of the Registered Stock andSecurities (Primary Dealers) Regulations No. 01 of 2009 dated 24.06.2009 made by the Minister of Finance under theaforesaid Local Treasury Bills Ordinance and the Registered Stock and Securities Ordinance, respectively, havingconsidered the serious regulatory and supervisory concerns on the affairs of the Entrust Securities PLC, its weak financialposition, has decided, with effect from 04 January 2016, to suspend the Board of Directors of the said Entrust SecuritiesPLC, to vest the management of the said Entrust Securities PLC with the National Savings Bank, and to appoint the Boardof Directors of the National Savings Bank to act as the Board of Directors of the said Entrust Securities PLC until furthernotice in the interest of maintaining monetary, banking and financial system stability of the country.

LAKSHMAN ARJUNA MAHENDRAN,Chairman of the Monetary Board andGovernor,Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

Central Bank of Sri Lanka,Colombo,22nd January, 2016.
