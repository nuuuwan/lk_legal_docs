PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Wages Board for the Marine Transport and Operational Services Trade

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 7

I, W. D. J. Seneviratne, Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations hereby notify under Section 7(1) of the WagesBoards Ordinance that I intend to make an order regarding the Marine Transport and Operational Services Trade underSection 6(1) of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136).

Objection if any, to the aforesaid order will be received by me until 12.00 noon on 24th October 2016.

Every such objection must be made in writing and must contain a statement of the grounds upon which suchobjection is taken.

W. D. J. SENEVIRATNE,Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.Department of Labour,Colombo 05,04th October 2016.

ORDER

The provisions of Part II of the Wages Board Ordinance, No. 27 of 1941 shall apply to the following trade. Undermentioned worker categories engaged in marine transport, operational services and other related transport and operationalservices in and out of dock, harbour and port ; and other workers engaged in incidental tasks thereto shall come under theWages Board for the marine transport and operational services trade.
