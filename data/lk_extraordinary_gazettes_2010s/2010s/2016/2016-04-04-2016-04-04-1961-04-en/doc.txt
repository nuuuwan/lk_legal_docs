SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Southern Province Industrial Development Authority (Amendment) Statute

Be it, enacted by the Southern Province Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :-

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk

PRINTED AT THE DEPARTMENT OF GOVERNMENT PRINTING, SRI LANKA.

1. This Statute may be cited as the Southern Province Industrial Development Authority(Amendment) Statute No. .... of 2016 and shall come into operation on such date as the Governor mayassent to this Statute.

2. Section 4 of the Southern Province Industrial Development Authority Statute No. 1 of 2002(hereinafter in this Statute referred to as the “Statute”) is hereby amended by the insertion immediatelyafter paragraph (a) thereof, the following new paragraph : -

“(aa) to encourage the formation of societies and associations by the industrialists within theprovince for the promotion and advancement of the products of their industries ; ”

3. Section 6 of the Statute is hereby amended by the insertion immediately after paragraph (17),the following new paragraph : -

“(18) register and issue a certificate of registration to any Society or Association Establishedby the Industrialists within the province for the promotion and advancement of theproducts of their industries. ”

4. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts, the Sinhala text shallprevail.

ARJUNA UDAYAKANTHA WELARATNE,Secretary,Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Productionand Development, Environmental Affairs,Rural Industries, Electricity, Ruraland Estate Infrastructure Development,Southern Province.
