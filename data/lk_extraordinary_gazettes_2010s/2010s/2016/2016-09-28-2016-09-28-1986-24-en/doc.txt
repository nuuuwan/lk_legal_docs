Amended order Pertaining to the Delegation of powers in the Western Provincial Public Service underSection 32(2a) of Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987, amended by the Provincial Councils (Amendment)

Act, No. 28 of 1990

IT is hereby notified that without any detriment to actions taken under Schedule 06 of the Order dated 23.12.2015,published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification dated 21st January 2016 No. 1950/47 of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka with regard to delegation of powers in terms of appointments, transfers, dismissals and disciplinarycontrol of officers in the Western Provincial Public Service as per Section 32(2a) of the Provincial Councils Act, No. 42of 1987 as amended by the Provincial Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1990, the said Order is amended by theremoval of the said Schedule 06 and by the insertion instead thereof the Schedule No. 06 as stated below hereof witheffect from the twentieth of September 2016.
