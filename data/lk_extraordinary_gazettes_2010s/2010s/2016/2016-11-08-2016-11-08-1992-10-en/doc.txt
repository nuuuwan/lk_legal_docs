LAND (RESTRICTIONS ON ALIENATION) ACT, No. 38 OF 2014

Order under Section 3(2)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me in terms of Section 3(2) of the Land (Restrictions on Alienation), Act, No. 38 of2014, I, Ravi Karunanayake, being the Minister of Finance in consultation with the Minister of Lands and with the priorwritten approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, do by this order exempt Avic International Hotels Lanka Limited which isengaged in and Infrastructure Development Project which is a mixed development project with an apartment complex atNo. 418, 420 and 422, R. A. De Mel Mawatha, Colombo 03 identified as a Strategic Development Project in terms of theStrategic Development Projects Act, No. 14 of 2008, from the application of the provisions of Section 2 of the Land(Restrictions on Alienation) Act, No. 38 of 2014, in relation to the land defined as Lot A as depicted in the Plan No. 4839dated 3rd July, 2004 prepared by Gamini B. Dodanwela, Licensed Surveyor, bearing Assessment No. 418, R. A. De MelMawatha, Colombo 03.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.
