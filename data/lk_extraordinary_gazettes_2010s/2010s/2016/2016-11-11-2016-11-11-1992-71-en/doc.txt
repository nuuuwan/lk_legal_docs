THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978ENACTED BY THE NATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

Order under Section 15(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 15(1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 enacted bythe National State Assembly, I, Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Magapolis and Western Development do by thisOrder, vest with the Urban Development Authority, with effect from today the land situated is Lunupokuna ward within Limitsof the Municipal Council of Colombo described morefully in the Schedule hereto, being the land belonging to the ColomboMunicipal Council.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA M.P.,Minister of Megapolis & Western Development.Ministry of Megapolis & Western Development,17 th & 18 th Floors,‘‘Suhurupaya’’,Subuthipura Road,Battaramulla,31st August, 2016.Schedule

The land is containing an extent of 2A.- 0R.22.13P. depicted as Lot Nos. 1 to 4 in P Plan No. A 5353 dated 20.06.1964prepared by the Surveyor General situated at Lunupokuna Ward in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Colombo within theMunicipal Council limits of Colombo, in the District of Colombo, Western Province.

The land is bounded as follows :

North: Aluth Mawatha Road, Canal and Lot No. 9 in P P A 2948 ;East: Lot No. 9 in P P A 2948 and No. 8 in P P 2948 ;South: Lot No. 7 in P P A 2948 and Asst. Nos. 266, 262/1 to 262/16 and 262 Aluth Mawatha Path ;West: Path, Asst. Nos. 266, 262/1 to 262/16 & 262 Aluth Mawatha Road, Asst. No. 254 Aluth Mawatha Road, Asst. No. 250/6 Aluth Mawatha Road.
