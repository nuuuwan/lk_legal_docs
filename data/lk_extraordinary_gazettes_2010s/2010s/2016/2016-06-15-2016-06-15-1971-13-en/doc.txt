REGULATIONS MADE UNDER PARAGRAPHS (d) AND (e) SUBSECTION (2) OF SECTION 36 OF THESRI LANKA SUSTAINABLE ENERGY AUTHORITY ACT, No. 35 OF 2007

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 67 read with paragraphs (d) and (e) of Subsection (2) of Section 36 ofthe Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Act, No. 35 of 2007 and on the recommendation of the Sri Lanka SustainableEnergy Authority, I, Ranjith Siyambalapitiya as the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy do by this order make theunder mentioned regulations for Energy Performance Standards of Appliances (Double capped Tubular Fluorescent Lampsand Fluorescent Lamp Ballasts).

RANJITH SIYAMBALAPITIYA,Minister of Power and Renewable Energy.31st May 2016.

L.D.B.8/2007(II)Annex A

Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Act, No. 35 of 2007

Regulations made by the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, under Section 67 read with paragraphs (d) and(e) of Subsection (2) of Section 36 of the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Act, No. 35 of 2007.

RANJITH SIYAMBALAPITIYA,Minister of Power and Renewable Energy.Colombo,31st May 2016.

REGULATIONS

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Energy Performance Standards of Appliances (Double-capped TubularFluorescent Lamps and Fluorescent Lamp Ballasts) Regulations, No. 1 of 2016.

2. No person shall import into Sri Lanka or manufacture in Sri Lanka, any item specified in Column I of theSchedule hereto unless it conforms to the Sri Lanka Standard (SLS) specified in the corresponding entry in Column II ofthe Schedule hereto.

3. Every importer or a manufacturer of any item specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto shall -

(a)affix to the item or to the cover or to the wrapper or to the box which contains it, an energy label whichconforms to the Sri Lanka Standard specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of the Schedulehereto ; and

(b)when required by the Authority, submit a certificate issued by the Sri Lanka Standards Institution, certify-ing that the item imported or manufactured, conforms to the standard specified in the correspondingentry in Column II of the Schedule hereto.

4. No person shall store, display, sell either wholesale or retail, offer for sale or distribute any item specified inColumn I of the Schedule hereto, whether imported into Sri Lanka or manufactured in Sri Lanka, to which an energy label isaffixed to such item or to the cover or to the wrapper or to the box in which the item is contained, which does not complywith the requirement imposed by regulation 3(a) hereto.

5. In these Regulations -

“Act” means the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Act, No. 35 of 2007 ;

“Authority” means the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority established by the Sri Lanka Sustainable EnergyAuthority Act, No. 35 of 2007 ; and

“Sri Lanka Standards Institution” means the Sri Lanka Standards Institution established by the Sri Lanka Stan-dards Institution Act, No. 6 of 1984.
