PART III — LANDS

Land Reform Commission Notices

Statutory Determination No. : 4639Unique No. : Co/1807Co/1808

STATUTORY DETERMINATION UNDER SECTION 19 OF THE LAND REFORM LAW No. 01 OF 1972

WHEREAS Late Dr. Gamini Corea of No. 21, Norton Lodge, Horton Place, Colombo 07 had made a Statutory Declarationunder Section 18 of the Law, by virtue of the powers vested in it under Section 19 of the law, a Statutory Determinationhad been made by the Commission under Statutory Determination No. 3244 published in Government Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 291/18 dated 16.11.1977 specifying the portion of agricultural land which the statutory lessee should be allowed toretain.

Moreover late Mrs. Alis Frida Corea who was residing at the same address and the statutory declarant of Co/1808 and the late mother of late Dr. Gamini Corea, the statutory declarant of Co/1807, had also made a Statutory Declarationand accordingly Statutory Determination had been made under Statutory Determination No. 3533 published in GovernmentGazette Extraordinary No. 21/4 dated 29.01.1979 and Mrs. Alis Frida Corea, the statutory declarant of Co/1808 demisedon or about 19.10.1978, late Dr. Gamini Corea became as owner of land in excess of the ceiling as the sole legal heir ofMrs. Alis Frida Corea.

Therefore, by virtue of the Section 05 of the land Reform Law No. 01 of 1972, as amended by Land Reform(Special Provisions) Act, No. 39 of 1981 and Land Reform (Special Provisions) Act, No. 14 of 1986, the extent ofagricultural lands owned by late Dr. Gamini Corea in excess of the ceiling is vested in the Land Reform Commission.

As such new Statutory Determination specifying the portion of land due to the estate of said late Dr. GaminiCorea the declarant in Co/1807 is allowed to retain, had to be determined and is specifically described in the followingSchedule.

Hence, the Statutory Determination already published in the name of Dr. Gamini Corea, the statutory declarant ofCo/1807 under the Statutory Determination No. 3244 in the Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 291/18 dated16.11.1977 is hereby revoked.

T. A. SUMANATHISSA THAMBUGALA,Chairman,Land Reform Commission.No. C 82, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha,Colombo 07,15th March 2016.
