NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 81/0097 Galle District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 810150 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village Kapumulugoda of (Block 03) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Nilad-hari Division of 44 C - Kapumulugoda in the Divisional Secre-tariat Division of Hikkaduwa in the District of Galle in the Prov-ince of Southern should submit his claim to the undersigned be-fore 22nd December, 2016 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accord-ingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 810150 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - Generals’ office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. K.A.K. RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 09th November 2016 11-1262/1
