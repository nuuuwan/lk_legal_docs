THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)AS AMENDED BY THE LAND ACQUISITION(AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 28 OF 1964

Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: VSW/DS/LB/Acqu/ Pan/ Police.

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act, As amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intendsto accquire the following land which is required for a publicpurpose :-

Description of the land to be acquired:

Preliminary Plan: Ya 2843; Situation: Village - Pandath-tharippu J/146; DS Division: Valikamam South West,Sandilipay; District: Jaffna; Province: Northern.

Lot No. 01Name of land: Mappapulam; Description: Garden containssix permanent buildings, Part of one permanent building, Part

of one overhead water tank (under construction) and twopits; Claimant: State; Extent: 0.3163 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally or by agent duly authorized inwriting, before me at Valikamam South West, SandilippayDivisional Secretariat office on 03rd March 2017 at 10.00 a.mand are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (induplicate) on or before the 20th February 2017 the nature oftheir interests in the land, the particulars of their claims forcompensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amountof compensation and the details of the computation of suchamount. All deeds and Documents in support of claim shouldbe produced before me at the inquiry.

YASOTHA UTHAYAKUMAR,Divisional Secretary,Valikamam South West, Sandilipay.
