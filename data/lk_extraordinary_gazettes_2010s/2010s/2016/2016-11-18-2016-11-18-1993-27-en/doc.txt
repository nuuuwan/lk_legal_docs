LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

My No. : 4-3/1 (Appointments - Acquisition) - Volume III

IT is hereby informed that the term “Divisional Secretary’s Divisions of Kurunegala, Kegalle and Kandy” that appeared inthe notice published in the Gazette No. 1988/39 dated 14.10.2016 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka forthe appointment of an Acquiring Officer shall be amended as “All the Divisional Secretary’s Divisions of Kurunegala,Kegalle and Kandy districts”.
