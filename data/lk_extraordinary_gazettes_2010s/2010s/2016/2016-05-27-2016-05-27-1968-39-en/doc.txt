SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

IT is hereby notified that the Department hereinafter referred to as “Southern Provincial Department of RuralDevelopment” is established from 18.04.2016 to function under the Ministry of Sports and Youth Affairs, RuralDevelopment, Cultural and Art Affairs, Social Welfare, Probation and Child Care Services, Women Affairs and HomeEconomic Enhancement, Housing and Construction and Manpower and Employment of the Southern Province in order toexercise powers vested in the Provincial Council under the 13th Amendment to the Constitution.

DR. HEMAKUMARA NANAYAKKARA,Hon. Governor of the Southern Province,
