STAMP DUTY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 12 OF 2006

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Stamp Duty (Special Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 2006, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, do by this Order amend the Order made under that Section and published in theGazette Extraordinary No. 1465/19 dated October 05, 2006 as last amended by the Order published in the GazetteExtraordinary No. 1933/14 dated September 21,2015, as specified in the SCHEDULE hereto.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,31st December 2015.
