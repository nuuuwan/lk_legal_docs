L.D.B. 12/2015

CONVENTION ON THE CIVIL ASPECTS OF INTERNATIONALCHILD ABDUCTION ACT, No. 10 OF 2001

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 2 of the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child AbductionAct, No. 10 of 2001, I, Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, Minister of Justice, do by this Order specify–

(a) the countries described in the Schedule hereto, as countries in respect of which the provisions of this Act shallapply ; and

(b) 5th September 2015 as the date with effect from which the provisions of this Act shall apply, in respect of suchspecified countries.

WIJEYADASA RAJAPAKSHE,Minister of Justice.

Colombo,09th March 2016.
