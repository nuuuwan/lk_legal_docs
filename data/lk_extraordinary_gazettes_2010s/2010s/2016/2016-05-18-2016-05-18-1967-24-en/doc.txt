PART III - LANDS NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998 (SECTION 55) Reference the intestate property of late M. Kumarage Simon Singngo who resided in the village of Magammana Portion, in the Divi-sional Secretary’s Division of Homagama , in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Kumarage Simon Singngo described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 29th April, 2016 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
