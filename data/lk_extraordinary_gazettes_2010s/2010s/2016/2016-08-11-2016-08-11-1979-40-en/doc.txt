The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

1A

ORDER UNDER SECTION 34(2) OF THE REGISTRATION OF PERSONS (AMENDMENT)ACT, No. 08 OF 2016

BY virtue of powers vested with me under Section 34(2) of the Registration of Persons (Amendment) Act, No. 08 of2016, I, S. B. Navinna, the Minister of Internal Affairs, Wayamba Development and Cultural Affairs hereby ordered that,the Sections 6, 9, 14, 16 and 17 of the Registration of Persons Act, No. 32 of 1968 and every regulation (except theregistration of persons regulations 1990 published in the Gazette No. 637/6 dated 1990.11.20) made under that Act inforce immediately prior to the commencement of the Registration of Persons (Amendment) Act, No. 08 of 2016, shallcontinue to be in operation up to 30.06.2017, until the necessary infrastruture arrangements and technologicalmethodologies are made to give effect to the amendments made to Sections 6, 9, 14, 16 and 17 of the Registration ofPersons Act, No. 32 of 1968, by the Registration of Persons (Amendment) Act, No. 08 of 2016.

S. B. NAVINNA,The Minister of Internal Affairs,Wayamba Development and Cultrual Affairs.

at Battaramulla,01st of August 2016.
