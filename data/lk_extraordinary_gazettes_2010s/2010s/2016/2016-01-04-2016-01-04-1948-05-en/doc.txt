PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THEUVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Uva Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership ofthe Uva Province Provincial Council by reason of being elected as a member of the Parliament ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by the Election Commission uner Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligibleperson to fill such vacancy ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has nominated Mr. MuruganSachchithananthan to fill such vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 02 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Murugan Sachchithananthan, elected as a menber of the above Provincial Council from the AdministrativeDistrict of Badulla in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,N. J. ABEYSEKERE,S. RATNAJEEVAN, H. HOOLE,Chairman.Member.Member.

Election Secretariat,Rajagiriya,04th January, 2016.
