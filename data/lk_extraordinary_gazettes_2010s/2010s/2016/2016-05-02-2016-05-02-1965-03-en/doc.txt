APPROVAL FOR THE AMENDED DEVELOPMENT PLAN PREPARED FOR THE KEGALLE

URBAN DEVELOPMENT AREA

GENERAL public is hereby noticed that the Amended Development Plan prepared for the Kegalle Urban Council area inaccordance with the powers vested under Section 8“H” of the Urban Development Authority Act, (Amended) bearingNo. 04 of 1982 has been approved on 19.04.2016 by the Hon. Patalee Champika Ranawaka the Minister of Megapolis andWestern Development by virtue of the powers vested with him under Section 8“F” of the Urban Development AuthorityAct, (Amended) No. 4 of 1982.

RANJIT FERNANDO,

Chairman,Urban Development Authority.

26th April 2016.

Approval for the Amended Development Plan prepared for the Kegalle Urban Development Area

I, Patalee Champika Ranawaka the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development by virtue of the powersvested with me under Section 8“H” of the Urban Development Authority Act, (Amended) No. 4 of 1982 hereby approvedthe Amended Development Plan prepared for the Kegalle Urban Area of the Kegalle Urban Council jurisdiction consideringthe recommendation made by the Board of Management of the Urban Development Authority at its meeting held on05.09.2012.

PATALEE CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA,

Minister of Megapolis and

Western Development.
