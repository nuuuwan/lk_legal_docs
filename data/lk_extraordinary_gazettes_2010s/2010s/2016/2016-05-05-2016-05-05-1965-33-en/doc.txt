REGISTRATION OF PERSONS ACT No. 32 OF 1968

Notification under Section 36(2)

BY virtue of powers vested in me under section 36(2) of the Registration of Persons Act No. 32 of 1968 I, S. B. NavinnaMinister of Internal Affairs, Wayamba Development and Cultural Affairs hereby notify that a maximum fee of one hundredand fifty rupees should be charged by a registered photographer, in respect of supplying three copies of a photograph aftertaking a photograph of a person applies for obtaining an identity card or duplicate of an identity card after registration.

I hereby cancel the notification published in Gazette No. 14998 of 18th February 1972 in this regard.

S. B. NAVINNA,Minister of Internal Affairs, Wayamba Developmentand Cultural Affairs.

Battaramulla,05th May 2016.
