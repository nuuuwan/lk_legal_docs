PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Direction under Section 54(3) of National Sports Association Regulations

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 54(3), Regulations of National Sports Associations, published inGazette Extraordinary No. 1825/38 of 30th August 2013, made under Section 41 of the Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973.

I, Dayasiri Jayasekara, Minister of Sports do by this Order inform the Sri Lanka Auto Mobile Sports Associationthat Mr. Samasundara Ekanayake Rajawardhana Tennakoon Mudiyanselage Saman Priyantha Bandara, Director General ofthe Department of Sports Development has been appointed as the Competent Authority, in terms of the Section 39(1) of theSports Act, No. 25 of 1973 for the smooth functioning of that Association and direct that Mr. Samasundara EkanayakeRajawardhana Tennakoon Mudiyanselage Saman Priyantha Bandara, Director General of the Department of SportsDevelopment, to convene the election of the Sri Lanka Auto Mobile Sports Association to elect new office bearers until anelection is held lawfully.

DAYASIRI JAYASEKARA (Attorney-at-law),Minister of Sports.

Colombo,28th September 2016.
