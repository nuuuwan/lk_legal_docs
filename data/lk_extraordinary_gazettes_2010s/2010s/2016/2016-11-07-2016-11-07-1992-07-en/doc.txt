PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Delegation of Powers by the Public Service Commission in terms of Article 56(1) of the Constitution

01. The Schedule “B” published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1989/29 dated 19.10.2016 wherein the powerswere delegated to the Education Service Committee by the Public Service Commission in terms of the provisionsin Article 56(1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka is hereby amended witheffect from 20.10.2016 after having incorporated following provisions into the provisions governing thedelegation of powers set out in such Schedule.

02. Other matters in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1989/29 dated 19.10.2016 wherein the powers were delegatedby the Public Service Commission shall remain unchanged.

By the Order of the Public Service Commission.

H. M. GAMAINI SENEVIRATHNA,Secretary,Public Service Commission.
