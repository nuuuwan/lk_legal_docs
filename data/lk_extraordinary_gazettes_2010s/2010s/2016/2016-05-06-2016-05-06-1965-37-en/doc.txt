THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Delegation of Powers by the National Police Commission in terms of Article 155J

BY virtue of the powers vested in the National Police Commission in terms of Article 155J of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the said National Police Commission does hereby -

1. Delegate to Inspector General of Police and other Police Officers in terms of Article 155J specified in Column III ofthe Schedule hereto, its power of appointment, promotion, transfer, disciplinary control and dismissal as are specified inColumn II of the Schedule hereto, in respect of the categories of Police Officers as are specified in Column I of theSchedule hereto.

Provided that in the event of any question arising in respect of any delegation or as to the implementation of delegationmade by the National Police Commission so made, the decision of the National Police Commission shall be final andconclusive.

Provided further that, if any of the Police Officer specified in Column III is the complainant or a witness or a potentialwitness at any disciplinary inquiry against a police officer specified in Column I, the National Police Commission shallnotwithstanding any delegation which may have been made by it, continue to exercise its power of appointment, promotion,transfer, disciplinary control and dismissal as are specified in Column II of the Schedule hereto as if no delegation inrespect thereof had been made.

2. Specify that in the exercise of the powers herein delegated, Inspector General of Police and the other police officersto whom such powers are delegated shall at all times comply with the Procedural Rules, in respect of appointment,promotion and transfer of police officers, made by the National Police Commission in terms of Article 155G of theConstitution and also with such conditions and any Rules, Regulations and other Procedures which the said Commissionmay from time to time determine :
