PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

EXCISE ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 52)

Excise Notification No. 990

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 22(2) of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended from timeto time, I, Ravi Karunanayake, Minister of Finance, do by this Order declare that the excise duty imposed by the ExciseNotification No. 989 and published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 1981/87 of 26.08.2016 shall not be charged on foreignliqour imported for the purpose of re-export or to sell at duty free shops for foreign currency, with effect from 04.10.2016.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,03rd October 2016.
