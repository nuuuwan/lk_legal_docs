UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the S L T CAMPUS (PVT) LTD, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 andbearing registration number PV 101137, do by this Order recognize the S L T CAMPUS (PVT) LTD as a Degree AwardingInstitute for the purpose of developing Higher Education therein, leading to the award of the Degrees specified in ColumnI of the Schedule II hereto of which the abbreviations are set out in the respective corresponding entries in Column II ofthe Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in the Schedule I hereto.

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

S L T CAMPUS (PVT) LTD shall adhere to the minimum entry qualification of 3 ‘S’ passes in the Physical Science streamin one and the same sitting at GCE Advanced Level Examination or equivalent, as per UGC guidelines when admittingstudents for the following Degree Programmes.

Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineering in ElectronicsB Sc Hons. Eng. (Electronics and Telecommunication)and Telecommunications

Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineering in ElectronicsB Sc Hons. Eng. (Electronics and Engineeringand Engineering ManagementManagement)
