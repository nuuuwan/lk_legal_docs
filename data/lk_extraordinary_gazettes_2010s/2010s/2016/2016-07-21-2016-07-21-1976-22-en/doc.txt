Co-operative Employees Commission Statute No. 1 of 2016 (Amendment) of Uva Provincial

Council

This Statute is imposed to amend the Uva Provincial Cooperative Employees Commission StatuteNo. 04 of 2010, and,

Incorporated by the Uva Provincial Council, by virtue of the powers vested in the ProvincialCouncils by the 13th Amendment of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka.

01.This Statute may be cited as the “Uva Provincial Council Co-operative EmployeesCommission Statute No. 1 of 2016 (amended) and shall come into operation on suchdate as the Governor of the province may approve or appoint, unless the context requiresotherwise.

02.Uva Provincial Cooperative Employees Commission Statute No. 04 of 2010(hereinafter referred to as the “main statute”) is hereby amended by the followingsubstitution for sub-sections (1) & (3) of the Section 5 of the said statute.

(1)The Commission shall be consisting of five (05) members appointed by theMinister-in-charge of Co-operative Affairs of Uva Provincial Council, referredto as the “Minister” hereinafter.

(3)Three (03) of the members of the Co-operative Employees Commission shallbe appointed from the retired government officials who were in a staff gradeposition, and one of the three should be a person with a sound knowledge in theCo-operative field.

and

03.Sub-sections (2), (3) and (4) of Section 7 of the Uva Provincial Cooperative EmployeesCommission Statute No. 04 of 2010 is hereby replaced by the following substitutions.

(2)Three (3) members shall constitute the quorum for a meeting of the commission.

(3)If at any meeting only four (04) members of the commission are present andsuch members are divided in opinion as to the decision to be taken on any question,such question shall be referred to a meeting at which the all five (05) membersof the commission are present.

(4)If at any meeting, at which the five (05) members of the commission are present,and the members of the commission are divided in opinion as the decision to begiven on any question, such question shall be decided according to the opinionof the majority of the members, but if the members are equally divided, then, theChairman shall have a casting vote.

In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil text of this statute, the Sinhala textshall prevail.
