Government Notifications

PAYMENT DEVICES FRAUDS ACT, No. 30 OF 2006

Order under Section 8(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Payment Devices Frauds Act, No. 30 of2006, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby appoint the followingpersons and the Institution to the Panel of Experts for the purposes of Sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the aforesaid Act.

1.Mr. K. G. P. Sirikumara2.Mrs. C. C. P. Moragoda3.Mr. Ashan De Soysa4.Dr. C. R. De Silva5.Mr. Jerome C. Henricus6.Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Response Team (SLCERT)

The Order made under Sub-section (1) of the Payment Devices Frauds Act, No. 30 of 2006, and published inGazette Extraordinary No. 1536/28 of February 14, 2008 is hereby rescinded, without prejudice to anything duly donethereunder.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,31st August 2016.
