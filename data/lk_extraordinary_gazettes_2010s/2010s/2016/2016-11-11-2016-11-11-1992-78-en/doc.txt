PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

L. D. B. 1/ 2003

MUTUAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS ACT, No. 25 OF 2002

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by sub-section (3) of Section 2 of the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act,No. 25 of 2002, I, Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, Minister of Justice, do by this Order declare that the provisions of the aforesaidAct shall apply in respect of the Agreement between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka andthe Government of the Ukraine made at Kew on the Twenty Fifth day of June, Two Thousand and Sixteen. This Agreementwhich was made between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Ukraine, which is a Non- CommonwealthCountry, deals with mutual legal assistance in criminal matters and concerns inter alia the improved effectiveness on thepart of both parties to the Agreement through cooperation in relation to combating crimes including terrorism within theirrespective jurisdictions.

WIJEYADASA RAJAPAKSHE,

Minister of Justice.

Colombo,09th November 2016.
