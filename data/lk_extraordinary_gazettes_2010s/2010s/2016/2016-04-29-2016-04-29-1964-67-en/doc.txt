SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

IT is hereby notified that the Department hereinafter referred to as “Southern Provincial Department of Sports” is establishedfrom 18.04.2016 to function under the Ministry of Sports and Youth Affairs, Rural Development, Cultural and Art Affairs, SocialWelfare, Probation and Child Care Services, Women Affairs and Home Economic Enhancement, Housing and Constructionand Manpower and Employment of the Southern Province in order to exercise powers vested in the Provincial Council underthe 13th amendment to the Constitution.

Dr. HEMAKUMARA NANAYAKKARA,Hon. Governor of the Southern Province.
