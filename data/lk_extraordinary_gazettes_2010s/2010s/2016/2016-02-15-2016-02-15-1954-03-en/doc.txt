ORDER UNDER SECTION 123 (1) OF PRADESHIYA SABHAS ACT, No. 15 OF 1987 READ WITH SECTION 02 OFPROVINCIAL COUNCILS (CONSEQUENTIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 12 OF 1989

I, Zainul Abdeen Nazeer Ahamed, the Chief Minister and the Minister of Finance and Planning, Law and Order, LocalGovernment and Regional Administration, Rural Development, Transport, Tourism and Environment, Building andConstruction, Rural Industries and Housing of the Eastern Province, do hereby order published the notification publishedin Extraordinary Gazette bearing the No. 1934/ 10 dated 29.09.2015 hereby cancelled and revoked forthwith under and powervested in me in Section 123(1) of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987 read with Section 02 of the ProvincialCouncils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989.

ZAINU ABDEEN NAZEER AHAMED,Chief Minister and the Minister of Finance andPlanning, Law and Order, Local Governmentand Regional Administration, RuralDevelopment, Transport, Tourism andEnvironment, Building and ConstructionRural Industries and Housing.
