Government Notifications

L.D.B. 21/78

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order establishing the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka, which Order is deemed, byvirtue of the provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be an Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1948/58 of January 08, 2016) as follows :

(1) by the addition immediately after entry “9. Department of Sociology” appearing in Column II whichcorresponds to the item “Faculty of Arts” appearing in Column I of the Schedule of the following entries :-

“10. Department of Buddhist Studies” ;

(2) by the addition immediately after entry “14. Department of Surgery and Anesthesiology” appearing inColumn II which corresponds to the item “Faculty of Medicine” appearing in Column I of the Schedule ofthe following entries :-

“15. Department of Medical Humanities 16. Department of Medical Education 17. Department of Allied Health Sciences.”

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,03rd August 2016.
