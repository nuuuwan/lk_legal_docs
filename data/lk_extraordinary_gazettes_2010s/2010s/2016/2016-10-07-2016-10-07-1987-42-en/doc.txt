PROMOTION APPROVED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

IT is hereby notified that Air Marshal Gagan Pulasthi Bulathsinghala RWP, RSP, USP, MSc (Def Stu) in Mgt, M Phil, ndc,psc has been promoted to the rank of Air Chief Marshal of the Sri Lanka Air Force, with effect from 11th September 2016.
