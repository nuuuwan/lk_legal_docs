SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, LakshmanYapa Abeywardana, Acting Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this Order imposein respect of list of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the ratesspecified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 03 monthscommencing from 1st July 2016, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity LevyAct, No. 48 of 2007.

LAKSHMAN YAPA ABEYWARDANA,

Acting Minister of FinanceMinistry of Finance,Colombo 01.30th June 2016.
