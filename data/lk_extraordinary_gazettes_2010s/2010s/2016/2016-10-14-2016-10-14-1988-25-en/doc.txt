NOTICE BY THE DIRECTOR GENERAL OF CIVIL AVIATION UNDER THE AIR NAVIGATIONREGULATION 196

MEMBERS of the public are hereby notified that I have received on 06th September 2016 an application dated 05th July2016 from Sakurai Aviation Limited, at No. 121/3, Kirulapone Avenue, Colombo 05, seeking from this office an AirOperator Certificate/Airline Licence to engage in Domestic Scheduled Operations, Charter Operations and Aerial Workto engage in Domestic Operations in pursuance of the Air Navigation Regulations of 1955. Business name of the Companyhas been registered as Sakurai Aviation Limited and the trade name as “Sakurai Aviation Limited”. The Directors of theSakurai Aviation Limited are declared to be as follows.

1.Mr. Malape Gama Ethi Rallalage Asanga Prabath Yasawardana2.Mr. Hapuarachchige Don Nimal Jayantha3.Mr. Warnakulasuriya Rushan Malinda Fernando

Sakurai Aviation Limited has nominated Mr. Tharanga Sarathchandra Piyawansa to be the Accountable Manager incharge of the proposed operations of air services.

A copy of the complete Application of the aforementioned applicant is kept at the Technical Library, GroundFloor of Head Quarters of the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka, No. 04, Hunupitiya Road, Colombo 02, for theperusal of the members of the public, during office hours of any working days.

Members of the public are hereby informed that I have decided to commence after 07th November 2016 thecertification process of this application as per the established procedures to examine the degree of applicant’s compliancewith the applicable Air Navigation Regulations, International Standards and Recommended practices specified by ICAOand associated local requirements adopted by this office and ascertain their level of competence for the safe, secure,efficient operation of proposed air services, prior to granting them the requisite Air Operator Certificate, Airline Licence,
