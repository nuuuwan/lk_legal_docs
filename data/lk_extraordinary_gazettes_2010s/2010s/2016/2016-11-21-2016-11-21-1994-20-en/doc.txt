SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, RaviKarunanayake, Minister of Finance, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respectof list of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at the rates specifiedin the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencingfrom 21st November 2016, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act,No. 48 of 2007.

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.Ministry of Finance,Colombo 01,21st November, 2016.
