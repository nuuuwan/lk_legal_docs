ORDER UNDER SECTION 22(A) OF GREATER COLOMBO ECONOMIC COMMISSION LAW(BOARD OF INVESTMENT LAW) No. 04 OF 1978 AS AMENDED BY ACT, No. 43 OF 1980

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 22(a) of Greater Colombo Economic Commission Law (Board ofInvestment) No. 04 of 1978 as amended by Act, No. 43 of 1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka, upon the recommendation of the Minister-in-Charge of the subject of development strategies andinternational trade hereby declare areas described in the Schedule below to be a licensed zones for the purpose of thisAct.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo,November 08, 2016.

Schedule I

All that divided and defined allotment of land depicted as Lot No. 1 of Plan No. 476 dated 27th April 1999 madeby Mr. H. K. Mahinda, Licensed Surveyor and leveller for the land called E-dallawatta - Serapis farm (being a part of landNo. T. P. 118723) (being a resurvey and an amalgamation of Lot No. 1 and Lot No. 2 of pre tracing No. 99/01 dated 12thApril 1999 made by Mr. H. K. Mahinda, Licensed Surveyor and Leveller) situated in the village of Kulipitiya in theGrama Niladhari Division of Serapis watta No. 933 in Dambadeni Hathpattu west in Udapola Orthota Korale, East, in theDivisional Secretary’s Division of Polgahawela in Kurunegala District in North Western Province containing in extent offifty acres (50A., 0R., 0P.) or twenty point two three four three Hectares (20.2343 Hectares) in extent and all buildingsand everything else standing thereon.

The said Land is bounded as follows :

North:Kuda Oya and reservation for railway line ;East:Balance portion of Serapis farm ;South:Balance portion of Serapis farm and main road ;West:Main road, Road, Ihalagama, Jana Udagama and Kuda Oya.
