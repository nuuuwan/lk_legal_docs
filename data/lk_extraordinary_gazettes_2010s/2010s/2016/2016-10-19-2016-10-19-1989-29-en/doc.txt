PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Delegation of Powers by the Public Service Commission in terms of Article 57 (1) of the Constitution

01. The Public Service Commission in terms of the provisions in Article 57(1) of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka hereby delegates to the Public Officers stated in Column II of the Schedule“A” hereto, the powers set out in Column I thereof.

02. However, where there exists an uncertainty concerning the scope of any delegation of powers made by thePublic Service Commission or the discharge of any delegation of powers so made by the said Commission, the decisionof the Public Service Commission shall be final and conclusive.

03. Moreover, in the event that a Public Officer referred to in Column II of the Schedule hereto, is either thecomplainant or a witness or a potential witness at any disciplinary inquiry in respect of an officer belonging to a categoryset out in the corresponding item in Column I of the Schedule hereto, the powers delegated in respect of such officershall be deemed to have been rescinded and the Public Service Commission shall exercise the powers so delegated inrespect of such officer, as if no such delegation has been made in respect of such officer.

04. The provisions relating to the subject of Education concerning the delegation of powers set out in Part I ofthe Schedule published in Gazette Extraordinary No.1941/41 dated 20.11.2015 as amended by Gazette ExtraordinaryNo.1955/22 dated 25.02.2016 that delegated powers in terms of Article 57(1) of the Constitution of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka are hereby rescinded, without prejudice to any action taken or purported to be taken underthe Delegation of Powers published in the aforesaid Gazette.

Delegation of Powers by the Public Service Commission in terms of Article 56 (1) of the Constitution

01. The Public Service Commission in terms of the provisions in Article 56 (1) of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka hereby delegates to the Education Service Committee appointed by thePublic Service Commission as stated in the Schedule “B” hereto, the powers set out in Column II in respect of thecategory of officers stated in column I thereof.

02. However, where there exists an uncertainty concerning the scope of any delegation of powers made by thePublic Service Commission or the discharge of any delegation of powers so made by the said Commission, the decisionof the Public Service Commission shall be final and conclusive.

03. All matters pertaining to the powers delegated to the Education Service Committee that are pedningbefore the Public Service Commission or the Secretary to the Ministry of the Minister in charge of the subject ofEducation on the date prior to the effective date of this delegation of powers shall, from the effective date of thisdelegation of powers, be vested in the Education Service Committee.
