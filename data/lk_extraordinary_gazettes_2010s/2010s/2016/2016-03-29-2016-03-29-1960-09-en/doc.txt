THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Educations and Highways, do by this Order, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,amend the Schedule to the Order establishing the University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka, which is deemed, by virtue of theprovisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be an order made under Section 21 of the aforesaid Act, (as lastamended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1819/27 of July 18, 2013) by the addition immediately afterthe entry “Faculty of Engineering” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entries in Column II thereof, of thefollowing :-

“Column IColumn II

Faculty of Technology1.Department of Bio Systems Technology2.Department of Engineering Technology3.Department of Inter Disciplinary Studies”.

LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,28th March, 2016.
