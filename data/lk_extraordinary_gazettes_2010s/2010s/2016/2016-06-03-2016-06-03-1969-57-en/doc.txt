Government Notifications

LOCAL TREASURY BILLS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 417)

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Finance under Section 16 of the Local Treasury Bills Ordinance (Chapter 417).

RAVI KARUNANAYAKE,Minister of Finance.

Colombo,03rd June 2016.

Regulations

The Local Treasury Bills (Primary Dealers) Regulations No. 01 of 2009 published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1607/18 of June 24, 2009 is hereby amended in regulation 9 as follows :-

(1) in Paragraph (1) thereof, by the substitution for the words and figures “The Registrar of Public Debt appointedunder the Local Treasury Bills Ordinance (Chapter 417), on behalf of the Central Bank” of the words andfigures “The Director of the Department of Supervision of Non-Bank Financial Institutions, of the CentralBank, appointed in terms of Monetary Law Act, (Chapter 422)” ; and

(2) in paragraph (2) thereof, by the substitution for the words “the Registrar of Public Debt” of the words “theDirector of the Department of Supervision of Non-Bank Financial Institutions.”
