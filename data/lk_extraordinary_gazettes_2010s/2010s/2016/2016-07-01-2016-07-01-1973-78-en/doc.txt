PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

CIVIL AVIATION AUTHORITY OF SRI LANKA ACT, No. 34 OF 2002

Order under Section 9

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 9 of the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No. 34 of 2002, andin pursuance of the Cabinet decision taken on 14th June 2016 on the Cabinet Memorandum No. 16/1031/709/035-1 dated31st May 2016, on “Operation and Maintenance of Batticaloa Airport” submitted by me, I, Nimal Siripala De Silva, Ministerof Transport and Civil Aviation, do by this Order, transfer and vest in the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka the Batticaloaairport which it listed under Second Schedule Aerodromes to the aforementioned Act with immediate effect.

NIMAL SIRIPALA DE SILVA (M.P.),Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation.

Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,30th June, 2016.
