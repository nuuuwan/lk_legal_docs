CEYLON ELECTRICITY BOARD ACT, No. 17 OF 1969

Order under Section 57

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 57 of the Ceylon Electricity Board Act, No.17 of 1969, I, RanjithSiyambalapitiya, Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, do by this order, approve the proposed acquisition of the landrequired by the Ceylon Electricity Board and described in the Schedule hereto.

RANJITH SIYAMBALAPITIYA,Minister of Power and Renewable Energy.

28th March, 2016.

The land situated in the village of Nugepola in Grama Niladari’s Division of Gallella (No.164), in the DivisionalSecretary’s Division of Rathnapura, in the Uda Pattu of Nawadun Korale, in the Administrative District of Rathnapura,in the Province of Sabaragamuwa of Sri Lanka containing in extent of 1.4508 Hectares (03 Acres 02 Roods and 13.6 Perches)shown in sketched plan No. CEB/ REATDP/ TE/ 51 dated 10.02.2016 prepared by Ceylon Electricity Board and bounded asfollows :-
