NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 32/0575 Kandy District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 320333 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Haedeniya (Block 04) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Divi-sion of 472 - Haedeniya in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Harishpaththuwa in the District of Kandy in the Province of Cen-tral should submit his claim to the undersigned before 24th May, 2016 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 320333 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - Generals’ office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. K.A.K.
