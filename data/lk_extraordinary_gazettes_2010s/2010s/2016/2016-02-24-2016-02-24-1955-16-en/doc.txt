REGISTRATION OF PERSONS ACT, No. 32 OF 1968

REGISTRATION of Persons regulations made by the Minister of Internal Affairs, Wayamba Development and CulturalAffairs under Section 52 of the Registration of Persons Act, No. 32 of 1968 Published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 1946/ 31 dated 22.12.2015, have been approved by the Parliament on 10.02.2016.
