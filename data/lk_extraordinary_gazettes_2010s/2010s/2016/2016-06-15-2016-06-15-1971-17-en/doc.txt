(10) Sub- paragraph 11.2 is hereby repealed and the following new sub- paragraph 11.2 substituted therefore :-

11.2 Method of Selection :-

Selection for posts of Director, District Director of Agriculture, Additional Director, Registrar ofPesticides Control , Principle Agriculture Scientist and Principle Agriculturist will be made in accordancewith a procedure approved by the Public Service Commission by calling applications from the officerswho have three(03) years experience in Grade I of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Service and afterwardscalling candidates out of them, not more than thrice the existing vacancies according to the seniority ofGrade I for the interview.

In instances officers with three(03) years experience in Grade I have not applied for the posts, selectionwill be made in accordance with a procedure approved by the Public Service Commission by callingapplications from Officers in Grade I of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Service and afterwards calling candidatesout of them , not more than thrice of the existing vacancies according to the order of Seniority of GradeI for the interview.

Selection for posts of Deputy Director, Deputy Registrar of Pesticides Control will be made in accordancewith a procedure approved by the Public Service Commission by calling applications from officers inGrade I or Officers who have three(03) years experience in Grade II of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Serviceand afterwards, calling candidates, out of them, not more than thrice of the existing vacancies accordingto the order of Seniority for the interview.

In instances, officers in Grade I or officers who have three(03) years experience in Grade II have notapplied for the posts , selection will be made in accordance with a procedure approved by the PublicService Commission by calling applications from Officers in Grade II of the Sri Lanka Agricultural Serviceand afterwards, calling candidates out of them, not more than thrice of the existing vacancies accordingto the order of Seniority in Grade II for the interview.
