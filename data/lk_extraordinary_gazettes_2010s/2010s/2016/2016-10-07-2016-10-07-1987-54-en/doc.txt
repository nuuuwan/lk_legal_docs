NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 41/0104 Puttalam District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 410114 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village Nindeni of (Block 07) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of 618 A – Thiladiya the Divisional Secretariat Division of Putta-lam in the District of Puttalam in the Province of North Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 10th Novem-ber, 2016 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
