PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Rules on Procedure and Quorum for meetings of Committees appointed by the Public Service Commission

RULES made by the Public Service Commission under Article 56(3) of the Constitution, in respect of the procedure andquorum for meetings of the Committee appointed by the Public Service Commission under Article 56 (1) of the Constitution.

1. These Rules shall be known as the “Rules for the Procedure and Quorum of Committees made by the PublicService Commission under Article 56(3) of the Constitution.

2. The member designated by the Public Service Commission as Chairman, shall preside at all meetings.

3. In the absence of the Chairman from a meeting of the Committee, one of the other two members shall chairsuch meeting.

4. The quorum for a meeting shall be two members.

5. A decision taken at any meeting by two members shall constitute a valid decision of the Committee.

6. A Committee shall, in consultation with the Public Service Commission, meet as often as is necessary toensure the efficient discharge of the functions delegated to the Committee.

7. The Secretary to a Committee appointed by the Public Service Commission, acting under Article 56(4) of theConstitution, shall maintain Minutes of every meeting of the Committee which shall contain a true record ofthe proceedings of such meetings.

8. The Minutes so maintained shall be accepted as a true record of the proceedings of a meeting when confirmedas such, by the Committee.
