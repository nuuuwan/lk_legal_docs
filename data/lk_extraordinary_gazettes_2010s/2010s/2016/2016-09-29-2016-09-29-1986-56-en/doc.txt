CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION

Method for Determination of Customs Value under the Schedule ‘E‘ of the Customs (Amendment) Act, No. 2 of2003 for Disposal of Vehicles imported on 100% Duty/Tax Free Basis

WITH the provisions of the Schedule ‘E’ of the Customs (Amendment) Act, No. 2 of 2003, a method for determination ofCustoms value for disposal of motor vehicles imported and registered on or before November 19, 2015 on 100% duty/taxfree basis by the Diplomats and Diplomatic Organizations is hereby established.

Accordingly, the Customs value for disposal of motor vehicles imported and registered on or before November19, 2015 on 100% duty/tax free basis by the Diplomats and Diplomatic Oragnizations is a value, established on the basis ofthe period of usage of such vehicle, from the CIF Value of such vehicle at the time of importation as stipulated in theSchedule (1) below.

Period between the date of first registration and the date of disposal of the relevant vehicle shall be taken for thecomputation of the period of usage of such vehicle. The Fiscal levies payable at the time of disposal of such vehicle to becalculated at the rates of duties prevailed at the time of importation of such vehicle.
