UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella, theMinister of Higher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respectof the Horizon College of Business and Technology (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under theCompanies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 62877, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to theOrder made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1944/ 3 of December 07, 2015 by theaddition immediately after the entry ‘‘Bachelor of Management Honours in Marketing’’ appearing in Column I and it’scorresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the following, subject to the conditions specified below :-

CONDITIONS

Horizon College of Business and Technology (Private) Limited (hereinafter referred to as the ‘‘Institute”)Shall -

(1)in respect of the Bachelor of Education programme adhere to the minimum University entry qualificationof minimum 3 passes at the General Certificate of Education Examination (Advanced Level) stipulated bythe University Grants Commission or minimum of 3 passes in London (Advanced Level) Examination inone sitting.

(2)in respect of the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and Bachelor of Science Honours inInformation Technology shall comply with the following requirements :-

The Institute shall, submit the following to the specified Authority :-

(a)within three months from the publication of this Order clearly state the results of the foreignexaminations qualifications which are equivalent to the General Certificate of Education Examination(Advanced Level) conducted by the Department of Examination, Sri Lanka.

(b)prior to the commencement of the Degree programme strengthen the Academic staff with personswho have the relevant research postgraduate qualifications (PhD), (MPhil).
