THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of1980, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka upon the recommendation of theMinister-in-Charge of the subject of urban Development, being of the opinion that the land specified in the Schedulebelow is urgently required for the purpose of allocating the land for religious places which were relocated by MihidusenpuraHousing Project Stage II, do by this order, declare that the said land is required for such purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,05th April 2018.
