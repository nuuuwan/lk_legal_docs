FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AMBALANGODA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Ambalangoda Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front the recognized political party to which KukulegePadmasiri Dayananda Silva the Member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon returned member if People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMagage Champa Sumudu Kumari to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Kanda Uda Chandralal, returning officer of the Ambalangoda Urban Council acting in termsof Selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Magage ChampaSumudu Kumari was selected as the member of the Ambalangoda Urban Council.

K. U. CHANDRALAL,

Returning Officer,Ambalangoda Urban Council.

District Elections Office,Galle,14th December, 2018,
