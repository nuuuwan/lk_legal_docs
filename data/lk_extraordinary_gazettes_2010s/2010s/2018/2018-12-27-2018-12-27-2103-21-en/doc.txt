FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAWATHAGAMA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member of Mawathagama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichWeththige Upul Priyantha Perera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon returned member if United People’s Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a Member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Farook Muwad Farook to fill such vacancy ;

Now therefore I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, Returning Officer of the Mawathagama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that FarookMuwad Farook was selected as the Member of the Mawathagama Pradeshiya Sabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Mawathagama Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kurunegala,26th December, 2018,
