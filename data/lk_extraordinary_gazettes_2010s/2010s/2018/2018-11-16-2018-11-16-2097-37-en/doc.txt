EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification

BUSINESS HOURS FOR LIQUOR LICENSED ESTABLISHMENTS

IT is hereby notified that the following Order issued by the Minister in Charge of the subject of Finance, by virtue of thepowers vested in him by Section (1) of Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended, and published as theExcise Notificaton No. 03/2018 in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2054/41 of January 18, 2018, in order to amend theExcise Notification No. 686 published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 126/3 dated February 02, 1981, to declare thatbusiness hours of the liquor licensed establishments set out in the Schedule hereto shall come into force with effect from18 January 2018, was passed by Parliament on June 07, 2018.

S. R. ATTYGALLE,Secretary,Ministry of Finance and Economic Affaris.Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs,Colombo 01,November 15, 2018.
