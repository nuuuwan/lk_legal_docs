SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

2018

SECOND SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

Authorization

I, do hereby grant Permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council todisburse as per the second supplementary financial statement for the year 2018, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 27 (a) of the provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Twenty Nine Billion Six Hundred andSeventy Million Nine Hundred and Forty Thousand Only (Rs. 29,670,940,000.00) for the service of the period beginning on01st of January, 2018 and ending 31st December, 2018, which is specified in annual financial statement - 2018 is hereby amendedas, Rupees Thirty Billion Five Hundred and Forty Two Million Two Thousand only (Rs. 30,542,002,000.00) and grant permissionto disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2018 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date of summoningthe 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilwhich occurs first.

NILUKA EKANAYAKE,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province.

24th day of July, 2018.
