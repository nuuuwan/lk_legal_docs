AWARD

01. The Honourable Minister of Labour and LabourRelations by virtue of the powers vested in him by Section4(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, Chapter 131 of theLegislative Enactments of Ceylon (1956 revised edition)as amended by Acts Nos. 14 of 1957, 4 of 1962 and 39 of1968 (read with Industrial Disputes - Special Provisions)Act, No. 37 of 1968 appointed me by his order dated03.12.2010 and referred the dispute between the aforesaidparties to me for settlement by Arbitration.

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid paties is :-

02. “Whether any injustice was caused to Mr. A.Rajapaksha who was in service under the Mahaweli Authorityof Sri Lanka as the Assistant Director (PersonnelAdministration) due to non backdating the said post to11.09.2002 by the Authority and if an injustice was caused,to what relief Mr. A. Rajapaksha is entitled”

Applicant was reprsented by Mr. P. L. S. Perera ofSamastha Lanka Eksath Sevaka Sangamaya and Mr. TyugoAnthony Attorney at Law, while respondent Organization wasrepresented by Mrs. Prasanga Aillpperuma, Attorney at Law.

To expedite resolution of the matter in dispute, applicantwas requested to submit a sworn affidavit, in order to crossexamination the applicant subsequently, After days of crossexamination, Applicant on 18th July, 2013 submitted in opencourt, the following proposals -

(a) That he does will not wish to obtain the services ofthe Samastha Lanka Eksath Sevak Sangamaya and

(b) That in lieu of the matter in dispute the backdatingof the post of Asst. Director (PersonnelAdministration) w. e. f. 11.11.2002 substituteappointing him a Resident Manager w. e. f.11.09.2002 and pay up arrears of salaries applicablethereto.

Lawyer for the respondent vehemently objected to theproposed Amendments in that, the matter in issue had beenargued for 09 days and evidence of the witnesses are yet tobe heard, and if acceded to the request of the applicant. Itcould be disastrous for the respondent.

Since the last day of hearing i. e. 18th July 2013, therehad been no submission from the applicant for the last fouryears.

In the circumstances, I concede that the applicant hadlost interest in proceeding with the case. Besides, thearbitrator is not mandated to modify or amend the matter indispute stipulated in the reference as well as the legalpersonality who applied for relief, in this instance theSamastha Lanka Eksath Sevaka Sangamaya. No relief as suchis granted.

This is just and a fair award.

P. NAVARATNE,Arbitrator.

07th November, 2017.
