EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification

EXCISE DUTY ON IMPORT OF NON-POTABLE SPIRITS

IT is hereby notified that the following Order issued by Honorable Minister of Finance and Mass Media, by virtue of thepower vested in him by Sections 2, 12, 22 and 25, read with Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52) as amended fromtime to time, and published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2044/33 of 09.11.2017 to impose an Excise duty of RupeesFifteen (Rs. 15.00) per bulk Kilogram of Non-Potable Spirits imported into Sri Lanka with effect from 10 November 2017 as perrules set out hereto, was passed by Parliment on 11 December 2017.

R. H. S. SAMARATUNGA, Secretary,

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,29 December 2017.

RULES

1. General

i. I hereby declare all types of alcohol mentioned in the Scedule I as liquor for the purposes of the Provisions of the

Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52).

ii. These rules may be cited as " rules for non-potable spirits" and shall come into operation from the date of 10

November 2017. The Provisions of this notification shall not in any manner influence the Orders that exist at presentin relation to potable liquor, denatured spirits and molasses.
