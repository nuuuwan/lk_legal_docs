THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

NOTIFICATION

Wages Board for the Baking Trade

IT is hereby notified under Section 29(3) of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136), that the decisions of the Wages Boardfor the Baking Trade made Section 30 of that Ordinance and specified in the Schedule here to have been approved by theHon. Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

S. A. N. SARANATISSA,Secretary,Ministry of Labour and Trade Union Relations.

Colombo 05.10th July, 2018,
