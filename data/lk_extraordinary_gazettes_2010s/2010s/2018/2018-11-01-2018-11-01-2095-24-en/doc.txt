NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Hathurusingha Gedara Piyasena who resided in the village of Ankolipitiya, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Udapalatha, in the District of Kandy. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Hathurusingha Gedara Pi-yasena described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 30th November 2018 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property. In case of failure to do so, exparte action will be taken to register title under Section 55 (2) and (3) of the Registration of Title Act. No. 21 of 1998. K.A.K.RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 15th October, 2018
