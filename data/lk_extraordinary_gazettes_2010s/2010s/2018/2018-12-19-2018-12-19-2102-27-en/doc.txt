THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

HAVING been duly directed by the Minister of Lands andParliamentary Reforms under sub-section (1) of section 2 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the LandAcquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, I hereby givepublic notice as required by sub-section (1) of the section 7that land in the area described below is required for a publicpurpose.

Description of land/servitude to be acquired :

The allotment of land called Walagamawaththa havingExtent of 0.0126 Hectare depicted as Lot No. 3516 as per thesurvey plan of Padiyathalawa Topo.PP 49, SupplementNo. 54 surveyed by the surveyor General, situated in thevillage of Meegaswatta with in the minor Division ofBinthenna Paththu South under the Padiyathalawa DivisionalSecretariat in Ampara District in the Eastern Province.

Boundaries of the Land

North: Lot No. 3517; East: Lot No. 3517; South: Lot No.3517 and 1622; West: Lot No. 1622.

The owner who claim ownership or have any other relationto the above described land/servitude or any person actingunder their written directions should meet me at DivisionalSecretariat Padiyathalawa on 09.01.2019 at 10.00 a.m. Further,the nature of ownership to the above land/servitude detailsof the reason to request compensation for land acquisition,the amount of compensation and the mode of calculation ofthe compensation should be submitted in written documentwith two copies on or before 09.01.2019.

R. M. N. WIJAYATHUNGA,

Divisional Secretary,

Padiyathalawa.
