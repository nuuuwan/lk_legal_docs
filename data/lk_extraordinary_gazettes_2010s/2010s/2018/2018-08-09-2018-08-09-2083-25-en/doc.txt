REGISTRATION OF PERSONS ACT, No. 32 OF 1968

Notification under Section 9(2)(bb)(i)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 9(2)(bb)(i) of Registration of Persons Act, No. 32 of 1968,I, S. B. Navinna, Minister of Internal Affairs and Wayamba Development hereby inform that a fee of One Hundred Rupeesshould be paid in respect of an application forwarded under aforesaid Section 9, for registration and obtain a nationalidentity card for the first time with effect from 01.09.2018.

I hereby cancel the notification published in this regard in the Gazette No. 1922/25 of 08th July 2015.

S. B. NAWINNE,Minister of Internal Affairs and Wayamba Development.Battaramulla,07th August 2018.
