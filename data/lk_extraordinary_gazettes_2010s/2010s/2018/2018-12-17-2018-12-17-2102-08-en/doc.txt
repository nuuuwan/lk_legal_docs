NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 52/0996 Colombo District It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 520811 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Karagampitiya (Block 04) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Nilad-hari Division of No. 539/42A - Malwatta in the Divisional Secre-tariat Division of Dehiwala in the District of Colombo in the Province of Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 21st January, 2019 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accord-ingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
