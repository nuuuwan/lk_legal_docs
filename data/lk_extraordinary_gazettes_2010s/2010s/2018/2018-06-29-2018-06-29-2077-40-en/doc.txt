STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether Mr. K.W.N. Perera, working at the Ceylon Fishery Harbours Corporation had been caused injustice by not been given thepromotion entitled as at 01.01`.2014, non - approval of Accident leave for the accident which had occured on 25.06.2015 while coming towork, cutting off 22 leaves from his leave entitlement, considering the 57 balance leave as no - pay leave and recovering it from his salary, aswell as Suspending his Salary increments and if such injustice had been caused, to what reliefs he is entitled.
