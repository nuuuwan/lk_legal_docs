CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003

Order under Section 20(5)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 20(5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, theConsumer Affairs Authority do by this Order remove the item Full Cream Milk Powder (Local & Imported) from OrderNo. 39 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1975/68 on 14th July 2016.
