THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Lakshman Kiriella,Minister of Higher Education and Highways, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1603/35 of May 28,2009 (as last amended, by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2045/17 of November 15, 2017), whichspecifies the Faculties and the “Department of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka, which is deemed, byvirtue of the provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the saidAct, by the addition immediately after the entry ‘‘ 13. Department of Philosophy and Psychology” appearing inColumn II, of the Schedule which corresponds to the entry ‘‘Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences’’ appearing inColumn I of the Schedule of the following entry :-

“14. Department of English Language Teaching”.LAKSHMAN KIRIELLA,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.Colombo,04th January 2018.
