CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha

ENTERTAINMENT TAXATION

ACCORDING to the powers conferred in (Chapter 267) second part (I) Sub - section of Entertainment Tax Ordinance, under-mentioned resolution determined by the Secretary of Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha which was approved by the Minister incharge for Local Governments, Central Provincial Council as per the powers specified in Section II of the Ordinance onEntertainment Taxation is here by published based on its second Sub section.

R. M. P. S. RATHNAYAKE,Secretary,Chief, Education and Local Government Ministry,Central Province.

At Pallekele.14th February, 2018.

RESOLUTION

On the powers conferred to me in section 9(3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, when it is held to conduct the entertainmentprograms in the Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha Authority, it has been determined to impose as entertainment tax of 10% from theticket fee as per (2) 1 of No. 12 of 1946 Tax Ordinance and Section 37 of the 1984 Tax Amendment Act.

CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha

ENTERTAINMENT TAXATION

ACCORDING to the powers conferred in (Chapter 267) second part (I) Sub - section of Entertainment Tax Ordinance, under-mentioned resolution determined by the Secretary of Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha which was approved by the Minister incharge for Local Governments, Central Provincial Council as per the powers specified in Section II of the Ordinance onEntertainment Taxation is here by published based on its second Sub section.

R. M. P. S. RATHNAYAKE,Secretary,Chief, Education and Local Government Ministry,Central Province.

At Pallekele.14th February, 2018.

RESOLUTION

On the powers conferred to me in section 9(3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, when it is held to conduct the entertainmentprograms in the Kundasale Pradeshiya Sabha Authority, it has been determined to impose an entertainment tax of 7.5% of theticket fee for cinematic view and 20% of the entrance fee for all other entertainment programs as per the Section (2) 1 of No. 12of 1946 Tax Ordinance and Section 37 of the 1984 Tax Amendment Act.
