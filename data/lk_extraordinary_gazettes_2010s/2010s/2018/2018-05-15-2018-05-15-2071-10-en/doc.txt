THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS ACT, No. 44 OF 1980

IN pursuance of Section 12(1) of the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980, it is hereby notified that the revisionof registers of electors for the year 2018 commences on May 15, 2018.

02. Copies of registers of electors now in force or of portions thereof will be exhibited by Registering Officersin their offices and following offices.

03. Every person qualified to be an elector in any Electoral District should inspect the reigster of electors toascertain whether his/her name is included in the register.

04. From 15.05.2018 the enumerators will distribute enumeration forms to chief occupants of each householdto register the names of the persons who are qualified to be electors for the year 2018. Collection of enumeration formsdully filled by the chief occupants will commence from 01st of June 2018. The Election Commission requests everychief occupant to give the assistance to enumerator by furnishing without delay, the essential and accurate information ofall the persons who are qualified to be registered as electors in his/her household.
