THE NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL ACT, No. 47 OF 1980

Order under Section 24C and Section 24D

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 24C and Section 24D of the National Environmental Act, No. 47 of 1980,read with Section 51 of the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri LankaI, Maithripala Sirisena, President, do by this Order, declare-

(a)that the area of land, the limits of which are described in Schedule hereto shall be an EnvironmentalProtection Area for the purposes of the aforesaid Act, and shall be called the “Pansalathenne-MaussawaEnvironmental Protection Area” ; and

(b)that for so long as this Order is in force :-

(i)any planning scheme or project within the aforesaid Pansalathenne-Maussawa EnvironmentalProection Area which is in conflict with the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall cease to operatefrom the date of the making hereof ;

(ii)no person other than the Authority shall exercise any power or discharge any function within thelimits of the aforesaid Pansalathenne-Maussawa Environmental Protection Area ; and

(iii)the use of any land area within the aforesaid Pansalathenne-Maussawa Environmental ProtectionArea for any activity, shall be totally prohibited.

(c)a Monitoring Committee consisting of the members set out below shall monitor the Pansalathenne-Maussawa Environmental Protection Area and make recommendations to the Authority on conservationand sustainable management thereof :-
