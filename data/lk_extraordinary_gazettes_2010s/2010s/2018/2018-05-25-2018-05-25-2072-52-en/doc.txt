FINANCE ACT, No. 11 OF 1963

Order under Section 72

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 72 read with Section 71 of the Finance Act, No. 11 of 1963, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order vest in the People's Bank established under thePeople's Bank Act, No. 29 of 1961, with effect from, 25th May Two Thousand and Eighteen, the properties described inthe Schedule hereto.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Colombo,May 25, 2018.

SCHEDULE

All that divided and defined allotment of land marked as Lot 4-1B1 called as “Kanaththewatta and Delgahawatta”depicted in the Plan No. 887 and dated 13.10.2007 made by Licensed Surveyor B. K. S. Bamunusingha situated in GramasewaDivision of Kalalgoda No. 493, Divisional Secretary's Division of Maharagama in Palle Pattu in Hewagam Korale in theDistrict of Colombo in Western Province and bounded on the North by Kanaththewatta, on the East by road, on the Southby Lot No. 4-1B2, on the West by Lot No. 4-1A depicted in the Plan No. 255/303 containing in extent of ten perches(00 A., 00 R., 10 P.) together with soil, trees, everything standing thereon and the right to use the roadways.

This land is registered in folio G-1657/245, 915/22 in the Land Registry, Homagama.
