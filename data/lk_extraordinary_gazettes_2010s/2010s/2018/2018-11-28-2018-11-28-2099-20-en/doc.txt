TRUSTS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 87)

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs and Justice under Section 115A read withSection 6A and section 19A of the Trusts Ordinance (Chapter 87).

SUSIL PREMAJAYANTHA,Minister of Public Administration,Home Affairs and Justice.

Colombo,November 26, 2018.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trusts (Information Relating to Express Trusts) Regulations 2018.

2.(1) Upon creation of an express trust under Section 6 of the Trusts Ordinance in relation to any immovable ormovable property, the trustee of such trust shall, for the purpose of subsection (1) of Section 19A of thatOrdinance, keep records of information specified in Schedule I.

(2) The information kept under Sub-regulation (1) shall be verified and updated every three months to thegreatest extent possible.

3. The Registrar General shall, for the purpose of subsection (1) of Section 6A of the Trusts Ordinance, preparedand maintain a register containing the information specified in Schedule II in respect of every express trust.

4.(1) The trustee of any immovable property or movable property in respect of an express trust shall, for thepurpose of subsection (2) of Section 6A, forward the information specified in Scheduel III

6. For the purpose of these regulations -

“valid document of identity” means -

(a) in the case of a person who is citizen of Sri Lanka

(i) a National Identity Card issued by the Commissioner General for the Registration of Persons ;

(ii) a valid passport ;

(iii) a valid driving licence ; or

(iv) in the absence of any of the documents referred to in (i), (ii) or (iii) above, a photograph certified bya Notary Public or a Justice of the Peace ; and

(b) in the case of a person who is not a citizen of Sri Lanka, a valid passport and a valid Visa.
