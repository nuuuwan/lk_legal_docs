FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AKKARAIPATTU MUNICIPALCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of returned member in Akkaraipattu Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the National Congress, the recognized political party to which MuhammadismailMuhammadu Rafeek the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decideupon the member of that ward 07 Nooraniya, Akkaraipattu Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a periodof thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Muhammadibra Lebbe Muhammadu Shabuddeen to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thilina Wickramarathne, Returning Officer of the Akkaraipattu Municipal Council acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare MuhammadibraLebbe Muhammadu Shabuddeen was elected as the member of the ward 07 Nooraniya, Akkaraipattu Municipal Council.

THILINA WICKRAMARATHNE,Returning Officer,Akkaraipattu Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Ampara,09th April 2018.
