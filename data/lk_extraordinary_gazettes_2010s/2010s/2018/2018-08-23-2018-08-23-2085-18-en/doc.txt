FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE POLONNARUWA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Polonnaruwa Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending consecutivemeetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichMeregngna Lionel Silva the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateMeregngna Lionel Silva to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Leelanath Wikrama Arachchi, returning officer of the Polonnaruwa Municipal Council , actingin terms of selection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that MeregngnaLionel Silva was selected as the member of the Polonnaruwa Municipal Council.

LEELANATH WIKRAMA ARACHCHI,Returning Officer,Polonnaruwa Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Polonnaruwa,23rd August, 2018,
