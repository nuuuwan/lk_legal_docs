EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, as amendedI, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance & Mass Media, do by this Order declare that the rate of Excise dutyapplicable on every article specified in the Columns I, II and III of the Schedule hereto, shall replace correspoding everyarticle specified under the Schedule I of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No.2044/32 dated November 09, 2017,issued under the Section 3 of this Act, Shall be as specified in the corresponding entry in Column IV of the Schedulewith effect from August 01, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01.31st July 2018.
