NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)a OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Eheliyagoda Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Suranga Ambagahatenne, Returning Officer of the Eheliyagoda Pradeshiya Sabha do hereby declare under Section10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Kelaniyage Rathnapala of the EheliyagodaPradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has ceased to be a member of theSocialist Party of Sri Lanka.

SURANGA AMBAGAHATENNE,Returning Officer,Eheliyagoda Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Ratnapura,17th September, 2018.
