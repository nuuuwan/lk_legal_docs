PRADESHIYA SABHAS ACT, No. 15 OF 1987

Order

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 18 of the Interpretation Ordinance (Chapter 2), I, Faiszer Musthapha,Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government do by this Order, repeal the paragraph 2 of the Order related toElpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2043/56 of 02nd November 2017 and all theamendments subsequently made thereto, by virtue of the powers vested in me under sub section (1) of Section 2, Section4 and Section 6 of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987 to be read with the Section 18 of interpretation Ordinance.

FAISZER MUSTHAPHA,Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government.
