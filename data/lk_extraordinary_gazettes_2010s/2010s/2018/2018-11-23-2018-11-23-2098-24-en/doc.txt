FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MUTHUR PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Muthur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending three consecutivemeetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichKirubairaja Sathyarooban the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Navareththinarasa Hariharakumar to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, Sangarappillei Suthakaran, Returning Officer of the Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatNavareththinarasa Hariharakumar was selected as the member of the Muthur Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. SUTHAKARAN,Returning Officer,Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Trincomalee,23rd November 2018.
