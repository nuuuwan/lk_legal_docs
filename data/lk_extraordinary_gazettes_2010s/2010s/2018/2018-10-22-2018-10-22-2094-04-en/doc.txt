EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAWANELLA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Mawanella Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which MuhammaduSali Muhammadu Sareepu the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that 23- Madulbowa of Mawanella Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Muhammadu Lareef Muhammadu Mansoor to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Mawanella Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatMuhammadu Lareef Muhammadu Mansoor was elected as the member of the 23- Madulbowa of Mawanella PradeshiyaSabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Mawanella Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kegalle,19th October 2018.
