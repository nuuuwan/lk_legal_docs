EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE TOWN & GRAVETSPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Termination ofParty membership;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Eelam People’s Democratic Party, the recognized political party to whichKirubairajah Naguleshwaran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member if Eelam People’s Democratic Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared returned as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Shanmugam Balaganeshan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Jesuthasan Jeniton, Returning Officer of the Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ShanmugamBalaganeshan was selected as the member of the Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha.

J. JENITON,Returning Officer,Trincomalee Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Trincomalee,07th November 2018.
