SERVICE MINUTE OF THE PUBLIC MANAGEMENT ASSISTANTS’ SERVICE - REVISION No. 02

IT has been ordered by the Public Service Commission to revise the Minute of Public Management Assistants’ Servicerevised by the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 2032/26 dated 16.08.2017and published in the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1840/34 dated 11thDecember 2013, as follows :

01. The Section 7.3.2.1 is revised as follows :

7.3.2.1 Educational Qualifications

Shall have passed the G. C. E. (O/L) Examination in six (06) subjects including Sinhala/Tamil/English andMathematics/Arithmetic/Pure Mathematics/Elementary Mathematics/Commercial Artithmetic with at least twocredit passes at not more than two sittings.

02. The term ”Efficiency Bar Examination” shall be included in substitution for the term “Examination” in Sections02, 04 and 05 under Annex 06.

03. Following shall be included as No. 06 at the end of Annex 06.

06. Officers shall be exempted from the requirement of passing the Efficiency Bar Examination prescribed forthe officers in Grade I on successful completion of the 10 day course including the basic principles of which aSenior Officer in public service should be aware, within 05 years from the date of promotion to Grade I.

i. Conducting authority of the course : Director General of Combined Services

ii. Duration of the course : The notification for calling applications relevant to the training course prescribedfor Grade I shall be published in the first quarter of every year and the number of training course conductedshall be determined in considering the number of applications received.
