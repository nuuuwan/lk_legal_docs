FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02 to which Bombuwala Dewage Josaphin the memberelected to Ward No. 13 Rukmale of Maharagama Urban Council belonged was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 13, Rukmale of Maharagama Urban Council who belongs tosuch independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified under Section 8 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateHeenpellage Dona Piyumi Prasadini to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, returning officer of Maharagama UrbanCouncil acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do herebydeclare that Heenpellage Dona Piyumi Prasadini was elected as the member of the Ward No. 13 Rukmale of Maharagama UrbanCouncil.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Elections Office,Colombo,21st November, 2018,

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02 to which Hansani Ruwanthi the member elected toWard No. 15 Kottawa South of Maharagama Urban Council belonged was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 15, Kottawa South of Maharagama Urban Council whobelongs to such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified under Section 8 of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;
