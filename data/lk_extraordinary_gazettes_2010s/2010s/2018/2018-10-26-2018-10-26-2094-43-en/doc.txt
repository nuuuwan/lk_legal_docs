NOTIFICATION

P.S. No. : PS/CSA/00/1/3/1/1

IT is hereby notified that His Excellency the President in the exercise of powers conferred upon him under the Constitutionof the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, has removed Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, who was appointed byHis Excellency the President under Article 42(4) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lankafrom the office of the Prime Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, with immediate effect.

By order of His Excellency,

UDAYA R. SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,26th October, 2018.

PROCLAMATION BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA

IN terms of the powers vested in me by the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, MaithripalaSirisena, President do hereby dissolve the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic with immediate effect.

Given at Colombo on this Twenty Sixth day of October, Two Thousand and Eighteen.

By His Excellency's Command,

UDAYA R SENEVIRATNE,Secretary to the President.
