FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(1 a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

And Whereas the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Bombuwala Dewage Sujatha Dharmapriya theMember elected to Ward No. 01, Mirihana South of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 01 Mirihana South of Maharagama UrbanCouncil who belongs to such Independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified under Section 8 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

And Whereas the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateHewa Gajaman Paththinilage Chithrani Sugathapala to fill such vacancy ;

Now Therefore I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, returning officer of Maharagama Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatHewa Gajaman Paththinilage Chithrani Sugathapala was elected as the Member of the Ward No. 01 Mirihana South ofMaharagama Urban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,04th June, 2018.
