Proclamations & C., by the President

PROCLAMATION BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE PRESIDENT

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA

KNOW YE that by virtue of the powers vested in me by Article 70(4) of the Chapter XI of the Constitution of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Maithripala Sirisena, President, do by this Proclamation summon Parliament to meet atthe Parliament Hall, Sri Jayawardenepura, on the Eighth day of May, Two Thousand and Eighteen at 02.15 p.m.

Given at Colombo on this Twenty Third day of April, Two Thousand and Eighteen.

By Order of His Excellency,

AUSTIN FERNANDO,Secretary to the President.
