(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Perumbuli Achchige Anura who resided in the village of Homagama, in the Divisional Sec-retary’s Division of Homagama, in the District of Colombo. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Perumbuli Achchige Anura described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 30th July, 2018 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
