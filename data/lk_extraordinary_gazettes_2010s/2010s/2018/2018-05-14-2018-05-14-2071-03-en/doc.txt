THE UNITED NATIONS ACT, No. 45 OF 1968

Order under regulation 19(1) of the United Nations (Sanctions in relation to DemocraticPeople's Republic of Korea) Regulations of 2017

I, Kapila Mudantha Waidyaratne, Competent Authority appointed for the purpose of the United Nations (Sanctions inrelations to Democratic People's Republic of Korea) Regulations of 2017, published in the Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 2039/32 of October 6, 2017 acting in terms of the powers vested in me by regulation 19(1) of the said Regulations,do by this Order, freeze all funds, other financial assets and economic resources in Sri Lanka which are owned or controlleddirectly or indirectly by any person and entity designated and listed under regulation 19(3) of the above regulations andpublished in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2067/14 of April 19, 2018 and any other person entity who acts on behalf ofor under the direction of such designated persons and entities, to or for the benefit of such designated persons andentities.

KAPILA WAIDYARATNE,President's Counsel,Competent Authority,Secretary,Ministry of Defence.

Colombo,11th May 2018.
