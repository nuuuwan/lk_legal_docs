THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (Chapter 460)

Notice under Section 07

My Reference No.: BKP/LAND/ACQUI/A3/22.Land Ministry Ref. No.: 4-3/15/2016/VE/317'

I, hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of the Section 07of the land Acquisition Act as amended by the landAcquisition (Amendment) Act, No.28 of 1964, thatGovernment intends to acquire the following land which isrequired for resettlement of persons displaced by landslidesin 2016 in Bulathkohupitiya Divisional Secretary's Division.

Description of the land to be Acquired:

Three allotments (1, 2, 3) of land depicted in the schedulein the preliminary plan No. P.P.K./ 3582 prepared by theSupirintendent of Survey, Kegalle situated in the village ofRangalla in the Grama Niladhari Division of Rangalla in theDivisional Secretary's Division of Bulathkohupitiya in theDistrict of Kegalle in Sabaragamuwa Province :

01. Lot No. 01, 02, 03Name of the land: Yatideriya Estate - Rangalla, LappanaDivision; Names and Addresses of Claimant: 01. LandReforms Commission, No. 475, Kaduwela Road, Battaramulla.02. Managing Director, Kegalle Plantation Company, No. 310,High Level Road, Maharagama. 03. Ministry of Plantation

Industries, 2nd Floor, Sethsiripaya Phase 11, Sethsiripaya;Extent: 4.0516 Hectares.

Date of examination for claims for compensation on30/10/2018 at 10.00 a.m. for allotment No. 3 P.P.K. 3582.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at Bulathkohupitiya Divisional Secretariaton the aforesaid date and time.

Further, you are here by requested to notify to (me in writing)in duplicate on or before 23.10.2018 the nature of their interestsin the land particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation andthe details of the computation of such amount.
