FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WANATHAVILLUWAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Wanathavilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the Independent Group 01, the Independent Group to which Senadheera DumunnageDouglas Indika Senadeera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Independent Group 01 who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group, has communicated his decision to nominateKatuwala Kande Gamladdalage Pradeep Aruna Kantha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE N. Regan, Returning Officer of the Wanathavilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms ofSection 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Katuwala KandeGamladdalage Pradeep Aruna Kantha was selected as the member of the Wanathavilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Wanathavilluwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Puttalam,12th June, 2018.
