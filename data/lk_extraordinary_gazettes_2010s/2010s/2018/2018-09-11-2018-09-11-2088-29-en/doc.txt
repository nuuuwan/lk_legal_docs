LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: 17/592/ATH.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intendsto acquire the following land which is required for publicpurpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

Allotments of land, total extent to about 0.0225 Hectaredepicted in Plan No. CO 9704 prepared by Superintendentof survey, Colombo District, situated in the village ofMirihana in 523, Mirihana North Grama Niladhari Divisionin Divisional Secretaries, Division of Maharagama inColombo District Western Province and bounded asfollows.

1. Lot No. 1Name of the Land: Unidentified; Description: Open bareland; Claimant of the Land: Unidentified; Extent: 0.0184Hectare.

2. Lot No. 2Name of the Land: Unidentified; Description: Road;Claimant of the Land: Unidentified; Extent: 0.0036 Hectare.

3. Lot No. 3Name of the Land: Kotte Raja Maha Vihara premises;Description: Home garden; Claimant of the Land: Kotte RajaMaha Viharaya; Extent: 0.0005 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at the Divisional Secretariat ofMaharagama on the 15 of September 2018 at 09.00 a.m.

And are hereby requested to notify me in writing (induplicate) on or before the 01 of September 2018 the natureof their in interests in the land, the particulars of their claimsfor compensation for the acquisition of the said land, theamount of compensation and the details of the computationof such amount. In order to proof the ownership relevantdocuments and deeds are submitted on the date of inquiry.

M.M.K. DILRUKSHI WALPOLA,

Divisional Secretary,

Maharagama.
