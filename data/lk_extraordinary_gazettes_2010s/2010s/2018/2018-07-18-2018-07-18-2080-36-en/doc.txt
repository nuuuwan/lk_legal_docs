SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from 19th July, 2018, subject to provisions of SubSection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,18th July, 2018.

Order under Section 2

CORRECTION NOTICE

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose, in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from June 14, 2018, subject to provisions of SubSection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2063/13 of 19.03.2018 in respect of Black gram classifiable under the HSCode 0713.31.29 is hereby rescinded with effect from June 14, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,13th June, 2018.
