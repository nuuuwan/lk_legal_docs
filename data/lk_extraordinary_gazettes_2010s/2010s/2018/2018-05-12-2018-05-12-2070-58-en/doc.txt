FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Hewathondilage Jayaseeli the Member electedto Ward No. 01, Mirihana South of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) ofthe aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 01 Mirihana South of Maharagama Urban Councilwho belongs to such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance(Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateBombuwala Dewage Sujatha Dharmapriya to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, returning officer of Maharagama Urban Councilacting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatBombuwala Dewage Sujatha Dharmapriya was elected as the member of the Ward No. 01 Mirihana South of MaharagamaUrban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,12th May, 2018,
