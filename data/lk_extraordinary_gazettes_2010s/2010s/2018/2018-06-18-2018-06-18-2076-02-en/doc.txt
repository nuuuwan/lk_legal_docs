Government Notifications

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, P. S. M. Charles, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 18th June, 2018, all duties of Customs as wellas other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid and received atthe Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2075/1 of11th June, 2018 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similar notificationto be published on or before 24th June, 2018.

P. S. M. CHARLES,Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,14th June, 2018.
