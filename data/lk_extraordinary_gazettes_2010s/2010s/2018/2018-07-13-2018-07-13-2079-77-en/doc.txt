EXTRADITION LAW, No. 8 OF 1977

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 (1) of the Extradition Law, No. 8 of 1977, I, Maithreepala Sirisena,President do by this Order declare that, since a Treaty was signed at Ankara on the 02nd day of December 2008 betweenthe Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Turkey, a foreign State, in order to establish a bilaterallegal mechanism governing the transfer of persons subject to prosecution and sentenced persons and to improve theefficiency of justice which would develop Co-operation between the State Parties to the Treaty in the prevention ofoffences and inflict of punishment in respect of such offences and that the parties to the Treaty undertake to extradite toeach other, upon request and subject to the provisions of the Treaty, the persons who are found within the territories of therespective State Parties for prosecution or execution of sentences ; and that the provisions of such Treaty is in conformitywith the provisions of Section 4 of the Extradition Law, No. 8 of 1977; the provisions of the said Law No. 8 of 1977 shallapply in respect of such foreign State, subject to such modification, limitation or condition as specified in the Treaty.

MAITHREEPALA SIRISENA,President.

Colombo,20th June, 2018.
