FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHARAGAMA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Bombuwala Dewage Sarath Dharmapriya theMember elected to Ward No. 02 Mirihana North of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under section66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 02 Mirihana North of Maharagama UrbanCouncil who belongs to such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateChamidu Lakshan Ranasinghe to fill such vacancy;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama UrbanCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Chamidu Lakshan Ranasinghe was elected as the Member of the Ward No. 02 Mirihana North of Maharagama UrbanCouncil.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo.13th June, 2018.
