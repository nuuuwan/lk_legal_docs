STAMP DUTY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 12 OF 2006

Order under Section 3

By virtue of the powers vested in me under section 3 of the Stamp Duty (Special Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 2006, I,Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order amend the Order made under that Section andpublished in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1465/19 dated October 05, 2006 as last amended by the Order published in theGazette Extraordinary No. 1947/45 dated January 1, 2016, with effect from October 1, 2018, as specified in the SCHEDULEhereto.

Mangala Samaraweera,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,01st of October, 2018.
