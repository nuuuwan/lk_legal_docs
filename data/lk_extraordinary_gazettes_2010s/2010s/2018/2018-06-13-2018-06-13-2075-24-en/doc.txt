EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 11/2018

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended, I, Mangala Samaraweera,Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order amend the Excise Notification No. 03/2018, published in the ExtraordinaryGazette No. 2054/41 dated January 18, 2018 in order to revise the opening and closing hours of Toddy Taverns, specified in theItem No. 02 of Schedule of that Notification by substituting the opening and closing hours specified in the Schedule belowwith effect from June 13, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,June 13, 2018.

SCHEDULE

Column IColumn II

License TypeOpening and Closing Hours

(i) Toddy TavernsFrom 11.00 a.m. to 2.00 p.m. and From 5.00 p.m. to 8.00 p.m.

(ii) Toddy Taverns (Operated by the Co-operative System)From 8.00 a.m. to 9.00 p.m.

02. Definition : “Under the Co-operative System” means the Cooperative Societies and Clusters registered by the ProvincialDepartment of Cooperative Development.
