EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989 as amended,I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order declare that, the Order made underSection 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act No. 13 of 1989 and published by the Extraordinary Gazette NotificationNo. 2073/41 dated May 31, 2018 is hereby rescinded effective from June 14, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.
