MINUTE OF SRI LANKA ARCHITECTS ’ SERVICE

FOLLOWING amendments are made to the Minute of Sri Lanka Architects' Service published in the Gazette Extraordinaryof the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1877/28 dated 28.08.2014 as amended by the Gazette Extraordinaryof the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1882/18 dated 01.10.2014, No. 1952/20 dated 02.02.2016 andNo. 1994/68 dated 24.11.2016, and the other matters and conditions thereof remain unchanged.

(1) 7.3.2.1. Educational/ Professional Qualifications and Experience

I. Officers who have completed the qualifications in 7.2.2.1. who have completed an active and satisfactory

period of service of 05 years in the Public or Provincial Public Service and who draw Salaries under theSalary code of MN 2 or above referred to in the Public Administration Circular 06/2006

II.Officers who have passed the Standard Course - Part I at Sri Lanka Institute of Architects and who havecompleted 10 years of active and satisfactory service in the Public Service/ Provincial Public ServiceAnd

III. Draughtsman in Special Class of the Sri Lanka Technological Service with an immediately preceding 05

years of satisfactory period of Service

(2) 7.3.2.3. Other

II. Should have completed a satisfactory period of Service during the immediately preceding 05 years

IV. Should have confirmed in the Service.

(3) 10.2.1 Qualifications to be fulfilled

I. Should have completed a five (05) years of active and satisfactory period of Service in Grade II of the

category of Service and should have earned five (05) salary increments that fall due.
