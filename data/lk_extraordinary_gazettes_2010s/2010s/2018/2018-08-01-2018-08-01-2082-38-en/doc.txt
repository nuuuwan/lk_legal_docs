SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Authorization

I, do hereby grant Permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council todisburse as per the first supplementary financial statement for the year 2018, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section27 (a) of the provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Twenty Nine Billion Six Hundred andThirty Six Million One Hundred and Forty Thousand (Rs. 29,636,140,000.00) for the service of the period beginning on 01st ofJanuary, 2018 and ending 31st December, 2018, which is specified in annual financial statement - 2018 is hereby amended as,Rupees Twenty Nine Billion Six Hundred and Seventy Million, Nine Hundred and Forty Thousand only (Rs.29,670,940,000.00)and grant permission to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2018 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date of summoningthe 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilwhich occurs first

Niluka Ekanayake,Honourable Governor,Sabaragamuwa Province,21st day of June, 2018.
