EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ADDALACHCHENEIPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a member of Addalachchenei Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the All Ceylon Makkal Congress, the recognized political party to whichMuhammathu Ameer Ansil the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon returned member of that No. 05 - Minhaj, of Addalachchenei Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such partyand is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared electedas a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Hasanbay Mohmethu Siraj to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Don Thilina Ishan Wickramarathna, Returning Officer of the Addalachchenei PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Hasanbay Mohmethu Siraj was elected as the member of the No. 05 - Minhaj, Addalachchenai Pradeshiya Sabha.

THILANA WICKRAMARATHNA,Returning Officer,Addalachchenai Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Ampara,03rd September 2018.
