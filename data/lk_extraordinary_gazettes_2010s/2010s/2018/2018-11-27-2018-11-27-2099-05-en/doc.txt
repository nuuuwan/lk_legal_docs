Order of Delegating Power

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Co-operative Societies Statute No. 05 of 2009 of North Central ProvincialCouncil Marappullige Jayasinghe, Governor of Governor's Office of North Central Province do hereby delegates allpowers mentioned in the column III of the Schedule to the persons mentioned in the column I who has been appointed toperform the duties and functions of the post of Assistant Commissioner of Co-operative Development (Departmental) toassist the North Central Provincial Commissioner of Co-operative Development and Registrar, from the date mentionedin column II in terms of the powers under Co-operative Societies statute No. 05 of 2009 that shall be read withCo-operative Societies Amendment Statute No. 01 of 2011 of North Central Provincial Council.

MARAPPULLIGE JAYASINGHE,

Governor,North Central Province.Governor's Office,North Central Province,17th November, 2018.
