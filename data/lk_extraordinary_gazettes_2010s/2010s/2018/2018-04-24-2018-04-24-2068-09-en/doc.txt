EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989 as amended,I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order declare that the rate of Excise dutycharged, levied and collected on imporation of motor vehicle bearing Chassis No. MR0KB8CC401054677 and EngineNo. 2GD-0416778, consigned to and cleared by General (Rtd) Hamilton Wanasinghe, holder of the National IdentityCard No. 332310097 V shall be 35% of the payable Excise duty as specified in the Extraordinary Gazette NotificationNo. 2044/32 dated November 09, 2017. The imported motor vehicle should registered in the name of the consignee andshould not be transferred for the period of 05 years from the date of registation. The above concessionary rate of Exciseduty is granted as per the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,24th April 2018.
