VALUE ADDED TAX ACT, No. 14 OF 2002 AS AMENDED BY ACT, No. 06 OF 2005

Order under Section 2A

BY virtue of the powrs vested in me by Section 2A of the Value added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002, as amended by Act, No. 06 of2005, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order declare that the Value Added Tax shallbe charged on the importation of goods specified under H.S. Codes and descriptions in Column I and II of the Schedulehereto, at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column III, of such Schedule.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,September 17, 2018.
