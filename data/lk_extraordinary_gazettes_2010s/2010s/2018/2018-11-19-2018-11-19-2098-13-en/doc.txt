EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHARA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending consecutivemeetings without Permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to which WanniArachchige Susanthasiri the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Sri Lanka Freedom Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified underSection 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared returned as a member within a period ofthirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision tonominate Wanni Arachchige Susanthasiri to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the Mahara PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Wanni Arachchige Susanthasiri was selected as the member of the Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. J. S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Gampaha,16th November 2018.
