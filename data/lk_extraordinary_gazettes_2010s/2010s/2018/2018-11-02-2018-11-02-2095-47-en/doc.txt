NOTICE UNDER SECTION 3(1) OF THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT,No. 10 OF 2017

Supplementary Register of Electors - 2018

FOR THE SRI LANKAN CITIZENS WHO HAVE BEEN INTERNALLY DISPLACED AS A RESULT OF ANY ACTIONS OFA TERRORIST MILITANT OR OTHER GROUP DURING THE RECENT PAST AND WHOSE NAMES HAVE NOT BEENREGISTERED AS ELECTORS ELSEWHERE IN THE ISLAND

THE calling for applications in respect of the preparation of the Supplementary Register on the above matter hasexpired on 01.11.2018. Any person who is eligible under the Registration of Electors (Special Provisions) Act to beregistered in the supplementary register but whose name does not appear in the said register, may appeal in writing to theRegistering Officer of the respective Election Office on the format set out in the aforesaid Registration of Electors Act,in order to have his/her name entered or retained as the case may be in the register. Every appeal in this regard should bemade so as to reach the Registering Officer of the respective District Election Office within two weeks (until 15thNovember 2018) from the date of this notice published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Any person whose name is included in the supplementary register relating to the aforementioned electoral districtmay object to the entry or retention of his/her own name or that any other person in the register. Every objection should bemade in writing in duplicate and should be substantially in Form “C” set out in the aforesaid Act and be submitted to theRegistering Officer of the respective District Election Office within two weeks (until 15th November 2018) from the date of therespective notice published in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Forms to make a claim, to enter or retain a name in the register or to submit objections to any claim to enter or retainany name in the register may be obtained from the Election Office of the respective administrative district.

By order of the Election Commission.

H. M. T. D. HERATH,Secretary,Election Commission.At the Elections Secretariat,Rajagiriya,on this 02nd day November, 2018.
