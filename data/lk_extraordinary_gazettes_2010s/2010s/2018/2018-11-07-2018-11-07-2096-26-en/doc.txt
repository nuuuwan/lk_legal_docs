THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

WHEREAS, I have, in terms of paragraph (2) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka, determined the ministry and the subjects and functions assigned to the State Minister of Petroleum ResourcesDevelopment appointed by me as a Minister who is not a member of the Cabinet of Ministers in the exercise of powersvested in me under paragraph (1) of Article 44 of the Constitution, it is hereby notified that the subjects and functionsof the aforesaid Ministry and the Departments, State Corporations and Statutory Institutions pertaining to the saidMinistry shall be as set out in the schedule hereto from November 07, 2018.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,

President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,November 07, 2018.
