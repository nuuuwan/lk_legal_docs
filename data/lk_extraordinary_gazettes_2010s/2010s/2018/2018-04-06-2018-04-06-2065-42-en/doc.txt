PORTS AND AIRPORTS DEVELOPMENT LEVY ACT, No. 18 OF 2011

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Sub-section 3 of Section (3) of the Ports and Airports Development Levy Act,No. 18 of 2011, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media do hereby this Order, amend theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2049/16 of 13.12.2017 by,

1. The addition of the following Sub Paragraph immediately after Sub Paragraph (b) of Paragraph 1 :-

(c) “Ports and Airports Development Levy, shall be charged and levied on importation of each article specifiedin Column III corresponding to the H. S. Headings in Column I and H. S. Code in Column II of the ScheduleIII hereto, a concessionary rate of 2.5% in its corresponding entry in Column IV, on the recommendationof the Secretary, Ministry of Industry and Commerce based on a set of predetermined criteria.”

2. The addition of Schedule hereto as Schedule III, immediately after the Schedule II of the said ExtraordinaryGazette Notification No. 2049/16.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,April 5th 2018.
