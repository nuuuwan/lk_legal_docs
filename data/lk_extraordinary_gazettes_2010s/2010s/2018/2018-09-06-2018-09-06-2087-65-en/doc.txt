NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mrs. Manel Hil who resided in the village of Balangoda, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Balangoda, in the District of Rathnapura. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mrs. Manel Hil described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 28th September, 2018 stating his/their right or inter-est to such intestate property. K.A.K.RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 09th August, 2018.
