CONTROL OF PESTICIDES ACT, No. 33 OF 1980

Order under Section 9

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, by Section 9 of the Control of Pesticides Act, No. 33 of 1980, I, Jayakody ArachchigeSumith, Registrar of Pesticides, do by this Order-

(i)declare that every pesticide in such trade Name as specified in the Schedule hereto shall be an approvedpesticide; and

(ii)Classify such pesticides as restricted pesticides.
