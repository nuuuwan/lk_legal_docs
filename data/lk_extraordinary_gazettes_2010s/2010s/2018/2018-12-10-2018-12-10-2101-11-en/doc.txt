SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council - 2018

FOURTH SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OF PROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT, NO. 42

OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SIXTH

SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF

SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26 th SEPTEMBER, 2017- 2018

1.This permission may be cited as ''Permission to disburse under fourth financial statement - 2018 of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council'' and come in to operation from 13th, November 2018.

2.The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Billion FiveHundred and Seventy Nine Million, Five Hundred and Fifty Four Thousand Six Hundred and Eighty OneCents Eighty Seven Only (Rs.30,579,554,681.87) fot the service of the period beginning on 01st January2018 and ending on 31st December, 2018, which is specified in 2 nd paragraph of Gazette Notification No. 2052/ 15dated 03.01.2018, which is amended by third supplementary financial statement for the year 2018 and assentedon 12.09.2018, is hereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Two Billion Eight Hundred and Thirty Four Million, FourHundred and Eight Thousand One Hundred and Twelve and Cents Thirty Five Only (Rs. 32,834,408,112.35) andgrant permission to Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same fromthe Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

Accordingly the first Schedule of the said Gazette Notification is amended as the first schedule hereof andgrant permission to disburse the same.

3.Advanced accounts limits which is specified in second schedule of Gazette Notification No. 2052/ 15 dated03.01.2018 is hereby amended as second schedule of this notification and grant permission to Chief Secretaryof this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil Fund.

4.This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2018 or upto the date that expires three months (03) fromthe date of summoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start thefunctions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which occurs first.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Councilto disburse as per the Fourth Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2018, by virtue of powers vested in me underSection 27(a) of the Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Billion Five Hundredand Seventy Nine Million, Five Hundred and Fiffty Four Thousand Six Hundred and Eighty seven only (Rs.30,579,554,681.87) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2018 and ending 31st December, 2018,which is specified in annual financial statement - 2018 (amended) is hereby amended as, Rupees Thirty Two Billion EightHundred and Thirty Five Only (Rs. 32,834,408,112.35) and grant permission to disburse the same from the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2018 or upto the date that expires 03 months from the date ofsummoning the 07th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa ProvincialCouncil which occurs first.
