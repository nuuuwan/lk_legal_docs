CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Naming of Rikillagaskada Bus Stand as ‘‘Saliya Dissanayake Bus Stand’’ under the Provincial

Passenger Transport Services Authority Statute No. 01 of year 2000

I, Ediriweera Weerawardena, Minister of Road Development, Transport, Power and Energy, Housing and Construction ofthe Central Provincial Council hereby announce under the powers vested in me by sub clause ‘‘O’’ of paragraph 19 of theCentral Provincial Passenger Transport Authority Statute No. 01 of year 2000 of the Central Provincial Council, Name theRikillagaskada Bus Stand owned the Central Province Passenger Transport Authority by an Extra Special Gazette NotificationNo. 1210/ 2 dated 12.11.2001 of the Sri Lanka Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as ‘‘Saliya Dissanayake Bus Stand’’with effect from 11 June 2018.

EDIRIWEERA WEERAWARDENA,Minister of Road Development, Transport, Power and

Energy, Housing and Construction,

Central Province.
