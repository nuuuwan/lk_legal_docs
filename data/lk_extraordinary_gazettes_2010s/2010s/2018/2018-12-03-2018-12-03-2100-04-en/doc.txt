EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALVETTITHURAI URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valvettithurai Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress, the recognized political party to whichSivasothy Navakody the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, todecide upon returned member if Ahila Ilankai Thamil Congress Urban Council who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared Selected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Thamotharampillai Sathasivam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, Returning Officer of the Valvettithurai Urban Council, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that ThamotharampillaiSathasivam was selected as the member of Valvettithurai Urban Council.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Valvettithurai Urban Council.

District Election Office,Jaffna,29th November 2018.
