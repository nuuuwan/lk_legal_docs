Provincial Councils Notifications

WESTERN PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Amendment rules formulated under the Cooperative Employees' Pensions Statute No. 06 of 2010of Western Provincial Council

AS per powers entrusted to me under Section 23 of the Cooperative Employees' Pensions Statute No. 06 of 2010 of WesternProvince, I, Kejitan Lalith Wanigarathne, the Minister of Roads, Transport, Cooperative Development and Trade, Housingand Construction, Estate Infrastructure Facilities, Industries and Rural Development do hereby amend the rules formulatedunder the said Section of Cooperative Employees' Pensions Statute No.06 of 2010 and published in the Extraordinary Gazettepaper No. 1698/22 dated 24th March 2011 as follows. These amendment rules shall be enforced from the date of theirpublication in the Gazette paper.

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk

1A PG 4192 — 1407 (08/2018)

(1) By substituting the following rule instead of rule 3(2), the said rule is amended as follows:

According to the matters contained in this rule, an employees recruited to the permanent servicesubsequent to the date of enforcement of these rules, within 90 days from the date of confirmation of hisservice by the Wstern Provincial Cooperative Employees' Commission shall present to the Director anapplication prepared as prescribed by these rules.

(2) Inclusion of a new rule as 3(3) immediately after rule 3(2) as follows.

In case any problematic situation occurs regarding contribution to the Western Provincial CooperativeEmployee's Pension Scheme, the Director shall reach into a decision on the agreement of the ControlBoard.

Amendingrule 3(2)

Inclusion ofa new rule asrule 3(3)
