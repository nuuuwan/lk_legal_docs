ELECTIONS COMMISSION

Notice of the Election Commission

LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS - 10TH FEBRUARY 2018

IT is hereby informed that the results of the counting in each wards related to each of the 340 local authorities, of whichthe election was held on the 10th February, 2018, are being exhibited at relevant District Election Offices, Local Authorities,Grama Niladhari offices relevant to each ward, and in the website of the Elections Commission.

Further, it is informed that these results sheets have also been issued to the political parties contested.
