WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Order under Section 32(2) of Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987 amended by Provincial Council's

(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1990 assigning the powers of Western Province Public Service

I, K. C. Logeswaran, the Governor of Western Province by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 32(1) of ProvincialCouncil Act, No. 42 of 1987 as amended by Provincial Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1990, do hereby delegatepowers vested in me regarding appointments, transfers, promotions, dismissal from service and disciplinary control ofofficer of Western Provincial Public Service to the Provincial Public Service Commission in terms of Section 32(2) ofthe said Act, subject to conditions stipulated in Schedules 01 with effect from 16th April 2018. Powers of appointment toposts in Schedule 02 shall continue to be with the Governor of the Western Province,

Officers aggrieved by the decision of either the disciplinary authority and/or the Provincial Public ServiceCommission shall have a right of appeal to the Governor.

Delegation of powers published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 1859/32 dated 24.04.2014 is hereby repealedwithout prejudice to actions taken under said order.

K. C. LOGESWARAN,Governor of the Western Province.Governor's Secretariat of Western Province,No. 109, Level 05,Rotunda Tower,Colombo 03,22nd March 2018.
