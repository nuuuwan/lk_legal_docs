LICENSING OF SHIPPING AGENTS, FREIGHT FORWARDERS, NON- VESSELOPERATING COMMON CARRIERS AND CONTAINER OPERATORS ACT, No. 10 OF 1972

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Ports and Shipping under Section 10 read with Section 3 (5) of the Licensing ofShipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non- Vessel Operating Common Carriers, and Container Operators Act, No. 10 of1972.

MAHINDA SAMARASINGHE,Minister of Ports and Shipping.

Colombo,May 04th, 2018.

Regulations

The Shipping Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1991, published in Gazette Extraordinary No.684/ 9 of October17, 1991 are hereby amended by the repeal of regulation 7 thereof and the substitution therefor, of the following-

''7. A Shipping Agent shall levy any charge from a Principal for all services rendered in terms of the tariffrecommended by the Ceylon Association of Shipping Agents (CASA) and approved by the Minister in charge ofsubject of Ports and Shipping.''.
