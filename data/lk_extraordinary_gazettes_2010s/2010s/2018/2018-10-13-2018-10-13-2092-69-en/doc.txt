Notice under Section 7

I hereby give notice, under Sub - section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as Amended by the landAcquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that theGovernment intends to acquire the followingland/servitude* which is required for a public purpose:

Description of the land or servitude to beacquired:

The land named as Batadombagahawatta in total extentof 0.0010 Hectare (A: 0 R: 0 P : 0.3955) marked as lot No.1 inPreliminary Plan bearing No. B.P.G. 4336 made by SurveyorGeneral situated in the Village of Gonapinuwala, in theDivisional Secretariat Gonapinuwala, District of Galle.
