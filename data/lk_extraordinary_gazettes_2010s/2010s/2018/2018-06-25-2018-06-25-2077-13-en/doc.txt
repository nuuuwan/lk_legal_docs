MINUTE OF SRI LANKA TECHNOLOGICAL SERVICE - AMENDMENT No. 02

THE following amendments are made to the Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Technological Service published in theGazette Extraordinary No. 1930/12 dated 01.09.2015 as amended by the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1995/10 dated29.11.2016 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the other matters and conditions set out therein shallremain unchanged.

01. Note 08 in Page No. 7A should be amened as follows.

08. Note :-

(I) The officers who have completed five (05) years of service after their promotion to Grade I of the

Sri Lanka Technological Service as at the effective date of this Service Minute are exempted fromthe requirement of passing the third efficiency bar examination ;

(II) In addition to the officers in Grade I, the officers who have pass the second efficiency bar

examination are also able to complete the course prescribed for the third efficiency bar examination.

By order of the Public Service Commisison.

M. A. B. DAYA SENARATH,

Secretary,Public Service Commission.
