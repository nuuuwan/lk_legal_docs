PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

THE SPORTS LAW, No. 25 OF 1973

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Sports under Section 41 read with Section 31 of the Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973.

FAISZER MUSTHAPA,President’s Councel,Minister of Provincial Councils,

Local Government and Sports.

Colombo,25th October, 2018.

Regulations

Whereas by Gazette Notification bearing No. 2073/15 dated 31.05.2018, I Faiszer Musthapa, PC, Minister of ProvincialCouncils, Local Government and Sports appointed Mr. Hewa Thondilage Kamal Pathmasiri, Secretary of the Ministry ofProvincial Councils, Local Government and Sports as the Competent Authority of Sri Lanka Cricket with effect from01st June 2018 to continue with administration and perform any such other functions necessary for the due administrationof Sri Lanka Cricket.

And whereas Sri Lanka Cricket has consequently undertaken to the International Cricket Council, the internationalGoverning body for the Sport of Cricket to appoint an advisory committee to assist and advise the said competent authorityin carrying out the administration and perform any such other functions necessary for the due administration of Sri LankaCricket until such time an election is held to elect office bearers to Sri Lanka Cricket.

Accordingly, I Faiszer Musthapa, PC Minister of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports, in terms ofSection 41 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973 as amended hereby appoint -

(a) The Secretary to the Ministry of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports as the Competent

Authority of Sri Lanka Cricket with effect from 01st October 2018 to continue with the administrationand perform any such other functions necessary for the due administration of Sri Lanka Cricket.

This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk
