UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Wijayadasa Rajapakshe,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, inrespect of the KAATSU-Highly Advanced Medical Technology Training Centre (Pvt) Limited (KIU), a company incorporatedin Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 66705, do by this Order, amendthe Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1961/34 of April 08,2016 by addition immediately after the entry “Bachelor of Management Honours in Accounting” appearing in Column Iand its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of theSchedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto.

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

KAATSU-Highly Advanced Medical Technology Training Centre (Pvt) Limited (KIU) shall, when admittingstudents for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Education Honours inPhysical Science, adhere to the minimum entry qualifications of three (03) ‘S’ passes among the subjects CombinedMathematics or Higher Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, Information & Communication Technology and Mathematicsin one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advance Level) Examination or equivalent.
