L.D.B. 9/2001

MUTUAL ASSISTANCE IN CIVIL AND COMMERCIAL MATTERS ACT, No. 39 OF 2000

Order under Section 4

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 4 of the Mutual Assistance in Civil and Commercial Matters Act,No. 39 of 2000, I, Thalatha Atukorale, Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms, do by this Order declare that the provisionsof Part II of the aforesaid Act, shall apply in respect of the countries specified in the Schedule hereto, being countrieswhich are parties to the Hague Convention on the Service Abroad of Judicial and Extrajudicial Documents in Civil andCommercial Matters of November 15, 1965.

The Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1309/2 of October 06, 2003 is hereby rescinded withoutprejudice to anything previously lawfully done thereunder.

THALATHA ATUKORALE,Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms.

Colombo,25th September, 2018.
