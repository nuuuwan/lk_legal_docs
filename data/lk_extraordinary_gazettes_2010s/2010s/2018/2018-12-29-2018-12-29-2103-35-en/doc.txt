EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTH PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being Ceased tobe a Member of the Party ;

And whereas the Secretary of the Ilanki Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which ThiyakarasaPirakash the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon themember of that ward 01- Erlalai - North East of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision to nominateAnnathanam Richchard Thavappiragasam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha,acting in terms of Section 66A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatAnnathanam Richchard Thavappiragasam was elected as the member of the Ward 01- Erlalai- North East of Valikamam SouthPradeshiya Sabha.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Jaffna,28th December 2018.
