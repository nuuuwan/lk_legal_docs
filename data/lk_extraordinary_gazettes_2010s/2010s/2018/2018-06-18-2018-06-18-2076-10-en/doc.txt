EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HOROWPOTHANAPRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Horowpothana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to which lhalaDivulwewe Wimalawansha Himi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that 02 - Maradanmaduwa of Horowpothana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Kirihamige Tharanga Kumarasingha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Rathnayaka Ralalage Asanka Sathis Kumara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of theHorowpothana Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance(Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Kirihamige Tharanga Kumarasingha was elected as the member of the 02-Maradanmaduwa of Horowpothana Pradeshiya Sabha.

ASANKA RATHNAYAKA,Returning Officer,Horowpothana Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Anuradhapura,18th June, 2018.
