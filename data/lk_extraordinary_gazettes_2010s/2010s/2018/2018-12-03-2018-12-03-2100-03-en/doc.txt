EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, as amended,I, Mahinda Rajapaksa, Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs, do by this Order declare that, the rate of Excise dutyapplicable on every article specified in the Columns I, II and III of the Schedule hereto, shall replace corresponding everyarticle specified under the Schedule I of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2044/32 dated November 09, 2017and Schedule of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2097/3 dated November 12,2018, issued under Section 3of this Act, shall be as specified in the corresponding entry in Column IV of the Schedule, with effect from December04, 2018.

The order made under Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989 and published in the ExtraordinaryGazette Notification No. 2097/3 dated November 12, 2018 is hereby rescinded with effect from December 04, 2018.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs.Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs,Colombo 01,03rd December, 2018.
