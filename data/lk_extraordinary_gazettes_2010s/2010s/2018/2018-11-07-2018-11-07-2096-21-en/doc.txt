SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MahindaRajapaksa, Minister of Finance and Economic Affaris of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from November 08, 2018, subject to provisionsof Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs.

Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs.,Colombo 01,05th November, 2018.
