SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby approve the amendments of the limits of the Advanced Accounts that are stated in the Sixth schedule of the FourthFinancial Statement for the year 2017 of this Provincial Council, by virtue of powers vested in me under section 27 (a) of theProvincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanaka ended on 26th September, 2017.

Accordingly, the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council are hereby grantedpermission to bear the necessary express subjected to those limitations.

This permission will be terminated on 31st of December, 2017.

MARSHAL PERERA,Honourable Governor - Sabaragamuwa Province.

08th of December, 2017.

2017 FOURTH SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

Grant Permission by virtue of powers vested in meunder section 27(A) of Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987as the term of the sixth Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

1.This Permission may be cited as the "Permission to spend as per Fourth Supplementary Financial Statement of theSabaragamuwa Provincial Council" and will be effective from 08.12.2017.

2.The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Thirty Two Billion Four Hundredand Twenty Million, Six Hundred and Seventeen Thousand Three Hundred and Seventy one and Cents Ninety Nineonly (Rs. 32,420,617,371.99) for the service of the period beginning on 1st of January, 2017 and ending 31st December,2017 in this statute referred to as the "Financial Year 2017" which is specified in 2nd paragraph of the AppropriationStatute No. 04 of 2016 as amended by Appropriation statute No. 01 of 2017, Appropriation Statute No. 02 of 2017, andAppropriation Statute No. 04 of 2017 the first schedule is hereby amended as the expenditure of the SabaragamuwaProvincial Council which is estimated, Rupees Thirty Two Billion Nine Hundred and Eighty Six Million, One Hundredand Forty One Thousand and Nineteen and Cents Ninety Nine Only (Rs. 32,986,141,019,99) for the service of theperiod beginning on 1st of January, 2017 and ending 31st December, 2017 in this statute referred to as the "Financialyear 2017" as the First Schedule hereof and permission is hereby granted unto the Chief Secretary of the Province andto the Accounting Officer to spend that amount out of the Provincial Council Fund.
