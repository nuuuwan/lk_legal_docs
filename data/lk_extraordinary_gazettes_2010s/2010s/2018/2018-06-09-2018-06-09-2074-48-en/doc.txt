PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the North Western Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the North Western Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commissionunder Section 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membershipof the North Western Province Provincial Council by reason of Death ;

And whereas the Secretary of the United People’s Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to which theformer member Ananda Chandralal belonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaidAct, to nominate an eligible person to fill such vacancy;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has nominated Mr. Dingiri Banda HerathHerath Mudiyanselage to fill such vacancy;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. Dingiri Banda Herath Herath Mudiyanselage, elected as a member of the above Provincial Councilfrom the Administrative District of Kurunegala in respect of which such vacancy occurred.
