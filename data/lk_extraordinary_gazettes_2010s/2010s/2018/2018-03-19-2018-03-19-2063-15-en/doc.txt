MEDIATION BOARDS COMMISSION

Notice Calling for Nominations for the Appointment of Mediators

THE Mediation Boards Commission, by virtue of the powers vested in it by Section 5 (1) of The Mediation Boards Act,No.72 of 1988, invites nominations for the appointment of Chairman and Mediators in respect of resolving land disputesrelating to Possession or Ownership for the following Panels of Special Mediation Board (Land) in terms of Sections 3of the Mediation (Special Categories of Disputes) Act.

Special Mediation Board Areas

Mullaitivu DistrictVavuniya DistrictMannar DistrictAmpara District

02. The Mediation Boards Commission hereby gives notice calling nominations upon (A) the Individuals, (B) theBodies, Organizations and Institutions of non political nature, and (C) the District Secretaries/ DivisionalSecretaries specified hereinafter under 02 (II) to submit names of person who are eligible under 02 (I) forappointment as Mediators to the Panel of Mediators for the said Special Mediation Board areas:

(I) The following Persons are entitled to be appointed to the Panel of a particular Special Mediation BoardArea whose names may be so submitted for appointment.

(a) A person eligible to be appointed as a Member of the Panel of Mediators in respect of the SpecialMediation Boards shall be between 35 to 70 years of age and ;

(b) has any professional qualification obtained from a recognized professional body established by orunder any written law ;

(c) has been or is public officer.

(ii) A Head of a Place or religious worship or of school situated in that particular SpecialMediation Board area;

(iii) A retired Head of a Department or School principal residing in that particular SpecialMediation Board area;

(B) Any board, Organizations or Institution which has been in existence at least for a period of fiveyears and which is engaged in carrying out or doing anyone or more of the under mentioned objectives;

(i) The enhancement of educational, religious, moral or spiritual, advancement of the community

(ii) The enhancement of social welfare and eradication of poverty

(iii) The enhancement of rural or community development.

(iv) The enhancement of culture of sports

(v) The enhancement of projects, programmes and activities intending providing and supplyingbasic needs of the peoples such as health, food and shelter etc.

(II) The District Secretaries of the Administrative District in which that Special Mediation BoardArea is situated;

03.(a) Individual and Boards, Organizations and Institutions referred to in Paragraph (02) above may submitnominations of such Public Officers as are specified in Paragraph 2(a) and 2(b) above. An individual may notnominate his or her name for nominations.

(b) District Secretary referred to in Paragraph 2(c) above may submit nominations of Public Officers, of whomnot more than five may be appointed by the Commission to the relevant Panel of Mediators.

04.Form of Nominations:

(a) Individuals should submit nominations substantially in FORM “A” below;

(d) Boards, Organizations and Institutions should submit nominations substantially in FORM “B” below; and.

(c) District Secretaries, Divisional Secretaries should submit nominations substantially in FORM “C” below;

Each nomination should be in a separate FORM and should without fail contain the recommendation of thenominator as indicated in the next paragraph.
