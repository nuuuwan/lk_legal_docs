FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE SEETHAWAKAPURAURBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Seethawakapura Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of death;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to whichWannakuwaththewaduge Don Ajith Lakshman Perera the member belonged, was required by me underSection 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 01 Kiriwandala North ofSeethawakapura Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authoritiesElections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Diddeni Gamage Janaka Roshan Pushpakumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of the SeethawakapuraUrban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Diddeni Gamage Janaka Roshan Pushpakumara was elected as the member of the Ward No. 01Kiriwandala North of Seethawakapura Urban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,Returning Officer,Seethawakapura Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,30th October 2018.
