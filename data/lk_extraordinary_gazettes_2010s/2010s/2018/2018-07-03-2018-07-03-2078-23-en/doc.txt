FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER

SECTION 66A(1) (1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Sarath Abegunawardana the Member appointed toWard No. 05 Udahamulla of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of theaforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 05 Udahamulla of Maharagama Urban Council who belongsto such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is Qualified under section 8 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Membr within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominate YakalleKankanange Thilaka Premalal Weerasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (1a) of the Local Auhtorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatYakalle Kankanange Thilaka Premalal Weerasinghe was elected as the Member of the Ward No. 05 Udahamulla of MaharagamaUrban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,

Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,03rd July 2018.
