FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE

VALIKAMAM EAST PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66 A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen Vacant by reason of resignation:

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the All Ceylon Tamil Congress the recognized political party to which ShanthirasekaramPiratheepan the member belonged, was required by me under section 66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturned member of All Ceylon Tamil Congress who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of Thirty (30) days;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateIlayavan Mayilvakanam to fill such vacancy;

NOW THEREFORE Thanabalasingam Akilan, returning Officer of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha acting in termsof selection 66 A (1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ilayavan Mayilvakanamwas selected as the member of the Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. AKILAN, Returning Officer, Valikamam East Pradeshiya Sabha.
