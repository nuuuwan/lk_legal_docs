THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 2 OF 1980

Order under Section 2

I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka by virtue of powers vested in meunder Section 2 of the Urban Development Projects (Special Provisions) Act, No. 2 of 1980, being of the opinion uponthe recommendation of the Minister - in - Charge of the subject of Urban Development that the land specified in thebelow-mentioned schedule is urgently required for Bandaragama Twon Development Project do by this Order, declarethat the said allotment of land is required for the relevant purpose.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,28th October, 2018.
