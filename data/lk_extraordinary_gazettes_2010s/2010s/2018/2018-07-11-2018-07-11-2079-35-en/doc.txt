THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25 A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Wijayadasa Rajapakshe,the Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, inrespect of the Saegis Campus (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of2007 and bearing registration number PV 72548, do by this Order and subject to the conditions specified in the ScheduleI hereto, recognize the Saegis Campus (Private) Limited as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developingHigher Education therein, leading to the award of the Degree specified in Column I of the Schedule II hereto, of which theabbreviation is set out in the corresponding entry in Column II of the Schedule II hereto :-

SCHEDULE I

Conditions

Saegis Campus (Private) Limited (hereinafter referred to as the ‘‘Institute”) shall when admitting students for thefollowing Degree Programme specified in the Schedule II hereto, adhere to the minimum entry qualification of three (3)‘S’ passes in any stream in one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advanced Level) Examination or equivalent, in complyingwith University Grants Commission Circular No. 995 dated 28th September 2012.
