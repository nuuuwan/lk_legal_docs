SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose, in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, at therates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from October 01, 2018, subject to provisions ofSub-Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,28th September, 2018.
