EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 02/2018

REVISION OF THE CONDITIONS SET OUT IN THE EXCISE NOTIFICATION No. 666

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended from time to time,I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media do by this Order amend the Schedule in the ExciseNotification No. 666 of the Extraordinary Gazette dated 31 December 1979, by-

(1) repealing sub-paragraph (v) of paragraph 5 and substituting thereof with following sub-paragraph,

“ (v) Any persons under the age of eighteen years.”,

(2) removing sub-paragraph (c) of paragraph 11, and

(3) renumbering sub-paragraph (d) as sub-paragraph (c) in paragraph 11.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass MediaMinistry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,10 January 2018.
