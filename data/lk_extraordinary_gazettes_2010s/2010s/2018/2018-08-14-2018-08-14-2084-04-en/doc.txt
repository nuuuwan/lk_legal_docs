THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW, No. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978, I, PataleChampika Ranavaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development being of the opinion that the area described inColumn I of the Second Schedule appended hereto and specified in the corresponding metes and bounds entry in ColumnII of the same Schedule, excluding the area described as already declared Urban Development Areas in the First Scheduleappended hereto do by this Order declare such area to be Urban Development Area.

PATALEE CHAMPIKA RANAVAKA (M. P.),Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.

Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th and 18th Floor,“Suhurupaya”, Subuthipura Road,Battaramulla,30th July, 2018.
