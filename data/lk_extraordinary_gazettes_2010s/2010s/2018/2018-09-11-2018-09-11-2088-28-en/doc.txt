MUTUAL ASSISTANCE IN CRIMINAL MATTERS ACT, No. 25 of 2002

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub-section (1) of Section 2 of the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act, No. 25 of 2002, I, Thalatha Atukorale, Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms, do by this Order declare that the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall apply in respect of any country that is -

(a) a party to -

(i) the United Nations Convention against corruption speciﬁ ed in the Schedule I hereto ; and

(ii) the Council of Europe Convention on Cybercrime speciﬁ ed in the Schedule II hereto ;

(b) a member of the Commonwealth speciﬁ ed in the Schedule III hereto.

The Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1297/1 of July 14, 2003, is hereby rescinded without prejudice to anything previously lawfully done thereunder.

THALATHA ATUKORALE, Minister of Justice and Prison Reforms.

Colombo, 10th September, 2018.
