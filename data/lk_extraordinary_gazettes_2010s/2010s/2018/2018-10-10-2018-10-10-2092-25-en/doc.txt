NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO

LAND PARCELS

Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998

(Section 12)

NOTICE No : 12/0187 Polonnaruwa District

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner-ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 120171 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 12 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Irigeoya (Block 02) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of 30 - Mahasengama in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Hingurakgoda in the District of Polonnaruwa in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 12th November, 2018 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly pub-lish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 120171 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Set-tlement, or the Surveyor - Generals’ office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Set-tlement. K. A. K. RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Land Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No.1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla 01st October, 2018.
