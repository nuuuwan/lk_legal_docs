L.D.B 13/2016

THE ARMY ACT (CHAPTER 357)

REGULATIONS made by the President under Section 155 read with Section 29 of the Army Act (Chapter 357), read withArticle 4 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and Section 51 of the NineteenthAmendment to the Constitution.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Colombo,04th January, 2018.Regulations

The Army Pensions and Gratuity Code 1981 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 562/11 of June 15, 1989 asamended from time to time is hereby further amended as follows :-

(1) in regulation 19, by the insertion immediately after sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1) thereof, of the followingproviso :-

“Provided however, where such disablement or death, if caused by terrorist activities, is certified byan Army Medical Board, to be attributable to military service or to the performance of non-military dutiesunder Section 23 or 23A of the Army Act, the aggregate amount of the pension so awarded to an officer orin respect of an officer, together with the Army Service Pension, if any, shall not exceed 100% of pay perannum.”.

(2) in regulation 28, by the insertion immediately after sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1) thereof, of the followingproviso :

“Provided however, where such disablement or death, if caused by terrorist activities, is certified byan Army Medical Board, to be attributable to military service or to the performance of non-military dutiesunder Section 23 or 23A of the Army Act, the aggregate amount of the pension so awarded to a soldier or inrespect of a soldier, together with the Army Service Pension, if any, shall not exceed 100% of pay perannum.”.

THE NAVY ACT (CHAPTER 358)

REGULATIONS made by the President under Section 161 of the Navy Act (Chapter 358), read with Article 4 of theConstitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and Section 51 of the Nineteenth Amendment to theConstitution.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.Colombo,04th January, 2018.Regulations

The Navy Pensions and Gratuities Code 1981 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 654/10 of March 20,1991 as amended from time to time is hereby further amended as follows :-

(1) in regulation 16, by the insertion immediately after sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1) thereof, of the followingproviso :-

“Provided however, where such disablement or death, if caused by terrorist activities, is certified byan Navy Medical Board, to be attributable to Naval service or to the performance of Naval duties underSection 21 of the Navy Act, the aggregate amount of the pension so awarded to an officer or in respect ofan officer, together with the Navy Service Pension, if any, shall not exceed 100% of pay per annum.”.

(2) in regulation 25, by the insertion immediately after sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1) thereof, of the followingproviso :–

“Provided however, where such disablement or death, if caused by terrorist activities, is certified byan Navy Medical Board, to be attributable to Naval service or to the performance of Naval duties underSection 21 of the Navy Act, the aggregate amount of the pension so awarded to a sailor or in respect of asalior, together with the Navy Service Pension, if any, shall not exceed 100% of pay per annum.”.
