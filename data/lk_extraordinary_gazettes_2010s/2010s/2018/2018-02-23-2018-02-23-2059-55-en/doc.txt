FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

THE order under Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451), as amended by Acts, No. 13 of 1966,No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) as amendedby Acts, No. 13 of 1966, No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I,Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Mahaweli Development and Environment do by this order declare the Knuckles ReservedForest adjoining to Lali Ambe, Kola Ela, Haewood, Halgolla, Puhuarambe forest of 13.0867 hectares bounded by thelimit set forth in the Schedules No. 01, 02, 03. 04 and 05 hereto as Lali Ambe, Kola Ela, Hoewood, Halgolla, PuhuarambeReserved Forest from 15 day of February 2018.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,Minister of Mahaweli Development and Environment.Ministry of Mahaweli Development and Environment,Battaramulla,15th February 2018.
