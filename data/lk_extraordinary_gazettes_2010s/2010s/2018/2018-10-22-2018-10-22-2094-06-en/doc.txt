I hereby give notice under Sub Section (1) of Section (7) of theLand Acquisition Act 1964 that Government intends to acquirethe following lands which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

The following allotments of land from Lot No. 01 to Lot No.02 depicted in the Preliminary Plan No. K/ 3559 dated20.06.2018 prepared by the Surveyour General, within theboundaries shown below situated in the Village of Atalugama inthe Grama Niladhari Division No. 116C Kanangama in theWestern Minor Division in the Atalugam Korale in the DivisionalSecretary's Division of Dehiovita in the District of Kegalle inSabaragamuwa Province.

01. Lot No. 01Name of Land: Not indicated ; Description of the Land: Road;Claimant: Government; First Claimant: Kelani Valley PlantationsCompany, No. 100, Deans Road, Colombo 10; Extent: 0.0513Hectare.

02. Lot No. 02Name of Land: Dewalakanda Estate - AkkaramalayaDivision; Description of the Land: Estate, RubberCultivation aged 15-20 years; Claimant: Government;First Claimant: Kelani Valley Plantations Company,No. 100, Deans Road, Colombo 10; Extent: 1.7049Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands arehereby required to appear personally, or by agents dulyauthorized in writing, before me at Dehiovita DivisionalSecretariat on the 27th day of December 2018, at 10.00a.m.

Further, you are hereby requested to notify to mein writing (in duplicate) on or before 13 day of December,2018, the nature of their interests in the land, theparticulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said land, the amount of compensationand details of computation of such amount.
