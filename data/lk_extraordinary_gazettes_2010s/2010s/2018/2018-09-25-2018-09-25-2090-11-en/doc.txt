THE NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL ACT, No. 47 OF 1980

Order under Section 24C and 24D

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Section 24C of the National Environmental Act, No. 47 of 1980, read with Section51 of the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Maithripala Sirisena,President, do by this Order amend the Order, made under Section 24C and 24D of the aforesaid Act and published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 1466/26 of October 13, 2006, declaring an environmental protection area called the “MuthurajawelaEnvironmental Protection Area.”

(a) in paragraph (c) thereof, by the substitution for the words and figures, “September 15, 2006” of the words and
