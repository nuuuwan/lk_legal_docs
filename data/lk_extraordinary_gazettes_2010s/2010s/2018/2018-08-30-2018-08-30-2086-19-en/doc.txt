THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Rules under Section 137

RULES made under Section 137 read with Section 70C and 70D of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, by the SpecifiedAuthority appointed under Section 70B of the aforesaid Act.

Specified Authority.Colombo,28th August, 2018.

RULES

The Specified Authority (Powers relating to Recognition of Institutes as Degree Awarding Institutes) Rules No. 1 of 2013published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1824/21 dated August 22, 2013 as last amended by Gazette Extraordinary No.1891/9 dated December 02, 2014 are hereby further amended as follows :-

1. by the repeal of rule 2 and the substitution therefor of the following :-

“2. The guidelines set-out in the Schedule I and Schedule II to these rules shall be applicable forthe recognition of any State or Non-State Institute as a Degree Awarding Institute and for the obtainingof power to grant Degree or Degrees by such institute, on the payment of a processing fee as providedunder Schedule III to these rules.”.

2. by the repeal of rule 7 and the substitution therefor of the following :-

“7. Any Institute which intends to obtain recognition as a Degree Awarding Institute and obtains thepower to confer a degree or degrees (hereinafter referred to as “the applicant”) shall submit to theSpecified Authority, an application consisting of Part I and Part II, containing particulars given in theguidelines as set out in the Schedule I and Schedule II and on the payment of the processing fee as set outin the Schedule III, for the Institutional Review and the Subject Review.”.

3. by the repeal of rule 14 and the substitution therefor of the following :-

“14. The applicant may re-submit the application returned at the first review, together with therequired information, to the Specified Authority within a period of three months from the date of suchreturn. No processing free shall be charged for the first re-submission of the application. The guidelinesset out in the Schedule I shall be complied with. 4. by the repeal of rule 20 and the substitution therefor of the following :-

“20. The applicant may re-submit the application for review with the required information, to theSpecified Authority within a period of three months from the date of such return, and in accordance withthe requirements specified in the Schedule II to these rules. No processing fee shall be charged for thefirst re-submission of the application.”.

5. by the insertion immediately after the rule 34 of the following new part as Part IV :-

“PART IV

Processing Charge

35. Institutional Review and Subject Review shall be subject to a processing fee as set out in theSchedule III to these rules.

36. Any Degree Awarding Institute which does not comply with the requirements at the second orsubsequent reviews shall be subject to a payment of fifty percent (50%) of the processing chargesspecified in item (b) of the Schedule III.

37. Where the study programme is conducted at a different location, a payment of twenty percent(20%) of the processing charges for the Institutional Review shall be additionally charged for evaluatingsuch different location.”.

6. by the renumbering of rule 35 as rule 38.

7. in the renumbered rule 38 the definition of the expression “Specified Authority” shall be repealed and thefollowing substituted therefor :-

“ “Specified Authority” means the person appointed as a Specified Authority under Section 70B ofthe Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 ;”.
