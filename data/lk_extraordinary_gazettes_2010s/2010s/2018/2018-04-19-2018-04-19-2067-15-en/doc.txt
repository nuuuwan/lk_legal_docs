BY virtue the powers vested in me by section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I Kabir Hashim, the Minister ofHigher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act in respect of the ColomboInternational Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the CompaniesAct, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 17745, do by this Order, amend the shedule to the Order made underthe said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2053/39 of January 11, 2018 by the addition immediately after theentry “Bachelor of Education Honours in physical Sciences” appearing in column I and it’s corresponding entry in columnII respectively of the Shedule II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto,subject to the conditions specified in schedule I hereto:-

KABIR HASHIM,Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo 01,18th April , 2018.

SCHEDULE I

CONDITIONS

Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited, shall, when admitting students for the degreeprogrammes specified in schedule II hereto in respect of:

(a)Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineering in Mechanical Engineering, Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineeringin Automotive Engineering, Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineering in Electronic and TelecommunicationEngineering and Bachelor of Science Honours in Engineering in Civil Engineering, adhere to minimum entry qualificationof three (03) ‘S’ passes in Physical Science stream in one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advance Level) Examinationor equivalent.
