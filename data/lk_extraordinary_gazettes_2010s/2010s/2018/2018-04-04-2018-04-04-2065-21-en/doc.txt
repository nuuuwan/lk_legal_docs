FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE TRINCOMALEE TOWN &GRAVETS PRADESHIYA SABAH UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIESELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Trincomalee Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason ofresignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which RasathuraiSivaraja the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon themember of that Ward 02 Puliyankulam if Trincomalee Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and isnot disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Eliyezer Gnananayagam Gnanagunalan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, returning officer of the Trincomalee Town & Gravets, acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that EliyezerGnananayagam Gnanagunalan was elected as the member of the Ward 02 Puliyankulam of Trincomalee Town & GravetsPradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Trincomalee Town & Gravets Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Trincomalee,04th April, 2018,

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE TRINCOMALEE URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Trincomalee Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which ThurairasaDhanaraj the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide uponthe member of that Ward 01 Anbuvalipuram if Trincomalee Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Mr. Nagarasa Rasanayagam to fill such vacancy ;
