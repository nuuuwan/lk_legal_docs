Government Notifications

L.D.B. 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. WijayadasaRajapaksa, Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1603/35 of May 28, 2009(as last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2053/ 24 of January 10, 2018), which specifies theFaculties and the Departments of the University of the Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of theprovisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by theaddition immediately after the entry ‘‘16. Department of Medical Education and Health Sciences’’ appearing in ColumnII of the Schedule which corresponds to the item ‘‘ Faculty of Medical Sciences’’ appearing Column I of that Schedule,of the following new entry :-

‘‘17. Department of Allied Health Sciences’’.

Dr. WIJAYADASA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs.

Colombo,06th July 2018.
