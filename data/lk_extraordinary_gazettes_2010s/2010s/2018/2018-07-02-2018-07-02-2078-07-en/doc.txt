FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KURUNEGALA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichBoyagane Dewage Jagath Manjula Wijerathne the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 25 - Katupitiya if Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Ranasinghe Arachchilage Asanka Pradeep Ranasinghe to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Gamalathge Dilip Nishantha, returning officer of the Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that RanasingheArachchilage Asanka Pradeep Ranasinghe was elected as the member of the 25 - Katupitiya of Kurunegala PradeshiyaSabha.

GAMALATHGE DILIP NISHANTHA,Returning Officer,Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kurunegala,02nd July, 2018,
