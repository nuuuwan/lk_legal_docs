FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL

UNDER SECTION 66 A (1) (1a) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of Member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of failing to make and subscribean oath or affirmation in terms of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

And Whereas the Leader of Independent Group No. 02 to which Ranadheerage Lakmali Dilrukshi the Memberappointed to Ward No. 08 Thalawathugoda of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) (a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 08 Thalawathugoda of Maharagama UrbanCouncil who belongs to such Independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is Qualified under Section 8 ofthe Local Authorities Elections Ordiance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Member within a period ofthirty (30) days;

And Whereas the leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominate HarshinieKemanganee Sepalage to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66A (1)(1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatHarshinie Kemanganee Sepalage was elected as the member of the Ward No. 08 Thalawathugoda of Maharagama UrbanCouncil.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,

Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,12th July, 2018,Colombo.
