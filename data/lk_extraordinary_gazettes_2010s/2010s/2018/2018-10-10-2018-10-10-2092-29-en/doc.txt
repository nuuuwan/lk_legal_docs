It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Thilakarathna Arachchige Chithrasena described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 16th November, 2018 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property.
