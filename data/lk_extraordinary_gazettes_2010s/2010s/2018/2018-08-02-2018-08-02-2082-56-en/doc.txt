Notice under Section 7

Reference No.: 4/3/1/93.

I, hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of theLand Acquisition Act as Amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intendsto acquire the following Land/Servitude which is required fora public purpose.

Description of the Land or Servitude to be acquired:
