CIVIL AVIATION ACT, No 14 OF 2010

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation under Section 117 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14of 2010 read with Sections 66 and 69 of the aforesaid Act.

NIMAL SIRIPALA DE SILVA,Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation.

Colombo,26th June, 2018.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Licensing of Personnel and Training Institutions)Regulation No. 01 of 2018.

2. The Director-General of Civil Aviation (hereinafter referred to as the “Director -General”) may on beingsatisfied with the fulfillment of the requirements as specified in Column IV of the Schedule hereto and also specified inrespective implementing Standards, by any person or institution for a licence or permit, as the case may be, to provide anytraining course in respect of any activity specified in Column I, issue the respective licence or permit, or refuse to issuethe licence or permit for reasons assigned therefor. The category of such licence or permit, as the case may be, isspecified in Column II of the Schedule hereto and the guide lines to the Implementing Standards specified in Column IVof the Schedule shall be in the Manuals as specified in Column III thereof.

3. The procedure for the issuance, amendment, renewal, ground for suspension and revocation of any licence orpermit, as the case may be, issued under regulation 2, the terms and conditions applicable to any such licence or permitand the privileges attached thereto shall be in compliance with the relevant implementing Standards for each such activityas specified in Column IV of the Schedule and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1713/7 dated July 05, 2011.

4. The application processing fee, the licence or permit fee and the fee for any renewal thereof shall be as specifiedin the Civil Aviation Authority Fees and Charges Regulation No. 1 of 2014 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1869/32 dated July 02, 2014.

5. Every licence holder shall prior to the date of expiry of such licence or permits, as the case may be, issuedunder regulation 2, make an application to the Director-General for the renewal of such licence or permit.

6. The Director-General may on having proof of any contravention of any provision relating to aviation safetyrequirements issued under the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010 or any regulation made thereunder, by any person orinstitution to whom or which a licence or permit has been issued under these regulations, suspend or revoke such licenceor permit, as the case may be.

7. Any person or institution aggrieved by a decision made by the Director-General under these regulations may,before the expiry of thirty working days from the date of communication of such decision to such person or institution,prefer an appeal to the Secretary to the Ministry of the Minister under Section 112 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of2010.

8. The following regulations of the Air Navigation Regulations of 1955 published in Gazette ExtraordinaryNo. 10812 dated 01.07.1955 are hereby repealed without prejudice to anything previously done thereunder

(a)regulations 22 to 30 of Chapter (IV) ;(b)regulations 48 to 73 of Chapter (V) and ;(c)regulations 74 to 80 of Chapter (VI) ;

9. For the purpose of these regulations -

“Convention” shall have the same meaning as assignged in the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010 ;“Director-General” means the Director-General of Civil Aviation appointed under Section 11 of the CivilAviation Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No. 34 of 2002.
