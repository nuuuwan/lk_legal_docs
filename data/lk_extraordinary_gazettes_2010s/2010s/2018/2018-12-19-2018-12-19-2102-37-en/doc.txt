NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Anthonige Maikal Susantha who resided in the village of Waradala, in the Divisional Secre-tary’s Division of Diulapitiya, in the District of Gampaha. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Anthonige Maikal Susantha described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 28th January, 2019 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property. K.A.K.RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 12th December, 2018
