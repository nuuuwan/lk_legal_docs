THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Order under Section 49 A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, by Section 49 A of the Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460) read with Article 44 ofthe Constitution, I, Malik Samarawickrama, Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order, declare that the land specified in the Schedule hereto is required by theBoard of Investment of Sri Lanka being a public corporation established by the Board of investment of Sri Lanka Law,No. 4 of 1978.

MALIK SAMARAWICKRAMA,Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade.Colombo,05th September, 2018.
