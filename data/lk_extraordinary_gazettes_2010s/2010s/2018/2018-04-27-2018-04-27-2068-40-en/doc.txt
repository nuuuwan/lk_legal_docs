EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NORWOOD PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of returned member in Norwood Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ceylon Workers Congress, (P. Wing), the recognized political party towhich Kalimuthu Sivasamy the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that ward 16 Wanaraja, Norwood Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared electedas a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Kanapathy Kulandevel Ravi to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pulasthi Pathirana, Returning Officer of the Nuwara Eliya acting in terms of Section66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare Kanapathy Kulandevel Ravi waselected as the member of the ward 16 Wanaraja, Norwood Pradeshiya Sabha.

PULASTHI PATHIRANA,Returning Officer,Norwood Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Nuwara Eliya,24th April, 2018.
