Provincial Councils Notifications

Authorized officer appointment

UVA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Rent Act, No. 7 of 1972

I, Akalan Senthil Thondaman, Uva Provincial Minister of Road Development, Housing, Water Supply, Estate Infrastructureand Cooperatives, by virtue of powers vested in me by section 5 (1) of Rent Statute No. 05 of 2010 of the Provincial Councilof Uva Province which should be read along with the Rent Act, No. 7 of 1972, am here by announcing that Mr. Wasala BandaraMudiyansalage Jayantha Pushpakumara of the Public Management Assistant service Supra Class was appointed as the"Authorized Officer" for Badulla and Monaragala districts with effect from 01.10.2017 to accomplish duties and tasks demisedfrom the Rent Act, No. 7 of 1972.

AKALAN SENTHIL THONDAMAN,Uva Provincial Minister of RoadDevelopment, Housing, Water Supply,Estate Infrastructure and Cooperatives.

29th day of March 2018,Badulla,At the office of the Minister,R. H. Gunawardane Mawatha.
