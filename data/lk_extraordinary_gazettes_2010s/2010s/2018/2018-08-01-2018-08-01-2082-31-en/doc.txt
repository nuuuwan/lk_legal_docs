SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 02 months commencing from August 01, 2018, subject to provisions of SubSection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in the ExtraordinaryGazette Notification No. 2070/2 of 08.05.2018 in respect of Fish and Gazette Notification No. 2075/31 of 14.06.2018 in respectof Sail Fish, Marlin Fish and Thora Fish are rescinded with effect from August 01, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,July 31, 2018.
