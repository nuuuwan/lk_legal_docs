EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, NO. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order,

a. amend Item No. 1(b) and 1(c) of the Schedule II of the Order made under that Section and published by theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2044/32 dated November 09, 2017 by substitution of the followingSchedule A hereto ; and

b. amend Item No. 02 and 03 of the Order made under that Section and published by the Extraordinary GazetteNotification No. 2047/25 dated November 28, 2017 by substitution of the following Schedule B hereto.

This Order shall come into effect from February 16, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,15th February 2018.
