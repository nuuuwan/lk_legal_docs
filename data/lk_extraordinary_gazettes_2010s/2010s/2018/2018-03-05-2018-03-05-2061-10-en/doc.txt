CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Pujapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha (Divisional Council of Pujapitiya)

IMPOSING OF ENTERTAINMENT TAX

IT is hereby informed, according to sub section (2) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance Chapter 267, that the followingproposal which is decided by the Secretary of the Pujapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha (Divisional Council of Pujapitiya), in termsof the provisions of Section 2(1) of the above Ordinance, has been accepted by the Minister in-charge for the subject oflocal governments of the provincial council of the central province, in terms of the provisions of sub section (2) of thesaid ordinance.

R. M. G. S. RATHNAYAKE,

Secretary,Chief, Education and Local

Government Ministry,

Central Province.

At Pallekale,12th October 2018.

PROPOSAL

I have decided to levy an Entertaiment Tax of 10% from the ticket fares (except the entertainment tax) within thelimits of the Pujapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha (Divisional Council of Pujapitiya), in terms of the powers vested to me accordingto Section 9(3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 and in terms of provisions of Section 2(1) of the EntertainmentTax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 further amended by the Entertainment Tax Act, No. 37 of 1984.
