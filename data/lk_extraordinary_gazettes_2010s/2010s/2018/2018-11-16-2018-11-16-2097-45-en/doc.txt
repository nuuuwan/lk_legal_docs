LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDMENT) ACT NO 28OF 1964 (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: DIK/LAN/02/47.

I, here by give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act. No. 28 of 1964 (amendment) thatthe Government intends to acquire the following Land whichis required for a public purpose.

Details of the land to be obtained :

Following lands mentioned in the preliminary Plan No. 2743of surveyor General Matara and situated in Village ofBatheegama Grama Niladhari Division of Batheegama Westin Dickwella Divisional Secretariat Matara District.

01. Lot No. 01Name of Land: Wanniarachchikankanamgegedarawatta,Iththabenewatta, Rathuralagewatta, Ihalaweralugahawatta,Damaniyagahawatta and Diyawarakagahakoratuwa;

Description: Scrub; Claimed by: W. D. Sujeewa Prasanna,145/48, Lesly Ranagala Road, Borella, Colombo; Extent: 0.9603Hectare.

All Persons interested in the aforesaid land are herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at the Dickwella Divisional Secretary'sOffice on 19th December, 2018 at 10.00 a.m. and are herebyrequested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or beforethe 12th December, 2018 the nature of their interests in theland, the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount ofcompensation and the details of the computation of suchamount. All deeds and documents in support of the claimsshould be produced before me at the inquiry.

B. S. RANJITHA,Divisional Secretary,Divisional Secretariat,Dickwella.
