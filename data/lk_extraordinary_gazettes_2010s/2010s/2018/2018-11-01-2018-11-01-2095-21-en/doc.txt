SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MahindaRajapaksa, Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of list of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 03 months commencing from November 02, 2018, subject to provisionsof Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Orders made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2075/31 of 14.06.2018 in respect of Black gram classifiable under the HSCode 0713.31.29 and Gazette Notification No. 2070/6 of 09.05.2018 in respect of lentils classifiable under the HSCodes 0713.40.11, 0713.40.12, 0713.40.21, 0713.40.22 and Gazette Notification No. 2087/76 of 07.09.2018 in respectof Chickpeas classifiable under the HS Codes 0713.20.10, 0713.20.20 are rescinded with efffect from November 02,2018.

MAHINDA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs.Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs,Colombo 01,01st November 2018.
