AGRARIAN DEVELOPMENT ACT, No. 46 OF 2000

Determination under Section 35(1)

IN accordance to the powers vested in me under Section 35(1) of the Agrarian Development Act, No. 46 of 2000 asamended by the Agrarian Development (Amendment) Act, No. 46 of 2011, I, W. M. M. B. Weerasekara, CommissionerGeneral of Agrarian Development hereby determined that the specified rent in the Column II for one acre cultivated ineach cultivation season in any paddy land extent, for agricultural crops specified in Column I of the Schedule of thisNotification is the rent to be paid by tenant cultivator to the landlord, where any extent of paddy land is cultivated with anyother agricultural crop other than paddy under said Act.

W. M. M. B. WEERASEKARA,Commissioner General of Agrarian Development.
