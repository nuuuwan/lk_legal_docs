WESTERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Rent Act of 1972

ACCORDING to the powers entrusted to me as per 13th Amendment to the Constitution of 1978 and under Section 43 of theRent Act of 1972, I, Kejitan Lalith Wanigarathne, the Minister of Provincial Roads, Transport, Cooperative Development andTrade, Housing and Construction, Estate Infrastructure Facilities, Industries and Rural Development of Western Province dohereby make the following order as per Section 44 of the said Act.

KEJITAN LALITH WANIGARATHNE,Minister of Provincial Roads, Transport, Cooperative Developmentand Trade, Housing and Construction, Estate InfrastructureFacilities, Industries and Rural Development ofWestern Province.
