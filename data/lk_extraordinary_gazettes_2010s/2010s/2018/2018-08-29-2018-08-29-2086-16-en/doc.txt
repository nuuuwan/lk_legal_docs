EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KALPITIYA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Muslim Congress, the recognized political party to which AbdulRaidu Muhammadu Razak Ameen the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon returned member of Sri Lanka Muslim Congress who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities election ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Abdul Rahuman Mohamad Jeseem to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in termsof Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Abdul RahumanMohamad Jeseem was selected as the member of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. REGAN,Returning Officer,Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Puttalam,28th August, 2018.
