EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989 as amended,I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order declare that, the Excise Duty on everyarticle specified in the Column I, II and III of the Schedule hereto, shall be payable at the rate specified in the correspondingentry in Column IV of the Schedule hereto, with effect from September 18, 2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA, Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,17th September, 2018.
