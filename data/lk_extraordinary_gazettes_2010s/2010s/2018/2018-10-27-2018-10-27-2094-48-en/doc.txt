MY Reference No. : 4/1/5/21.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the LandAcquisition (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 1964, that theGovernment intends to acquire the servitude over thefollowing Land, which is required for a public purpose:
