FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66 A (1) (1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation:

And Whereas the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which Nuwan Madhushanka Fernando the member appointedto Ward No. 18 Maharagama South of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a)of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 18 Maharagama South of Maharagama Urban Councilwho belongs to such independent group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is qualified under Section 8 of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Member within a period of thirty (30)days.

And Where as the Leader of aforesaid independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateAngoda Liyanage Pradeep Chaminda to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66 A (1) (1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatAngoda Liyanage Pradeep Chaminda was elected as the member of the Ward No. 18 Maharagama South of MaharagamaUrban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,

Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.District Election Office,Colombo.14th August, 2018
