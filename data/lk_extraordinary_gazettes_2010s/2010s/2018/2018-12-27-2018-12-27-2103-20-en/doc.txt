FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MATARA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member of Matara Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which MohammedFazul Ajwad the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide uponreturned member if People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 ofthe Local Authorities Electioins Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a Member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Hewa Kuda Anthonige Somaweera to fill such vacancy ;

Now therefore I, Thananthri Withanage Poshitha Kaumadi, Returning Officer of the Matara Municipal Council,acting in terms of section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that HewaKuda Anthonige Somaweera was selected as the Member of the Matara Municipal Council.

T. W. POSHITHA KAUMADI,Returning Officer,Matara Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Matara,26th December, 2018,
