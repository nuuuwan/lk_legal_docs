ANNOUNCEMENT UNDER SECTION 76A OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES

ELECTIONS ACT, (CHAPTER 262) SUSPENSION OF PROPAGANDA

ACTIVITIES

AS it is the responsiblity of the Elections Commission to conduct free and fair elections in accordance with the Article104 B (1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and as it is empowered to issue directives inorder to overcome the obstacles in the conduct of elections to be held under the Local Authorities Elections Act(Chapter 262) as amended by Local Authorities Elections (Amendments) Act, No. 22 of 2012, it is hereby announced that thefollowing directives are issued by the Commission.

01. Taking photographs and videoing inside the polling station.

It is hereby announced that taking photographs or videoing stages of marking ballot papers or marked ballot papersinside postal voting centres or in polling stations or distribution or publishing of photographs of marked ballot papersthrough print, electronic, social or any other media is prohibited.

It is also announced that the Elections Commission will issue permission in writing when there is a need to takephotographs or to video any special occasion or procedure inside a polling station, for the purpose of preparing officialreports and collection of photographs on the poll
