Government Notifications

PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Western Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Western Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission under Section65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the WesternProvince Provincial Council by reason of Death ;

And Whereas the Secretary of the Democratic Party, the recognized political party to which the member belongedwas required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligible person to fill suchvacancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has nominated Mr. Janaka Kuruppu to fillsuch vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988,read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby declareMr. Janaka Kuruppu, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative District of Gampaha inrespect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYA,N. J. ABEYSEKERE,S. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLE,Chairman,Member,Member,Election Commission.Election Commission.Election Commission.
