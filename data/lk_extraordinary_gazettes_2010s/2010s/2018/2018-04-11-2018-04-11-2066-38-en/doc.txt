THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE COCONUT GROWING TRADE

IT hereby notified under regulation 30 of the Wages Boards Regulations, 1971, that Mr. S. K. L. Obeysekara and Mr.Albert Peiris have been appointed as the Employers' Representatives of the Wages Board for the Coconut Growing Tradein place of Mr. Malin Gunathilake and Mr. Sunil Poholiyadda by the Hon. Minister of Labour, Trade Union Relations andSabaragamuwa Development under Section 9 of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136). This appointment is validonly upto 16.02.2019.

S. A. N. SARANATHISSA,Secretary,Ministry of Labour, Trade Unions Relationsand Sabaragamuwa Development.

Labour Secretariat,Colombo 05,09th April 2018.
