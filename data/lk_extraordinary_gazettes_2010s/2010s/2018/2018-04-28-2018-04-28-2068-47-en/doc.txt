THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW, No. 41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978, I, PataliChampika Ranawaka, Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, being of the opinion that the areas specified inColumns I excluding the already declared areas mentioned under Schedule I, Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha Area, ScheduleII, Mawathagama Pradeshiya Sabha Area, Schedule III, Polgahawela Pradeshiya Sabha Area in Column I respectively hereto,having the metes and bounds specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of the Schedules hereto are suitable fordevelopment, do by this order declare such areas to be an urban development area.

PATALI CHAMPIKA RANAWAKA (MP),Minister of Megapolis and Western Development.Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development,17th & 18th Floor,“Suhurupaya”Sri Subuthipura Road,Battaramulla,06th April 2018.
