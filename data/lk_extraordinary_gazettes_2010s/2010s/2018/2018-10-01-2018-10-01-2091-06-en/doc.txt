PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

L.D.B. 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Wijayadasa Rajapaksa,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by thisOrder, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1603/35 of May 28, 2009 (as last amendedby Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2079/11 of July 09, 2018), which specifies the Faculties and Departmentsof the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139 of theaforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the addition immediately after the entry‘‘10. Department of Sports Science’’ appearing in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item ‘‘ Faculty ofApplied Sciences’’ appearing in Column I of that Schedule, of the following new entry :-

‘‘11. Department of Polymer Science’’.

Dr. WIJAYADASA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs.

Colombo,28th September 2018.
