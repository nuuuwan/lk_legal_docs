SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy, atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule. This Order shall be valid for a period of 06months commencing from 16th February 2018, subject to provisions of Sub section (3) of Section 2 of the SpecialCommodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,15th February, 2018.
