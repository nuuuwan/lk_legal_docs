1A - PG 4078 — 107 (06/2018)

RULES ON RECRUITMENT, APPOINTMENT, TRANSFER, PROMOTION AND DISCIPLINARYCONTROL OF POLICE OFFICERS

RULES made by the National Police Commission by virtue of the powers vested in it under Article 155 FFF of theConstitution.

P. H. MANATUNGA,Chairman,National Police Commission.

Colombo,11th June, 2018.

Rules

1.These rules may be cited as the Rules on recruitment, appointment, transfer, promotion and disciplinarycontrol of Police Officers, approved by the Commission on May 08, 2018.

2.The National Police Commission when appointing chairperson/s and members to the interview panels forthe recruitment/ appointment/ selection of candidates to the posts of Police Officers may appoint achairperson/ s to chair such Panels.

3.The Rule in (2) above shall apply to the recruitment and appointment of Police Officers, notwithstanding anyexisting rules or rules of procedure to the contrary.
