(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

L.D.B 21/78 (xiv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 20 (4) (b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 20 (4) (b) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. WijayadasaRajapaksha, Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, on being satisfied that the administration of the affairs ofthe Postgraduate Institute of Archeology have been seriously dislocated and that the authorities of the said Institute havefailed to restore normal conditions, do by this Order, appoint Professor Prishantha Gunawardhana to be the CompetentAuthority with effect from April 24, 2018 to June 30, 2018 for the purpose of exercising, performing and discharging inlieu of Director of Postgraduate Institute of Archeology, any power, duty or function which has been conferred orimposed on, or assigned to the said Director under the said Act, or any Order, Ordinance, Statute, By- Law, Regulation orRule made thereunder.

DR. WIJAYADASA RAJAPAKSHA,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs.Colombo,28th June, 2018.
