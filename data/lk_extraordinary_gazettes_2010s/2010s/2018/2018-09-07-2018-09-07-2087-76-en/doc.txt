SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, EranWickramaratne, Acting Minister of Finance and Mass Media the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by thisorder impose in respect of list of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special CommodityLevy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of one year commencing from September 08, 2018, subject to provisionsof Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

ERAN WICKRAMARATNE,Acting Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,07th September, 2018.
