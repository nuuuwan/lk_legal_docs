CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, H. P. Jayananda, Addl. Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 28th May, 2018, all duties of Customsas well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid andreceived at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2072/1 of21st May, 2018 is hereby rescinded and the said Rates of Exchange are Scheduled to be rescinded by a similarnotification to be published on or before 03rd June, 2018.

H. P. JAYANANDA,Addl. Director General of Customs,For Director General of Customs.Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,25th May, 2018.
