FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE YATAWATTA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Yatawatta Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the Independent Group , the Independent Group to which EkanayakeMudiyanselage Pahala Arampola Malala Bandara Ekanayake the member belonged, was required by me under Section66 A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member if Independent Group who belongs to suchIndependent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the aforesaid Independent Group, has communicated his decision to nominateEramudumade Gedara Buddhika Prasad Weerasekara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Prathapasinghe Arachchige Kumarinee Dasanthi, Returning Officer of the YatawattaPradeshiya Sabha , acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Eramudumade Gedara Buddhika Prasad Weerasekara was Selected as the member of the YatawattaPradeshiya Sabha.

P. A. K. DASANTHI,Returning Officer,Yatawatta Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Matale,17th December 2018.
