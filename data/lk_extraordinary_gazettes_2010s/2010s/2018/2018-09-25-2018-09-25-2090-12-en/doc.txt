FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NUWARAGAM PALATHA EAST

PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS

ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member of Nuwaragam Palatha East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to which RanaweeraArachchilage Pradeep Nilaweera the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A (1) (a) of the aforesaidordinance, to decide upon the member of that 4- Vijayapura if Nuwaragam Palatha East Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateBasnayaka Adhikaram Gunarathna Mudiyanselage Kumuduni Geethamala Gunarathna Kumari to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Mohideen Bawa Mohamed Sufyan, returning officer of the Nuwaragam Palatha East PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of section 66A (1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatBasnayaka Adhikaram Gunarathna Mudiyanselage Kumuduni Geethamala Gunarathna Kumari was elected as the member ofthe 4- Vijayapura of Nuwaragam Palatha East Pradeshiya Sabha.

M. B. M. SUFYAN,Returning Officer,Nuwaragam Palatha East Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Anuradhapura,25th September 2018.
