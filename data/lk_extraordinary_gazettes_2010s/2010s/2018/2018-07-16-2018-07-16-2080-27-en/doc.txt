TOURISM ACT, No. 38 OF 2005

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 70 of the Tourism Act, No. 38 of 2005, I, John Antony EmmanualAmarathunga, Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, do hereby order in line with the Section49(a) of the Land Acquisitions Act, that the land mentioned in the Schedule below is required to be acquired for thepurpose of development of tourism by Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority.

JOHN ANTONY EMMANUAL AMARATHUNGA,Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs

Colombo,12th July, 2018,

Schedule

The land 145.4 hectares (359 Acre., 1Rood, 06 Perches) in extent depicted as Lot Numbers A of SurveyorGeneral's Tracing No. MA/DSO/2017/126, situated in the village of Pavilupaddankaddykudiyeruppu, in the Grama NiladhariDivision of Thullukudiyiruppu in Mannar town Divisional Secretary's Division in Mannar District of Northern Province.

North: Suvamithotam (part of same land) and Road (R. D. A.) ;East: Suvamithotam (part of same land) ;South: Suvamithotam (part of same land) and Coastal Reservation ;West: Suvamithotam (part of same land).
