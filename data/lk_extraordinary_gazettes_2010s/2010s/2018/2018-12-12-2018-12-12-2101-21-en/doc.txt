LAND ACQUISITION ACT, (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Ref. No.: KDS/PDB/4/3/2/6.

Land Ministry Ref. No.: 4-3/9/2014/WS/144.

I do hereby give notice under sub section (1) of Section 7 ofthe Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends toacquire the following land which is required for a publicpurpose.

Description of the Land to be Acquired :

The allotment of land in total extent of about 0.0625Hectares. depicted in the Preliminary Plan Maha/6776surveyed and prepared by the Senior Surveyor Superintendentof Kandy on behalf of the Surveyor General and situated inthe village of Paranagama in Pallegampaha Korale minordivision of Pathadumbara Division in Kandy District..

Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Bogahamudune watte alias NaidageHena alias Jambugolla Watte; Name and Address of theclaimant: R. M. Praneeth Priyanka, No. 33, Dambagaha-muduna, Jambugahapitiya; Extent: 0.0625 Hectare.

Boundaries:

North:Lot Nos. S469, E468 and D468 of Preliminary Plan3176; East: Lot No. D468, E468 and T469 of Preliminary Plan3176; South: Lot No. T469 and S469 of Preliminary Plan 3176;West: Lot No. S469 of Preliminary Plan 3176.

I do hereby give public notice that all persons interested inthe aforesaid lands are required to appear before mepersonally, or by agent duly authorized in writing at theDivisional Secretariat, Pathadumbara, on the 30th day ofJanuary 2019, at 10.00 a.m. and they are hereby requested tonotify to me in writing with duplicates on or before the 23rdday of January, 2019 the nature of their interests in theaforesaid land and the amount and the particulars of theirclaims for compensation and the details of the computationof such amount.
