NOTIFICATION

IT is hereby notified that His Excellency, the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces has been graciously pleased to confer the Vishista Sewa Vibhushanaya at an Investiture to beheld at the President Secretariat on the Nineteenth day of May in the year Two Thousand and Eighteen on the officersnamed in the Schedule hereto in terms of Clause Four of the Presidential Warrant instituting the Vishista Sewa Vibhushanayapublished in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 156/5 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka issued on the Firstday of September in the year One Thousand Nine Hundred and Eighty One.

By His Excellency's command

AUSTIN FERNANDO,Secretary to the President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo,18th May, 2018
