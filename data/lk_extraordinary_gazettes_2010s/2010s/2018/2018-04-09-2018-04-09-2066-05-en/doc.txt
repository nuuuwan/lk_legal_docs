BANK OF CEYLON ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 397)

Order under Section 7(2)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Subsection (2) of Section 7 of the Bank of Ceylon Ordinance (Chapter 397), I,Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order determine that the paid-up capital of theBank of Ceylon shall be increased from rupees Fifteen Billion to rupees Twenty Billion.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Colombo,05th April 2018.
