Notification

IT is hereby notified that whereas the Subjects and functions and departments, state corporations and statutory institutionsto be assigned to the Ministers appointed under Article 43 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka have been determined ;

the subjects, functions, departments, state corporations and statutory institutions under the charge of the saidMinisters shall be as set out in the Schedule given below with effect from the date of this Notification.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA,President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,November 09, 2018.
