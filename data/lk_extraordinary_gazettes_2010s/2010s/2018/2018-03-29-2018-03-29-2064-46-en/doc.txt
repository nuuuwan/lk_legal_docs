FINANCE BUSINESS ACT, No. 42 OF 2011

CANCELLATION of a licence to carry on finance business under Section 37 of the Finance Business Act, No. 42 of 2011.

DR. INDRAJIT COOMARASWAMY,Chairman of the Monetary Boardand the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The public is hereby informed that in terms of Section 37 of the Finance Business Act, No. 42 of 2011, theMonetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, has decided to cancel the licence issued to Central Investments andFinance PLC under the said Finance Business Act with effect from 5th March 2018. Accordingly, Central Investementsand Finance PLC is not permitted to carry on finance business with effect from such date.
