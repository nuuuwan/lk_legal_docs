SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, MangalaSamaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this orderimpose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, a Special Commodity Levy atthe rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from August 25th 2018, subject to provisions ofSub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2075/31 of 14.06.2018 in respect of Coconut Kernel classifiable under the HSCode 0801.19.90 and Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2073/37 of 31.05.2018 in respect of Vegetable/PalmOils classifiable under the HS Headings 15.07, 15.11, 15.12 and 15.13 are hereby rescinded with effect from August 25,2018.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,24th August, 2018.
