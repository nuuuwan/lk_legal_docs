EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MANTHAI WEST PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) (a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Manthai West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the United National Party the recognized Political Party to which AbdulHameedu Sanoos the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 04, Vidataltivu of Manthai West Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall bedeclared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Saveriyan Ferdinant to fill such vacancy ;

Now therefore I. Samarasinghe Appuhamilage Darsha Praveena, Returning Officer of the Manthai WestPradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter262) do hereby declare that Saveriyan Ferdinant was selected as the member of the Ward No. 04, Vidataltivu ofManthai West Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. A. DARSHA PRAVEENA,Returning Officer,Manthai West Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Mannar,21st June, 2018.
