INLAND REVENUE ACT, No. 24 OF 2017

Notice under Sections 18

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 18(4) of the Inland Revenue Act, No. 24 of 2017 in relation to thecircumstances in which losses on financial instruments may only be set against gains on financial instruments, I, DisanayakeMudiyanselage Lalith Ivan Disanayake, Commissioner General of Inland Revenue, do by this notice specify any circumstancein which loss on a financial instrument is occurred as the circumstance in which loss on a financial instrument set againstthe gain from same type of financial instrument.

D. M. L. I. DISANAYAKE,Commissioner General of Inland Revenue.

Department of Inland Revenue,Colombo 02,01st April 2018.
