SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

Order under Section 32 and 33

I, Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports in pursuance of powers vested in meunder Section 32(b) and 33 of Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 do hereby suspend with immediate effect the registration of theSri Lanka Aquatic Sports Union which is the National Association for Aquatic Sports and appoint the following InterimCommittee as a provisional arrangement to continue activities of the said Association.

1.Mr. S. R. W. M. R. P. Sathkumara-(Chairman)2.Mr. Ashane Fernando-(Member)3.Mr. W. Piyal Hewage-(Member)

Moreover, I do hereby appoint Director General of the Department of Sports Development as the Competent Authority witheffect from 16th November 2018, to appoint the Election Committee and make necessary arrangement to conduct the electionin terms of the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2089/56 dated 22nd September 2018 published in order to convene and conduct theelection of the Sri Lanka Aquatic Sports Union.

FAISZER MUSTHAPHA (President’s Counsel),Minister of Provincial Councils,Local Government and Sports.

Ministry of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports,Colombo,16th November 2018.
