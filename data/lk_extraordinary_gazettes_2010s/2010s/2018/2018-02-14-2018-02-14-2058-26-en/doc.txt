MEDIATION (SPECIAL CATEGORIES OF DISPUTES) ACT, No. 21 OF 2003

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Mediation (Special Categories of Disputes) Act,No. 21 of 2003, I Thalatha Athukorale, Minister of Justice, do by this Order Specify the area set out in the Schedulehereto, as Special Mediation Board Area within which the settlement of disputes relating to the possession or ownershipof lands as determined by Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1901/ 8 of February 10, 2015 shall becarried out, under the provisions of such Act.

SCHEDULE

Each Administrative District of Mullaitivu, Vavuniya, Mannar and Ampara.

THALATHA ATHUKORALE,

Minister of Justice.
