FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GALLE MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Galle Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which KariyawasamThelikada Gamage Thisara Amarathunga the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaidOrdinance, to decide upon returned member if People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within aperiod of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Keerthi Kumara Seneviratne to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Jayasinghe Arachchillage Samantha Piyaleel Jayasinghe, returning officer of the GalleMunicipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Keerthi Kumara Seneviratne was selected as the member of the Galle Municipal Council.

J. A. S. P. JAYASINGHE,Returning Officer,Galle Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Galle,09th November, 2018,
