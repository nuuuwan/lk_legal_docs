THE LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 28

OF 1964 (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

DIK/LAN/02/46.

I, hereby give notice, under Sub section (1) of Section 7 ofLand Acquisition Act, No. 28 of 1964 (Amendment) that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land which isrequired for a public purpose.

Details of the land to be Obtained :

Following land mentioned in the preliminary plan No. 2754of Surveyor General and situated in Village of WalasgalaGrama Niladhari Division of Dickwella North in DickwellaDivisional Secretariat Matara District.

Lot No. 01Name of Land: Willapaththinigahawaththa alias Mahahena;Description: Home Garden, Mixed Cultivation is there; Claimedby: M. M. Leelarathna, Dambagasara, Walasgala, Dickwella;Extent: 0.2914 Hectare.

All Persons interested in the aforesaid land is herebyrequired to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing before me at Dickwella Divisional Secretary's Officeon 09th January, 2019 at 10.30 a.m. are hereby requested tonotify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before 30thDecember, 2018 the nature of their interest in the land, theparticulars of there claims for compensation for the acquisitionof the said land the amount of compensation and the detailsof the computation of such amount. All deeds and documentsin support of the claims should be produced before me at theinquiry.

B. S. RANJITHA,Divisional Secretary,Divisional Secretariat Dickwella.
