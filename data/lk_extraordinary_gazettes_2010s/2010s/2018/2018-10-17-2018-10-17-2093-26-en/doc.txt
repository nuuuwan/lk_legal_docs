AMENDING THE NAME OF THE INSTITUTE OF POST HARVEST TECHNOLOGY

THE name of the Institute of Post Harvest Technology established in terms of Gazette Extraordinary No. 1137/10 dated19 th June 2000 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under State Agricultural Corporation Act “No. 11 of1972 ” and being operated under the purview of the Ministry of Agriculture is hereby amended as “National Post HarvestManagement Institute” as per approval granted on 21.05.2018 by the Cabinet of Ministers of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka.

B. WIJAYARATHNE,

Secretary,Ministry of Agriculture.

October 12, 2018.
