NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO PROPERTY OF INTESTATE

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT NO. 21 OF 1998

(SECTION 55)

Reference the intestate property of late Mr. Buddhika Asanga Neththikumara who resided in the village of Kirimatiya, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Four Gravets and Gangawatakorale, in the District of Kandy. It is hereby notified that any person/s who have a claim to or interest in the intestate property of the late Mr. Buddhika Asanga Neththi-kumara described in the Schedule hereto should submit his/their claim to the undersigned in writing before 26th October, 2018 stating his/their right or interest to such intestate property. K.A.K.RANJITH DHARMAPALA, Commissioner of Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Madura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla. 11th September, 2018.
