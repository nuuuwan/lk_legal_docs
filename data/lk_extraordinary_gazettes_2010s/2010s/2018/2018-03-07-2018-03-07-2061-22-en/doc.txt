SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, EranWickramaratne, Acting Minister of Finance and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by thisorder impose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special CommodityLevy at the rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from March 08, 2018 subject to provisons ofSub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

ERAN WICKRAMARATNE,Acting Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,07th March, 2018.
