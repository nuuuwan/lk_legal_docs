Provincial Councils Notifications

SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Pawnbrokers' Statute No. 01 of 2013 of the Southern Provincial Council

I, Gusthingnawadu Shan Wijayalal De Silva, Chief Minister and the Minister incharge of Finance do hereby order the belowmentioned orders as to be effected from midnight on 31st October, 2018/ 01.11.2018 in terms of the powers assigned to me underthe section 6 (3) of the Pawnbrokers' Statute No. 01 of 2013 of the Southern Provincial Council.

ORDER

ANNUAL LICENCE FEE FOR RENEWING PAWNBROKING BUSINESS - RS. 5000.00

02. Application of the fee of Rs. 1000/- which had been enacted as the annual license fee for renewing pawnbrokingbusiness in terms of the provisions laid down by the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2040/ 39 dated 31st of October,2013 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka shall be invalidated since the said provision is scheduled to be effectivefrom midnight on 31st of October, 2018/ 01.11.2018 and this shall only be applicable on the pawnbroking license fee emergedthereafter.

GUSTHINGNAWADU SHAN WIJAYALAL DE SILVA, Chief Minister and Minister of Finance and Planning,

Law and Order, Engineering Services, Health and Indigenous Medicine, Local Government and Transport &

Tourism of the Southern Provincial Council.

At the Chief Minister' s Office,Galle.01st day of June 2018.
