Proclamations & C., by the President

PRESIDENTIAL DIRECTIVE

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA

WHEREAS, in pursuance of powers vested in me by Article 33 (2) of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka,National Salaries and Cadre Commission was established by the Presidential Directive on March 03, 2016

Whereas, the time given for the completion of the functions and responsibilities assigned to the said Commissionwill expire on March 02, 2018,

And whereas, since the need for the said Commission to be continued further has been identified,

I, Maithripala Sirisena, President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby enlarge the timefor completion of the said functions and responsibilities of the said Commission by further two years from 03.03.2018.

Given at Colombo under the seal of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, on this 28th day ofFebruary, 2018.

By His Excellency's Command,

SUMITH ABEYSINGHE,Acting Secretary to the President.
