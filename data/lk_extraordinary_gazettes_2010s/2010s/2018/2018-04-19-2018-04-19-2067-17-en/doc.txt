PART 1: SECTION (1) - GENERAL

UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I Kabir Hashim, the Minister ofHigher Education and Highways, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act in respect of the NationalSchool of Business Management Limited, a Company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 andbearing Registration Number PB 4833, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made under the said Act, andpublished in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2053/40 of January 11, 2018 by the addition immediately after the entry "Bachelor ofScience Honours in Computer System Engineering" appearing in column I and it's corresponding entry in Column II respectivelyof the Schedule II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and Column II respectively of the Schedule II hereto,subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

KABIR HASHIM,

Minister of Higher Education and Highways.

Colombo ,18th April 2018.
