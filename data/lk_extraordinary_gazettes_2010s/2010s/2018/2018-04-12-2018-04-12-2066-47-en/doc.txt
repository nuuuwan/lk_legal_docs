CEYLON ELECTRICITY BOARD ACT, No. 17 OF 1969

Order under Section 57

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 57 of the Ceylon Electricity Board Act, No. 17 of 1969, I, RanjithSiyambalapitiya, Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, do by this order, approve the proposed acquisition of landsrequired by the Ceylon Electricity Board and described in the Schedule hereto.
