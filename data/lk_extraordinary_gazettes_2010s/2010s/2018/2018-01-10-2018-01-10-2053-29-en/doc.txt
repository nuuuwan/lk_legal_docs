01. Lot No. 01Name of the Land: Kandhewaththa; Description: Scrub;Claimant:Y. M. Muthumenika, Y. M. Heen Banda, Y. M.Abeyrathna, Mangoda, Medhamahanuwara; Extent: 2.8448Hectares.
