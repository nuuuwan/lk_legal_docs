Notice

BY virtue of the powers vested in me in the Minister in charge of the subject, under Section 289(1) of Municipal CouncilOrdinance (Chapter 252) that should be read with Section 2 of Provincial Council (Incidental Provisions) Act, No. 12 of1989, I, Marappulige Jayasinghe, Governor of the North Central Province, do hereby make regulation pertaining to thefollowing allowances to the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Member of Municipal Council. These regulations shall be effectedfrom 01st of July 2018.

MARAPPULIGE JAYASINGHE,Governor of North Central Province,North Central Province.

Governor’s Office,North Central Province,13th June, 2018.

02. Travelling expenses and subsistence :

I.A sum of Rs. not more than 500.00 subsistence for one day can be paid for Mayor of the MunicipalCouncil subjected to the Provisions of Establishment Code and these allowances can be paid maximum10 days for a month.

II.Subsistence allowances are paid only duties out of Municipal Council limit. These allowances shouldbe paid incompliance with the provisions of Establishment Code.

III.These allowances will not be paid for travelling within the Municipal Council limits.

IV.Commuted travelling allowances and subsistence are allowed only for the official visit of respectiveMunicipal Council. Except, having an official invitation for function seminar or meeting. It is notentitled to obtain travelling expenses or subsistence under Municipal Council fund.

03. Refreshment Allowances :

I.A monthly allowances of Rs. 3000.00 can be paid to the Mayor of Municipal Council as refreshmentallowance. The expenditure Head should be submitted for the purpose.

II.A monthly allowance of Rs. 750.00 can be paid to the Deputy Mayor of Municipal Council asrefreshment allowance. The Expenditure Head should be submitted for the purpose.

III.For the Members of Municipal Council participation for meeting of Municipal Council each memberfor each meeting could be spent Rs. 100.00 under Municipal Council Fund.

04. Telephone Allowance :

I.Permission will be given to pay following allowances to the Mayor and Deputy Mayor for the residencetelephone under Municipal Council Fund.

II.To the Mayor for a month Rs. 4000.00

III.To Deputy Mayor for a month Rs. 2500.00

05. Attendence allowances for Municipal Council and Committee Meetings

I.An allowance of Rs. 500.00 can be paid to the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Members of MunicipalCouncil for the attendence of one meeting under Municipal Council Fund.

06. Allowancs to personal staff of the Mayor of Municipal Council :

I.The Mayor of the Municipal Council can appoint a Personal Assistant and a maximum amount ofRs. 15,000.00 could be paid for him/her under Municipal Council Fund.

II.The Mayor of the Municipal Council can appoint a Public Relation Officer and a maximum amount ofRs. 10,000.00 could be paid for him/her under Municipal Council Fund.

NORTH CENTRAL PROVINCE

Notice

BY virtue of the powers vested in me in the Minister in charge of the subject, under Section 184(1)(c)(d)(f) of PradeshiyaSabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 that should be read with Section 2 of Provincial Council (Incidential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of1989, I, Marappulige Jayasinghe, Governor of the North Central Province, do hereby make regulation pertaining to thefollowing allowances to the Chairman, Vice Chairman amd Members of Pradeshiya Sabha. These regulations shall beeffected from 01st of July 2018.

MARAPPULIGE JAYASINGHE,Governor of North Central Province,North Central Province.

Governor’s Office,North Central Province,13th June, 2018.

REGULATIONS

01. Fuel limits to Chairman of Pradeshiya Sabha for official visit of Pradeshiya Sabha :

I.If vehicle of Pradeshiya Sabha is used and for a Pradeshiya Sabha comprise of one DivisionalSecretariat, not more than 400 liters diesel for a period of one month, or

II.The expenses may be incurred for not more than 500 liters diesel for one month for a PradeshiyaSabha comprise of two Divisional Secretariats.

III.In the event of non-availability of Pradeshiya Sabha vehicles, personal vehicles could be utilized underthe approval of Minister incharge of the subject within the said fuel limitation and expenditure couldbe incurred under Pradeshiya Sabha Fund.

02. Travelling expenses and subsistence :

I.A sum of Rs. not more than 500.00 subsistence for one day can be paid for Chairman of the PradeshiyaSabha subject to the Provisions of Establishment Code and these allowances can be paid maximum 10days for a month.

II.Substistence allowances are paid only duties out of Pradeshiya Sabha limit. These allowances shouldbe paid incompliance with the provisions of Establishment Code.

III.These allowances will not be paid for travellings within the Pradeshiya Sabha limits.

IV.Commuted travelling allowances and subsistence are allowed only for the official visit of respectivePradeshiya Sabha. Except, having an official invitation for function seminar or meeting. It is not entitledto obtain travelling expenses or subsistence under Pradeshiya Sabha fund.

03. Refreshment Allowances :

I.A monthly allowances of Rs. 2000.00 can be paid to the chairman of Pradeshiya Sabha as refreshmentallowance. The expenditure Head should be submitted for the purpose.
