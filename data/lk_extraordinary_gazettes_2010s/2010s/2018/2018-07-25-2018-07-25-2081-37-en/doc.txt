FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF WELIMADA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member in Welimada Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the People’s Liberation Front, the recognized political party to which WijesekaraMudiyanselage Gunawardhana the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance,to decide upon returned member People’s Liberation Front who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty(30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateDissananayake Mudiyanselage Ajith Kumara to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Hathurusinghalage Indika Ranjan Hathurusinghe, returning officer of Welimada PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Dissananayake Mudiyanselage Ajith Kumara was selected as the member of the Welimada Pradeshiya Sabha.

H. I. R. HATHURUSINGHE,Returning Officer,Welimada Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Badulla,24th July, 2018,
