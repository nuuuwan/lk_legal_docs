PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS ACT, No. 2 OF 1988

Filling of a Vacancy in the Western Province Provincial Council

WHEREAS the Secretary of the Western Province Provincial Council has informed the Election Commission underSection 65(1) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988 that a vacancy has occurred in the membership ofthe Western Province Provincial Council by reason of resignation ;

And whereas the Secretary of the United Progressive Alliance, the recognized political party to which the memberbelonged was required by the Election Commission under Section 65(2) of the aforesaid Act, to nominate an eligibleperson to fill such vancancy ;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Pary, has nominated Mr. S. Kuhawaradan to fillsuch vacancy ;

The Election Commission, acting in terms of Section 65(2) of the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of1988, read with the Nineteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, dohereby declare Mr. S. Kuhawaradan, elected as a member of the above Provincial Council from the Administrative Districtof Colombo in respect of which such vacancy occurred.

MAHINDA DESHAPRIYAN. J. ABEYESEKERES. RATNAJEEVAN H. HOOLEChairmanMemberMemberElection CommissionElection CommissionElection Commission
