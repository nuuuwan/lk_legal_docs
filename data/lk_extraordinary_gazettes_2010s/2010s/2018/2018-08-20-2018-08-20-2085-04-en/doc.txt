Land Reform Commission Notices

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 29 OF THE LAND REFORM LAW No. 1 OF 1972 AS AMENDED BY LANDREFORM (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 39 OF 1981 AND LAND REFORM (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)ACT, No. 14 OF 1986

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under the Section 29 of the Law, I hereby, direct every person who is interested in anyagricutlural land or lands described in the Schedule hereto immediately before the date on which such land or lands havebeen vested in the Land Reform Commission to make, within one month from 20.09.2018 a written claim in triplicate to thewhole or any part of the compensation payable under the Law in respect of such land or lands and to specify in the claim.

a)His name and addressb)Nature of his interest in such land/landsc)Particulars of his claim andd)How much of such compensation is claimed by him

All claims should be sent by registered post addressed to the Chairman Land Reform Commission.

SAMPATH SUBASINGHE ARACHCHI,Chairman,Land Reform Commission.

Office of the Land Reform Commission,No. 475,Kaduwela Road,Battaramulla,16th August, 2018.
