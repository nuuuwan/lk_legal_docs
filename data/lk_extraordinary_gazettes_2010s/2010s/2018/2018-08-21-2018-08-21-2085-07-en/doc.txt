FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF VALIKAMAM SOUTH PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which RasaKumarasami the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decideupon the member of that 12-Chunnakam South if Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is notdisqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared elected as a memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominateRaththinasingam Kenkatharan to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, returning officer of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha, actingin terms of selection 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatRaththinasingam Kenkatharan was elected as the member of the 12-Chunnakam South of Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Jaffna,20th August, 2018.
