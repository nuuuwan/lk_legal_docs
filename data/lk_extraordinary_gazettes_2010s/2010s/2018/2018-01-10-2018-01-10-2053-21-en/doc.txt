EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 01/2018

BUSINESS HOURS FOR LIQUOR LICENSED ESTABLISHMENTS

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section (1) of Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended fromtime to time, I, Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media do by this Order declare that business hoursof the liquor licensed establishments set out in the Schedule hereto shall come into force with effect from 11 January,2018.

The Excise Notification No. 675 published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 88/7 dated 14 May 1980 is hereby rescinded.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,09 January 2018.
