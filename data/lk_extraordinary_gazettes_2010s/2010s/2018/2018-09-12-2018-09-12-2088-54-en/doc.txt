PART IV (A) – PROVINCIAL COUNCILS

Provincial Councils Notifications

CENTRAL PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Gampola Urban Council

ENTERTAINMENT TAXATION

ACCORDING to the powers conferred in (Chapter 267) Second Part (1) Sub-section of Entertainment Tax Ordinance,under-mentioned resolution determined by the Secretary of Gampola Urban Council which was approved by the Minister incharge for Local Government, Central Provincial Council as per the powers specified in Section II of the Ordinance onEntertainment Taxation is hereby published based on its second Sub-section.

R. M. P. S. RATHNAYAKE,Secretary,Chief, Ministry of Education and Local Government,Central Province.

At Pallekele,14th of February, 2018.

RESOLUTION

On the powers conferred to me in Section 27(3) of Urban Council Act, when it is held to conduct the entertainment programsin the Gampola Urban Council Authority as specified in Section III of the Ordinance on Entertainment Taxation, it has beendetermined to impose an Entertainment tax of 10% when the entrance fee is between Rs. 1.00 to Rs. 200, 12% when the entrancefee is between Rs. 201 to Rs. 500, 15% when the entrance fee is over Rs. 501 and 7.5% of the value of the ticket when oncinematic view as per the Section (2) 1 of No. 12 of 1946 Tax Ordinance and Section 37 of the 1984 Tax Amendment Act.
