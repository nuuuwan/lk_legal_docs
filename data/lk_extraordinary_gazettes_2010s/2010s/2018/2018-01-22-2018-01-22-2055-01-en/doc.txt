THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) ASAMENDED BY THE LAND ACQUISITION (AMENDMENT)

ACT, No. 28 OF 1964

Notice under Section 7

I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the LandAcquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition(Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends toacquire the following land which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the land /servitude to be acquired:

An allotment of land depicted as Lot Nos. 01 and 02 in SurveyorGeneral's preliminary Plan No. G 3734 and situated in the Village ofNugethota in the Divisional Secretary's Division ofWelivitiya-Divithura in Galle District, Southern Province.

01. Lot No. 01Name of Land: Polgahavila Group Thuththudoova ; Description:There are 42 buildings with gardens; Extent: 1.9609 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 02Name of Land: Kohiladeniya Kumbura; Description:Open bare land; Extent: 0.0912 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land /lands onwhich servitude is to be acquired* are hereby requiredto appear personally, or by agents duly authorised inwriting, before me at The Divisional SecretariatWelivitiya - Divithura on the 08th day of February 2018at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify to me inwriting (in duplicate) on or before the 01st day ofFebruary 2018, the nature of their interests in the land,the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount ofcompensation and the details of the Computation of suchamount.

A. M. A. U. G. KARIYAVASAM,

Divisional Secretary,Welivitiya-Divithura.

Divisional Secretariat,Welivitiya - Divithura,12th January, 2018.
