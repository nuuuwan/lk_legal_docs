THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notification

WHEREAS, I have, in terms of paragraphs (2) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic ofSri Lanka, determined the ministry and the subjects and functions assigned to the State Minister of National Unity & Co-existence appointed by me as a Minister who is not a member of the Cabinet of Ministers in the exercise of powers vestedin me under paragraphs (1) of Article 44 of the Constitution, it is hereby notified that the subjects and functions of theaforesaid Ministry and the Departments, State Corporations and Statutory Institutions pertaining to the said Ministry shallbe as set out in the schedule hereto from May 11, 2018.

MAITHRIPALA SIRISENA, President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo - 01,May 28, 2018.
