FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER

SECTION 66A(1) (1a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 292)

WHEREAS the office of Member in Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of Independent Group No. 02, to which MAYINATHTHUGE Rasika Udeni the Member electedto Ward No. 07 Pamunuwa of Maharagama Urban Council belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of theaforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 07 Pamunuwa of Maharagama Urban Council who belongsto such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 and is Qualified under section 8 of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as member within a period of thirty (30)days;

AND WHEREAS the Leader of aforesaid Independent Group No. 02 has communicated his decision to nominateHeenatigala Madinage Rasika to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sudu Dewage Shanika Anuruddha Rupasinghe, Returning Officer of Maharagama Urban Council,acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (1a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatHeenatigala Madinage Rasika was elected as the Member of the Ward No. 07 Pamunuwa of Maharagama Urban Council.

S. D. S. A. RUPASINGHE,

Returning Officer,Maharagama Urban Council.

District Election Office,Colombo,20th June 2018.
