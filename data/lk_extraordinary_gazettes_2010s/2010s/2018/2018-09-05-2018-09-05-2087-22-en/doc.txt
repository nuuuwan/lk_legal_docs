EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KEGALLE PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna the recognized political party to which KaluArachchilalage Janaranjana Saman Kumara Kalu Arachchi the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1)(a)of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that 14-Meedeniya of Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declaredelected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Dapanage Nisala Sanjeewa to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that DapanageNisala Sanjeewa was elected as the member of the No. 14 - Meedeniya of Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Kegalle Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kegalle,04th September, 2018.
