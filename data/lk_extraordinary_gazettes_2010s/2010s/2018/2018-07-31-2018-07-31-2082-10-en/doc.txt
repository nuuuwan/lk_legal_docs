EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, as amended,Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Finance and Mass Media, do by this Order declare that ;

(i) The rate of Excise duty applicable on every article specified in the Columns I, II and III of the Schedule A hereto,shall replace corresponding every article specified under the Schedule I of the Extraordinary Gazette NotificationNo. 2044/32 dated November 09, 2017, issued under the Section 3 of this Act, as specified in the correspondingentry in Column IV of the Schedule A with effect from August 01, 2018.

(ii) However, the rate of Excise duty applicable on importation of motor vehicles specified in Columns I, II and III ofthe Schedule B hereto, imported based on Letters of Credit (LC) established on or before August 01, 2018 andcleared from Sri Lanka Customs on or before January 31, 2019, shall be as specified in the corresponding entryin Column IV of the Schedule A or B, which ever is lower, hereto.

MANGALA SAMARAWEERA,Minister of Finance and Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01.July 31, 2018.
