The qualifications set out under 7.1 of the Service Minute of the Combined Serviceof Officers in the field of Sports published in the Gazette No. 1009 dated 02 January1998 will be made applicable in place of the education and professionalqualifications referred to under 7.2.3.2.1 of the said Service Minute in therecruitment to the post of Sport Coach in the category of Supervisory ManagementAssistant Technical Service of the Combined Service of Officers in the field ofsports up to 26.09.2021, the date on which the said officers complete five (05)years of service from the date of the said Service Minute takes effect.

Note:

The officers appointed to the post of Sports Coach in terms of interim provisionsreferred to under 13.4 without the completion of qualifications of Technical Diplomarelating to the field of Sports at the level of National Vocational Qualification (NVQ06) recognized by the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission referred tounder 7.2.3.2.1 (ii) of the said Service Minute,

(a)Should complete the qualification referred to under 7.2.3.2.1(ii) of thesaid Service Minute before the completion of a period of five (05)years following the appointment to the post.

(b)The salary increments of the officers who do not complete thequalification as per (a) above will be deferred from the date theycomplete a period of five (05) years up to the date they complete thequalification referred to under 7.2.3.2.1(ii).

The officers who have earned five (05) salary increment having completed a periodof five (05) years of active and satisfactory service in Grade II of the ExecutiveService Category of Combined Service of Officers in the field of Sports and whohave demonstrated a performance at satisfactory level or above during the entireperiod of five (05) years preceding the date of promotion in accordance withapproved procedure of performance appraisal will be promoted to Grade I of theExecutive Service Category according to number of vacancies existing in the postin Grade I of Executive Service Category irrespective of the qualification referredto in 10.6.1.1.(i) and (ii).
