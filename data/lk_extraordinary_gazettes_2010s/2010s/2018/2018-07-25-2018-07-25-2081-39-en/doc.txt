EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF DEHIWALA - MOUNT LAVINIYAMUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Dehiwala - Mount Laviniya Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of death ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to whichHapange Ranjan De Silva the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance,to decide upon the member of that 25 Rathmalana West, if Dehiwala - Mount Laviniya Municipal Council who belongs tosuch party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and whoshall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party, has communicated his decision tonominate Rambukkana Maggonage Asanga Padmal Perera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Pathiraja Arachchillage Sunil Senarathna, Returning Officer of Dehiwala - Mount LaviniyaMunicipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) dohereby declare that Rambukkana Maggonage Asanga Padmal Perera was elected as the member of the 25 Rathmalana Westof Dehiwala - Mount Laviniya Municipal Council.

P. A. S. SENARATHNAReturning Officer,Dehiwala - Mount Laviniya Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Colombo City,25th July, 2018.
