PENSION MINUTE

Notification under Section 51 of the Pensions Minute dated 05th February 1934 revised by

the Pension Minute (Amendment) Act, No. 13 of 1948

NOTIFICATION

THE Pension Minute is further revised with the inclusion of new Sections mentioned below with the removal of therevision which has been made including Sub Section I by the Gazette notification of Democratic Socialist Republic of SriLanka No. 1750/1 dated 19th March, 2012, after Section 02 of the Pension Minute dated 05.02.1934, which has beenrevised from time to time. Provisions of the Minute under Section 2 and 3 shall further remain unchanged.

01. (a)When the pension of the retired Government Officers is revised re-employing them on contract basis orexigency of service on or after 19th March, 2012, the total gratuity paid to them previously shall be recoveredfrom the new award granted to them.

(b)When the pension of Officers who have been re-employed before 19th March, 2012 is revised the balance ofthe gratuity excluding the value of the gratuity recovered up to the relevant date from the gratuity paidaccording to the award made to them previously should be deducted from the gratuity which is entitled afterthe revision and if there is any amount to be paid back it should be paid after making necessary adjustments.

R. M. RANJITH MADDUMABANDARA,
