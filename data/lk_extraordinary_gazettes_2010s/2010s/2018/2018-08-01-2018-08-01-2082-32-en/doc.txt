L.D.B. 21/78(xv)

THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. WijayadasaRajapaksa, Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission,do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order made under Section 21 of the said Act, published in GazetteExtraordinary No. 896/2 of November 07, 1995, and last amended by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary,No. 2056/5 of January 29, 2018, establishing the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, by the addition immediatelyafter the item ‘‘Faculty of Technology’’ in Column I of the Schedule and its corresponding entries in Column II of theSchedule of the following item and entries :-

‘‘Faculty of Medicine1.Department of Anatomy2.Department of Biochemistry3.Department of Physiology4.Department of Parasitology5.Department of Microbiology6.Department of Forensic Medicine7.Department of Pathology8.Department of Community Medicine9.Department of Family Medicine10.Department of Pharmacology11.Department of Surgery12.Department of Medicine13.Department of Pediatrics14.Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology15.Department of Psychiatry.’’.

Dr. WIJAYADASA RAJAPAKSA,Minister of Higher Education and Cultural Affairs.

Colombo,31st July, 2018.
