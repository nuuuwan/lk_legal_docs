SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY ACT, No. 48 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, EranWickramaratne, Acting Minister of Finance and Mass Media, the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by thisorder impose in respect of the list of commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special CommodityLevy, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid for a period of 06 months commencing from May 08, 2018 subject to provisions of Subsection (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

The Order made under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 and published in theExtraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2044/26 of 08.11.2017 in respect of Sprats and Dried Fish is rescinded witheffect from May 08, 2018.

ERAN WICKRAMARATNE,Acting Minister of Finance amd Mass Media.

Ministry of Finance and Mass Media,Colombo 01,04th May, 2018.
