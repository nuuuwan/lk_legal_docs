This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk1A G 28481 — 22 (09/2018)

Amending clauses of the Central Province Road Development Authority Statute No. 02 of year2003 published in Gazette No. 1 2007, 510/ 43 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

by the Provincial Council Central Province

I, Ediriweera Weerawardena, Minister of Road Development, Tranport, Power and Energy, Housing and Construction of theCentral Provincial Council hereby announce under the powers vested in me by the clause No. 22 (1) of the Section V of theCentral Provincial Road Development Authority Statute No. 02 of year 2003 of the Central Provincial Council, clause 10 (4)and clause 10 (1) of part III of the Central Province Road Development Authority Statute No. 02, 2003 published on theGazette published on 17 th August 2007 by Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka will come into force with effectfrom 01.09.2018 as mentioned below :-

ClauseExisting clauseA General Manager for the Authority should be appointed for the Authority10(1)with the approval of the Minister by the Authority

To be amended asA person fulfill required qualifications should be appointed as the GeneralManager according to the approved Recruiting Procedure and an approvalof the Director Board

ClauseExisting clause The General Manager should not be removed from the post without a prior10(4)written approval from the Minister

To be amended asClause 4 will be removed

EDIRIWEERA WEERAWARDENA,Minister of Road Development, Tranport, Power and

Energy, Housing and Construction

Central Province.
