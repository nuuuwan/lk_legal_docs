Government Notifications

ELECTIONS COMMISSION

Notice of the Election Commission

LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS - 10TH FEBRUARY 2018

IT is hereby informed that the results issued by Returning Officers of each Local Authority giving the number of votesreceived by each recognized political party and independent group contested for 340 Local Authorities, of which theelection was held on the 10th February 2018, the number of members from each recognized political party/independentgroup elected at ward level and the total number of members to be returned by each recognized political party/independentgroup, are being exhibited at relevant District Election Offices, local authorities and in the website of the ElectionsCommission.

Further, it is informed that these results have also been issued to each political party and independent group.
