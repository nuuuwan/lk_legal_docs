PILGRIMAGE ORDINANCE

33rd Higher Ordination Ceremony of Sri Rohana Maha Sangasabha of SyamopaliWanshika Maha Nikaya

IT is hereby informed that the 33rd Higher Ordination Ceremony of Sri Rohana Sanga Sabha in Divisional Secretary’sDivision of Kamburupitiya, Matara District will be held on 22nd and 23rd of April 2018 at Rohana Sanga Sabha Headquartersof Siri Rathanajothi Temple of Urapola and pilgrims should refrain from carrying with them any plastic or polythene materialsand standing orders and regulations under Section 2 of the Pilgrimage Ordinance (Chapter 175 - L. D. B. 11/ 60) declarade on14th October 1991 and published in the Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, will be in force during theperiod in which the ceremony is held.

NILMINI HERATH,Divisional Secretary,Kamburupitiya.

At Divisional Secretariat of Kamburupitiya,10th April 2018.
