EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WARAKAPOLA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the United Peoples Freedom Alliance, the recognized political party to whichBope Arachchilage Chandana Kirthisiri Wijethunga the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66 A (1) of theaforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member if United Peoples Freedom Alliance who belongs to such party andis not disqualified under Section 9 of the local authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as amember within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated his decision to nominateKarunanayaka Thenannehelage Ananda Karunanayake to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, Sajith Wasantha Welgama, Returning Officer of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha, acting interms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that KarunanayakaThenannehelage Ananda Karunanayake was selected as the member of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha.

SAJITH WASANTHA WELGAMA,Returning Officer,Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Kegalle,03rd December 2018.
