THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE RETAIL & WHOLESALE TRADE

IT hereby notified under regulation 30 of the Wages Boards Regulations published in Gazette No. 14961 of 04.06.1971that the Hon. Minister of Labour, Trade Union Relations and Sabargamuwa Development has been pleased to appoint thefollowing persons to be members of the Wages Board for the Retail & Wholesale Trade, by virtue of the powers vestedin the Hon.
