THE CEYLON ELECTRICITY BOARD ACT BEARING No. 17 OF 1969

Order made in terms of Section 57

IN pursuance of the power vested with me as Minister of Power and Renewable Energy by virtue of the Ceylon ElectricityBoard Act bearing No. 17 of 1969, I, Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, hereby grant my approval to acquire the land which needs tobe acquired by the Ceylon Electricity Board and which is described in the Schedule hereto.

RANJITH SIYAMBALAPITIYA,Minister of Power and Renewaable Energy.

30th July, 2018.

SCHEDULE

All that divided and defined allotment of land marked Lot No. 1 depicting in Plan No. 879 dated 28.10.2016 made by aLicensed Surveyor, of the land situated at Batuwangala in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 233- Batuwangala within theDivisional Secretariat - Neluwa in the Administrative District of Galle Southern Province, and bounded in terms of theabove plan as follows :

On the North by:Land owned S. D. L. Albert ;On the East by:Remaining portion of the main land that comes under this land ;On the South by:Remaining portion of the main land that comes under this land ;On the West by:Road reservation of Neluwa - Dellawa Road.
