EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE JA- ELA URBANCOUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Ja- Ela Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the Independent Group, to which Jayasundara Arachchige Chamal Lakshitha themember belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returnedmember of Independent Group who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of thelocal authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominateJayakodi Arachchige Noel Deckster Perera to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Karunasingha Jayarathnage Sampath Madhawa, Returning Officer of the Ja-Ela UrbanCouncil, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatJayakodi Arachchige Noel Deckster Perera was selected as the member of the Ja- Ela Urban Council.

K. J.S. MADHAWA,Returning Officer,Ja- Ela Urban Council.

District Election Office,Gampaha,16th October 2018.
