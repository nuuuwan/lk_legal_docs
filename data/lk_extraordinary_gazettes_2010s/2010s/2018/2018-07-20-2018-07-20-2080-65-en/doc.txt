Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE JAFFNA MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of Member of Jaffna Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending three consecutivemeetings without permission ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Tamil United Liberation Front, the recognized political party to whichSutharsing Vijayakanth the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, todecide upon the member of that 22 Thirunagar if Jaffna Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualifiedunder Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a Memberwithin a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision tonominate Jeyatheeswaran Narmatha to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Thanabalasingam Akilan, returning officer of the Jaffna Municipal Council, acting in termsof Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that JeyatheeswaranNarmatha was elected as the Member of the 22 Thirunagar 0f Jaffna Municipal Council.

T. AKILAN,Returning Officer,Jaffna Municipal Council.

District Election Office,Jaffna,19th July, 2018.
