Regulations

The National Associations of Sports Regulations, No. 01 of 2016, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1990/23 ofOctober 27, 2016 and National Association of Sports Regulations, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2006/13 ofFebruary 13, 2017 are hereby amended as follows :-

(1) By interpolating the following Regulation after Regulation 2 (4) of Sports Regulations published in Gazette

Extraordinary No. 1990/23 dated 27.10.2016.

officer of the Election Department or the member of Election commission or any retired Senior PublicOfficial with experience in conducting elections who are not active members of the said Association inrespect of Grade “A” Category Association.

II. One retired Judge of the High Court and/or the court of Appeal of Sri Lanka ; and two retired senior

Notification 2006/13 of 13.02.2017 thereof the following new regulations.

(d) “On appointment of the Election Committee as stated above nominations shall be called within a period of

not les than two months from the said date of appointment of the Election Committee”.

(e) “The Election Committee shall have the authority to determine the elegibility of all candidates qualified to

be elected as office bearers in terms of Sport Law, the constitution of the respective National Associationof sports & the applicable regulations.”.

(f) “The Election Committee shall have the authority to formulate an eligibility criteria of voters list and

scrutinize the voters list pertaining to their eligibility to cast a vote to elect office bearers, to call nominationand to finalize the same in order to conduct the election process.”.

(g) “The Election Committee shall finalize the voters list within a period of 14 days from the date of

appointment of the said Committee as per regulation (xiv)(d) and inform to all members of the NationalAssociation of Sports.”.

“The Election Committee shall finalize the list of candidates eligible to elect as office bearers at least 21days prior to the date schedule for the election.”.
