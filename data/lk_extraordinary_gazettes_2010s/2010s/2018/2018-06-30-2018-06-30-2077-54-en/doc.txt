CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Waththegama Urban Council

IMPOSING ENTERTAINMENT TAX

IT is hereby informed, according to sub section (2) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance chapter 267, that the following proposalwhich is decided by the secretary of the Waththegama Urban Council, in terms of the provisions of Section 2 (1) of the aboveOrdinance, has been accepted by the Minister in-charge for the subject of local governments of the provincial council of thecentral province, in terms of the provisions of sub section (2) of the said ordinance.

R. M. G. S. RATHNAYAKE,Secretary,Chief, Education and Local Government Ministry,Central Province.

At Pallekele.14th February, 2018.

PROPOSAL

I have decided to levy an Entertainment Tax of 20 % from the ticket fee (with out entertainment tax) within the limits ofthe Waththegama Urban Council, in terms of the powers vested to me according to chapter 255, section 27 (3) of the UrbanCouncil act and in terms of provisions of section 2 (1) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 further amended bythe entertainment tax act No. 37 of 1984.
