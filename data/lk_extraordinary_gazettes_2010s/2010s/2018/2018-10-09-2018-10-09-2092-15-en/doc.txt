FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MANNAR PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Mannar Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party, the recognized political party to which AboobuckerTharseen the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide uponreturned member if Sri Lanka Freedom Party who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of theLocal Authorities Elections Ordinance and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30)days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party, has communicated his decision tonominate Aliyar Sabeer to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE I, Samarasinghe Appuhamilage Darsha Praveena, Returning Officer of the Mannar PradeshiyaSabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare thatAliyar Sabeer was selected as the member of the Mannar Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. A. DARSHA PRAVEENA,Returning Officer,Mannar Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Election Office,Mannar.08th October, 2018.
