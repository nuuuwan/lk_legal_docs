AN ACT TO AMEND THE PRADESHIYA SABHA ACT, NO. 15 OF 1987

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Pradeshiya Sabha(Amendment) Act, No. of 2014 and shall come into operationon such date as the Minister may appoint by Order Publishedin the Gazette.

2.Section 169 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of1987 as amended by Local Authorities (Special Provisions)Act, No. 21 of 2012 is hereby further amended by the repealof the Proviso to that section.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
