AN ACT TO AMEND THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Local AuthoritiesElections (Amendment) Act, No. of 2014 and shall comeinto operation on such date as the Minister may appoint byOrder published in the Gazette.

2.Section 66B of the Local Authorities ElectionsOrdinance (Chapter 262) as last amended by Act, No. 22 of2012 is hereby further amended as follows :—

(1)in subsection (2) of that section by the substitutionfor the words “shall, subject to the provisions ofsubsection (4) of this section, call upon theSecretary” of the words “shall, call upon theSecretary” ;

(2)by the repeal of subsection (4) of that section.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between theSinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, Sinhala text shall prevail.
