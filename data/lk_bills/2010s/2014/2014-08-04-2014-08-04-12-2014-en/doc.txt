AN ACT TO AMEND THE URBAN COUNCILS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 255)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Urban Councils(Amendment) Act, No. of 2014 and shall come intooperation on such date as the Minister may appoint by OrderPublished in the Gazette.

2.Section 178A of the Urban Councils Ordinance(Chapter 255) as amended by the Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 is hereby further amendedby the repeal of the Proviso to that section.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
