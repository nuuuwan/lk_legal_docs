AN ACT TO AMEND THE MUNICIPAL COUNCILS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 252)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Municipal Councils(Amendment) Act, No. of 2014 and shall come intooperation on such date as the Minister may appoint by OrderPublished in the Gazette.

2.Section 215A of the Municipal Councils Ordinance(Chapter 252) as amended by Local Authorities (SpecialProvisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 is hereby further amendedby the repeal of the Proviso to that section.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
