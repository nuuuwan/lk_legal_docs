PART III

AMENDMENTS TO THE PRADESHIYA SABHA ACT

12.Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of1987 (hereinafter in this Part referred to as the “principalenactment”) is hereby repealed and the following section issubtituted therefore:—

4. (1) A Pradeshiya Sabha constituted byan Order under subsection (1) of section 2,shall consist of:—

(a) such number of elected members asdetermined by the Minister by Ordermade under section 3C of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance( Cap. 262); and

(b)such number of other membersnot exceeding thirty per centum of thetotal number of elected members asdetermined under paragraph (a), to bereturned as members under the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance (Cap.262), to represent those electors whohave not secured any representationin the Sabha, at an election held forthe election of members .

(2) Where the number constituting thirtyper centum referred to in paragraph (b) ofsubsection (1) is an integer and fraction, theinteger shall be deemed to be the numberwhich shall constitute such thirty per centum,for the purpose of that subsection.”.
