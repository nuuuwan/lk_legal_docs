AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE PILIYANDALA DHARMASHASTRODAYA

PIRIVEN VIHARASTHA SANWARDHANA SABHAWA

WHEREAS a Sabhawa called and known as the “PiliyandalaDharmashastrodaya Piriven Viharastha SanwardhanaSabhawa” has heretofore been formed at Piliyandala for thepurpose of effectually carrying out its objects and transactingall matters connected with the Sabhawa, according to therules agreed to, by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Sabhawa has applied to beincorporated in order to enable it more effectively to carryout and fulfil the several objects and purposes for which itwas formed and it will be for the public advantage to grantthe application :

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1. This Act may be cited as the PiliyandalaDharmashastrodaya Piriven Viharastha SanwardhanaSabhawa (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2010.

2.From and after the date of commencement of this Act,such and so many persons as now are members of thePiliyandala Dharmashastrodaya Pririven ViharasthaSanwardhana Sabhawa (hereinafter referred to as the“Sabhawa”) or shall hereafter be admitted members of theCorporation hereby constituted, shall be a body corporate(hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”) with perpetualsuccession, under the name and style of the “PiliyandalaDharmashastrodaya Piriven Viharastha SanwardhanaSabhawa” and by that name may sue and be sued in allcourts with full power and authority to have and use acommon seal and to alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to maintain buildings owned by the Viharaya andto construct and maintain new buildings as andwhen necessary;

(b)to conduct activities for the promotion of religiousfavour among Buddhists and the people of the area;

(c)to promte, encourage and assist in, activities for thespiritual, cultural, social and physical welfare of itsmembers;

(d)to safeguard and propagate Buddhism ;

(e)to establish monasteries and forest hermitages andto provide environmental facilities appropriate forthe accomplishment of its objects ;

(f)to ordain youth with the requisite qualificationsand religious devotion and to provide them withthe four requisites;

(g)to preserve and disseminate the TheravadhiBuddhist philosophy and way of life;

(h)to maintain friendly relations with other BuddhistOrganisations, in and outside Sri Lanka;

(i)to open a hospital for sick monks and to providemedical facilities to all the monks leading amonastic life;

(j)to teach the dhamma and the Vinaya to monks, totrain them in yoga exercises and to make themproficient in Pali, Sinhala and oriental and westernlanguages;
