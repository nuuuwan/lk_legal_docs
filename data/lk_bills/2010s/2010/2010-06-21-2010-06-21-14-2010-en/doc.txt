Thidora-Theatre Institute for Disability OrientedResearch and Advocacy (Incorporation)1

AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE THIDORA-THEATRE INSTITUTE FORDISABILITY ORIENTED RESEARCH AND ADVOCACY

WHEREAS an Association called and known as the“Thidora-Theatre Institute for Disability Oriented Researchand Advocacy ” has been formed for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out and transacting all objects and matters connectedwith the said Association according to the rules agreed to byits members :

AND WHEREAS the said Association has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated, and it will be for the public advantage to grantsuch application :

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Thidora-TheatreInstitute for Disability Oriented Research and Advocacy(Incorporation) Act, No. of 2010.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as are now members of the“Thidora-Theatre Institute for Disability Oriented Researchand Advocacy” (hereinafter referred to as “the Institution”)and such other persons as shall hereinafter be admittedas members of the Corporation hereby constituted shallbe a body corporate with perpetual succession, under thename and style of “Thidora-Theatre Institute forDisability Oriented Research and Advocacy” (hereinafterreferred to as “the Corporation”) and by that name maysue and be sued in all courts, with full power and authority tohave and to use a common seal and to alter the same at itswill and pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to promote and enhance the creativity of thedisabled and disadvantaged in Sri Lanka and tofacilitate their integration into the Sri Lankansociety ;

(b)to promote the self respect for the disabled anddesadvantage amongst the Sri Lankan society ;

(c)to act against discriminations against the disabledand disadvantaged in Sri Lanka and to intervenein drafting and implementing laws for theirprotection ; and

(d)to collaborate with other organizations havingsimilar objectives to those of the Corporationwhether in Sri Lanka or abroad.

(2) The founder member of the Institution Mr. RohanaDeva Perera shall be the Chairman of the Board of Directorsfor life.
