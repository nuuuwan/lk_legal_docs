TOBACCO TAX (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Tobacco Tax Act, No. 08 of 1999

To be presented in Parliament by Hon. (Dr) Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, M.P.for Colombo District

(Private Member’s Bill)

1Tobacco Tax (Amendment)

Short title.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE TOBACCO TAX ACT, NO. 08 OF 1999

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Tobacco Tax(Amendment) Act, No. of 2019.

2.Section 15 of the Principal Act is hereby amended byrepealing sub-section 15 (1) of the said Act and by thesubstitution of the following paragraph:-

“15. (1) Every person is guilty of an offence underthis Act shall on conviction after summary trialbefore a Magistrate be liable to a fine of threetimes of the value of the goods forfeited by theDepartment of Customs or the ExciseDepartment under this Act or imprisonment ofeither description for a term not exceeding fiveyears.”

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
