L.D.—O. 58/2019

AN ACT TO AMEND THE APPROPRIATION ACT, NO. 6 OF 2019

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Appropriation(Amendment) Act, No. of 2019.

2.Section 2 of the Appropriation Act, No. 6 of 2019 ishereby amended in paragraph (b) of subsection (1) thereof,by the substitution for the words “rupees two thousandseventy nine billion” of the words “rupees two thousandtwo hundred and seventy nine billion”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
