Amendment ofsection 19 ofAct, No. 42 of1987.

L.D.—O. 64/2016

AN ACT TO AMEND THE PROVINCIAL COUNCILSACT, NO. 42 OF 1987

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Provincial Councils(Amendment) Act, No. of 2019.

2.Section 19 of the Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of1987 is hereby amended by the repeal of paragraph (d) ofsubsection (1) of that section and the substitution of thefollowing paragraph therefor:—

“(d)all other receipts of the Provincial Council otherthan the receipts of the Municipal Councils, UrbanCouncils and the Pradeshiya Sabhas under theMunicipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252),Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255) and thePradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987respectively.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala

and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.

Sinhala text toprevail in caseofinconsistency.
