STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause inserts the new section 68A in the Mortgage Act (Chapter 89), and the legal effect of that section is to limit the applicability of Part III of the Mortgage Act in relation to movable property upon coming into operation of the Secured Transactions Act, No. of 2019.

L.D.—O. 65/2017

AN ACT TO AMEND THE MORTGAGE ACT (CHAPTER 89)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Mortgage (Amendment)Act, No. of 2019 and shall come into operation on suchdate as the Minister may appoint by Order published inthe Gazette.

2.The following new section is hereby insertedimmediately after section 68 of the Mortgage Act(Chapter 89) and shall have effect as section 68A of thatenactment:—

68A. The provisions of Part III of this Actshall only apply to any movable property in sofar as they are not inconsistent with theprovisions of the Secured Transactions Act,No. of 2019, from and after the date of cominginto operation of the said Secured TransactionsAct.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
