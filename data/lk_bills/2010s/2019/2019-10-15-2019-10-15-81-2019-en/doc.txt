L.D.—O. 34/2019

AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAND (RESTRICTIONS ON ALIENATION)ACT, NO. 38 OF 2014

Be it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Land (Restrictions onAlienation) (Amendment) Act, No. of 2019.

2.Section 3 of the Land (Restrictions on Alienation)Act, No. 38 of 2014 is hereby amended in subsection (1) bythe repeal of paragraph (i) and the substitution therefor ofthe following paragraph:—

“(i)any land, the title of which is transferred, to acompany referred to in paragraph (b) of subsection(1) of section 2, listed in the Colombo StockExchange.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between theSinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shallprevail.
