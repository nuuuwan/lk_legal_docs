Short title.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE HOLIDAYS ACT, NO. 29 OF 1971

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Holidays (Amendment)Act, No. of 2019.

2.The following new section is hereby insertedimmediately after section 14 of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of1971 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”)and shall have effect as section 14A of that enactment:—

“14A. No person shall on any Full MoonPoya Day or any Sunday till 3.00 p.m. conductor caused to be conducted or facilitate for anytuition class for students attending schools atany premises or any location.”.

3.Section 17 of this Act is hereby amended by deletingthe words “One thousand rupees” and by substitutiontherefor of the following words “Ten thousand rupees”.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
