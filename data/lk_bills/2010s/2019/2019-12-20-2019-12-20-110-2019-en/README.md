1Marriages (General) (Amendment)

AN ACT TO AMEND THE MARRIAGES (GENERAL)ORDINANCE NO. 19 OF 1907

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Marriages (General)(Amendment) Act, No. of 2019.

2.The Preamble of the Principle enactment is herebyamended by deletion of the words “other than the marriagesof Muslims”.

3.Notwithstanding anything contrary to the provisionsof the Principle enactment or the provisions of any other law,the Marriages (General) Ordinance No. 19 of 1907 shallprevail.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
