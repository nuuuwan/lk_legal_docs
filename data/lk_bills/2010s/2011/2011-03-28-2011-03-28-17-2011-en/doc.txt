AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR CERTAIN CONSEQUENTIAL MATTERS

ARISING OUT OF THE ENACTMENT OF THE EMPLOYEES PENSIONBENEFITS FUND ACT AND THE OVERSEAS EMPLOYEES PENSIONBENEFITS FUND ACT, PURSUANT TO A BUDGET PROPOSAL IN THE2011 BUDGET ; AND TO PROVIDE FOR MATTERS CONNECTED THEREWITH

OR INCIDENTAL THERETO.

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Pensions (ConsequentialProvisions) Act, No. of 2011.

2.For the avoidance of doubts it is hereby declaredthat the provisions of the Acts specified in the First Scheduleto this Act shall be deemed to have no application in respectof the enactments specified in the Second Schedule to thisAct, and specifically in relation to the provisions dealingwith administration of the Acts and its application,membership, contributions and mode of collection ofcontributions.

3.(1) Parliament may by Resolution amend the FirstSchedule and the Second Schedule to this Act, by addingthereto or omitting therefrom any Act from any of theaforesaid Schedules in order to give effect to the changeswhich may be brought about in the policy of the Governmentin relation to the payment of pensions.

(2) Upon the Resolution seeking to amend the FirstSchedule and the Second Schedule being approved byParliament in accordance with the Standing Orders ofParliament, the Secretary to the Ministry of the Ministershall publish a Notification in the Gazette setting out thecontent of the Resolution in such manner as indicates theamendment made to the Schedule.
