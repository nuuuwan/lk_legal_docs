STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends the Long Title to the Coast Conservation Act, No. 57 of1981, (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the Long Title as amendedindicates the new area of Coastal Resource Management which is brought within the ambitof the principal enactment.

Clause 3 : This clause makes a general amendment to the principal enactment wherebythe there is substituted for the words “Coast Conservation Act”, “Director of CoastConservation”, “Director” and “coast conservation” appearing therein, the words “CoastConservation and Coastal Resource Management”, “Director-General of CoastConservation”, “Director-General” and “coast conservation and coastal resourcemanagement” respectively.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 3 of the principal enactment and the section asamended provides for the appointment of Directors, Deputy Directors and AssistantDirectors.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 4 of the principal enactment and the section asamended widens the scope of the powers to include-

(a)the co-ordination of activities of other agencies, within the coastal zone ;

(b)the preparation and implementation of coastal zone management plans ;

(c)the dissemination of information on coast conservation ;

(d)the conduct of research and the implementation of the coast conservationmanagement programmes.

Clause 6 : This clause replaces section 5 of the principal enactment and the section asreplaced provides for a wider delegation of powers by the Director-General.

Clause 7 : This clause replaces section 6 of the principal enactment and the section asreplaced provides for a change in the membership comprising the Advisory Council.

Clause 8 : This clause replaces section 11 of the principal enactment and the section asreplaced requires the Director-General to conduct a survey of all resources and activitieswithin the coastal zone and to publish a report embodying the same.

Clause 9 : This clause amends section 12 of the principal enactment and the section asamended requires the director-General to prepare a Coastal Zone and Coastal ResourceManagement Plan within three years from the date of operation of this Act and also sets outthe matters to be included in the Plan.
