AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE MADAHAPOLA SRI RATNAPALARAMAYARELIGIOUS AND BUDDHIST EDUCATIONAL DEVELOPMENTFOUNDATION

WHEREAS a society called and known as the “MadahapolaSri Ratnapalaramaya Religious and Buddhist EducationalDevelopment Foundation”, has heretofore been formed atMadahapola for the purpose of effectually carrying out itsobjects and transacting all matters connected with the saidfoundation according to the rules agreed to by the members.

AND WHEREAS the said society has heretofore successfullycarried out and transacted the several objects and matters forwhich it was formed and has applied to be incorporated andas acceding to this request will be for the public interest.

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Madahapola SriRatnapalaramaya Religious and Buddhist EducationalDevelopment Foundation (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2011.

2.From and after the commencement of this Act, suchand so many persons as now are members of the MadahapolaSri Ratnapalaramaya Religious and Buddhist EducationalFoundation (hereinafter referred to as “the Foundation”) andsuch other persons as shall hereafter be admitted as membersof the corporation hereby constituted, shall be, and becomea body corporate with perpetual succession under the nameand style of the “Madahapola Sri RatnapalaramayaReligious and Buddhist Educational DevelopmentFoundation” (hereinafter referred to as “The Corporation”)and by that name may sue and be sued in all courts with fullpower and authority to have and use a common seal, andalter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:—

(a)protect and nourish Buddhism, safeguard the rightsof Buddhists, Protect the spiritual, educational,cultural and social rights of Buddhists;

(b)bring about moral and economic progress of thesociety through quantitative and qualitativedevelopment of Buddhist Bhikkus in historicalYapahuwa area;

(c)draw advisory and financial support fromCenters of Bhikku Organizations, GovernmentInstitutions, Non-Governmental Organizations,Sasana Development Fund of the Dalada Maligawa,the Sri Siddartha Saranankara Welfare Foundationof Malwathu Viharaya and foreign embassies;

(d)lay the foundation for a long term program forreligious development by establishing a fund andincorporating it;

(e)construct a building complex with residentialfacilities for about 100 Bhikkus, endowed withcapabilities for the development of religiousactivities, modern communication skills andproficiency in languages;

(f)drawing the attention of relevant authorities to theneed to pave the way for a life style based onrighteous employment in a hydraulic civilization;

(g)provide facilities for the temples to serve the publicbased on the concept ‘from temple to the peoplerather than from people to the temple’;

(h)take necessary steps to lead the youth on righteouspaths by moving them away from misdirected paths;
