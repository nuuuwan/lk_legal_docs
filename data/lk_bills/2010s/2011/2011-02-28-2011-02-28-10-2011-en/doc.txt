1Recovery of Loans by Banks (Special Provisions)(Amendment)

L.D.—O. 20/2011

AN ACT TO AMEND THE RECOVERY OF LOANS BY BANKS (SPECIALPROVISIONS) ACT, NO. 4 OF 1990

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Recovery of Loans byBanks (Special Provisions) (Amendment) Act, No. of 2011.

2.The Recovery of Loans by Banks (Special Provisions)Act, No. 4 of 1990 is hereby amended in section 5A thereof,by the substitution for the word “amount” wherever suchword appears in that section, of the words “principal amountborrowed”.

3.In the event of an inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text to prevail.

Short title.

Amendmentof section 5Aof theRecovery ofLoans byBanks(SpecialProvisions)Act, No. 4 of1990.

In case of aninconsistencythe Sinhalatext toprevail.
