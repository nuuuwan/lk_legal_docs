AN ACT TO AMEND THE PRESCRIPTION (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)ACT, NO. 5 OF 2016

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Prescription (SpecialProvisions) (Amendment) Act, No. of 2018.

2.Section 4 of the Prescription (Special Provisions) Act,No. 5 of 2016 is hereby amended as follows:—

(1)by the substitution for the words “two years afterthe coming into operation of this Act.” of the words“four years from the date of coming into operationof this Act.”;

(2)by the substitution for the words “two years afterthe date of operation of this Act.”, in the marginalnote to that section, of the words “four years fromthe date of coming into operation of this Act.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
