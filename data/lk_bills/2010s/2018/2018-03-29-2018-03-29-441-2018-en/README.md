COMMISSIONS OF INQUIRY (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Commissions of Inquiry Act (Chapter 393)

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause inserts new sections 24A, 24B, 24C, 24D, and 24E to the Commissionsof Inquiry Act (Chapter 393) (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and thelegal effect of the sections as inserted is to provide for the Commission to InvestigateAllegations of Bribery or Corruption to consider material collected in the course of aninvestigation or an inquiry by a Commission of Inquiry and direct the Director General forthe Prevention of Bribery or Corruption to institute Crimianl Proceedings.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 27 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof that section as amended is to insert definitions for the expressions “Bribery Act”,“Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption”, “Declaration of Assetsand Liabilities Law”, “Director General”, “law enforcement authority”, “Penal Code” and“Prevention of Money Laundering Act”.
