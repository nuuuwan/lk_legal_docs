LAND (RESTRICTIONS ON ALIENATION)(AMENDMENT)

to amend the Land (Restrictions on Alienation) Act, No. 38 of 2014

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 3 of the Land (Restrictions on Alienation)Act, No. 38 of 2014 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to extend the application of the exemption granted bythat section to certain properties.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 5A of the principal enactment, and the legal effectof the section as amended is to amend the effective date of that section.
