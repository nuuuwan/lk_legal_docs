STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 30 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52) (hereinafterreferred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect as amended is to expand theCategories of persons required to keep instruments.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 32 of the principal enactment and the legal effectas amended is to empower the Minister to make rules for the issue of security features to beaffixed on an excisable article and to levy charges for the provision of such security features,with a view to eliminate any revenue loss from duties on an excisable article.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE EXCISE ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 52)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Excise (Amendment)Act, No. of 2018.

2.Section 30 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52)(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is herebyamended as follows:—

(1)in subsection (1) by the substitution for the words“Every person who manufactures or sells excisablearticle under a licence” of the words “Every personwho imports, exports, transports, manufactures,sells, possess or stores any excisable article under alicence, permit or pass;

(2)in the marginal note to that section for the words“certain licensees” of the words “holder of a licence,permit or pass”.

3.Section 32 of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the insertion immediately after paragraph (j) ofthe following new paragraphs:—

“(ja)prescribing the types of security features to beaffixed on an excisable article when such excisablearticle is imported, exported, transported,manufactured, sold, possessed or stored;

(jb)levying charges for issuing of the security featuresreferred to in paragraph (ja) to be affixed on anexcisable article when such excisable article isimported, exported, transported, manufactured,sold, possessed or stored;”.
