STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 2 of the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”), and the legal effect of the section asamended is to introduce the reduction of the rate to 11% and to restrict the deferment bya Strategic Development Project under SVAT only during the project implementationperiod.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 3 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section is to extend the liability on wholesale and retail trade where the total turnoverfor a period of 3 months exceeds Rs.100 million.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 5 of the principal enactment to provide for theremoval of the condition referred to in the consideration on termination of the lease.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 7 of the principal enactment to extend theregistration of buying office under SVAT Scheme.

Clause 6 : This clause amends section 8 of the principal enactment in order to removethe deemed tax liability on locally produced fresh milk.

Clause 7 : This clause amends section 10 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to extend the liable threshold for registration.

Clause 8 : This clause amends section 16 of the principal enactment to provide for thepublication in the official website of the Inland Revenue, the inactive files which may beupdated on monthly basis.

Clause 9 : This clause amends section 17 of the principal enactment which isconsequential to the amendment made by clause 16 of the Act.

Clause 10 : This clause amends section 19 of the principal enactment which isconsequential to the amendment made by clause 16 of the Act.

Clause 11 : This clause amends section 21of the principal enactment which isconsequential to the amendment made by clause 16 of the Act.

Clause 12 : This clause amends section 22 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section is to provide for the issue of guidelines by the Commissioner-Generalfor the application of financial consolidation process.

Clause 13 : This clause amends sections 25Aof the principal enactment and isconsequential to the amendments made by the registration threshold.

Clause 14 : This clause amends sections 25C of the principal enactment and isconsequential to the amendment made to reduce the rate to 11%.

Clause 15 : This clause amends section 25F of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to bring the leasing transactions liable to VAT onfinancial services.

Clause 16 : This clause inserts a new section to provide for the use of electroniccommunication or electronic records.

Clause 17,18, 19,20, 21,22 and 23 : These clauses amend sections 26, 28, 34,41,44,47and 66 of the principal enactment and is consequential to the amendment made by clause16 of this Act.

Clause 24 : This clause amends section 83 of the principal enactment to substitute thedefinition of the expression “taxable period”.

Clause 25 : This clause amends Schedule I of the principal enactment to introducecertain exemptions to PART II of that Schedule.

Clause 26 : This clause amends the amendment made to the principal enactment bysection 13 of the Amendment Act No 7 of 2014, for better clarification.
