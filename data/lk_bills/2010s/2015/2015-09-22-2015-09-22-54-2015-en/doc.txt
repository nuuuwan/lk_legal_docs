STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 3 of the Nation Building Tax Act, No. 9 of 2009(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of the section asamended is -

(a)to introduce a fresh calculation method for the payment of tax, in respect of thesupply of financial services;

(b)to impose liability on a person engaged in the business of real estate anddevelopment to pay tax for the income arising from such business; and

(c)to exempt certain articles from the liability of the payment of tax.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 6 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to exclude a person other than a manufacturer to be entitledfor tax credit under this Act.

Clause 4 : This clause inserts a new section 6B in the principal enactment and facilitatesthe use of electronic communications or electronic records.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 10 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to replace the definition of ‘service’.

Clause 6 : This clause amends the First Schedule to the principal enactment and thelegal effect of the said Schedule as amended is to revise the present criteria of exemptionrelating to certain articles.

Clause 7 : This clause specify dates on which certain provisions come into force.

Clause 8 : This validates the acts performed by the Commissioner-General of InlandRevenue or the Director-General of Customs, in respect of the collection of tax during theperiod commencing from January 1, 2015 and ending on the date of operation of this Act.
