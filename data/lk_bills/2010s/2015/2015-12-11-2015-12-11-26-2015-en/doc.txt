STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 135 of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15of 1979 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) by including section 291c ofthe Penal Code in paragraph (e) of subsection (1) thereof, and the legal effect of the sectionas amended is to make the Attorney General’s sanction a pre-requisite to take cognizance ofan offence punishable under section 291C.

Clause 3 : This clause amends the First Schedule to the principal enactment and makesan amendment consequential to the amendment made by clause 2.
