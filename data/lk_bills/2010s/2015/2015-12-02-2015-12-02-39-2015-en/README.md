AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SRI LANKA HIRA FOUNDATION

WHEREAS an Association called and known as the“Sri Lanka Hira Foundation” has heretofore been formed10/18, Lake Drive, Colombo-08 the purpose of effectuallycarrying out and transacting all objects and mattersconnected with the said Foundation according to the rulesagreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Association has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated and it will be for the public advantage to grantsuch application.

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the “Sri Lanka HiraFoundation” (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2015.

2.From and after the commencement of this Act, suchand so many persons as now are members of the “Sri LankaHira Foundation” (hereinafter referred to as the “Foundation”)or shall hereafter be admitted as members of the Corporationhereby constituted shall be a body corporate (hereinafterreferred to as the “Corporation”) with perpetual succession,under the name and style of the “Sri Lanka Foundation” andby that name may sue and be sued in all courts, with fullpower and authority to have and use a common seal andalter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—
