1Sri Lanka Association for Fisheries andAquatic Resources (Incorporation)

AN ACT TO INCORPORATE SRI LANKA ASSOCIATION FOR FISHERIES ANDAQUATIC RESOURCES

WHEREAS an Association called and known as the “SriLanka Association for Fisheries and Aquatic Resources” hasheretofore been established for the promotion of effectiveinteraction and co-operation among those involved inresearch and development activities in fisheries and aquaticresources in Sri Lanka and for the purpose of effectivelycarrying out its objects according to the Constitution agreedto by its members :

AND WHEREAS the said Association has applied to beincorporated in order to enable it more effectively to carryout and fulfill the several objects and purposes for which itwas established, and it will be for the public advantage togrant the application :

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka Associationfor Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (Incorporation) Act,No. of 2015.

2.From and after the commencement of this Act suchand so many persons as now are members of Sri LankaAssociation for Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (hereinafterreferred to as “the Association”) or shall hereafter be admittedas members of the Association hereby constituted shall (solong as they continue to be members) be and become a bodycorporate with perpetual succession under the style and nameof the “Sri Lanka Association for Fisheries and AquaticResources” and by that name may sue and be sued in allCourts, with full power and authority to have and to use acommon seal and to alter the same at their pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation iscontinued are hereby declared to be—

(a) to promote effective interaction and co-operationamong those involved in research and developmentactivities in fisheries and aquatic resources in SriLanka ;

(b) to liaise and co-operate with Government and non-Governmental Organizations concerned in suchactivities in Sri Lanka ;

(c) to create and propagate awareness of the importanceand the ways of sound utilization cultivation,conservation and development of aquatic resourcesin Sri Lanka ;

(d) to serve as a Forum for Scientists and other personnelto discuss issues and policies related to developmentand conservation of aquatic resources ; and

(e) to establish liaison with other ScientificOrganizations.

4.Subject to the provisions of this Act and any otherwritten law, the Corporation shall have the powers to do,perform and execute all such acts, matters and thingswhatsoever as are necessary or desirable for the promotion orfurtherance of the objects of the Corporation or any one ofthem including the power to open, operate and close bankaccounts, to borrow or raise monies with or without security,to receive or collect grants and donations, to invest its funds,and to engage, employ and dismiss personnel required forthe carrying out of the objects of the Association.

5.(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall, subject to theConstitution of the Corporation, be administered by anExecutive Committee consisting of officers and such otherpersons as may be provided for in the Constitution and electedin accordance therewith.

8.The Corporation shall be able and capable in law tocharge—

(a) such fees as the Association may deem reasonablefor admission to any course or examinationconducted by the Association ;

(b) fees for the election of persons to any class ofmembership of the Association ; and

(c) such subscriptions and fees as the Association maydeem reasonable in respect of services provided bythe Corporation.

9.All debts and liabilities of the Association existing atthe time of coming into operation of this Act shall be paid ordischarged by the Corporation, and all, debts due to, andsubscriptions, and fees payable to the Association shall bepaid to the Corporation for the purposes of this Act.

10.The Corporation shall have the power to appointany Committee and to take such other steps as may be foundnecessary, from time to time, for the furtherance of its objects.

11.The seal of the Corporation shall not be affixed toany instrument whatsoever except in the presence of suchpersons as the Committee shall authorize from time to timeby Standing Order, who shall sign their names to theinstrument in token of their presence, and such signing shallbe independent of the signing of any person as a witness.

12.Nothing contained in this Act shall prejudice oraffect the rights of the Republic or any body politic orcorporate or of any other persons, except such as arementioned in this Act and those claiming by, from or underthem.
