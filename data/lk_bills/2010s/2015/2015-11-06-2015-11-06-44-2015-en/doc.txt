AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE INSTITUTE OF SRI LANKAPROFESSIONAL PSYCHO SOCIAL WORK

WHEREAS an Institute called and known as the “Instituteof Sri Lanka Professional Psycho Social Work” wasestablished in Sri Lanka by a group of dedicatedprofessionals in the field of Psycho Social Work for thepurpose of introducing and promoting professionalismamong those engaged in the said field and maintaining thehighest professional standards among the professionalpsycho social work fraternity:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute was registered underSection 5 (2) of the Voluntary Social Services Organizations(Registration & Supervision) Act, No. 31 of 1980 as amendedby Act, No. 8 of 1998 on the Thirty First day of October TwoThousand and Seven:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute was recognized andregistered under bearing No. P20/0052 as a Training Instituteby the Tertiary and Vocational Commission of Sri Lankaunder Tertiary Education and Vocational Act, No. 20 of1990 and Government Gazette No. 887/8 dated 07. 09. 1995:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute has resolved by specialresolution that the Institute be incorporated by an act ofParliament, for the general advancement of the profession ofPsycho Social Work and for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out its objects according to the Articles of theConstitution agreed to by its member:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated, and it will be for the public advantage to grantthe application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Institute of Sri LankaProfessional Psycho Social Work (Incorporation)Act,No. of 2015.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theInstitute of Sri Lanka Professional Psycho Social Work(hereinafter referred to as “the Institute”) or shall hereafterbe admitted as members of the Corporation herebyconstituted, shall be a body corporate (hereinafter referredto as “the Corporation”) with perpetual succession underthe name and style of the “Institute of Sri Lanka ProfessionalPsycho Social Work”, and by that name may sue and besued with full power and authority to have and use a commonseal and alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:

(a)to upgrade the standard of qualitative psycho socialwork and to promote, foster and safe guard theprofessional ethics, respect, rights, powers andprivileges of members engaged in psycho socialwork profession;

(b)to introduce new psycho social strategies in areasof psycho social professional education, training,research and publication, which are necessary toimprove knowledge, skills and attitudes ofprofessional psycho social work and therebydevelop and strengthen the community;

(c)to promote and conduct social developmentprojects and programs and render assistance tointroduce new social principles thereon;
