AN ACT TO AMEND THE FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCESACT, NO. 2 OF 1996

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Fisheries and AquaticResources (Amendment) Act, No. of 2016.

2.The following new section is hereby insertedimmediately after section 28 of the Fisheries and AquaticResources Act, No. 2 of 1996 (hereinafter referred to as the“principal enactment”) and shall have effect as section28A of that enactment.

28A. (1) No person shall engage in or causeany other person to engage in fishingoperations carried out by Trawl Nets, includingthose using nets without two wings but usingvertical poles at the two free ends of the net.

(2) No person shall use, posses, import,transport, purchase or sell Trawl Nets for thepurpose of carrying out trawling operations inSri Lanka waters.

(3) No license shall be issued under Part IIof the principal enactment for fishingoperations carried out by Trawl Nets inSri Lanka waters.

(4) Any license, which has already beenissued under the principal enactment forfishing operations carried out by Trawl Nets inSri Lanka waters, shall not be renewed, and

3.The following new section is hereby insertedimmediately after section 49(6) of the principal enactmentand shall have effect as section 49(7) of that enactment.

49 (7). Any person who contravenes or failsto comply with the provisions of section 28Aof this Act shall commit an offence and shallon conviction after summary trial before aMagistrate be liable to an imprisonment for aterm not exceeding two years or to a fine notless than fifty thousand rupees.”.

4.Section 66 of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the insertion, immediately after the definitionof the expression “Sri Lanka Waters”, of the following newdefinitions:—

“Trawl Net” means towing nets having a coneshaped body and a bag or cod end (madiya)connected to the apex with or without twowings connected to the other end.

“Trawling” means any fishing operation carried outby dragging a Trawl Net mechanically alongthe bed or in mid water.
