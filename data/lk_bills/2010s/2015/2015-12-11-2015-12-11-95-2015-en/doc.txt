AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SEYYED SHEIKH KOYA THANGALMOWLANA FOUNDATION

WHEREAS an Foundation called and known as the "SeyyedSheikh Koya Thangal Mowlana Foundation" has heretoforebeen formed in Sri Lanka for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out its objects and transacting all matters connectedwith the said Foundation according to the rules agreed to byits members:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established, and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be expedient to grant the saidapplication:

BE it therefore, enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Seyyed Sheikh KoyaThangal Mowlana Foundation (Incorporation) Act, No. of2015.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of the“Seyyed Sheikh Koya Thangal Mowlana Foundation”(hereinafter referred to as the “Foundation”) or shall hereafterbe admitted as members of the Corporation herebyconstituted, shall be a body corporate with perpetualsuccession, under the name and style of the "Seyyed SheikhKoya Thangal Mowlana Foundation" (hereinafter referredto as the "Corporation") and by that name may sue and besued with full power and authority to have and use a commonseal and to alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to promote peace, goodwill, understanding andCooperation among all communities and people ofSri Lanka;

(b)to establish thakkiyas, Arabic TheologicalColleges and Orphanages for boys;

(c)to manage and run the institutions that are alreadyin existence such as Kattankudy MuhiyaddeenThakkiya, Kankeyanodai, Muhiyadeen Thakkiya(in Batticaloa District) Masjidur Riffaiyyi andArabic Theological College and Muslim Orpangethere is known as Madrasathul and Muslim Orpangein Kandy District;

(d)to Manage the establishments and Institutions thathave been founded by Seyyed Sheikh Koya Thangalthe Predecessor of present Spiritual leader SeyyedMohamed Buhary Nalla Koya Thangal;

(f)to foster the spirit of unity and to promote goodwilland friendship among Muslims and othercommunities;
