AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE KOTASARA PIYANGALA RAJAMAHAVIHARASTHA DEVELOPMENT SOCIETY

WHEREAS a Foundation called and known as the “KotasaraPiyangala Rajamaha Viharastha Development Society” hasheretofore been established in Sri Lanka for the purpose ofeffectually carrying out and transacting all objects andmatters connected with the said Foundation according tothe rules agreed to by its members.

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the application.

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Kotasara PiyangalaRajamaha Viharastha Development Society (Incorporation)Act, No. of 2015.

2.From and after the date of commencement of this Act,such and so many persons as presently are members of the“Kotasara Piyangala Rajamaha Viharastha DevelopmentSociety” (hereinafter referred to as the “Foundation”) or shallhereafter be admitted as members of the Corporation herebyconstituted shall be a body corporate (hereinafter referred toas the “Corporation”) with perpetual succession under thename and style of the “Kotasara Piyangala RajamahaViharastha Development Society” and by that name may sueand be sued in all courts, with full power and authority tohave and use a common seal and to alter the same at itspleasure.

3.The general objects for which Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)provision of necessities, maintenance and carryingout Wegama Sri Saddarmalankara Vidyayatanaya,Wegama Sri Sobhita Dharmayatanaya, MapakadaSri Swarnajothi Dhamma School, Kotasara PeraheraOrganization and Kotasara Sathara PeruwaAvamangalyadara Samithi affiliated to the KotasaraPiyangala Raja Maha Viharastha SanwardenaSabhawa, to organize activities to bring aboutreligious fervour among Buddhists and to promotesocial and economic activities of the people in thearea ;

(b)to preserve and develop as a place of religious andcultural importance by the erection of a parapetwall and Wahalkada in the above mentionedWiharastha New Dhamma Hall, creation of anorchard in the premises of this Institute ;

(c)to provide housing and health facilities and waterto poor and destitute in the area ;

(d)to provide facilities to poor children in the relevantdistrict to improve their education ;

(e)to promote, encourage and assist in, activities forthe spiritual, cultural, social and physical welfare ofits members ;

(f)to safeguard and propagate Buddhism ;

(g)to communicate the message contained in theteachings of the Buddha and to propogate Buddhistculture with a view to promoting world peace ;

(h)to present the Buddhist point of view on any matter,on behalf of the Buddist community at conferencesand seminars on global problems relating toeducation culture, communications survival,development and peace, convened by anyinternational, governmental or non-governmentalorganizations, whether religious or secular ;
