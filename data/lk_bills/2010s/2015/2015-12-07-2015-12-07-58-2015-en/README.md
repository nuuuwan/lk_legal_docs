AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE DAYA SARANA DEVELOPMENTFOUNDATION

WHEREAS a Foundation called and known as the“Daya Sarana Development Foundation” has beenestablished in Sri Lanka for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out and transacting all objects and mattersconnected with the said Foundation according to the rulesagreed to the members:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Daya SaranaDevelopment Foundation (Incorporation) Act,No. of 2016.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theDaya Sarana Development Foundation (hereinafter referredto as “the Foundation”) or shall hereafter be admitted asnow are members of the Corporation hereby constituted,shall be a body corporate (hereinafter referred to as the“Corporation”) with perpetual succession under the nameand style of the “Daya Sarana Development Foundation”,and by that name may sue and be sued in all courts, with fullpower and authority to have and use a common seal and toalter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:—

(a)to promote religious harmony, mutualunderstanding, spiritual development , goodwilland friendship among the youth in the area throughreligious , cultural, educational and economicinteraction;

(b)to protect, support and ensure welfare, interests,safety, integrity and status of people in the area andto safeguard their rights and privileges;

(c)to provide for the physical welfare and personaldevelopment of teenagers through youthdevelopment centers including gumasiums;

(d)to provide additional educational support by givingnecessary training in various fields which willenhance their personal qualification levels in orderto meet the competition and necessary requirementsin finding employment;

(e)to hold and conduct periodical technical trainingcamps for the youth to develop their skills with theultimate aim of projecting them towards findingsuitable employment;

(f)to establish carpentry and masonry and any othersuitable vocational training school in central townsand the like with a view to educate and train youthfor a vocation;

(g)to establish motor mechanism training centers(Garages) in the area in order to train the youth inmotor vehicle repair trade and arrange periodicallectures conducted by professionals in the trade;

(h)to liaise with the Foreign Employemtn Bureau underthe Central Government to facilitate applicationsfrom eligible youth in the area and recommend themfor suitable foreign job opportunities;
