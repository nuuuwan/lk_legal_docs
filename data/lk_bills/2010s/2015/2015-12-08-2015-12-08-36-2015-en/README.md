AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SERETSE KHAMA FOUNDATION TRUST

WHEREAS a trrust called and known as the “The SeretseKhama Foundation Trust” has heretofore been ceated in SriLanka for the purpose of effectually carrying out andtransacting all objects and matters connected with the saidFoundation Trust:

AND WHEREAS the Board of Trustees of the saidFoundation Trust has heretofore successfully carried outand transacted the several objects and matters for which thesaid Foundation Trust was created on 20th January 2006,and has applied to be incorporated, and it will be for thepublic advantage to grant the application :

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as The Seretse KhamaFoundation Trust (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2015.

2.From and after the date of commencement of this Act,such and so many persons as presently are members of the“The Seretse Khama Foundation Trust” (hereinafter referredto as the “Foundation Trust”) and whose names are set out inthe schedule of this Act or shall hereafter be admitted asmembers of the Corporation hereby, constituted, shall be abody corporate (hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”)with perpetual succession under the name and style of “TheSeretse Khama Foundation Trust” and by that name may sueand be sued in all courts with full power and authority to haveand to use a Common Seal and alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to perpetuate the memory of Sir SecretseKhama,the first President of Botswana and spread hismessage of peace and unity in Sri Lanka;
