STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause inserts new section 291c in the Penal Code (Chapter 19) and thelegal effect of the section is to make provision to convict and punish persons who cause orinstigate acts of violence or hostility which lead to religious, racial or communal disharmonybetween different racial or religious groups.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE PENAL CODE (CHAPTER 19)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Penal Code(Amendment) Act, No. of 2015.

2.The following section is hereby inserted immediatelyafter section 291B of the Penal Code (Chapter 19) and shallhave effect as section 291C of that Code:—

291C. Whoever, by the use of words spoken,written or intended to be read, or by signs, orby visible representation, or otherwise, intendsto cause or attempts to cause or instigates orattempts to instigate, acts of violence orreligious, racial or communal disharmony, orfeelings of ill-will or hostility, betweencommunities or different classes of persons ordifferent racial or religious groups, shall bepunished with imprisonment of eitherdescription for a term which may extend totwo years.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
