STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

This Bill seeks to amend to Economic Service Charge Act, No. 13 of 2006 and the legaleffect of such amendments are to provide for—

(a)the rate of the Economic Service Charge being paid on the turnover arising on orafter April 1, 2008 from the export of any articles or goods not to exceed 0.25 per centum ;

(b)the remainder if any, of the amount paid as Economic Service Charge paid prior toMarch 1, 2006, after the deduction of income tax therefrom, shall be deemed tobe a payment made in respect of the quarter commencing on April 1, 2008 ;

(c)amendments to be made to the Schedule to the Act, by amending the rate in items4, 5 and 11 of the Scheduele and the insertion of two new items 4a and 11a to suchSchedule.
