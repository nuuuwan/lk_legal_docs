AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE MEDICINA ALTERNATIVA INSTITUTE

WHEREAS a Medical Institute called as “MedicinaAlternativa” has heretofore been established for the purposeof effectually carrying out and transacting all the objectsand matters connected with the said Institute according tothe rules agreed to by its members:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Medicina AlternativaInstitute (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2012.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, the members of the Medicina Alternativa Institute whoshall be appointed by votes with reference to the Constitutionof this institute (hereinafter referred to as “Institute”) shallbe a body corporate (hereinafter referred to as the“Corporation”) with perpetual succession under the nameand style of the “Medicina Alternativa Institute” and by thatname may sue and be sued in all Courts, with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and alter the sameat its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:

(a)to promote the alternative medical science in thecountry and inforeign countries and to provide theeducation free of charge;

(b)to treat the poor and needy patients through themethods of altanative medical free of charge;

(c)to provide facilities to uplift the educationalactivites of poor children;
