AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE BAREERAH MUSLIM LADIES ARABICCOLLEGE

WHEREAS a college known as "Bareerah Muslim LadiesArabic College" has heretofore been formed in Sri Lanka atMawathupola, Akurana-09, Kandy district for the purposeof effectually carrying out and transacting all objects andmatters connected with the said College, according to therules agreed to by the Board of management of the College.

AND WHEREAS the said College has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated and it will be for public advantage to grantsuch application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:–

1.This Act may be cited as the Bareerah Muslim LadiesArabic College (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2012.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theBareerah Muslim Ladies Arabic College (hereinafter referredto as the "College") and shall hereafter be admitted asmembers of the Corporation hereby constituted, shall be abody corporate with perpetual succession under the nameand style of "the Bareerah Muslim Ladies Arabic College"(hereinafter referred to as "the Corporation") and by thatname may sue and be sued in all courts, with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and alter the sameat its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:–

(a)to produce Islamic Theologists to lead thecommunity under the Islamic law of "Shariaa";

(b)to protect Islam;
