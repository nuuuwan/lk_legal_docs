AN ACT TO AMEND THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATICSOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA.

WHEREAS the Constitution of the Democrtic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka has guaranteed and assured to protectand foster the Buddha Sasana and the rights of all otherreligions:

WHEREAS it has become a timely need to enact provisionsinto the Constitution for the purpose of maintenance andpreservation of religious dignity and holiness with a view toachieving of unity and concord of the Nation:

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Nineteenth Amendmentto the Constitution.

2. The Article 91 of the Constitution be amended byinserting the following:—

“91(1) (d) (xiii) a priest of any religion”.

3.This amendment shall come into operation soon afterthe expiry or the dissolution of the seventh Parliament.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
