STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Defence Services Command and Staff College (Amendment) Bill

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 7 of the Defence Services Command and StaffCollege Act, No. 5 of 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and thelegal effect of the section as amended is to include the Vice-Chancellor of the GeneralSir John Kotelawala Defence University in the Board of Management of the Staff Collegeinstead of the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Kelaniya.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 15 of the principal enactment and the legel effectof the section as amended is consequential to the amendment made by clause 2.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE DEFENCE SERVICES COMMAND AND STAFFCOLLEGE ACT, NO. 05 OF 2008

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the DefenceServices Command and Staff College (Amendment) Act,No. of 2013.

2.Section 7 of the Defence Services Command and StaffCollege Act, No. 05 of 2008 (hereinafter referred to as “theprincipal enactment”) is hereby amended in subsection (1)of that section by the repeal of paragraph (f) of that subsectionand the substitution therefor, of the following paragraph:—

“(f)the Vice-Chancellor of the General Sir JohnKotelawala Defence University;”.

3.Section 15 of the principal enactment is herebyamended in subsection (1) of that section by the repeal ofparagraph (b) of that subsection and the substitution therefor,of the following paragraph:—

“(b)a representative nominated by the Vice-Chancellorof the General Sir John Kotelawala DefenceUniversity;”.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
