AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE WAREHOUSE PROJECT

WHEREAS an Association called and known as the“Warehouse Project” has heretofore been formed in Colombofor the purpose of effectually carrying out and transactingthe objects and matters connected with the said Associationaccording to the rules agreed to by its members :

AND WHEREAS the said Warehouse Project has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant such application :

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Warehouse Project(Incorporation) Act, No. of 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of the“Warehouse Project” or shall hereafter be admitted asmembers of the Corporation hereby constituted, shall be abody corporate with perpetual succession, under the nameand style of the " Warehouse Project" (hereinafter referred toas “the Corporation”) and by that name may sue, and besued in all Courts, with full power and authority to have anduse a common seal and the alter same at its pleasure.

(a)to provide social services to the less privilegedpersons in all parts of the country by helping themto meet their basic needs, train and developindividual skills, help find employment andimprove their standard of living;
