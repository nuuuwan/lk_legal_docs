AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE FEDERATION OF PHOTOGRAPHICSOCIETIES OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS a Federation called and known as the “TheFederation of Photographic Societies of Sri Lanka” hasheretofore been formed in Sri Lanka for the purpose ofeffectually carrying out its objects and transacting all mattersconnected with the said Federation according to the rulesagreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Federation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established, and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be expedient to grant the saidapplication:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the The Federation ofPhotographic Societies of Sri Lanka (Incorporation) Act,No. of 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many person as are now members of TheFederation of Photographic Societies of Sri Lanka (hereinafterreferred to as the “Federation”) or shall hereafter be admittedas members of the Corporation hereby constituted, shall bea body corporate with perpetual succession, under the nameand style of The “Federation of Photographic Societies ofSri Lanka” (hereinafter referred to as “the Corporation”) andby that name may sue and be sued, with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and to alter thesame at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Federation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to popularize the art of photography as an artisticand technical media;

(b)to avail an oppoutunity in order to learn thephotographic art theoretically and practically freeof charge and to obtain any other professionalqualification in the field of photography;

(c)to facilitate the school students to learn thephotographic art free of charge as an aestheticsubject;

(d)to take action in order to keep the photographic artalive, and to safeguard the common rights of themembers of the societies;

(e)to publish any useful publication under thesponsorship of the federation with the view ofpromoting the field;

(f)to popularize the same among the photographicsocieties so established; and

(g)to develope co-operation among the photographicsocieties internally and internationally in order toachieve the above objectivies; to educate how toprotect environment and to prepare for the future;and to develop co-operation and friendship amongthe societies.
