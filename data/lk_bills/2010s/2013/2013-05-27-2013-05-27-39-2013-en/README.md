AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SRI LANKA CULTURAL FOUNDATION

FOR THE BUILDING OF CULTURE AND MORALS

WHEREAS a Foundation called and known as “Sri LankaCultural Foundation for the building of Culture and Morals”has been established for the purpose of effectually carryingout and transacting all objects and matters connectedwith the said Foundation according to the rules agreedto by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the said application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka CulturalFoundation for the Building of Culture and Morals(Incorporation) Act, No. of 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement ofthis Act, such and so many persons as now presently aremembers of the “Sri Lanka Cultural Foundation for theBuilding of Culture and Morals” (hereinafter referred to asthe “Foundation”) or shall hereafter be admitted as membersof the Corporatio hereby constituted, shall be a bodycorporate (hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”) withperpetual succession, under the name and style of the“Sri Lanka Cultural Foundation for the Building of Cultureand Morals” and by that name may sue and be sued in allcourts with full power and authority to have and use acommon seal and alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to resolve and encourage member unions for thepromulgation of relief services functions, and toprevent the poverty stricken situations from whichmen suffer;

(b)to form and foster a union for cultural services inevery country and to provide for incentives andpromotion and to establish the policies of the mainorganization within this union;

(c)to create and assist the projects for pre-disasterpreparation, at the national, regional andinternational level;

(d)to establish and utilize the required resources forsocial services, cultural restoration activities andpersonality development;

(e)to treat all nationals with equality, respecting theircultures, protecting all wayward artists andproviding relief work to them, as much as possible;

(f)to organize with close cooperation of the nationalunions and member unions in accordance with thecommon aims of the Corporation as well ascoordinating and operating;

(g)to provide an efficient organizational frameworkand information distribution infrastructure facilitiesat the national and regional level;

(h)to present certificates of skills esteemed certificatesof incentives, esteemed incentives, honorary consolawards, training artists and conducting programmes,creating the required background for them and itscontinuous maintenance;
