AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE JAMIAH RAHMANIYA ARABICCOLLEGE, AKURANA

WHEREAS an Association called and known as the “JamiahRahmaniya Arabic College, Akurana” has heretofore beenformed for the purpose of effectually carrying out andtransacting all objects and matters connected with the saidCollege according to the rules agreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said College has heretofore successfullycarried out and transacted the several objects and matters forwhich it was formed and has applied to be incorporated andit will be for the public advantage to grant such application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Jamiah RahmaniyaArabic College, Akurana (Incorporation) Act, No. 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of the“Jamiah Rahmaniya Arabic College, Akurana” (hereinafterreferred to as the “College”) or shall hereafter be admittedas members of the Corporation hereby constituted shall bea body corporate (hereinafter referred to as the“Corporation”) with perpetual succession under the nameand style of “Jamiah Rahmaniya Arabic College, Akurana”and by that name may sue and be sued in all courts ,with fullpower and authority to have and use a common seal andalter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to administer the Jamiah Rahmaniya ArabicCollege, Akurana;
