AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE INSTITUTE FOR RESEARCH ANDDEVELOPMENT, SRI LANKA

WHEREAS an Institute called and known as the“Institute for Research and Development” has beenestablished in Sri Lanka in year 2000, and first registered asa Not for Profit Organization under the companies act in2004 (registration number N(A) 1170) and under the newamemdments to the act it was reregistered in 2011 as a Notfor Profit Professional Guarantee Limited Organization(registration number GA 635) under the name of Forum forResearch and Development for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out and transacting all objects and matters connectedwith the said institution according to the rules agreed to byits members. To fulfill the aspirations and expectations ofthe research community and the public in order to expandand extend research and related activities within the country.In addition to be accountable and transparent to the public:

AND WHEREAS the said research Institute has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted objects and mattersfor which it was established, with regional and internationalacclaim and has applied to be incorporated, and it will be forthe public advantage to grant the application :

BE it therefore, enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Institute for Researchand Development, Sri Lanka (Incorporation) Act, No. of2013.

2.From and after date of commencement of this Act, suchand so many persons as presently are members of the Institutefor Research and Development (hereinafter referred to as the“Institute”) or shall hereafter be admitted as members of theCorporation hereby constituted, shall be a body corporate,
