AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE STUDY CENTRE FOR THE ADVANCEMENT

OF TECHNOLOGY AND SOCIAL WELFARE

WHEREAS a Centre called and known as the “StudyCentre for the Advancement of Technology and SocialWelfare” has heretofore been formed in Sri Lanka at 75C,Dutugemunu Mawatha, Dehiwala for the purpose ofeffectually carrying out its objects and transacting all mattersconnected with the said Centre according to the rules agreedto by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Centre has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted several objects andmatters for which it was established, and has applied to beincorporated, and it will be expedient to grant the saidapplication:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Study Centre for theAdvancement of Technology and Social Welfare(Incorporation) Act, No. of 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theStudy Centre for the Advancement of Technology and SocialWelfare (hereinafter referred to as the “Centre”) or shallhereafter be admitted as members of the Corporation herebyconstituted, shall be a body corporate with perpetualsuccession, under the name and style of the “Study Centrefor the Advancement of Technology and Social Welfare”(hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”) and by thatname may sue and be sued, with full power and authority tohave and use a common seal and to alter the same at itspleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to initiate the establishment of favourable culturalheritage among Sri Lankans and to revive the SriLankan culture among the Sri Lankan community,living in other countries including Australia;

(b)to enlighten the Sri Lankans and internationalcommunity residing in other countries includingAustralia about the Sri Lankan framework relatingto the spheres of culture, economy, politics andsport;

(c)to discuss and take necessary action regarding theissues and other responsibilities which affect theSri Lankans, living in other countries includingAustralia;

(d)to assist the Sri Lankans residing around the worldto exchange the education, the development andthe technology and maintain close ties with the SriLankans for the socio economic and culturaldevelopment;

(e)to organize social welfare activities for the benefitof Sri Lankans, specially the aged, youth, womenand non-privileged groups and to assist theinstitutions and community needs in Sri Lanka,Australia and other countries;

(f)to introduce the modern technology, arts, modernmanagement and busness skills to Sri Lankans,residing in Sri Lanka, Australia and other countries,to organize educational programmes continuouslyand also assist in professional improvement;

(g)to provide advices and assistance to the supply ofbasic needs of the Sri Lankans and students comingto Australia and other countries;
