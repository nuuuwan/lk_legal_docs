STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 28 of the Mines and Minerals Act, No. 33 of1992 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of thesection as amended is to provide for the Minister to prescribe the minerals that are requiredfor construction purposes which requires a licence for transportation of the same.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 33 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to prescribe reserved Minerals which requires theapproval of the Minister, to obtain a licence.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 64 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to add two new paragraphs in respect of which theMinister may make regulations.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE MINES AND MINERALSACT, NO. 33 OF 1992

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Mines and Minerals(Amendment) Act, No. of 2013.

2.Section 28 of the Mines and Minerals Act, No. 33 of1992 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) ishereby amended by the addition of a new subsectionimmediately after subsection (4) as follows :—

“(5) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in anyother provisions of this Act, the Minister may, from timeto time, with the written recommendation of the Bureau,prescribe such minerals required for construction purposeswhich requires a licence for transportation of the same.”.
