AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE DENSRI EDUCATION FOUNDATION

WHEREAS a Foundation called and known as the “DensriEducation Foundation ” has been established in Sri Lankafor the purpose of effectually carrying out and transactingall objects and matters connected with the said Foundationaccording to the rules agreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Densri EducationFoundation (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2013.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as are members of the “DensriEducation Foundation” (hereinafter referred to as the“Foundation”) or shall hereafter be admitted members of theCorporation hereby constituted, shall be a body corporate(hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”) with perpetualsuccession under the name and style of “Densri EducationFoundation”, and by that name may sue and be sued in allcourts, with full power and authority to have and use acommon seal and to alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to initiate, maintain and uplift the educationalactivities, such as establishing pre-schools indifferent parts of the island, especially in rural andwar-affected areas for those who have no access topre-school education presently;
