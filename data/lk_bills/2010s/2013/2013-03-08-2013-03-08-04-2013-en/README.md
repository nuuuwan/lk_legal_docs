STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 24 of the Marriage Registration Ordinance (Chap-ter 112) (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) by specifying the mode ofpayment of fees to the registrar of the division and the District Registrar.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 27 of the principal enactment by revising theamount of fees to be paid to the District Registrar and specifying the mode of payment.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 34 by specifying the fees to be paid to the Registrar.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 38 by revising the amount of fees to be paid andspecifies the mode of payment.

Clause 6 : This clause amends section 51 by requiring the applicant to pay an amount inplace of affixing stamps.

Clause 7 : This clause amends section 62 of the principal enactment by giving the powerto the Minister to revise fees enumerated in the second schedule by rules published in theGazette.

Clause 8 : This clause replaces the second schedule to this Act.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE MARRIAGE REGISTRATION ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 112)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Marriage Registration(Amendment) Act, No. of 2013 and shall be deemed to havecome into operation from January 1, 2013.

2.Section 24 of the Marriage Registration Ordinance(Chapter 112) (hereinafter referred to as the “principalenactment”) is hereby amended by the repeal of subsection(6) of that section, and the substitution therefor, of thefollowing subsection :—

“(6) Every notice to a Registrar of a Division or to aDistrict Registrar as the case may be, under subsection(1), (2) or (3) of section 23 or every notice under subsection(4) thereof, shall be accompanied by a receipt issued bythe Registrar of such Division or such District Registraras the case may be, in proof of payment of a sum of rupeesten or a sum of rupees thirty respectively or such otheramount as may be prescribed by the Minister from time totime.”.

3.Section 27 of the principal enactment is herebyamemded by the repeal of subsection (4) of that section andthe substitution therefor, of the following subsection :—

“(4) Where the declaration is made before the DistrictRegistrar, the party making the declaration shall pay rupeesone hundred or such amount as may be prescribed by theMinister from time to time and where the declaration ismade before the Registrar of a Division it shall beaccompanied by a receipt issued by the District Registrarin proof of payment of a sum of rupees one hundred orsuch amount as may be prescribed.”.

4.Section 34 of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the repeal of subsection (5) of that section andthe substitution therefor, of the following subsection :—

“(5) The minister shall, within seven days from thedate of the solemnizing of the marriage, separate from theregister book the duplicate statement of the marriage andtransmit the same to the District Registrar within whosedistrict the marriage was solemnized together with a feespecified in the second schedule to this Act, as may beprescribed by the Minister from time to time, which ispayable to such Registrar for the registration of suchmarriage.”.

5.Section 38 of the principal enactment is herebyamemded by the repeal of subsection (2) of that section andthe substitution therefor, of the following subsection :—

“(2) In case the female party belongs to a class otherthan that described in the preceding subsection or is inthe opinion of the District Registrar, not entitled to thebenefits of that subsection, it shall be lawful for the DistrictRegistrar upon the application of one of the parties to theindented marriage, and which application shall beaccompanied by a receipt issued by the District Registrarin proof of payment of a sum of rupees three thousandfive hundred or such othe amount as may be prescribedby the Minister from time to time, to issue a licenseempowering a registrar to solemnize the marriage at suchplace and hour as the parties my prefer and as they may benamed in this licence.”.

6.Section 51 of the principal enactment is herebyamemded by the repeal of subsection (2) of that section andthe substitution therefor, of the following subsection :—

“(2) The applicant shall pay in respect of every writtenapplication and in respect of every certified copy orcertified extract thereof such amount as may be prescribedby the Minister from time to time.”.
