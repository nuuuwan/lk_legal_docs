AN ACT TO AMEND THE UNIVERSITIESACT, NO. 16 OF 1978

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Universities(Amendment) Act, No. of 2016.

2.Section 75 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is herebyrepealed and the following new section is substitutedtherefor:—

75. (1) The retirement age of a public officeras may be determined by the Government, fromtime to time, shall apply with effect from thesame date as applicable to a public officer inrespect of the retirement age of the holder ofany post, other than that of a teacher.

(2) (a) the Commission or the governingauthority of any Higher EducationalInstitution to which the holder of such post isattached or in the case where such person isattached to a Higher Educational Institute, thegoverning authority of the Higher EducationalInstitution to which such institute is affiliatedmay based on the recommendations of theInstitute suspend the holder of such postpending an inquiry by the Commission or suchgoverning authority or the Institute, as the casemay be, for misconduct, inefficiency ordereliction of duty; or
