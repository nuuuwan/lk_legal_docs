Preamble.

Short title.

Incorporationof theJanakaraliyaCulturalFoundation.

AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE JANAKARALIYACULTURAL FOUNDATION

WHERE a Foundation called and known as the“Janakaraliya Cultural Foundation” has heretofore beenformed for the purpose of effectually carrying out andtransacting all objects and matters connected with thesaid Foundation according to the rules agreed to by itsmembers:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated and it will be for the public advantage to grantsuch application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Janakaraliya CulturalFoundation (Incorporation) Act, No. 2016.

2.From and after the date of commencement ofthis Act, such and so may persons as now are membersof the “Janakaraliya Cultural Foundation” (hereinafterreferred to as the “Foundation”) or shall hereafter beadmitted as members of the Corporation hereby constitutedshall be a body corporate (hereinafter referred to as the“Corporation”) with perpetual succession under the nameand style of “Janakaraliya Cultural Foundation” and by thatname may sue and be sued in all courts, with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and alter the sameat its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to build awareness amongst the people of everystrata about the importance of cultural and spiritualdevelopment parallel to the material developmentand the importance and possibility of the usage ofdrama and theatre and other arts as supportivehuman activites within that integral development,to build a sensitive, creative, wise and activesociety;

(b)to provide access for the majority of people in thecountry to theatre of performance without anyethnic, socio-cultural or political discrimination,by broadening the usage of it and motivating themthrough various methods to participate in dramaand theatre and arts;

(c)to use and support to sue drama and theatre andother arts to enable majority of society tounderstand and build self esteem, whileundrstanding tolerating, appreciating, admiringothers and their various ideologies, inclinations,behaviours, cultures that enable them tocommunicate and interact with each othereffectively and as a result support building a societyin which everybody can live in harmony and peace;

(d)to support to create a society to understand thenature and build a good relationship with the naturethat enables living in harmony with nature;

(e)to prepare, print, publish, issue, edit and cirulatebooks, brochures, pamphlets, journals, magazines,periodicals, articles, bulletins and audio visualmaterials and to disseminate information andknowledge on drama and theatre and other arts andits ancillary arts as educational subjects and also toutilize audio-visual media to fulfill the samepurpose.

4.(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall, subject to theother provisions of this Act and the rules of the Corporation,made under Section 6, be administerd by a Board ofDirectors consisting of Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer andanother five members elected in accordance with such rules.

(2) The first Board of the Corporation shall consist of themembers of the Board of Administration of the Foundationholding office on the day immediately preceding the date ofhis election.

(3) A member of the Board of Directors shall unless hevacates his office earlier by death or resignation, holds officefor a period of four years reckoned from the date of hiselection.

(4) If any member of the Board of Directors vacates hisoffice prior to the expiry of his term, his successor elected inaccordance with rules of the Corporation shall hold officeonly for the unexpired term of office of the member who hesucceeds.
