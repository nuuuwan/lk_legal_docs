Statement of Legal Effect

Clause 2 : This clause amends the Long Title to the Registration of Deaths (Temporary

Provisions) Act, No. 19 of 2010 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and

is consequential to the inclusion of the new Part 1A to the said enactment.

Clause 3 : This clause amends the Preamble to the principal enactment and the legal

effect of the preamble as amended is to specify the scope of application of the principal

enactment consequent to the intention of Part 1A.

Clause 4 : This clause makes a general amendment to the principal enactment and the

legal effect of the amendment is to name the principal enactment as a special provisions to

more accurately specify the intention envisaged in the said enactment.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 1 of the principal enactment and the legal effect

of the section as amended is to prescribe different dates of extensions in respect of

different Parts of the principal enactment.

Clause 6 : This clause amends section 2 of the principal enactment and is consequential

to the amendment made to the Schedule.

Clause 7 : This clause amends section 6 of the principal enactment by the addition of

subsection (5) and the legal effect of the amendment is to prohibit the issue of a Certificate

of Death in respect of a missing person prior to cancellation of a Certificate of Absence.

Clause 8 : This clause inserts a new section 7A and the legal effect of the section as

inserted is to provide for relief to the District Court in respect of an applicant dissatisfied

with the decision of the Registrar-General.

Clause 9 : This clause amends section 8 of the principal enactment and is consequential

to the amendment made to the Schedule.

Clause 10 : This clause inserts a new Part 1A in the principal enactment and the new

part provides for the registration of persons missing due to the conflict in the Northern and

Eastern Provinces, political unrest or civil disturbances or enforced disappearances and

the issuance of a certificate of absence to the relative of the missing person.

Clause 11 : This clause amends section 10 of the principal enactment and is consequential

to the amendment made to the Schedule.

Clause 12 : This clause repeals section 14 of the principal enactment and is consequential

to the operation of Parts 1 and 1A.

Clause 13 : This clause amends section 15 of the principal enactment and the legal

effect of the section as amended is to extend the provisions relating to offences and

penalties.

Clause 14 : This clause amends section 17 of the principal enactment by the insertion

of new definitions for the words “Minister” and “relative”.

Clause 15 : This clause amends the schedule to the principal enactment by inclusion of

the new Forms “B” and “C”. Form B specifies the format of application for registration

of a missing person and for the issue of a certificate of Absence. Form C specifies the

format of certificate of Absence.
