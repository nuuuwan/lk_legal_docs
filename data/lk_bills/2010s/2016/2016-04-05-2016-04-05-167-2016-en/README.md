AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE NADUATHUL ULAMA ARABICCOLLEGE, MUTUR

WHEREAS an College called and known as the“Naduathul Ulama Arabic College” has been formed atMutur, Sri Lanka for the purpose of effectually fulfillingand carrying out all the objects and matters connected withthe said “Naduathul Ulama Arabic College” according tothe rules agreed to by its members and whereas the saidinstitution has heretofore successfully carried out andtransacted the several objects and matters for which it wasestablished:

AND the Board of Management of the said “Mutur NaduathulUlama Arabic College” has applied to incorporate the collegeand it will be for the public advantage to grant suchapplication:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Naduathul Ulama ArabicCollege, Mutur (Incorporation) Act, No. 2016.

2.From and after the date of commencement of this Act,the persons who had heretofore managed and carried on theaffairs of the Mutur Naduathul Ulama Arabic College(hereinafter referred to as the “College”) and the persons whoshall hereafter be admitted to the Board of Management inaccordance with the provisions of this Act and rules madethereunder, shall be and become an incorporated body(hereinafter referred to as the“ Corporation”) with the perpetualsuccession under the name and the style of “Mutur NaduathulUlama Arabic College” and by that name may sue and be suedin all courts ,with full power and authority to have and use acommon seal and alter the same at its will and pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to improve the religious, educational, social,economical and cultural development of Muslimsin Sri Lanka;

(b)to encourge the teaching of Arabic Language, itsgrammar, literature, verse and prose and poems, andother similar subects to enable the students to gainthe complete knowledge, skills and abilities inArabic Language;

(c)to improve the teaching of ‘Sharia’ or Islamic Lawin accordance with ‘Fiqh’ ‘Tafseer’ and ‘Hadeeths’and other similar subjects which are available inArabic language;

(d)to encourage the teaching of recital of ‘Al Quran’according to ‘Thajweed’ and ‘Hifdhul Quran’, therecital from memory of the ‘Holy Quran’;

(e)to encourage the teaching of other subjects similarto these, which are available in Arabic Languageand are helpful to the students to gain an intenseknowledge in matters mentioned in paragraph (a),(b), (c) and (d); and

(f)to do all the acts and things which are necessary orconducive to the attainment of the above mentionedobjects or any one of them since they are notinconsistent with the provisions of this Act or anyother written law :

Provided always that the subjects referred to in this sectionshall be exclusively in accordance with the “SunnathWaljamaath”.

4.Subject to the provisions of this Act and any otherwritten law the Corporation shall have the power to do,perform and execute all such acts, matters and thingswhatsoever as are necessary, desirable or expendient for thepromotion or furtherance of the objects of the Corporationor any one of them including the power to open, operate andclose bank accounts, to borrow or raise money with or withoutsecurity, to invest its funds, to engage, employ and dismissofficers and servants for the carrying out of the objects of theCorporation.

5.(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall, subject to therules of the Corporation be administered by a Board ofManagement consisting of members elected in accordancewith the rules of the Corporation.

(2) The first Board of Management of the Corporationshall be the members of the Board of Management ofCollege holding office on the day preceding the date ofcommencement of this Act.

6.(1) It shall be lawful for the Corporation from timeto time at any General Meeting of the Members and by thevotes of not less than two-thirds of the members present andvoting to make rules which are not inconsistent with theprovisions of this Act or any other written law for themanagement of the affairs of the Corporation andaccomplishment of its objects, such rules when made may atthe meeting and in like manner be altered, added to, amendedor rescinded.

(2) The rules of the College in force on the precedingdate of the commencement of this Act, shall be deemed to bethe rules of the Corporation made under this section.

(3) The members of the Corporation shall be subject tothe rules of the Corporation.

7.All debts and liabilities of the college existing on theday preceding the date of commencement of this Act shallbe paid by the Corporation and debt due to and subscriptionsand contributions payable to the college on day shall bepaid to the Corporation for the purpose of this Act.

8.The Corporation shall be able in law to take and holdany property movable or immovable which may becomevested it by virtue of any purchase, grant, gift, testamentarydisposition or otherwise and all such property shall be heldby the Corporation for the purpose of this Act and subject tothe Corporation with full power to sell, mortgage, lease, orotherwise dispose of all the same.

9.The money and property of the Corporationwhensoever deriving shall be applied solely towards thepromotion of its objects as set forth herein, and no portionthereof shall be paid or transfrerred directly or indirectly byway of divident, loan, or otherwise howsoever by way ofprofit to the members of the Corporation :

Provided however, that the provisions of this sectionshall not prohibit—

(a)the payment in good faith of reasonable and properremuneration to any officer or employee of theCorporation for any service rendered by him to theCorporation;
