AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE AL-JAMIATHUL GHAWSIYYAH

WHEREAS an Institute called and known as the “Al-Jamiathul Ghawsiyyah ” has heretofore been established byHis Holiness Assaiyyidh Asseikh Abdur Rasheed KoyaThangal Al-Qadhiriyyah Wa, - Refaiyyah devoted genuineservices for Islam and for the purpose of effectually carryingout and transacting all objects and matters connected withthe said Institute according to the rules agreed to by HisHoliness Assaiyyidh Asseikh Abdur Rasheed Koya ThangalAl-Qadhiriyyah Wa, - Refaiyyah:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and administered smoothly theseveral objects and matters for which it was formed and hasapplied to be incorporated and it is for the public advantageto grant such application:

Be it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Al-JamiathulGhawsiyyah (Incorporation) Act, No. 2016.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many incorporation of the persons aspresently are members of the Al-Jamiathul Ghawsiyyah(hereinafter referred to as the “Institute”) or shall hereafterbe admitted Ghawsiyyah members of the Corporation herebyconstituted shall be a body corporate (hereinafter referred toas the “Corporation”) with perpetual succession under nameand style of the “Al-Jamiathul Ghawsiyyah” and by thatname may sue and be sued in all courts with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and alter the sameat its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isestablished are hereby declared to be—

(a)to conduct classes to promote religious discoursesof the Holy Quran, Hadhees, Fiqhu and Hiflu;

6.(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall, subject to therules in force for the time being fo the Corporation, beadministered by a Board of Administration consisting ofsuch number of persons as may be provided for in such rulesand appointed periodically by the Life Patrons at their solediscretion.

(2) The first Board of Administration of the Corporationshall be the Executive Committee of the Corporation holdingoffice on the day preceding the date of Commencement ofthe Law.

(3) It shall be lawful for the Corporation, from time to time,at the discretion of the Life Patrons to make rules, notinconsistent with the provisions of this Act or any otherwritten law.

(5) The lands and buildings of the Institute is the propertyof the Late Founder His Holiness Assaiyyidh AsshheikhKoya Thangal Al-Qadhiriyyah War-Rifa’iyyah(Qaddasallahu Sirrahul Azeez) and his heirs and successors.
