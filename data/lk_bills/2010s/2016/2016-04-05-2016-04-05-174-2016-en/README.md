AN ACT TO AMEND THE SRI LANKA INSTITUTE OF INTERIOR DESIGNERS(INCORPORATION) ACT, NO. 2 OF 1998

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka Institute ofInterior Designers (Incorporation) (Amendment) Act,No. of 2016.

2.The aim of the Bill is to amend the section No. 4 ofthe Act under the general powers of Corporation, to includethe following:—

(1)membership in the Corporation is a requirement forall Practitioners in the filed of Interior Design andRecognition of the Professional Status and Licencefor practice of an Interior Designer, InteriorArchitect, Interior Technologist and InteriorDecorater will be for those who have obtainedapproval from the Corporation fulfilling therequirements and abiding by the Rules &Regulations and Ethical Code of Practice;

(2)the Corporation will be the responsible body toaward Charter Status (Chartered Interior Designer)to those who specialize and qualify for suchrecognition in the field of Interior Design;

(3)the Corporation will provide a Quality AssuranceCertificate for Market Acceptance on such InteriorProducts of good Designer value and productionquality;

(4)Recommendation by the Corporation will benecessary for the awarding of Certificates forcompetency in Technical skills in the filed ofInterior Decoration and Interior Technology;
