STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 43 of the Medical Ordinance (Chapter 105)(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of the section asamended is to incorporate consequential amendments to section 43 in compliance with theprovisions of new section 43A.

Clause 3 : This clause inserts new section 43A and 43B in the principal enactment andthe section makes provision for the provisional registration of dentists under the principalenactment and for the rights of persons provisionally registered as dentists.
