STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 431 of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15of 1979 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of thesection as amended is to—

(a)make provision to reduce the period granted for a person to establish his claim forseized property taken under section 29 or stolen; and

(b)increase the value of the property for which notification is required under thatsection.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 432 of the principal enactment and provides for aconsequential amendment to the marginal note of that section.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE CODE OF CRIMINAL PROCEDUREACT, NO. 15 OF 1979

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Code of CriminalProcedure (Amendment) Act, No. of 2016.

2.Section 431 of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act,No. 15 of 1979 (hereinafter referred to as the “principalenactment”) is hereby amended as follows:—

(a)in subsection (2) of that section, by the substitutionfor the words “within six months” of the words“within two months”; and

(b)in subsection (3) of that section, by the substitutionfor the words “two thousand five hundred rupees ormore.” of the words “twenty five thousand rupeesor more.”.

3.Section 432 of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the repeal of the marginal note to that sectionand the substitution therefor of the following:—

“Procedure where no claimant appears within twomonths.”.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
