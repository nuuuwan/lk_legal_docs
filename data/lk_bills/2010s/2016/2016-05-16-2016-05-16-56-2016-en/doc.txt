AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SRI LANKA ASSOCIATION FORCHILD DEVELOPMENT (SLACD)

WHEREAS a Association called and known as the“Sri Lanka Association for Child Development (SLACD)”has heretofore being formed in Sri Lanka for the purpose ofeffectually carrying out and transacting the objects andmatters connected with the said Association according tothe rules agreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Association has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant such application.

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka Associationfor Child Development (SLACD) (Incorporation) Act,No. of 2016.

2.From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as presently are members ofthe “Sri Lanka Association for Child Development(SLACD)” (hereinafter referred to as the “Association”) orshall hereafter be admitted as members of the Corporationhereby constituted, shall be a body corporate (hereinafterreferred to as the “Corporation”) with perpetual successionunder the name and style of the “Sri Lanka Association forChild Development (SLACD)” and by that name may sueand be sued in all courts, with full power and authority tohave and use a common seal and alter the same at its pleasure.

3.The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to promote, facilitate and support the advancementof professional knowledge and skills in childdevelopment;

(b)to carry out, promote, facilitate and support researchand interest in child development and developmentdisorders in children;

(c)to strive for the development of children and welfareof children with development disorders;

(d)to advocate to improve the quality of life of childrenwith development disorders and children in generaland their families;

(e)the purpose of the SLACD shall be to promote,facilitate and support advancement of knowledgeand skills;

(f)research and interest in child development anddevelopmental disorders in children; and

(g)the SLACD will strive to apply these purposes forthe welfare of children concerned, their familiesand the community through activities determinedby the Council of the SLACD.

SLACD may receive, use and hold donations and gifts,financial and in other kind, in order to carry forward theabove mentioned purposes.

SLACD shall always be operated solely for the aforesaidpurposes, without discrimination, provided that no part ofits resources are used for the benefit of or be distributable toany member, or office bearer of the SLACD or any private
