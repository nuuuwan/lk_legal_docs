JUDICATURE (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 4 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978 and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to increase the number of judges of the High Court.

Judicature (Amendment)1

L.D.—O. 32/2017

AN ACT TO AMEND THE JUDICATURE ACT, NO. 2 OF 1978

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Judicature (Amendment)Act, No. of 2017.

2.Section 4 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978 is herebyamended by the repeal of paragraph (a) of that section andthe substitution therefor of the following paragraph:—

“(a) not less than ten and not more than eighty five judges,each of whom shall be known as a “Judge of theHigh Court;”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
