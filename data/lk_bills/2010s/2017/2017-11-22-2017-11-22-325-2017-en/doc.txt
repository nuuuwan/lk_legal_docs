PART I

ESTABLISHMENT OF THE COMMISSION

4.The object and purpose of this Part shall be –

(a)to establish the Securities and ExchangeCommission of Sri Lanka for the creation andmaintenance of a fair, efficient andtransparent securities market;

(b)to protect the interests of investors both localand foreign;

(c)to ensure the maintenance of highprofessional standards in the provision ofservices under this Act; and

(d)to mitigate systemic risks in the financialsystem.
