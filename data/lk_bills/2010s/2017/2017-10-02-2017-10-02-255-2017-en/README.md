AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE THAREEQATHUL AROOSIYYATHILQAADHIRIYYAH ASSOCIATION OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS His Holiness Qutbuz Zaman Madihus SibtainAl- Allama As-Shaikh Imamul Aroos Sayyid MuhammadMapillai Lebbai Alim Waliyullah has established, foundedand maintained several Mosques, Thakkiyas, Madrasas,Charitable Trusts, Muslim Shrines and Places of Religiousresort and other institutions in Sri Lanka:

AND WHEREAS His Holiness Qutbuz Zaman MadihusSibtain Al- Allama As-Shaikh Imamul Aroos SayyidMuhammad Mapillai Lebbai Alim Waliyullah formed anAssociation called the Thareeqathul AroosiyyathilQaadhiriyyah Association in Sri Lanka and abroad for thepurpose of effectually carrying out the objects andtransacting all matters connected with the said Associationaccording to the practices, rites, customs and rules laid downby the said Spiritual Head and agreed by its members :

AND WHEREAS the said Association has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the said application :

SECOND SCHEDULE

The Rules and Regulations of the Corporation

1. Membership to the Corporation shall be open to all personsprofessing the sunnath wal jamaath Islamic faith.

2. Unless otherwise amended all rules hithertobefore relating to theAssociation shall continue to apply to the Corporation including rulesrelating to—

(a)the conduct of spiritual activities;

(b)the persons who would provide spiritual and religiousservices;

(c)the number of trustees, their appointment, removal,disciplinary control and dismissal ;

(d)the number of members, their admission, removal,disciplinary control and expulsion;

(e)succession of the Spiritual Head; and

(f)rules relating to the basic tenets of the Corporation.

3. These rules may be changed, varied, amended or rescinded fromtime to time except those rules relating to the basic tenets of theCorporation.
