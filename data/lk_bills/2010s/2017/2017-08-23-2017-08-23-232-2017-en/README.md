AN ACT TO AMEND THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATICSOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Twentieth Amendment tothe Constitution.

2.The following new Article is hereby insertedimmediately after Article 154D of the Constitution of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka (hereinafterreferred to as the “Constitution”) and shall have effect asArticle 154DD of the Constitution:—

154DD. The election of members to allProvincial Councils shall be held on the samedate and the Parliament shall determine the dateon which all the Provincial Councils shall standdissolved (in this Chapter referred to as the“specified date”):

Provided that, such specified date shall notbe later than the expiration of the term of thelast constituted Provincial Council.”.

3.Article 154E of the Constitution is hereby amendedby the substitution for the words “as a dissolution of theCouncil.” of the following:—

“as a dissolution of the Council:

Provided, however, upon the determination of thespecified date under Article 154DD—

(a)the term of office of any Provincial Councilending prior to the specified date shall be deemed
