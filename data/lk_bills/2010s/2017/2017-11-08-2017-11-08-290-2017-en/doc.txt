STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends the Fisheries (Regulation of Foreign Fishing Boats) Act(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) by making a general amendment andthe legal effect of the section as amended is to transfer the functions of the Secretary inrelation to the subject to the Director General.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 3 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to include Director General of Coast Guard Department andany other person designated by name or by office by the Minister in writing as personswho are authorized to implement and enforce the provisions of the Act.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 13 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is–

(1)to enable any authorized officer to–

(i)bring any abandoned foreign fishing boat to the nearest port;

(ii)produce any foreign fishing boats seized or detained as the case may beunder this Act with the crew where such boat is not abandoned, beforethe Magistrate instead of High-Court in order to overcome practicaldifficulties; and

(2)to transfer the authority of the Director or the Government agent to the DirectorGeneral.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 13 of the principal enactment by introducingnew section 13A and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for thesubmission of a notice to the consular officer of the country to which the abandoned orseized foreign fishing boat belongs with details specified therein and for recovery of costand incidental expenses accrued, to the Government of Sri Lanka in respect of the detention.

Clause 6 : This clause amends section 15 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to impose enhanced fines based on the length of the fishingboat for the offences committed contravening section 4 and section 5 of the Act in theterritorial waters and the Exclusive Economic Zone of Sri Lanka.

Clause 7 : This clause amends section 16 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to enable the Magistrates to exercise power in respect ofoffences committed under this Act, and to enhance the fine in respect of violation ofconditions of a permit by a foreign fishing boat.

Clause 8 : This clause amends section 17 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to enable Magistrates to exercise power in respect of enhancedfines for the offence of obstructing authorized officers, taking into consideration thelength of the fishing boat.

Clause 9 : This clause inserts new section 17A to the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to make all offences committed under this Act cognizableand non-bailable.

Clause 10 : This clause amends section 18 of the principal enactment and is consequentialto the amendment made by clause 8.

Clause 11 : This clause amends section 19 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to enable High Court to hear the original appeals givingan opportunity to be heard again by Court of Appeal and to change the designation ofDirector as the Director General.

Clause 12 : This clause inserts new sections 19A, 19B and 19C to the principal enactmentand the legal effect of the section as amended is to vest the ownership of the abandonedforeign fishing boat in the State until legal entitlement is proved by a relevant party withinthe prescribed period to the Magistrate.

Clause 13 : This clause amends section 20 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to provide for the imposition of a penalty by theDirector General for offences committed for the first time in the Exclusive EconomicZone together with procedure to be adhered to.

Clause 14 : This clause amends section 20 of the principal enactment by the insertionof new section 20A to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section asamended is to provide for the appointment of a panel of experts.

Clause 15 : This clause amends section 21 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to provide for the release of any boat on the provisionof a satisfactory bond or security until the conclusion of any prosecution and to enable theMagistrate to exercise powers in respect of offences committed under this Act.

Clause 16 : This clause amends section 23 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to make provision for the presumption, on violation ofthe provisions of the Act where a foreign fishing boat is found in Sri Lanka Waters.

Clause 17 : This clause amends section 24 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to enable the Magistrate to exercise powers in respect ofoffences committed under this Act.

Clause 18 : This clause amends section 26 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to widen the regulation making power for theimplementation of standards stipulated in International conventions and agreements.

Clause 19 : This clause amends section 26 of the principal enactment by the insertionof new section 26A and the legal effect of the section as amended is to impose a fine inrespect of violation of regulations.

Clause 20 : This clause amends section 27 of the principal enactment by the insertionof new section 27A and the legal effect of that section as amended is to establish a fundcalled and known as Fisheries Reward Fund.

Clause 21 : This clause amends section 28 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to include new definitions to the expressions “abandonedforeign fishing boat” and “Director General” and to substitute definitions for the expressions“fish”, “fishing”, “local fishing boat”, “related activities”, “Secretary” and “Sri LankaWaters”.
