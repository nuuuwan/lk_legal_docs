AN ACT TO AMEND THE PRADESHIYA SABHA ACT, NO. 15 OF 1987

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Pradeshiya Sabha(Amendment) Act, No. of 2017.

2.Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of1987 is hereby repealed and the following sectionsubstituted therefor:—

4.(1) Each Pradeshiya Sabha shall consistof such number of members—

(a)as specified by the Minister by Orderpublished in the Gazette, based onthe Orders published under sections3C and 3D of the Local AuthoritiesElections Ordinance (Chapter 262);

(b)equivalent to forty per centum of thenumber of members as specified bythe Minister by Order made underparagraph (a), who shall be returnedin terms of section 65B of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance(Chapter 262); and

(c)determined by the Commissionerunder paragraph (a) of subsection (4)of section 65B of the LocalAuthorities Elections Ordinance(Chapter 262).
