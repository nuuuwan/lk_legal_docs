PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONS(AMENDMENT)

to amend the Provincial Councils Elections Act, No. 2 of 1988

1Provincial Councils Elections (Amendment)

Short title.

L.D.—O. 40/2017

AN ACT TO AMEND THE PROVINCIAL COUNCILS ELECTIONSACT, NO. 2 OF 1988

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Provincial CouncilsElections (Amendment) Act, No. of 2017.

2.Section 10 of the Provincial Councils Elections Act,No. 2 of 1988 is hereby amended in subsection (1) of thatsection, by the substitution for the words and figures from"Within one week" to "an election to such Council.", of thewords and figures "Within one week from the date specifiedin terms of Article 154DD of the Constitution, theCommissioner shall publish a notice of his intention to holdan election to all Provincial Councils.".

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
