STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 5 of the Commercial Mediation Centre ofSri Lanka Act, No. 44 of 2000 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”), and thelegal effect of the section as amended is to increase the number of members of the Board ofManagement of the Commercial Mediation Centre with a view to ensuring better co-ordinationamong the line agencies.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 6 of the principal enactment and the legal effect ofthe section as amended is to enable the Minister to appoint the Chairman of the Board ofManagement and to specify the manner by which the Chairman can be removed from office.
