FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES(AMENDMENT)

to amend the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 28 of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No.2 of 1996 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) by the introduction of newsection 28A and the legal effect of the new section is to prohibit, fishing operations utilizingTrawl Nets towed by motorized or mechanized fishing boats and the sale, import andtransport of such nets and the issuance of license relating to utilization of such nets.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 49 of the principal enactment by the addition of anew subsection which makes it an offence to contravene the provisions of section 28A.

Clause 4: This clause amends section 66 of the principal enactment by the introductionof a definition for the expression “Trawl Net”.

Clause 5: This clause amends all the laws which are contrary to the content of theprovisions of section 28A, making section 28A to prevail over such laws.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCESACT, NO. 2 OF 1996

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Fisheries and AquaticResources (Amendment) Act, No. of 2017.

2.The following new section is hereby insertedimmediately after section 28 of the Fisheries and AquaticResources Act, No. 2 of 1996 (hereinafter referred to as the“principal enactment”) and shall have effect as section28A of that enactment:—

28A. (1) (a) No person shall engage in orcause any other person to be engaged in fishingoperations utilizing Trawl Nets, including netswithout two wings or using vertical poles atthe two free ends of the net, to be towed bymotorized or mechanized fishing boats.

(b) No person shall use, possess, import,transport, purchase or sell any Trawl Net, to betowed by a motorized or mechanized fishingboat for the purpose of carrying out fishingoperations in Sri Lanka Waters.

(2) No license shall be issued by theDirector-General under Part II of this Act forfishing operations carried out by Trawl Nets tobe towed by a motorized or mechanized fishingboat in Sri Lanka Waters.
