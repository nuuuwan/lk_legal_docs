AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE INTERNATIONAL WOMEN’S ANDCHILDREN’S RIGHTS COUNCIL (IWCRC)

WHEREAS a Council called and known as the “InternationalWomen’s and Children’s Rights Council (IWCRC)” hasheretofore been established in Sri Lanka for the purpose ofeffectually carrying out its objects and transacting all mattersconnected with the said Council according to the rulesagreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Council has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant such application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the International Women’sand Children’s Rights Council (IWCRC) (Incorporation)Act, No. of 2017.

2.(1) From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as precently are members ofthe International Women’s and Children’s Rights Council(IWCRC) (hereinafter referred to as the “Council”) or shallhereafter be admitted as mambers of the Corporation herebyconstituted, shall be a body corporate with perpetualsuccession under the name and style of the “InternationalWomen’s and Children’s Rights Council (IWCRC)”(hereinafter referred to as “the Corporation”) and by thatname may sue and be sued in all Courts with full power andauthority to have and use a common seal and alter the sameat its pleasure.

(2) The Corporation shall be deemed to be a VoluntarySocial Service Organization within the meaning, and for thepurpose of the Voluntary Social Services Organizations(Registration of the Supervision) Act, No. 31 of 1980 andthe provisions of that Act shall apply to and in relation tothe management of the affairs of the Corporation.

3.(1) The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:—

(a)to safeguard, protect and promte the rights ofwomen and children everywhere within thebroad perspective of the protection of HumanRights;

(b)to support and uplift the lives of mothers andchildren who are victims of social injusticesand disabilities;

(c)to prevent the occurrence of all forms of abuse,violence and crimes against women andchildren;

(d)to assist needy school children financially orotherwise by way of donations, prizes andscholarships enabling them to continue theireducation and encourage their regularattendance at school and to assist them in theireducational and other development activities;

(e)to provide shelter and safe house facilities tomothers and children of disrupted families andimplement programmes for their well beingincluding family reunion; and

(f)to do all such other acts, matters and things asare incidental or conducive to the attainmentof the objectives of the Corporation.
