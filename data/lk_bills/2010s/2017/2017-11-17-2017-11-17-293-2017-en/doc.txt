STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 2 of the Appropriation Act, No. 24 of 2016 and the legaleffect of that section as amended is to increase the borrowing limit of the Government for year2017.

Appropriation (Amendment)1

L.D.—O. 58/2017

AN ACT TO AMEND THE APPROPRIATION ACT, NO. 24 OF 2016

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Appropriation(Amendment) Act, No. of 2017.

2.Section 2 of the Appropriation Act, No. 24 of 2016 ishereby amended in subsection (1) of that section, by thesubstitution in paragraph (b) of that subsection for the words“shall not exceed rupees one thousand five hundred seventynine billion one hundred ten million four hundred thirty sixthousand”, of the words “shall not exceed rupees one thousandsix hundred twenty nine billion”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
