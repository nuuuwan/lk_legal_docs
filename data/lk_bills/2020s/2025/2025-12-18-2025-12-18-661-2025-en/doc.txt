Short title

Insertion of new section 54AA in Chapter 218

“Prohibition against manufacturing, possession or trafficking of dangerous drugs on high seas.

PL 15590 - 337 (12/2025)

An Act To Amend The Poisons, Opium And Dangerous

Drugs Ordinance (Chapter 218)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1. This Act may be cited as the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs (Amendment) Act, No. of 2025.

2. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 54A of the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance (Chapter 218) (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and shall have effect as section 54AA of that Ordinance:-

54AA. (1) Any person who, whether he is a citizen of Sri Lanka or not,-

(a) on the high seas, while on board a ship registered in Sri Lanka or in a State other than Sri Lanka or a stateless ship; and

(b) except as permitted by, or otherwise than in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter or a licence of the Director,

manufactures, possesses or trafficks any dangerous drug set out in Part III of the Third Schedule, in excess of the amount set out in Column II of Part III of the said Schedule, shall

be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction by the High Court, be liable to the penalty set out in the corresponding entry in Column III of Part III of the said Schedule.

(2) Any stateless ship engaged in the commission of an offence referred to in subsection (1) shall be subject to forfeiture and the provisions of section 79 shall mutatis mutandis apply in relation to such ship.

(3) For the purposes of this section-

“high seas” shall have the meaning

assigned to it under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS);

“manufacture” shall have the same

meaning as in subsection (3) of section 54A;

“stateless ship” means any vessel that

is not registered under the laws of any State or does not possess a flag;

“traffick” shall have the same meaning as

in subsection (3) of section 54A.”.
