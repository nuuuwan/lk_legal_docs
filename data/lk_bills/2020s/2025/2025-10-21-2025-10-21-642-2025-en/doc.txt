Betting and Gaming Levy (Amendment)02

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 2 of the Betting and Gaming Levy Act, No. 40 of 1988 and the legal effect of the section as amended is to –

(a) limit the gross collection of ten per centum to have been applicable only for the time period April 1, 2023 to October 1, 2025;

(b) increase the gross collection levy from persons carrying on a business of bookmaker or business of gambling to eighteen per centum from October 1, 2025; and

(c) increase the casino entrance levy from persons who are citizens of Sri Lanka, to United States Dollars one hundred.
