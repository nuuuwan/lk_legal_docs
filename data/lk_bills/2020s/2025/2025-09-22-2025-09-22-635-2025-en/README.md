STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends the section 7 of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects Law, No.1 of 1976 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of that section as amended is to provide for those who have been enrolled as Associates to the Institute on the day immediately preceding the date of coming into operation of this Act, to be considered as members of the Institute.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 12 of the principal enactment and is consequential to the amendment made by the clause 2.

Clause 4: This clause is consequential to the amendment made by the clause 2.

An Act to amend the Sri Lanka Institute of

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (Amendment) Act, No. of 2025.

2. Section 7 of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects Law, No. 1 of 1976 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended in subsection (1) thereof, by the substitution for all the words starting from “The corporate members” to the word, “Associates:” of the following:-

“With effect from the date of coming into operation of this Act, the corporate members of the Institute shall consist of Fellows and Members:”. 3. Section 12 of the principal enactment is hereby amended as follows:-

(1) in subsection (1) thereof, by the substitution for the words “and an Associate the initials “A.I.A (Sri Lanka)”.” of the words “and a Member the initials “M.I.A (Sri Lanka)”.” ;

(2) by the repeal of subsection (4) thereof, and the substitution therefor of the following :-
