Presented by the Hon. (Mrs.) Rohini Kumari Wijerathna, M. P. for Matale District on 22nd of July, 2025

(Published in the Gazette on July 04, 2025)

Ordered by Parliament to be printed

1Land Development (Amendment)

Short title

AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAND DEVELOPMENT ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 464)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1. This Act may be cited as the Land Development (Amendment) Act, No. of 2025.

2. Section 48A of the principal enactment is hereby amended by the repeal of the words “or upon his or her marriage” in subsection 48A 2(c).

3. Section 48B of the principal enactment is hereby amended by the repeal of subsection 48B 1(a).

4. Section 68 of the principal enactment is hereby amended in subsection (1) of that section by the substitution for the words “fails to succeed” of the words “fails without reasonable cause to succeed”.

5. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
