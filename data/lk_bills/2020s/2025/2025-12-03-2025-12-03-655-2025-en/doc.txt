Universities (Amendment)02

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 49 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of that section as amended is to make provisions to –

(a) enable the Faculty Board to elect a Dean from among the Senior Professors, Professors, Associate Professors and Senior Lecturers (Grade I) of the Faculty;

(b) enable the Council to remove a Dean from his office prior to the completion of his term of office; and

(c) introduce limitations with respect to the term of office of a Dean of a Faculty.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 51 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of that section as amended is to make provisions to –

(a) specify the procedure to be followed by the Council when appointing a Head of a Department of Study;

(b) enable the Council to remove the Head of a Department of Study from his office prior to the completion of his term of office; and

(c) introduce limitations applicable with respect to the terms of office of a Head of a Department of Study.

“The Dean shall be elected by the Faculty Board from among the Senior Professors, Professors, Associate Professors and Senior Lecturers (Grade I), of the Faculty.”; and

(2) by the insertion immediately after the re-lettered paragraph (a) of subsection (1), of the following paragraph: -
