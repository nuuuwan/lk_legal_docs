Social Security Contribution Levy (Amendment)02

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends the First Schedule to the Social Security Contribution Levy Act, No. 25 of 2022, and the legal effect of that schedule as amended is to -

(a) restrict the exemption provided under item 24 of PART IA by setting out a time period applicable to any institution referred to in such item;

(b) make the exemption provided under item 4 of PART IB clearer to include the wholesale and retail sale of such exempted articles;

(c) expand the exemption provided under item 4 of PART II by applying the exemption to services provided in relation to international transportation by container terminal operators; and

(d) exempt the financial services under PART II.

An Act to amend the Social Security Contribution Levy

Act, No. 25 of 2022

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Social Security Contribution Levy (Amendment) Act, No. of 2025.

(2) The provisions of this Act, other than the provisions of subsections (1), (2) and (3) of section 2, shall come into operation on the date on which the Bill becomes an Act of Parliament.

(3) The provisions of subsections (1), (2) and (3) of section 2 shall be deemed to have come into operation on February 18, 2025, July 1, 2025 and January 1, 2026 respectively.

2. The First Schedule to the Social Security Contribution Levy Act, No. 25 of 2022 is hereby amended as follows: -

(1) in item 24 of PART IA thereof, by the substitution for the words “Ceylon

Electricity Board to supply”, of the words and figures “Ceylon Electricity Board prior to

February 18, 2025 to supply”;

(2) by the repeal of item 4 of PART IB thereof, and the substitution therefor of the following:-

“4. (a) petrol, diesel or kerosene sold in a filling

station prior to July 1, 2025; and

(b) petrol, diesel or kerosene, effective from July

1, 2025;”; and
