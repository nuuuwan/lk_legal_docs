3. Public debt management is the process of establishingand executing a medium to long term strategy for managingpublic debt with the following objectives:–

(a)to meet financing needs and debt paymentobligations on a timely basis;

(b)to borrow at the lowest costs as possible over themedium to long term, consistent with a prudentdegree of risk; and

(c)to promote the development of the domestic debtsecurities market.

Provided that, the Minister shall take into considerationthe following when issuing such loan guarantees: -
