L.D.- O. 60/2024

AN ACT TO AMEND THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATICSOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Twenty SecondAmendment to the Constitution.

2. Article 83 of the Constitution of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka is hereby amended in paragraph (b)thereof, by the substitution for the words “to over six years,”,of the words “to over five years,”.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.Sinhala textto prevail incase ofinconsistency
