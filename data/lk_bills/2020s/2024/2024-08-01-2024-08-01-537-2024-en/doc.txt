L. D.- O. 29/2024

AN ACT TO AMEND THE NATIONAL MINIMUM WAGE OF WORKERSACT, NO. 3 OF 2016

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows :-

1. This Act may be cited as the National Minimum Wageof Workers (Amendment) Act, No. of 2024.

2. Section 3 of the National Minimum Wage of WorkersAct, No. 3 of 2016 is hereby amended in subsection (1)thereof, by the substitution for the words “twelve thousandfive hundred rupees and the national minimum daily wageof a worker shall be five hundred rupees”, of the words“seventeen thousand five hundred rupees and the nationalminimum daily wage of a worker shall be seven hundredrupees”.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
