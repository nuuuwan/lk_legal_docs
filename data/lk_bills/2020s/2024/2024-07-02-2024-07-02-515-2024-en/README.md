MEDICAL (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Medical Ordinance (Chapter 105)

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause amends section 19 of the Medical Ordinance (Chapter 105)(hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) in order to add certain matters inrespect of which regulations can be made by the Minister.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 19C of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is to provide for the appointment of a Committee toadvice the Minister regarding any recommendation made by the Medical Council inrespect of any recognized university or institution under subsection (1) of section 19C.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 19E of the principal enactment for betterexplanation of the term “any university or institution”.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 29 of the principal enactment and is consequentialto the amendment made by clause 2.
