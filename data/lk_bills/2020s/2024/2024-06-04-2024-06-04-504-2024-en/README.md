AN ACT TO AMEND THE INSTITUTE OF QUANTITY SURVEYORS,SRI LANKA (INCORPORATION) ACT, NO. 20 OF 2007

WHEREAS it has become necessary, inter alia, to alter theCorporate Membership categories and associated names ofMembers of the Institute of Quantity Surveyors, Sri Lanka:

AND WHEREAS the Institute of Quantity Surveyors,Sri Lanka has requested making of necessary amendmentsto the Institute of Quantity Surveyors, Sri Lanka(Incorporation) Act to provide for above requirements andmatters incidental thereto and it is expendient to affect suchamendment:
