1Land Development (Amendment)

Short title

AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAND DEVELOPMENT ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 464)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1. This Act may be cited as the Land Development(Amendment) Act, No. of 2024.

2. Section 48A of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the repeal of the words “or upon his or hermarriage” in subsection 48A 2(c).

3. Section 48B of the principal enactment is herebyamended by the repeal of subsection 48B 1(a).

4. Section 68 of the principal enactment is herebyamended in subsection (1) of that section by the substitutionfor the words “fails to succeed” of the words “fails withoutreasonable cause to succeed”.

5.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
