PART I

OBJECTS OF THE ACT

2. The objects of this Act, in relation to the generation,transmission, distribution, trade, supply and procurement ofelectricity shall be, -

(a) to ensure improved Electricity Industryperformance through independent andaccountable corporate entities responsible for theprovision and maintenance, of a well-coordinated,efficient and economical system of electricitysupply throughout Sri Lanka at all times, throughtransparent policies;

(b) to facilitate the establishment of independent andaccountable corporate entities for the efficientsupply of electricity throughout the country andto protect the interests of the consumers;

(c) to promote and facilitate the establishment andfunctioning of the Wholesale Electricity Market;

(d) to promote competition in the Electricity Industryby eliminating preferential treatment and barriersto entry, allowing open competitive procurement
