PART V

PROTECTION OF THE RIGHTS OF AGGRIEVED PERSONS ANDWITNESSES

17. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any otherwritten law, the Commission shall develop and enforce asystem for the protection of aggrieved persons and witnesses.

18. (1) The Commission shall recommend the granting ofmaterial or symbolic reparations to individual aggrievedpersons and witnesses who engage with the Commission,and who are entitled in the opinion of the Commission tosuch reparations, including but not limited to restitution,financial compensation, welfare services and rehabilitation.
