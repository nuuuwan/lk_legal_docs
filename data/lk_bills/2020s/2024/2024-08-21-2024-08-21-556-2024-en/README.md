STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends certain sections of the Penal Code (Chapter 19) whichappear in the Schedule to the Bill, and the legal effect of the sections as amended is toincrease the fines imposed in respect of the relevant offences.

L.D.—O. 45/2019

AN ACT TO AMEND THE PENAL CODE ( CHAPTER 19 )

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Penal Code (Amendment)Act, No. of 2024.

2. The sections of the Penal Code (Chapter 19) specifiedin Column I of the Schedule hereto, are hereby amended bythe substitution for the words specified in the correspondingentry in column III, of the words specified in thecorresponding entry in Column IV of that Schedule for thepurpose of increasing penalties imposed in respect of theoffences specified in Column II thereof.

3. For the avoidance of doubt, it is hereby declared thatthe provisions of section 2 shall not apply in respect of anoffence referred to in the Schedule, where such offence wascommitted prior to the date of coming into operation of thisAct.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
