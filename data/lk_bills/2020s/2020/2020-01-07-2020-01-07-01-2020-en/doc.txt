1Kandyan Marriage and Divorce (Repeal)

AN ACT TO REPEAL THE KANDYAN MARRIAGE AND DIVORCEACT, NO. 44 OF 1952

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Kandyan Marriage andDivorce (Repeal) Act, No. of 2020.

2.The Kandyan Marriage and Divorce Act, No. 44 of1952 is hereby repealed.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
