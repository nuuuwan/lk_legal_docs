Short title.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAND DEVELOPMENT ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 464)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1. This Act may be cited as the Land Development(Amendment) Act, No. of 2020.

2.Rule 1(b) and rule 2 of the Third Schedule of theprincipal Act should be replaced with the following rules:-

“1(b). Title to a holding for the purposes of Section 72shall devolve on the relatives of the permit holder orowner in the order of priority in which they are respectivelymentioned in the subjoined table, the older beingpreferred to the younger if there are more relatives thanone in any group:

Provided that nothing in this rule should give priorityto the older person when a land has been developed byother person than the older person.

Table

(i)Children;

(ii)Grand sons and daughters;

(iii)Parents;

(iv) Brothers and Sisters;

(v) Uncles and Aunts;

(vi) Nephews and Nieces.

In this rule “relative” means, a relative by blood and notby marriage and “children” includes adopted children too.
