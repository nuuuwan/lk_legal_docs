AN ACT TO AMEND THE MARRIAGE REGISTRATION ORDINANCE

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Marriage Registration(Amendment) Act, No. of 2020.

2.The Long title of the Marriage Registration OrdinanceNo. 19 of 1907 (hereinafter referred to as the “principleenactment”) is amended to remove the words “OTHERTHAN THE MARRIAGES OF MUSLIMS AND” therein.

3.In the Section 64 substitute the interpretation of“marriage” with the following :- “marriage” means, “anymarriage contracted under this ordinance save and exceptmarriages contracted under the Kandyan Marriage andDivorce Act or the Muslim Marriage and Divorce Act”.

4.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
