AN ACT TO AMEND THE CIVIL PROCEDURE CODE (SECTION 627)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Civil Procedure Code(Amendment) Act, No. of 2020.

2.Section 627 of the Civil Procedure Code is amendedto remove the words “between persons professing Islam”and replace such words with the words “contracted in termsof the Muslim Marriage and Divorce Act”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
