Short Title

Amendment of section 44 of Act, No. 35 of 2018

Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency

PL 15740 - 460 (04/2026)

L.D.-O. 75/2025

An Act to amend the Finance Act, No. 35 of 2018

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1. This Act may be cited as the Finance (Amendment) Act, No. of 2026.

2. Section 44 of the Finance Act, No. 35 of 2018 is hereby amended as follows :-

(1) in subsection (1) thereof, by the substitution for the words “for each cellular tower.” of the following: -

“for each cellular tower:

Provided that, the levy shall not be charged in respect of any newly constructed cellular tower which commences commercial operations on or after January 1, 2026, for a period of five years commencing from the date of commencement of this Act.”;

(2) in subsection (2) thereof, by the substitution for the words “ The levy shall be paid” of the words “Subject to the provisions of subsection (1), the levy shall be paid ”.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
