1Kandyan Marriage and Divorce (Amendment)

PL 15680- 112(02/2026)

Short title

Amendment of section 32 of the Principal enactment

An Act to amend the Kandyan Marriage and Divorce

Act (Chapter 113)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1. This Act may be cited as the Kandyan Marriage and Divorce (Amendment) Act, No. of 2026.

2. Section 32 of the principal enactment is hereby amended as follows: -

(1) by the substitution for paragraph (a), of the

following:-

(a) “Adultery by the husband or wife after

marrige.”; and

(2) by the repeal of paragraph (b) thereof.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
