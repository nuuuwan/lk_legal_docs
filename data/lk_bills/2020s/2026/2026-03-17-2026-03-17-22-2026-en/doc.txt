Clause 2 : This clause amends section 8 of the Inland Revenue Act, No. 24 of 2017 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions to exclude gains derived from the realisation of motor vehicles when calculating a person’s gains and profits from other sources.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 10 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is –

(a) to make provisions not to allow a deduction in respect of the payment for a transaction, unless such payment is made by way of a deposit into a bank account; and

(b) to rectify an error in the Sinhala text.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 18 of the principal enactment to improve the clarity of that section.

Clause 5 : This clause amends section 46 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to extend the applicability of the section to the transfer of assets to the Government of Sri Lanka or to a University established or deemed to be established under the Universities Act, No.16 of 1978.

Clause 6 : This clause amends section 52 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions to carry forward certain qualifying payments and deduct them in succeeding years of assessment.

Clause 7 : This clause inserts new section 52A in the principal enactment and the legal effect of that section is to make provisions to specify the types of receipts received under a life insurance policy which shall be excluded and included when calculating the assessable income of an individual.

Clause 8 : This clause amends section 59 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for the consequences of the failure to issue a certificate to the unit holder, by a unit trust.

Clause 9 : This clause amends section 67 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to –

(a) improve the clarity of the section; and

(b) make provisions enabling the Commissioner-General to specify the necessary adjustments in line with revisions to the Sri Lanka Accounting Standards applicable to the business of insurance.

Clause 10 : This clause amends section 68 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions enabling Commissioner-General to grant a tax credit to charitable institutions subject to certain conditions.

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 11 : This clause amends section 69 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for the circumstances in which an individual shall not be deemed to be a resident for income tax purposes.

Clause 12 : This clause amends section 72 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions to deduct payments having no source in Sri Lanka and incurred on the export of goods or services from Sri Lanka, when calculating income.

Clause 13 : This clause amends section 79 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions to allow a non-resident person to deduct head office expenditure with effect from April 1, 2025.

Clause 14 : This clause amends section 84 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to require the National Gem and Jewellery Authority to file tax returns and to make provisions to exempt certain interest payments from withholding tax.

Clause 15 : This clause amends section 85 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to broaden the scope of applicability of withholding tax in respect of independent service providers.

Clause 16 : This clause amends section 86 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to enable the Commissioner-General to specify the procedures, formats and forms for filing annual statements and quarterly statements in respect of withholding tax deductions.

Clause 17 : This clause amends section 87 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to ensure that withholding agents issue withholding certificates free of charge.

Clause 18 : This clause amends section 90 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for the method of calculation of the instalments of tax payable by an instalment payer with effect from April 1, 2026.

Clause 19 : This clause amends section 91 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to limit the requirement of filing an estimate of tax, to taxable periods prior to April 1, 2026.

Clause 20 : This clause amends section 92 of the principal enactment and it is consequential to the amendment referred to in clause 19.

Clause 21 : This clause amends section 94 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to –

(a) exempt specified individuals from the requirement to file a tax return; and

(b) require any person carrying on a Business of Strategic Importance under the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, to file a tax return.

Clause 22 : This clause amends section 95 of the principal enactment for the purpose of clarity.

Clause 23 : This clause inserts new section 96A in the principal enactment and the legal effect of that section is to provide for the granting of tax credit on arrears of salary received by an employee.

Clause 24 : This clause amends section 100 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to broaden the scope of the section in relation to disclosure of information.

Clause 25 : This clause amends section 102 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to specify a time period for companies to register with the Commissioner-General.

Clause 26 : This clause amends section 103 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to specify the circumstances in which a person is required to submit the TIN Certificate.

Clause 27 : This clause amends section 113 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to enable senior citizens to file tax returns either in writing or electronically.

Clause 28 : This clause amends section 122 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to prevent the production, in any judicial or Tax Appeals Commission proceedings, of any document or information that was not submitted to the Commissioner-General within the specified time limits.

Clause 29 : This clause amends section 135 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for the instances in which any amended assessment or additional assessment shall not be made on a return.

Clause 30 : This clause amends section 150 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for the payment of tax refunds in relation to senior citizens and persons receiving payments under contracts.

Clause 31 : This clause amends section 163 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to make provisions for the procedure of instituting action against persons for failure to pay tax.

Clause 32 : This clause inserts new section 175A in the principal enactment and the legal effect of that section is to make provisions in respect of service of summons and notices.

Clause 33 : This clause inserts new section 178A in the principal enactment and the legal effect of that section is to make withholding agents and individuals liable to a penalty for failure to comply with the procedure specified by the Commissioner-General and for providing false information.

Clause 34 : This clause inserts new Chapter XVIIA in the principal enactment and the legal effect of that Chapter is to make provisions for instituting action in respect of failure to comply with the requirement under the principal enactment.

Clause 35 : This clause amends section 193 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to provide for certain conditions for compounding offences.Clause 36 : This clause amends section 195 of the principal enactment for the purpose of clarity.

Clause 37 : This clause amends section 202 of the principal enactment and the legal effect of the section as amended is to improve the clarity of the application of the section.

Clause 38 : This clause amends section 203 of the principal enactment for the purpose of clarity.

Clause 39 : This clause amends the First Schedule to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the Schedule as amended is to specify rates of tax for individuals, partnerships, trusts, companies, unit trusts and non-governmental organizations effective from the date of coming into operation of this Act.

Clause 40 : This clause amends the Second Schedule to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the Schedule as amended is to specify the requirements for granting incentives for certain investments.

Clause 41 : This clause amends the Third Schedule to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the Schedule as amended is to provide further clarification in relation to the exemptions granted for certain projects and businesses.

Clause 42 : This clause amends the Fourth Schedule to the principal enactment, to rectify an error in the Sinhala text of that Schedule.

Clause 43 : This clause amends the Fifth Schedule to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the Schedule as amended is to permit the deduction for a qualifying payment for donations made to the charitable institutions subject to certain conditions.

Clause 44 : This clause amends the Sixth Schedule to the principal enactment and the legal effect of the Schedule as amended is to specify a period of time in respect of application of certain temporary concessions.

Clause 45 : This clause makes provisions to write off interest imposed under certain provisions of the principal enactment, the Surcharge Act, No. 14 of 2022 and the Finance Act, No. 35 of 2018.
