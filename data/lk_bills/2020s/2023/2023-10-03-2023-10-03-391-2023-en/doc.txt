1. This Act may be cited as the Online Safety Act,No. of 2023.

2. The provisions of this Act shall apply where –

(a)a person commits an offence under this Act inrespect of a citizen of Sri Lanka, while being presentin Sri Lanka or outside Sri Lanka;

(b)a loss or damage is caused within or outsideSri Lanka by the commission of an offence underthis Act, to the State or to a person resident inSri Lanka; or

(c)an offence under this Act, wherever committed, iscommitted by a person who is a citizen of Sri Lanka.

(a)to protect persons against damage caused bycommunication of false statements or threatening,alarming or distressing statements;
