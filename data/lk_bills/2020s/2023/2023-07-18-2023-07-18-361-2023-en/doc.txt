SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVY (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This Clause amends section 7 of the Principal Enactment so as to ensure thatany Order made by the Minister in terms of sections 2 or 5 of the Special Commodity LevyAct, No. 48 of 2007 will come into force only upon the approval thereof by Parliament.

Short title

AN ACT TO AMEND THE SPECIAL COMMODITY LEVYACT, NO. 48 OF 2007

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Special Commodity Levy(Amendment) Act, No. of 2023.

2.Section 7 of the Special Commodity LevyAct, No. 48 of 2007 (hereinafter referred to as the “principalenactment”) is hereby amended by the deletion of paragraphs(i) to (iv) thereof, and the substitution therefore of thefollowing new paragraphs which shall take effect asparagraphs (i) and (ii) thereof:—

“(i)be published in the Gazette, and shall as soon asconvenient thereafter be placed before Parliamentfor its approval;

(ii)only come into force upon its approval byParliament.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
