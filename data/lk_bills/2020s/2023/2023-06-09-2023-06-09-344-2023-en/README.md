VALUE ADDED TAX (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

This Bill amends section 2A of the Principal Enactment so as to ensure that any Ordermade by the Minister thereunder will come into force only upon the approval thereof byParliament.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE VALUE ADDED TAX ACT, NO. 14 OF 2002

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax(Amendment) Act, No. of 2023.

2.Section 2A of the Value Added Tax Act, No.14 of2002 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) ishereby amended by the deletion of subsections (2), (3), (4)and (5) thereof, and the substitution therefor of the followingnew subsections which shall take effect as subsections (2)and (3) thereof:-

“(2)Every such Order shall be published in the Gazette,and shall as soon as convenient thereafter be placedbefore Parliament for its approval.

(3)Such Order shall only come into force upon itsapproval by Parliament.”.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
