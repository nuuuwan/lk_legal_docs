2. Section 48 of the Finance Act, No. 35 of 2018(hereinafter in this part referred to as the “principalenactment”) is hereby amended by the insertion of thefollowing subsections immediately after subsection (2)thereof :–

“(2A) There shall be charged with effect fromthe date of commencement of this Act, a levy of25 cents per each mobile short message, on bulkadvertisements sent through a fixed telephoneline.

(2B) The levy imposed under subsection (2A),shall be paid by the advertiser who intends toadvertise by way of the mobile short messages inrespect of which the levy is charged.”.
