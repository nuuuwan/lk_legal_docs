AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SAHANA COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENTFOUNDATION (INCORPORATION)

WHEREAS an Foundation called and known as the “SahanaCommunity Development Foundation” has heretofore beenformed in for the purpose of effectually carrying out andtransacating the objects and matters connected with the saidFoundation according to the rules agreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Foundation has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was formed and has applied to beincorporated and it will be for the public advantage to grantthe said application :

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sahana CommunityDevelopment Foundation (Incorporation) Act, No. of2021.

2.From and after the date of commencement of this Act,such and so many persons as now are members of the SahanaCommunity Development Foundation (hereinafter referred toas the “Corporation”) or shall hereafter be admitted membersof the Corporation hereby constituted, shall be a body corporate(hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”) with perpetualsuccession, under the name and style of “Sahana CommunityDevelopment Foundation” and by that name may sue and besued with full power and authority to have and use a commonseal and to alter the same at its pleasure.

3.(1) The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a) to conduct workshops, discussions and seminars onthe matters of educational interest;
