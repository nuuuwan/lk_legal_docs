AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE INSTITUTE OF SPORT FOR PEACE,EDUCATION, EMPOWERMENT AND DEVELOPMENT (SPEED)

WHEREAS an Institute called and known as the“Institute of Sport for Peace, Education, Empowerment andDevelopment (SPEED) ” has heretofore been established inSri Lanka for the purpose of effectually carrying out itsobjects and transacting all matters connected with the saidInstitute according to the rules agreed to by its members:

AND WHEREAS the said Institute has heretoforesuccessfully carried out and transacted the several objectsand matters for which it was established and has applied tobe incorporated and it will be for the public advantage togrant the said application:

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Institute of Sport forPeace, Education, Empowerment and Development(Incorporation) Act, No. of 2021.

2.(1) From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theInstitute of Sport for Peace, Education, Empowerment andDevelopment (hereinafter referred to as the “Institute”) orshall hereafter be admitted as members of the Corporationhereby constituted shall be a body corporate with perpetualsuccession, under the name and style of the “Institute ofSport for Peace, Education, Empowerment andDevelopment” (hereinafter referred to as “the Corporation”),and by that name may sue and be sued with full power andauthority to have, and use a common seal and to alter thesame at its pleasure.

3.(1) The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be:—

(a)to increase performance of Sri Lanka and SouthAsian nations in International sporting events byestablishing a platform for the upholding ofprinciples and values of Olympism and to supportOlympic movement;

(d)to promote gender equality and the empowermentof women through suporting their career andadvancement in sport, in particular their progressivehigh performance in international sporting events;

(e)to advance the use of sport as a vehicle to promotehealth and prevent drug abuse through physicaleducation, foster the inclusion and well-being ofpersons with disabilities and facilitate socialinclusion of marginalized groups;
