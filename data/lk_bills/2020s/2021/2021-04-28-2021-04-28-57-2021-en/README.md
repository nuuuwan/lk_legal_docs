AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SRI LANKA INSURANCE INSTITUTE

WHEREAS an association of insurance professionals calledand known as the “Sri Lanka Insurance Institute” hasheretofore been established for the purpose of effectuallycarrying out and transacting all matters connected with thesaid institute according to the constitution agreed to by itsmembers:

AND WHEREAS the said association together with itsCouncil has heretofore successfully carried out andtransacted the several objects and matters for which it wasestablished, and has applied to be incorporated, and it willbe for the public advantage to grant the said application.

BE it therefore enacted by Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka InsuranceInstitute (Incorporation) Act, No. of 2021.
