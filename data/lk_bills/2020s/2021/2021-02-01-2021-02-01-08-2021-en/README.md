AN ACT TO REPEAL THE MUSLIM MARRIAGE AND DIVORCEACT, NO. 13 OF 1951

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Muslim Marriage andDivorce (Repeal) Act, No. of 2021.

2.The Muslim Marriage and Divorce Act, No. 13 of1951 is hereby repealed.

3.In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
