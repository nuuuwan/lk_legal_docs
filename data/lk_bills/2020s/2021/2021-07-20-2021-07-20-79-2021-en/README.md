AN ACT TO AMEND THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, NO. 16 OF 1978

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

1.This Act may be cited as the Universities(Amendment) Act, No. of 2021.

2.The Universities Act, No.16 of 1978 (hereinafterreferred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amendedby the insertion immediately after section 23, of thefollowing new section which shall have effect as section23A, of that enactment: -

23A. (1) The Minister may, in consultationwith the Commission, by an Order (hereinafterreferred to as a “University for a specificpurpose Order”)-

(a)establish a University for a specificpurpose, which shall be a bodycorporate, with perpetual successionand a common seal for the purpose ofproviding, promoting and developinghigher education in all such branchesof learning as shall be specified insuch Order to those who are notstudents of any of the institutionsreferred to in sections 21, 22, 23, 24,24A and 25;

(b)assign a name and style to suchUniversity; and

(c)specify the location or site which shallbe the seat of such University.
