AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE KELANIYA BUDDHIST WOMEN'SCHARITABLE SOCIETY

WHEREAS a Society called and known as the “KelaniyaBuddhist Women's Charitable Society” has heretofore beenestablished in Kribathgoda of Gampaha District in Sri Lankafor the purpose of effectually carrying out it's objects andtransacting all matters connected with the said Societyaccording to the rules agreed to by its members for fifty sixyears (56):

AND WHEREAS the said Society has heretofore successfullycarried out and transacted the several objects and matters forwhich it was formed and has applied to be incorporated andit will be for the public advantage to grant the saidapplication:

BE it therefore enacted by Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Kelaniya BuddhistWomen's Charitable Society (Incorporation) Act, No. of2021.

2.(1) From and after the date of commencement of thisAct, such and so many persons as now are members of theKelaniya Buddhist Women's Charitable Society (hereinafterreferred to as the “Society”) and shall hereafter be admittedas members of the Corporation hereby constituted, shall bea body corporate, with perpetual succession under the nameand style of the “Kelaniya Buddhist Women's CharitableSociety” (hereinafter referred to as “Corporation”) and bythat name may sue and be sued, with full power and authorityto have, and use a common seal and to alter the same at itspleasure.

3. The general objects for which the Corporation isconstituted are hereby declared to be—

(a)to provide facilities necessary for the enhancing ofmeditation methods to the Buddhists;

(b)to collect the books and magazines of Buddhismpublished worldwide and make arrangements toaccess them easily by the Buddhists;

(c)to coordinate and improve proper relation with thesocial servants, intellectuals, donators andbenevolent people and with some other similarwelfare societies and foundations in Sri Lanka bythis Corporation;

(d)to print, publish and distribute the books, hand billsand magazines which may be required by theCorporation for the promotion and development ofobjects of the Corporation;

(e)to cordinate with other unions, societies andorganizations having similar objects of membersof the Corporation;

(f)to act in order to convince Buddhism for every oneand to take necessary actions to propagateBuddhism all over the world.
