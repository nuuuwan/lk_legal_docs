BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1.This Act may be cited as the Prohibition of ObscenePublications Act, No. of 2021.

PART I

APPLICATION OF THE ACT

2.The provisions of this Act shall apply where–

(a)any act which constitutes an offence under this Acttakes place in Sri Lanka irrespective of whether theperson who commits such offence is in Sri Lanka ornot;

(b)a person commits an offence under this Act inSri Lanka, while in Sri Lanka; or

(c)an offence under this Act, wherever committed, iscommitted by a person who is a citizen of Sri Lanka.
