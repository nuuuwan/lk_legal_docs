AN ACT TO AMEND THE LIONS CLUBS INTERNATIONAL DISTRICT306A- NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION TRUST(INCORPORATION) ACT, NO. 28 OF1986

WHEREAS the Corporate members of the Lions ClubsInternational District 306A - National DevelopmentFoundation Trust (Incorporation) incorporated under theLions Clubs International District 306A - NationalDevelopment Foundation Trust (Incorporation) Act, No. 28of 1986 have passed a resolution to amend the name of saidTrust:

AND WHEREAS the said Trust, for the purpose of giveneffect to the said resolution, has applied to change the nameof such Trust; and it will be expedient to grant suchapplication:

BE it therefore, enacted by the Parliament of the DemocraticSocialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

1.This Act may be cited as the Lions Clubs InternationalDistrict 306A - National Development Foundation Trust(Incorporation) (Amendment) Act, No. of 2021.

2.In every context in which the Lions Clubs InternationalDistrict 306A - National Development Foundation Trust ismentioned whether by that name or by the name “Lions ClubsInternational District 306A - National Development FoundationTrust” or by the abbreviation “Trust” in the Institute of LionsClubs International District 306A - National DevelopmentFoundation Trust (Incorporation) Act or any notice or otherdocument issued under that Act, there shall, unless the contextotherwise requires, be substituted for the expressions “LionsClubs International District 306A - National DevelopmentFoundation Trust”, the expression “Lions Clubs InternationalDistrict 306A 1 and District 306A 2 - National DevelopmentFoundation Trust”.
