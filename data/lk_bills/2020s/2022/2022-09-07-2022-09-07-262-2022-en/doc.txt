PART I

ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SRI LANKA NATIONAL COMMISSION FORUNESCO

2.(1) There shall be established a Commission whichshall be called the Sri Lanka National Commission forUNESCO (hereinafter referred to as the “Commission”).

(2) The Commission shall, by the name assigned to it bysubsection (1), be a body corporate with perpetual successionand a common seal and may sue and be sued in such name.

3.The objects of the Commission shall be:-

(a) to provide the required forum for internationaland intellectual co-operation, through thefacilitation of a globally connected networkingsystem pertaining to field of competencies ofUNESCO ;

(b) to facilitate the exchange of information in areasrelated to education, science, culture, massmedia and communication through local,regional and global programmes organized byUNESCO, thus acting as a clearing house forinformation in the related fields ofcompetencies of UNESCO ;
