AN ACT TO AMEND THE LICENSING OF SHIPPING AGENTS, FREIGHTFORWARDERS, NON-VESSEL OPERATING COMMON CARRIERS ANDCONTAINER OPERATORS ACT, NO. 10 OF 1972

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic SocialistRepublic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

1. This Act may be cited as the Licensing of ShippingAgents, Freight Forwarders, Non-Vessel Operating CommonCarriers and Container Operators (Amendment) Act,No. …… of 2022.

2. Section 3 of the Licensing of Shipping Agents, FreightForwarders, Non- Vessel Operating Common Carriers andContainer Operators Act, No. 10 of 1972 is hereby amendedin subsection (5) thereof as follows:-

(1)in paragraph (e) thereof, by the substitution for thewords “contravention of such standards.” of thewords “contravention of such standards; and”;

(2)by the addition at the end of the paragraph (e) ofthat subsection, of the following new paragraph:-

“(f) a charge, levy and tariff imposed by therelevant service providers of shipping agents,freight forwarders, non-vessel operatingcommon carriers and container operators forthe services rendered by them, and regulatingthe procedure of imposing and collecting suchcharge, levy and tariff .”.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhalaand Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
