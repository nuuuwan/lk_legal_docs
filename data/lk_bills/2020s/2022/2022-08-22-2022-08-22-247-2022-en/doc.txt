SRI LANKA BAPTIST SANGAMAYA(INCORPORATION) (AMENDMENT)

To be presented in Parliament by Hon. (Dr.) Harsha de Silva, M.P.for Colombo District

to amend the Sri Lanka Baptist Sangamaya (Incorporation)Act, No. 54 of 1944

(Private Members’ Bill)

1Sri Lanka Baptist Sangamaya (Incorporation)(Amendment)

AN ACT TO AMEND THE SRI LANKA BAPTIST SANGAMAYA(INCORPORATION) ACT, NO. 54 OF 1944

WHEREAS the members of the Sri Lanka Baptist Sangamayaformally known as Ceylon Baptist Council incorporatedunder Ceylon Baptist Council Ordinance No. 54 of 1944 asamended by the Sri Lanka Baptist Sangamaya (Incorporation)(Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 1991 have passed a resolutionto amend the name of the Sangamaya:

AND WHEREAS the said Sangamaya, for the purpose ofgiving effect to such resolution has applied to change thename of such Sangamaya and it will be expedient to grantsuch application:

NOW THEREFORE, be it enacted by the Parliament of theDemocratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

1.This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka BaptistSangamaya (Incorporation) (Amendment) Act, No. of 2022.

2.In every context in which the Sri Lanka BaptistSangamaya is mentioned (whether by that name or by theabbreviation of “the Sangamaya”) in the Sri Lanka BaptistSangamaya Act, No. 14 of 1991 (Incorporation) (Amendment)or in any notice or other document issued under that Act,there shall, unless the context otherwise requires, besubstituted for the expressions “the Sri Lanka BaptistSangamaya” or “the Sangamaya”, the expressions “Sri LankaBaptist Church or “the Church”, as the case may be.

3.Every contract, agreement or other instrument ordocument whatsoever made, issued or executed prior to thedate of commencement of that Act, by or in favour “the SriLanka Baptist Sangamaya.” shall be deemed on and after thedate of commencement of that Act to be and to have beenmade, issued or executed by or in favour of “Sri Lanka BaptistChurch”.
