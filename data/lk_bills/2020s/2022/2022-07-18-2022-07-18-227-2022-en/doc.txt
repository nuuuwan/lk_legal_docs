STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2 : This clause replaces section 32 of the Registration of Documents Ordinance(Chapter 117) (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect ofthe section as amended is to enhance the scope of provisions relating to presentation of acaveat for registration and to define certain terms.

Clause 3 : This clause amends section 33 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to enhance the scope of this section relating to withdrawal andcancellation of a caveat.

Clause 4 : This clause amends section 50 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is to make provisions for clarity.

Clause 5 : This clause amends the Second Schedule to the principal enactment and isconsequential to the amendments made by the above clauses.
