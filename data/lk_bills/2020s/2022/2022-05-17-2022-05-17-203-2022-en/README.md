SRI LANKA ELECTRICITY (AMENDMENT)

to amend the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009

STATEMENT OF LEGAL EFFECT

Clause 2: This clause amends section 8 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of2009 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) and the legal effect of thesection as amended is to allow any person to participate in a bidding process for thegeneration of electricity.

Clause 3: This clause amends section 9 of the principal enactment and the legal effectof the section as amended is -

(a) to allow any person to apply for the issue of a generation licence to generateelectricity; and

(b) to take away the restriction imposed on a person to be eligible to apply for theissue of a generation licence to generate electricity over and above thegeneration capacity of 25 M.W. and also to allow any person to apply for thesame without any demarcation on the generation capacity.

Clause 4: This clause amends section 43 of the principal enactment and the legaleffect of the section as amended is -

(a) the repeal of subsection (3) thereof as an consequential amendment made inrelation to the amendment made to subsection (1) of section 9; and

(b) to expand the application of the provisions of the proviso to any existinggeneration plant that is to be developed and also to rectify an omission in thatsubsection.
