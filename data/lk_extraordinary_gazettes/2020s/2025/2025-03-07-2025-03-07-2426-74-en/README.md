Appointment of a Fellow Member

01(a)

In the event of appointment as a Fellow Member to the Council, the individual must have maintained continuous and active membership in the category of Member of Sri Lanka Council of Archaeologists (MSLCA) for a period exceeding five years;

and The individual must provide evidence of having functioned as a professional in the field of archaeology for a minimum period of ten years after obtaining their first degree.
