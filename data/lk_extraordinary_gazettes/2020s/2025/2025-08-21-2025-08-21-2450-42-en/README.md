SPORTS ACT, No. 25 OF 1973

The order made by the Minister of Sports under the Sections 32 and 33 of theSports Act, No. 25 of 1973

The Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2386/08 dated 29.05.2024 is hereby amended.

I, Sunil Kumara Gamage, Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports, temporarily suspend the registration of National Sports Associations / Federations for the sports stated below in terms of the powers vested with me under the Sections 32 and 33 of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and amendments. And, the Director General of the Department of Sports is hereby appointed as the competency authority with effect from 19th of August, 2025 to carry out administrative and other necessary matters of the said national sports federations in an appropriate manner as a temporary procedure, and to call and conduct an election.

1. Sri Lanka Cycling Federation2. Sri Lanka Automobile Sports3. Netball Federation of Sri Lanka

Sunil Kumara Gamage (M.P.) Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports.

Colombo,19th August, 2025.
