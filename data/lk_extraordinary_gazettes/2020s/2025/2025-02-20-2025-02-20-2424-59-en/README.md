Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of powers vested in me by section 27(1)(b) read with section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (Guarantee) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number GL 24, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order, amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2415/20 of December 16, 2024, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Science Honours in Psychology” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

Schedule I

Conditions

Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (Guarantee) Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of :-

1. Bachelor of Science Honours in Quantity Surveying with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of;

1.1. Simple pass (S) for at least one (01) subject in category (a) and two (02) subjects in category (b) in one and the same sitting at the G.C.E (Advanced Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examination of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification
