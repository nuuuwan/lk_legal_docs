Local Authorities Elections - 2025

Notice under Section 27A of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)

It is hereby notified that the political parties currently recognized as political parties for the purpose of elections under Section 7 of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, are also considered recognized political parties for the purpose of Elections to Local Authorities as per Section 27A of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 24 of 1977, the names of political parties recognized as political parties for the purpose of elections to Local Authorities are set out in the following Schedule.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

Ameer Faaiz, A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

Election Commission,Sarana Mawatha,Rajagiriya,On this 03rd day of March, 2025.
