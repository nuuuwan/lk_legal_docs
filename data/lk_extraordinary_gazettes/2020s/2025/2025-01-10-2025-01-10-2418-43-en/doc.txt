EXCISE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 13 OF 1989

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development do by this Order declare that :-

1. (a) The Excise Duty on every article specified in Column III of the Schedule I hereto shall be payable at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column IV of the Schedule I;

(b) The concessionary Excise Duty on excisable articles specified in Column II of the Schedule II hereto shall be payable at the rate specified in Column III of the Schedule II wherever applicable; and

(c) Wherever two rates are specified in Column IV of the Schedule I and Column III of the Schedule II hereto, the applicable rate for the purpose of calculating the Excise Duty payable shall be the rate which yields the higher amount payable as Excise Duty.

2. The Orders made under Section 3 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No.13 of 1989 and published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2364/36 of 31.12.2023 is hereby rescinded.

3. This Order shall come into effect from January 11, 2025.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development,Colombo 01,January 10, 2025.
