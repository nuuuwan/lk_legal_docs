Government Notifications

Food Act, No. 26 of 1980

Regulations made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media, in consultation with the Food Advisory Committee under Section 32 of the Food Act, No. 26 of 1980.

Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa (M.P.) Minister of Health and Mass Media.

Colombo,28th June, 2025.

Regulations

The Food (Trans-Fat) Regulation 2022, Published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2319/41 of February 14, 2023 as amended by regulation published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2364/25 dated of December 27, 2023 and Gazette Extraordinary No. 2416/54 dated of December 28, 2024 are hereby amended by the repeal of regulation 1 thereof, and the substitution thereof the following regulation.

“1. These regulations may be cited as Food (Trans-Fat) Regulations, 2022 and shall come into operation with effects

from January 01, 2026.”
