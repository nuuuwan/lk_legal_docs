Immigrants and Emigrants act (Chapter 351)

The Order made under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Immigrants and Emigrants Act (Chapter 351) I, Ananda Wijepala, Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs, do hereby rescind the order published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2430/40 on 05th day of April 2025.

Ananda Wijepala, Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs.

Colombo, 07th April, 2025.
