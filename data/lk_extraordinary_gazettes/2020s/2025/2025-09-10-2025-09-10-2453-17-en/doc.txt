PART III – LANDSPART III – LANDS

Land Acquisition Notices

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation do hereby declare, under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the servitude of land described below in Paragraph 1 is needed for a public purpose and is acquired under the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act. The description of the land of which servitude is acquired is given in the Paragraph 2.

1. Description of the Servitude.– Acquisition of servitudes of alternative allotments of lands for the access road and to lay pipe lines of Pichchamal Watta Service Reservoir of Kundasale - Haragama Water Supply Project.

2. Description of the Land.– An allotment of land described by the name and extent given below in the Advance Tracing No. Maha/MHN/2020/277 dated 28.10.2020, prepared by the Surveyor-General situated in the Village called Kirimetiya in the Grama Niladhari Division of

Gurudeniya - West (278) in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kandy Four Gravets and Gangawata Korale in the District of Kandy, Central Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot ‘A’ – Wettige Watta – Extent: 0.0767 Hectare North: Lot Nos. 218, 214 and 215 depicted in the C.M. 320301; East: Lot Nos. 215, 217, 216, 222, 221, 220, 227, 226, 225, 224, 223 and 14 depicted in the C.M. 320301; South: Road (Pradeshiya Sabha) and Lot Nos. 97 and 95 depicted in the C.M. 320301; West: Lot Nos. 95, 94, 93, 92, 218 and 214 depicted in the C.M. 320301.

K. D. Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation.
