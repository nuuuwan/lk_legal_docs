PART III - LANDS Title Registration NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 11/0733 ANURADHAPURA DISTRICT IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 110207 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of 2nd Step, Anuradhapura Town ( In the Urban council ) (Block 20) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.251 – 02nd Step Part I in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Nuwara Gampalatha East in the District of Anuradhapura in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 17th of July 2025 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
