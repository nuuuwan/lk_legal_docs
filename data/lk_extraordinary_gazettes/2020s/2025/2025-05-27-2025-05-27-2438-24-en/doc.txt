THE PROCEEDS OF CRIME ACT, No. 5 OF 2025

Order under subsection (2) of section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by subsection (2) of section 1 of the Proceeds of Crime Act. No. 5 of 2025, I, Harshana Nanayakkara, Minister of Justice and National Integration do by this Order, appoint June 1, 2025, as the date on which the provisions of the aforesaid Act shall come into operation.

Harshana Nanayakkara, Minister of Justice and National Integration.

Colombo,May 22, 2025.
