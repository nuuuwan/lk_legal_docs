public financial management act, no. 44 of 2024

order under Sub-section (3) Of Section 4

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub-section (3) of Section 4 of the Public Financial Management Act, No. 44 of 2024, read with paragraph (3) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, do hereby, by this Order, delegate to the Secretary to the Treasury the powers specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, as provided for in the sections listed in the corresponding entries in Column II of that Schedule.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President. Colombo,January 23rd, 2025.
