Establishment of Patient Safety and Accreditation Bureau for the Ministry of Health and National Healthcare Accreditation Council for Sri Lanka

The Directorate of Healthcare Quality and Safety (DHQS) of the Ministry of Health is the National Focal Point for implementing the healthcare quality assurance program of the health system in the country since 2012, under the theme of “centrally driven, locally led, clinically oriented, patient- centered and continuous quality improvement”.

Following the Cabinet decision dated 05th August 2024, the Ministry of Health and Mass Media is taking necessary steps to implement the following two proposals:

1. To establish the “Patient Safety and Accreditation Bureau” by expanding the role of the Directorate of Healthcare Quality and Safety (DHQS). The Organization structure is in Annexure -1.
