(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE AKKARAIPATTU MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Akkaraipattu Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of death;

AND WHEREAS the secretary of the All Ceylon Makkal Congress, the recognized political party to which Nagoorthamby Muhammed Sirajudeen the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Akkaraipattu Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under, Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Adamlebbe Saboor to fill such vacancy;

Therefore, I, Aththanayaka Mudiyanselage Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning officer of the Akkaraipattu Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Adamlebbe Saboor was selected as the member of the Akkaraipattu Municipal Council. Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning officer Akkaraipattu Municipal Council. District Election Office,Ampara,25th August, 2025.
