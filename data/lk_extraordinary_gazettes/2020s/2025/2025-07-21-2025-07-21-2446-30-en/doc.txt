STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is

Whether Mr. K. Shantha kumara Amar Perera who worked at the Bandarawela Depot of the Sri Lanka Transport Board and retired had been caused injustice by

- being appointed as the cover- up Assistant Manager (Transport) from 06.04.2015,- being appointed as the Depot Manager of same Depot from 05.02.2019,- and by being removed from the then held post of Depot Manager from 19.10.2020 and being vested the duties of Fuel controller without even being given the post of Assistant Manager (Transport) (Cover- up), the post which he had held from 2015 and if so, to what relief he is entitled.

H. M. D. N. K. WATALIYADDA, Commissioner General of Labour.
