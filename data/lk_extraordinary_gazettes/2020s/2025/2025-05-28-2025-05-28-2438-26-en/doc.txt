Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Benedict XVI Catholic International Institute of Higher Education, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 115017 and located at No. 495, Minuwangoda Road, Bolawalana, Negambo, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order, amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2359/14 of November 21, 2023, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Education Honours in Teaching English as a Second Language” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

Schedule I
