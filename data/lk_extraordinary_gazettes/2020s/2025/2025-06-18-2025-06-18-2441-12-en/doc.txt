LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Notice given under Section 66 C (1) of the Local Government Elections Ordinanceas amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

HAVING act in exercise of the powers vested in me under Section 66C(1) of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012 (Chapter 262), I hereby declare that the candidates elected to the posts of Mayor/Deputy Mayor, Chairman/Vice Chairman of the Local Governments in the Sabaragamuwa Province shall be as specifically indicated in the following schedule.

L. M. P. W. Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province. Department of Local Government,Office Complex of Provincial Council, New Town,Ratnapura. On 18th June 2025.
