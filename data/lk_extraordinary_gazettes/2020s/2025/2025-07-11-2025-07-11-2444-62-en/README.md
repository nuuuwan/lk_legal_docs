ARANAYAKA PRADESHIYA SABHA

THE Hon. Governor of Sabaragamuwa Province, in accordance with the provisions of the series of regulations regarding the preparation of plans and publication of notices indicating the names of the roads and narrow roads maintained by the Pradeshiya Sabhas in the Sabaragamuwa Province Published in the special Gazette No. 2316/20 and the order made under Section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987 dated 17.01.2025 and the annexure included in the notification published in the Gazette No. 2419/18 has decided that all the roads and narrow roads in the Sabaragamuwa Province should be owned by the Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha as no case has been filed in the competent court on the date hereby announced for the public's information that the decision has been made.

It is further notifiedthat a list of the said roads and narrow roads in included in the Schedule hereto and that each road and narrow road included therein shall be deemed to be the roads and narrow roads vested in the Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha under Sub section (5) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act.

P.G.U. Jayawickrama , Secretary and Power and Activity Implementing Offic Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha. Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha,19th of June, 2025.
