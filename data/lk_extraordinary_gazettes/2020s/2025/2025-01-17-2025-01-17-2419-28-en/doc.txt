Pradeshiya Sabha - aranayaka

Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha Gazette Notification. Read with Section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987, published by the Hon. Governer of the Sabaragamuwa Province in the special Gazette No. 2316/29 and dated 24th January 2023 to publish notices showing the preparation of plans and list of names of the roads and narrow paths maintained by the Pradeshiya Sabha of Sabaragamuwa Province. In accordance with the work assigned to the Pradeshiya Sabha of Sabaragamuwa Province in the series of orders herein it is hereby announced for the public's knowledge that it has been decided under Secretary's decision No.40 dated 30.12.2024 that the roads and narrow roads included in the following schedule should be declared as roads and narrow roads owned by the Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha.

If it is the opinion of the party claiming the said land that any road or narrow path included in the said Schedule passes through any or part of land, to file a suit in the competent court before the expiry of three months from the date of this notification to prove his/her right to the said land.

And it is the further announced that every road and narrow path included in the said Schedule for which no case has been assigned before the expiry of the said period of three months shall be deemed to be a road or narrow path owned by the Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha.

P. G. U. Jayawikrama, Secretary and implementation officer, Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha.

At Aranayaka Pradeshiya Sabha,Aranayaka.31st December, 2024.
