EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT

(CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Ministry of Lands’ Reference No.: 4-3/7/2020/hw/205.

My Reference No.: DIVPG/LND/LA AQURE/6/13.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following lands/servitudes which are required for a public purpose.

Description of the land to be acquired:

The allotments of land depicted in the Sheet No. 01 of Block No. 01 of Cadastral Map No. 421705 dated 09.06.2025 prepared by the Surveyor General, having the names and the extents as mentioned below, situated in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 881 of Ahugoda West, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Polgahawela, in Kurunegala District, in North Western Province, described as follows:

01. Lot No. 1 - Name of the land: -; Claimant: L. H. M. Chaminda Bandara and others, Udawatta, Ahugoda, Pothuhera; Extent: 0.0228 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 2 - Name of the land: Udawattehena; Claimant: A. M. Gnanawathi, Ahugoda, Pothuhera; Extent: 0.0073 Hectare. 03. Lot No. 3 - Name of the land: Udawattehena; Claimant: A. M. Thilakarathne Banda, Ahugoda, Pothuhera; Extent: 0.0065 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands / servitudes on which servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at the Divisional Secretariat Polgahawela, 31st day of July 2025 at 10.00 a.m.

Moreover, it is hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 17.07.2025, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said lands/servitudes, the amount of compensation and details of computation of such amount. R. M. S. N. Rajapakshe, Divisional Secretary, Polgahawela. At Divisional Secretariat, Polgahawela, 07th July, 2025.
