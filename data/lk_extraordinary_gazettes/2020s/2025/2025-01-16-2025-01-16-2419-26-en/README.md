Local Authorities Election - 2023

Enforcing the Gazette Notifications on Media Guidelines for a further period of three Months

It is hereby, notified that the Gazette Notification No. 2313/33, dated 04.01.2023 on Media Guidelines and the Gazette Notification No. 2318/78, dated 11.02.2023 on Media Guidelines issued as a supplementary to the said Gazette Notification both issued in respect of the Local Authorities Election - 2023, which were previcusly extended for a further period of three (03) months by the Gazette Notification No. 2405/45, dated 11.10.2024, will be in force for a further period of three (03) months from 12.01.2025.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

Ameer Faaiz, A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

At Election Commission,Sarana Mawatha,Rajagiriya,On this 12th day of January, 2025.
