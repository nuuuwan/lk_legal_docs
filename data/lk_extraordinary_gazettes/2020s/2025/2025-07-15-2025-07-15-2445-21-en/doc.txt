Local Government Notifications

LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Notice given under Section 66C(4) of Local Government Elections Ordinance as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

I, Nishantha Saman Kumara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province, hereby give notice under subsection 66C(4) of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, that the first meeting of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, which was converned in accordance with the powers vested in the Commissioner of Local Government under section 66c, and which was not able to perform the function of electing the Chairman and Vice Chairman of the Local Government Institution mentioned in the Schedule below due to lack of a quorum, shall be reconvened on the date and time specified in the said Schedule at the place specified therein.

Nishantha Saman Kumara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province.

Department of Local Government,Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council, New Town, Ratnapura,On 15th July 2025.
