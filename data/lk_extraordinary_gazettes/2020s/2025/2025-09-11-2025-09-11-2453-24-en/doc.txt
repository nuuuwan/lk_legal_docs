THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

ORDINANCE made by the University Grants Commission under Section 18 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978 read with Section 23(2) of the said Act.

Ordinance

1. This Ordinance may be cited as the Open University of Sri Lanka (Amendment) Ordinance, No. 02 of 2025 and shall come into operation on the date of its publication in the Gazette.

2. The Open University of Sri Lanka Ordinance, No. 01 of 1990, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 591/18 of January 05, 1990 as last amended by Ordinance published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2157/9 of January 07, 2020 is hereby further amended in paragraph 16 by the repeal of sub-paragraph (3) (i) of that paragraph and the substitution therefor, of the following :-
