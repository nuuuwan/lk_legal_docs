Government Notifications

L.D.-B. 19/78 (ViI)

the judicature act, No. 2 of 1978

Regulations made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration under Section 61 read with Sub-section (1A) of Section 45 of the Judicature Act, No. 2 of 1978.

Harshana Nanayakkara, Minister of Justice and National Integration.

Colombo,12th June, 2025.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Justices of the Peace Regulations No. 1 of 2025.

2. Every mediator appointed for the purposes of the Mediation Board Act, No. 72 of 1988 by the Commission appointed under Section 2 of that Act shall be an ex-officio Justice of the Peace for the respective Mediation Board area to which such mediator is appointed during his tenure.
