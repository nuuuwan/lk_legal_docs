UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of "The American College of Higher Education (Private) Limited", a Company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 7412 and located at No. 502, Peradeniya Road, Kandy, do by this Order subject to such conditions as specified in Schedule I hereto, recognize "The American College of Higher Education (Private) Limited" as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing Higher Education therein, leading to the award of the Degree specified in Column I of the Schedule II hereto, of which the abbreviation is set out in the corresponding entry in Column II of the Schedule II hereto:-

SCHEDULE I

Conditions

1. The American College of Higher Education (Private) Limited (hereinafter referred to as the "institute") shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Honours in Psychology with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of;

(a) Three (03) simple passes (S) in any stream in one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advance Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification and a simple pass (S) for
