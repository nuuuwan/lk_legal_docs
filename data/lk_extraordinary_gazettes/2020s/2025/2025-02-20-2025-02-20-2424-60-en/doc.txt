Ordinance

1. This Ordinance may be cited as the Institute of Allergology and Immunology Ordinance, No.02 of 2025 and shall come into operation with effect from18.02.2025.

2. This Ordinance shall apply to the Institute of Allergology and Immunology of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura (hereinafter referred to as the “Institute”) established by Order made by the Minister under section 24A (1) of the Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2424/58 of 2025.

3. The Institute shall be affiliated to the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka (hereinafter referred to as the “University”).

PART I

Powers, Duties and Functions of the Institute

4. Subject to the provisions of this Ordinance, provisions of the Act and any appropriate instrument made thereunder the Institute shall have the following powers, duties and functions:-

(a) to initiate, facilitate and conduct basic and applied scientific research in the specialities of Allergology, Immunology, Molecular Medicine and Biomedical Engineering to better understand, treat and ultimately prevent allergic, immunologic and infectious diseases in Sri Lanka and strengthen such research;

(b) to admit students to the Institute;

(c) upon the recommendations of the Institute and with the concurrence of the University and with the approval of the Commission, to provide instructions to the students of the Institute and to conduct training and research in the major specialities of Allergology, Immunology, Molecular Medicine and Bio Medical Engineering;

(d) with the approval of the Council, to conduct examinations for the purposes of ascertaining the persons who have acquired proficiency in the major specialities of Allergology, Immunology, Molecular Medicine and Bio Medical Engineering;

(e) with the approval of the Council and with the concurrence of the Commission, to determine Bachelor’s Degrees, Postgraduate Degrees, Postgraduate Diplomas, Higher Diplomas, Postgraduate Certificates, and other academic distinctions to be awarded to the eligible students who have pursued above mentioned approved courses of study and who have passed the examinations of the Institute in the major specialities of Allergology, Immunology, Molecular Medicine and Biomedical Engineering;

(f) to recommend to the Council, students who having passed the examinations and having satisfied such other conditions as the University may, in consultation with the Institute, prescribed by By-laws, eligible for the award of-

(i) Bachelor’s degrees;

(ii) Postgraduate diplomas;

(iii) Higher diplomas;

(iv) Postgraduate certificates;

(v) Master of Philosophy (MPhil);

(vi) Masters with course work and a research component;

(vii) Doctor of Philosophy (PhD);

(viii) Doctor of Medicine (MD) in Allergology and Clinical Immunology with Board Certification; and

(ix) any other appropriate academic distinctions of the University;

(g) to cooperate with Universities or Institutions in Sri Lanka or abroad having similar powers, duties and functions of the Institute by way of exchange of teachers, scholars, students or otherwise;

(h) to recognize examinations passed, qualifications gained and the period of learning or study pursued by persons seeking admission to or by students of the Higher Educational Institutions, Higher Educational Institutes or any other degree awarding institutions as being equivalent to such examinations and periods of learning or study in the Institute or such part thereof as may be recognized by By-laws and to withdraw such recognition at any time;

PART II

the director and other officers of the institute

5. The Director and the holder of any other post specified in this Ordinance as an Officer shall be the officers of the Institute for the purposes of this Ordinance.

6. (1) The first Director of the Institute shall be appointed by the Commission.

(2) Every subsequent Director shall be appointed by the Council with the concurrence of the Commission from among the panel of three persons nominated by the Board of Management of the Institute referred to in paragraph 10:

Provided however, no person shall be appointed as the Director for more than two consecutive terms.

(3) Where a member of the staff of a Higher Educational Institution or a Higher Educational Institute is appointed as the Director, such Institution or Institute, as the case may be, shall release such member of the staff so appointed enabling him to accept the post of Director of the Institute.

(4) The Director shall, unless he vacates office earlier or is removed from office under subparagraph (5), hold office for a period of three years from the date of his appointment or until he completes his age of retirement, whichever event occurs earlier.

(5) Director may be removed from office by the Commission on the recommendation of the Council on a vote of censure passed by not less than two-third of the total membership of the Board of Management at a special meeting convened for such purpose. The members present at such special meeting shall elect from among themselves a person, other than the Director, to preside at such special meeting.

(6) The Director shall be subject to the disciplinary control of the Council.

(7) A Director who resigns or is removed from office or completes his tenure may, if he was a member of the staff of a Higher Educational Institution or of a Higher Educational Institute immediately prior to his appointment as Director, revert to his substantive post in such Higher Educational Institution or Higher Educational Institute, as the case may be, unless he has not completed his age of retirement.

(8) Where the Director by reason of leave, illness, absence from Sri Lanka or any other cause is temporarily unable to perform his duties, the Commission shall, within seven days of the occurrence of such inability, make such arrangements as it may think fit to appoint any other eligible person to perform the duties of the Director and until such arrangements are made, the Registrar of the University shall perform the duties of the Director.

(9) Where a vacancy occurs in the office of Director, the Commission shall, within seven days of the occurrence of such vacancy, make such arrangements as it may think fit for the performance of the duties of the office of the Director until a permanent appointment is made under paragraph 6(2) and until such arrangements are made, the Registrar of the University shall perform the duties of the office of the Director.

Provided however, where if any elected or appointed member vacates his office prior to the expiry of his term, his successor shall, unless he vacates his office earlier, hold office for the unexpired portion of the term of office of his predecessor:

Provided further, that each elected or appointed member of any Board of Study or any Speciality Board of Study shall hold office so long as he retains the status or membership by virtue of which he was eligible for election or appointment as a member of such Board of Study or such Speciality Board of Study, as the case may be, and shall cease to be such member on resignation, retirement or transfer from the Training School, University, Department of Study or Professional Body, as the case may be, from which he was so elected or appointed to be such member.

(2) A member of any Board of Study or any Speciality Board of Study may resign from his office by a letter addressed to that effect to the Director.

(3) Any elected, appointed or nominated member of any Board of Study or any Speciality Board of Study, who without leave of absence fails to attend three consecutive meetings of such Board of Study or such Speciality Board of Study, as the case may be, shall be deemed to have vacated his office as a member of such Board of Study or such Speciality Board of Study and the Director shall take necessary steps to appoint a suitable person to fill the resulting vacancy.

(4) The Board shall have the power to dissolve any Board of Study or any Speciality Board of Study or remove a member of any Board of Study or any Speciality Board of Study on a vote of censure passed by not less than two-thirds of the total membership of the Board.

(b) Immunology; and

(c) Molecular Medicine and Biomedical Engineering.

PART VIII

Finance

26. (1) The Institute shall have its own Fund.

(2) There shall be paid into the Fund -

(a) fees to be paid to the Institute in accordance with the provisions of any appropriate instrument;

(b) income from endowments, grants and gifts;

(c) all such sums of money as may be voted from time to time by Parliament and disbursed by the Commission as grants for the purpose of the Institute; and

(d) all other moneys accruing to the Institute from any source whatsoever.

(3) A summary of all sums of money paid into the Fund within the course of a particular month classified under the appropriate heads of revenue shall, before the end of the next ensuing month, be submitted by the Director to the Commission for its information.

27. (1) It shall be the duty of the Director-

(a) to keep the accounts of the Institute in such form and manner as may be determined by the Council;

(b) to receive all moneys paid into the Fund and to credit such moneys to the appropriate heads of accounts;

(c) to make all authorized payments; and

(d) to prepare for the Board such triennial estimates as required by the Commission and the annual financial appropriations for the ensuing year:

Provided however, the income derived from grants or endowments made for specific purposes, shall be separately accounted for in the accounts of the Institute and no payment shall be made from such income for the general purposes of the Institute or for any purpose or object other than that for which such grants or endowments were respectively made.

PART IX

General Provisions

31. (1) The staff of the Institute shall consist of such number officers, teachers and other employees as it may, with the concurrence of the University and with the approval of the Commission, deem necessary for the efficient exercise, performance and discharge of the powers, duties and functions of the Institute.

(2) The staff of the Institute may either be provided on release by the University from among its own staff or be permanently appointed by the Commission or the University, as the case may be, and the provisions of Part X of the Act shall, mutatis mutandis, apply to and in relation to appointments made to the staff of the Institute.

(3) Where an officer, teacher or other employee of the University has been released for service in the Institute on a full-time basis, the Institute shall pay him the salary and the allowances of his substantive post in the University. In any other case, the Institute may pay the officer or other employee concerned, such allowance as it may determine in consultation with the Council and with the approval of the Commission.

(4) The staff of the Institute shall be subject to the direction and the disciplinary control of the Director.

(5) The Staff of the Institute shall be subject to the disciplinary rules and procedures applicable to the Staff of the University.

32. (1) Where any question arises as to whether any person has been duly elected, appointed or nominated, as the case may be, to any Authority or Body of the Instinite, as the case may be, or as to whether any person is entitled to be a member of any authority’ or other body of the Institute, such question shall be referred to the Commission whose decision thereof shall be final.

(2) No act, resolution or proceeding of any Authority or other Body of the Institute shall be invalidated merely by reason of the existence of any vacancy among its members or of any defect in the appointment of any member thereof.

(3) Upon coming into operation of this Ordinance and subject to the provisions of this Ordinance, the Act and any appropriate instrument made thereunder, the University shall have the power to grant and confer Bachelor’s degrees, Postgraduate diplomas, higher diplomas, Postgraduate certificates Master of Philosophy (MPhil), Masters with course work and a research component, Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), Doctor of Medicine (MD) with Board Certification and other academic distinctions in major specialties of Allergology and Immunology and Molecular Medicine upon the recommendation of the Institute.

PART X

Interpretation

33. In this Ordinance unless the context otherwise requires-

“Act” means the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978;

appropriate instrument” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

“Commission” means the University Grants Commission established under section 2 of the Act;

“Council” means the Council of the University;

“Examiner” means a person who is at present an examiner in a Training School or an institute, or a permanent staff member in a relevant speciality of the Ministry of Health, or an academic in the relevant departments of the relevant faculties of the Universities established under the Act, or a relevant specialist of the Ministry of the Minister assigned the subject of Health, who shall be in active service in the Universities established under the Act or in the Ministry of the Minister assigned the subject of Health, or in any government institution recognized by the Board:

Provided however that, all such examiners shall have five years’ experience in teaching and training with recognized degrees as may be determined by the Board and approved by the Senate on the recommendation of the Board of Study or the Speciality Board of Study concerned;

“Higher Educational Institution” and “Higher Educational Institute” shall have the same meanings assigned to them by the Act;

“professional body” means an association, college or any other body corporate other than a trade union in any speciality in medicine including Allergology and Immunology which is incorporated by an Act of Parliament or which is registered under the Companies Act, No. 07 of 2007 or any other professional body corporate or unincorporate, recognised by the Board;“Senate” means the Senate of the University;

“Teacher” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it by the Act; and

“University” means University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka established under the provisions of the Act.
