DEHIOWITA PRADESHIYA SABHA

I hereby give notice for information of General Public that as per Secretary’s Decision No. 05 and dated 30.12.2024, roads and paths included in this Schedule should be declared as roads and paths belonged to Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha, in terms of task assigned to Pradeshiya Sabhas in Sabaragamuwa Province by the series of orders with regard to publishing notices indicating register and preparation of plans for roads and paths maintained by Sabaragamuwa Province, having been declared in Extraordinary Gazette Notiﬁ cation No 2316/20 dated 24th of January, 2023 by Hon Governor in Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council read with Section 24 of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987.

If opinion of party claiming to a land that is any road or a path included in the said Schedule is inclusive in any partition of land, I hereby notify that actions should be taken to institute an action in the appropriate court before expiration of a period of three months from this Notice for proving own possession for the said land.

Further, I notify that every road and path included in the said Schedule which hasn’t been instituted an action before expiration of the said period of three months should be considered as a road or a path belonged to Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha.

W. D. S. P. WALPOLA, Secretary and Executing Powers Duties and Functions Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha. Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha, Dehiowita. On 20th day of January, 2025.
