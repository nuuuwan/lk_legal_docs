NOTICE UNDER LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Declaration of appointment made by virtue of the statutory responsibility vested in me under the Section No. 66c of and No. 66d of Chapter No. 262 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance amended by No. 22 of 2012 of the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act

I, Adam Lebbe Mohamed Azmi, Commissioner of Local Government of Eastern Province, by virtue of the statutory responsibility vested in me under the Section No. 66c of and No. 66d of Chapter No. 262 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance amended by No. 22 of 2012 of the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, do hereby notify that Mayor/Chairman, Deputy Mayor/Vice Chairman have been appointed on the date depicted in the Column No. IV of the corresponding chart of the Schedule here, as depicted in the Column No. III of the corresponding chart of the Schedule here, for each Local Government Authorities as depicted Column No. II in the corresponding chart of the Schedule here, located in the specified Administrative District as depicted Column No. I of the corresponding chart of the Schedule here situated in the Eastern Province.

A. L. M. Azmi, Commissioner of Local Government, Eastern Province. Office of the Department of Local Government,Eastern Province,Kanniya Road, Varothaya Nagar, Trincomalee,On this 04th day of July, 2025.
