SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OF PROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT, NO. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SIXTH SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26TH SEPTEMBER, 2017

An order to provide for the services during the year from 01st January, 2025 to 31st December, 2025, to authorize the raising of loans within the province of Sabaragamuwa for the purpose of such service, to make financial provisions in respect of certain activities of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council during that year, to enable the payment, by way of advances out of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council fund or any other fund or monies of, or at the disposal of, the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council fund, and to make provision for matters connected with or incidental to the aforesaid matter.

BE it enacted by me, the Governor of the Sabaragamuwa Province of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows.

1. I do hereby grant permission unto the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Province to spend Rupees Sixty one billion seven hundred thirty eight million one hundred and thirty eight thousand only, (Rs. 61,738,138,000/-) and which estimated for the services of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council for the One year duration beginning on 01st January, 2025 and ending on 31st December, 2025, by virtue of powers vested in me under section 27(A) of Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987 as the term of the Sixth Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council ended on 26th September, 2017.

2. (1). Without prejudice to any other law authorizing any expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which estimate will be Rupees Sixty one billion seven hundred.

5. The debit balance, outstanding at the end of financial year 2025 of any activity specified in column (iv) of the second schedule to this order shall not exceed the maximum limit specified in the corresponding entry in column (vii) of that schedule, and the total liabilities of that activity at the end of that financial year shall not exceed the maximum limit specified in the corresponding entry column (viii) of that schedule.

6. Whenever at any time during the financial year 2025, the receipts of that Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council from any activity specified in column (iv) of the second schedule to this order are insufficient to meet the expenditure incurred by the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council on such activity, the governor may, from time to time, by order direct that such sums may deem necessary to meet such expenditure shall be payable by way of advances out of Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council fund or any other fund or monies of or at the disposal of, the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council, so however that the aggregate of the sums so advances does not exceed the maximum limit of entry in column (v) of that schedule. Any sums so advanced in respect of such activity shall be refunded to the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council fund in such manner as the Governor may by order direct.

7. (1). Any money which, by virtue of the provisions of the first schedule to this order, have been allocated to recurrent expenditure under any programme appearing under any head specified in that schedule but have not been expended or may likely to be unexpended, may be transferred to the allocation of recurrent expenditure or capital expenditure under any other programme within that head by order of the Chief Secretary to the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or any other officer authorized by him.
