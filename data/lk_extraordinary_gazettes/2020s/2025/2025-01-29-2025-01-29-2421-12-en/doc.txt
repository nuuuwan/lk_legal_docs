Pilgrimages Ordinance

It is hereby notified that the annual Lenten season of Hiniduma Calvary Shrine (St. Ann's Church) located within the two Grama Niladhari Divisions of Hiniduma South and Malhathawa in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Thawalama in the District of Galle, Southern Province will be held during the period from Wednesday the 5th March 2025 to Sunday the 20th April 2025 as per the regulations made under the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2057/33 dated 09.02.2018 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka in terms of the Section 2 of the Pilgrimages Ordinance (Cap.175).

W. A. Dharmasiri, District Secretary/ Government Agent, Adminisrative District, Galle.

22nd January 2025.
