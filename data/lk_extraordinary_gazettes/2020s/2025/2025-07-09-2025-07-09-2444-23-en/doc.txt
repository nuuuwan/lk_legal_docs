The Customs Ordinance

Order under Section 19A

BY Virtue of the power vested in me under Section 19(A) of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235) as amended by the Customs (Amendment) Law No. 35 of 1974 and the Customs (Amendment) Act, No. 83 of 1988, I, Anura Kumara Disanayaka, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development do by this Order exempt the goods specified in Column V of the Schedule hereto, imported by or consigned to, person or institutions specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule from payment of the Customs Import Duty, leviable thereon as specified in the corresponding entry in Column VIII of that Schedule subject to such conditions.

However, any importer or person by whom or on whose behalf any goods, specified and imported under this Order are subsequently to be sold or disposed of contrary to the conditions specified, such importer or person shall obtain prior approval of the Director General of Customs and pay the Customs Import Duty payable on such goods at the time of sale or disposal to the Director General of Customs in terms of Section 19 (A) (3). Any goods sold or disposed of in contravention of such Section shall be liable to be forfeited.

Anura Kumara Disanayaka, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, Colombo 01,July 07, 2025.
