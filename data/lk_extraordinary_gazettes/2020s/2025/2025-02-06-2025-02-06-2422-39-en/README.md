L.D.-B 6/2017Civil Aviation Authority act, no. 34 of 2002

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation under section 39 of Civil Aviation Authority Act No. 34 of 2002 read with paragraphs (p) and (s) of section 7 and section 9 of that Act. Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Aviation.

Colombo,03rd February, 2025.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Imposition of Charges for provisions of Aeronautical facilities and lands appertaining) Regulations No. 02 of 2025.

2. The Service Provider specified in Column (I) of the Schedule hereto, to whom the aeronautical facilities and lands appertaining thereto as specified in Column (II) of that Schedule, which are transferred to and vested in the Civil Aviation Authority in terms of section 9 of the aforesaid Act, are made available in terms of section 8 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010, shall pay to the Director General of the Civil Aviation Authority the charge specified in Column (III) of that Schedule for the usage of such aeronautical facilities and lands appertaining thereto.

3. For the purposes of these regulations-

"Authority' means the Civil Aviation Authority established under section 2 of the Civil Aviation Authority Act of

Sri Lanka, No. 34 of 2002;

"Aeronautical facilities and land appertaining thereto" ' means the aeronautical facilities and land appertaining thereto as specified in the Third Schedule of the Civil Aviation Authority Act of Sri Lanka No. 34 of 2002;

"Director General' means the Director General of Civil Aviation appointed under section 11 of the Civil Aviation

Authority Act of Sri Lanka, No. 34 of 2002;

"Service provider' means the Statutory Service Provider appointed by section 6(3) of the Civil Aviation

Act, No. 14 of 2010.
