NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 16 SUB - SECTIONS (2D) OF THE COAST CONSERVATION AND COASTAL RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT. No. 57 OF 1981

Proposed project on 288 Units of Apartment Project - No. 13, Archbishop Nicholas Marcus Fernando Mawatha, Bolawalana, Negombo

Environmental Impact Assessment Report

IT is hereby notified that the Report of Environmental Impact Assessment study on proposed 288 Units of Apartment Project, No. 13, Archbishop Nicholas Marcus Fernando Mawatha, Bolawalana, Negombo has been submitted by the Home Land Skyline (Pvt.)
