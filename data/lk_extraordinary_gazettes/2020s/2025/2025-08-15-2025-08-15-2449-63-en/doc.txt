(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE POLONNARUWA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of member of Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Munaweera Kankanange Bandula Asoka Munaweera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 05 Kadawalawewe of Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days,

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Wickramasinghe Mudiyanselage Chintha Lakmali Wickramasingha to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Manoji T Gurusingha, returning Officer of the Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Wickramasingha Mudiyanselage Chintha Lakmali Wickramasingha was elected as the member of the ward Number 05 Kadawalawewa of Polonnaruwa Pradeshya Sabha. Manoji T Gurusinghe, Returning Officer, Polonnaruwa Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Polonnaruwa,11th August, 2025.
