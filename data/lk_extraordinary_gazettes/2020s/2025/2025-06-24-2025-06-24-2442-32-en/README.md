THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose: Description of the Land to be Acquired : Allotment of land in extent 1.4431 of Hectare shown as Lot 01 in CM26005 dated 10.01.2025 Prepared by the Surveyor General Situated in within the Pradeshiya Sabha limits of Trincomalee, No. 243, Uppuveli Grama Niladhari Division, Divisional Secretary's Division of Town & Gravets Trincomalee, Trincomalee District and bounded as follows: 01. Lot No. 01 Name of land:-;Extent: 1.4431 Hec. North: Part of Lot 2 in FVP 27;East: Sea Shore; South: Sea Shore and Part of Lot 2 in FVP 27;West: Part of Lot 2 in FVP 27;

All persons interested in the aforesaid land on which servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing before me at Divisional Secretariat Trincomalee on the 30th day of July 2025 at 11.00 o'clock. And you are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 07 th day of July 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensatin and the details of the computation of such amount. All deeds and documents in support of the claims should be produced before me at the inquiry. N.MATHIVANNAN, Divisional Secretary, Town & Gravets Trincomalee. Divisional Secretariat, Trincomalee, 16th June, 2025. EOG 06 - 0248
