PART III – LANDSPART III – LANDS

Land Acquisition Notices

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation do hereby declare, under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the lands described below are required for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

Acquisition of allotments of land (Kolongaswatta) for the construction of Kolongaswatta Service Reservoir of Kundasale Haragama Water Supply Project.

The allotments of land described as following names and extents, depicted in the Advance Tracing No. Maha/KND/2022/167 dated 10.02.2023, prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in the Village of Kengalla in the Minor Division of Udagampaha Korale in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 701 of Ihalawela in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kundasale in the District of Kandy, Central Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot ‘A’ – Kolongahawatta – Extent: 0.0633 Hectare North: Kolongahawatta claimed by B. Indrarathne, Kolongahawatta claimed by M. G. Gunasena and Kolongahawatta claimed by D. A. G. G. Chandrakanthi; East:

Kolongahawatta claimed by D. A. G. G. Chandrakanthi, Lot B and Kolongahawatta claimed by R. Muththulingam; South: Kolongahawatta claimed by R. Muththulingam and Kolongahawatta claimed by R. M. U. U. A. Rajapaksha; West: Kolongahawatta claimed by R. M. U. U. A. Rajapaksha and Kolongahawatta claimed by B. Indrarathne.

02. Lot ‘B’ – Kolongahawatta – Extent: 0.0109 Hectare North: Lot A and Kolongahawatta claimed by D. A. G. G. Chandrakanthi; East: Kolongahawatta claimed by D. A. G. G. Chandrakanthi, and Kolongahawatta claimed by R. Muththulingam; South: Kolongahawatta claimed by R. Muththulingam; West: Kolongahawatta claimed by R. Muththulingam and Lot A.
