(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM SOUTH WEST PRADESHIYA SABHA

Under Section 66A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)

WHEREAS the Office of the member of Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Democratic Tamil National Alliance, the recognized politial party to which Selvarasa Kaseepaselvan the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha of Ward number 05 - Sillalai who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days ;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Shanmugam Krishnalingam to fill such vacancy ;

NOW THEREFORE, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of section 6A(1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Shanmugam Krishnalingam was elected as the member of the ward number 05 - Sillalai of Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Valikamam South West Pradeshiya Sabha.District Elections Office,Jaffna,05th August, 2025.
