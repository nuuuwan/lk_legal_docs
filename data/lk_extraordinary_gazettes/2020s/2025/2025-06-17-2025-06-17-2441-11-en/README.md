The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)Part IV (B) — Local Government

filling of a vacancy in the office of member of the Weligama Urban Council

under section 66A(1) (a) of the local authorities elections ordinance (chapter 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Weligama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

And whereas the secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Mohamed Mohamed Mufallal the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that No. 3 Galbokka of Weligama Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Umarul Jiffry Mohammed Afras to fill such vacancy;

Now therefore Pulasthi Serasingha Pathirana Returning Officer of the Weligama Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Umarul Jiffry Mohammed Afras was elected as the member of the No. 3 Galbokka of Weligama Urban Council. P. S. Pathirana, Returning Officer, Weligama Urban Council. District Election Office,Matara,16th June, 2025.
