Local Government Notifications

LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Notice given under Sub-section 66C(4) of the Local Government Elections Ordinance as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

I, Lekam Mudiyanselage Pragnakeerthi Wijesundara Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province, hereby give notice under subsection 66C(4) of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, that the first meeting of the Local Governments which was convened in accordance with the powers vested in the Commissioner of Local Government Election under subsection 66C of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, and was not able to perform the function of electing the Chairman and Vice-Chairman of the Local Government Institution mentioned in the Schedule below due to lack of a quorum, shall be reconvened on the date and time specified in the said Schedule at the place specified therein.

L. M. P. W. Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province. Department of Local Government,Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council, New Town, Ratnapura,On 26th June 2025.
