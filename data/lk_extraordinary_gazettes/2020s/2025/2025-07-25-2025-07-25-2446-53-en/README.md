DERANIYAGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA THE Hon. Governor of Sabaragamuwa Province has made the following orders in the series “Regulations on the preparation of plans and publication of notices indicating the names of roads and narrow roads maintained by the Pradeshiya Sabha’s in the Sabaragamuwa Province” published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2316/20 and dated 24th January 2023 and the order made under Section 24 (1) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, out of the roads and narrow roads considered to be owned by the Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha in the Schedule included in the notification published in the Gazette No. 2427/32 dated 14th March 2025, the roads and narrow roads for which no case has been filed in a competent court before the date specified in the said notification are confirmed to be considered to be owned by the Council. It is hereby notified for the information of the public that it has been decided under decision No. 01 dated 17.06.2025 that all the roads and narrow roads shall be owned by the Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

A nomenclature of the said roads and narrow roads is included in the Schedule hereto and it is further announced that every road and narrow road included therein should be considered as roads and narrow roads owned by the Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha under sub-section (5) of section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act.

R.D. Nalin Pushpakumara Secretary and Authority / Powers implementation officer Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

At the Office of the Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha,

17th June, 2025.
