ISSUANCE OF LICENSES

The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka, exercising its powers under Section 13(1) of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009 (as amended), has extended and issued new licenses to the Ceylon Electricity Board for the following activities. These new licenses will be valid for a period of 15 years, effective from October 08, 2024.
