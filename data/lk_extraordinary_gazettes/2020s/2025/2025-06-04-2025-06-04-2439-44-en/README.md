Local Government Notifications

SECTION 66 C OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE, CHAPTER (262)

Notice of the First Meeting of the Council

I, Erandi Umanga Mendis, the Commissioner of Local Government, Southern Province, do hereby inform that by virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 66C of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, the first meeting for the purpose of electing the Mayor/Deputy Mayor, Chairman/Vice Chairman for the Local Authorities listed in the Schedule below within the Southern Province, is scheduled to be held at the specified time on the date correspondingly indicated under the schedule below at the place mentioned therein.

Erandi Umanga Mendis, Commissioner of Local Government, Southern Province.

Department of Local Government,Southern Province,6th Floor, District Secretariat Building, Galle,On this 03rd day of June, 2025.
