No. 2447/54 – FRIDAY, AUGUST 01, 2025

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My Ref. No. : KDS/MDB/4/2/1/207. Min. of Lands Ref. No. : 4-3/2/2015/HA/191. I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460), as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land as it is required for a public purpose:- Description of the land to be acquired: All that allotment of land called "Aliyawatta" in total extent of about 0.0177 Ha., (7.0 Perches) depicted as Lot No. 1 in the preliminary Plan No. MAHA 7098 dated 21.10.2021, surveyed, prepared and approved by the Senior Superintendent of Surveys of Kandy District, on behalf of the Surveyor General, situated at the Village of

Alliyadda, in Alliyadda Grama Niladhari Division, No. 745, in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Medadumbara, in Kandy District, in Central Province and bounded as follows: Lot No. 01 - Name of the land: Aliyawatta; Name and address of the claimant: Mr. S. K. Wijerathne, No. 200B, Alliyadda, Werapitiya - Extent: 0.0177 Ha (7 Perches).

Boundaries: North: Aliyawatta claimed by S. K. Wijerathne; East: Aliyawatta claimed by S. K. Wijerathne; South: Canal road and Aliyawatta claimed by N. T. Anoma Vineethamala; West: Aliyawatta claimed by N. T. Anoma Vineethamala and Aliyawatta claimed by S. K. Wijerathne. All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connection to the aforesaid land hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on October 15th 2025, at 10.00 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat, Medadumbara.
