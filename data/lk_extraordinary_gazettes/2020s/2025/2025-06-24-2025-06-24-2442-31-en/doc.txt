EXTRAORDINARY

The Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: MO/DS/LAN/06/01/41. Land Ministry No. : 4-3/4/2015/D/66.

I hereby give notice, under Sub Section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as Amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land / servitude which is required for the 121 infantry headquarters of the Sri Lanka Army, Kumbukkana.

Particulars of the land to be acquired.

The plots of land depicted in sheet No. 1 in Volume No. 3 in the Cadastral Map No. 720106 dated 20.03.2025 prepared by the Senior Superintendent of Surveys of Monaragala, situated in the Village Called Kavudawa (a part), No. 131A Kumbukkana Grama Niladhari Division of Monaragala Divisional Secretary’s Division in Monaragala District, Uva Province.

Schedule 01. Lot No. : 01 Name of land: Parawila Watta; Ownership requested by Land Reform Commission; Extent: 4.7844 Hectares.

02. Lot No. : 04 Name of land: Parawila Watta; Ownership requested by Land Reform Commission; Extent: 0.3220 Hectare.

03. Lot No. : 06 Name of land: Parawila Watta; Ownership requested by Land Reform Commission; Extent: 0.0687 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land / lands on which a servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Monaragala on the 24th day of November, 2025 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 17th day of November 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land /servitude the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

W. D. P. C. Samarasekara, Divisional Secretary, Monaragala. At Divisional Secretariat Monaragala, 18th June, 2025. EOG 06- 0245
