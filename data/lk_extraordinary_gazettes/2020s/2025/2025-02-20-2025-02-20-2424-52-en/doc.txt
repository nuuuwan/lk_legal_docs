THE UNITED NATIONS ACT, No. 45 OF 1968

Order under regulation 5 of the United Nations Regulations No. 1 of 2012

I, Hadigalage Sampath Sajeewa Thuyacontha, Competent Authority appointed for the purpose of the United Nations Regulations No. 1 of 2012, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1758/19 of May 15, 2012, acting in terms of the powers vested in me by regulation 5 of the said Regulations, do by this Order, freeze all funds, other financial assets and economic resources belonging to or owned or held by any natural or legal person, group or entity designated and listed under regulation 4 of the above Regulations and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1854/41 of March 21, 2014, as amended from time to time and last amended by the amendment to the list published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2424/51 of February 20, 2025.

The Order made under regulation 5 of the United Nations Regulations No. 1 of 2012 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1863/25 of May 22, 2014 is hereby revoked without prejudice to anything done thereunder.

Air Vice Marshal Sampath Thuyacontha (Retd), WWV RWP and two Bars, RSP and Bar, USP, MMSc (Strat Stu-China), MSc (Def Stu) in Mgt., MSc (Def & Strat Stu), fndu (China), psc, Secretary, Ministry of Defence. Competent Authority.

Colombo, 20th February, 2025.
