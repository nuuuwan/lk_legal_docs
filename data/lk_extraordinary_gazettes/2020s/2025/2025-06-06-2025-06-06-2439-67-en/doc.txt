Provincial Councils Notifications

Central Province Provincial Council

Minute of the Central Provincial Management Service Officers Service - Amendment No. 04

The Hon. Governor of Central Province has approved to amend the Minute of the Central Provincial Management Service Officers Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1869/4 dated 30.06.2014, as follows:

1. The Section 8.3 of Para. 8 is revised as follows :

8.3 Authority for conducting Examination :

Central Provincial Public Service Commission or The Commissioner General of Examination or any other related institution.

2. Section 03 of the annexure 04 is revised as follows :

03. Authority for conducting the examination

Central Provincial Public Service Commission or Commissioner General of Examination or any other related institution.
