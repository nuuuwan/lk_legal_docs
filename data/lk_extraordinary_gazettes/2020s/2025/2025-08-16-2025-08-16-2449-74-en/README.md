THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My Reference No. of the Divisional Secretary: KDS/KGK/4/9/2017/1/LA. Reference No. of the Ministry of Lands: 4-3/11/2016/UD/521. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquire the following land which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land to be acquired:- The allotments of land depicted as Lot No. 3 in the Preliminary Plan No. Maha 1993 dated 29.05.2020 Prepared by Senior Superintendent of Surveys of Kandy on behalf of the Surveyor General, situated at the Bahirawakanda Division No. 18 - Katukele (within the Kandy Municipal Council Limit) in the Grama Niladhari's Division (225) Katukele in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Kandy Four Gravets and Gangawata Korale in the District of Kandy, Central Province and containing in extent 0.0023 Hectares.

01. Lot No. 03 Name of Land: Assessment No. 274/4; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: C. K. J. S. Karunarathne and Others; Extent: 0.0023 Hectare. I hereby give notice to the public that every person interested in the said land/servitude which is to be acquired is informed to appear personally, or by agent duly authorized in writing, before me on 29.09.2025 at 10.00 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat of Kandy Four Gravets and Gangawata Korale. Further more the nature of their interests in the land/lands, the particulars of their claim for compensation, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount should be notified me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 22.09.2025.
