EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My No.: MUL/LND/02/ACQ/28. Reference No. of Ministry of Lands: 4-3/5/2020/HA/21. I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land which is required for public purpose:- Description of the land to be acquired: Following allotments of land in the extent of 0.0312 Hectares and depicted as Lot No. 01 and 02 of Paper No. 1 to Zone No. 02 of the Cadastral Plan bearing No. 820432 dated 03.04.2025 prepared by the Surveyor General situated in the village of Beragama South, in the Grama Niladhari Division of Beragama South in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Mulatiyana in Matara District, Southern Province. Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Dolamanga, Galgodahena; Description of the Land: Garden, There is a mixed crop cultivation; Claimant: H. H. Amarasiri; Extent: 0.0030 Hectare.

Lot No. 2 Name of the Land: Wathurawalahena and Ketalaketiya, Galgodahena; Description of the Land: Garden, Permanent Building, Well and a Toilet; Claimant: Indrajith Balasooriya; Extent: 0.0282 Hectare. All persons interest in the aforesaid land/servitude* is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Mulatiyana on the 10th October, 2025, at 10.00 a.m. And are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before 04th September, 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. Wංඇൽඒൺ D. Sൺආൺඋൺ඄඄ඈൽඒ, Divisional Secretary, Mulatiyana. On 13th August, 2025, Divisional Secretariat, Mulatiyana. EOG 08 - 0278
