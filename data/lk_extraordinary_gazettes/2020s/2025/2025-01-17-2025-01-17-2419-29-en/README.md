Ordinance

1. This Ordinance may be cited as the Postgraduate Institute of Indigenous Medicine (Amendment) Ordinance, No. 01 of 2025 and shall come into operation with effect from 15th January 2025.

2. The Postgraduate Institute of Indigenous Medicine Ordinance No. 05 of 2017, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2048/46 of December 08, 2017 is hereby amended as follows :

(1) in paragraph 9 thereof -

(a) by the repeal of sub-subparagraph (e) of subparagraph (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:-

“(e) the Commissioner-General for Ayurveda;”;

(5) in paragraph 24 thereof by the repeal of the definitions of the expressions “Speciality of Ayurveda Medicine”, “Speciality of Siddha Medicine” and “Speciality of Unani Medicine” and the substitution therefor of the following definitions respectively: -

“Speciality of Ayurveda Medicine” means Kayachikitsa, ShalyaShalakya, Sthreeroga and Prasutitantra, Kaumarabhritya, Deshiya Chikitsa, Swasthavritta, Agadatantra. Dravyaguna Vignana, Bhaisajya Kalpana and Rasa shastra, Shareera Vignana and Maulika Siddhanta;

“Speciality of Siddha Medicine” means Siddha Maruthuvam, Noi Naadal Chikitsai, Virana Chikitsai, Aruvai Maruthuwam, Siroroga Maruthuvam, Pen Noyiyalum, Mahalir Maruthuvam, Mahapettru Maruthuvam, Kuzhanthai Maruthuvam, Paramparai Maruthuvam, Samooha Nala Maruthuvam, Nanjiyal, Gunapadam, Siddha Marunthiyal and Siddha Moola Thatthuvam; and

“Speciality of Unani Medicine” means Moalejat, Ilmul Jarahat, Amraz-e- Ain Anf Uzn Wa Halq, Amraz-e-Niswan Wa Qabalat Wa Atfal, Amraz-e-Atfal, Deshiya Ilaj, Tahaffuzi Wa Samaji Tibb, Ilmul Sumoon, Ilmul Advia, Ilmul Saidla, Tashreeh-e-Badan, Manafi-ul- Aza, Kulliyat.”
