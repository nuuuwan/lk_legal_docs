The universities Act, No. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27 (1) (b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27 (1) (b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the, CINEC Campus (Private) Limited a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 17745 and located at Millnnium Drive, IT Park, Malabe 10115 being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2403/17 of September 25, 2024, by the addition immediately after entry "Bachelor of Education Honours in Early Childhood Education" appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Columns I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto:-
