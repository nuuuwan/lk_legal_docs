RAILWAY DEPARTMENT

General Manager's Office

Appointing a special steering committee as per the Railway Daily-Paid Workers’ Benevolent Association Act bearing the number 03 of 1965

IN terms of the power, vested upon the Secretary to the Ministry of Transport, Highways Ports and Civil Aviation pursuant to the clause 30 A (I) Ceylon Railway Workers’ Benevolent Association Act, the Extraordinary Gazette bearing number 2373/37 and issued on 29.02.2024 will be cancelled on the approval, given through the letter bearing his number MT/03/05/21/17-1-1 and dated 06.03.2025.

Further, a special Steering Committee consisting with the below cited members will be appointed pursuant to the provisions of the Act.

● Mrs. A.R.C. Kaushalya Bandara - Director (Administration) - Committee Chairperson● Mr. S.W.G.A. Ishanka - Railway Accountant (Mechanical) - Committee Member● Mr. J.A. Wellappili - Administrative Officer (C.M.E.) - Committee Member

J. I. D. Jayasundara, General Manager - Railways.
