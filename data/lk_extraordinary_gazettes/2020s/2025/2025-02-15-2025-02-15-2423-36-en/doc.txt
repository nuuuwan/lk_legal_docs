the air force act (CHAPTER 359)

Regulations made by the Minister of Defence under Section 29 and 155 of the Air Force Act (Chapter 359).

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Defence.Colombo,12th January, 2025.

Regulations

The Sri Lanka Air Force Women's Wing (Regular and Regular Reserve) Regulations, 1987, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 539/15 of January 7, 1989 are hereby amended as follows:-

(1) by the substitution in sub-regulation (4) of regulation 20, for the words “age of forty five years or on completion

of a maximum period of twenty years of service, whichever occurs earlier.”, of the words and figures “age of fifty five (55) years or on completion of a period of twenty (20) years of service.”;

(2) by the substitution in regulation 21, for the words “age of fifty years,” of the words and figures “age of sixty (60)years.”; and

(3) by the substitution in sub-regulation (3) of regulation 35, for the words “maximum period of twenty years whichever occurs earlier.”, of the words and figures “period of twenty two (22) years.”.
