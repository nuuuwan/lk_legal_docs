Central Bank of Sri Lanka Notices

Order made under Section 57 of the Banking (special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023

By virtue of the powers vested in terms of Section 57 of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, by this Order declares that the provisions of Part I and Part II of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act relating to resolution of licensed banks shall apply to all finance companies licensed by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe,Chairman of the Governing Board and theGovernor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.
