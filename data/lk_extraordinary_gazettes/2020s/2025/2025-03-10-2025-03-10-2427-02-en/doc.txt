IN exercise of the powers vested in the Election Commission under Section 26 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and subject to Section 3 of the Local Authorities Elections (Special Provisions) Act, No. 1 of 2025, nomination notice for the election of members to Local Authorities in each administrative district, situated within the Administrative District specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto and specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of the said Schedule, is hereby issued on 10th March 2025.
