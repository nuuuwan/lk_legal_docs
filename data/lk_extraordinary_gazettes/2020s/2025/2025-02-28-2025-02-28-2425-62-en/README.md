Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)

Order under Section 76A

THE Election Commission, in the exercise of the powers vested in it by Section 76A of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance, hereby clarifies that the provision in Section 28 (2A) (b) of the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 30 of 2003, which states “not less than fifty per centum of the total number of candidates nominated in the Additional Nomination Paper”, shall be interpreted to mean “Not less than fifty per centum of the candidates in the second nomination paper shall consist of women candidates.”

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Ameer Faaiz, Chairman, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.
