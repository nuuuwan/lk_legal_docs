L. D.-B. 6/2017Civil aviation act, no. 14 of 2010

Regulations made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation under paragraphs (r) and (s) of subsection (2) of section 117 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010 read with sections 6, 8, and 31 of that Act.

Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation.

Colombo,03rd February, 2025.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Imposition of Charges for Aerodrome Facilities) Regulations No. 01 of 2025.

2. The Service Provider specified in Column (I) of the Schedule hereto who engages in the provision of aeronautical services in Aerodromes specified in Column (II) of that Schedule in terms of section 8 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010 shall pay to the Director General of the Civil Aviation Authority the charge specified in Column (III) of that Schedule for the usage of such Aerodromes and the land appertaining to such Aerodrome.

3. For the purposes of these regulations-

'Aerodromes' shall have the same meaning assigned to it as in section 124 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010;

'Aeronautical Services' means the aeronautical services referred to in section 31 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010;

'Director General' means the Director General of Civil Aviation appointed under section 11 of the Civil Aviation

Authority Act of Sri Lanka, No. 34 of 2002;

'Service Provider' means the Statutory Service Provider appointed by under section 6(3) of the Civil Aviation

Act, No. 14 of 2010.
