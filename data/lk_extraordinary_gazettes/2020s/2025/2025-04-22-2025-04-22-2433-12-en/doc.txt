The Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40)

Order

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 12 of the Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40), I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President, do by this order call out with effect from April 27, 2025 all the members of the Armed Forces specified in the First Schedule hereto, for the maintenance of public order in the areas specified in the Second Schedule hereto.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President.

April 22, 2025,
