THE ANTIQUITIES ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 188)

Notiﬁcation under Section 33

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 33 of the Antiquities Ordinance (Chapter 188), I, Delath Thusitha Mendis, Director General of Archaeology, do by this notiﬁcation declare with the approval of the Land Commissioner, that the area of the state land speciﬁed in the Schedule, to this Notiﬁcation shall be an “Archaeological Reserve” for the purposes of the aforesaid Ordinance. DELATH THUSITHA MENDIS, Director General of Archaeology.Colombo,08th July, 2025.

SCHEDULE

Area Reserved for Archaeological purposes of Kadawathgama Rajapihilla

All that allotment of land depicted as Lot ‘A’ in the Surveyor General’s Tracing No. Maha/YIN/2023/605 dated 20.10.2023, of the land called as “Kadawathgama Rajapihilla” situated in the village of Kadawathgama in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 204 of Kadawathgama, Central Province Korale (Yatinuwara) in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Yatinuwara, Kandy District, Central Province bounded as follows and which the said allotments of land containing in extent of Seven Decimal Twelve Perches (7.12 Perches) alias Naught Demial Naught One Eight Naught (0.0180 Hectare) is bounded as follows:

North : Lee Magaskotuwa Home Garden of Herath Ranasinghe;East : Home Garden of Herath Ranasinghe and Lot C;South : Footpath and Lot C; West : Footpath and Stream.
