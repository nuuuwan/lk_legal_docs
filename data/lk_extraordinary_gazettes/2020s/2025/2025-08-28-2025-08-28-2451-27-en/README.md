REGULATIONS MADE BY THE MINISTER OF YOUTH AFFAIRS AND SPORTS UNDER SECTION 41 READ WITH SECTION 31 OF THE SPORTS LAW No. 25 OF 1973 AS AMENDED

01. I, Sunil Kumara Gamage, Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 41 read with Section 31 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973 as amended, do hereby amend the Extraordinary Gazette bearing No. 2437/24 dated 21.05.2025, in the manner set out below.

02. The Regulations of Sports Associations No. 01 of 2025, published under the aforesaid Gazette No. 2437/24 dated 21.05.2025, are hereby amended by the substitution of the following sub-paragraphs for sub-paragraphs (2) and (3) of Regulation 4:

(2) Only individuals who possess the following qualifications shall be eligible for election to the positions set out in Section 4(1) (i), (ii), and (iv):

(i) Have represented Sri Lanka in an international competition which has the approval of the Ministry of Sports; and/or

(ii) Have secured first (1st), second (2nd), or third (3rd) place in an individual event at National Senior and/or Junior Championships organized by the relevant National Association or at the National Sports Festival; and/or Have been a member of a team which won the championship, runners-up, or second runners-up positions in team events; and/or

(iv) Has at least fifteen years of professional experience as a Chartered Accountant and/or has at least fifteen years of professional experience as an Attorney-at-Law and/or has at least fifteen years of professional experience as a Doctor with full membership of the Sri Lanka Medical Council and/or has at least fifteen years of professional experience as a Chartered Engineer and/or A person holding a Bachelor’s Degree or a Postgraduate Degree in Sports or Sports Science from any university approved by the University Grants Commission of Sri Lanka.

(3) Only individuals who satisfy the following qualifications shall be eligible for election to the positions referred to in Section 4(1)(iii):

(i) Be a Chartered Accountant; or

(ii) Be a member of Chartered Institute of Management Accountants recognized in Sri Lanka; or

(iii) Be a member of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants; or

(iv) Hold a Bachelor’s Degree or a Postgraduate Degree in Management and/or Commerce from any university approved by the University Grants Commission of Sri Lanka.

Sunil Kumara Gamage (M.P.), Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports.
