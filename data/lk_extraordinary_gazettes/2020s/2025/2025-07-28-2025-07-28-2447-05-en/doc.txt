STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether the flight staff representing the Flight Attendents Union of the Sri Lanka Airline Limited have been caused injustice by the deprival of the right of enjoying casual and sick leave, which is a condition of employment and if so, to what relief they are entitled.
