LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My No.: බිය3/1/1/504. I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land, Acquisition (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude for public purpose. Description of the land to be acquired:- All that allotment of land in total extent of about 0.1381 hectare, depeicted as lot No. 01 in the preliminary plan dated 23.12.2024 and block No. 6, Cadastral Map No. 512405, prepared by the Superintendent of Surveys, situated at the Village Bollegala, No. 278/A, in Adhikari Pattuwa, in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Biyagama in Gampaha District, Western Province and bounded as follows: Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Pelawatta; Description: Road; Claimant: Somalatha Gunasekara, No. 346, Manelwatta Road, Bollegala; Extent: 0.1381 Hectare.(0 Ac. 1 Ro. 14.6 Perches)

All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connection to the aforesaid land, servitude hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on the following days and time at the Divisional Secretariat, Biyagama. Date Lot No. Time 28.07.2025 01 10.00 a.m. And further, you are hereby requested to notify me in writing in duplicate before the 21.07.2025 the nature of their interests in the land/lands, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.
