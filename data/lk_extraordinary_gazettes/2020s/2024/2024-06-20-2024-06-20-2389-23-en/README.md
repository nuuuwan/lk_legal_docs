By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, 1, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of Sri Lanka International Buddhist Academy (SIBA), a company duly incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act. No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number GA 2232, and located at Pallekele, Kundasale being recognized as a degree awarding institute, do by this Order, amend the Order made under the aforesaid Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1948/11 of January 06, 2016, by the repeal of the contents under the heading Schedule and the substitution there for of the following:

Schedule I

Conditions
