Architects registration board

further to the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka (Extraordinary) No. 2386/07 - Tuesday, May 28,2024.

The Architects Registration Board, as per the functions and powers vested upon it by the Section 8C (1) and (2) of Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 1996 has directed me to publish the Institutions the qualifications of which are recognized for the time being for the purpose of registration as Part IV of Schedule B of the Act.

N. W. W. Wijekoon,

Registrar, Architects Registration Board.

Sri Lanka Institute of Architects,No. 120/7, Vidya Mawatha,Colombo 7,21st November 2024.
