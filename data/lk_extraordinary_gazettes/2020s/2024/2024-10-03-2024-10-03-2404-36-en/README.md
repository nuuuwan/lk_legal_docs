the antiquities ordinance (Chapter 188)

Order under Section 33

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 33 of the Antiquities Ordinance (Chapter 188) I, Delath Thusitha Mendis, the Director-General of Archaeology, with the approval of the Land Commissioner General, do by this Notification declare that the area of State land specified in the Schedule hereto shall be an “archaeological reserve” for the purpose of the aforesaid Ordinance.

Delath Thusitha Mendis, Director - General of Archaeology.

Colombo, 17th September, 2024.

Limits of the area of land comprising ‘‘Weragalamookalana” to be declared as an Archaeological Reserve

(a) The said Lot No. A containing in extent 0.4516 hectares (1A-0R-18.55P) is bounded according to the said Tracing on the,
