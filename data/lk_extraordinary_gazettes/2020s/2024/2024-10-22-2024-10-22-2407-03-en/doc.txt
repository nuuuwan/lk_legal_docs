Part II

7.2.5 Method of Recruitment

7.2.5.1 Recruitment shall be made on the results of a written examination. Details of the competitive examination for recruitment are mentioned in Schedule II.

7.2.5.1.1 The matters specified under Conducting Authority of the written examination will be substituted by

the following.

Conducting Authority of the written examination – Commissioner General of Examinations or any other institution approved by the appointing Authority, as the case may be.

7.2.5.2.1 The matters specified therein will be substituted by the following.

Applicants are called for the interview according to the order of the marks obtained at the written examination. The number of candidates called for the interview for recruitment under each subject area (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical and Earth Resources) shall be determined by the public Service Commission.

The Schedule II therein shall be substituted by the following Schedule II.

Schedule II

Objectives and methodology of the written examination conducted for direct recruitments.

(a) This examination shall be conducted in order to determine the order of recruitment for Engineers who have satisfied

qualifications as per 7.2.3. above and apply for post of Engineer in Grade III of sri Lanka Engineering Service. Therefore, the above examination shall be limited to a written aptitude examination for which 100 marks are allocated. The question paper shall consist of questions of multiple-choice and the duration of the paper shall be 1 ½ hours. The order of recruitment shall be determined on the marks obtained by the candidates at the above examination.

(b) This examination will be conducted in Sinhala, Tamil and English languages. Candidates can sit the examination in any one of these languages of their choice. Candidates will not be allowed to change the medium of examination produced by them in the application form.

(c) This examination has been designed with the objective of measuring the ability of the candidate on critical reasoning, analytical power and the ability of decision making based on the knowledge gained through the study of Engineering Science mentioned in 7.2.3 above
