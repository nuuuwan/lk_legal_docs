Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007

Order under Section 5

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 5 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby make an Order to waive-off balance amount after collecting Rs. 1 per Kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 200 per Kg on importation of Dates, classified under the HS Code of 0804.10.10 and 0804.10.20 levied by Order, published the Gazette Notification No. 2360/52 dated November 30, 2023 to be received as donation or gift on no foreign exchange basis from any foreign governments, organizations including business entities, volunteer associations and well-wishers on the recommendation of the Secretary, Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs.

This Order shall be valid from March 02, 2024 to April 11, 2024 subject to provision of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01,01st March, 2024.
