Local Authorities elections ordinance (chapter 262)

Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha

BY virtue of the power vested in the Election Commission by Section 27 (1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by Act, No. 24 of 1987, read with Section 4(1) of the said Act, do hereby appoint the officer specified in Column III of the schedule hereto as the Returning Officer for the Local Authority mentioned in the corresponding entry in Column II, situated in the Administrative District mentioned in the corresponding entry in Column I, for the purpose of electing members to the Local Authority in the said Administrative District.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Ameer Faaiz, Chairman, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.
