Government Notifications

the holidayS act, No. 29 of 1971

Order under Section 10 (1) (a)

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 10(1)(a) of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971, I, Dinesh Gunawardena, Prime Minister and the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, and Local Government, do by this order declare, 23rd of September 2024, a public holiday.

Dinesh Gunawardena (M.P.), Prime Minister and the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government.

Ministry of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government,Nila Medura,Elvitigala Mawatha,Narahenpita,Colombo 05,On 21st September, 2024.
