Government Notifications

1A - PG 6732 — 379 (05/2024)

Welfare Benefits Act, No. 24 of 2002

By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 09 of the Welfare Benefits Act, No. 24 of 2002, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby declare the Aswesuma Welfare Benefit Payment Scheme as specified in the following schedules with effect from 15th June of 2024.

The Notifications published in the Extraordinary Gazette Nos. 2328/13 dated 21.04.2023 and No. 2372/03 dated 19.02.2024 are hereby rescinded.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

May 16th, 2024
