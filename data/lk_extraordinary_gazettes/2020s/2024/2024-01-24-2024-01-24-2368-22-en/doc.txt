Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973

regulation made by the Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs under Section 41 to read with Section 31 of the Sports Law, No. 25 of 1973. Harin Fernando (M. P.), Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs.At Colombo,23rd January, 2024.

Regulation

I, Harin Fernando, the Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs hereby cancel the Gazette (Extraordinary) No. 2342/48 dated 28th of July 2023 and the Gazette (Extraordinary) No. 2344/22 dated 09th of August 2023 with immediate effect and the following Interim Committees appointed to exercise the powers, functions and duties of the relevant Sports Bodies shall be dissolved immediately.

1. Sri Lanka Karate Do Federation 2. Sri Lanka Automobile Sports

In accordance with the provisions of the Sports Act, No. 25 of 1973 and the valid Sports Regulations, the above National Sports Bodies are hereby authorized to continue with the officials/committee members elected in the previous election until the end of the term of their office and a re-election is held.
