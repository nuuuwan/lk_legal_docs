Special commodity levy act, no. 48 of 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this order impose in respect of the commodity specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

This Order shall be valid until December 31,2024 commencing from May 18, 2024 subject to provisions of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01,May 16, 2024.
