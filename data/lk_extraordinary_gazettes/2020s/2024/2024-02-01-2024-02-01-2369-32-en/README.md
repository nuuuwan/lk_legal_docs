EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 03/2024

EXCISE LICENCE FEES

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25 read with Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended from time to time, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies do hereby direct that the annual Excise license fees specified in Schedule I hereto shall come in to effect from February 01, 2024.

The Excise Notification No. 01/2024 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2366/38 dated January 12, 2024 is hereby rescinded.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, EconomicStabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01, February 01, 2024.

7A

1. The difference between the already paid amount of the annual license fee specified in sub-column II of Schedule I above and the license fee revised by this notification, shall be calculated as determined by the Commissioner General of Excise in relation to the remaining period of the current year and it has to be paid within a month from the effective date of this Excise Notification.

2. The difference between the already paid amount of the security deposit amount specified in sub-column III of Schedule I above and the security deposit amount as amended by this notification shall be paid to the Commissioner General of Excise within a period of one month from the effective date of this notification.

3. The one-time fee for industry entry specified under sub-column IV of the above schedule I shall be a non-refundable fee to be paid by the licensee only once at the first issuing of the relevant license and it should be paid to the Commissioner General of Excise from the effective date of this Excise Notification.
