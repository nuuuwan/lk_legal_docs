THE Annual Feast of the Our Lady's Ragama Lanka Basillica Church - 2024

Gampaha District

IT is hereby announced to the general public that The Annual Feast of Ragama Thewatta Lanka Basillica Church 2024 (Feast of the Patients) will be held on 25th of August in 2024 and the duration of the Annual Feast is from 17.08.2024 to 26.08.2024.

1. This will be brought to the attention of the general public on the regulations published in Gazette No. 1953/43 dated 12th February 2016 under the Pilgrimages Ordinance.

2. These publicity - issued laws will be valid during the period of this ceremony.

Lalinda Gamage, District Secretary/ Government Agent, Gampaha.

At District Secretariat - Gampaha,30th of July 2024.
