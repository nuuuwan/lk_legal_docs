constitution of the democratic socialist republic of sri lanka

Order under Article 98 (8) of the Constitution

IN terms of Section 18 of the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980, the main electoral register for the year 2023 related to the revision of the electoral registers for the year 2023 was certified on 31st October 2023.

02. Whereas main electoral register 2023, supplementary electoral register 2023(1) supplementary electoral register 2023(2) and supplementary electoral register 2023(3) remained in effect as on February 01, 2024, in pursuance of the powers vested in the Election Commission under Article 98 (8) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the Election Commission by this Order certify that the number set out in Column II of the Schedule hereto be the number of members which each electoral district specified in the corresponding entry in Column I of the Schedule hereto is entitled to return by virtue of the Proclamation under Article 97 and Article 98.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Ameer Faaiz, Chairman, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.
