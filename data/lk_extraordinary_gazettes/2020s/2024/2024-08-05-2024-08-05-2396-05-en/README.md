PILGRIMAGES ORDINANCE

IN terms of the regulations published in the Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 1620 dated 18th of September 2009 under the Section 2 of the Pilgrimages Ordinance, it is hereby notified that Annual Religious Festival 2024 of Rotumba Pethmaga Ancient Temple (Rotumba Purana Rajamaha Viharaya) of Pasgoda Divisional Secretariat, Matara District, Southern Province will be held from 27th day of August to 02nd day of September 2024. W. P. Shanika I. Perera, Divisional Secretary, Pasgoda. at Divisional Secretariat, Pasgoda,01st day of August, 2024.
