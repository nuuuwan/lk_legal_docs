Notice under Section 53(1) (b) of the Statute of Central Province Co-operative Societies Statute No. 10 of 1990 as amended by the Statute No. 04 of 1993

ALL members, officers, employees and non-members who have done business with one of the following Co-operative societies in the Nuwara Eliya district of the central province where the registration has been canceled and the liquidation proceedings are being carried out are being called in relation to the money owed to the institutions. Accordingly the submission of claims should be made through an application form prepared in the following format and one application form should be sent to the liquidator by registered mail on or before 16.11.2024 Alternatively you can meet and deliver at the following address 16.11.2024 I further note that the claim submitted after 16.11.2024 will not be considered.

Amila Navaratne, Commissioner of Co-operative Development & Registrar of Co-operative Societies, Central Province.

Central Provincial Department of Co-operative Development,P.O. Box 02,Ehalepola Kumarihamy Mawatha,Bogambara,Kandy,16th August, 2024.
