By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, 1, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (Guarantee) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act.
