Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the KIU Campus (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 66705 and located at No. 249/1, Malabe Road, Koswatta, Battaramulla being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2353/17, of October 09, 2023, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Laws Honours” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto:-

Schedule I

Conditions

1. KIU Campus (Private) Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Honours in Data Science, complete with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of;

a. At least three (3) simple passes (S) in Physical Science stream Engineering Technology stream in one and the same sitting at the G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification; or
