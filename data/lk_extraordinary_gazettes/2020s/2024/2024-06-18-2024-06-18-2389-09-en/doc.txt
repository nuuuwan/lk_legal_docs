Southern Province Provincial Council

Matara Pradeshiya Sabha

Imposition of entertainment Tax

Notice made under section 2 (1) of No. 12 of Entertainment Tax Ordinance 1946

AS per the powers vested by No. 12 of 1989 Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act I, William Wijesinghe Gamage, Honourable Governor of Southern Province hereby approve the decision taken under desicion No. 2023/08/08/03 of decision book on 08.08.2023 by Matara Pradeshiya Sabha as per the powers vested by sub section 2(1) of Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 to impose and recover a Tax of ten percent (10%) of payment made for the entrance for any purpose of entertainment which are described in Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 revised by No. 37 of 1984 Entertainment Tax (Amendment) ordinance.

Dr. William Wijesinghe Gamage, Honourable Governor of Southern Province.

At Governor's Secretariat,On 26th, January, 2024.

Decision

I hereby decide that an amount of money similar to 10% (Ten percent) from the payment made for any entertainment activity mentioned in the aforementioned ordinance maintained within the Matara Pradeshiya Sabha administration limits as per sub section 2 (1) of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 revised by No. 37 of 1984 Entertainment Tax (Amendment) Ordinance should be imposed and recovered as a Tax and it should take effect from 01.09.2023. Southern Province Provincial Council

Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha

entertainment Tax

Notice made under section (2) of Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946

I, William Wijesinghe Gamage - the Governor of Southern Province do hereby notify that by virtue of powers vested in me by Provincial Council Act (supplementary provisions) No. 12 of 1989 I have approved the proposal passed by pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha under Sabha decision No. 01 X dated 20.12.2022 as per provisions of section 2(1) of Entertainment Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 to impose and recover an entertainment Tax of 10% of the admission fee for the events of entertainment declared by Entertainment Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 amended by Entertainment Tax (Amendment) Ordinance No. 17 of 1984. Dr William Wijesinghe Gamage, Honourable Governor of Southern Province.

At Governor's Secretariat,On 29th December, 2023.

Proposal

By virtue of powers vested by sub section 2(1) of Entertainment Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 amended by Entertainment Tax (Amendment) Ordinance No. 17 of 1984, Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha hereby proposes to impose and recover an amount similar to Ten percent (10%) of payment as a Tax for an entertainment event described in the Ordinance aforesaid which are held in the administrative area of Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha and this proposal shall be effective from 01.01.2023. it is futher proposed that the entertainment Tax aforesaid should be paid to Pitabeddara Pradeshiya Sabha by the organizer or organizers concerned before the day of holding that event of entertainment.

Decision

In terms of the powers conferred on the Secretary of the Welivitiya Divithura Pradeshiya Sabha by sub-section (1) of the second section of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance Act No. 12 of 1946 (being the 267th Authority) to be read with section 9 (3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987. to levy an amount equal to 10% (ten percent) of a payment made to any entertainment activity described in the above mentioned Entertainment Tax Ordinance conducted in the areas within the administrative limits of the Welivitiya Divithura Pradeshiya Sabha as a Tax and that this decision shall be effective from the dale of its publication in the Government Gazette. Furthermore, I also decide that the entertainment Tax levied above should be properly paid to the Welivitiya Divithura local council by the relevant organizer or organizers before the day of the said entertainment.
