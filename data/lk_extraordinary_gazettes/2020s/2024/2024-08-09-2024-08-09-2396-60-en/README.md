STRATEGIC DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS ACT, No. 14 OF 2008

Order under Sub - section (4) of Section 3

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by Sub - section (4) of Section 3 of the Strategic Development Projects Act, No. 14 of 2008 as amended, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe , Minister of Investment Promotion, further to the Order published under Sub - section (4) of Section 3 of the said Strategic Development Projects Act, No. 14 of 2008 in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1996/26 dated December 06, 2016 in respect of CHEC PORT CITY COLOMBO (PRIVATE) LIMITED, do by this Order declare that,

Paragraph (4) of the Order published under Section 3(4) of the Strategic Development Projects Act, No. 14 of 2008 in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1996/26 dated December 06, 2016 wherein the Project Company was granted a Project Implementation Period (PIP) of Eight (08) years commencing from the date of September 07, 2016 and ending on September 06, 2024, is hereby amended by extending the said Project Implementation Period by a further period of Thirty Three (33) months and Twenty Four (24) days with effect from September 07, 2024 and ending on June 30, 2027 to complete the balance implementation work and commence commercial operations of the entire project of the Project Company.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion.08th August, 2024.
