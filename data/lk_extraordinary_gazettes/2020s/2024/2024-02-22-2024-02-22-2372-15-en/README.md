Statement of matter in dispute

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether Mr. S. R. Weeraddana who worked at CML - MTD Construction Limited is entitled for

- half months pay for the month of April 2019 and - salary from March 2020 to september 2020 the period during which employees were informed to stay at home due to the Covid - 19 pandemic

and whether injustice had been caused to him by not getting then and if so, to what relief he is entitled.

H. K. K. A. Jayasundara, Commissioner General of Labour.
