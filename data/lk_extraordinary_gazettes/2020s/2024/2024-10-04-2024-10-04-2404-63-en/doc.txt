NOTICE UNDER SECTION 7(4)(b)PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS ACT, NO. 01 OF 1981

01. The list of political parties that are entitled to be treated as recognized political parties for the purpose of elections under section 7(4) (b) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 01 of 1981, is indicated in Schedule (A) below.

02. The political parties that cannot submit nominations as recognized political parties for the upcoming Parliamentary

Election scheduled for the 14th November 2024, due to disputes involing the office of party secretary, are indicated in Schedule (B).

03. The Gazette Extraordinary No. 2403/67 September 28, 2024 is hereby rescinded.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.
