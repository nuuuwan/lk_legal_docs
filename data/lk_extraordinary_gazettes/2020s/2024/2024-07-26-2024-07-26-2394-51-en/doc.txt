presidential Election - 2024

Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981

Order under Section 2

WhEreas the term of the President expires on November 17, 2024 and,

Whereas the poll for the election of the President shall be taken not less than one month and not more than two months before the expiration of the term of office of the President in office, as stipulated in Article 31 (3) of the Constitution,

In terms of the powers vested in the Election Commission by Section 2 of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981 read with Article 104 B of the Constitution it is hereby ordered.

(I) 15th August 2024 as the date of nomination of candidates for the Election of the President ;

(II) office of the Election Commission, Sarana Mawatha, Rajagiriya, as the place of nomination of candidates for the said election ; and

(III) 21st September, 2024, as the date for holding the poll for that election.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Ameer Faaiz, Chairman, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.
