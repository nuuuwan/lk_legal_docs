THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

Notiﬁcation

WHEREAS I have in the exercise of powers vested in me in terms of Paragraph (1) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, determined the number of Ministers of the Cabinet of Ministers and the Ministries and the assignment of subjects and functions and Departments, Statutory Institutions and Public Corporations to the said Ministers;

It is now hereby notiﬁ ed that the subjects and functions and Departments, Statutory Institutions and Public Corporations in the charge of various Ministers shall be as set out in the schedule given below, from the date of this Notiﬁ cation.

ANURA KUMARA DISSANAYAKE, President.

Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01, November 25, 2024.
