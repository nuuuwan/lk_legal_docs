ANNUAL ESALA FESTIVaL sapugoda kamburupitiya - 2024

in terms of the regulations published in 308 numbered Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka issued on 27th of June 1984, under the Pilgrimages Ordinance (Chapter 175), it is hereby notified that Annual Esala Festival 2024 of Ilangamgoda Ancient Rajamaha Viharaya (Sapugoda Pirivena) Sapugoda Kamburupitiya in Matara District, Southern Province will be held from 20th of July to 28th of July 2024. Chintha P. W. Wanniarachchi, Divisional Secretary, Kamburupitiya.

At Divisional Secretariat, Kamburupitiya,20th day of May, 2024.
