REVISION OF ELECTORAL REGISTERS

Notice that the Supplementary Electoral Register 2023(3) has been certified under the Registration of Electors Act

NOTICE is given under sub-section 20A (10) of the Registration of Electors Act for the Registering Officer of each electoral district that the 2023(3) Supplementary Electoral Register containing the names of persons who attained the age of 18 years on a certain date within the period from 01 October 2023 to 31 January 2024 was certified on 01 February 2024 in terms of the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980 as amended by the Registration of Electors (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2021; the said registers have been certified by the Assistant Registering Officer in the corresponding entry in Column II for the electoral districts mentioned in column I of the schedule below; and that the said registers can be inspected during the normal office hours of the District Election Office/Offices mentioned in the corresponding entry in Column III thereof.

Saman Sri Ratnayake, Commissioner General of Elections.

At the Elections Secretariat, Rajagiriya,on 01 February, 2024.
