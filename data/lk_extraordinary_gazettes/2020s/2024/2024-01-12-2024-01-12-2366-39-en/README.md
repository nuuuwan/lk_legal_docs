EXCISE ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 52)

Excise Notification No. 02/2024

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 32 to read with Section 25 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies do hereby declare that the following rules made by me shall come into effect from January 12, 2024.

The Excise Notification No. 902 published in the Extraordinary Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka bearing No. 1544/17 dated April 10, 2008 and the provisions in paragraph (2) of the Excise Notification No. 983 published in the Extraordinary Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka bearing No. 1945/17 dated December 15, 2015 are hereby rescinded.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, Colombo 01,January 12, 2024.

Rules

1. These Rules may be cited as the ‘Excise Notification No. 02 /2024’.

2. In terms of the Excise Ordinance No.08 of 1912 (Chapter 52) hereinafter referred to as ‘the Ordinance’;

I. From and after the date of coming into effect of these rules, all persons expecting to obtain any liquor selling license which is contemporarily issued, shall forward an application to the Commissioner General of Excise by using the application form specified in Schedule I hereto.

II. A request shall be made to the Commissioner General of Excise, by using the application form specified in the Schedule II hereto by all those persons expecting to renew an existing excise license for the sale of liquor for the coming year, by using the application form specified in the Schedule III hereto by an applicant for the co-licensure of any excise license for the sale of liquor, by using the application form specified in Schedule IV hereto by all persons expecting to transfer the ownership of an excise license for the sale of liquor, and by using the application form specified in Schedule V hereto by all persons expecting to transfer of location of any such licensed premises.

III. An amount of Rs. 25,000.00 shall be paid as a non-refundable application fee in cases of applying for a new excise license for the sale of liquor, an extension of the validity period of such existing license, transfer of ownership of such license, applying for the co-licensure of such license or transfer of location of the licensed premises.

IV. However, Payment of the application fee mentioned in above paragraph III should not be applicable to the Excise Licenses for the sale of liquor of the categories of Excise F.L. 14 Occasional License, Excise F.L. 15 Supplementary License to extend Hours of Sale and Excise F.L.16 Auctioneers License.

V. In cases of applying for a new excise license for the sale of liquor, transfer of ownership of such license, applying for co- licensure of such license or transfer of the location of the licensed premises, those application forms can be obtained from the Excise Head Office by paying the prescribed application fee. However, application forms for extending the validity period of such licenses can be obtained from the Excise Head Office or Offices of the Assistant Commissioners of Excise located island wide.

VI. Applications obtained as per the provisions of these rules shall be completed and submitted along with relevant documents to the Excise Officer-in-Charge of the excise area of authority, where the proposed premises for the license is located in cases of new licenses, and where the existing licensed premises is located in cases of existing licenses.

VII. However, the Commissioner General of Excise reserves the right to search and extract any information relating to any applicant from any document file available at the Excise Department or to request and obtain any information necessary for considering the issuance of the license applied for, including details of the income tax or other tax payments of the applicant.

VIII. All applications submitted as per the above Schedule VI should be completed in all aspects and all the required documents should be annexed. Incomplete applications or applications submitted without necessary documents or which does not conform to the guidelines and conditions set out in the Schedule VI to these rules will be rejected.

IX. However, the issuance of any application under the provisions of this section should not be a guarantee for the issuance of a new excise license for the sale of liquor, the transfer of ownership of an excise license or the appointment of a co- licensure to a license or the approval for the transfer of location of a licensed premises as applied for by such application.

3. Upon receipt of all requests under Rule 2 above, the Commissioner General of Excise shall consider those applications in accordance with the provisions of the Excise Ordinance and shall satisfy such applications are in accordance with the guidelines and conditions specified in Schedule VI hereto.

4. Relevant license fee specified for the particular type of license for the particular period of time should be paid by the relevant licensee in cases of granting a new excise license to sell liquor or extending the period of validity of such license already been granted under these rules.

5. All excise licenses for the sale of liquor are issued for a period of one calendar year (January 01 to December 31), and the relevant license fee for the particular year should be paid at least seven working days before the date of commencement of the relevant fiscal year. However, in case of not receiving the said license fee by the relevant Divisional Secretary within seven working days from the commencement of the relevant financial year, permission should be granted to pay the said license fee subjected to a fine of one percent of the license fee within a period of four weeks. Afterwards that time period of four weeks the said fine should be three percent of the license fee.

6. Those licenses issued at some point in the middle of the year, will be so issued only for the rest of the year. In respect of those licenses issued in the middle of the year, license fees should be charged proportionately for the rest of that year.

7. Further, every person who transfer the right to any existing excise license for the sale of liquor, become a co-licensee of such license or transfers the location of the said license, shall for the time being pay the following fees in respect thereof.

Fee for the transfer of location of a license - Rs. 250,000/-

Fee for the transfer of rights of an Excise F.L.4 license In case of a legal heir - Rs. 250,000/- In case of a non- legal heir - Rs. 15,000,000/-

Fee for the transfer of rights of an excise license other than Excise F.L.4 license In case of a legal heir - Rs. 250,000/- In case of a non- legal heir - Rs. 2,500,000/-

8. Generally, these guidelines and conditions shall be applicable until they are amended or repealed regarding the issuance of excise licenses for the sale of liquor in respect of a calendar year beginning on the 1st day of January of a particular year and ending on the 31st day of December of that year, as well as for any period beginning from a date after the initial date of a particular year and ending on or prior to December 31 of that year.

9. Every person to whom a liquor license is been granted should write and sign a counterpart agreement covering the validity period of their license as prescribed by the Excise Ordinance. Further, the security deposit applicable for the time being in respect of the type of excise license for the sale of liquor applied for shall be deposited as a security for the proper execution of his agreement.

10. If the Commissioner General of Excise refuses granting a liquor license to the applicant, he should inform the applicant those reasons for holding such opinion by registered mail within 30 days of the receipt of the relevant application, and also should inform the applicant to submit if he has reasons to justify granting of a license for him. Accordingly, those reasons against the reasons given by the Commissioner General of Excise should be forwarded to him in writing within two weeks of the receipt of the notice of the Commissioner General of Excise by registered post, along with any documentary to prove the facts presented.

(b) Licensed premises or proposed premises for the sale of Beer, Ale, Stout and Wine only, by retail not for consumption on the premises shall have a floor area of ​at least 100 square feet, and Licensed premises or proposed premises for the sale of Beer, Ale, Stout and Wine only, by retail for consumption on the premises shall have a floor area of ​at least 250 square feet.

(2) Extension of the validity of existing Excise Licenses for the sale of liquor

I. Current holders of excise licenses are not entitled to automatic renewal thereof, and upon the expiry of the validity period of the said licenses, if the relevant licensee intends to extend further the validity period of the said license, he shall submit his application within 30 days in advance of each year according to these guidelines and conditions.

II. The application that submitted as prescribed above will be considered by the Commissioner General of Excise in accordance with these guidelines and conditions and in accordance with any other provisions laid down in the Excise Ordinance.

III. Applications for a license in respect of a licensed premises already in operation should have been fully paid and settled for all claims due in respect of the license already in operation or the license previously in operation or any other license in the name of the applicant.

IV. If there is an inquiry still pending in respect of such licenses already in operation, to execute a license on the same premises or to execute another license in the name of the applicant will be considered upon a security deposit equivalent to a compounding fee that may be imposed by the Commissioner General of Excise in accordance with the Excise (Amendment) Act No. 37 of 1990 and subject to the results of such inquiry.

(3) Transfer the ownership of an existing Excise License for the sale of liquor:

I. A request for transfer the ownership of an excise license to another person will be considered only after paying the relevant application fee and obtaining an application form in the name of the proposed transferee, duly completed and submitted along with the relevant documents, and only if the proposed transferee fulfills all the requirements mentioned in these rules to be completed in order to obtain the license.

II. If the proposed transferee is not a legal heir of the excise license holder concerned, the proposed transferee should be liable to pay the fee prescribed in the present for transferring the right of the license.

(4) Applying for the co-licensure of an existing Excise License for the sale of liquor

I. A request to grant the co-licensure to another person or persons in addition to the current license holder or license holders related to any excise license will be considered only if the relevant application fee is paid and obtaining an application form or application forms in the name of the proposed co-licensee or co-licensees and duly completing the relevant documents and only if the proposed co-licensee or co-licensees meet all the requirements of these rules to be completed to obtain the licensure.

II. The proposed co-licensee should pay the relevant fee prescribed in the present for the appointment as a co-licensee.

(5) Transferring the location of an existing Excise License for the sale of liquor:

I. Transfer of premises on which an excise license has been granted for the sale of liquor is not normally permitted. However, in cases of a natural disaster or due to a Government development activity or any other reason considered reasonable by the Commissioner General of Excise such as proximity to other licenses and uneven distribution of licensed premises etc., it should be permissible to transfer the location to which an excise license is issued within the same district in which it was in operation.

II. However, the rule contained in the section I above, shall not apply for the wholesale licenses for the sale of liquor.

III. Transfer of the location of a premises granted with an excise license would be permitted only if the proposed premises conforms to the rules, guidelines and conditions set out in this notification.

Schedule VII

Criteria for the approval of restaurants and hotels related to the location and establishment of the licensed premises or proposed premises to sell liquor by retail for onsite consumption that does not require the approval of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority.

1. Criteria for Location:(a) The location and the surrounding environment should be suitable for the establishment and operating of a

Restaurant/ Hotel

(b) The appearance and construction of the building shall be in good condition.

2. Criteria for the extent of floor area:

(a) The minimum floor area should be 500 square feet

(b) There should be sufficient space to arrange tables and chairs to provide seating accommodation for at least 25 persons at a time.

3. Criteria for Building:

(a) The whole household including all ancillaries thereof (that is the building, furniture, furnishings, equipment, shelves, fixtures, etc.) should be of high standard and properly maintained.

(b) Should have a sufficient number of electric fans and air conditioning facility.

(c) Tables and chairs as well as furniture, amenities and equipment should be of appropriate high standard.

(d) Proper ventilation should be provided with.

(e) Facilities for listening to live or recorded music should be available.

(f) Household and space thereof should be free from creatures, insects and rodents.

4. The kitchen:

(a) The kitchen should be separated from the serving area and tidily maintained

(b) Cookery, cutlery and glassware should be of good quality and without damage.

(c) Table linen should be of quality material and clean.

(d) The kitchen and pantry should be well designed and equipped to ensure efficient and hygienic preparation of food and should be well maintained, clean and in hygienic condition.

(e) The kitchen should be ready with suitable wood, gas ovens, and electrical stoves/cookers.

(f) Kitchen floors and walls should be cleaned and of impervious material.

(g) Kitchen utensils should be adequate of good quality and be cleaned.

(h) Kitchen tables and shelves should be built of good quality material and should be of impervious finish.

(i) There should be adequate cold storage facilities.

(j) There should be separate facilities for purpose of washing of crockery, cutlery, tableware, cooking utensil, equipment, pots and pans etc.,

(k) Separate facilities should be available for cleaning hands of staff.

(l) Kitchen drains and outlets from sinks should be in good condition and unexposed within the building.

5. Foods:(a) Food served to guests should be of good quality, hygienic, and clean.

(b) Food required for serving must always be available when Restaurant /Hotel is kept opened.

(c) Drinking water should be boiled and filtered or otherwise suitably treated.

6. Criteria for Toilet Facilities:(a) There should be toilets with modern sanitary facilities, with separate toilets for females and males.

(b) Toilets should be maintained in excellent condition and all fittings and equipment should be in good working order.

(c) All toilets to be provided with water on tap, soap, towels, etc.

(d) There should be separate toilets with modern sanitary facilities for the use of the staff.
