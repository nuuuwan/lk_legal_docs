Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007 , I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this Order impose in respect of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto a Special Commodity Levy at the rate specified in the corresponding entry in Column II of that Schedule.

Schedule

Column I Column IICommodity DetailsSpecial Commodity LevyH.S. HeadingH.S. CodeDescription1.07.010701.90Other - PotatoesRs.60.00 per Kg2.07.030703.10.20B’ OnionsRs.30.00 per Kg

This Order shall be valid for a period commencing from October 03, 2024 to November 30, 2024 subject to provisions of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.
