constitution of the democratic socialist republic of sri lanka

Order under Article 98 (8) of the Constitution

IN terms of Section 18 of the Registration of Electors Act No. 44 of 1980, the main electoral register for the year 2024 related to the revision of the electoral registers for the year 2024 was certified on 01 July 2024.

02. Whereas the main electoral register 2024 remained in effect as on July 01, 2024, in pursuance of the powers vested in the Election Commission under Article 98 (8) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the Election Commission by this Order certify that the number set out in Column II of the Schedule hereto be the number of members which each electoral district specified in the corresponding entry in Column I of the Schedule hereto is entitled to return by virtue of the Proclamation under Article 97 and Article 98.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission,Rajagiriya,17th July, 2024.
