Notice of poll

Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)

Notice under sub-section 38(1)(c)

Election of Members to the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha of Galle District

It is hereby notified in terms of sub-section 38(1)(c) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that the elections will be contested in relation to the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha in Galle District and the poll for this local authority will be taken on October 26, 2024 from 7.00 a.m. to 4.00 p. m. .

W. A. Dharmasiri, Returning Officer, Galle Administrative District.

At Galle District Secretariat,On 12th September, 2024.
