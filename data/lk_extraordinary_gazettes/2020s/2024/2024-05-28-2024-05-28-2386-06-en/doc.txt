Notice of approval of the development plan for the

Municipal COuncil area of Kaduwela

Notice is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8(G) of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 04 of 1982 that I, Prasanna Ranathunga, the Minister in charge of the subject of Urban Development & Housing, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8(F) of the said Act, No. 04 of 1982 have approved the Development Plan for the Municipal Council Area of Kaduwela, prepared under Section 8(A) of the said Act on the 16th Day of May, 2024.

Prasanna Ranathunga (M. P.), Minister of Urban Development and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development and Housing,12th Floor,“Sethsiripaya” Stage II,Battaramulla,21st May, 2024.
