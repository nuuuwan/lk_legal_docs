Presidential election - 2024

Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981

Notice under section 64(2)

ELECTION Commission does hereby notify under Section 64(2) of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981, that, Mr. Anura Kumara Dissanayake has been declared elected as the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, under Section 61 of the aforesaid Act.
