Notice under Section 53(1) (b) of the Statute of Central Province Co-operative

Societies No. 10 of 1990 as amended by the Statute No. 04 of 1993

ALL members, officers, employees and non-members who have done business with one of the following Co-operative societies in the Nuwara Eliya district of the central province where the registration has been canceled and the liquidation proceedings are being carried out are being called in relation to the money owed to the institutions.
