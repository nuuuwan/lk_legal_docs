Establishment of National Hospital, Galle

CONSIDERING the socio-economic benefits gained by the catchment population of the Southern Part of the country and the Teaching Hospital - Karapitiya has already fulfilled required service criteria for categorizing as a National Hospital, it was proposed to establish National Hospital, Galle replacing the Teaching Hospital - Karapitiya.

As per the decision of the Cabinet of Ministers made at the Cabinet meeting held on 13.05.2024, the Teaching Hospital - Karapitiya was upgraded and re-categorized as the National Hospital, Galle.

Dr. P. G. Mahipala, Secretary, Ministry of Health.
