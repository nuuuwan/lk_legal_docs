(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

order under Section 10(1)(a) of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 10(1)(a) of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971, I, Dinesh Gunawardena, Prime Minister and the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government, do by this order declare, 15th of April 2024, a public holiday.

Dinesh Gunawardena, Prime Minister and the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government.

Ministry of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government, Nila Medura,Elvitigala Mawatha,Narahenpita,Colombo 05,On 10th April, 2024.
