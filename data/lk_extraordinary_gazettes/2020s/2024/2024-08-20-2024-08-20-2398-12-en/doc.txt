Provincial Councils Notifications

Western Province Provincial Council

Criteria to be adopted when prescribing service charges for sewerage services maintained by the Colombo Municipal Council within its authority area

I hereby announce that as per the provisions prescribed to me under Section 4 of the "Order relevant to prescribing charges on maintenance of sewerage service by Colombo Municipal Council in the Colombo Municipal Council authority area", which was prescribed under Section 326 of the Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) read with Section 2 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989 and which was published by the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2310/22 dated 14.12.2022 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the criteria decided by me as applicable when prescribing charges for sewerage services maintained within the Colombo Municipal Council Authority Area will be in included into the below mentioned Schedule and published hereby.

S. K. Jayasundara, Commissioner of Local Government, Western Province.

At the Western Provincial Council Building,No. 204,Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha,Battaramulla,On 16th day of August, 2024.

03. Service charge for sewerage shall be determined on the basis of water consumption of each premises and for that purpose, only the method of charging declared in the notification issued by the Minister in Charge of the Water Supply subject from time to time with amendments under Section 84 of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board Act No. 02 of 1974 shall be applied.

04. The water consumption of each premises which will be required in charging service charges for sewerage shall be obtained from the National Water Supply and Drainage Board.

05. In the event several other alternative water resources are used other than the water supply of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board an additional fee appropriately determined by the Colombo Municipal Council shall be charged as a usage fee and such fee shall not exceed the usage fee charged by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board.

06. In the event only sewerage service is obtained without obtaining the water supply from the National Water Supply and Drainage Board, usage fee shall be levied on an estimated water consumption value determined subsequent to a field inspection conducted by the officers of the Colombo Municipal Council together with the consumer's participation and in accordance to the method of charging stated in the above No. 03.

07. Although the water consumption of any month is zero, the monthly sewerage charge shall be applied for all premises.

08. In the event the sewerage service is removed from the operative condition at any point, a re-connection fee and an additional fee appropriately determined by the Colombo Municipal Council shall be charged according to the cost that will be spent to bring the said connection to re-operative level and such charges shall not exceed the charges levied by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board for this task.
