filling of a vacancy under paragraph 13(b) of article 99 of the constitution of the Democratic Socialist republic of sri lanka

WHEREAS, the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64 (1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Ninth Parliament by reason of the resignation of Mr. Lokugasthota Vitharanage Chaminda Wijesiri, who was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the Electoral District No. 19 - Badulla under Section 62 of the aforesaid Act;

And whereas, the Returning Ofﬁcer, for the Electoral District which returned the said member was directed by the Election Commission under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 to ﬁll this vacancy as provided for under Sub Article 99(13)(B) of the Constitution;

And whereas, the said Returning Ofﬁcer acting under Section 64(2) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 and in terms of Sub Article 99(13)(B) of the Constitution has made a return in form "M" set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. Wasalathilaka Gedara Nayana Priyankara Wasalathilaka was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said Electoral District;
