Provincial Councils Notifications

Western Province Provincial Council

Notification made under Section 3 (8) of the Provincial Road Development AuthorityStatute No. 04 of 1989 of the Western Provincial Council

I, Roshan Goonetileke, the Governor of the Western Province, in terms of the powers vested in me under Article 154 (C) of the 13th Amendment to the Constitution and Section 3 (8) of the Provincial Road Development Authority Statute No. 04 of 1989 of the Western Provincial Council do hereby notify the removal of Upali Kodikara who functions as a member and the chairman of the Provincial Road Development Authority of the Western Province from the membership of the Authority as it is deemed satisfied that the said Upali Kodikara has failed to execute the powers and duties of the Authority properly when functioning in his office.

Roshan Goonetileke, RWP and BAR VSV USP Phd (Hon), FIM (Sri Lanka) ndc psc, Marshal of the Sri Lanka Air Force The Governor, Western Province.At Governors' Office - Western Province,Provincial Council Office Complex,Battaramulla,On 23rd day of August, 2024.
