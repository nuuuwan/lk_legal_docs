REVISION OF ELECTORAL REGISTERS

Notice that the Supplementary Electoral Register 2024(2) has been certified under the Registration of Electors Act

In terms of Section 20 A (10) of the Registration of Electors Act, notice is given on behalf of the Registering Officer of each electoral district that the Supplementary Electoral Register 2024(2) including the names of the registered electors, who has attained the age of 18 years on a specified date within the period from 01st June 2024 eptember 2024 has been certified on 01st October 2024 in accordance with the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980 as amended by the Registration of Electors (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2021; that the electoral register for the electoral districts mentioned in column I of the schedule below has been certified by the Assistant Registering Officer in the corresponding entry in Column II thereof; that inspection may be made during normal office hours at the District Election Office/offices mentioned in the corresponding entry in Column III thereof.

Saman Sri Ratnayake, Commissioner General of Elections, Election Commission.

On 01st October, 2024,At the Elections Commission, Rajagiriya.
