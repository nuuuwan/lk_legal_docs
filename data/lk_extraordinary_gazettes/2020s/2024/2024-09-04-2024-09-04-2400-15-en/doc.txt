Forest Conservation Ordinance

THE order under sub section 4(2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of powers vested in me under sub section 4(2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation do by this Order release the block of land of the extent 10.0 hectares and bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedule given below, of the Puliyankulama Reserved Forest which is of the extent of 9,361.0 hectares and was declared as a Reserved Forest by Gazette Extraordinary No. 1770/13 dated 08th August 2012, from the Reserved status from 29th day of August, 2024.

Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation.

Ministry of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation,No. 07, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07,29th August, 2024.
