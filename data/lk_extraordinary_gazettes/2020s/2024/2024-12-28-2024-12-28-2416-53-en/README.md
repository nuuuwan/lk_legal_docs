Regulations

The Food (Iodization of salt) Regulations 2023, published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2332/03 of May 15, 2023 as amended by regulations published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2364/27 of December 27, 2023 is hereby amended by the repeal of regulation 1 thereof, and the substitution thereof the following regulation :

“1. These regulations may be cited as Food (Iodization of Salt) Regulations, 2023 and shall come into operation with effects from July 01, 2025.”
