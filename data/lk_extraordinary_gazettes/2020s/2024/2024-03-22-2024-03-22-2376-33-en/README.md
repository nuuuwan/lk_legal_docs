Pilgrimages Ordinance

39th Ordination Ceremony (Upasampada Ceremony) of Sri Rohana Maha Sanga Sabha of Maha Vihara Wansika Syamopali Maha Nikaya

IN terms of regulations mentioned in the Section 2 of the Pilgrimages Ordinance (Chapter 175), L. D. B 11/60 and dated 14th October 1991, it has been published that the 39th Ordination Ceremony (Upasampada Ceremony) of Sri Rohana Maha Sanga Sabha of Kamburupitiya Divisional Secretary’s Division in Matara District will be held from 31st of May and 1st, 2nd of June, 2024 at the “Seema Malaka” of Rohana Sanga Sabha Headquarters of Urapola Siri Rathanajothi Piriven Viharaya and the devotees are requested not to bring plastic and Polythene based products. And also it is hereby informed that the standing orders and regulations published by the Government Gazette of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka shall be valid within this time period of the Ordination Ceremony (Upasampada Ceremony).

Chintha P. W. Wanniarachchi, Divisional Secretary, Kamburupitiya.

At Divisional Secretariat,Kamburupitiya,22nd day of February, 2024.
