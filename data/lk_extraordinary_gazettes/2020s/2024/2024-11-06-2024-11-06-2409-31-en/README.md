NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No : 12/0457 POLONNARUWA DISTRICT IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 120164 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the Village of Vinsar park (Block 03) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.73 - Siriketha in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Hingurakgoda in the District of Polonnaruwa in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 29th of November 2024 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such deter-mination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 120164 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s Office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Settlement. P. C. D. Sං඀ൾඋൺ, Commissioner General of Land Title Settlement.
