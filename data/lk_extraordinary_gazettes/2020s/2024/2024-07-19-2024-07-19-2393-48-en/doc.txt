The Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460)Declaration under Section 5

I, Nalaka Jude Harin Fernando, Minister of Tourism and Lands hereby declare under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the lands described below are needed for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

The allotments of land called Viharawatta in extent of about 3.8868 Hectares in depicted Lots A and B in Advance Tracing No. KA/PLN/2020/396 prepared by Surveyor General dated 29.06.2022 situated in the village of Viharagama in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Palindanuwara in Kalutara District and bounded as follows:

01. Lot A – Viharawatta – Extent of about: 3.5793 Hectare North: Lot B in this tracing and Viharawatte claimed by land Reform Commission; East: Viharawatta, claimed by Land Reform Commission and K.V. Karunawathi; South: Viharawatta, claimed by K.V. Karunawathi, Road and Patabadiwatta claimed by K.V. Udaris and others; West: Patabadiwatta claimed by K.V. Udaris and others.

02. Lot B – Viharawatta – Extent of about: 0.3075 Hectare North: Road, Viharawatta claimed by land Reforms Commission, Lot A in this tracing and Viharawatta, claimed by K.V. Karunawathi; East: Viharawatta, claimed by Land Reforms Commission and Lot A in this tracing and Viharawatta, claimed by K.V. Karunawathi; South: Patabadiwatta claimed by K.V. Udaris, Lot A in this tracing; West: Patabadiwatta claimed by K.V. Udaris and others, Lot A in this tracing. Harin Fernando, Minister of Tourism and Lands.
