Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981

Notice under Section 30

In accordance with under Section 30 of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981, it is hereby notified that the notice issued by the Election Commission under Section 21 of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981, and published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2397/51 dated 15.08.2024, is amended by substituting the words and figures “ will be conducted till the time at which the ballot papers are handed over to all the voters waiting in the queue/queues at the polling station by 4.00 p.m.; the ballot papers shall be issued to those voters “ with ” “will be held till 4.00 p.m. ” in paragraph (B) of the

notice.
