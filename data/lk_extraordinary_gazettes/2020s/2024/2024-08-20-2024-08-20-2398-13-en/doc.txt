Regulations

The Fertilizer (Import, Manufacture and Formulation) Regulations, No. 1 of 2010, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1,683/5 of December 06, 2010 is hereby amended as follows :-

1. by the repeal of regulation 3 thereof and the substitution therefor of the following regulation :-

''3. The fee payable under Section 6 of the Act, in respect of the application for a licence shall be as follows :-

(a)a licence to import fertilizer-Rs.150,000.00

(b)a licence to manufacture inorganic fertilizer-Rs.150,000.00

(c)a licence to manufacture organic or eco-friendly fertilizer-Rs.20,000.00

(d)a licence to formulate fertilizer-Rs.150,000.00

Provided that, a person who manufactures organic or eco-friendly fertilizer for his own consumption shall not be required to obtain a licence under the provisions of Section 6 of the Act.'' and

2. by the repeal of regulation 4 thereof and the substitution therefor of the following regulation :-

''4. The fee payable under Section 8 of the Act, in respect of the application for the renewal of a licence shall be as follows :-

(a)a licence to import fertilizer-Rs.100,000.00

(b)a licence to manufacture inorganic fertilizer-Rs.100,000.00

(c)a licence to manufacture organic or eco-friendly fertilizer-Rs.15,000.00

(d)a licence to formulate fertilizer-Rs.100,000.00
