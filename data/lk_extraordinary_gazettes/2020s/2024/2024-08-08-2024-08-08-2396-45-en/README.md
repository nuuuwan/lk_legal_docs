Government Notifications

Forest Conservation Ordinance

The order under sub section 4(2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of powers vested in me under sub section 4(2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation do by this Order release the block of forest land of the extent 38.0 hectares and bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedules 1, 2 & 3 given below, of the Kankamkulam Reserved Forest which is of the extent of 957.17 hectares and was declared as a Reserved Forest by Gazette Extraordinary No. 2257/59 dated 10th December 2021, from the Reserved status from. 07th day of August, 2024.

Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation.

Ministry of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation, No. 07, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07,07th day of August. 2024.
