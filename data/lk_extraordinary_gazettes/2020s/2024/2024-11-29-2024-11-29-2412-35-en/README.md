THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1) ) read with Section 22

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) (b) read with Section 22 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Science and Technology, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order under Section 22 of the aforesaid Act, published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 928/16, of June 20, 1996 (as amended from time to time) which specifies the Faculties and Departments of the Sri Palee Campus of the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka, by the repeal of Column 1 and Column 2 of the said Schedule and the substitution therefor of the following: -

“SCHEDULE

Column 1 Column 2

Faculty of Mass Media Department of Mass Media Department of Languages Department of Computer Studies Department of Performing Arts”.

Harini Amarasuriya, Minister of Education, Science and Technology.

Colombo.13th November 2024.
