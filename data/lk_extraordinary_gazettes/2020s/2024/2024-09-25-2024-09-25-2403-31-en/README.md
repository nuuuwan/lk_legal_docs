Appointment of Authorized officers under the medical Ordinance

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 69/A/2 of the Medical Ordinance as amended by Act, No. 30 of 1987 and Act, No. 25 of 1988, I, Dr. Ramesh Pathirana Minister of Health do make order and declare by this notification, by appointing the following officers as Authorized Officers for the purpose of the said Act in respect of the area assigned to them in the schedule mentioned below and in any other area in respect of which they may be appointed to act on the exigencies of service.

they are appointed in this capacity by virtue of their ex officio status who are existing in the service morefuly set out in the schedule below, who are holding such posts as well as officers who will be appointed to such posts from time to time and officers serving in an acting capacity for another officer.

All these appointment shall be valid until the officer exists in the service
