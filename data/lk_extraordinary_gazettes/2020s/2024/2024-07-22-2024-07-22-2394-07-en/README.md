THE COLOMBO PORT CITY ECONOMIC COMMISSION ACT, No. 11 OF 2021

Order under Section 53

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 53 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion, having considered the recommendations of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission and upon the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, do by this Order specify that-

(a) the Cabinet of Ministers, by its decision No. 24/1314/631/033 dated, July 09, 2024 has approved the designation

of the business of the authorised person specified in Schedule I hereto as a Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Mall Operations;

(b) the rationale for considering such business as a Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Mall Operations is

as specified in Schedule II hereto;

(c) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments shall be as specified in Schedule III hereto; and

(d) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments specified shall commence and cease to be operative

on the dates specified in Schedule IV hereto.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion.

Colombo, July 19, 2024.

Schedule II

Rationale for the consideration of the Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Mall Operations

The objectives of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, as stated in Section 5 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021 has been considered in designating the businesses as Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Mall Operations in order to ensure the success of establishing the Colombo Port City, having regard to the national interest or in the advancement of the national economy as stated in Section 53(5) of the said Act.

The Duty Free Mall Operator, PORT CITY DUTY FREE (PVT) LTD, (PCDF) a wholly owned subsidiary of CHEC Port City Colombo (Private) Limited whose parent company China Communications and Construction Company (CCCC) is ranked 63rd in the Global Fortune 500. PCDF, has built a state-of-the-art Duty Free Mall, comprising 7000 square meters of retail space with a Foreign Direct Investment of USD 7 million. PCDF Mall will consist of several Duty Free retail operators and other retailers so as to ensure the Duty Free Mall remains an attractive destination for the targeted tourists from India and China, transforming Colombo as a touristic shopping destination.

The Duty Free Mall consists of a customer verification system which seamlessly validates through the Department of Immigration and Emigration the permitted customer entry into the Duty Free area. The state-of-the-art customer verification system provides real time sales and Inventory data to Sri Lanka Customs and the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, to ensure compliance and adherence to the sale of Duty Free goods.

The Duty Free Mall will be expanded to 25,000 square meters, when re-located to its permanent location at the Colombo International Financial Center at the Colombo Port City, where further investment in the retail podium and enhanced employment is expected.
