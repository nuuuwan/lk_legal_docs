Service Minute of the Management Service officers Service - Amendment No. 04

the Public Service Commission has ordered to amend the Service Minute of the Management Service Officers Service Published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1840/34 dated 11 December 2013 as amended by the below mentioned Gazettes Extraordinary of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows.

Amendment No. 01 - Gazette Extraordinary No. 2032/26 dated 16 August 2017Amendment No. 02 - Gazette Extraordinary No. 2052/26 dated 04 January 2018Amendment No. 03 - Gazette Extraordinary No. 2140/4 dated 09 September 2019

1. Section 7.2.2.1 (a) of the Service Minute should be amended as follows. 7.2.2.1 Educational Qualifications

(a) Should have passed six (06) subject with credit passes for four subjects including Language (Sinhala/Tamil/

English) and Mathematics at the G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) Examination at one sitting.

(The medium of language in which the applicant sits the competitive examination for the recruitment to the Management Service Officers Service shall be considered as the Language for which a credit pass should have been obtained at the G.C.E (Ordinary Level) Examination.)

2. Following Note should be inserted below the Section 7.2.2.1 (b) of the Service Minute

Note: The final decision on making applicable a qualification obtained at an examination equivalent to G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) or G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Examinations conducted by other local or foreign educational institutions for granting the appointment shall be made by the Director General of Combined Services upon the verification from the relevant educational institutions and Commissioner General of Examinations.

S. Alokabandara, Director General of Combined Services.
