Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act No. 48 of 2007, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby order to charge the Special Commodity Levy in respect of the commodities specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto at the rates specified in the corresponding entry in the Column II of that Shedule for a period from November 02, 2024 to December 31, 2024 as the validity period of one year of the existing Order, published in the Gazette Notification No. 2356/20 dated November 01, 2023 is expired on November 01, 2024.
