Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987

Order

i, Harini Amarasuriya, Prime Minister and Minister of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour; in view of the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha considered as a Pradeshiya Sabha, dissolved by Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2373/51 dated 01st of March 2024 in terms of Sub-section (4) of Section 66B of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262);

(1) Declare that the area indicated in Part I of Schedule mentioned in the Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 453/11 dated 12th of May 1987 shall be a Pradeshiya Sabha area for the purpose of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987.

(2) Order the limits indicated in Part II of the said Schedule, as the administrative limits of the said Pradeshiya Sabha.

(3) Assign the following name to the said Pradeshiya Sabha after constituting a Pradeshiya Sabha for the area of Pradeshiya Sabha stated in paragraph (1) of this Order.

“Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha”

in terms of powers vested in me by Sub-section 2(1) and Sections 4 and 6 of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987.

(4) I order that the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha shall consist of a number of members elected and returned as per paragraphs i, ii, iii indicated below.

i. Seventeen members elected as per paragraph (a) of Sub-section (1) of Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987.

ii. Eleven members returned as per paragraph (b) of Sub-section (1) of Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987 and,

iii. A number of members determined by the Commissioner of Elections under paragraph (a) of Sub-section (4) of Section 65B of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as per paragraph (c) of Sub-section (1) of Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No. 15 of 1987.

(5) The term of office of the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha shall commence from 05th of November 2024.
