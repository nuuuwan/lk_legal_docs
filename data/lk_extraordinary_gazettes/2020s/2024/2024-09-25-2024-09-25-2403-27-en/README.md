Rules of the Central bank of Sri lanka new pension scheme

1. The following Rule made by the Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka in terms of the provisions of Section 10(c) read with Section 10(b) of the Monetary Law Act, No. 58 of 1949, in respect of the pension benefits of a person appointed as the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka on or after 01.01.1998, is rescinded with effect from 11.09.2024.
