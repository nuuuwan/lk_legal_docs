FILLING OF A VACANCY UNDER PARAGRAPH 13(b) OF ARTICLE 99 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

WHEREAS, the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Parliament by reason of the resignation of Mr. Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Member of Parliament of the Electoral District No. 01- Colombo who assumed duties as the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, with effect from 23rd of September, 2024 ;

And Whereas, the Returning Officer, for the electoral district which returned the said member was directed by the Election Commission under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, to fill this vacancy as provided for under Sub Article 99(13)(b) of the Constitution;

AND Whereas, the said Returning Officer acting under Section 64(2) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, and in terms of Sub Article 99(13)(b) of the Constitution has made a return in Form “M” set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. Lakshman Nipuna Arachchi was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said electoral district;
