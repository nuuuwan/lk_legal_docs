Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the National School of Business Management Limited (NSBM), a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PB 4833 and located at Mahenwatte, Pitipana, Homagama and, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2356/32 of November 03, 2023, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Management Honours in Law and Business Studies” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

Schedule I

Conditions

1. National School of Business Management Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Master of Education in Educational Management & Leadership with a minimum of 60 credits (SLQF Level 10), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of ;

(a) A Bachelor’s degree including 30 credits in the relevant subject area, or
