Government Notification

Notification

P.S. No. PS/CSA/00/1/1.

It is hereby notified that Honourable Dinesh Chandra Rupasingha Gunawardena, M.P., Prime Minister, has resigned the Office of Prime Minister of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Article 47(2)(b) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, with effect from September 23, 2024.

By order of the Honourable President,

Dr. N.S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.
