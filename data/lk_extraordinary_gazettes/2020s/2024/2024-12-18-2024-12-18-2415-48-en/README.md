The Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 of the National State Assembly

Cancellation of the Vesting Order under Section 15(1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in the then Minister of Megapolis and Western Development under Section 15(1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, the land described in the schedule hereto was vested with the Urban Development Authority, by the Order under Section 15(1) published in Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2008/25 dated 02.03.2017.

I, Anura Karunathilaka, Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing do hereby cancel the aforesaid Vesting Order under Section 15(1) published in Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2008/25 dated 02.03.2017 since the subject property is not required for the development activities of the Urban Development Authority.

Anura Karunathilaka (M.P),Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development, Construction and Housing,12th Floor,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,09th December, 2024.
