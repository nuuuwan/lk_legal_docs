the universities act, no. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, 1, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of Sri Lanka International Buddhist Academy (SIBA) a company duly incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act. No.7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number GA 2232, and located at Pallekele, Kundasale being recognized as a degree awarding institute, do by this Order, amend the Order made under the aforesaid Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1979/21 of August 09, 2016, by the repeal of the contents under the heading Schedule and the substitution there for of the following:-

Schedule I

Conditions

1. Sri Lanka International Buddhist Academy (SIBA) shall, when admitting students for the degree program specified

in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Technology Information Technology and Communication with a minimum of 90 credits (SLQF 5) adhere to the minimum entry qualification of at least three (3) simple passes (S) in any stream in one and the same sitting at the GCE (Advanced Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or an equivalent qualification.

subject to the five (5) year periodic review conducted by the Specified Authority.
