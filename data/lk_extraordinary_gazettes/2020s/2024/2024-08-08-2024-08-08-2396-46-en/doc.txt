the Forest Conservation ordinance

The order under Sub section 4 (2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451), No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtute of powers vested in me under sub Section 4 (2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451), No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation do by ths Order release the block of forest land of the extent 98.0 hectares and bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedules 1, 2 & 3 given below, of the Elamaradankulam Reserved Forest which is of the extent of 429.17 hectares and was declared as a Reserved Forest by Gazette Extraorinary No. 2257/61 dated 10th December 2021, from the Reserved status from 07th day of August, 2024.

Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation.

Ministy of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation,No. 07, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07,07th day of August 2024.
