(Chapter 276)

Rules made by the Incorporated Council of Legal Education with the concurrence of the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms under section 7 of the Incorporated Council of Legal Education Ordinance (Chapter 276).

Incorporated Council of Legal Education (Signed for and on behalf of the Incorporated Council of Legal Education)Colombo,10th June, 2024.

Rules

Rules made under section 7 of the Council of Legal Education Ordinance (Chapter 276) as amended from time to time, are hereby further amended in rule 23 thereof as follows:-

(1) in sub paragraph (iii) of paragraph (a) of sub rule (3) thereof, by the substitution for the words “Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka.” of the word “Council.”;

(3) by the repeal of proviso to sub rule (8) thereof;

(4) by the repeal of sub rule (10) thereof, and the substitution therefor of the following:-

“ (10) (a) A candidate for the General Entrance Examination referred to in sub rule (2) shall be required to sit for the following written question papers in-

(i) General Knowledge;

(ii) General Intelligence;

(iii) English Language Competency; and

(iv) Sinhala or Tamil Language Competency.

(b) A candidate may opt to answer the General Knowledge and General Intelligence question papers referred to in sub paragraphs (i) and (ii) of paragraph (a) of this sub rule in the Sinhala, Tamil or English medium.

(c) A candidate who has scored a mark which is less than forty per centum for any one or more of the question papers referred to in paragraph (a) shall not be selected for admission.

(d) The marks obtained in the General Knowledge and General Intelligence question papers shall be taken into consideration when selecting candidates eligible for admission, in the order of merit.

(e) The time allocated to answer each question paper at the said Examination shall be as follows:-

(i) One hour for the General Knowledge question paper;

(ii) One hour for the General Intelligence question paper;

(iii) One and a half hours for the English Language Competency question paper; and

(iv) One and a half hours for Sinhala or Tamil Language Competency question paper.”.
