THE COLOMBO PORT CITY ECONOMIC COMMISSION ACT, No. 11 OF 2021

Order under Section 53

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 53 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion, having considered the recommendations of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission and upon the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, do by this Order specify that-

(a) the Cabinet of Ministers, by its decision No. 24/1315/631/034 dated, July 09, 2024 has approved the designation

of the business of the authorised person specified in Schedule I hereto as a Business Strategic Importance - Duty Free Retail Operations;

(b) the rationale for considering such business as a Business of Strategic Importance Duty Free Retail Operations is

as specified in Schedule II hereto;

(c) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments shall be as specified in Schedule III hereto; and

(d) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments specified shall commence and cease to be operative

on the dates specified in Schedule IV hereto.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion.

Colombo, July 19, 2024.

Schedule II

Rationale for the consideration of the Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Retail Operations

The objectives of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, as stated in Section 5 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021 have been considered in designating the business as Business of Strategic Importance - Duty Free Mall Operations in order to ensure the success of establishing the Colombo Port City, having regard to the national interest or in the advancement of the national economy as stated in Section 53(5) of the said Act.

ODF PTE LTD is a company incorporated in Singapore which specialises in Duty Free retail and affiliated to FY Group. ODF PTE LTD is scheduled to manage the Duty-Free mall at the Singapore Flyer, a prominent tourist destination, covering an expansive 8700 square meters of retail space.

ODF PTE LTD with a foreign Direct Investment of USD 5.3 million and the recruitment of over 50 employees is to start operations in Port City downtown. With access to multiple suppliers and branded agencies, ODF PTE LTD will infuse a range of premium luxary brands, and adopt the use of pioneering digital retail technology, such as augmented reality promotions for its duty-free retail operations in the Colombo Port City, which will be the first of its kind in Sri Lanka. Augmented reality in the retail industry are interactive experiences that combine the real world and computer-generated content. the content can span multiple sensory modalities such as visuals, auditory, olfactory.
