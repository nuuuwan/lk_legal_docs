L.D.B. 104/44 (vi) A

Trust ordinance (Chapter 87)

Order under Section 114

WHEREAS the Trustees of the “Sarana Development Trust” namely-

1. Sabapathy Sivagurunathan; 2. Mahinda Hattaka; 3. Nimal Gardiya Punchihewa; 4. Tudugala Mudalige Gregory Wimal Fernando; 5. Thapassara Muhandiramlage Premawardane and 6. Hetti Wattage Sumana Ariyadasa.

have applied to the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms for their incorporation under Section 114 of the Trusts Ordinance (Chapter 87);

Now Therefore, by virtue of the powers vested in by Section 114 of the aforesaid Ordinance, I, M. U. M. Ali Sabri, Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms do by this Order authorize the incorporation of the said Trustees and direct and declare that –

(a) the Trustees of the said Trust named above and their successors for the time being duly appointed under the Deed of Trust bearing No. 682 of August 16, 2011 and attested by Bopitiya Gamage Neil Gunasekera Notary Public, Colombo which Deed was amended by Deed of Trust bearing No. 1108 of September 3, 2020, attested by Sujatha Kumari Kulatunga, Notary Public of Colombo, shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal under the name and style of the “Sarana Development Trust”;

(b) the said Trustees may, under the said name and style, purchase, receive, hold, take on lease or otherwise acquire, sell, exchange, mortgage, pledge, lease or otherwise dispose of any property movable or immovable, on behalf of the said Trust; and

(c) the said Trustees shall, by the said name and style, be able and competent in law to sue and to be sued, to answer and to be answered, to defend and be defended, in any court in Sri Lanka or elsewhere in all cases and actions arising in relation to the said Trust.

This Order may be revoked, or from time to time be varied or amended in such manner as appear to me to be necessary.

M. U. M. Ali Sabry, Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms.
