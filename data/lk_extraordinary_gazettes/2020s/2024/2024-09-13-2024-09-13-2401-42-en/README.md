Notice Under Section 38(1)

Election of Chairman, Vice Chairman and Members of the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha

NOTICE is hereby given under Section 38(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that the above Election is contested and that a poll will be taken between 7.00 a.m. to 4.00 p.m. on 26th October 2024 and that :-

(a) the names of the candidates in the first nomination paper as indicated by the candidates of each Recognized

Political Party and Independent Group for Election as Chairman, Vice Chairman and Members of the above mentioned Local Authority, the numbers and names of the respective wards and the names of candidates in the second nomination paper to be returned ; and the approved Election Symbol allotted to each party and Independent Group,
