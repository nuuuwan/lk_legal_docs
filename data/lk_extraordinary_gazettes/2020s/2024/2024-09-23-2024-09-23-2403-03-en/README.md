P.S. No. PS/CSA/00/1/1/1 Proclamation

Anura Kumara Dissanayake

WHEREAS, pursuant to an Election held for the election of a President in terms of Paragraph (3) of Article 31 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, Honourable Anura Kumara Dissanayake was declared elected as the President in terms of Section 61 of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981 by the Election Commission, on the Twenty Second Day of September, Two Thousand and Twenty Four.

And whereas, in terms of Paragraph (4) of Article 31 of the said Constitution, the term of Office of the President commenced on the Twenty Second Day of September, Two Thousand and Twenty Four;

Now therefore, this proclamation gives notice of the fact that, in terms of Article 32 and Paragraph (7) of Article 157A of the said Constitution, Honourable Anura Kumara Dissanayake has, by making and subscribing the affirmation set out in the Fourth Schedule and the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution, assumed the Office of President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka for a term of five years, on the Twenty Third Day of September, Two Thousand and Twenty Four.

Given at Colombo on this Twenty Third Day of September, Two Thousand and Twenty Four.

By Order of the Honourable President, Dr. N. S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,September 23rd, 2024.
