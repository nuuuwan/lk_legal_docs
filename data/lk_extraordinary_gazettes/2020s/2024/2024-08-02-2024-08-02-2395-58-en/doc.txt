Interpretation Ordinance

Notice of cancellation of the Gazette Notification No. 2351/02 dated 25.09.2023 cancelling the name of "Sri Lanka Swadeshiya Vaidya Sabawa" under Section 18 of the Interpretation Ordinance.

I, Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, the Minister of industries, hereby notify that Gazette Notification bearing No. 2351/02 dated 25.09.2023 striking off the society named " Sri Lanka Swadeshiya Vaidya Sabawa" which is registered under Section 3 and Section 4 of the Societies Ordinance (123 chapter) and published under Gazette dated 2317/70-2023 and cancelling the name thereof, is hereby cancelled.

Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, Minister of industries.

Ministry of Industries,No. 73/1,Galle Road,Colombo 03,31st day of July 2024.
