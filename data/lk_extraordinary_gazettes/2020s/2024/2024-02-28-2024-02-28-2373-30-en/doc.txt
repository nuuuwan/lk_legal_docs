WHEREAS, the Secretary General of Parliament has informed the Election Commission, under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, that a vacancy has occurred in the membership of the Ninth Parliament by reason of the resignation of Mr. Uddika Premarathna, who was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the Electoral District No. 17 - Anuradhapura under Section 62 of the aforesaid Act;

And whereas, the Returning Ofﬁcer, for the Electoral District which returned the said member was directed by the Election Commission under Section 64(1) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 to ﬁll this vacancy as provided for under Sub Article 99(13)(B) of the Constitution;

And whereas, the said Returning Ofﬁcer acting under Section 64(2) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981 and in terms of Sub Article 99(13)(B) of the Constitution has made a return in form "M" set out in the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act, to the effect that Mr. S. C. Muthukumarana was declared elected as a Member of Parliament for the said Electoral District;
