approval of the development plan for the pradeshiya Sabha limit of baddegama

NOTICE is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8 (G) of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 4 of 1982 that I, Prasanna Ranathunga, the Minister in charge of the subject of Urban Development & Housing, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8 (F) of the said Act, No. 4 of 1982 have approved the Development Plan for the Pradeshiya Sabha limit of Baddegama prepared under Section 8 (A) of the said Act on the day of 18th September, 2024.

Prasanna Ranathunga (M.P.), Minister of Urban Development and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development and Housing,12th Floor,“Sethsiripaya”, Stage II,Battaramulla,19th September, 2024.
