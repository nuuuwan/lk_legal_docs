By virtue of powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, 1, Dr. A. D. No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number GL 24, being recognized as a degree awarding institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2384/28 of May 16, 2024, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Arts Honours in English Studies” appearing in Column I and it’s corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in schedule I hereto :-
