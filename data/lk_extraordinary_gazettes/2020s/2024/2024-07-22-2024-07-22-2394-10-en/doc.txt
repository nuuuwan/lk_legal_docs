THE COLOMBO PORT CITY ECONOMIC COMMISSION ACT, No. 11 OF 2021

Order under Section 53

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 53 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion, having considered the recommendations of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission and upon the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, do by this Order specify that-

(a) the Cabinet of Ministers, by its decision No. 24/1304/631/032 dated, July 09, 2024 has approved the designation

of the business of the authorised person specified in Schedule I hereto as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance;

(b) the rationale for considering such business as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance is as specified in

Schedule II hereto;

(c) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments shall be as specified in Schedule III hereto; and

(d) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments specified shall commence and cease to be operative

on the dates specified in Schedule IV hereto.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion.

Colombo, July 19, 2024.

Schedule II

Rationale for the consideration of the Secondary Business of Strategic Importance

The objectives of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, as stipulated in Section 5 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 1 1 of 2021, have been considered in designating the business as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance, in order to ensure the success of establishing the Colombo Port City, having regard to the national interest or the advancement of the national economy as provided for in Section 53(5) of the said Act.

PAGERO LANKA SOFTWARE ENGINEERING (PVT) LTD is a company incorporated in Sri Lanka, and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Pagero AB, a company incorporated in Sweden. With an annual turnover of over USD 50 million, Pagero AB operates as one of the largest B2B networks globally, connecting buyers and sellers across international markets. Pagero AB's platform enables the swift and secure digital exchange of critical documents such as orders, invoices and payments, empowering businesses to streamline their operations with ease. Moreover, Pagero is dedicated to promoting environmental responsibility by reducing paper consumption and carbon emissions associated with traditional document exchange methods.

PAGERO LANKA SOFTWARE ENGINEERING (PVT) LTD registered as an offshore company in the Colombo Port City, is positioned to be an extended Research and Development centre for Pagero AB, poised to drive innovation in eDocument/elnvoicing solutions for global partners. PAGERO LANKA SOFTWARE ENGINEERING (PVT) LTD will recruit approximately 100 skilled software engineers within two years of commencing operations in the Colombo Port City and further employ 50 individuals in the second year. With a proposed investment of USD 100,000, further expansion plans forecasts an injection of approximately USD 2 million through employee remunerations over the subsequent years, demonstrating its commitment to fostering economic growth in Sri Lanka.
