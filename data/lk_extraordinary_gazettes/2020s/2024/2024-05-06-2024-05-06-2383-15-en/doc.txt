No. 2383/15 – MONDAY, MAY 06, 2024

FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

THE Order under Section 4(2) that has to be read with the Section 64(1) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 13 of the 1966 as amended by Acts No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 4(2) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451), I, Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Attorney-at-Law, the Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation do by this Order release the block of forest land of the extent 6.67 Hectares of the Puwarasankulam Reserved Forest bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedule given below and was declared as a Reserved Forest by Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 8750 dated 30th May 1941 from the Reserved status from 19th day of April, 2024.

Pavithra Wanniarachchi, (Attorney-at-Law) Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation.

Ministry of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation,Colombo 07.

19th day of April, 2024.
