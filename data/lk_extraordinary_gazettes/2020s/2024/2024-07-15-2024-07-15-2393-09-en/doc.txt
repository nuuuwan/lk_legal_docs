pilgrimage ordinance

Sri Siththira Velayutha Swami Temple Annual Festival - 2024

Divisional Secretariat of Thirukkovil Division in Ampara District

IT is hereby notified for the information of the pilgrims who attend the above festival and others concerned that the above festival commences on 18.07.2024 and terminates on 04.08.2024 with water cutting ceremony.

The attention of the pilgrims who attend the above festival and others concerned is drawn to standing regulation published in the Government Gazette No. 10247 dated 11th May 1951 which will be in force during above festival.

The camp of the above has been enlarged as follows :

North : By Maha Vidyalaya Road, Thambiluvil East : By Fisheries Road, Thirukkovil, South : By Beach Road, Thirukkovil, West : By Main Road, Thirukkovil.

T. Gajenthiran, Divisional Secretary.

Divisional Secretariat,Thirukkovil,
