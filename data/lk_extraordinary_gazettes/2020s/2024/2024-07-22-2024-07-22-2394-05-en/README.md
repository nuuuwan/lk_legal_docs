National water supply and drainage Board act, No. 2 of 1974

Order under Section 92

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, under Section 92 of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board Act, No. 2 of 1974, I, Jeevan Thondaman, Minister of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development, do hereby approve the acquisition of the immobile properties of the allotment of land described in the following schedule as it is required by the said Board, as an additional allotment of land has been used to construct Mawathagama Water Tank, Officer In Charge Office, Stores and Package Plant of Mawathagama Water Supply Project.

Jeevan Thondaman, Minister of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development.
