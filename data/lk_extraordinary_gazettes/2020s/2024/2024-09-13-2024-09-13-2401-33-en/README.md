Eastern Province Provincial Council

1A - PG 6872 - 29 (09/2024) This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk

Notification given by the Governor of the Eastern Province

by virtue of the powers vested in me, M. Senthil Thondaman the Governor of Eastern Province under Section 154(c) of the 13th amendment of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this notified in 1 and 2 regarding the matters related to provincial schools with effect from 12.09.2024.

1. The procedures for involving non-governmental organizations, international non-governmental organizations and sponsors in providing physical resources and conducting development activities at provincial schools are as follows :

1.1. Distributing equipment : This involves specifying the types of equipment, amounts, detailed recipient information, method of distribution, intended activities and relevant requests and recommendations.

1.2. Providing physical resources (building and construction) : This includes outlining the purpose of construction, details of beneficiaries, building plans and drawings, construction location, construction budgets and estimates, relevant requests, recommendations and a draft of the plagues (Establishment and Opening)

Approval from the Provincial Secretary of Education is required for these activities. The submission of the documents mentioned above is mandatory. The provincial director of education is responsible for organizing and managing the event or the ceremony.

2. The provincial school band is to be used only for school-related events of the specific schools. When utilizing the school band for other events, the event organizer must seek permission from the Provincial Secretary of Education through the Principal, the Zonal Director of Education, and the Provincial Director of Education. Adequate facilities and care, including drinking water, refreshments and appropriate shelter should be provided for the children by the organizers.

Using the band for events promoting political agendas, non-education-related events, and events requiring bus transportation to distant locations is strictly prohibited.

M. Senthil Thondaman, Governor of the Eastern Province.
