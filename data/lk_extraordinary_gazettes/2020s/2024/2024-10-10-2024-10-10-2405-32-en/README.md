Rules made under Article 154 c read with Article 154 f of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and Sub-section 19(5) of Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987

AS per the powers vested in me under Article 154 c read with Article 154 f of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and Sub-section 19(5) of Provincial Councils Act, No. 42 of 1987, I, Hanif Yusoof, the Governor of Western Province do hereby announce that the "Rules (Amendment) on Procurement Affairs relevant to the Western Provincial Council Fund, No. 02 of 2024" are declared herewith.

Hanif Yusoof, Governor, Western Province.

At the Governor's Office, Western Province,Located at the Western Provincial Council Building,Battaramulla,On 09th day of October, 2024.

RULES

1. These rules will be enforced to amend the Rules on Procurement Affairs Relevant to the Provincial Council Fund of the Western Province published in the Extraordinary Gazette paper No. 2186/25 dated 30th July 2020 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka amended by Rules (Amended) on Procurement Affairs relevant to the Provincial Council Fund of the Western Province No. 1 of 2024 published by the Extraordinary Gazette paper No.; 2394/70 dated 26.07.2024 of the Democratic Socialist Repubic of Sri Lanka.

2. These rules shall be cited as Rules (Amendment) on Procurement Affairs Relevant to the Provincial Council Fund of the Western Province, No. 02 of 2024.

3. The words "Fifty Million" stated under first subsection in Section 7 of Rules on Procurement Affairs Relevant to the Provincial Council Fund of the Western Province published by the Extraordinary Gazette paper No.; 2186/25 dated 30th July 2020 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka amended by Rules (Amendment) on Procurement Affairs relevant to the Provincial Council Fund of the Western Province No. 1 of 2024 published by the Extraordinary Gazette paper No.; 2394/70 dated 26.07.2024 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka will be repealed hereby and amended by substituting the words "Fifteen Million".

4. Should any inconsistency arise among the Sinhala, English and Tamil texts of these rules, Sinhala text shall prevail.
