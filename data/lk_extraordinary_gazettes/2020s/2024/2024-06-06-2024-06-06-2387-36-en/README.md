Regulations

01. These Regulations may be cited as the Regulations to provide Housing Aids for Low Income recipients of Western Province No. 02 of 2024.

02. The Housing Aids provided to low income recipients by the Housing Development Fund of Western Province shall be in accordance with the values mentioned under following categories.

CategoryValue

01.5 'a' (1)Building a new house The Maximum amount of aid paid to a receipient(2 installments)

(i) When ready to commence the work- Rs. 150,000.00

(ii) To complete the remaining work of the partly built house - Rs. 150,000.00

Rs. 300,000.00

02.5 'a' (2)Improving the existing house and to complete the partly built house (2 installments)

( i) When ready to commence the work- Rs. 100,000.00

(ii) When completed half of the due work - Rs. 100,000.00

Rs. 200,000.00

03. Providing of the Housing Aids under these Regulations shall be done while maintaining a minimum balance of Rs. Sixty (60) Million in the Housing Development Fund all the time.

04. The Regulations related to providing Housing Aids for Low Income Recipients of Western Province published in the Extraordinary Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, No. 2318/51 dated 10th February, 2023 shall be repealed from the date on which these Regulations come into effect.

05. Under these Regulations, "a low income recipient" means a person who earns a monthly income not exceeding a maximum of Rs. Fifteen Thousand.

06. In the event of any inconsistency between Sinhala, Tamil and English texts of these Regulations the Sinhala text shall prevail.
