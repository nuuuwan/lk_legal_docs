The Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40)

Order

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 12 of the Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40), I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, President, do by this order call out with effect from July 22, 2024 all the members of the Armed Forces specified in the First Schedule hereto, for the maintenance of Public Order in the areas specified in the Second Schedule hereto.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, President.

July 22nd, 2024.
