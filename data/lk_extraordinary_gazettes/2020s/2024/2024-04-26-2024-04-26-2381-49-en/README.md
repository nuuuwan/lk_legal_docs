REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS ACT, No. 44 OF 1980

iN the exercise of the powers vested by Section 8 of the Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980, the Election Commission does hereby cancel the Gazette No. 1967/3 dated 16.05.2016 and the Gazette No. 2035/20 dated 06.09.2017 issued by the Election Commission and appoint for the Electoral Districts specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto, the Registering Officers and Assistant Registering Officers mentioned by their designations in the corresponding entry in Column II and III respectively.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.
