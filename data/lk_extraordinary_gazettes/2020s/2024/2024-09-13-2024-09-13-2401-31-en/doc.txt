In exercise of the powers conferred to me, M. Senthil Thondaman as the Governor of the Eastern Province, I hereby notified that under section 154C 13 of the Thirteenth Amendment of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, with effect from 11.09.2024, the 1128 meters long coastal walkway of the Inner Harbour Road under the Jurisdiction of Trincomalee Municipal Council as shown in Schedule 1 below will be changed as shown in Schedule 2.

Schedule 1Schedule 2

This particular walkway was being used without any name being mentioned.

This paricular walkway will be now known as the "Ocean Breeze walkway" in the future.

This walkway starts at 60 meters from the Port Police Station (Location Point 8.5679427, 81.2311127) and ends at a distance of 51 meters from The Sinhala Maha Vidyalaya (Location Point 8.5757138, 81.2252488).

This walkway is located 60 meters away from the Port Police Station (Location point 8.5679427, 81.2311127) starts from stupa, naming inscription and historical inscription and ends at stupi and naming inscription constructed 51 meters away from the Sinhala Maha Vidyalaya (Location point 8.5757138. 81.2252488).

The 1128-meter walkway was already found to be 3 to 4 feet wide.

The area of the walkway will be changed to 110 meters in front of Sinhala MahaVidyalaya to be 4 feet wide and the remaining 1018 meters to 6 feet wide.
