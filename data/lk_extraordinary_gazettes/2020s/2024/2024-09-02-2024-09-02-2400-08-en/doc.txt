NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No. 11/0538 Anuradhapura District IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the owner ship or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 110511 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Nallapambukulama ( Within Anuradhapura Municipal Council Area ) (Block 11) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.572 - Henawatta in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Mihinthale in the District of Anuradhapura in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 1st of October 2024 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an exparte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 110511 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s Office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Title Settlement. P. C. D. SIGERA, Commissioner General of Land Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No.1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla,, 28th of August 2024. EOG 09-0023/1
