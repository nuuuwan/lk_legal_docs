NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL ACT, No. 47 OF 1980

Order under Section 24C and Section 24D

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 24C and Section 24D of the National Environmental Act, No. 47 of 1980 read with Article 44(3) of the Constitution I, Ranil Wickramasinghe, President, do by this Order, declare-

(a) That the area of land in which the metes and bounds are set out in the Schedule I hereto shall be an Environmental Protection Area for the purposes of the Act and shall be called the “ Warnagalawatta Environmental Protection Area” (hereinafter referred to as the “Protection Area”);

(b) That for so long as this Order is in force -

(i) Any planning scheme or project within the Protection Area which is in conﬂ ict with the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall cease to operate in the Protection Area from the date of operation of this Order;

(ii) No person other than the Authority shall exercise, perform, and discharge any powers, duties or functions relating to the planning and development within the Protection Area:

Provided that, where the Authority delegates any of its powers, duties or functions relating to the planning and development within the Protection Area to any Government Department, corporation, statutory board, local

SCHEDULE II

Part I

Activities permitted within the Protection Area

The physical planning and development within the Protection Area shall be only for the following activitics:-

1. Maintenance of existing infrastructure and buildings (two-religious places, two - Ambalamas, Erathna - Sripada Road and Pilgrims Support Facilitating Centres). 2. Operation and maintenance of existing mini hydro power project. 3. Management of invasive alien species. 4. Educational and Research activities. 5. Excavation, conservation and maintenance of Archeological sites. 6. Construction and maintenance of vehicle park proposed by the Ministry of Provincial Roads Development, Rural Infrastructure, Tourism, Sports and Youth Aﬀ airs in the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council. 7. Maintenance activities of existing road from Erathna to mini hydro power project at Warnagala. 8. Activities related to the sustainability of the Protection Area.

Part II

Conditions to be applied in carrying out the activities set out in Part I of this Schedule within the Protection Area

1. Any physical planning and development set out in Part I of this Schedule shall be carried out subject to the following conditions:-

(a) such physical planning and development shall be carried out subject to the provisions of the Act;

(b) results of such educational and research activities shall be submitted to the Authority along with a copy of the study or research report;

(c) such physical planning and development shall be carried out in such manner as to-

(i) not hinder the sustainability of the Protection Area; and

(ii) not disturb fauna and ﬂ ora habitats;

(d) invasive alien species shall not be introduced to the Protection Area;

(e) any kind of waste or wastewater shall not be disposed to the Protection Area;

(f) reclamation of land, activities relevant to land degradation and soil erosion shall not be undertaken in the Protection Area; and

(g) suﬃ cient environmental ﬂ ow shall be maintained in downstream of the Kuru Ganga from weir of the existing mini hydro power project. 2. Where any Government Department, corporation, statutory board, local authority or any public oﬃ cer carries out any physical planning and development within the Protection Area under the provisions of section 26, the Authority shall prepare and adopt an administrative levy scheme in order to process and evaluate project proposals relating to such physical planning and development and shall monitor such activities.
