AUTHORIZATION

I, do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Oﬃ cers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse as per the Sixth Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2024, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.
