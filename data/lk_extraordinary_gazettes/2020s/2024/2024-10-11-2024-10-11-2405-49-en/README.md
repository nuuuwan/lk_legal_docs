Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981

Notice under Section 24 (1) (a), (c) and 33

Notice is hereby given under Sections 24 (I) (a) and (c) and Section 33 of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 01 of 1981 that -

I. a contest will be held for the election of Members of Parliament for each electoral district mentioned in the Schedule hereto;and

II. polling in each of these electoral districts will be held on Thursday, 14th November, 2024, from 7.00 am to 4.00 p.m, continuing until all voters waiting in the queue/ queues at the polling station have received their ballot papers.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Ameer Faaiz, Chairman, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.
