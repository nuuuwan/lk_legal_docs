the colombo port city economic commission act, no. 11 of 2021

Order under Section 53

BY VIRTUE of the powers vested in me by section 53 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion, having considered the recommendations of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission and upon the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, do by this Order specify that-

(a) the Cabinet of Ministers, by its decision No. 23/2530/631/033 dated 01.01.2024 has approved the designation of the business of the authorised person specified in Schedule I hereto as a Business of Strategic Importance;

(b) the rationale for considering such business as a Business of Strategic Importance is as specified in Schedule II hereto;

(c) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments shall be as specified in Schedule III hereto; and

(d) the exemptions or incentives granted under the enactments specified shall commence and cease to be operative on the dates specified in Schedule IV hereto. Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Investment Promotion.

Colombo.January 08, 2024.

SCHEDULE II

Rationale for the consideration of the Business of Strategic Importance

The objectives of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, as stipulated in section 5 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, have been considered in designating the business as a Business of Strategic Importance, in order to ensure the success of establishing the Colombo Port City, having regard to the national interest or the advancement of the national economy as provided for in section 53(5) of the said Act.

Asiri Hospital Holdings PLC, a leading healthcare service provider, presently has 6 hospitals in Sri Lanka with a capacity of over 780 beds. Asiri Hospital Holdings PLC will partner with Mayo Foundation for Medical Education and Research, USA for the development of the Colombo Port City Hospital project with a foreign direct investment of over USD 100 million.

Construction of the Asiri Port City Hospital is scheduled to be completed within three years, with operations expected to commence from year four. The generation of employment at Asiri Port City Hospital for approximately 700 personnel will result in income earning opportunities in designated foreign currencies.

Asiri Port City Hospital will create a state-of-the art medical facility, consisting of 500 beds and new treatments and surgical procedures such as organ transplant surgery, cosmetic surgery, sports medicine and an advanced cancer treatment center. Asiri Port City Hospital intends to transform Colombo to a medical tourism hub in South-East Asia, and create the platform for local doctors to be trained and work with foreign medical specialists adopting global best medical practices.
