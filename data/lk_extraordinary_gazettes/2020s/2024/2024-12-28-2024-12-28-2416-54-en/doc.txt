Regulations

The Food (Trans-Fat) Regulations 2022, published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2319/41 of February 14, 2023 as amended by regulations published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2364/25 of December 27, 2023 is hereby amended by the repeal of regulation 1 thereof, and the substitution thereof, of the following regulation :

“1. These regulations may be cited as Food (Trans-Fat) Regulations, 2022 and shall come into operation with effects from

July 01, 2025.”
