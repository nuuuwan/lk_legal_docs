Ministry of Power and Energy

Petroleum Development Authority of Sri Lanka

Petroleum Resources (Exploration and Development) Block Map of Sri Lanka

By virtue of the powers vested in Petroleum Development Authority of Sri Lanka by section 4(a), Section 16 of the Petroleum Resources Act, No. 21 of 2021 and Sub Regulation 2 (1) (e) of Petroleum Resources (Exploration and Development Block Map) Regulations No. 1 of 2024, Director General of Petroleum Development Authority of Sri Lanka do hereby update and publish the Petroleum Resources (Exploration and Development) Block Map which outlines the blocks on offer for Exploration Licenses, Development Licenses and Joint studies for the information of the General Public.

Director General (Actg). Petroleum Development Authority of Sri Lanka,

At Colombo,12th of March, 2024.
