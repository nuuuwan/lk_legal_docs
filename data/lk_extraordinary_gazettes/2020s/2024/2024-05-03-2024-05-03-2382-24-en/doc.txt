THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, by Section 27(1)(b) read with section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978,

I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (ICASL), established under the Institute of Chartered Accountants Act, No. 23 of 1959 and located at No. 30A, Malalasekara Mawatha, Colombo 7, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order –

(1) Amend the Order made under the said Act and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1801/5 of March 12, 2013, as last amended by the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2302/17 of October 19, 2022, as follows :–

(a) By the substitution for all the words from “do by this Order, grant the Degree awarding status” to “in Column II of the Schedule.’’, of the words ‘‘do by this Order and subject to the conditions speciﬁ ed in Schedule I hereto, recognise the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (ICASL) as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing higher education therein, leading to the award of the degrees speciﬁ ed in Column I of the Schedule II hereto, of which the abbreviations are set out in the corresponding entries respectively in Column II of the Schedule II.’’; and

(b) By the repeal of Schedule to the Order and the substitution therefor of the following Schedules:–
