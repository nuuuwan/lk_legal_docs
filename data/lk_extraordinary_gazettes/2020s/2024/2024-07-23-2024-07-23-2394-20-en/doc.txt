the urban development authority law No. 41 of 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41, 1978, I, Prasanna Ranathunga, the Minister of Urban Development and Housing being of the opinion that area specified in Column I of the Schedule hereto and having the metes and bounds specified in the corresponding entry in column II of that Schedule is suitable for development, do by this Order, declare such area to be an Urban Development Area for the purpose of that Section.

Prasanna Ranathunga (M.P), Minister of Urban Development and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development and Housing,12th Floor,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,11th July, 2024.

Column I

Total land area Consisting of the landmarks and boundaries described in Column II situated within the Dimbulagala Pradeshiya Sabha area, belonging to Grama Niladhari Divisions of No. 246 Aralaganwila West, No. 245 Aralaganwila East and No. 238 Pimburaththewa West, situated within the Dimbulagala Divisional Secretariat Division of Polonnaruwa District in the North Central Province.

Schedule

Aralaganwila Urban Development Area

Column II

North:

From the starting point where the Northern boundary of No. 246 Aralaganwila West Grama Niladhari Division and the Southern boundary of No. 247 Ratmalthanna Grama Niladhari Division intersects with the Left Bank Main Cenal (ZD canal), along the Northern boundary of No. 246 Aralaganwila West Grama Niladhari Division by traveling in East and North direction where B 502 Manampitiya - Aralaganwila - Maduruoya road and the Northern boundary of the No. 245 Aralaganwila East Grama Niladhari Division intersect and, from that point onwards Northern boundary of No. 245 Aralaganwila East Grama Niladhari Division in the Eastern direction to Kuda Oya which is the eastern boundary of No. 245 Aralaganwila East Grama Niladhari Division.

East:

From the last mentioned boundary along the Eastern boundary of No. 245 Aralaganwila East Grama Niladhari Division and the Kuda Oya boundary to the Eastern boundary of No. 238 Pimburaththewa Wast Grama Niladhari Division and from there along the eastern boundary of No. 238 Pimburaththewa West Grama Niladhari Division to the centre point located at Aralaganwila Wewa Spill.

South:

From the last mentioned point, along Aralaganwila wewa upper side and Southern boundary of No. 238 Pimburatthewa West Grama Niladhari Division crossing the B502 Manampitiya - Aralaganwila - Maduruoya road to the Pimburatthewa canal and from then on, along the Southern boundary of that Grama Niladhari Division and the Pimburanththwa Canal in North - East and West direction to the point where it intersects with the southern boundary of No. 246 Aralaganwila West Grama Niladhari Division, and thence along the Southern boundary of No. 246 Aralaganwila West Grama Niladhari Division to the Left Bank Main Canal (ZD Canal);

West:

from the last mentioned point along the Western boundary of No. 246 Aralaganwila West Grama Niladhari Division and the left bank main canal (ZD canal) while traveling in a Northern direction which is met by Starting point of enclosed area.
