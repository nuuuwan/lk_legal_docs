Local Government Notiﬁ cations

PRADESHIYA SABHA - BIBILE.

UNDER Section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, and in accordance with the directions issued by the Honourable Governor of Uva Province, published through Extraordinary Gazette No. 2318/41 dated February 10, 2023, and Gazette No. 2414/13 dated December 11, 2024, regarding the preparation of plans and name list of streets and narrow roads managed by Pradeshiya Sabhas in Uva Province, it has been decide under Decision No. 358 dated September 30, 2024, that the streets and narrow roads listed in the schedule below are to be declared as streets and narrow roads belonging to the Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha. This declaration is made public for the information of the general public.

If any street or narrow road included in the said schedule passes through or is part of a private land, and if the concerned landowner claims ownership of such land, they are required to ﬁ le a case in the appropriate court asserting their ownership rights within three months from the date of this notiﬁ cation.

Furthermore, it is hereby announced that if no such legal action is initiated within the stipulated three-month period, all streets and narrow roads included in the schedule shall be deemed as belonging to the Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha.

D.M. ANUSHA S.DISSANAYAKECouncil Secretary,Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha. at Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha On 13th December 2024

Decision

Under the directions of the Honourable Governor of Uva Province, as published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2318/41 dated February 10, 2023, and the orders outlined in the regulations pertaining to the notiﬁ cation of the preparation of plans and name list of streets and narrow roads managed by the Pradeshiya Sabhas of Uva Province, issued under Section 24(1) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, it is hereby decided that the streets and narrow roads listed in the annexed schedule are to be considered as streets and narrow roads managed by the Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha.
