Imports and Exports Control Act, No. 1 of 1969

IN terms of the powers vested in me by Section 20 read together with Sub-Section 4(1) and Section 14 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 as amended by Act, No. 48 of 1985 and Act, No. 28 of 1987, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies promulgate following Regulations.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01,March 15, 2024.

Regulations

1. These Regulations may be cited as the “Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 05 of 2024”. 2. These Regulations are issued as per the decision of the Cabinet of Ministers dated February 26, 2024, on the Memorandum No. 24/0309/628/018 dated February 07, 2024, submitted by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation read together with the Observations of the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies dated February 26, 2024, on the said Memorandum.

3. Regulation Nos. 3.1 and 3.2, of this Gazette hereinafter set out, are issued as integral parts of the Regulations of the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 02 of 2023, published in the Gazette No. 2312/78 dated January 01, 2023, as amended.

3.1 Notwithstanding the Regulation No. 4 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 02 of 2023, referred above, importation of goods as described in the Schedule below shall be allowed by the Director General of Sri Lanka Customs on recommendation of the Chief Accounting Officer of the respective Ministry in charge of the subject, subject to other prevailing Rules and Regulations applicable on importation of motor vehicles, classified under the HS Chapter 87.

3.2 Notwithstanding the Regulation No. 4 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 02 of 2023 referred above, importation of motor vehicles by Government organizations as donation or as per the provisions of Project Agreements exclusively for the purpose of direct usage shall be allowed for Customs clearance by the Director General of Sri Lanka Customs on the approval of Controller General of Department of Imports and Exports Control, subject to recommendation of the Secretary to the Treasury.

4. The Special Import License Regulations No. 01 of 2023, published in the Gazette No. 2312/77 dated January 01, 2023, as amended subsequently, is hereby further amended.

4.1 Notwithstanding the Regulation No. 9 of the Special Import License Regulations No. 01 of 2023, referred above, the Controller General of Imports and Exports shall issue Import Control License (ICL) for importation of Ambulances listed out in the Schedule V of the said Regulations, which are imported on “No Foreign Exchange (NFE)” basis for the purpose of direct usage of public or private hospitals, subject to recommendation of the Secretary to the Treasury.

5. To achieve the objectives of these Regulations, the Controller General of Imports and Exports shall issue “Operational Instructions” to the Director General of Customs, licensed banks or any other relevant authorities.

6. The final decision shall be made by the Controller General of Imports and Exports on any issues arising in relation to these Regulations.
