SRI LANKA RAILWAY UNIFORM STAFF BENEVOLENT FUND ORDINANCE

Rules made by the Corporation known as the Sri Lanka Railway Uniform Staff Benevolent Fund under Section 16 of the Railway Uniform Staff Benevolent Fund Ordinance (Chapter 284) and conferred by the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies by virtue of the powers vested in him by the aforesaid section.

Secretary, Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Colombo,On 20th day of February 2024.

RULES

The rules published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 305 of July 6th 1984 as amended from time to time and last amended by Gazette Extraordinary No. 2117/17of April 3, 2019 are hereby further amended as follows :-

1. In rule 19 -

(a) by the repeal of paragraph (1) thereof and the substitution therefor, of the following paragraph : -

“ (1) Where a member loses his membership in the Corporation under paragraph 1 of Rule 8, such member shall be paid a sum of Rupees Fifteen Thousand Rupees (Rs.15000/-) (hereinafter referred to as the Honorary Gratuity) for each and every month for a period of six months from the date of retirement totally amounting to ninety thousand rupees (Rs.90,000/-)”;

(b) by the repeal of sub paragraph (4) thereof and the substitution therefor, of the following sub paragraph -

“(4) Every member who wishes to become entitled for the Honorary Gratuity shall make a contribution of Rupees Four Thousand Five Hundred (Rs.4500/-) per annum at the rate of Rupees Three Hundred and Seventy five (Rs 375/-) per month for a period of ten years. All recoveries in respect of honorary gratuity shall be deposited in a fixed deposit in a State Bank. The interest accrued from the fixed deposit shall be credited to the honorary gratuity savings account and shall be used for the payment of honorary gratuity;”

2. In rule 21 -

(a) by the repeal of paragraph (1) thereof and substitution therefor, of the following paragraph -

“ Festival Loans (l) (a) Any member who has completed a period of one year as a member and wishes to obtain festival loan shall be entitled to such loan of Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand (Rs. 25000/-) once a year. Any member who has obtained the said festival loan shall settle such amount together with an interest at the rate of twelve per centum (12%) in ten monthly installments of Rupees Two Thousand and five Hundred (Rs 2500/-).

(b) No member shall be entitled to obtain a festival loan, until any previous festival loan obtained by him if any , is fully settled by such member .
