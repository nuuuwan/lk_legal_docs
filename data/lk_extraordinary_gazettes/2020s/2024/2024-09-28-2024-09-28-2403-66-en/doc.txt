Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 01 of 1981

For the purpose of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 01 of 1981 the Election Commission does hereby -

01. revise the symbols in the schedule published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2395/07, dated July 29, 2024; and

02. approve the symbols in the Schedule A and Schedule B appended hereto.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

Ameer Faaiz, A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.
