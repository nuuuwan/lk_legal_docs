the universities act, no. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A and 70C

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1 )(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the SLT CAMPUS (PVT) LTD, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 101137 and located at Ingiriya Road, Meepe, Padukka which is recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute and since under the certificate dated March 20, 2024 issued by the Registrar General of Companies the said SLT CAMPUS (PVT) LTD has been incorporated as the Sri Lanka Tecchnology Campus (PVT) LTD, do by this Order, amend the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1995/39 of December 02, 2016, by the substitution for the words “SLT CAMPUS (PVT) LTD” wherever those words appear in the said Order, of the words “Sri Lanka Tecchnology Campus (PVT) LTD” and every reference to the “SLT CAMPUS (PVT) LTD” in any Order, notice, contract, communication or other documents made in terms of the aforesaid sections shall be read and construed as a reference to the “Sri Lanka Tecchnology Campus (PVT) LTD”.

Further, any power exercised, or any duty or function performed by the said SLT CAMPUS (PVT) LTD prior to the date of coming into effect of this Order in pursuance of any power conferred on it by the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1995/39 of December 02, 2016 shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly exercised and performed by the Sri Lanka Tecchnology Campus (PVT) LTD.

Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, Minister of Education.Colombo,14th May, 2024.
