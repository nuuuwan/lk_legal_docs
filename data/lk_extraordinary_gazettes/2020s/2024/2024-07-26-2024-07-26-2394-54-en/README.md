Presidential Election - 2024

Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981

Notice under Section 7(1)

In the exercise of the powers vested in the Election Commission by Section 7(1) of the Presidential Elections Act, No. 15 of 1981, we do hereby appoint the Assistant Returning Officers mentioned by designation in Column II of the Schedule here for Electoral District in the corresponding entry in Column I of that Schedule.
