Provincial Councils Notifications

central Province provincial council

Notification issued by the Hon. Governor

I, Sarath B. S. Abayakoon, Governor of Central Province, by virtue of powers vested in me under Article 154(c) of the 13th amendment to the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do hereby announce that the Department of Provincial Engineering Services - Central Province, which is currently under the purview of Chief Ministry & the Ministry of Education under subjects not covered by by any other ministry, has been brought under the purview of the Chief Secretariat - Central Province and accordingly, it shall be operated under Deputy Chief Secretary (Engineering Services) under the administration of the Chief Secretary - Central Province.

Subjects of the Ministry before amendmentSubjects of the Ministry after amendment

The Chief Ministry and the Ministry of Finance and Planning, Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration, Manpower, Education and Cultural Affairs, Land, Trade and Commerce, Food Supply and Distribution Affairs, Tourism, Sports and Investment Coordination, and subjects not covered by other ministries - Central Province

The Chief Ministry and the Ministry of Finance and Planning, Law and Order, Local Government and Provincial Administration, Manpower, Education and Cultural Affairs, Land, Trade and Commerce, Food Supply and Distribution Affairs, Tourism, Sports and Investment Coordination, and Subjects not covered by other ministries (Except the Department of Engineering Services) - Central Province
