Approval of the Development plan for limit of Gampaha Municipal Council and pradeshiya sabha

I, Prasanna Ranathunga, Minister of Urban Development and Housing do hereby approve the Development Plan for the enactment of Development plan for limit of Gampaha Municipal Council & Pradeshiya Sabha after consideration of recommendation made by the Board of Management of the Urban Development Authority on 12th June, 2024 by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8 "F" of the Urban Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 1982.

Approval of the Development plan for limit of Gampaha Municipal Council and pradeshiya sabha

Notice is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8(G) of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 4 of 1982 that I, Prasanna Ranathunga, the Minister in charge of the subject of Urban Development & Housing, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8(F) of the said Act, No. 4 of 1982 have approved the Development Plan for the Limit of Gampaha Municipal Council and Pradeshiya Sabha prepared under Section 8 (A) of the said Act on the day of 19th July, 2024.

Prasanna Ranathunga (M.P), Minister of Urban Development and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development and Housing,12th Floor,"Sethsiripaya" Stage II,Battaramulla,26th July, 2024.
