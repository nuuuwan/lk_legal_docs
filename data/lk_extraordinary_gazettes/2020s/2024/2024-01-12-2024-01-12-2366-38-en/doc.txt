EXCISE ORDINANCE

Excise Notification No. 01/2024

Annual EXCISE LICENsE FEES

Fees Charged for Licenses

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 25 read with Section 32 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended from time to time, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies do hereby notify that the annual excise license fees specified in Schedule I hereto shall come in to force with effect from January 12, 2024.

The Excise Notification No. 1004 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2049/4 of December 11, 2017 and the Excise Notification No. 09/2018 published in Gazette Extraortinary No. 2065/57 of April 06, 2018 are hereby rescinded.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, EconomicStabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01, January 12, 2024.
