casino business (regulation) act, No. 17 of 2010

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Section 4 of the Casino Business (Regulation) Act, No. 17 of 2010, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, hereby amend the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2366/33 dated January 12, 2024, and the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2369/42 dated February 02, 2024 as follows :

(1) Substitution for the words and figures, “Casino Business (Regulation) Act, No. 17 of 2010”, wherever it appears of the words and figures “Casino Business (Regulation) Act, No. 17 of 2010”; and

(2) Substitution for the word “regulation”, wherever it appears of the word “regulation”.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, Colombo 01,April 22, 2024.
