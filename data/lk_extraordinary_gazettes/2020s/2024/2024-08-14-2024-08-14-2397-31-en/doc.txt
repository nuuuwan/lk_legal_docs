Approval of the Development plan for limit of Ampara Urban Council

and Part of pradeshiya sabha Limit of Namal Oya

Notice is hereby given to the General Public of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka under Section 8(G) of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 as amended by the Act, No. 4 of 1982 that I, Prasanna Ranathunga, the Minister in charge of the subject of Urban Development & Housing, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 8(F) of the said Act, No. 4 of 1982 have approved the Development Plan for the Limit of Ampara Urban Council & Part of Pradeshiya Sabha limit of Namal Oya prepared under Section 8 (A) of the said Act on the day of 07th August, 2024.

Prasanna Ranathunga (M.P), Minister of Urban Development and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development and Housing,12th Floor,“Sethsiripaya" Stage II,Battaramulla,09th August, 2024.
