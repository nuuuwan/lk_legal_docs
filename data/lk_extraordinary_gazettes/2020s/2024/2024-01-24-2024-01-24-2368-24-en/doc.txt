notification under the finance act, No. 35 of 2018

Luxury Tax on Motor Vehicles

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 51 read with Section 19 of the Finance Act, No. 35 of 2018, i, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, do hereby, amend Paragraph 5(a) of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2147/72 dated October 31, 2019 as last amended by the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2334/24 dated May 31, 2023 by ;

1. The substitution for the words and figures “on or before September 15, 2023” of the words and figures of “during

the period commencing from May 01, 2022 and ending on September 15, 2023”;

2. The substitution for the words and figures “October 31, 2023” of the words and figures of “June 30, 2024”; and

3. The substitution for the words and figures “January 31, 2024” of the words and figures of “September 30, 2024”;

Ranil Wickremesinghe,Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies,Colombo 01,January 24, 2024.
