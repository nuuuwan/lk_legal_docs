Proclamation by the Hon. President

Ranil Wickremesinghe,

KNOW YE that by virtue of the powers vested in me by Article 70 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, President, do by this Proclamation, prorogue Parliament with effect from midnight of the Twenty Sixth day of January, Two Thousand and Twenty ‍Four and hereby fix the Seventh day of February, Two Thousand and Twenty Four, at 10.30 a.m. for the commencement of the next session and summon Parliament to meet in the Chamber of Parliament, Sri Jayewardenepura.

Given at Colombo on this 26th day of January, Two Thousand and Twenty Four.

By Command of the Hon. President,

E. M. S. B. Ekanayake, Secretary to the President.
