PRADESHIYA SABHA - ALAWWA

IT is hereby notified for public information that I have decided under the Decision No. 2288 of Decision Book dated 03.01.2024, that the roads and thoroughfares for which legal actions have not been taken in a proper court before the date specified in the said notification, and all roads and thoroughfares which have been confirmed to be deemed to be owned by the Pradeshiya Sabha Alawwa in accordance with the decree announced by that Court, from among the roads and thoroughfares considered to be owned by the Pradeshiya Sabha Alawwa in the Schedule set out in the Notification published in the Extraodinary Gazette No. 2342/60 dated 29.07.2023, by virtue of powers vested under the Orders in the series of "Orders with regard to the preparation of plans for roads and thoroughfares maintained by the Pradeshiya Sabhas in the North Western Province and publication of advertisements specifying the name list" by Hon. Governor in the North Western Province which has been published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2238/35 dated 30th July 2021 and the order made under Section 24(1) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987.

IT is further notified that a list of such roads and thoroughfares has been includeg in the schedule here to and every road of and thorough fares included therein shall be deed as roads and thoroug me owned by the Pradesiya Sabha Alawwa under Sub-section (5) of Section 24 of the pradeshiya Sabha Act.

J. M. P. Jayasekara, Secretary, (Officer of executing powers and duties) Pradeshiya Sabha - Alawwa.Head Office of Pradeshiya Sabha Alawwa,08th January 2024.
