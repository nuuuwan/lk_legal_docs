The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Notification

IT is hereby notified that the Hon. President, having consulted with the Hon. Prime Minister under Article 44(3) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, has determined that the ministerial portfolios indicated below should be in his charge.

1. Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism 2. Minister of Energy 3. Minister of Agriculture, Lands, Livestock, Irrigation, Fisheries and Aquatic Resources

By Order of the Honourable President,

Dr. N. S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,September 25, 2024.
