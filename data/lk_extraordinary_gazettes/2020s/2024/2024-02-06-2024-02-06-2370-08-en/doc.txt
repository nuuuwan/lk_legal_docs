Notice Calling for Nominations for the Appointment of Mediators

THE Mediation Boards Commission, by virtue of the powers vested in it by Section 4 (1) of The Mediation (Special Categories of Disputes) Act, No. 21 of 2003, invites nominations for the appointment of Chairman and Mediators for the following Panels of Special Mediation Board (Land) in respect of resolving all disputes relating to State Lands and Private Lands subject to the provisions contained in the Extraordinary Gazette Notiﬁ cation No. 2261/68 dated 06.01.2022.

(I) The following Persons are entitled to be appointed to the Panel of a particular Special Mediation Board Area whose names may be so submitted for appointment.

(a) A person eligible to be appointed as a Member of the Panel of Mediators in respect of the Special Mediation Boards shall be between 35 to 70 years of age and; (b) has any professional qualiﬁ cation obtained from a recognized professional body established by or under any written law; (c) has been or is public oﬃ cer.

(i) A Public Oﬃ cer or a Provincial Public Oﬃ cer serving as the Head of a Department or as the Regional Head of a Department in an oﬃ ce located within the Administrative District at which that particular Special Mediation Board area is situated: (ii) A Head of a Place or religious worship or of school situated in that particular Special Mediation Board area; (iii) A retired Head of a Department or School principal residing in that particular Special Mediation Board area; (B) Any Board, Organizations or Institution which has been in existence at least for a period of ﬁ ve years and which is engaged in carrying out or doing anyone or more of the under mentioned objectives;

(i) The enhancement of educational, religious, moral or spiritual, advancement of the community (ii) The enhancement of social welfare and eradication of poverty (iii) The enhancement of rural or community development. (iv) The enhancement of culture of sports (v) The enhancement of projects, programmes and activities intending providing and supplying basic needs of the peoples such as health, food and shelter etc.

(C) The following District Secretaries/Divisional Secretaries are entitled to submit nominations for appointment to the Panel of a particular Special Mediation Board Area:-

(II) The District Secretaries of the Administrative District in which that Special Mediation Board Area is situated;

03. (a) Individual and Boards, Organizations and Institutions referred to in Paragraph (02) above may submit nominations of such Public Oﬃ cers as are speciﬁ ed in Paragraph 2(a) and 2(b) above. An individual may not nominate his or her name for nominations.
