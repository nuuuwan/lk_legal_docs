Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41, 1978,1 Anura Karunathilaka, the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing being opinion that area specified in Column I of the Schedule here to and having the metes and bounds specified in the corresponding entry in column II of that Schedule is suitable for development, do by this Order, declare such area to be an Urban Development Area for the purpose of that Section.

Anura Karunathilaka, (M.P) Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing.

Ministry of Urban Development, Construction and Housing,12th Floor,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,17th December, 2024.
