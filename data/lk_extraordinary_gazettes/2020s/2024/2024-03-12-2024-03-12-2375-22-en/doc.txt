universities act, no. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Horizon College of Business & Technology (Private) Ltd, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 62877 and located at No. 482/B, Millenium Drive, Malabe, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2348/37 of September 07, 2023, by the addition immediately after entry “Master of Education” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

Schedule I

Conditions

1. Horizon College of Business & Technology (Private) Ltd shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Honours in Data Science, complete with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of ;

(a) At least three (03) simple passes (S) in Physical Science Stream of Engineering Technology stream in one and the same sitting at the G. C. E. (Advanced Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification ; or

universities act, no. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education, having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the CINEC Campus (Pvt) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 17745 and located at Millennium Drive, IT Park, Malabe 10115, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order, amend the Schedule I to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2343/03 of July 31, 2023, by the repeal of the condition 1 (a) of Schedule I and the substitution therefor of the following :

1. CINEC Campus (Private) Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of ;

(a) Bachelor of Education Honours in Sports and Physical Education with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of three (3) ‘simple passes’ (S) in any subject stream in one and same sitting at the G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification with School or Zonal or National or Provincial or International Level achievements in sports. Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, Minister of Education.

Colombo,06th March, 2024.
