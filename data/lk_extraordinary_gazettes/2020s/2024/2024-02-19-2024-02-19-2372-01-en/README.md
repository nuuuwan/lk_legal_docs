CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL)

Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notiﬁ ed that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)I, P. B. S. C.
