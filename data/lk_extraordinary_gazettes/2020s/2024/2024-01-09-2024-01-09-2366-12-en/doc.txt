Local Government Notifications

Pradeshiya Sabha Nawagaththegama

IT is hereby notified for public information that it has been decided under the Decision No. 398 dated 07.12.2023 that the roads and thoroughfares for which legal actions have not been taken in a proper court before the date specified in the said notification, and all roads and thoroughfares which have been confirmed to be deemed to be owned by the Pradeshiya Sabha Nawagaththegama in accordance with the decree announced by that Court, from among the roads and thoroughfares considered to be owned by the Pradeshia Sabha Nawagaththegama in the Schedule set out in the Notification published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2342/43 dated 28.07.2023, by virtue of powers vested under the Orders in the series of “Orders with regard to the Preparation of plans for roads and thoroughfares maintained by the Pradeshiya Sabhas in the North Western Province and publication of advertisements specifying the name list” by Hon. Governor in the North Western Province which has been published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2238/35 dated 30th July 2021 and the orders made under Section 24 (1) of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987.

It is further notified that a list of such roads and thoroughfares has been included in the Schedule hereto and every road and thoroughfares included therein shall be deemed as roads and thoroughfares owned by the Pradeshiya Sabha Nawagaththegama under sub-section (5) of Section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act.

M. W. L. Somachandra, Secretary and Officer who Executes Powers, Duties and Functions Pradeshiya Sabha Nawagaththegama.

At the Head Office of Pradeshiya Sabha Nawagaththegama,07th December, 2023.

Decision
