inland revenue act, no. 24 of 2017

Regulation under Section 123 (8)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Sub-section (8) of Section 123 of the Inland Revenue Act, No. 24 of 2017 as amended by the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 2023 (herein after referred to as IR Act), I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, do by this regulation prescribe the information to be shared on a regular basis to the Commissioner General of Inland Revenue (hereinafter referred to as CGIR). As provided in the IR Act, notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any other written law, the persons including Government Institutions shall provide information as prescribed in the Schedule I hereto, to the CGIR, subject to the conditions of the Schedule II of this Notification.

This Notification shall come into effect from April 01, 2024. Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, Colombo 01,March 21, 2024.

Schedule I

(1) The Registrar-General of the Registrar General’s Department shall provide on a monthly basis and through the web-based platform or any other electronic platform approved by the CGIR: -

(a) Information on ownership or title of the registered land/property including transfers – (i) Name/s of owner/s or titleholder/s; (ii) National Identity Card Number/ Passport Number / Registrar of Company Number (hereinafter referred to as “NIC/PP/ROC” in this Notification) of titleholder/s; (iii) Type of transfer; (iv) In case of sale – (iva) Name of seller/s; (ivb) NIC/PP/ROC of seller/s; (v) Location of the land/property; (vi) Value of land/property as per the ownership/title document; (vii) Name of the Notary; (viii) National Identity Card Number of the Notary.

(b) Information on registered lease agreements – (i) Name of lessee/s; (ii) NIC/PP/ROC of lessee/s; (iii) Name of lessor/s; (iv) NIC/PP/ROC of lessor/s; (v) Address of the property; (vi) Date of the lease agreement; (vii) Value of lease; (viii) Lease rental.

(2) The Registrar-General of Companies of Department of the Registrar of Companies shall provide on a real time basis and through the web-based platform or any other electronic platform approved by the CGIR: -

(a) Information on registered companies – (i) Registrar of Company Number; (ii) Name of the company; (iii) Date of incorporation; (iv) Address of the company; (v) Capital stock value; (vi) Name/s of director/s; (vii) National Identity Card Number/ Passport Number of director/s.

(b) Information on shareholders- (i) Registrar of Company Number; (ii) Name of the company; (iii) Address of the company;

Schedule II

1. Information received by the CGIR in accordance with Schedule I shall be incorporated into the risk module of Revenue Administration Management Information System (RAMIS) of the Inland Revenue Department.

2. Persons referred to in Paragraph 5 and 6 of the Schedule I shall provide all information for every quarter on or before the twentieth day of the month after the end of each relevant quarter.

3. If any information is not maintained by any person or Government Institution prior to the date of this Notification, such person or institution shall commence to maintain such information on or after July 1, 2024 and complied for this Notification in respect of such information, effective from such date.

4. The CGIR shall, by in written notice or by public notice, notify any person or Government Institution, the date of commencement of furnishing of information under Schedule I.

5. For the purpose of this Notification –

(a) “Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka” means Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka established under the Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka Act, No. 18 of 1990;

(b) “Financial Institution” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the IR Act;

(c) “Government Institutions” means any Department or undertaking of the Government of Sri Lanka and includes any public corporation;

(d) “Person” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the IR Act;

(e) “Quarter” means the period of three months commencing on the first day of January, April, July or October of any year.
