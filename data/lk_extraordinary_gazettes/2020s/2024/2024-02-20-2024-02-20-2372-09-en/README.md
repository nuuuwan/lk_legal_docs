TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 269)

Order under Section 6(2)(b) and Section 21(2)(b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 6(2)(b) and Section 21(2)(b) of the Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Chapter 269), I, Prasanna Ranathunga, Minister of Urban Development and Housing do by this Order –
