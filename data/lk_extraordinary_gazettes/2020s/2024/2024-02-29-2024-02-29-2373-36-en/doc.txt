The universities Act, No. 16 of 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the SLT Campus (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 101137 and located at Ingiriya Road, Meepe, Padukka, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. No. 2348/37 of September 07, 2023 by the addition immediately after the entry “Master of Business Administration” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively in the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto:-
