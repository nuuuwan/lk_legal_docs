L.D.B 15/2024

PUBLIC DEBT MANAGEMENT ACT, NO. 33 OF 2024

Order under Sub section (2) of Section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub section (2) of Section 1 of the Public Debt Management Act, No. 33 of 2024, read with paragraph (3) of Article 44 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, I, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, do by this Order, appoint, 25th November, 2024 as the date on which the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall come into operation.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President.

Colombo,23rd November, 2024
