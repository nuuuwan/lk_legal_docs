It is hereby notified that the Honourable President, in terms of Article 52(1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, has appointed Mr. S. Alokabandara as the Secretary to the Ministry of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour, with effect from October 03, 2024.

By order of the Honourable President,

Dr. N. S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.
