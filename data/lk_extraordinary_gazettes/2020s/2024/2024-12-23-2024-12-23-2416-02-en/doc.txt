Order under Section 4(a) 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under the Section 4(a)1 of the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation Act, No. 15 of 1968 as amended by Act, No. 27 of 1976, Act, No. 52 of 1982, Act, No. 35 of 2006, Act, No. 11 of 2019 and Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation (Amended) Act, No. 13 of 2021, I, Anura Karunathilake, Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing, in consultation with all the respective Local Government Institutions do by this order reservations are declared, for all canal, sub canal and feeder canals where water flows, or led to flow or constructed with the objective of causing water flow, or as created naturally for the purpose, situated within the boundaries of North Central Province of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka morefully described in Schedule I, from the both banks of the canal up to the limits indicated in meters in the Schedule II. Any temporary or/and other sort of buildings or structures should not be constructed in this canal reservation area and a permission should be obtained subject to the Terms and Conditions stipulated by the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation in the third schedule and/or conditions to be enforced from time to time in the future in doing any sort of such activity.

Anura Karunathilake (M.P.), Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing.

12th December, 2024.

Schedule I

All main canals, all sub canals and all feeder canals where water flows or caused to flow water or canals constructed for the purpose of flowing water or those being created naturally for the purpose situated within the North Central Province of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and bounded as,

North by : Areas of the Administrative Districts of Vavuniya, Mannar, Mullaitivu, Trincomalee and Batticaloa

East by : Areas of the Administrative District of Trincomalee, Batticaloa, Ampara, Matale, Vavuniya and Mannar

South by : Areas of the Administrative Districts of Batticaloa, Ampara, Matale, Mannar, Kurunegala and Puttalam

West by : Areas of the Administrative Districts of Matale, Kurunegala, Puttalam, Vavuniya and Mannar

Schedule III

Terms and Conditions.

(a) Construction of any sort of building or structure and (or) filling of land shall not be done within the area declared as a canal reservation without having a written approval of the Chief Executive Officer Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation.

(b) As described in the schedule II the minimum extent of reservation shall physically be available.

(c) Linking storm water drains without prior approval of the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation, severage lines, disposing or throwing industrial waste or any sort of pollutants shall not be done or shall not take any attempt to do such an action.

(d) A Canal reservation could be used as an access road only in an instance where an alternative is not available, but that way shall not be covered by tar, after paving stones or concreting or any other application.

(e) Any individual, Society Institution, or Local Authority, shall not lease the canal reservation for commercial activities or any other activity.

(f) Lands in the canal reservation shall not used for parking vehicles, garages and cultivations.

(h) Any individual authorized by the Chief Executive Officer of the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation for the Activities stipulate in the Act, shall have the power to access the declared area and anyone who disturb such access could be a convicted guilty under the provisions of the Act.

(j) The terms and conditions and also the terms and conditions that will impose in this connection in the future shall be adhered to.
