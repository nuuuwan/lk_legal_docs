Government Notifications

Forest Conservation Ordinance

The order under sub section 3(1) that has to be read with Sub section 4(1) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of powers vested in me under sub section 3(1) that has to be read with Sub section 4(1) of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation do by this Order declare the State lands of the extent 195.0 hectares bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedule hereto as Valaivaittikulam Reserved Forest from 07th day of August, 2024.

Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation.

Ministry of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation, No. 07, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07,07th day of August, 2024.
