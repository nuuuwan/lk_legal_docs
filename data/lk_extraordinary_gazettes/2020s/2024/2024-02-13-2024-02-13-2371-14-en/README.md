CIVIL PROCEDURE CODE (CHAPTER 101)

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms under Section 840 of the Civil Procedure Code (Chapter 101) read with Section 214 of the said Code. Dr. Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe, Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms.Colombo,02nd February, 2024.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Civil Procedure (Costs) Regulations, 2024 and shall come into operation on 01st of March, 2024

2. All bills of costs, whether between a party and parties, or between a registered attorney and a client, shall be taxed by the Registrar of the court at the rates as set out in the Schedule hereto.
