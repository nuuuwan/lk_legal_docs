Southern Province Provincial Council

Imaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha

Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946

IN terms of the powers conferred on the Secretary of the Imaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha by sub-section (1) of section two of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance No. 12 of 1946 (being the 267th Authority) to be read with section 9 (3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, it is announce that, I, William Wijesinghe Gamage, The Governor of the Southern Province, approve the following decision taken under number 150 dated 10.04.2023 shall be read with paragraph (a) of Sub-section (1) of the Second section of the Provincial Councils (Incidental Provisions) Act No. 12 of 1989 in pursuance of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section two of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance.

Dr. William Wijesinghe Gamage, The Governor of Southern Province.

At Governor's Office,Galle,16th February, 2024.

Decision

The Imaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha in terms of the powers conferred on the Secretary of the Imaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha by sub-section (1) of section two of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance Act No. 12 of 1946 (being the 267th Authority) to be read with section 9 (3) of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, An amount equal to 15% (fifteen percent) of any payment made to any entertainment activity described in the above entertainment tax ordinance conducted in the area within the administrative limits of the Sabha to be imposed and levied as a tax and I also decide that this decision should be effective from the date of it’s publication in the Gazette. I also decide that the entertainment tax imposed above shall be paid to the Pradeshiya Sabha by the relevant organizer or organizers before the date of the said entertainment function..
