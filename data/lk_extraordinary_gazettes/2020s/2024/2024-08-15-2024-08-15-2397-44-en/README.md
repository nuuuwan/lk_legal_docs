Government Notifications

L.D.-B. 6/2024

secured Transactions Act, No. 17 of 2024

Order under Subsection (2) of Section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Subsection (2) of Section 1 of the Secured Transactions Act, No. 17 of 2024, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, do by this Order, appoint August 12, 2024, as the date on which the provisions of the aforesaid Act shall come into operation.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies.

Colombo,15th August, 2024.
