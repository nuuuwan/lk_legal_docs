2. By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 5 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, do hereby make an order to waive - off balance amount after collecting Rs. 6 per kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of 10% or Rs. 400 per kg on importation of Mackerel (Scomber scombrus, scomber australasicus, scombe japanicus) classified under the HS Code of 0303.54 and Jack and horse mackerel (Trachurus Spp.) classified under the HS Code of 0303.55 listed out in the Schedule of the above, classified under HS Heading of 03.03 for production of canned fish by local canned fish manufactures on the recommendation of the Secretary, Ministry in charge of subject of Fisheries.

3. This Order shall be valid for a period commencing from October 14, 2024 to December 31, 2024 subject to provisions of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism.
