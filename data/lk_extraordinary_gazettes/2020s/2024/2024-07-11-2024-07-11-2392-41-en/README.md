the societies ordinance

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sections 3 & 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), I, Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, Minister of Ministry of Industries, do by this notification ;

01. Authorize the purpose for which the Society known as “96 PASU MAHAJANA BANKU SEWAKA WISHRAMIKA SANGAMAYA” situated 52B, Galgode Gedara, Pasyala as a purpose to which the powers and facilities of that Ordinance ought to be extended ; and

02. Limit the application of that Ordinance to aforesaid society by exempting it from the provision of Section 5(4) of that Ordinance.

Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, Minister of Industries.
