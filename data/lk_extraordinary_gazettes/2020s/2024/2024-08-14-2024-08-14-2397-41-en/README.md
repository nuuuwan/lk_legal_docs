Notification

It is hereby notified that the Hon. President, having consulted with the Hon. Prime Minister under Article 44(3) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, has determined that the ministerial portfolios indicated below should be in his charge.

1. Minister of Tourism and Lands 2. Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs 3. Minister of Labour and Foreign Employment

By order of the Hon. President,

E. M. S. B. Ekanayake, Secretary to the President.Presidential Secretariat,Colombo 01,August 14, 2024.
