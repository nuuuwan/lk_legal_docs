Order No.: 90

CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 09 OF 2003 (Amended)

Order under Section 20(5)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 20 (5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 09 of 2003, the Consumer Affairs Authority orders that no Manufacturer, Producer, Supplier, Distributor or Trader shall Sell, Supply, Expose or Offer for sale, Display for sale the Local varieties of Rice listed below, exceeding the Maximum Prices given hereunder.

VarietyMaximum Wholesale PriceMaximum Retail Price

White/Red Raw Rice - LocalRs. 215/- per kgRs. 220/- per kg

White/Red Nadu Rice - Steamed /Boiled - Local(excluding Mottaikarupan and Attakari)Rs. 225/- per kgRs. 230/- per kg

White/Red Samba Rice - Raw and Steamed /Boiled - Local (excluding Suduru Samba)Rs. 235/- per kgRs. 240/- per kg

Keeri Samba Rice- Raw and Steamed/ Boiled - LocalRs. 255/- per kgRs. 260/- per kg

This Order shall come into effect from 9th December, 2024.
