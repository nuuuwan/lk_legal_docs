Filling of a vacancy in the office of Chairman of the elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha under section 66b(2) of the local authorities elections ordinance (chapter 262) as amended by local authorities election (amended)

act, no. 16 of 2017

WHEREAS, the office of the chairman of Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Kolamba Thanthreege Nishantha Perera, the former chairman belonged, was required by me under section 66B(2) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the chairman of Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared elected as chairman within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Lelkada Lokuge Sanath Priyantha Sumanasiri to fill such vacancy;

Now therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66B (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local Authorities Election (amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Lelkada Lokuge Sanath Priyantha Sumanasiri elected as Chairman of the Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Elpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Galle,28th November, 2024.
