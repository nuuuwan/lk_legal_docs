Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. A. D. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Education having obtained a report under section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the CINEC Campus (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 17745 and located at Millennium Drive, IT Park, Malabe 10115, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2362/46 of December 13, 2023, by the addition immediately after entry “Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Columns I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto :-

Schedule I

Conditions

1. CINEC Campus (Private) Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Education Honours in Early Childhood Education complete with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6), adhere to the minimum entry qualification of ;

(a) Three (3) simple passes (S) in any subject stream in one and the same sitting at the G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination conducted by the Department of Examination of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification; and
