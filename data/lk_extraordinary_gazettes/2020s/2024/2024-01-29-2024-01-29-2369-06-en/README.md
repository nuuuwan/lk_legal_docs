APPOINTMENT OF AUTHORIZED OFFICERS UNDER THE PRIVATEMEDICAL INSTITUTIONS (REGISTRATION) ACT, No. 21 OF 2006

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Sub section 4 of the Section 14 of the Private Medical Institutions (Registration) Act, No. 21 of 2006, I, Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, Minister of Health do make order by this Notification, by appointing the following officers mentioned in the Schedule hereto shall be the Authorized Officers for the purpose of the said Act in respect of the area assigned to them in the Schedule mentioned below and in any other area in respect to which they may be appointed to act on the exigencies of services. They are appointed by virtue of their ex officio status and this will cover the existing officers in the Schedule hereto who are holding such posts as well as officers who will be appointed to such posts from time to time. All these appointments shall be valid until the officer exists in the service.

All such appointments already made under the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2011/33 dated 24.03.2017 shall be valid and intact.

Dr. Ramesh Pathirana, Minister of Health. Colombo,20th December, 2023.
