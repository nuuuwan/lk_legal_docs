Order under Section 76A

BY virtue of the powers vested in the Election Commission under Section 76 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262), it is hereby notified that paragraph (a) of Sub-section (1) of Section 31 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance should be amended by the substitution for the words “Sub-section (5) of Section 28” of the words “Sub-section (2 c) and Sub-section (5) of Section 28”.
