excise notification No. 05/2024

REVISION OF THE CONDITIONS SET OUT IN THE EXCISE NOTIFICATION No. 666

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 25 of the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52), as amended from time to time, I, Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies do hereby amend the Schedule in the Excise Notification No. 666 of the Extraordinary Gazette dated December 31, 1979 as amended by Excise Notification No. 04/2018 of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2054/42, dated January 18, 2018, with effect from August 14, 2024 as follows;

(1) Substituting sub-paragraph (v) of paragraph 5 of the following sub-paragraph,

“(v) Any persons under the age of eighteen years.”, and

(2) Repealing sub-paragraph (d) of paragraph 11.

Ranil Wickremesinghe, Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization, & National Policies.Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization & National Policies,Colombo 01.August 13, 2024.
