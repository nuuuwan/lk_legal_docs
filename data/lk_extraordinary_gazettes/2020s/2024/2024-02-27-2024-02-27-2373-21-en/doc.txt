Order under Section 4

Mahinda Amaraweera, Minister of Agriculture.Battaramulla,07th February, 2024.

Schedule

1. Professor Indika Gawarammana. Senior Professor and Director, South Asian Clinical Toxicology Research Collaboration, Faculty of Medicine, University of Peradeniya.
