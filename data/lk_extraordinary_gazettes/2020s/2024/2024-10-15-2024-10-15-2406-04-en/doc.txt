1. This permission may be cited as “Permission to disburse under the fourth supplementary financial statement - 2024 of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council” and come in to operation from 16.08.2024.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Fifty Two Billion Three Hundred and Eighty Four Million Eight Hundred and Thirty Three Thousand Four Hundred and Forty Eight and Cents Forty only (Rs. 52,384,833,448.40) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2024 and ending 31st December 2024, which is speciﬁ ed as “Financial Year 2024” in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue – Expenditure Estimates prepared for the year 2024, is hereby amended as, Rupees Fifty Two Billion Eight Hundred and Senenty Two Million Four Hundred and Thirty Eight Thousand One Hundred and Senenty One and Cents Eighty Two only (Rs. 52,872,438,171.82) and grant permission to the Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Oﬃ cers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Oﬃ cers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse as per the Fourth Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2024, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act, No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

I, do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Oﬃ cers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to spend a sum of Rupees Fifty Two Billion Eight Hundred and Seventy Two Million Four Hundred and Thirty Eight Thousand One Hundred and Seventy One and Cents Eighty Two only (Rs. 52,872,438,171.82) out of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund, which is estimated for the expenses of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council for the one year period that starts 01st of January, 2024 and end on 31st of December, 2024.
