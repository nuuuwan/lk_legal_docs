THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A and 70C

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the ESOFT METRO CAMPUS (PRIVATE) LIMITED a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 94112 and located at No. 03, De Fonseka Place, Colombo 04 which is recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute and since under the certificate dated April 25, 2025 issued by the Registrar General of Companies the said ESOFT METRO CAMPUS (PRIVATE) LIMITED has been incorporated as the ESOFT UNI (PRIVATE) LIMITED, do by this Order, amend the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2139/25 of September 03.2019, by the substitution for the words “ESOFT METRO CAMPUS (PRIVATE) LIMITED” wherever those words appear in the said Order, of the words “ESOFT UNI (PRIVATE) LIMITED” and every reference to the “ESOFT METRO CAMPUS (PRIVATE) LIMITED” in any Order, notice, contract, communication or other documents made in terms of the aforesaid sections shall be read and construed as a reference to the “ESOFT UNI (PRIVATE) LIMITED”.

Further, any power exercised, or any duty or function performed by the said ESOFT METRO CAMPUS (PRIVATET) LIMITED prior to the date of coming into effect of this Order in pursuance of any power conferred on it by the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2139/25 of September 03, 2019 shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly exercised and performed by the ESOFT UNI (PRIVATE) LIMITED.

Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, Minister of Education. Higher Education and Vocational Education.

Colombo,08th October, 2025.
