FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEMORAWEWA PRADESHIYA SABHA

Under Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Morawewa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resign,

And Whereas the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to which, Kadaira Velupille Kokila Devi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Morawewa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of Thirty (30) days;

And Whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his dicision to nominate Manthree Vithanage Sugandika Kumari to fill such vacany;

Now therefore I, Selladhore Kaliuganathan Devaneranjan returning Officer of the Morawewa Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Manthree Vithanage Sugandika Kumari was selected as the member of the Morawewa Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. K. Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer, Morawewa Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Trincomalee,07th November, 2025.
