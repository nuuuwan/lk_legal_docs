Provincial Councils Notifications

SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Orders under the Co-operative Employees’ Pension Statute No. 02 of 2019 of the Southern Provincial Council

As the Southern Provincial Council has been dissolved, the powers vested upon the Minister in charge of the subject under Section 24 of Co-operative Employees’ Pension Statute No.02 of 2019, have been assigned to me, the Governor of the Southern Province by Article 154 C of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and in terms of such powers, do hereby exclude Section 6.ii (C) and chart No.01 of schedule (A) of orders of the Co-operative Employees’ Pension Statute No.02 of 2019 and substitute thereof with the following section and chart No. 01 and the said set of orders are amended and enacted. These orders shall be effective from 01.04.2024.

Madduma Kankanamalage Bandula Harischandra, Governor, Southern Province.
