THE TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 269)

Order under section 6(2) (b) and section 21 (2) (b)

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 6(2) (b) and section 21(2) (b) of the Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Chapter 269), I, Anura Karunathilaka, Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing, do, by this Order,

(a) declare that the area of land called “Rambadagalle Monaragala Rajamaha Vihara Area” speciﬁed in the Schedule hereto shall be an urban development area for the purpose of the aforesaid Ordinance, and

(b) direct that an outline physical plan shall be prepared for the “Rambadagalle Monaragala Rajamaha Vihara Area”
