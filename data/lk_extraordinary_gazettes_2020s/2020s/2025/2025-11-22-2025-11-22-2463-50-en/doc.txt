No. 2463/50 – SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 2025 - 22.11.2025

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 File No.: KDS/PDB/4/3/20/3. Land Ministry's Ref. No.: 4-3/5/2020/HA/23. I do hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to obtain land described below for a public purpose. Description of the land to be acquired : The allotment of land called Jambugahamadiththa in an extent of about 0.0253 Hectare, depicted in the Cadastral Map No. 321099, Zone No. 01, Paper No. 01 and 04.11.2025 dated plan, surveyed and prepared by the Surveyor General and situated in Medagedara Gammedda Village of No. 633 Napana Grama Niladhari Division in Udagampaha Korale Minor Division of Pathadumbara Divisional Secretary's Division in Kandy District, Central Province. Lot No. 01 - Name of the Land: Jambugahamadiththa; Name and address of the claimant: Rev. Muthugala Dhammakkanda Thero,

The Chief Incumbent Thero, Napana Purana Vihara, Madawala Bazaar; Extent: 0.0253 Hectare. North: Jambugahamadiththa claimed by Napana Purana Vihara; East: Jambugahamadiththa claimed by Napana Purana Vihara; South: Road (Pradeshiya Sabha); West: Jambugahamadiththa claimed by Napana Purana Vihara. All persons interested in the aforesaid land are required to appear before me personally, or by agent duly authorized in writing at the Divisional Secretariat, Pathadumbara, on the 18th day of December, 2025, at 10.00 a.m. and they are hereby requested to notify me in writing with duplicates on or before the 11th day of December 2025, the nature of their interests in the aforesaid land and the amount and the particulars of their claims for compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. S. N. K. Sൾඋൺඌංඇ඀ඁൾ, Divisional Secretary, Pathadumbara. At Divisional Secretariat, Pathadumbara, On 19th Day of November, 2025. EOG 11 - 0178
