Government Notifications

THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No.41 OF 1978

Order under Section 3

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 as amended, I, Anura Karunathilaka, the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing being of the opinion that the area specified in Column I of the Schedule here to and having the metes and bounds specified in the corresponding entry in column II of that Schedule is suitable for development, do by this Order, declare such area to be an Urban Development Area for the purposes of that Section.

Anura Karunathilaka, (M.P) Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing.
