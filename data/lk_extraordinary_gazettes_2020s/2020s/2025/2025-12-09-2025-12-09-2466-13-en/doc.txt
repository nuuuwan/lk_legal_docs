Land Acquisition Notices

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation do hereby declare, under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the land described below is needed for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

Acquisition of servitude right over the allotments of land for the access road (Werellagaswewa) of Nelligala Service Reservoir of Kundasale Haragama Water Supply Project.

The allotments of land deﬁ ned as following name and extent, depicted in the Advance Tracing Maha/KND/2022/168 and dated 31.05.2022, prepared by the Surveyor General, situated at the Village of Degaldoruwa, in No. 652, Degaldoruwa Grama Niladhari Division, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kundasale, in Kandy District, Central Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot ‘A’ – Extent: 0.0267 Hectare North: Werellagaswewa claimed by S.B.Construction, Werellagaswewa claimed by H.M.A.B.Herath and Werellagaswewa claimed by N.Wickramasooriya; East:

Road (Pradeshiya Sabha) Werellagaswewa claimed by N.Wickramasooriya; South: Werellagaswewa claimed by N.Wickramasooriya, Werellagaswewa claimed by G.Rathnasinghe, Werellagaswewa claimed by M.L.Gunasinghe and Lot B; West: Werellagaswewa claimed by G.Rathnasinghe, Werellagaswewa claimed by M.L.Gunasinghe, Lot B and Road.
