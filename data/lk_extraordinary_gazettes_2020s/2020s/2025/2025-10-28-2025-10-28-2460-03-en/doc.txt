No. 2460/03 – TUESDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2025

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My Ref. No.: 09/03/01/2022/04. Land Ministry’s Ref. No.: 4-3/9/2021/PC/418. Notice is hereby given under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land/servitude intended to be acquired:

The following land, in extent of 0.4060 Hectare, situated in the Village of Balagalla, in Grama Niladhari Division No. 50, Balagalla West, within the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Divulapitiya, in the Gampaha District, Western Province, as shown in lot number 1 in Preliminary Plan No. 510006 (Zone 7) and Sheet 17, prepared by the Surveyor General, dated 16.09.2025. 01. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Warallagahalanda; Description: Home Garden; Claimant: Jayakodi Arachchige Kithsiri Jayakodi; Statutory Provinsions and Reason for Survey:

For acquisition under the Land Acquisition Act; Extent: 0.4060 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid land or servitude, claiming an interest, right, or any other connection thereto, are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at the Divisional Secretariat, Divulapitiya, on the 26th day of November, 2025, at 10.00 a.m. Furthermore, they are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate), on or before the 19th day of November, 2025 the nature of their interests in the said land/servitude, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation, and the details of the computation of such amount.

gfg W.W.M.D. KUMARI, Divisional Secretary, Divulapitiya Division. At the Divisional Secretariat, Divulapitiya. 24th October, 2025, EOG 10-0256
