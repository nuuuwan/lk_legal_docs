THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Reference No. of Div. Sec. : BLDS/LND/1/2/20. Lands Ministry's Ref. No.:03J/92/FR/101. I do hereby give notice, under Sub section (1) Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following Land/ Servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the Land / Servitude to be acquired :

The allotment of land in extent of 4.0740 Hectares mentioned in the Schedule below, of land in Block No. 12, Sheet No. 03 of Map No. 620083, Cadastral in prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in Grama Niladhari Division No. 254 B Ampitiyawatta in the Village of Massenna in Divisional Secretary's Division of Balangoda in the Ratnapura District in the Sabaragamuwa Province.

01. Lot No. 03 Name of the Land : Massennawatta; Claimant:-1.R.A. Jayasinghe, 2. A.D.Asanka Nirmal, 3. A.D.Naleen

Asanka; Extent : 4.0740 Hectares. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude are hereby required to appear personally or by agent duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Balangoda on the 24th November 2025 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 17 th November 2025 the nature of their interests in the land/lands, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. ANURADHA JAYAKODY, Divisional Secretary(Acting), Balangoda. At the Divisional Secretariat, Balangoda. On 13th day of October 2025. EOG 10 - 0171
