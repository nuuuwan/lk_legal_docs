IMPORTS AND EXPORTS (CONTROL) ACT, NO. 1 OF 1969

IN terms of the powers vested in me by Section 20 read together with Sub-Section 4(1) and Section 14 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 as amended by Act, No. 48 of 1985 and Act, No. 28 of 1987,1, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development promulgate following Regulations.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development,Colombo 01.December 01, 2025.

REGULATIONS

1. These Regulations may be cited as the “Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 10 of 2025”. 2. These Regulations are issued as an interim measure to expedite clearance of donated goods from overseas for those impacted by Cyclone Ditwah.

3. Notwithstanding the existing Regulations promulgated under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969, importation of goods, to be received from any foreign states (governments), organizations or persons under the autho-rization of the Secretary to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and as donations in the name of (Consignee) Disaster Management Center or any Government entity will be exempted from the applicable Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations as follows, until further notice.

3.1 Director General of Customs shall release such goods that are subject to Import Control License (ICL) requirement excluding goods for which the issuance of Import Control Licenses is suspended, by the Special Import License Regulations No. 01 of 2023, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2312/77 dated January 01, 2023, as amended, without an Import Control License (ICL), from Sri Lanka Customs;

a. Subject to approval / permit of applicable state organizations that are directly linked through ASYCUDA system with Sri Lanka Customs,

b. In consultation with the Controller General of Imports and Exports in other instances except 3.1 (a) above.

3.2 Director General of Customs shall release such goods categorized under HS Codes 1006.30.19 and 1006.30.29, that are subject to Import Control License (ICL) requirement but issuance of Import Control License (ICL) is suspended by the Special Import License Regulations No. 01 of 2023, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2312/77 dated January 01, 2023, as amended, from Sri Lanka Customs.

3.3 Director General of Customs shall release Only such goods categorized under HS Codes 8467.81 (Chain saw) and 3924.10.90 (single-use plastic) that are listed as import banned by the Special Import License Regulations No. 01 of 2023, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2312/77 dated January 01, 2023, as amended, from Sri Lanka Customs.

3.4 Director General of Customs shall release such goods that are subject to Standardization and Quality Control Regulations under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.l of 1969, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2384/35 dated May 17, 2024, and listed out in Schedule I of this Gazette, without obtaining approval from the Sri Lanka Standards institution, from Sri Lanka Customs.

4. All other imports of goods to be received as donations, if not processed in accordance with Regulation No. 3 above, will be governed by the prevailing Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations.

5. The Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 01 of 2019, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2107/45 dated January 25, 2019, is hereby amended.

5.1 Notwithstanding the Regulation No. 3 (a) of the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 01 of 2019, referred above, importation of unregistered (Brand- new) Ambulances as donations or as grants exclusively for the purpose of direct usage of 1990 Suwaseriya Foundation shall be allowed for Customs Clearance without Air Bags by the Director General of Sri Lanka Customs subject to other prevailing rules and regulations applicable on importation of motor vehicles, classified under the HS Chapter 87, in terms of the decision of the Cabinet of Ministers dated July 09, 2024 on the Memorandum No. 24/1261/610/048 dated June 18, 2024, titled ‘Obtaining Ambulances for the 1990 Suwa Seriya Foundation as Free Grants’, of the Minister of Health.

5.2 Total number of 150 unregistered (Brand-new) Ambulances shall be allowed to import under these Regulations.

5.3 Ambulances import under these Regulation shall be registered in the name of 1990 Suwaseriya Foundation and not transferable without prior approval of the Controller General of Imports and Exports.

6. To achieve the objectives of these regulations, the Controller General of imports and exports shall issue “Operational Instructions” to the director General of Customs, licensed banks or any other relevant authorities.
