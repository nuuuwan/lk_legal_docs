THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 No.: BKP/LAND/ACQUI/D2/3/5-2. Land Ministry's No.: 4-3/18/2021/WS/03. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, government intends to acquire allotments of lands mentioned in the Schedule below for which land called Parawatta has been annexed for construction of Water Storage Tank under Ruwanwella Water Scheme in Divisional Secreatry's Division of Bulathkohupitiya. Description of the Land to be Acquired : Lot No. 01 mentioned in the Schedule annexed in Preliminary Plan No. 610471 prepared by Superintendent of Surveys of Kegalle situated in the village of Panapitiya in Grama Niladhari Division of Panapitiya in Divisional Secreatry's Division of Bulathkohupitiya in Kegalle District in Sabaragamuwa Province.

Examination Date for compensation claims: For Lot No. 01 of No. 610471 03.11.2025 10.00a.m. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/ servitude are hereby required to appear personally or by agents duly authorized in writing before me at Divisional Secretariat, Bulathkohupitiya on the above date. Further, it is hereby notified me in writing (in duplicated) before 27.10.2025 regarding the nature of their interests in the land/lands, particulars of their claims for compensation for acquisition of the said land/ servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of computation of such amount. H.R.M.P.ABEYRATHNA, Divisional Secretary/ Land Acquiring Officer, Bulathkohupitiya. Divisional Secretariat, Bulathkohupitiya. 01st July, 2025. EOG 07 - 0081
