NOTICE No : 31/0449 Matale District IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 310056 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Bulagala (Block 01, Sheet No. 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No. E/450B - Bulagala in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Dambulla in the District of Matale in the Province of Central Province should submit his claim to the undersigned before 08th of September 2025 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
