The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Padiyathalawa Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Aththanayaka Mudiyanselage Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer of the Padiyathalawa Pradeshiya Sabha do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Gonapeenuwala Withanage Chathun Irangika of the Padiyathalawa Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of the Member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has been ceased to be a member of the Sarvajana Balaya.. Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer, Padiyathalawa Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Ampara, 12th December, 2025.
