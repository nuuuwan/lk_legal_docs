PRADESHIYA SABHA ACT, No. 15 OF 1987

BY virtue of the powers vested in me, by the section 24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, read with section 2 of the Provincial Council Act, No. 12 of 1989 (Consequential Provisions), I, Wasantha Jinadasa, the Governor of the North Central Province do hereby publish that following order made by me .

Wasantha Jinadasa, Governor of North Central Province

At Anuradhapura,21st of July, 2025.

Orders

1). It is hereby notified that this order is issued granting permission to extend the time period to North Central Provincial Pradeshiya Sabhas to act as the section No.24 of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act and ordering to publish required procedures depicting the list of road and lanes and to establish the provisions to implement the matters regarding them and all other consequential matters as appropriately, whereas the roads and lanes that are considered as belonging to pradeshiya Sabha as per the section 23 of aforesaid Act, whereas the Maps of the roads and lanes should be prepared, boundary stones should be established and the boundaries should be marked under section No. 24 of the said Act within three years with effect from the date of enactment of the Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, namely, prior to 11th of May 1990, whereas required orders have not been established up to now though it is ordered that the notices should be published as the order have been made.
