SOUTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Co-operative Societies Statue, No. 06 of 2019 of the Southern Province Provincial Council Orders on Delegation of Powers

SINCE the Southern Provincial Council has been dissolved, the powers vested in the Minister in charge of the subject of Co-operatives of the Southern Provincial Council by Section 2.(4) of the Co-operative Societies Statute No. 06 of 2019 of the Southern Province Provincial Council, have been assigned to me by Article 154 (C) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and in terms of such powers, I, Madduma Kankanamalage Bandula Harischandra, the Governor of the Southern Province, hereby delegate all powers mentioned in column III of the schedule below, which are vested in the Commissioner Registrar of Co-operative Societies in terms of the Co-operative Societies Statue No. 06 of 2019 of the Southern Province Provincial Council and the rules made there under, and all powers mentioned in column III that are not inconsistent with the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act No. 05 of 1972 read with the preamble of the said Co-operative Societies Statute, to the person mentioned in column I of the schedule below to assist the Commissioner of Co-operative Development of the Southern Province, from the date mentioned in column II of the said schedule.
