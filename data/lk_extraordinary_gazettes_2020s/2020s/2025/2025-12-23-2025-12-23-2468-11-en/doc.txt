The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE ATHTHANAGALLA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Aththanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Wannakuwaththa Mitiwaduge Hansika Perera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 10 Katuwasgoda of Aththanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Jayasekara Subasinghe Arachchige Dhammika Jayasekara to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Aththanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Jayasekara Subasinghe Arachchige Dhammika Jayasekara was elected as the member of the Ward Number 10, Katuwasgoda of Aththanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Aththanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Gampaha,23rd December, 2025.
