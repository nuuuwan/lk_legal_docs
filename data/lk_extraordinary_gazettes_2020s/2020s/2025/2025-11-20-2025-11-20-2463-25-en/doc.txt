The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PELIYAGODA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Peliyagoda Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Loronsu Hewage Disna Niranjala Kumari the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward number 05 Rohana Vihara Pedesa of Peliyagoda Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Dissanayaka Mudiyanselage Rathna Lanka Bandara to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Peliyagoda Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Dissanayaka Mudiyanselage Rathna Lanka Bandara was elected as the member of the Ward Number 05 Rohana Vihara Pedesa of Peliyagoda Urban Council. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Peliyagoda Urban Council. District Elections Office,Gampaha,18th November, 2025.
