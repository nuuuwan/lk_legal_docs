STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether Mrs. W. M. Piyaseeli who was employed as Assistant Manager (Finance) of Bandarawela Depot of the Sri Lanka Transport Board had been entrusted with the responsibility of collecting training fees from apprentices of the Bandarawela Driver Training School and whether she had been caused injustice by being punished for not collecting the same and if so, to what relief she is entitled.

H. M. D. N. K. WATALIYADDA, Commissioner General of Labour.
