THE SOCIETIES ORDINANCE.

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 & 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), 1, Wasantha Samarasinghe Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development do by this notiﬁ cation;

1) Authorize the registration of the society namely, TAHOMA ORGANIC GROWERS' ASSOCIATION of No.454, Metiyakgama, Karambe, Maho to carry out the following purposes under Section 3(b) of the Societies Ordinance No. 16 of 1891:

i. To organize individuals engaged in cultivation using organic farming methods, to sell those products through any company, and to improve the living standards of members through the beneﬁ ts, allowances, and other assistance received from such production, while acting in accordance with the standards relevant to obtaining such beneﬁ ts, funds, and other aid.

ii. To identify the needs of members who cultivate fruits, coconuts, and spices organically, as well as those in the conversion period to become organic farmers, and to implement projects to fulﬁ ll those needs.

iii. To properly manage the beneﬁ ts and allowances in order to achieve the objectives of the association.

iv. To plan and implement activities aimed at collectively improving the living standards of the association's members and their family members.

By virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 & 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), 1, Wasantha Samarasinghe Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development do by this notiﬁ cation;

1) Authorize the registration of the society namely, BIKERHOLICS MOTORCYCLE CLUB of No. 716/3, KULASEWANA MAWATHA, KOTTAWA, PANNIPITIYA to carry out the purposes under Section 3(b) of the Societies Ordinance No. 16 of 1891.

THE SOCIETIES ORDINANCE

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 & 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123), 1, Wasantha Samarasinghe Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development do by this notiﬁ cation;

1) Authorize the registration of the society namely, DIFFERENTLY ABLED SOCIETY PUNGUDUTIVU of KURIKADDUVAN ROAD, 9TH DIVISION, PUNGUDUTIVU to carry out the following purposes under Section 3(b) of the Societies Ordinance No. 16 of 1891.

i. Recognition and Preservation: Uphold the legacy and honor the contributions of those instrumental in founding the organization, ensuring their names and legacies endure.

ii. Empowerment: Enhance the lives of diﬀ erently abled individuals by providing them with essential amenities and empowering resources.

iii. Education and Skill Development: Enable learning through tailored programs for diﬀ erently abled individuals

iv. Employment Opportunities: Create pathways to meaningful employment, fostering economic independence and purpose within the diﬀ erently abled community.

v. Emergency Response: Provide swift and comprehensive assistance during crises and natural disasters, ensuring the safety and well-being of all members.

vi. Family Assistance: Extend support to families navigating the challenges and triumphs alongside their diﬀ erently abled loved ones, oﬀ ering guidance and resources as needed.

vii. Inclusive Community Building: Foster a society where diﬀ erences are celebrated, promoting inclusivity and understanding through community engagement initiatives.

viii. Advocacy and Rights: Champion the rights and dignity of diﬀ erently abled individuals, advocating for inclusive policies and societal reforms.

ix. Awareness and Education: Raise public awareness and dispel misconceptions surrounding disability, fostering empathy and understanding within society.

x. Collaborative Partnerships: Forge partnerships with diverse stakeholders, leveraging collective expertise and resources to amplify impact and reach.

xi. Technological Innovation: Harness the power of technology to develop innovative solutions that enhance accessibility and improve the quality of life for the diﬀ erently abled.

xii. Health and Wellness: Prioritize the physical and mental well-being of diﬀ erently abled individuals, providing access to healthcare services and mental health support.

xiii. Cultural Enrichment: Promote cultural diversity and artistic expression within the diﬀ erently abled community, nurturing creativity and self- expression.
