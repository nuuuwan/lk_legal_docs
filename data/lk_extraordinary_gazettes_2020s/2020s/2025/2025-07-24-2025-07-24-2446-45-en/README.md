The Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: DSNI/LAN/10/ACQ/51. Ministry of Land Reference No.: 4-3/8/2020/CEB/05.

I hereby inform that the Government intends to acquire the following land which is required for a public purpose under Sub-Section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964.

Details of the land expected to be acquired : The lot of land mentioned below in Paper No. 01 of Zone No. 01 in Cadastral Map No. 810697, A. G. P. Plan No. 580 of Maththaka Village, Divisional Secretary’s Division of Niyagama, District of Galle, Southern Province.

1. Lot No. 01 Name of the land: Maththakawelyaya alias Hathawila Kumbura; Usage of the current land: Government; Names of claimants: Marthupitiya Epage Kularathna Epa of Udagedarawatta, Maththaka and others; Extent: 21.7 perches.

All persons claiming ownership, rights or other affiliation to the aforesaid land can apply to themselves or their authorized persons on the 18th day of September, 2025 and can meet me at the Divisional Secretariat Niyagama at 10.00 a.m.

Further, before the 11th day of September 2025 they should inform me in 2 copies of the document regarding the nature of their ownership of the land and the details of the claim for compensation for obtaining the same land/possession and the amount of compensation and the amount calculated.

G. P. A. Thilini, Divisional Secretary, Cover up Duties, Niyagama. Divisional Secretariat of, Niyagama, 18th July, 2025. EOG 07 - 0297
