The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NAGODA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Nagoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Nagoda Gamage Deepthi Kanchana Chathuranga the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Nagoda Pradeshiya Sabha of No. 05 Nagoda who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Kuragodage Jayantha Premalal to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, returning officer of the Nagoda Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that No. 5 Nagoda was elected as the member of the Kuragodage Jayantha Premalal of Nagoda Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Nagoda Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Galle,22nd August, 2025.
