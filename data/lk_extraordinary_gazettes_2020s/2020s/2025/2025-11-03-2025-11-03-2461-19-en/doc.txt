Local Government Notifications

KATARAGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA

Providing the Public with the opportunity to examine the Draft Budget for the year 2026

THE public has been given the opportunity to inspect the Draft Budget prepared for the year 2026 of the Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha, and I would like to inform you that the Draft Budget has been kept at the Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha Library from 03rd November 2025 to 11th November 2025 from 9.00 a.m. to 4.00 p.m. on Government working days.

H. M. D. Harath, Chairman, Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha. EOG 11 - 0035
