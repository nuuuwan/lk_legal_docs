NOTIFICATION MADE UNDER SECTION 66c(4) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE AMENDED BY THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 22 OF 2012

AS per the powers entrusted to me under Section 66c(4) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, I, Sarangika Kalhari Jayasundara, the Commissioner of Local Government in the Western Province do hereby announce that the first meeting held for the purpose of electing Chairman, Deputy Chairman for the Local Government Authority of the Western Province stated in the schedule hereto under will be held on the date correspondingly set out under the same schedule at the time and place specified therein.

Sarangika Kalhari Jayasundara, Commissioner of Local Government, Western Province.

At the Department of Local Government of Western Province,Located in the Western ProvincialCouncil Building at No. 204,Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Battaramulla,04th November 2025.
