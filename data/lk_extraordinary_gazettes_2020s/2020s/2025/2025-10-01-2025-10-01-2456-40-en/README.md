(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE THUMPANE PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death;

And whereas the secretary of the United National Alliance the recognized political party to which Bishrul Amjat Muthalib the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A (I) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days,

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Seyyadu Ahamed Mohamad Rimsad to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Hewage Dhanushka Naleen Sampath Fernando, returning officer of the Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of section 66A (I) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Seyyadu Ahamed Mohamad Rimsad. was selected as the member of the Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha. Dr. H. D. N. Sampath Fernando, Returning Officer, Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kandy,30th September, 2025.
