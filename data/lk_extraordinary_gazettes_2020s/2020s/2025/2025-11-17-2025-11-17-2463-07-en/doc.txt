ANTIQUITIES ORDINANCE

Order

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 39"A" of the Antiquities Ordinance (Chapter 188) as amended by Act, No. 24 of 1998, I, the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, do hereby appoint by this order the members specified in the Schedule hereto as an Archaeology Advisory Committee for a period with effect from 01.12.2025 to 30.11.2027.

The order made through the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2460/56 of 01.11.2025 is cancelled with effect from 30.11.2025.

Dr. Hiniduma Sunil Senevi, Minister of Buddhasasana Religious and Cultural Affairs.

At Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs,On 17th November, 2025.
