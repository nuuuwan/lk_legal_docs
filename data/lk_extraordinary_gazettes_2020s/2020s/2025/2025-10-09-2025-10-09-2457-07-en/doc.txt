No. 2457/07 – THURSDAY, OCTOBER 09, 2025 - 09.10.2025

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice Under Section 7 My Reference No.: THI/LND/L2/ACQVI/163. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following lands which are required for a public purpose. Description of the lands to be acquired:- Allotments of Lot No. 01 and 02 (02 Lots) in total Extent of about 0.0050 Hectare, depicted in Preliminary Plan No. K.M. 820131 dated 16.10.2024 prepared by the Senior Superintendent of Surveys in Matara District on behalf of the Surveyor General, situated in the Village of Kapudoowa East in the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 335C, Kapudoowa East in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Thihagoda in Matara District in Southern Province. 01. Lot No. 01 Name of Land: Kapudoowa Walawwatta; Description: Open Waste Land; Claimant: State; Extent: 0.0011 Hectare.

02. Lot No. 02 Name of Land: Kapudoowa Walawwatta; Description: Road (Pradeshiya Sabha); Claimant: State; Extent: 0.0039 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are hereby required to appear personally or by agent duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Thihagoda on the 21st October 2025 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 14th of October 2025, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said lands, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.
