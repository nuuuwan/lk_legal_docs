L.D-B 9/73 (XII)A

THE FAUNA AND FLORA PROTECTION ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 469)

Order under Subsections (1) and (4) of Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by subsections (1) and (4) of section 2 of the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance (Chapter 469), I, Hettithanthri Patabendige Dammika, Minister of Environment, do by this Order declare that -

(a) the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2383/05 of May 06, 2024 is hereby rescinded; and

(b) the area of land described in the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1956/13 of March 01, 2016, shall, with effect from the date of publication of this Order, be a Nature Reserve and such Nature Reserve shall be called and known as the “Wedithalathive Nature Reserve”.

Hettithanthri Patabendige Dammika, Minister of Environment.

Colombo,21st October, 2025.
