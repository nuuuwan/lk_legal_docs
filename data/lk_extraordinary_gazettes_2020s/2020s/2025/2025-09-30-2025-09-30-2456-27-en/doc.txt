Rൾඌඈඅඎඍංඈඇ

In accordance with the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 2 of the Entertainment Tax Ordinance, Chapter 267, the Ambalangoda Urban Council proposes that a tax of 10% for cinema halls and an equal amount of 12.5% out of the total value of the entry tickets for all other entertainment activities shall be levied as a tax on the payment made for any entertainment activity described in the said Ordinance held within the administrative limits of the Council, and that this resolution shall be eﬀ ective from 01.10.2025. Further, Urban Council, Ambalangoda proposes that the above imposed entertainment tax shall be duly paid to the Ambalangoda Urban Council by the relevant organizer or organizers before the date of holding the said entertainment activity.
