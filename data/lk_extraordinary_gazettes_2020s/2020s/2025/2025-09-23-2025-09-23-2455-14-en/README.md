PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION OF SRI LANKA

Estimated expenditure for the Year 2026

THE Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka was established under the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka Act, No. 35 of 2002. In accordance with the requirements stipulated under Sections 33(2) and 33(3) of the said Act, the Commission is required to publish the estimated expenditure for the following year in the Government Gazette. Accordingly, the Commission approved budget for 2026 is forwarded herewith.

1. Electricity Sector Rs. 781, 991,853
