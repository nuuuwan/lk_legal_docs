The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NAMALOYA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Namaloya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Liyannalage Gedara Shanika Pubudi Hashendri the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned the member of Namaloya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Thelikada Widana Arachchige Suchithra Madushani to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Aththanayaka Mudiyanselage Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, returning officer of the Namaloya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Thelikada Widana Arachchige Suchithra Madushani was selected as the member of the Namaloya Pradeshiya Sabha. Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer, Namaloya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Ampara,10th December, 2025.
