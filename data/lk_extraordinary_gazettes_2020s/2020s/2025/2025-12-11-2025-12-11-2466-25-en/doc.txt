Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 66G OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE AS AMENDED BY THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 22 OF 2012

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 66G. of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance, chapter 262, as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act , No. 22 of 2012, I, Erandi Umanga Mendis the Commissioner of Local Government of the Department of Local Government, Southern Province, do hereby inform that the meeting for the purpose of electing a Chairman for a Local Authority within the Southern Province named in the schedule below, will be convened in terms of the section 66C.(4) of the said Ordinance at the specified time on the date correspondingly indicated under the schedule below at the venue mentioned therein

Erandi Umanga Mendis,Commissioner of Local Government,Southern Province.
