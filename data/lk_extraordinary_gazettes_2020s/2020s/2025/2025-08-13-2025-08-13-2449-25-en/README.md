The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MATARA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Matara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Pehembiya Liyanage Asoka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 11 Kekunadura Central Matara Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Peduru Arachchige Dona Pavithra Weeranayaka to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Pulasthi Serasingha Pathirana returning officer of the Matara Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Peduru Arachchige Dona Pavithra Weeranayaka was elected as the member of the Ward Number 11 Kekunadura Central of Matara Pradeshiya Sabha. P. S. Pathirana, Returning Officer, Matara Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Matara,07th August, 2025.
