EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My No.: NAT/LND/ACQUI/2021/02. Land Ministry Ref No.: 4-3/7/2021/HW/27. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of Land Acquisition Act No. 28 of 1964 (Amendment) that the Government intends to acquire the following land which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land to be acquired: The allotment of land depicted Sheet No. 1 at Block No. 04 in the Preliminary Plan No. Ca.M. 410463, situated in the Village called Mawila in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 504 of Ihala Mawila in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Naththandiya in Puttalam District of North Western Province and more fully described as follows.

Lot No. 01 Name of Land: Nabadagahakumbura; Claimant: J. A. S. N. Nishanthi; Extent: 0.1069 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid land are hereby required to appear personally or by agents duly authorized in writing before me at the Divisional Secretariat Naththandiya on 11.02.2026 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) on or before 05.02.2026 of their interest in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.
