NOTIFICATION BY MR. SPEAKER

Convening of the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on Thursday, the 18th of December, 2025 at 9.30 a.m. I, Dr. Jagath Wickramaratne, Speaker of Parliament do hereby notify that in pursuance of Standing Order 16 of the Standing Orders of Parliament, I have summoned the Parliament to meet on Thursday the 18th of December 2025 at 9.30 a.m. having been requested so to do by the Hon. Prime Minister. Dr. Jagath Wickramaratne, Speaker. Sri Jayewardenepura Kotte,12th December, 2025.
