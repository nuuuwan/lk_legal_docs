10.1.2. Promotion of Officers who pass the examination for promotion from Grade III to Grade II.

i. Officers who, as at or prior to the date on which eligibility for the promotion examination is considered, have completed a minimum of three (03) years of active and satisfactory service in Grade III, have earned three (03) salary increments, and are otherwise not disqualified for confirmation in the service, shall be eligible to apply for or appear at the promotion examination; however, in order to be promoted, it is mandatory that they also fulfill the qualifications specified under Section 10.1.2.1 in addition to passing this examination.

10.1.2.1 Qualifications to be considered:

i. Shall have been confirmed in the appointment.

ii. Shall have completed a minimum of five (05) years of active and satisfactory service in Grade III of the Service Category and earned five (05) salary increments.

18. Interim Provisions

18.1. Officers who were recruited on the results of the Grama Niladhari Recruitment Examination held on 03.09.2016 and

who subsequently pass the Promotion Examination to Grade II conducted after the publication of this amendment, shall be promoted to Grade II in the following manner.

i. Officers who pass the promotion examination and satisfied the qualifications specified in Section 10.1.2.1 shall be promoted to Grade II with effect from the date immediately following either the date of passing the promotion examination or the date of completion of five (05) years of active and satisfactory service in Grade III, whichever occurs later. The period of active and satisfactory service of more than five (05) years completed in Grade III by such officers prior to the date of passing the promotion examination shall be considered as a period of service in Grade II for the purpose of promotion.

18.2 Officers recruited to Grade III on the results of the Grama Niladhari Recruitment Examinations held on 10.03.2007 and 04.05.2013 can also sit for the Promotion Examination to Grade II conducted for the first time after the publication of this amendment. Officers in Grade II who pass the promotion examination shall be promoted to Grade I as follows.

i. Officers who pass the promotion examination shall be promoted to Grade I with effect from the date of examination or the date of completing five (05) years of active and satisfactory service in Grade II, whichever occurs later, provided that they have fulfilled the qualifications specified in Section 10.2.1.1. The entire period of active and satisfactory service exceeding fifteen (15) years, including the period of more than five (05) years completed prior to the date on which they passed the promotion examination, shall be considered as service period in Grade I for the purpose of promotion.

3. Syllabus of the examination:

Question Paper Syllabus

1. Establishments Procedure, Procedural Rules and Financial Systems

The objective of this Question paper is to test the proficiency of experience obtained in office activities and to test the knowledge on office systems adopted in Government Offices to cover subject fields such as principles of office procedures, importance of office procedures, written communication, filing methods and preparation of form etc.

This paper will consist of a section to test the knowledge of the candidate on matters indicated in volume 01 of the Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission and Volume I of the Establishments Code.

Questions to test the candidates’ knowledge and understanding of subjects such as financial control, custody of finance, receipts and payments, budgetary estimation, supplies, work and service exercised in Government Offices, departments and ministries, and Basics of board of survey and storing of goods.

2. Knowledge and Understanding of the rules applicable to the duties of the Grama Niladhari

Knowledge is tested regarding various orders and duties assigned to Grama Niladharis by Ministries, Departments, Provincial Councils, and other government statutory institutions, along with the study of incidents related to those subjects.

Ability of the candidate to understand and analyze the current social affairs.

To gain a complete understanding of the syllabus and the application procedure, candidates should pay attention to the Gazette notifications issued from time to time by the appointing authority.
