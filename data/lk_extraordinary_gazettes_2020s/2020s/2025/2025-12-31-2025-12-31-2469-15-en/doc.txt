FOOD ACT, No. 26 OF 1980

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media, in consultation with the Food Advisory Committee, under Section 32 of the Food Act, No. 26 of 1980.

Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa, Minister of Health and Mass Media. Colombo, 31st December, 2025.

Regulation

The Food (Iodization of Salt) Regulation 2023, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2332/03 of May 15, 2023, as amended by the regulation published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2364/27 dated December 27, 2023, Gazette Extraordinary No. 2416/53 dated December 28, 2024 and Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/09 dated June 30, 2025 are hereby amended by the repeal of Regulation 1 thereof and the substitution thereof with the following regulation:

“1. These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Iodization of Salt) Regulations, 2023 and shall come into operation with effect from 01st of July, 2026.”
