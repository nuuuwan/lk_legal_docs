(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALVETTITHURAIURBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valvettithurai Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the All Ceylon Tamil Congress, the recognized political party to which Appulingam Uthayasuriyan the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Valvettithurai Urban Council of ward number 05 Valvettithurai who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Mahalingam Kanakalingam Sivajilingam to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Valvettithurai Urban Council, acting in terms of section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mahalingam Kanakalingam Sivajilingam was elected as the member of the ward number 05 Valvettithurai of Valvettithurai Urban Council. R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Valvettithurai Urban Council. District Elections Office,Jaffna,07th October, 2025.
