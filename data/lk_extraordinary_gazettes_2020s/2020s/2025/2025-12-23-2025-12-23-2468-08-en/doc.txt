UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(l)(b) read with Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) (b) read with Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Sri Lanka Technology Campus (Pvt) Ltd, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 101137, and located at Ingiriya Road, Meepe, Padukka, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order, amend the Schedule II to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2373/36 of February 29, 2024, by the addition immediately after entry “Master of Science in Electronics’ & Communications Engineering” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto:-
