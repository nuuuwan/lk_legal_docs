L.D.B. 9/2005 (IV)TOURISM ACT, No. 38 OF 2005

Order under section 53

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the section 53 of the Tourism Act, No. 38 of 2005 1, Buddhika Hewawasam, Chairman of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority on the recommendation of the members of the Authority do by this Order revoke the following Orders made under Subsection (1) of section 53 of the Tourism Act, No. 38 of 2005, without prejudice to anything previously done thereunder:-

(a) Order Published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2349/02 of September 11, 2023; (b) Order Published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1697/24 of March 17, 2011; and (c) Order Published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1622/1 of October 05, 2009.

Buddhika Hewawasam, Chairman, Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority.

Colombo,03rd December, 2025.
