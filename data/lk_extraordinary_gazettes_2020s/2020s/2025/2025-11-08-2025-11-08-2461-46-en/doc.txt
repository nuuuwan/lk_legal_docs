SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Third Supplementary Financial Statement - 2025

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OF PROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT No. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26TH SEPTEMBER, 2017.2025

1. This permission may be cited as "Permission to disburse under the Third supplementary ﬁ nancial statement -2025 of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council" and come in to operation from 08.10.2025

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Two Billion Five Hundred Twenty Two Million Five Hundred Seventy Eight Thousand Seven Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents only (Rs. 62,522,578,739.45) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2025 and ending 31st December 2025, which is speciﬁ ed as "Financial Year 2025" in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue and Expenditure Estimate for 2025 approved on 03.04.2025, as amended by the First and Second Supplementary Estimates for 2025, is hereby amended as, Rupees Sixty Two Billion Six Hundred Sixty Four Million Three Hundred Eighteen Thousand Six Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents only (Rs. 62,664,318,639.45) and grant permission to the Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Oﬃ cers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

3. Accordingly the ﬁ rst schedule of the Annual ﬁ nancial statement and revenue- expenditure estimates-2025, is amended as the ﬁ rst schedule here of and grant permission to disburse the same.
