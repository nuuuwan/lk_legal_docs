THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

REVOCATION UNDER SECTION 39(1)

AN allotment of land speciﬁed morefully in the Order No. 87 of 2018 dated 08.05.2018 made under the Proviso (a) to Section 38 of the Land Acquisition Act and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2071/51 dated 16.05.2018 was vested in the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. The possession of the land depicted below has not yet been taken for and on behalf of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. the Order No. 87 of 2018 dated 08.05.2018 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2071/51 dated 16.05.2018.

K. D. Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation.
