PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

Delegation of Powers by the Public Service Commissionin terms of Article 56(1) and Article 57(1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

Delegation

1. (1) The Public Service Commission in terms of Article 56 (1) of the Constitution hereby delegates to the Education Service Committee, the powers set out in Column II in respect of the category/segment of officers specified in Column I in Part I of Schedule “A” hereto.

(2) The Education Services Committee means the Education Service Committee of the Public Service Commission established on 20.10.2016 by the decision of the Public Service Commission and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1989/27 dated 19.10.2016.

2. (1) The Public Service Commission in terms of Article 56 (1) of the Constitution hereby delegates to the Health Service Committee, the powers set out in Column II in respect of the category/segment of officers in Column I in Part I of Schedule “B” hereto.

6. Where a Member of the Committee referred to in Part I of Schedule A or Part I of Schedule B, to which the power of disciplinary control and/or dismissal is delegated, either is a complainant or is a witness or a potential witness at any formal disciplinary inquiry/ preliminary investigation in respect of an officer belonging to a category/segment set out in the corresponding item in Column I of Part I of Schedule A or Column I of Part I of Schedule B as the case may be the powers of disciplinary control and/or dismissal delegated to such Committee shall be deemed to have been rescinded in respect of such officer, and such powers shall be exercised by the Commission as if no such delegation has been made.

7. Where a Public Officer referred to in Part II of Schedule A, in Part II of Schedule B and in Schedule C to whom the power of disciplinary control and / or dismissal is delegated, either is a complainant or is a witness or a potential witness at any formal disciplinary inquiry / preliminary investigation in respect of an officer belonging to a category/segment set out in the corresponding item in Column I of Part II of Schedule A and Column I of Part II of Schedule B, and in Column I of Schedule C, the powers of disciplinary control and/or dismissal delegated to such Public Officer in respect of the officer under formal disciplinary inquiry / investigation shall be deemed to have been rescinded, and such powers shall be exercised by the Commission as if no such delegation has been made.

Conditions

8. All such powers as are delegated herein shall be exercised and discharged in compliance with such instructions, rules and regulations as are applicable to the exercise and discharge of such powers including the following provisions.

(1.) (a) An authority with delegated power shall comply with the provisions of Sections 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21 of Chapter II and the provisions of Sections 288 and 289 of Chapter XX of the Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission.

(b) Sections of the Procedural rules referred to in paragraph (a) shall be set out in Schedule “D” hereto.

(2.) (a) The Public Service Commission, in the discharge of its responsibility and accountability to Parliament, as required by Article 55(5) of the Constitution, may require the authorities with delegated power to submit all relevant records pertaining to the exercise of the delegated powers in randomly selected sample of cases and specific cases.

(b) The authority with the delegated powers shall submit the records referred to in paragraph (a) to the Public Service Commission as may be specified by the Commission

(3.) An authority with delegated power shall not enter into any settlement with any party in any legal proceedings connected with the delegated powers without the prior written approval of the Public Service Commission.

SCHEDULE 'D'Relevant Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission

CHAPTER II

Exercise of Delegated Powers

12. A committee or a public officer on whom powers have been delegated by the Public Service Commission shall be responsible and answerable to the Commission regarding the exercise of such delegated powers.

13. Where the powers of the Commission in respect of public officers have been delegated to a committee, such powers shall be exercised by the members of the committee collectively.

14. The authority with delegated power shall exercise such powers by itself and shall not re-delegate such powers to any other person or a committee.

15. All acts committed and decisions made by any person or a committee in violation of the provisions of the above 14 shall have no effect. The authority with delegated power who authorizes or facilitates such action and decision as well as the person or committee who exercises such power shall be personally liable for such harm or loss caused due to such act or decision.

16. The authority with delegated power shall exercise powers within the scope and limits of such delegation. Any act committed or decision made in excess of the scope and limits of such delegation shall have no effect. Such authority with delegated power or in case of a committee, every member of such committee shall be personally liable for any harm or loss caused by such acts or decisions.

17. Where the authority with delegated power makes any decision or commits any act on the instructions or orders or under the influence of anyone except in accordance with the procedural rules, guidelines, procedures and regulations issued by the Commission, making of such decisions or the commission of such acts shall be treated as an offence under the First Schedule of the disciplinary procedure concerning public officers. Such decisions or acts shall have no force or effect. The authority with the delegated power or in case of a committee, every member of such committee shall be personally liable for any harm or loss caused by such decisions or acts. However, this Section shall not preclude any action taken in pursuance of a decision made by the Administrative Appeals Tribunal or by a Court of Law properly constituted under the Constitution.

18. The authority with delegated power shall exercise such powers in accordance with the procedural rules, guidelines, procedures and regulations impartially, equitably, and in good faith and in a transparent manner.

19. The authority with delegated power shall act diligently on matters entrusted to him and any delay due to reasons beyond his control shall be reported to the Commission forthwith.

20. The authority with delegated power shall seek assistance and advice from the Commission where the necessity arises.

21. Decisions on any matter or interpretation not covered by these Procedural Rules shall be made by the Com-mission.
