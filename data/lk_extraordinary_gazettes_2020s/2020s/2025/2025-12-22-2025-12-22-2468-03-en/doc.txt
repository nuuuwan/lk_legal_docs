ADOPTION OF CHILDREN ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 61)

REGULATION made by the Minister of Women and Child Affairs under Paragraph (b) (i) of Sub section (5A) of Section 03 of the adoption of Children Ordinance.

Saroja Savithri Paulraj, Minister of Women and Child Affairs.

Colombo,18th December, 2025.

Regulation

The number of Adoption Orders that may be made by all courts for the calendar year 2026 in favor of applicants referred to in paragraph (b) (i) of Sub section (5A) of Section 3 shall be one hundred (100) only.
