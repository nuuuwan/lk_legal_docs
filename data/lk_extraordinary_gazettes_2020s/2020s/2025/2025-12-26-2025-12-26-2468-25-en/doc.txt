Part II - General

4. The Economic Census 2025/26 (Agricultural Activities) shall be conducted as follows.

ProcessTime Framei) Collecting information on agricultural activities using tablet computers or "Android" mobile devices.

During the 4th quarter of 2025 and the 1st quarter of 2026.

5. Every person shall provide agricultural information related to their housing unit/ institution to the Census officers appointed by the Superintendent or Commissioner to the respective Grama Niladhari Division.

6. Supervising officers appointed by the Superintendent or Commissioner shall check the accuracy and the status of the information entered into the device used for information collection.

Part III - DUTY OF THE CENSUS OFFICERS

8. Any power or duty delegated or entrusted on the Superintendent of Census by the Ordinance or any rule made there, under the general or special direction of the Superintendent, may be exercised or performed by a Deputy Superintendent of Census throughout the Island or by a Deputy Assistant Superintendent of Census in the area of his/her authority.

9. Any power or duty delegated or entrusted on a Commissioner by the Ordinance or any rule made thereunder may be exercised or performed by a Deputy/Assistant Commissioner within the area for which he/she is appointed, under the general or special direction of the Commissioner of that area.

10. Every Census Officer shall attend the training classes on the dates, at the times and in the places directed by the in charge of Superintendent or Commissioner or the Deputy/Assistant Commissioner of the area for which he/she is appointed as a Census Officer.

11. A Census Officer employed for the purpose of collecting information shall not, except in connection with that usual service, divulge or communicate to any other person, without lawful authority, any information obtained by him/her for the purpose of completing the schedule.

12. A Census Officer employed for the purpose of collecting information shall not disclose the information contained in the tablet computer or forms or other documents relating to the enumeration duties, to any unauthorized person until those are in his/her custody.

13. A Census Officer employed for the purpose of collecting information shall, upon being appointed to the service, complete and sign the following oath or affirmation.

6. A Census Officer may visit the housing units or institutions within his or her Grama Niladhari Division and obtain the required information for the census by inquiring into the questions pertaining to the particulars specified under rule I in this Section.

7. A Census Officer is permitted to re-inquire from any person regarding the information obtained on agricultural activities of individuals whenever the Superintendent deems it appropriate.

8. A Census Officer may, in order to satisfy him/herself that the particulars referred to in rule I in this Section regarding any person had been recorded, ask questions from that person or his/her family members regarding agricultural activities for recording all or some of the particulars referred to in rule I in this Section, and it shall be the duty of that person to answer these questions.
