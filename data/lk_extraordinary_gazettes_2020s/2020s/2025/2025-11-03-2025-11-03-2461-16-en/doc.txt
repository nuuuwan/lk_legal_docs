STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether Mr. W. M. Wicramaratne who worked as a clerk of the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Drugs Corporation had been subject to injustice by the following and if so, to what relief he is entitled.

1. Obtaining the services of the post from 16.02.2016 following the retirement of the chief store keeper and not availing the previledges of the post. 2. Being appointed from 16.02.2016 for acting in the post of stores and stocks control ofﬁcer without proper approval of the post instead of the post of chief Store Keeper and suspension of the post and the allowance from 01.02.2020.

H. M. D. N. K. WATALIYADDA, Commissioner General of Labour.
