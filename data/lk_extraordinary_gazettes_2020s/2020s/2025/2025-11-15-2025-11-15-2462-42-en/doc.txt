AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse as per the Fourth Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2025, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27(a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Two Billion Six Hundred Sixty Four Million Three Hundred Eighteen Thousand Six Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents only (Rs. 62.664.318.639.45) for the services of the period beginning on 01st January, 2025 and ending 31st December, 2025, which is specified in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue Expenditure Estimates for the year 2025, is amended as Rupees Sixty Two Billion Eight Hundred Seven Million Seven Hundred Eight Thousand Six Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents only (Rs. 62.807.708.639.45) and grant permission to disburse the same from, the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund. This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2025 or upto the date that expires three months (03) from the date of summoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which occurs first.

A. A. C. J. Rajarathna, Hon. Governor, Sabaragamuwa Province.

14th day of October 2025.
