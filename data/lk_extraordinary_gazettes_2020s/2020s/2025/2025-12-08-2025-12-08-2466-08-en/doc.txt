(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE KURUNEGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B (2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIESELECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the Office of Vice Chairman in Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of the resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Ganegoda Appuhamillage Chamila Prasadini Rathnawardane the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66B (2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Dewaraja Pathiranalage Anuradha Priyankara Gunathilaka to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayake Returning Officer of the Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66B (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare Dewaraja Pathiranalage Anuradha Priyankara Gunathilaka elected as Vice Chairman of the Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha. R. M. H. B. Rathnayake, Returning Officer, Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,08th December, 2025.
