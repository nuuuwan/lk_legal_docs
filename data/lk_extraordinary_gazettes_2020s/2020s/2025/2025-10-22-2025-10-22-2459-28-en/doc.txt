The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE UHANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Uhana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya the recognized political party to which Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Ghanadari Gunathilaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward No. 02 - Nugelanda of Uhana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Sajeewani Erandi Rathnayaka to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Aththanayaka Mudiyanselage Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer of the Uhana Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Sajeewani Erandi Rathnayaka was elected as the member of the Ward No. 02 - Nugelanda of Uhana Pradeshiya Sabha. Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer, Uhana Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Ampara,21st October, 2025.
