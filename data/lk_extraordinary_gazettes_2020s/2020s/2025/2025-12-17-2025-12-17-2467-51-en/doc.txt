THE PUBLIC SECURITY ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 40)

Notice under Section 2(7)

IT is hereby notified that the Proclamation made by the Hon. President under Section 2 of the Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40), bringing into operation the Provisions of Part II of the aforesaid Ordinance throughout Sri Lanka with effect from 28th November 2025 was approved by a Resolution of Parliament on 05th December 2025.

Dr. N. S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.

Colombo, December 17, 2025.
