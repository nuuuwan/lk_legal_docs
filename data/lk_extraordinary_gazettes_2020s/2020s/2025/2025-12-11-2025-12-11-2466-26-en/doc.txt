The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KALAWANA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kalawana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of failure to take the oath of office;

And whereas the Secretary of the Sarvajana Balaya, the recognized political party to which Nekathimulla Manannalage Sujeewa Pushpakumara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Kalawana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Udage Arachchilage Vineedarathna to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Kasthurisinghe Herath Mudiyanselage Sajith Priyankara Kasthurisinghe, returning officer of the Kalawana Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Udage Arachchilage Vineedarathna was selected as the member of the Kalawana Pradesiya Sabha. K. H. M. Sajith Kasthurisinghe, Returning Officer, Kalawana Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Ratnapura,10th December, 2025.
