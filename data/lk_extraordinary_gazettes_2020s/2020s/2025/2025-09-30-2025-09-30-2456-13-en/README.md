PILGRIMAGES ORDINANCE

It is hereby notified that the Annual Duruthu Maha Religious Festival and Procession of Pilana Purana Sri Maha Temple in the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 152, Pilana, Divisional Secretary’s Division of Akmeemana, District of Galle, Southern Province, will be held from 04th of December 2025 to 3rd of January 2026 as per the regulations published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette Notification No. 2146/18 dated 22.10.2019 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka in terms of the Section 2 of the Pilgimages Ordinance (Cap.175).
