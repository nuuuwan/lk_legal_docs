THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

1, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation, hereby declare under Sub section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act,that the lands described below are required for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

The lands referred to herein are situated in the North Western Province, within the Kurunegala District, Alawwa Divisional Secretary’s Division, and Ihala Nungamuwa Grama Niladhari Division (No. 966), at Nungamuwa Village, as morefully described in the Preliminary Plan No. KU/ALW/2022/2569 dated 29.12.2023, prepared by the Surveyor General.

01. Lot ‘A’ - Narampolawatta - Extent: 1.1389 Hectares North by: Narampolawatta claimed by A.M.C. Udayabandara and others, and Innawatta claimed by M.A.S. Bandara;East: Innawatta canal belonging to M.A.S. Bandara, and Innawatta

claimed by W.D.Nandawathi and W.D.A. Nilangan;South by: Innawatta claimed by W.D.A. Nilangan and the Dehelgamuwa Grama Niladhari Division;West by: Pahala Nungamuwa Grama Niladhari Division.

02. Lot ‘B’ - Narampolawatta - Extent: 0.1755 Hectare North by: Wagollawatta claimed by K.K. Leelawathi;East: Road (under Pradeshiya Sabha authority);South by: Pinagehena claimed by W.D. Seelawathi and the Dehelgamuwa Grama Niladhari Division;West by: Dehelgamuwa Grama Niladhari Division.
