I, the Commissioner of Municipal Council of Kalmunai, hereby inform the public that, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 286 (a) of Chapter 252 of the Municipal Councils Ordinance, as per the By-laws enacted under the Local Authorities (Standard By-laws) Act, No. 6 of 1952 and published in Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 541/17 of 20/01/1989, as per the Standard By-laws of the Local Authorities published in the Local Government Gazette No. 1093 of 13.08.1993 by an order of the Eastern Province Governor and based on the Management Committee Resolutions, all the following fees will be implemented from 01.01.2026 and any other fees not mentioned here will be levied according to the decisions of the Municipal Council, Kalmunai.
