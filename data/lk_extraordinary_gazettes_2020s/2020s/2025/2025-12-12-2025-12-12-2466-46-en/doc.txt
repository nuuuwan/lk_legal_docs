The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATARAGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Epa Kankanamge Nadeepa Asanthi Priyadarshani the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 7 Kataragama New Town of Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Rathnappullige Sanduni Nisansala to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Vithanage Don Jayan Lakmal, returning officer of the Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Rathnappullige Sanduni Nisansala was selected as the member of the Ward Number 7, Kataragama New Town of Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha. V. D. J. Lakmal, Returning Officer, Kataragama Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Moneragala,12th December, 2025.
