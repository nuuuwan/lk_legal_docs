THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

REVOCATION UNDER SECTION 39(1)

THE allotments of land speciﬁed morefully in the Order No. 315 of 2012 dated 18.12.2012 made under the Proviso (a) to Section 38 of the Land Acquisition Act and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1789/29 dated 19.12.2012 was vested in the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. The possession of the land depicted below has not yet been taken for and on behalf of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. Therefore, I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 39(1) of the Land Acquisition Act, do hereby revoke the Order only in respect of the lands speciﬁed in the Schedule of the Order No. 315 of 2012 dated 18.12.2012 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1789/29 dated 19.12.2012.

K. D. Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation.
