EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation

File No.: 4-3/5/2017/HA/537. Divisional Secretary's File No.: Ben/2/7/3/28.

I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of Land Acquisition Act as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the land/servitude to be acquired: The following allotment of land called "Adiyawatta" in total extent of 0.0547 Hectare (0:A 0:R 21.6:P) depicted as Lot No. 01 in C.M. No. 810912 of 25.08.2025 made by the Surveyor General situated in the Village of "Galgoda Gammedda" in the Grama Niladhari Division of 10A - Warakamulla, in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Bentota, District of Galle, Southern Province.

Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Adiyawatta; Current land use: Home Garden, Mix crop cultivation, a permanent building and a well; Claimant: Sri Vijaya Dharma Pirivena, Galgoda Gammedda, Maha Induruwa; Extent: 0.0547 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land on which servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat, Bentota on the 17th day of December, 2025 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 10th day of December, 2025 the nature of their interest in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

W. A. Janahitha, Divisional Secretary,(Covering Duties) Bentota. On 31st October, 2025 At Divisional Secretariat, Bentota. EOG 11 - 0127
