(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

L.D.-B 6/2017CIVIL AVIATION AUTHORITY OF SRI LANKA ACT, No. 34 OF 2002

Order under section 9

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 9 of the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No. 34 of 2002, I, Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, do by this Order amend the Order made under Section 9 of the aforesaid Act and Published in the Gazette Extraordinary, No.2142/74 dated September 26, 2019-

(a) by the substitution for the words, "the Jaffna International Airport", of the words "Palali Airport presently called and Known as the Jaffna International Airport", and

(b) by the repeal of the Schedule thereto and the substitution therefor, of the following: -

"SCHEDULE

All that divided and defined allotment of land Called the "Palali Airport presently called and known as the Jaffna International Airport depicted by Lots 1 to 456 in the Survey Plan PPA 1579 and situated within the Grama Niladhari divisions of Myladdy North, Myladdy South and Palali in Valikamam North of the Tellippalai Divisional Secretariat in the District of Jaffna, in the Northern Province, containing in extent Acres 349 - Roods 03 - Perches 35.9 together with the runway and the runway strip and the whole portion of the of the airport land located northwards abutting the runway.". Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation.

Colombo,1st September, 2025.
