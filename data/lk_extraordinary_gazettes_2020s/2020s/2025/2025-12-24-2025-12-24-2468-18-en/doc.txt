Central Bank of Sri Lanka Notices

CIVIL PROCEDURE CODE

Notification under Section 192

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of the powers vested in the Central Bank of Sri Lanka established under the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act, No. 16 of 2023, in terms of Section 192 of the Civil Procedure Code, as amended by the Civil Procedure Code (Amendment) Act, No. 06 of 1990, the Central Bank has, having regard to the current rates of bank interest, determined that with effect from 01st January 2026, the Legal Rate of Interest shall be eleven point three nine per centum (11.39%) per annum.

Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chairman of the Governing Board and Governor, Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

Central Bank of Sri Lanka,Colombo,19th December, 2025
