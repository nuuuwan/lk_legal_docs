FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

THE order under Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, 1, the Minister of Environment do by this Order declare the State land of the extent 450.58 Hectares located in the area bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedule 01 and 02 given below, as Kandeyaya Reserved Forest from 15 day of July 2025.

Dammika Patabendi, Minister of Environment.

At the Bajjangoda Open Air Theatre in Gampaha District,15th July, 2025.

FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

Notification

By virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 63 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009,1 do by this notification notify that the Kandeyaya Reserved Forest mentioned in the above order is subjected to the sole control of the Conservator General of Forests.

Dammika Patabendi, Minister of Environment.

At the Bajjangoda Open Air Theatre in Gampaha District,15th July, 2025.
