The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE KARANDENIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66B (2) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) AS AMENDED BY LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT No. 16 OF 2017

WHEREAS the office of Vice Chairman of Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Vice Chairman appointing as the Chairman;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya the recognized political party to which Ranasingha Hetti Arachchilage Saman Kumara Ranasingha the former Vice Chairman belonged, was required by me under Section 66B (2) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party who shall be declared elected as Vice Chairman within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Sameera Prasad Jayawardhana to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66B (2) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) as amended by Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2017 do hereby declare that Sameera Prasad Jayawardhana elected as Vice Chairman of the Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Galle,03rd December, 2025.
