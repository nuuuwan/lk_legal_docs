THE CENSUS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 143)

HIS Excellency the President and Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development has under Section 3 of the Census Ordinance (Chapter 143) appointed Mrs. Dematagodage Dona Gauri Anoja Seneviratne, Director General of Census and Statistics as superintendent of Census to superintend the taking of Economic Census (Agricultural Activities), directed to be taken in 2025/26 by order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2437/53 of 23rd May, 2025 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma Secretary, Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development

THE CENSUS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 143)

HIS Excellency the President as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development has under Section 4 of the Census Ordinance (Chapter 143) appointed the officer mentioned in Schedule I hereto as Deputy Superintendents of Census of Officer mentioned in Schedule II hereto as Assistant Superintendents of Census for the Economic Census (Agricultural Activities), directed to be taken in 2025/26 by the order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2437/53 of 23rd May, 2025 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Schedule I

All Additional Director Generals, All Directors and All Deputy Directors of the Department of Census and Statistics.

Schedule II

All Senior Statisticians and All Statisticians of the Department of Census and Statistics.

Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma Secretary, Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development

31st July, 2025.Ministry of Finance Planning and Economic Development.
