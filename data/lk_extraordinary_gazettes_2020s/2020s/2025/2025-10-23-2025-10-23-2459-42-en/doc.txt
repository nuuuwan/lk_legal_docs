The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KALPITIYA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Leader of the Independent Group -2, to which Haja Mohideen Mohamed Fairoos the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group -2 has communicated his decision to nominate Muhammadu Parook Muhammadu Fasli to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Siriniwasage Lakshitha Ruwan Jayanayake, Returning Officer of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Muhammadu Parook Muhammadu Fasli was selected as the member of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. S. L. R. Jayanayake, Returning Officer, Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Puttalam,23rd October, 2025.
