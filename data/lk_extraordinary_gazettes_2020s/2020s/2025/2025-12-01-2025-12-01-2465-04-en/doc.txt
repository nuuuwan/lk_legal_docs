PREVENTION OF OFFENCES RELATING TO SPORTS ACT, No. 24 OF 2019

Regulation made under Section 21

I, Sunil Kumara Gamage, the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports appoint Mr. M. P. C. C. De Silva, Assistant Superintendent of Police as the Director/ Head of the Special Investigation Unit of Prevention of Offences relating to Sports with effect from 15.09.2025 to 14.02.2026 for the unexpired term of office in terms of Section 21(8) of the Prevention of Offences in Sports Act, No. 24 of 2019 since Mr. W. A. Jayanath Wanasinghe, Assistant Superintendent of Police who was appointed as the Director/ Head of the Special Investigation Unit of Prevention of Offences relating to Sports by the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2345/11 dated 14.08.2023 in accordance with the Section 21 of the Prevention of Offences in Sports Act, No. 24 of 2019 is proceeding overseas and the Unit is hereby instructed to perform its duties in accordance with the powers and functions vested in it under this Act.

Sunil Kumara Gamage (M.P.) Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports.

At Colombo, 25th November 2025.
