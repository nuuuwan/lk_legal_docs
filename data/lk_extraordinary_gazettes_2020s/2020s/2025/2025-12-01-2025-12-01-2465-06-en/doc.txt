(2) Second Efficiency Bar

Officers should pass the Second Efficiency Bar Examination before expiry of three (03) years after being promoted to Grade II. Officers should submit a project report containing not less than 7,000 words on Teacher Education or Educational Management or a related subject area upon recommendation of the Board of the National Colleges of Education, recognized by the Secretary to the Ministry of Education

(3) Third Efficiency Bar

The Third Efficiency Bar of the Sri Lanka Teacher Educators Service shall be amended as follows:

Officers should have obtained a Postgraduate Diploma in Management or Postgraduate Diploma in Educational Management or a higher qualification, before the expiry of five (05) years after being promoted to Grade I. The said qualification shall not be a certificate that has been considered previously for recruitment or grade promotion purposes.

In addition to the above Efficiency Bar requirements, all officers should acquire such competencies and skills as may be determined by the Government from time to time.

Due to the removal of the existing Schedules “VIII”, “IX”, and “X”, the current Schedule “XI” shall be amended as Schedule “VIII”, and the current Schedule “XII” shall be amended as Schedule “IX”.
