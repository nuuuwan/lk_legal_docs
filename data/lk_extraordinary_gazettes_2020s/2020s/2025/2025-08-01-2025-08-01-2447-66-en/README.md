NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS

Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998

(Section 12)

NOTICE No. : 41/0425 PUTTALAM DISTRICT

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 410451 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Ihala Thabbowa (Block 07, Sheet No. 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.506 - Ihala Thabbowa in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Naththandiya in the District of Puttalam in the Province of North Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 11th of August 2025 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.

The Cadastral Map No. 410451 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office.

Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Settlement.

P.C.D. Sigera, Commissioner General of Land Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla, 11th July, 2025. EOG 08 - 0016/1
