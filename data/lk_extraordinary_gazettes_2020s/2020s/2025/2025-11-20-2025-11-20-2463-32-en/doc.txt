The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NATHTHANDIYA PRADESIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Naththandiya Pradesiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Warnakulasuriya Nalin Pasanna Fernando the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Naththandiya Pradesiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Meththasinghe Arachchige Janith Sanjeewa to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Siriniwasage Lakshitha Ruwan Jayanayake, returning officer of the Naththandiya Pradesiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Meththasinghe Arachchige Janith Sanjeewa was selected as the member of the Naththandiya Pradesiya Sabha. S. L. R. Jayanayake, Returning Officer, Naththandiya Pradesiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Puttalam,19th November, 2025.
