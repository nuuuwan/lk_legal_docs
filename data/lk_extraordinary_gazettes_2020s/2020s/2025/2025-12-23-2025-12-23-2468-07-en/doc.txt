NATIONAL CONSUMER PRICE INDEX COMPUTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF CENSUS AND STATISTICS

(Base : 2021 = 100) IT is hereby notified for general information that the National Consumer Price Index computed by the Department of Census and Statistics for the month of November 2025 was 207.2 (Two Hundred and Seven point Two).

K. M. D. S. D. Karunarathne, Director General of Census and Statistics.
