THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7 Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation

Ref. No.: 4-3/16/2018/H/35. Divisional Secretary’s Ref. No.: DS/VS/ACQ/

J-189.H/01. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose :

Description of the land or servitude to be acquired : Cadastral Map No. : CM 910210 Village : Inuvil G. N. Division : J/189 Inuvil East D.S. Division : Valikamam South, Uduvil District : Jaffna Province : Northern Purpose : To Construct Children Hospital 01. Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Kalvalaiyan Thoddamum Mullaikkaddaiyadium Alankaddaiyadium Pankara

Oddaiyum Kadduppulanaiyum Neeampathaium and other parcels; Description: Garden; Claimant: Not Known; Extent: 1.9344 Hectares.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/lands on which a servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat Valikamam South, Uduvil on 18th day of March, 2026, at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in three copies) on or before the 23rd day of February, 2026, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for computation of the said land/servitude the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

fg Premini Ponnampalam, Divisional Secretary, Valikamam South.

Divisional Secretariat of Valikamam South, Uduvil, 02nd December, 2025. EOG 12 - 0044
