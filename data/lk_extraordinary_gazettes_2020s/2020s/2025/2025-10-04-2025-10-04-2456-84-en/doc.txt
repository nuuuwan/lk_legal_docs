EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

My Ref. No.: LEI-CDP/SD/M/S/2-2. Land Ministry No.: 4-3/17/2021/LO/81. I hereby give public notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquire the following land for implementing Lunawa Environmental Enhancement and Community Development Project.

Description of the land to be acquired : Allotment of Land in extent of 0.0029 Hectare depicted in sheet No. 08 of the block No. 18 of the Cadastral Map No. 520210 dated 31.07.2025 prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in the Village Lakshapathiya, belong to No. 550, Lakshapathiya South Grama Niladhari Division in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Moratuwa in Colombo District of the Western Province described as follows :

Lot No. 230 Name of the Land: —; Present use of the land: Road; Claimant: State; Extent: 0.0029 Hectare.

All persons who claim ownerships, interest or any other connections to the above mentioned land/domain are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on 27.11.2025 at 09.30 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat Moratuwa.

Further hereby requested to notify me in writing (with two copies) the nature of their interest in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amounting on or before 13.11.2025. All deeds and relevant documents are support of claim should be provided at the inquiry.

fg S. P. Kiriwaththuduwa, Divisional Secretary, Moratuwa.

Divisional Secretariat of Moratuwa, 23rd September, 2025. EOG 10 - 0062
