PART III – LANDSPART III – LANDS

Land Acquisition Notices

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation do hereby declare, under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the land described below is needed for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

An allotment of land, in total extent of 2.7976 Hectares, depicted in the Advance Tracing No. GA/AKM/2015/527 dated 30.12.2015, prepared by the Surveyor-General, situated in the Village of Meththewela in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Akmeemana in the District of Galle in Southern Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot ‘A’ – Godadenikanda – Extent: 2.7976 Hectares North and East: Road and Godadenikanda (Keranketiya) Watta claimed by Land Reforms Commission; South: Road (Pradeshiya Sabha); West: Road (Pradeshiya Sabha) and Road.
