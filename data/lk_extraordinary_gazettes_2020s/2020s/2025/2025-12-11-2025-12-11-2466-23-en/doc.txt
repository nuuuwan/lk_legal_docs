The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KOTIKAWATTA MULLERIYAWA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of member of Kotikawatta Mulleriyawa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Mohamad Mihilar Neelar the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward number 15 Megoda Kolonnawa of Kotikawatta Mulleriyawa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Koswaththa Liyanage Sanjaya Madhushan to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Pakkiyarajah Ragunathan, Returning Officer of the Kotikawatta Mulleriyawa Pradeshiya Sabha , acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Koswaththa Liyanage Sanjaya Madhushan was elected as the member of the Ward number 15 Megoda Kolonnawa of Kotikawatta Mulleriyawa Pradeshiya Sabha. P. Ragunathan, Returning Officer, Kotikawatta Mulleriyawa Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Colombo,10th December, 2025.
