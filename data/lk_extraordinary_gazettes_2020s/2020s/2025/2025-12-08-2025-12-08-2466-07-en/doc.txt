(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KURUNEGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the oﬃ ce of member of Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya the recognized political party to which Ganegoda Appuhamillage Chamila Prasadini Rathnawardane the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 15 Bamunawala of Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabhawho belongs to such party and is not disqualiﬁ ed under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Wijekoon Mudiyanselage Maleesha Nishadi Rathnayaka to ﬁ ll such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayake returning oﬃ cer of the Kurunegala PradeshiyaSabha acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declarethat Wijekoon Mudiyanselage Maleesha Nishadi Rathnayaka was elected as the member of the 15 Bamunawala of Kurunegala Pradeshiya Sabha.
