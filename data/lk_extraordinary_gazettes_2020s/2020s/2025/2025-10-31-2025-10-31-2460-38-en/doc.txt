The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE HAMBANTOTA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Hambantota Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Herath Mudiyanselage Asiri Indika Herath the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of the Ward of 8 Alokapura of Hambantota Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Wanni Arachchi Kankanamge Chinthaka Priyadarshana to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Vithanage Don Jayan Lakmal, Returning Officer of the Hambantota Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Alokapura 08 was elected as the member of the Wanni Arachchi Kankanamge Chinthaka Priyadarshana was elected as the member of the ward of 8 Alokapura of Hambantota Municipal Council. V. D. J. Lakmal, Returning Officer, Hambantota Municipal Council. District Elections Office,Hambantota,31st October, 2025.
