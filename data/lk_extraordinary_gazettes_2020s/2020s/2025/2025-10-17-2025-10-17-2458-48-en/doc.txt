The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Local Government Notifications

PRADESHIYA SABHA KALPITIYA

The draft budget of Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha for the year 2026

IT is hereby notified that the draft budget document prepared by Pradeshiya Sabha Kalpitiya, under the Compilation and Enforcement of Pradeshiya Sabha Budget Rule No. 10 (2) to be read with Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987, published in the Gazette (Extraordinary) of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No. 2199/15 dated 29.10.2020 by the Honorable Governor of North Western Province has been made available to the public for inspection from 17th October 2025 to 29th October 2025 at the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha head office and Sub Offices during office hours.

A. S. M. Rikkas, Chairman, Pradeshiya Sabha Kalpitiya. At the office of Pradeshiya Sabha Kalpitiya,14th October 2025.
