By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 5 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, I do hereby order to waive-off the balance amount after collecting Rs. 10/kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 320/kg on importation of dehydrated coconut chips with outer brown testa (excluding copra) classified under HS Code of 0801.11.90 for the purpose of domestic production of coconut oil under the import licence requirement subject to recommendation of the Coconut Development Authority.
