THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My Ref. No. : HL/3/2/1/28. Tourism and Lands Ministry's Ref. No. : 03J/92/ FR/65. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land/servitude to be acquired : An Allotment of land in total extent of about 8.1300 Hectares depicted as Lot No. 1AF in the Topo P. P. No. 46, Inset 7 dated 03.02.2021 prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in the Village called Diyamigolla in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 154A of Kolongasthenna in Kandapalla Korale Minor Division in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Haldummulla in Badulla District of Uva Province and described as follows: Lot No. 1 AF Name of land: Eriyakatiya Kale; Description: Forest; Claimant: Amarasinghe Arachchige Don Hemapala Amarasinghe, Resident of No. 404, Rajamalwaththa, Moratuwa; Extent: 8.1300 Hectares.

Boundary Details : North By: Lot No. 1AG, 21, 1AE, 23, 25 1/2, and 25; East By: Lot No. 21, 1AE, 23, 25 1/2, 25 and 1; South by: Lot No. 1; West By: Lot No. 1AG Objective - To acquire the land called Eriyakatiya Kale Forest land for Forest conservation. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude are hereby required to appear personally or by agent s duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat Haldummulla on the 17th December, 2025 at 10.00 a.m and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 10th December, 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. K. A. J. Priyangika, Divisional Secretary, Haldummulla. At the Divisional Secretariat of Haldummulla, 22nd September, 2025 EOG 09 - 0236
