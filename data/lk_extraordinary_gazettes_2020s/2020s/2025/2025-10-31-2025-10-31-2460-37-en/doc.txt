The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATUWANA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Peduru Arachchige Sujith Muthukumarana the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Siriwardhanage Nishanka Rohana to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Vithanage Don Jayan Lakmal, Returning Officer of the Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Siriwardhanage Nishanka Rohana was selected as the member of the Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha. V. D. J. Lakmal, Returning Officer, Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Hambantota,31st October, 2025.
