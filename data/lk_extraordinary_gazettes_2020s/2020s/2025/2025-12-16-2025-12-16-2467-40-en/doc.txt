The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE SRI JAYAWARDENEPURA KOTTE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of return member of Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the United Republican Front the recognized political party to which Arachchige Rajitha Lalitha Kodithuwakku the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Nammuni Arachchi Hewa Welhenage Prasanna Ravindralal to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer of the Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Nammuni Arachchi Hewa Welhenage Prasanna Ravindralal was selected as the member of the Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council. Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer, Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council. Colombo City Election Office,16th December, 2025.
