(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MANMUNAI SOUTH ANDERUVIL PATTU PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Manmunai South and Eruvill Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of he has been ceased to be a member of the party;

And Whereas the Secretary of the Tamil Makkal Viduthalai Pulikal, the recognized political party to which Tharmareththinam Suthakaran the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Manmunai South and Eruvill Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha of Ward Number 09 Kottaikallar who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And Whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Nirojini Kalaivanan to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Manmunai South and Eruvill Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Nirojini Kalaivanan was elected as the member of the Ward Number 09 Kottaikallar of Manmunai South and Eruvill Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

N. Regan, Returning Officer, Manmunai South and Eruvill Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Batticaloa,17th November, 2025.
