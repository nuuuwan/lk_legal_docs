THE CENSUS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 143)

HON. President as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development has under Section 3 of the Census Ordinance (Chapter 143) appointed Mrs. Karunaratne Muhandiramlage Dona Shyamalee Deepika Karunaratne, Director General of Census and Statistics as Superintendent of Census to Superintend the taking of the Economic Census (Agricultural Activities), directed to be taken in 2025 and 2026 by order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2437/53 dated 23rd May, 2025 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma, Secretary, Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development,26th September, 2025.
