The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE DAMBULLA MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Dambulla Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to which Henarath Hetti Arachchige Jaliya Hemantha Opatha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned the member of Dambulla Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Rajaguru Rajakaruna Wasala Mudiyanselage Krishantha Madumal Hulangamuwa to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Hettige Asiri Eranga Pushpakumara, returning officer of the Dambulla Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Rajaguru Rajakaruna Wasala Mudiyanselage Krishantha Madumal Hulangamuwa was selected as the member of the Dambulla Municipal Council. H. A. E. Pushpakumara, Returning Officer, Dambulla Municipal Council. District Elections Office,Matale,31st October, 2025.
