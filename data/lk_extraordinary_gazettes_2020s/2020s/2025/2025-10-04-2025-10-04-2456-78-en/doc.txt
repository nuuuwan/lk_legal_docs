No. 2456/78 – SATURDAY, OCTOBER 04, 2025

EXTRAORDINARY

The Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460)

Notice under Section 7

Land Ministry’s Ref. No.: 4-3/18/2020/WS/238.

My No.: RUW/LAN/ACQTN/A2/264.

I hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Public Purpose : Acquisition of land for construction of Water Storage Tank under Water Scheme, Ruwanwella.

Description of the land to be acquired :- Allotment called Lot No. 01 in extent 0.1012 Hectare described below and depicted in Plan dated 06.08.2025 and in Zone No. 5, Sheet No. 1, Cadastral Map No. 610445, situated in the Village of Dewekkanda, in Grama Niladhari Division of No. 104 B, Mahalla in Dehigampal Korale in Divisional Secretary’s Division of Ruwanwella in the District of Kegaele in Sabaragamuwa Province.

01. Lot No. 1 Name of the land: Ruwanwella Estate, Original Claimant : Malwatta Valley Plantations PLC; Extent: 0.1012 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/Servitude are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat Ruwanwella on the 18th day of February, 2026 at 09.30 a.m.

Further, it is hereby notified me in writing (in duplicated) before 02nd day of February, 2026 regarding the nature of their interests in the land/lands, particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. Deeds and other documents for claims should be submitted at the examination date.

H. D. S. N. Hettige, Divisional Secretary, Ruwanwella. At Divisional Secretariat, Ruwanwella, 30th September, 2025. EOG 10 - 0054
