SRI LANKA RAILWAY DEPARTMENT

Office of General Manager - Railways

CONDUCTING THE SPECIAL ELECTION OF OFFICE - BEARERS AS PER THE CEYLON RAILWAYDAILY PAID WORKERS' BENEVOLENT ASSOCIATION ACT BEARING THE No. 03 OF 1965

IN accordance with the powers vested in the Secretary to the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development under the Section 30(A) 1 of the Ceylon Railway Daily Paid Workers’ Benevolent Association Act bearing the No. 03 of 1965, the Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 2429/36, issued on 27.03.2025, is hereby revoked and the approval has been granted to appoint the board of new office-bearers through the letter of the Secretary to the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development bearing the No. MT/03/05/21/17-1-1 Volume (තාව) and dated 17.11.2025.

02. Accordingly, it is notified that it is as follows the board of the officers elected by the members’ votes at the 49th special election of the office-bearers, held on 21.09.2025.

Chairman - Mr. L. H. Kamal Priyantha Silva

Vice President - Mr. T. M. Nishantha

Secretary - Mr. J. M. Sumedha Somarathne

Treasurer - Mr. D. Prabhath Kumara Silva
