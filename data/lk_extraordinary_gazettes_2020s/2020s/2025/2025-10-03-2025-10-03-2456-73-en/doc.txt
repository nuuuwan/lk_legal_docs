THE WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE TEA GROWING AND MANUFACTURING TRADE

IT is hereby notified, under regulation 30 of the Wages Board Ordinance (Chapter 136) that, in terms of section 9 of the said Ordinance, the Hon. Minister of Labour has appointed Mr. Vikum Pradeepa, Mr. Prasanna Wickramanayaka and Mr. Gandika Weligamage as Nominated members of the wages Board for the Tea Growing and Manufacturing Trade in place of Mr. Malaka Weerasinghe Arachchige, Mr. Gayan Ovitigala and Mr. V. Jeewanandaraja respectively, and Mr. Binesh Niyomal Pananwala and Mr. D.B.S.L Perera have been appointed as Employer representatives in place of Mr. B. Bulumulla and Mr. Pradeep Hemantha Bandara Liyanagedara respectively, and Mr. Rengasami Suresh Kumar has been appointed as Employee representative in place of Mr. Kitnan Selvaraj for the said wages board. This appointment is valid only up to 31.12.2026.

S. M. Piyatissa, Secretary, Ministry of Labour.

“Mehewara Piyesa”, Colombo 05, 02nd October 2025.

Notification

WAGES BOARD FOR THE RUBBER GROWING AND RAW RUBBER PROCESSING TRADE

S. M. Piyatissa, Secretary, Ministry of Labour.
