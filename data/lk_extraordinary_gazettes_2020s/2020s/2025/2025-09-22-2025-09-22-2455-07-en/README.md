THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, NO. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1) (b) read with Sections 25A and 70C

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) (b) read with Sections 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act in respect of the National School of Business Management. (Guarantee) Limited, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 07 of 2007 and bearing registration number GL 00303869, do by this Order, grant the degree awarding status, for the purpose of developing higher education leading to the conferment of Degrees and Postgraduate Diplomas specified in Column I of the Schedule I hereto of which the abbreviations are set out in the corresponding entries in Column II and subject to the conditions specified in Column III of the Schedule I hereto, to the National School of Business Management. (Guarantee) Limited, which is owned by the National School of Business Management Limited, in respect of which Degrees and Diplomas the awarding status had been granted to the National School of Business Management Limited by Orders published in Gazettes Extraordinary, specified in Schedule II hereto.
