Local Government Notifications

No. 2459/54 – SATURDAY, OCTOBER 25, 2025

PRADESHIYA SABHA - RAJANGANAYA

IT is hereby published to notify the public that a resolution has been passed at the Sabha meeting No. 2025/137 held on 09th October 2025 by declaring as the roads and lanes belongs to Pradeshiya Sabha ,that are included in the Schedule hereby as per the task assigned to Pradeshiya Sabha of North Central Province by the set of ‘Orders to publish the register of roads and lanes and preparation of mapping of roads and lanes maintained by Pradeshiya Sabha of North Central Province that have been Published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2449/15 dated 13th of August 2025 by Hon. Governor of North Central Province that shall be read with the Section No. 24 of Pradeshiya Sabha Act, No. 15 of 1987. It is hereby notified that if any party is on the opinion that any road or lane mentioned in the said Schedule falls through any land or part of any land, he shall, within three months from the date of this notification, institute proceedings in the appropriate court to establish his right of the land.

It is further notified that every road or lane mentioned in the said Schedule for which no such proceedings have been instituted before the expiration of three months shall be deemed to be a road or lane belongs to the Rajanganaya Pradeshiya Sabha.

R.M.I.C.B. Randeniya, Chairman, Rajanganaya Pradeshiya Sabha.

At the Rajanganaya Pradeshiya Sabha Office,13th October, 2025.
