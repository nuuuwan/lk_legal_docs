PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

1A- PG 7392 - 08 (12/2025) This Gazette Extraordinary can be downloaded from www.documents.gov.lk

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE Baddegama Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Wewelwala Hewage Raveendra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Baddegama Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Amal Indika Udawaththa of the Baddegama Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of the member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has been ceased to be a member of the Sarvajana Balaya.

W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Baddegama Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Galle,08th of December, 2025.
