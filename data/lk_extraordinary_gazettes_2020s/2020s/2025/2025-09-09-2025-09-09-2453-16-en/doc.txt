PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION OF SRI LANKA

Publishing of Revised Expenditure Budget - 2025

Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka was established under the Public Utilities Commission Act. No. 35 of 2002. As per the requirements stipulated under the section 33 (2) and 33 (3) of the said Act, the estimated expenditure of the next year and any amendments to the original budget to be published in the Government Gazette by the Commission. Accordingly, the Commission has approved certain revisions to the original budget and forward herewith the revised expenditure budget 2025.

1. Electricity Sector Rs. 493,047,374

Outcome :

1. Increase the affordability of electricity Rs. 66,379,454 2. Ensure Reasonable Electricity Charges Rs. 83,020,842 3. Improve the efficiency of the electricity industry Rs. 106,595,957 4. Improve the convenience of consumers Rs. 98,618,347 5. Improved safety for life and property Rs. 31,396,760 6. Enhanced Electric Vehicle charging infrastructure Rs. 29,250,116 7. Enhance Renewable Energy integration to achieve 70% Renewable Energy target Rs. 38,725,265 8. Corporate reports and information dissemination Rs. 39,060,633

2. Petroleum Sector Rs. 39,587,446

3. Water Sector Rs. 7,342,901

Total Budget Rs. 539,977,721
