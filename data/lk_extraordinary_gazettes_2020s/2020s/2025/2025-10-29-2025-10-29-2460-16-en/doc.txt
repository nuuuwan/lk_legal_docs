FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHARA PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of the Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Koonpiti Kankanamalage Ishan Kasun Umesh Perera the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Ranepura Dewage Manjula Saman Suraweera to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ranepura Dewage Manjula Saman Suraweera was selected as the member of the Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha.

W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Mahara Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Gampaha,29th October, 2025.
