Order No.: 95

CONSUMER AFFAIRS AUTHORITY ACT, No. 9 OF 2003 (AMENDED)

Order under Section 20(5)

ACTING under the powers vested in it by Section 20 (5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No 9. of 2003, the Consumer Affairs Authority orders that no local Manufacturer, Packer, Distributor or Trader shall Sell, Expose or Offer for Sale, Display for sale the types of canned fish listed below, above the Maximum Retail Price given here under.

Canned Fish TypeWeight(Gram)Maximum Retail Price(Per Can)

Tuna (Katsuwonus pelamis, Auxis rochei, Euthynnus affinis and Auxis thazard)

Net weight of 155 g and drained weight of 105g or aboveRs. 165/-

Net weight of 425 g and drained weight of 280g or aboveRs. 380/-
