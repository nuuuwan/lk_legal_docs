Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Koralai Pattu West Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Koralai Pattu West Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that, Mr. Mohamed Haniffa Mohamed Fairoos of the Koralai Pattu West Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has been ceased to be a member of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya.

N. Regan, Returning Officer, Koralai Pattu West Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Batticaloa,24th October, 2025.
