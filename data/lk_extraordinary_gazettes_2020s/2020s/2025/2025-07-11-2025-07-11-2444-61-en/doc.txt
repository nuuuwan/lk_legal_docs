REVISION OF ELECTORAL REGISTERS - 2025

Notice of the Election Commission on the Display of “A” and “B” Lists, Claims and Objections

Definition

1. “Act” - Registration of Electors Act, No. 44 of 1980 as amended by the Registration of Electors

(Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2021

2. Valid Electoral Register - The main register of the year 2024 and supplementary electoral registers 2024(1), 2024(2) and 2024(3)

3. “ ‘A’ List” - List of names of persons whose names appear in the operative electoral register but are

ineligible due to death or leaving the residence or for any other reason namely, persons who are not eligible to be in the valid electoral register and to be removed from the address

4. “ ‘B’ List” - List of persons whose names are not included in the eligible address in the operative

electoral register but are eligible for inclusion in the electoral register for the year 2025 either on completion of 18 years of age or residence or for any other reason

Instructions

01. On behalf of the Registering Officer of each district, it is hereby notified that the Registering Officers mentioned in

the Column II of the Schedule below for the electoral district mentioned in the corresponding Column I of the said Schedule have notified on 11.07.2025 that the preparation of Lists “A” and “B” has been completed and that the said lists or parts thereof are placed at the aforementioned exhibition centers for inspection by the public during office hours, as per Section 13 of the Registration of Electors Act.

02. Every person who is qualified in terms of Section 14 of the Registration of Electors Act to have his own name or

someone else’s name entered in the said electoral register applicable to each electoral district and whose name has been omitted from the electoral register and from List “B” or whose name has been included in List “A” may claim by himself or by someone else on his behalf, as the case may be, substantially in form “A” and “B” set out in the Schedule to above Registration of Electors Act to have his or someone else’s name entered or retained in the register, and submit it to reach the registering officer of each electoral district on or before August 07, 2025, within 4 weeks from the date of the publication of this notice in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka i.e. from July 11, 2025.

03. Any person whose name appears in the electoral register or in list “B” for any electoral district may object to the entry

of his own name or to the entry or retention of the name of any other person appearing in such register or list “B”. Every such objection shall be made in duplicate and shall be substantially in form “C” set out in the schedule to the above Act and shall be made so as to reach the registering officer of the Election Office. All objections to the inclusion of the name in the register should be sent to the registering officer of the District Elections Office on or before August 07, 2025, within 4 weeks from the date of the publication of this notice in the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka i.e. from July 11, 2025.

04. Every objection to the entry of the name of any claimant in the electoral register shall be made in duplicate and shall

be substantially in form “D” set out in the schedule to the above Act and shall be made so as to reach the registering officer within 14 days from the date on which the notice containing the name of claimants is exhibited at the District Election Office. This notice will be published in the District Election Offices on July 25, 2025 and August 11, 2025.
