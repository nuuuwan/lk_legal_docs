The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MINUWANGODA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of the death;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Hewathanthrige Sugath Chandramal Pieris the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the local authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Loku Balasuriyage Nimal Siripala to fill such vacancy;

Now Therefore I, W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, returning officer of the Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Loku Balasuriyage Nimal Siripala was selected as the member of the Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Gampaha,25th August, 2025.
