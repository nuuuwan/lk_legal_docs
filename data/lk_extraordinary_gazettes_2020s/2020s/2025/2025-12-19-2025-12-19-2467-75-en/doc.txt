6.2 Combined Carder: 6839

For the purpose of grading promotions, Grade III, II, I and Special Grades shall be treated as belonging to combined cadre.

7.2.2.1 Educational Qualiﬁcations

Shall have passed six subjects in G.C.E. Ordinary Level with at least two credits including Sinhala/ Tamil language at not more than two sittings.

7.2.2.2 Professional Qualiﬁcations

(1) Shall possess a valid driving license for at least ‘C’ and ‘Cl’ vehicle classes according to the old driving license classes issued by the Commissioner General of Motor Trafﬁc for driving motor cars and dual-purpose vehicles, or a valid driving license for at least ‘B’ class according to the new driving license, obtained at least 03 years prior to the date of recruitment.

7.2.2.3 Experience

Shall possess three years of experience as a vehicle driver after obtaining the driving license. (Experience shall be veriﬁed by certiﬁcates.)

7.2.4 Method of Recruitment

I. By means of a written examination, a trade test, and a general interview.II. The marks obtained in the written examination shall not be added to the marks of the trade test.III. A number of candidates equal to the number of vacancies shall be called for the trade test.

7.2.4.1 Written Examination

I. The written examination shall be conducted by an institution nominated by the Commissioner General of Examinations or the Appointing Authority.

II. The written examination consists of 02 question papers, namely the Aptitude Test and the General Question Paper.

III. Aptitude Test - A question paper consisting of 50 multiple-choice questions shall be provided, to be answered within a duration of 01 hour. Out of a total of 100 marks for the paper, a minimum of 40 marks or above must be obtained to pass.

IV. General Question Paper - A question paper of 01 hour duration, consisting of multiple-choice and short-answer questions, will be provided to test the General Knowledge, knowledge on Road Rules, Road Signs, and Motor Mechanism. Out of a total of 100 marks for the paper, a minimum of 40 marks or above must be obtained to pass.

V. To pass the written examination, a candidate must obtain 40 marks or above for each of the two question papers mentioned above.

VI. Candidates who obtain the highest marks in the written examination will qualify for the trade test, based on the district population ratio and the number of available vacancies.
