The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE NINTHAVUR PRADESHIYASABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to be a member of the party;

And whereas the Secretary of the All Ceylon Makkal Congress, the recognized political party to which Atham Bawa Asfer the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward number 06 Nellithivu Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha of who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Kuduvutheen Mohamed Jaleel to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Aththanayaka Mudiyanselage Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer of the Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Kuduvutheen Mohamed Jaleel was elected as the member of the Ward number 06 Nellithivu of Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha. Kasun Srinath Aththanayaka, Returning Officer, Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Ampara,10th November, 2025.
