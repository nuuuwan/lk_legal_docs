THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1) read with Section 21

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) read with Section 21 of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 420/25 of September 26, 1986 (as amended from time to time) which specifies the Faculties and Departments of the Eastern University, Sri Lanka, by the repeal of the item “Faculty of Arts and Culture” appearing in Column I of the Schedule and its corresponding entries in Column II of the Schedule (specified in the Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1523/16 of November 14, 2007) and the substitution therefore of the following :-
