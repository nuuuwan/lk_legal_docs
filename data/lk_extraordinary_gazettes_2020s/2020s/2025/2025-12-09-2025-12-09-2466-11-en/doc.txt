Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

DEPARTMENT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT - NORTHERN PROVINCE

Notice for meeting under the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

I, Sambasivam Sutharsan, Commissioner of Local Government, Northern Province by virtue of powers vested to me under Sub Section No. 66(G) of Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, hereby notify to convene the meeting for the election to fill the vacant of Chairman of Maritimepattu Pradeshiya Sabha in Northern Province on 22.12.2025, 11.00 a.m. at Council hall of the Maritimepattu Pradeshiya Sabha.

Sambasivam Sutharsan, Commissioner of Local Government, Northern Province. Department of Local Government,Northern Province,Elders Home Complex,A9 Road, Kaithady, Jaffna.08th December, 2025.
