THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

,, Notice under Section 7

Ref No.: KADU/LAND/01/144/WS. I hereby give notice, under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as Amendment by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the following land/ Servitude has been transferred to the Government for a public purpose.

Description of the Land/ Servitude transferred to the Government: All that allotment of land depicted as lot No. 01 in Sheet No. 01, block No. 01 in the Cadastral Map No. 522011 dated 18.06.2025, prepared by the Surveyor General, situated at the Village of Raggahawatta in No. 474/A, Raggahawatta Grama Niladhari Division, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kaduwela in Colombo District, Western province and bounded as follows.

01. Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Dissawatta alias Katugampalayaowita, Godellawatta, Raggahawatta alias Thimbirigahawatta; Land Usage: Home Garden, a permenant building and a part of a

temporary building is available; Extent : 0.5514 Ha. Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Mr. Manjula Palihena, Mr. P.D. Nalaka Palihena, Mrs. Shyamali Palihena. All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connections to the aforesaid land/ servitude hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on 16.10.2025 at 9.30 a.m. at he Divisional Secretariat, Kaduwela. And Further you are hereby requested to notify me in writing in duplicate before 13.10.2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

H.S. Sampath Idamgodage, Divisional Secretary, Kaduwela .

At Divisional Secretariat, Kaduwela, 30th September 2025.
