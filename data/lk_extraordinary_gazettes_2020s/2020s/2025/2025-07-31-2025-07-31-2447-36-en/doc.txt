FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE

THE order under Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009.

The Order

By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 3 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009, I, the Minister of Environment do by this Order declare the state land of the extent 1.36 Hectares located in the area bounded by the limits set forth in the Schedule given below, as Merawala 3 Mangrove Reserved Forest from 26th day of July 2025.

Dammika Patabendi,Minister of Environment.

At Karamba, Kalpitiya lagoon belt,26th July, 2025.

Notification

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 63 of the Forest Conservation Ordinance (Chapter 451) No. 16 of 1907, as amended by Act, No. 13 of 1966 (reprint), No. 56 of 1979, No. 13 of 1982, No. 84 of 1988, No. 23 of 1995 and No. 65 of 2009,1 do by this notification notify that the Merawala 3 Mangrove Reserved Forest mentioned in the above order is subjected to the sole control of the Conservator General of Forests.
