REGULATIONS MADE BY THE MINISTER OF YOUTH AFFAIRS AND SPORTS UNDER SECTIONS 32 AND 33 OF SPORTS LAW No. 25 OF 1973 AS AMENDED.

I, Sunil Kumara Gamage, Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports, in terms of the powers vested in me under Sections 32 and 33 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973, as amended, do hereby direct as follows;

1. I temporarily suspend the registration of Karate Do by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 32 and 33 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973 and its amendments.

2. By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 33 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973, as amended, I hereby appoint a Working Task Force consisting of the following members for the limited purpose of adopting the new Constitution of Karate Do, registering new eligible members of Karate Do, and conducting the Special General Meeting (SGM) and the Annual General Meeting (AGM) in accordance with the provisions of the said new Constitution.

1. Mr. M. H. Marso, Deputy Inspector General of Police (Retired)– Chairman

2. Mr. Niluka Karunaratne, Representative of National Olympic Committee

3. Mr. R. M. L. N. Bandara, Senior Superintendent of Police (Rtd.)

4. Mr. P. Upul Abewickrama

5. Mr. M. H. Ananda Kumara, Attorney at Law

Sunil Kumara Gamage (M.P.), Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports.
