The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE YAKKALAMULLA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Yakkalamulla Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Nishantha Kumara Sanduni Nisansala the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned the member of that Yakkalamulla Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Kananke Pathiranage Chandrika Damayanthi to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, returning officer of the Yakkalamulla Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Kananke Pathiranage Chandrika Damayanthi was selected as the return member of the Yakkalamulla Pradesiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Yakkalamulla Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Galle,17th November, 2025.
