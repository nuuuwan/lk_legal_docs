(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION (GENERAL) Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235)

RATES OF EXCHANGE

IT is hereby notified that by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 17 of the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235) I, Seevali Padmashantha Arukgoda, Director General of Customs, determine that with effect from 24.11.2025, all duties of Customs as well as other charges, penalties and forfeitures incurred under the Customs Ordinance (Chapter 235), shall be paid and received at the Rates of Exchange set out in the Schedule.

The notification relating to the Rates of Exchange published in Gazette No. 2463/01 of 17.11.2025 is hereby rescinded.

Seevali Padmashantha Arukgoda, Director General of Customs.

Sri Lanka Customs,Colombo 11,21st November, 2025.
