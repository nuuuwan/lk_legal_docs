The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BULATHSINHALA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of Return member of Bulathsinhala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the United National Party, the recognized political party to which Udumullage Raveendra Kumara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Bulathsinhala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Ranthatige Nishshanka Priyadharshana to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Wannakuwatta Waduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the Bulathsinhala Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ranthatige Nishshanka Priyadharshana was selected as the member of the Bulathsinhala Pradeshiya Sabha. Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer, Bulathsinhala Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kalutara.03rd December, 2025.
