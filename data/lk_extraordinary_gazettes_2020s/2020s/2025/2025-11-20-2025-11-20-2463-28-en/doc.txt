DECISION

As per the powers vested by Sub-section 2(1) of the Entertainment Ordinance, No. 12 of 1946, as amended by the Entertainment Tax (Amendment) Ordinance No. 37 of 1984, I, Secretary of the Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, have decided according to Decision No. 59 dated 23.01.2024 to impose and recover tax an amount equal to 10% (ten percent) of the payment made for any entertainment function described in the aforementioned Ordinance conducted in the area within the administrative limits of the Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, should be imposed as a tax and this decision should take eﬀ ect from the ﬁ rst day of the month following the month in which this proposed rule is published in the Gazette and I also decide that the relevant organizer or organizers should pay the appropriate amount to Kamburupitiya Pradeshiya Sabha before the date of the event of the entertainment function.
