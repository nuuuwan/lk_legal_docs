(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GOMARANKADAWALA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Gomarankadawala Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Rangika Pragad Dharmadasa the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 05 Galkadawala of Gomarankadawala Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Danapalage Damayanthi to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Selladhore Kaliuganathan Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer of the Gomarankadawala Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Danapalage Damayanthi was elected as the member of the Ward Number 05, Galkadawala of Gomarankadawala Pradeshiya Sabha.

S. K. Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer, Gomarankadawala Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Trincomalee,23rd December, 2025.
