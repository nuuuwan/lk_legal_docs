PROVINCIAL COUNCIL NOTIFICATION OF NORTHERN PROVINCE

Co-operative Societies Law No. 5 of 1972 as amended by Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 1983 and the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No. 11 of 1992 read in conjunction with Provincial Council (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

Conferment of Power

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by the Co-operative Societies Law No.5 of 1972 as amended by the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act. No.32 of 1983 and the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No.11 of 1992, read in conjunction with Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989. I, Mr. N.Vethanayahan, Governor of Northern Province, do hereby confer Miss. Segarajah Ahalya has been appointed as the Commissioner of Co-operative Development and Registrar of Co-opera tive Societies of the Northern Province to exercise such powers of the Registrar under the Co-operative Societies Law No.5 of 1972 as amended by Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No.32 of 1983 and the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, No.11 of 1992 and the rules there under in respect of the Northern Province, with effect from 25.08.2025 to be exercised by the aforesaid during her tenure of office.

N. Vethanayahan Governor, Northern Province.
