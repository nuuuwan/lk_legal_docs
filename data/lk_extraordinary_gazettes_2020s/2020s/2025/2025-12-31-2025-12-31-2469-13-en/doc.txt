FOOD ACT, No. 26 OF 1980

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media, in consultation with the Food Advisory Committee under Section 32 of the Food Act, No. 26 of 1980.

Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa, Minister of Health and Mass Media.

Colombo, 29th December, 2025.

The Food (Labelling and Advertising) Regulations, 2022, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2319/40 dated February 14, 2023, as amended by Gazette Extraordinary No. 2364/24 dated December 27, 2023, Gazette Extraordinary No. 2416/51 dated December 28, 2024, Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/06 dated June 30, 2025 and Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/27 dated July 01, 2025 are hereby further amended.

1. By repealing regulation 1(ii) of the aforementioned regulations, the following regulation shall be substituted: “ 1. (ii) Regulations 2 to 13(15) and Regulation 15, together with Schedules I to IX, shall come into operation on 1st July 2026.”
