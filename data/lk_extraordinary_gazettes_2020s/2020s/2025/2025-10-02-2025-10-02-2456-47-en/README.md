NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 16 (2D) OF THE COASTCONSERVATION AND COASTAL RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT. No. 57 OF 1981

Proposed 107 Units of Condominium Apartment Building and Separate Non - Condominium 17 VillaHouses Project at Mudillagahawatta, Kudawaskaduwa, Kalutara

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT REPORT

IT is hereby notified that the Report of Environmental Impact Assessment study on proposed 107 Units of Condominium Apartment Building and Separate Non - Condominium 17 Villa Houses Project at Mudillagahawatta, Kudawaskaduwa, Kalutara has been submitted by the Home Land Skyline (Pvt.)
