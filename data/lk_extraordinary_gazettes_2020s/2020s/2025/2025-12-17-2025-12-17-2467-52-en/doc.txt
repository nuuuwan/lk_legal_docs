L.D.-B 24/51(VII)

MOTOR TRAFFIC ACT (CHAPTER 203)

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development under section 237 read with Section 126 of the Motor Traffic Act (Chapter 203).

Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.Colombo,17th December 2025.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Motor Traffic (Temporary Extension of the Period of Validity of Driving Licences) Regulations, No. 04 of 2025.

2. The period of validity of any Driving Licence issued by the Commissioner General of Motor Traffic under Part VII of the Motor Traffic Act (Chapter 203) which is due to expire on a date during the period commencing from November 25, 2025 and ending on December 24, 2025, as specified in such Driving Licence, shall hereby be extended for one month from the date of expiry of the period of validity of the relevant Driving Licence.
