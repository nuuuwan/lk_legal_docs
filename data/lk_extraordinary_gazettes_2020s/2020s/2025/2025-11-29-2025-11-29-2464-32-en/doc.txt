Emergency (Miscellaneous Provisions and Powers) Regulations, No.1 of 2025

Appointment of Commissioner-General of Essential Services By virtue of the powers vested in me by paragraph (1) of Regulation 11 of the Emergency (Miscellaneous Provisions and Powers) Regulations, No.1 of 2025, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President, do hereby appoint Mr. B.K. Prabath Chandrakeerthi, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service to be the Commissioner-General of Essential Services for the whole of Sri Lanka to execute and coordinate all activities relating to the supply and maintenance of essential services with immediate effect. Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President. Colombo,29th of November 2025.
