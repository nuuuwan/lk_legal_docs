Local Government Notifications

PANDUWASNUWARA PRADESHIYA SABHA

Inspection of the Draft Budget statement year 2026

THE general public within the area of authority of the Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha is hereby informed that the Draft Budget of the Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha for the year 2026 is on display at the Pradeshiya Sabha office for inspection on working days of the week from 9.00 a.m. until 3.00 p.m.

Y. G. Sanjeewa Karunasagara, Chairman, Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha.

Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha,Hettipola,06th November 2025. EOG 11 - 0037
