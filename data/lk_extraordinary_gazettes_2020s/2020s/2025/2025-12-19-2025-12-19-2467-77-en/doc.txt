NOTIFICATION

I hereby notify that the regulations specified in the following Gazette Notifications, promulgated in terms of the powers vested with the Hon. Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development by Sections 4(1), 14 and 20 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 as amended by Act, No. 48 of 1985 and Act, No. 28 of 1987, were duly passed in Parliament.

Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma, Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development,Colombo 01,December 19, 2025.

Regulations passed in Parliament

1. The Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 02 of 2025, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2421/44 issued on January 31, 2025, allowing the importation of motor vehicles into Sri Lanka, were passed in Parliament on March 20, 2025.

2. The Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 03 of 2025, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2428/07 issued on March 19, 2025, regarding the submission of Export Inspection Certificates relating to the importation of motor vehicles, were passed in Parliament on May 22, 2025.
