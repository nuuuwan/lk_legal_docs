1A – PG7102 – 05 (05/2025)

MONERAGALA PRADESHIYA SABHA

THE Hon. Governor of Uva Province, read with Section 24 of the Provincial Councils Act, No. 15 of 1987, has published "Preparation and List of Roads and Narrow Roads maintained by the Local Councils of Uva Province" published by the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2318/41 dated 10th February 2023 and No. 2414/13 dated 11th December 2024. It is hereby notiﬁ ed to inform the public that in accordance with the provisions of Section 9:3 of the Provincial Councils Act No. 15 of 1987, it has been decided under Section 9:3 of the Local Councils Act, No. 15 of 1987 that the roads and narrow roads included in the following Sub Document should be declared as roads and narrow roads belonging to the Moneragala Pradeshiya Sabha in decided under No. 217 dated 26th March 2025 accordance with the functions assigned to the Local Councils of Uva Province in the series.

If the claimant is of the opinion that any road or narrow road included in the said Schedule will be included through any land or part of any land, it is hereby announced that a case should be ﬁ led in the appropriate Court before the expiry of three months from the date of this notiﬁ cation to prove its ownership of the land.

It is further announced that every road and narrow road included in the schedule where No such case has been registered before the expiry of the said three months period shall be treated as a road or narrow road owned by the Moneragala Pradeshiya Sabha.
