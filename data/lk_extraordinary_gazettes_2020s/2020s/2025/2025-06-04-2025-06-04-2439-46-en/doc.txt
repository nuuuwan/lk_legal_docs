Local Government Notifications

THIS IS THE NOTICE MADE UNDER SECTION 66C OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE AS AMENDED BY THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT No. 22 OF 2012

I, Lekam Mudiyanselage Pragnakeerthi Wijesundara Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province, by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 66C of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance as amended by the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, hereby announce that the first meeting for the puropose of electing the Mayor/ Chairman, Deputy Mayor/ Vice-Chairman for the Local Governments mentioned in the schedule situated in the Sabaragamuwa Province will be convened on the date and at the time and place mentioned in the said schedule.

L. M. P. W. Bandara,Commissioner of Local Government,Sabaragamuwa Province.

at the Department of Local Government - Sabaragamuwa Province,New Town, Ratnapura.On this 4th of June 2025.
