WAGES BOARDS ORDINANCE

Notification

APPOINTMENT OF A SECRETARY FOR THE WAGES BOARDS

IT is hereby notified that the Minister of Labour has appointed Mr. Hetti Arachchige Ajith Priyantha, Labour Officer, as the joint secretary, until further orders, for the Wages Boards of the following trades established under the Section 8 of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136).

It is further notified that this appointment will be effective from 30th October 2025.

S. M. Piyatissa, Secretary, Ministry of Labour.

Colombo, 30th October 2025.
