SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Second Supplementary Financial Statement 2025

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27 (A) OF PROVINCIAL COUNCIL ACT NO. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26TH SEPTEMBER, 2017.2025

1. This permission may be cited as “Permission to disburse under the Second supplementary financial statement - 2025 of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council” and come in to operation from 30.07.2025.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Two Billion Two Hundred Eight Million Fifty-five Thousand Eighty Two and Twelve Cents only (Rs. 62,208,055,082.12) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2025 and ending 31st December, 2025, which is specified as “Financial Year 2025” in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue and Expenditure Estimate for 2025 approvd on 03.04.2025, as amended by the First Supplementary Estimate for 2025 is hereby amended as, Rupees Sixty Two Billion Five Hundred Twenty Two Million Five Hundred Seventy Eight Thousand Seven Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents only (Rs. 62,522,578,739.45) and grant permission to the Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial council Fund.

3. Accordingly the first schedule of the Annual Financial statement and revenue - expenditure estimates - 2025, is amended as the first schedule here of and grant permission to disburse the same.

AUTHORIZATION

I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse as per the Second Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2025, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Two Billion Two Hundred Eight Million Fifty Five Thousand Eighty Two and Twelve Cents only (Rs. 62,208,055,082.12) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2025 and ending 31st December, 2025, which is specified in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue and Expenditure Estimates for the year 2025, is amended as Sixty Two Billion Five Hundred Twenty Two Million Five Hundred Seventy Eight Thousand Seven Hundred Thirty Nine and Forty Five Cents (Rs. 62,522,578,739.45) and grant permission to disbursse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2025 or upto the date that expires three months (03) from the date of summoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which occurs first.

A. A. C. J. Rajarathna,Hon. Governor, Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council.30th day of July, 2025.
