The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MINUWANGODA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Kaththota Ralalage Hemantha Chandrasiri the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward number 25 Dombawala of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Ranasinghe Arachcige Janaka Chaminda Ranasinghe to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ranasinghe Arachcige Janaka Chaminda Ranasinghe was elected as the member of the ward Number 25 Dombawala of Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Minuwangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Gampaha,13th October, 2025.
