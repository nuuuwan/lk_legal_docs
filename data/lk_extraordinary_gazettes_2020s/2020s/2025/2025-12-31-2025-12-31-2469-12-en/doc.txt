2. By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 5 of the Special Commodity Levy Act No. 48 of 2007, as listed out in the Schedule above, I, do hereby order to waive - off;

(i) balance amount after collecting Rs, 1 per Kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 200 per Kg levied on importation of Dates classified under the HS Code of 0804.10.10 and 0804.10.20, when Dates to be received as donations or gifts on no foreign exchange basis and not for commercial purposes from any foreign government, organization including business entities, volunteer associations and well-wishers on the recommendation of the Secretary, Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs pursuant to the decision of the Cabinet of Ministers dated December 29, 2025 on the Cabinet Paper No. 25/2420/822/059 titled "Obtaining tax concessions on imported Dates for the free distribution during Ramadan fasting period ". This Order shall be valid for a period commencing from January, 01, 2026 to March 20, 2026 subject to provisions of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

(ii) balance amount after collecting Rs. 6 per kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of 10% or Rs. 400 per kg on importation of Mackerel (Scomber scombrus, scomber australasicus, scombe japanicus) classified under the HS Code of 0303.54 and Jack and horse mackerel (Trachurus Spp.) classified under the HS Code of 0303.55 classified under HS Heading of 03.03 for production of canned fish by local canned fish manufactures on the recommendation of the Secretary to the Ministry in charge of the subject of Fisheries.

(iii) balance amount after collecting Rs. 10 per Kg from the applicable Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 40 per Kg on importation of Salt (Sodium Chloride BP), classified under the HS Code of 2501.00, by any pharmaceuticals manufacturing enterprise, registered with the National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) for the purpose of manufacturing pharmaceuticals, not for commercial purpose, on the recommendation of the Secretary to the Ministry in charge of the subject of Health.

3. Importation of Maize, Black gram, Green gram, Cowpeas and Kurakkan as listed out above shall be allowed on the recommendation of the Ministry in charge of the subject of Agriculture.

4. Except the Order 2(i) above, this Order shall be valid for a period commencing from January 01, 2026 to December 31, 2026 subject to provisions of Sub Section (3) of Section 2 of the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.
