The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE COLOMBO MUNICIPAL COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Colombo Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the secretary of the Sarvajana Balaya the recognized political party to which Mohideen Rifki Badurdeen the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Colombo Municipal Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Kanagaranjithan Branawan to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer of the Colombo Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Kanagaranjithan Branawan was selected as the member of the Colombo Municipal Council. Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer, Colombo Municipal Council. District Elections Office,Colombo City Election Office,28th October, 2025.
