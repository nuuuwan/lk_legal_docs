THE LAND ACQUISITION (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

My No.: DS/MN/LB/05/AQ/WPP/Phl.Ext/32.

Land Ministry No.: 4-3/11/2022/CEB/165. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose.

Description of the land or Servitude to be acquired:- The land allotment called Viyayadippannai Kaani, in total extent of about 0.1526 Hectares depicted as Lot No. 1 in plan No. Cadestral - 940055, Block No. 03, Sheet No. 01 and prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in the Village called Viyayadippannai in the Grama Niladari Division No. 55 of Pesalai West, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Mannar Town of Mannar District in Northern Province.

Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Viyayadippannaikaani; Present use of the land: Open Waste Land; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Arokiyam Alosiyas Dias of Pesalai; Extent: 0.1526 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land/lands on which servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in

writing, before me at 10.00 a.m. on the 16th day of October 2025 at Divisional Secretariat Mannar Town.

And further you are hereby requested to notify to me in writing (in duplicate) or or before the 09th day of October 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

fg K. Kantheepan, Divisional Secretary, Mannar Town.

Divisional Secretariat of Mannar Town, 10th September, 2025. EOG 09 - 0163
