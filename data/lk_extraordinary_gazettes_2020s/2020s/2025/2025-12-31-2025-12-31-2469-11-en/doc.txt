(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF THE PELMADULLA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A (1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) WHEREAS the office of return member of Pelmadulla Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Hewawasam Gamage Sachini Sewwandi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Pelmadulla Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Weranja Kamanee Wijerathna to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Kasthurisinghe Herath Mudiyanselage Sajith Priyankara Kasthurisinghe, Returning Officer of the Pelmadulla Pradesbiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A (1) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Weranja Kamanee Wijerathna was selected as the member of the Pelmadulla Pradeshiya Sabha.

K. H. M. Sajith P. Kasthurisinghe, Returning Officer, Pelmadulla Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Ratnapura,30th of December, 2025.
