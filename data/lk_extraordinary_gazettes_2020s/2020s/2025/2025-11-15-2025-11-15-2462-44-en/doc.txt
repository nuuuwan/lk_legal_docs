Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

DEPARTMENT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT - NORTHERN PROVINCE

Notice for meeting under the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

I, Sambasivam Sutharsan, Commissioner of Local Government, Northern Province by virtue of powers vested to me under Sub Section No. 66(G) of Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, hereby notify to convene the meeting for the election to fill the vacant of Chairman of Valvettithurai Urban Council in Northern Province on 28.11.2025, 9.30 a.m. at Council hall of the Valvettithurai Urban Council.

Sambasivam Sutharsan, Commissioner of Local Government, Northern Province. Department of Local Government,Northern Province,Elders Home Complex,A9 Road, Kaithady, Jaffna.13th November, 2025.
