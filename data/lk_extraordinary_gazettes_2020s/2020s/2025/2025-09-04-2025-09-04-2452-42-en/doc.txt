No. 2452/42 – THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 04, 2025

EXTRAORDINARY

The Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: MUL/LND/02/ACQ/70. Reference No. of Ministry of Lands: 4-3/15/2014/VE/281.

I hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following *land/servitude which is required for the public purpose.

Description of the *land/servitude to be acquired :- Following allotment of land in the extent of 0.0045 Hectare and depicted as Lot No. 1236 of Paper No. 49 of Additional No. 39 of the Final Village Plan bearing No. 90 dated 25.03.2019 prepared by the Surveyor General and situated in the Village of Parapamulla, in the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 276/A, Parapamulla West in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Mulatiyana in Matara District, Southern Province.

1. Lot No. 1236 Name of the land: Kudagalketiyemagedarawatta, Kudagalketiyewatta, Mahayayehena; Description of the land: Garden, Well and part of a permanent building;

Claimant: D. S. Dasanayaka Piya Sewana Kudagalketiyewatta Parapamulla West; Extent: 0.0045 Hec.

All persons interested in the aforesaid *land/Servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Mulatiyana on the 27th day of October, 2025 at 10.00 a.m.

And are hereby requested to notify me in writing with two copies on or before the 17th day of October, 2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said *land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

Windya D. Samarakkody, Divisional Secretary, Mulatiyana. Divisional Secretariat of, Mulatiyana, 29th August, 2025. EOG 09 - 0050
