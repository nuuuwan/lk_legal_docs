UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(l)(b) read with Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1) (b) read with Section 25 A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978,I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the KIU Campus (Private) Limited a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV66705 and located at No. 249/1, Malabe Road, Koswatta, Battaramulla, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order, amend the Schedule I to the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1896/22 of January 07,2015, by the addition of the following condition (vi) in Schedule I.

(vi) KIU Campus (Private) Limited shall, when admitting students for the degree programme specified in Schedule II hereto in respect of the Bachelor of Science Honours in Medical Science in Acupuncture complete with a minimum of 120 Credits (SLQF Level 6) adhere to the minimum entry qualification of three (3) simple passes (“S”) for Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Agricultural Science in one and the same sitting at the G.C.E. (Advanced Level) Examination conducted by the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka or equivalent qualification.
