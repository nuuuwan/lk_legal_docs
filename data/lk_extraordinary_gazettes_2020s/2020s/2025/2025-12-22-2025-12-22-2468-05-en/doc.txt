(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KOBEIGANE PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of the resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya the recognized political party to which Dissanayaka Mudiyanselage Eesha Hanshani Dissanayake the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 09 Baladora of Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Munasinghe Mudiyanselage Achala Wijayasinghe to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayake returning officer of the Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Munasinghe Mudiyanselage Achala Wijayasinghe was elected as the member of the No. 09 Baladora of Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha. R. M. H. B. Rathnayake, Returning Officer, Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,22nd December, 2025.
