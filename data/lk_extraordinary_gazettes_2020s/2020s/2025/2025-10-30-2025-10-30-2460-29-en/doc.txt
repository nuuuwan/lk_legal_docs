PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KARANDENIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262) WHEREAS the office of member of Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death;

And whereas the secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Maheel Rangajeewa Munasingha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward number 09 Borakanda of Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and its Not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities elections ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Mahadurage Suresh Madhusanka to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara returning officer of the Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mahadurage Suresh Madhusanka was elected as the member of the ward number 09, Borakanda of Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Karandeniya Pradeshiya Sabha.District Election Office,Galle,29th October, 2025.
