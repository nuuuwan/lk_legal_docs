FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KATUWANA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66 A (1) (a) OF THELOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Mahadurage Sugathadasa the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of the ward of 12 Namaneliya of Katuwana Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Dewa Bandanage Chaminda Kumara to fill such vacancy;
