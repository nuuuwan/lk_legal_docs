REVISION OF ELECTORAL REGISTERS

Notice that the main Electoral Register for the year 2025 has been certified under the Registration of Electors Act

UNDER Section 20(1) of the Registration of Electors Act, notice is given on behalf of the Registering Officer of each electoral district that the 2025 main electoral register has been certified on 31 October 2025 in terms of the Registration of Electors Act No. 44 of 1980; that the electoral districts mentioned in column I of the following schedule is certified by the Assistant Registering Officer in the corresponding entry in column II thereof; and that inspection may be made during normal office hours at the District Election Office/offices mentioned in the corresponding entry in Column III thereof.

Saman Sri Ratnayake, Commissioner General of Elections, Election Commission.Elections Commission, Rajagiriya,On 31st October 2025.
