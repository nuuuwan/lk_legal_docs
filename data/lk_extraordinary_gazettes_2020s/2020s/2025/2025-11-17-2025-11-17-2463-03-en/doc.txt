L.D.B 10/2025

GAMBLING REGULATORY AUTHORITY ACT, No. 17 OF 2025

Order under Sub section (2) of Section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub section (2) of section 1 of the Gambling Regulatory Authority Act, No. 17 of 2025, read with paragraph (3) of Article 44 of the Constitution, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka do by this order, appoint, December 1, 2025, as the date on which the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall come into operation.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President.

Colombo, 16th November, 2025.
