EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7 Reference No.: MTR/LND/ACQUI/L1/3997. Min. of Land Ref. No.: 4-3/9/2017/WS/458. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which are required for the public purpose mentioned below. The servitude of the land lots depicted as Lot No. 01, 02, 03 in Paper 01 of Zone 02 in Cadastral Plan bearing No. 820049 situated in the Village of Hungangoda in Grama Niladhari Division of No. 427, Nawimana South in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Matara Four Gravets, Matara District, Southern Province was acquired for constructing as access road to the land where the salt barrier is being built under Stage IV Water Supply Scheme Matara. Sub Schedule 1. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Pahlalawatta alias Welabadawatta; Description of land: Road; Original claimant of the land: Shantha Sandya Liyanage of No. 65, Mahawatta, Nawimana South, Nuwan Chandana Liyanage and Pehesara of Nawiman South, Champa Liyanage, Laksman Liyanage of Matara and others; Extent: 0.0100 Hectare.

2. Lot No. 02 Name of the Land: Ambagahawatta; Description of land: Road; Original claimant of the land: Nurani Sadara Liyanage of Ambagahawatta, Nawimana; Extent: 0.0050 Hectare. 3. Lot No. 03 Name of the Land: Paramagngnaketiya; Description of land: Road; Original claimant of the land: Pemananda Liyanage of "Samagi", Nawaimana South and others; Extent: 0.0296 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are hereby required to appear personally or by agent duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat of Matara Four Gravets on 24th of November 2025 at 10.00 a.m. And are hereby requested to notify me in writing with 02 copies on or before the 17th day of November 2025, the nature of their interests in the land/lands, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said lands, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

gfg Susantha Atthanayake, Divisional Secretary, Matara Four Gravets. On 14th day of October, 2025, At Divisional Secretariat Matara Four Gravets. EOG 10 - 0201
