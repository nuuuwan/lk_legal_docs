EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

Ref. No.: KADU/LAND/01/144/WS. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the following land/servitude has been transferred to the Government for a public purpose.

Description of the land/Servitude transferred to the Government:-

All that allotment of land depicted as Lot No. 01 in sheet No. 01, Block No. 01, in the Cadastral Map No. 522011 dated 18.06.2025, prepared by the Surveyor General, situated at the Village of Ratgahawatta, in Grama Niladhari Division, No. 474/A, Raggahawatta in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Kaduwela in Colombo District, Western Province and bounded as follows :

Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Dissawatta alias Katugampolayaowita, Godellawatta, Raggahawatta alias Thimbirigahawatta; Present use of the land: Home Garden, a permanent building and a part of a temporary

building is available; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Mr. Manjula Palihena, Mr. P. D. Nalaka Palihena, Mrs. Shyamali Palihena; Extent: 0.5514 Hectare.

All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connection to the aforesaid land/servitude hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on 16.10.2025 at 09.30 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat, Kaduwela.

And further you are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) before the 13.10.2025 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

fg H. S. Sampath Idamgodage, Divisional Secretary, Kaduwela.

Divisional Secretariat of Kaduwela, 30th September, 2025. EOG 09 - 0270
