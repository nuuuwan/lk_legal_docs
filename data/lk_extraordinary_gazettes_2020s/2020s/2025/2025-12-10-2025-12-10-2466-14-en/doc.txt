NOTIFICATION MADE UNDER SECTION 66(G) OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT INSTITUTES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE, CHAPTER 262, AS AMENDED BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT INSTITUTES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 22 OF 2012

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 66(G) of the Local Government Institute Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, As amended by Local Government Institutes Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, I, Herath Mudiyanselage Sanjeewanee Herath the Commissioner of Local Government in the North Western Province, hereby notify that the meeting for the purpose of electing the Vice Chairman for the Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha, will be held in the council meeting hall of Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha at 10.00 a.m. on 06.01.2026.

H. M. Sanjeewanee Herath, Commissioner of Local Government, North Western Province.

Department of Local Government,2nd Floor, Vidusara, Provincial Council Office Complex,Kurunegala,10th December 2025.

NOTIFICATION MADE UNDER SECTION 66C.(4) OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT INSTITUTES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE, CHAPTER 262, AS AMENDED BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT INSTITUTES ELECTIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 22 OF 2012

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Sub section 66C.(4) of the Local Government Institute Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, As amended by Local Government Institutes Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, I, Herath Mudiyanselage Sanjeewanee Herath the Commissioner of Local Government in the North Western Province, hereby notify that the meeting will be convened for the purpose of electing the Chairman and Vice Chairman for the Anamaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha, will be held in the council meeting hall of Anamaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha at 2.00 p.m. on 06.01.2026.
