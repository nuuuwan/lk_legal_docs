THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My Reference No. : KIDS/LND/09/ACQ/07/05/01. I hereby give notice, under Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following Land/ Servitude for a public purpose. The public purpose: To Construct a gantry to improve the reliability and quality of electricity supply in Idangoda area. Description of the Land / Survitude to be acquired : Lot No. 01 in Extent of 0.0506 Hectare depicted in Polio No. 1 of F.V.P. No. 325 of Zone No. 04 of the Cadastral Map No. 620623 of the field sheet No. R/KRE/2024/38 prepared by Ratnapura Survey Superintendent situated in Matuwagala Grama Niladhari Division of Kiriella Divisional Secretary's Division of Uda Paththuwa of Kuruwita Korale of Ratnapura District of Sabaragamuwa Province.

01. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land : Matuwagala Waththa; Claimant:-Balangoda Plantation Company Ltd, Farm Garden, Ratnapura; Extent : 0.0506 Hectare. (20 P.) And whereas every person interested in the aforesaid land/servitude shall be directed to appear before me personally or by agent duly authorized in writing on 10th day of November 2025 at 10.00 a.m. in Divisional Secretariat Kiriella. And hereby requested to notify to me in writing with duplicate on or before the 03rd day of November 2025, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. KUMARI GAMAETHIGE, Divisional Secretary, Kiriella. At the Divisional Secretariat, Kiriella. On 03rd day of September 2025. EOG 09 - 0097
