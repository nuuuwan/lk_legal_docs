LAND DEVELOPMENT ORDINANCE

UNDER Section 6(1) of the Land Development Ordinance, Mr. Malimbada Weda Arachchige Nalaka Tharanga is appointed as a Land Officer for Southern Province with effect from 13.11.2021 in addition to the duties of his post. This appointment will be in effect until he serves in the said post and if he had been previously appointed as a Land Officer for another province or district/s, it shall be thereby cancelled.
