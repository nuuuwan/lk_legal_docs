THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 File No. : DSK/LND/7/F1/12/ACQ/535. I hereby give notice under Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land required for a public purpose. Details of the land to be acquired : F. V. P. 152 (Additional No. 52) The land mentioned below is located in the Village of Walgammulla in the Katuwana Divisional Secretary's Division of the Hambantota District. 01. Lot No. 1623 Name of the Land: Mahawaththahena; Description of the Land: There is a garden, a mixed crop plantation with jackfruit, Coconut and part of a permanent building; Extent: 02 Acres All persons claiming title, rights or other interests in the above land/property, either in person or through their

duly authorized representatives, should appear before me at the Divisional Secretariat, Katuwana on 28th January, 2026 at 10.00 a.m. Furthermore, by 21st January 2026, I must be informed in writing in duplicate of the details of the facts for which compensation is requested for the acquisition of the said land in accordance with the nature of their rights in relation to that land/lands, the amount of compensation and how that amount was calculated. All deeds and other documents required to establish rights must be presented at the examination. R. A. Chathuri Damithra, Divisional Secretary, Katuwana. Divisional Secretariat, Katuwana, 16th of October, 2025. EOG 10 - 0244
