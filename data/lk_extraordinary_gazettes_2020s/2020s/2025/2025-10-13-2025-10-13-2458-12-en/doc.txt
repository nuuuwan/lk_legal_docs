UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(l)(b) read with Sections 25A and 70C

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing registration number PV 17745, which is recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, and since under the certificate dated March 23, 2019 issued by the Registrar General of Companies the said Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited has been incorporated as the CINEC Campus (Private) Limited, do by this Order, amend the Order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1818/7 of July 9, 2013, by the substitution for the words “Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited ” wherever those words appear in the said Order, of the words “ CINEC Campus (Private) Limited” and every reference to the “Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited ” in any Order, notice, contract, communication or other document made in terms of the aforesaid sections shall be read and construed as a reference to the “CINEC Campus (Private) Limited ”.

Further, any power exercised, or any duty or function performed by the said Colombo International Nautical and Engineering College (Private) Limited prior to the date of coming into effect of this Order in pursuance of any power conferred on it by the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1818/7 of July 9, 2013 shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly exercised and performed by the CINEC Campus (Private) Limited.

Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, Minister of Education. Higher Education and Vocational Education.

Colombo,26th September, 2025.
