EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice Under Section 7

My Ref. No.: KDS/TUM/4/1/6/1/3/68. Ministry of Land Ref. No.: 4-3/7/2021/HW/384. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government indents to acquire the following land/servitude* which is required for a public purpose :

Description of the land to be acquired : Undermentioned land shown in the Surveyor General’s plan of CM 321271, Block No. 01 Sheet No. 2, situated in the Village of Galagedara in the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 301 Galagedara West, in Thumpane Divisional Secretary’s Division in Kandy District of Central Province :

01. Lot No. 1 Name of the Land: Pallegewaththa; Description: Open bare land; Claimant: Government; Original Claimant: W. U. S. W. K. Wijerathna; Extent: 0.0044 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land on which a servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Divisional Secretariat Thumpane, on the 24th November, 2025, at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the17th November, 2025, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for computation of the said land/servitude the amount of compensation the details of the computation of such amount.

fg R. M. S. N. Rathnayake, Divisional Secretary, Thumpane.

Divisional Secretariat of Thumpane, 29th September, 2025. EOG 10 - 0153
