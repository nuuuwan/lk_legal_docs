(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MAHARAGAMA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Maharagama Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation.

And whereas the Leader of the Independent Group Number 01 to which Saman Pushpakumara Lankeshwara the member belonged, was required by me under section 66A (I) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Maharagama Urban Council who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days,

And whereas the Leader of the Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominate Angoda Liyanage Pradeep Chaminda to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, P. Ragunathan, Returning Officer of the Maharagama Urban Council acting in terms of section 66A (I) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Angoda Liyanage Pradeep Chaminda was selected as the member of the Maharagama Urban Council. P. Ragunathan, Returning Officer, Maharagama Urban Council. District Elections Office,Colombo,03rd October, 2025.
