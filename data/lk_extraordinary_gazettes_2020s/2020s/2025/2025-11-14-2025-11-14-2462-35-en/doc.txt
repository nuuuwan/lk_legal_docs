THE URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY LAW No. 41 OF 1978 OF THENATIONAL STATE ASSEMBLY

Cancellation of the Vesting Order under Section 15 (1)

BY virtue of the powers vested in former Hon. Minister of Mega polis & Western Development under Section 15 (1) of the Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978, the land described in the schedule mentioned below was vested with the Urban Development Authority, by the Order under Section 15 (1) published in Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2025/18 dated 27.06.2017.

I, Bimal Niroshan Rathnayake, Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development do hereby cancel the aforesaid Vesting Order under Section 15 (1) published in Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2025/18 dated 27.06.2017 since the subject property is no required for the purpose of the Urban Development Authority.

Bimal Niroshan Rathnayake (M.P.), Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.

Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development,12th Floor,Sethsiripaya Stage II,Battaramulla,03rd November, 2025.
