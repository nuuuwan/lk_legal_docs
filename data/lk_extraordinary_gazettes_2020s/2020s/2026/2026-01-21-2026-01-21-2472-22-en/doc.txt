REGISTRATION OF PERSONS ACT, No. 32 OF 1968

Order under Section 34

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 34 of Registration of Persons (Amendment) Act, No. 8 of 2016, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Digital Economy of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka do by this Order, until the necessary infrastructure, program and technical procedures are prepared, the date of expiry of the period of operation as the date published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1979/40 of 11 August 2016 of Sections 6, 9, 14,16, and 17 of the Registration of Persons Act, No. 32 of 1968, and every order made under that Act (except the Registration of Persons Order, 1990, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 637/6 of 20 November 1990) is hereby changed to 31.12.2026.

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, President and Minister of Digital Economy.18th January, 2026.
