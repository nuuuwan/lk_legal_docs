(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEMATALE MUNICIPAL COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a member of the Matale Municipal Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized Political party to which Mr. Muhamad Mansoor Muhamad Nawsad the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to nominate a candidate of that ward Number 6 - Railway Station Place of Matale Municipal Council who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated the decision to nominate Mr. Krishan Thushara Darshika Wasalage to fill the said vacancy;

Therefore I, Hettige Asiri Eranga Pushpakumara, Returning Officer of the Matale Municipal Council , acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262), do hereby declare that Mr. Krishan Thushara Darshika Wasalage was elected as the member of the Ward Number 6 - Railway Station Place of the Matale Municipal Council. H. A. E. Pushpakumara, Returning Officer, Matale Municipal Council. District Election Office,Matale,10th April, 2026.

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEMIRIGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a member of the Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized Political party to which Ms. Weerappulige Thilini Imalka Dias the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) (a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate a candidate of that ward Number 25 - Pasyala of Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated the decision to nominate Ms. Maha Pathirennehelage Shirani Dewika Nilmini to fill the said vacancy;

Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ms. Maha Pathirennenehelage Shirani Diwika Nilmini was elected as the member of the Ward Number 25 - Pasyala of the Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Mirigama Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Gampaha ,08th April, 2026.
