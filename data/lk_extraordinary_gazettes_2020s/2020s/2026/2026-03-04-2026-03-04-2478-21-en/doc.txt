Land Acquisition Notices

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Declaration under Section 5

I, Kuragamage Don Lal Kantha, Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation do hereby declare, under Sub-section (1) of Section 5 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the land described below is needed for a public purpose and will be acquired under the provisions of that Act.

An allotment of land described morefully in the Advance Tracing No. B/HLD/2021/585 dated 31.03.2022, prepared by the Surveyor-General, situated in the Village called Weerakoonagama (F.V.P. 289) in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 158 G of Weerakoonagama in the Minor Division of Kandapalla Korale in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Haldummulla in the District of Badulla in Uva Province and bounded as follows:
