No. 2470/55 – FRIDAY, JANUARY 09, 2026 - 09.01.2026

EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 Ministry of Agriculture, Live stocks, Land and Irrigation No.: 03J/84/LG/28. My Ref. No.: LA/1774. I hereby give notice, under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquire the following Land Servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land servitude to be acquired : Province : Northern District : Jaffna Divisional Secretariat Division: Valikamam South West, Sandilipay Grama Niladhari Division : Manipay South (J/139) Village : Manipay Plan : PP ya 1902 Lot No. : 1 & 2 Extent : 1.0746 Hectares

Land Name : 'Vinayagar Thoddam' Purpose : For Sellamuthu Playground Claimant : Trustee of Vannarpannai Sivan Temple, Jaffna. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/lands on which a servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear before me personally, or by agent duly authorized in writing at the Divisional Secretariat, Sandilipay, on the 09th day of March, 2026, at 10.00 a.m. and they are hereby requested to notify me in writing with duplicates on or before the 23rd day of February 2026, the nature of their interests in the land and the amount and the particulars of their claims for compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. K. Pංඋൺൻൺ඄ൺඋൺආඈඈඋඍඁඒ, Divisional Secretary, Valikamam Southwest, Sandilipay. At Divisional Secretariat, Valikamam South West, Sandilipay. On 29th day of December, 2025. EOG 01 - 0039
