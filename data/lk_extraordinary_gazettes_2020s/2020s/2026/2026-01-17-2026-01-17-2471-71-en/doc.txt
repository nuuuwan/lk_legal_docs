THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

I, hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7, ofthe Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the LandAcquisition (Amendment) Act, No.28 of 1964 that theGovernment intends to acquire the following land which isrequired for a public purpose:

Description of the land to be acquired :

Lots 1, 2 and 3 in extent of 0.4514 Hectare, mentioned in theSheet No. 2 in Block No. 1 in the Cadastral Map No. 830257prepared by the Surveyor General, situated in the Village ofPallerote in No. 184, Rote Grama Niladhari Division in theDivisional Secretary's, Division of Ambalanthota in the Districtof Hambanthota:

01. Lot No. 01Name of the Land : Pallerota Lebeema;Description of theland:Garden, mixed crop cultivation; Claimants: Private;Original Claimant: Owner cannot be traced; Extent : 0.0713Hectare.

02. Lot No. 02Name of the Land : Pallerota Lebeema;Description of theland:Garden, mixed crop cultivation; Claimants: Private;Original Claimant: Owner cannot be traced;Extent : 0.0817Hectare.

03. Lot No. 03Name of the Land : Pallerota Lebeema;Description of theland:Irrigation Canal; Claimants: Private; Original Claimant:U.A.Lenad, Pallerota, Mamadala;Extent : 0.2984 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid lands are herebyrequested to appear personally, or by agents duly authorizedin writing, before me at 10.30 a.m., on 23rd February 2026 at theDivisional Secretariat of Ambalanthota and are herebyrequested to notify me in writting (in duplicate) on or beforethe 16 th of February 2026 the nature of their interests in theland, the particulars of their claims for compensation for theacquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation andthe details of the computation of such amount.
