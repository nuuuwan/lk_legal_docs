THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: KDS/HAR/4/2/1/58. Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and

Irrigation No.: 4-3/18/2022/WS/56. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government indents to acquire the following land as it is needed to acquire an additional allotment of land for the Construction of Kulugammana Pump House of Kandy North-Pathadumbara Integrated Water Supply Project.

Description of the land to be acquired : All that allotment of land depicted as Lot No. 1 in TL 1, sheet No. 2, in block No. 07 in the Cadastral Map No. 320505, surveyed and prepared by the Senior Superintendent of Surveys, Kandy District, situated at the Village of Kulugammana, in Kulugammana Grama Niladhari Division, in No. 447, Kulugammana Siyapattuwa Minor Division, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Harispattuwa, in Kandy District, Central Province and bounded as follows :

01. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Pansalwatta alias Pansal Aramba, Walakadawatta Kumbura, Kajugahakotuwa, Walakadawatta Aramba and Bilinchgahakotuwa; Claimant: the State;

Original Claimant: Ven. Ekiriye Saddhananda Thero, Chief Incumbent of Kulugammana Viharaya and Public Trustee of the Department of Public Trustee; Extent: 0.0072 Hectare.

North by : Lot No. 2 and Lot No. 1 in sheet 1; East by : Lot No. 1 and 3 in sheet 1; South by : Lot No. 3 in sheet 1 and Lot No. 2 in the said plan; West by : Lot No. 2.

All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connection to the aforesaid land are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on the 23rd March, 2026 at 10.00 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat, Harispattuwa.

Further, you are hereby requested to notify me in writing in duplicate before the 16th March, 2026, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

fg Mohan Dharmadasa, Divisional Secretary, Harispattuwa.

Divisional Secretariat, Harispattuwa, 30th January, 2026. EOG 02 - 0077
