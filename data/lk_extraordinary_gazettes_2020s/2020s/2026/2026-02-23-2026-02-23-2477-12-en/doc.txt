PART III - LANDS Title Registration NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No. : 42/1261 KURUNEGALA DISTRICT IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 420922 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Deegithawa (Block 04, Sheet No. 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.1279 - Deegithawa in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Wariyapola in the District of Kurunegala in the Province of North Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 3rd of March 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
