LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Notice given under Section 66G of the Local Government Elections Ordinance as amended by theLocal Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

HAVING act in exercise of the powers vested in me under Section 66G of the Local Government Elections Ordinance, as amended by the Local Government Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012 (Chapter 262), I hereby declare that the candidate elected to the post of Chairman of the Balangoda Urban Council in the Sabaragamuwa Province shall be as specifically indicated in the following schedule.

L. M. P. W. Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province. Department of Local Government,Office Complex of Provincial Council,New Town,Ratnapura,13th of February 2026.
