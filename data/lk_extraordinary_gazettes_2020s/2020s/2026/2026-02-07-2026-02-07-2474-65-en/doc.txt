Regulations Made By The Minister of Youth Affairs And Sports Under Sections 32 And 33 of Sports Law No. 25 of 1973 as Amended.

I , Sunil Kumara Gamage, Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports, in terms of the powers vested in me under Sections 32 and 33 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973, as amended, do hereby direct as follows;

1. I hereby revoke, with immediate effect, the Extraordinary Gazette Notification bearing No. 2451/24 published on 27th August 2025.

2. I temporarily suspend the registration of Sri Lanka Karate Do Federation by virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 32 and 33 of the Sports Law No . 25 of 1973 and its amendments.

3. By virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 33 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973, as amended, I hereby appoint a Working Task Force consisting of the following members for the limited purpose of investigating the fraud and corrupt practices that have occurred, adopting the new Constitution of Sri Lanka Karate Do Federation, registering new eligible members of Sri Lanka Karate Do Federation, and conducting the Special General Meeting (SGM) and the Annual General Meeting (AGM) in accordance with the provisions of the said new Constitution.
