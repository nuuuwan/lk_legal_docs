2025

SEVENTH SUPPLEMENTARY FINANCIAL STATEMENT

GRANTING PERMISSION BY VIRTUE OF POWERS VESTED IN ME UNDER SECTION 27(A) OF PROVINCIAL

COUNCIL ACT No. 42 OF 1987 AS THE TERM OF THE SABARAGAMUWA PROVINCIAL COUNCIL OF THE

DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA ENDED ON 26TH SEPTEMBER, 2017.

2025

1. This permission may be cited as ‘‘Permission to disburse under the Seventh supplementary financial statement - 2025 of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council” and come in to operation from 31 .12.2025.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Five Billion Six Hundred Twenty Three Million One Hundred Six Thousand Nine Hundred Seventy Five and Sixteen Cents only (65,623,106,975.16) for the service of the period beginning on 01st of January, 2025 and ending 31st December 2025, which is specified as “Financial Year 2025” in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue and Expenditure Estimate for 2025 approved on 03.04.2025, as amended by the First, Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth and Sixth Supplementary Estimates for 2025, is hereby amended as, Rupees Sixty-Five Bilton Six Hundred Thirty-Three Million Thee Hundred Seventy-Seven Thousand Forty - Eight and Cents Seventy-Two (Rs. 65,633,377,048.72) and grant permission to the Chief Secretary of this Province and other Accounting Officers to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

AUTHORIZATION

1. I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to disburse as per the Seventh Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2025, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

2. The expenditure of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which is estimated Rupees Sixty Five Billion Six Hundred Twenty Three Million One Hundred Six Thousand Nine Hundred Seventy Five and Sixteen Cents only (65,623,106,975.16) for the services of the period beginning on 01st January, 2025 and ending 31st December, 2025, which is specified in the Annual Financial Statement and Revenue Expenditure Estimates for the year 2025, is amended as Rupees Sixty-Five Billion Six Hundred Thirty-Three Million Three Hundred Seventy-Seven Thousand Forty-Eight and Cents Seventy-Two (Rs. 65,633,377,048.72) and grant permission to disburse the same from the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council Fund.

3. This permission will be valid till 31st of December, 2025 or upto the date that expires three months (03) from the date of summoning the Seventh (07th) Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council or from the date re-start the functions of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council which occurs first.

A.A.C.J. Rajarathna,

Hon. Governor, Sabaragamuwa province,

31st day of December, 2025.

AUTHORIZATION

FOR 2025 ADVANCED ACCOUNTS

I do hereby approve to amend the advance account limits for the year 2025 stated in the Sixth Schedule of the Seventh Supplementary Financial Statement for the year 2025, by virtue of powers vested in me under Section 27 (a) of the Provincial Council Act No. 42 of 1987, as the term of the 06th Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka ended on 26th September, 2017.

Accordingly, I do hereby grant permission to the Chief Secretary and the Accounting Officers of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to bear the expenses subject to those limits.
