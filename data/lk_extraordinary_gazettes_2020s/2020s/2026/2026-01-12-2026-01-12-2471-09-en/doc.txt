THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

ORDER UNDER SECTION 39(a)(1)

THE allotments speciﬁed in the Order No. 141 of 2013 made under the Proviso (a) to Section 38 of the Land Acquisition Act, (Chapter 460) and published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1810/24 dated 14th May, 2013 are vested absolutely in the State and the possession of which has been taken for or on behalf of the State under Paragraph (a) of Section 40 of the aforesaid Act.
