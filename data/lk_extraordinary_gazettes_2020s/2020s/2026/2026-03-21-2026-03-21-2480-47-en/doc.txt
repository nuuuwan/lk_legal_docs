COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 12 OF 2025

Order under Sub section (2) of Section 1

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Sub section (2) of the Section 1 of the Companies ( Amendment) Act, No. 12 of 2025, I, Wasantha Samarasinghe, Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development, do by this Order, appoint, March 30, 2026 as the date on which the provisions of the aforesaid Act, shall come into operation.

Wasantha Samarasinghe, Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development.

Colombo,21st March, 2026.
