Central Bank of Sri Lanka Notices

EXTENSION OF THE TERM OF THE ADMINISTRATOR APPOINTED TONATION LANKA FINANCE PLC UNDER SECTION 18 OF THE BANKING(SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 17 OF 2023

PURSUANT to the powers vested in the Central Bank of Sri Lanka under Section 18 read together with Section 19 of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023, the tenure of Mr. P W D N R Rodrigo, who was appointed as the Administrator of Nation Lanka Finance PLC by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/57 dated 04.07.2025, effective from 04.07.2025, is hereby extended for a further period of six (06) months, commencing from 04.01.2026 and ending on 03.07.2026.

This extension is made to facilitate the continuation of administrative and statutory functions of the Administrator and to enable the implementation of an appropriate resolution tool for Nation Lanka Finance PLC in accordance with the provisions of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023.

Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chairman of the Governing Board and the, Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.
