THE AYURVEDA ACT, No. 31 OF 1961

RULES made by the Ayurvedic Medical Council under Paragraph (f) of Section 18 of the Ayurveda Act, No.31 of 1961 read with Section 19 and 50 of that Act. (Registar), Ayurvedic Medical Council.

Colombo, 20th March, 2026.Rule

1. The Registered Ayurvedic Medical Practitioners (Professional Conduct) Rules No. 01 of 2014 published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1884/36 of October 15, 2014 is hereby amended as follows :-

(1) by the insertion, immediately afrer rule 2 thereof, of the following new rule which shall have effect as rule 2A:-

“2A. The official seal of an Ayurveda medical practitioner shall only contain-

(a) the registration number of such practitioner;(b) the title of such practitioner; and(c) the professional qualifications approved by the Ayurveda Medical Council.”;and
