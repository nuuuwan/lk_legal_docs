NOTICE No. : 32/1129 KANDY DISTRICT

IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 320522 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Pallemulla (Block 03, Sheet No. 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No. 432 - Pallemulla in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Harispattuwa in the District of Kandy in the Province of Central Province should submit his claim to the undersigned before 03rd of March 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
