Order made under Section 203 of the Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255) read with Section 02 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989

IN terms of the powers vested in me I, Hanif Yusoof, the Governor of the Western Province, do hereby notify the following Order under Section 203 of the Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255) read with Section 02 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989.

Hanif Yusoof, The Governor, Western Province.

At Governors’ Office - Western Province,Provincial Council Office Complex,Battaramulla,On the 20th day of January, 2026.

Order

1. THIS Order is made to give effect to the extension of time for the fulfillment of legal provisions stipulated in Subsection 178 (a) of the Urban Councils Ordinance amended by Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 and Section 178 (1) of the said Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255) in relation to the Budget of the Boralesgamuwa Urban Council for year 2026.

2. In accordance with the provisions stipulated in Subsection 178 (a) of the Urban Councils Ordinance amended by Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 and Section 178 (1) of the said Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255), the Boralesgamuwa Urban Council has decided on 20.01.2026 to extend the period of time for the Boralesgamuwa Urban Council to follow the procedure regarding the Budget for year 2026 and has submitted a request in this regard; and,

3. The Commissioner of Local Government of the Western Province, after scrutinizing the facts, has reported that a fundamental need has arisen to extend the period of time for fulfillment of the legal procedure regarding the Budget for implementation of powers, duties and functions of the Boralesgamuwa Urban Council, I do hereby order that the period of time be extended to 30.01.2026, for fulfilling the procedures under the above mentioned Sections regarding the Budget of the Boralesgamuwa Urban Council for the year 2026.
