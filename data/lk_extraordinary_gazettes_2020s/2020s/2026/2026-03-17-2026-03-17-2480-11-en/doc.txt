THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 My No. : Biya 3/1/3/02/2017-ii. I, hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire following land which is required for a public purpose: Description of the land intend to acquire: An allotment of Land in total Extent of about 0.3035 Hectare (0 Acre. 3 rude 0 perch) depicted Lot 01 in Zone No. 02 sheet No. 01 in the Cadastral Map No. 512439 dated 17.02.2026 Prepared by the Superintendent of Surveys, situated in the Village called Siyambalape in Divisional Secretary's Division of Biyagama in Adhikari Pattuwa of Gampaha District, Western Province. 01. Lot No. 1 Name of the land: Delgahawatta; Description: Garden, 20 coconut trees of about 50-60 Old, 09 jack fruit trees about 25-30 Old, 11 jack fruit trees about 10-15 Old, 01 bread fruit tree about 10-15 old, 01 mango tree 10-15 old, 01 wild bread fruit tree 25-30 old; Claimant : cannot be found; Extent: 0.3035 Hectare (acre. 0 rude 3 perch 0)

All persons interested in claims, rights or other connections for the aforesaid lad, servitude are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at the Biyagama Divisional Secretariat on the following date and time.

Date Allotment Number Time 2026.04.20 01 10.00
