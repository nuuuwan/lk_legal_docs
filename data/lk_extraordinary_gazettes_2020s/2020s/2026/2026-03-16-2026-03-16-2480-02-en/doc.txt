KALPITIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA

By - Law on the Parking of Vehicles within the Limits of the Pradeshiya Sabha

IN terms of the powers vested under Subsection (I) of Section 3 of the Local Authorities (Adopted By - Laws) Act, No.06 of 1952 (Chapter 261), the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha hereby notifies that at the General Council Meeting held on 10.02.2026, under Resolution No.07/2026/02/11/16, the following proposal was duly adopted by the Council.

A. S. M. Rikkas,

Chairman.Kalpitiya Pradeshiyha Sabha.At the Office of Kalpitiya Pradeshiyha Sabha,2026 February 23rd day.

RESOLUTION

In terms of the powers vested in the Minister in charge of the subject of Local Government of the North Western Province under Section 2 (1) of the Local Authorities (Adopted By - Laws) Act, No. 06 of 1952 (Chapter 261), read with paragraph (a) of Subsection (1) of Section 2 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989, the standard By - Law relating to the Parking of Vehicles of within the limits of a Pradeshiya Sabha, made by the said Minister and published in Part IV (a) of the Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka No.1663 dated 16.07.2010, was adopted and made applicable to the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha area by Gazette notification No.1843 dated 27.12.2013.
