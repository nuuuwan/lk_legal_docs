(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE RAJGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Rajgama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Dadallagei Layanal Kumarasiri, the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 10 Mawadavila of Rajgama Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Manawaduge Siril Jayananda to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Rajgama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Manawaduge Siril Jayananda was elected as the member of the Ward Number 10 Mawadavila of Rajgama Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Rajgama Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Galle,29th of January, 2026.
