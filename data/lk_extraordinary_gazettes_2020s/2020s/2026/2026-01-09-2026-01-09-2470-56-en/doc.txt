THE PUBLIC SECURITY ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 40)

Notice under Section 2(7)

IT is hereby notified that the Proclamation made by the Hon. President under Section 2 of the Public Security Ordinance (Chapter 40), bringing into operation the Provisions of Part II of the aforesaid Ordinance throughout Sri Lanka with effect from 28th December 2025 was approved by a Resolution of Parliament on 06th January 2026.

Dr. N. S. Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.

Colombo, January 09, 2026.
