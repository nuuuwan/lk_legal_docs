ORDER UNDER SECTION 10 (1) (b)

of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 10 (1) (b) of the Holidays Act, No. 29 of 1971, I, A.H.M.H. Abayarathna, Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils, and Local Government, do by this order declare that consequent to the declaration of Vesak Week from 27th May to 2nd June 2026 in accordance with the Cabinet Decision No. අමප/26/0553/822/018 dated 30.03.2026, the public holiday granted for 2nd May 2026 shall be amended to 31st May 2026.

Prof. A.H.M.H. Abayarathna (M.P.). Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils, and Local Government. Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils, and Local Government, “Nila Medura” Elvitigala Mawatha,Narahenpita, Colombo 05.17th April, 2026.
