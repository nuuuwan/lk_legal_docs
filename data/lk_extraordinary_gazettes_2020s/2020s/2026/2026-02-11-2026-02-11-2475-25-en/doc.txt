I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964 that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land or Servitude to be acquired:

The land allotment called Olaithoduvaikaani, in total extent of about 0.7655 Hectare depicted as Lot No. 1 in Plan No. Cadestral - 940056, Block No. 03, sheet No. 04 and prepared by Surveyor General situated in the Village called Olaithoduvai in the Grama Niladhari Division No. MN/56 of Pesalai South in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Mannar Town of Mannar District in Northern Province. 01. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Olaithoduvaikaani; Present use of the land: Low jungle contain 24 Palmyra tree 15-20 years; Claimant: State; Original Claimant: Not Known; Extent: 0.7655 Hectare.

The dates and the time of the inquiries; All persons interested in the aforesaid land on which servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at 10.00 A.M on the 25th day of March 2026 at Divisional Secretariat, Mannar Town . And further you are hereby requested to notify, me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 18th day of March 2026 the nature of their interest in the land, the particulars of their claims for the compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. K. Kantheepan, Divisional Secretary, Mannar Town. Divisional Secretariat of Mannar Town, 03rd February, 2026. EOG 02-0098/1
