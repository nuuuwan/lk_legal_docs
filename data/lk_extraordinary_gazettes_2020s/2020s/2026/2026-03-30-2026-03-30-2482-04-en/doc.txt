THE ANTI-CORRUPTION ACT, No. 9 OF 2023

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration in consultation with the Commission to Investi-gate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption under Section 156 read with paragraph (ee) of Subsection (1) of Section 80 of the Anti-Corruption Act, No. 9 of 2023.

Harshana Nanayakkara, Minister of Justice and National Integration.Colombo,30th March, 2026.Regulations

1. These Regulations shall be cited as the Anti-Corruption (High-risk Categories Required to Submit the Declaration of Assets and Liabilities) Regulations, No. 01 of 2026.

2. In terms of paragraph (ee) of Subsection (1) of Section 80 of the Anti-Corruption Act, No. 9 of 2023, the categories of officers specified in the Schedule hereto are designated as high-risk categories.
