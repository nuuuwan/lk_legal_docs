NATIONAL CONSUMER PRICE INDEX COMPUTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF CENSUS AND STATISTICS

(Base : 2021 = 100) IT is hereby notified for general information that the National Consumer Price Index computed by the Department of Census and Statistics for the month of February 2026 was 209.4 (Two Hundred and Nine point Four).

K. M. D. S. D. Karunarathne, Director General of Census and Statistics.
