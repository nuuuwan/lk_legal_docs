EXTRAORDINARY

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My No.: DNK/LND1/DEACQ/12/20. Ministry of Land Ref. No.: 03J/88/E/22. I hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government indents to acquire the land/servitudes which is required for a public purpose :

Description of the land intended to be acquired : All that allotment of land called “Kosgahawatta”, in total extent of about 0.6750 hectare, depicted as Lot No. 01 in the preliminary plan No. PU/DAN/2000/198 dated 14/03/2001 of the Surveyor General, situated at the Village of Dankotuwa, in the Divisional Secretary’s Division of Dankotuwa, in Puttalam District, North Western Province and bounded as follows:

01. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Kosgahawatta; Claimant: Bishop of Chilaw Diocese; Extent: 0.6750 Hectare.

All persons who claim ownership, interest or any other connection to the aforesaid land are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me on the 11th March, 2026 at 10.00 a.m. at the Divisional Secretariat, Dankotuwa.

Further, you are hereby requested to notify me in writing in duplicate before the 04th March, 2026, the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.

fg W. Nilanthi Priyadarshani, Divisional Secretary (D.C.) Dankotuwa.

Divisional Secretariat, Dankotuwa, 16th January, 2026. EOG 01 - 00206
