PILGRIMAGES ORDINANCE

Season of Lent And Holy Week In Mawathagama Calvary Shrine ofCatholic Bishop Kurunegala (North Western)

IT is hereby notified to the general public that the Season of Lent and Holy Week which annually performing as a tradition will be held from 18th of February 2026 to 05th of April 2026 at Mawathagama Calvary Shrine situated in No. 681 Grama Niladhari Division in Mawathagama Divisional Secretary’s Division in the Administrative District of Kurunegala.

The regulations published under Pilgrimages Ordinance will be enforced during this Holy season.

D. M. K. C. Dissanayake, District Secretary / Government Agent, Kurunegala, Additional Secretary - Home Affairs.

At District Secretariat, Kurunegala,Thursday 22nd of January, 2026.
