EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under section 10A(l)(a) of the (chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Kamalathasan Rijivarnan of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has been ceased to be a member of the Thamizh Makkal Koottani.

R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha.

District Elections Office,Jaffna, 03rd March of 2026.
