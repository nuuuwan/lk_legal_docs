Government Notifications

THE SEASON OF LENT AND HOLY WEEK AT CALVARY SHRINE, MAGGONA IN THE CATHOLIC DIOCESE OF KALUTARA (WESTERN PROVINCE)

IT is hereby notified to the general public that the Annual Lent and Holy Week Season held at the Calvary Shrine, Maggona situated in the Divisional Secretariat Division, Beruwala in 741 B Kapugoda Grama Niladhari Division of the Kalutara Administrative District, Western Province will continue as usual from 18th February 2026 to 05th April 2026.

The regulations published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2018/06 and dated 08.05.2017 in terms of the Pilgrimages Ordinance will be enforced during this Holy season.

H. M. S. P. Herath, District Secretary / Government Agent, Administrative District, Kalutara.

At District Secretariat, Kalutara,Monday, 16th February, 2026.
