SPECIAL ALLOWANCE PAYABLE TO WORKERS IN THE COCOA, CARDAMOM AND PEPPER GROWING AND MANUFACTURING TRADE

IT is hereby notified, under Section 20(2)(b) of the Wages Boards Ordinance (Chapter 136), that the Cost of Living Index Number for the above trade for the month of November 2025, being 212.2 the special allowance in respect of a normal working day in the month of December 2025 will be as follows :-

The Cocoa, Cardamom and Pepper Growing and Manufacturing Trade

Class of Workers: Special Allowance for a Normal Working Day Daily Paid Employees: Rs. C. Workers 1852.39 H. M. D. N. K. Wataliyadda, Commissioner General of Labour.

Department of Labour,Colombo 05,02nd February, 2026.
