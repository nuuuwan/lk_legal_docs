SRI LANKA EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT, No. 40 OF 1979

Order under Section 14

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 14 of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No. 40 of 1979, I, Sunil Handunneththi, Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, do by this Order declare the followings;

i. Items/goods described under the H.S. Codes in Column II of the Schedule A hereto, together with their corresponding entries, described in the Schedule of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2325/06 dated 28.03.2023, are hereby deleted without prejudice to the goods imported thereunder;

ii Items/goods described under the H.S. Codes in Column II of the Schedule B hereto, together with their corresponding entries shall be inserted in to the Schedule of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2325/06 dated 28.03.2023 referred above and the CESS Levy shall be charged, levied and paid on all such goods enumerated in the Column III of the Schedule B hereto at the rates specified in the corresponding entries in the Column IV in the same Schedule hereto;

iii Items/goods described under the H.S. Codes in Column II of the Schedule C hereto, together with their corresponding entries shall be inserted in to the Schedule of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2365/50 dated 04.01.2024 and the CESS Levy shall be charged, levied and paid on all such goods enumerated in the Column III of the Schedule
