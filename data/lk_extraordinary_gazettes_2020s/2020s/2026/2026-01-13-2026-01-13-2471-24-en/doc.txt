EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 7(4) (b) OF THE

Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981

IN terms of Section 7(4) (a) of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, the symbols of the Political parties entitled to be considered as recognized political parties for the election purposes, as specified in the Schedule (A) and the Schedule (B) annexed hereto, are hereby approved by the Election Commission.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

Ameer Faaiz, A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

Election Commission,Sarana Mawatha,Rajagiriya,On this 13th day of January, 2026.
