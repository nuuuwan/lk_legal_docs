EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Notification made under Section 66(G) of the Local Government Institutes Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Institutes Elections (Amendment)Act, No. 22 of 2012

BY virtue of powers vested in me under Section 66(G) of the Local Government Institutes Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, As amended by the Local Government Institutes Election (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, I, Herath Mudiyanselage Sanjeewanee Herath the Commissioner of Local Government in the North Western Province, hereby notify that the meeting for the purpose of electing the Vice Chairman for the Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha, will be convened in terms of the section 66C.(4) of the said Ordinance, held in the council meeting of Kobeigane Pradeshiya Sabha at 10.00 a.m. on 03.02.2026.

H. M. Sanjeewanee Herath, Commissioner of Local Government, North Western Province. Department of Local Government,2nd Floor, Vidusara,Provincial Council Office Complex,Kurunegala,13th of January 2026.
