(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE PANDUWASNUWARA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Sitta Durayalage Pethum Madhusankha Somasiri the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward number 09 Bandara Koswatta Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Mallawa Arachchilage Manjula Prasanna Mallawa Arachchi to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayaka, returning officer of the Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mallawa Arachchilage Manjula Prasanna Mallawa Arachchi was elected as the member of the ward number 09 Bandara Koswatta Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha. R. M. H. B. Rathnayaka, Returning Officer, Panduwasnuwara Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,12th January, 2026.
