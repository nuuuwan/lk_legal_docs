Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha

I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mr. Ponnampalam Nadanasikamani of the Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has been ceased to be a member of the Thamizh Makkal Koottani.

R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Velanai Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Jaffna,23rd of January, 2026.
