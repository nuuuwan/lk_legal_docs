(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MANTHAI EAST PRADESHIYA SABHA

UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE(CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Manthai East Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending for council meetings for a period of three months without permission;

And whereas the Secretary of the All Ceylon Tamil Congress, the recognized political party to which Nagarasa Rajmohan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Manthai East Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Tharumarasa Pirasanthan to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Sinthusaa Sasikumar, Returning Officer of the Manthai East Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Tharumarasa Pirasanthan was elected as the member of the Manthai East Pradeshiya Sabha. Sinthusaa Sasikumar, Returning Officer, Manthai East Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Mullaitivu,26th February, 2026.
