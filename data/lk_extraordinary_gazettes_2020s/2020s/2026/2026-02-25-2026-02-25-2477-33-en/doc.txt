(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BINGIRIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Sarvajana Balaya, the recognized political party to which Lakmal Wickramaarachchi the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Randenigala Disawage Richard Abeywardhana to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Randenigala Disawage Richard Abeywardhana was elected as the member of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha. R. M. H. B. Rathnayaka, Returning Officer, Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,25th of February, 2026.
