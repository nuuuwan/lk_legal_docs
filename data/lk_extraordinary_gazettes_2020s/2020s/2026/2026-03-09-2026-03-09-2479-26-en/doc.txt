PART III - LANDS Title Registration NOTICE CALLING FOR CLAIMS TO LAND PARCELS Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 (Section 12) NOTICE No. : 41/0579 PUTTALAM DISTRICT IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 410150 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Galatabendiyaawa (Block 02, Sheet No. 02) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.643/1 - Mahameddewa in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Nawagaththegama in the District of Puttalam in the Province of North Western should submit his claim to the undersigned before 23rd of March 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 410150 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s Office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Title Settlement. P. C. D. SIGERA, Commissioner General of Land Title Settlement. Land Title Settlement Department, No. 1200/6, “Mihikatha Medura”, Rajamalwatta Road, Battaramulla, 23rd February, 2026. EOG 03 - 0094/1
