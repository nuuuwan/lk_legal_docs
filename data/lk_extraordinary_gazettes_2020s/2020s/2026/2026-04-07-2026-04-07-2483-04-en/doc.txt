(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BERUWALA URBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of the Beruwala Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of not attending meetings of the Council for a period of three months without permission;

And whereas the Leader of the Independent Group, to which Mr. Mohamed Ishhak the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate a candidate of that Ward Number 03 - China Fort of that Beruwala Urban Council who belongs to such Independent Group and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated the decision to nominate Mr. Ahamed Ilman Ahamed Ishkar to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, WannakuwattaWaduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the Beruwala Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mr. Ahamed Ilman Ahamed Ishkar was elected as the member of the of Ward Number 03 - China Fort of the Beruwala Urban Council. Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer, Beruwala Urban Council. District Elections Office,Kalutara,07th of April, 2026.
