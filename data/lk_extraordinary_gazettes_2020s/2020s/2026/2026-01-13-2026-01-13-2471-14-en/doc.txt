PART III — LANDS

Land Acquisition Notices

1A - G 046073 - 13 (01/2026)

THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460)

Notice under Section 7

My Ref. No. : DIVPG/LND/LA/AQURE/3/6/2019/01. Ministry of Land Reference No. : 4-3/7/2019/HW/160. I, hereby give notice, under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, as amended by the Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1964, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land or servitude to be acquired : Undermentioned land shown in the Surveyor General's plan of PP 2380 situated in the Village of Polwaththegedara in the Grama Niladari Division of No. 891, Madalassa Kalugamuwa, in Polgahawela Divisional Secretary's Division in Kurunegala District of North Western Province.

01. Lot No. 55 Name of the land: Ihalawela Pillewa; Description: Garden; Claimant : S. P. Malani Sunilwathi; Original Claimant : -; Extent: 0.0159 Hectare.

All persons interested in the aforesaid land on which a servitude is to be acquired are hereby required to appear personally or by agents duly authorized in writing, before me at Polgahawela Divisional Secretariat, on the 2026.01.29 at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the of 14.01.2026 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for acquisition for the said land/servitude the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount. R. M. S. N. Rajapaksha, Divisional Secretary, Polgahawela. Divisional Secretariat, Polgahawela, 06th of January, 2026. EOG 01-0117
