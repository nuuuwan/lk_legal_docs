NATIONAL CONSUMER PRICE INDEX COMPUTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF CENSUS AND STATISTICS

(Base : 2021 = 100) IT is hereby notified for general information that the National Consumer Price Index computed by the Department of Census and Statistics for the month of January 2026 was 211.4 (Two Hundred and Eleven point Four).

K. M. D. S. D. Karunarathne, Director General of Census and Statistics.
