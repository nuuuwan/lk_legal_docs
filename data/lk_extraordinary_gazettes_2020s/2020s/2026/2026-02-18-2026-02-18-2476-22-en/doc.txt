(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE WARAKAPOLA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Balasooriya Arachchillage Geyumi Kawya Balasooriya the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Weerasooriya Achchillage Mihiri Madhushi Weerasooriya to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Karunathilaka Ralahamilage Wasundara Erandi Dissanayake, Returning Officer of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Weerasooriya Achchillage Mihiri Madhushi Weerasooriya was elected as the member of the Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha. K. R. Wasundara E. Dissanayake, Returning Officer, Warakapola Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kegalle,18th of February, 2026.
