Appointments & C., by the Governors

NORTH CENTRAL PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Appointments made by the Hon. Governor of North Central Province

IT is hereby notify that, as per the instructions made by Hon. Governor, appointments of officers for the posts of secretaries of new Provincial Ministries have been made as follows by revising the subjects of the 05 ministries belongs to North Central Province with effect from 01.02.2026 by virtue of the power vested on him under the provisions made in the section No. 32(1) of Provincial Council's Act, No. 42 of 1987 and as per the provision made in the section No. 154 E(1) and (2) of the 13th amended constitution of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

1. In accordance with the subject revision of the Ministries of the North Central Provincial Council, Mr. K. M. H. S. K. Jayalath of Sri Lanka Administrative Service in Special Grade as the secretary to the Chief Ministry of North Central Provincial Council and Provincial Ministry of Education, Information Technology, Law and Order, Finance and Planning, Special Projects, Tourism, Sports and Cultural Affairs of North Central Province with effect from 01.02.2026.

2. In accordance with the subject revision of the Ministries of the North Central Provincial Council, Mr. M. Sriskanda Kumar of Sri Lanka Administrative Service in Special Grade as the secretary to the Ministry of Co-operatives, Trade and food, Transport, Industrial Development of North Central Province with effect from 01.02.2026 until further an order is made.
