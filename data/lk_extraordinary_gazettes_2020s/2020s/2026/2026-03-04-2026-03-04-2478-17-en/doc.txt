NORTHERN PROVINCE PROVINCIAL COUNCIL

Notice of Governor of the Northern Province

1. An inquiry to be conducted under Section 184 of the Urban Concils Ordinance (Chapter 255) read together with Section 2 of the Provincial Councils (Consequential Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989.

2. I, the Governor of the Northern Province, in terms of the powers vested in me by Section 184 of the Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255), read together with Section 2 of the Provincilal Councils (Consequntial Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 1989, have appointed Mr. K. Ariyanayagam, Retired District Judge, as the Commissioner to inquire into, examine, and report within three months on the administration and affairs of the Mannar Municipal Council, in terms of Section 184 (1A) of the said Ordinance.

3. Matters to be Inquired into/ Allegations

I. The Standard by Laws for conducting the meetings of the Mannar Urban Council, which were framed by the Minister in charge of the subject of Local Government of Northern Province, approved by the Northern Provincial Council, and duly published, have been adopted and are in force in the Mannar Urban Council.

However, the meetings held on the dates mentioned below have been conducted in violation of the said Standard by laws

Regarding the 2nd General Meeting of the Mannar Urban Council held on 09.07.2025

i. The total number of members of the Mannar Urban Council is 16, In terms of Section 4(v)(a) of the Standard By Law relating to the conduct of Council meetings, a minimum of 06 members is required to constitute a quorum for holding a meeting. Although the minutes of the meeting states that the meeting commenced at 9.30 a.m., according to the attendance register only 05 members had signed at 9.30 a.m. Therefore, the Council meeting held on 09.07.2025 was conducted without the required quorum in terms of Section 4(v)(a) of the procedural rules.

ii. Although the minutes states that the Council meeting held on 09.07.2025 commenced at 9.30 a.m. the Chairman had signed the attendance register only at 9.38 a.m. There is no indication in the minutes as to who presided over the meeting at 9.30 a.m. In terms of Section 24 of the Urban Councils Ordinance No. 61 of 1939, it is provided that when the Chairman is not present at a meeting, the Vice-Chairman shall preside, and in the absence of both the Chairman and the Vice-Chairman, the members present shall elect one among themselves to preside over the meeting. However, the said Council meeting had been conducted contrary to Section 24 of the Urban Councils Ordinance, and the minutes of the meeting have been signed by the Chairman as if he had presided over the meeting.

The meeting of the Mannar Urban Council which was held on 09.07.2025 was adjourned and reconvened on 17.07.2025.

iii. In terms of Section 4(v)(a) of the Standard by Laws, a minimum of 06 members in required to constitute a quorum for holding a Council meeting. At 9.30 a.m. on 17.07.2025, although the meeting was scheduled to commence, only 04 members were present at that time according to the attendance tegister. Therefore, the Council meeting held on 17.07.2025 was conducted without the required quorum in accordance with Section 4(v)(a) of the standard by Laws

iv. Although the minutes state that the Council meeting held on 17.07.2025 commenced at 9.30 a.m., the Chairman had signed the attendance register only at 9.43 a.m. There is no record in the minutes indicating who presided over the meeting at 9.30 a.m. In terms of Section 24 of the Urban Councils Ordinance No. 61 of 1939, it is provided that when the Chairman is not present at a meeting, the vice-chairman shall preside. and in the absence of both the Chairman and the Vice-Chairman, the members present shall elect one among themselves to preside over the meeting, However, the Council meeting held on 17.07.2025 was conducted contrary to Section 24 of the Urban Councils Ordinance, and the minutes have been signed by the Chairman as if he had presided over the meeting.

v. Nine resolutions were adopted at the meeting of the Mannar Urban Council held on 17th July 2025. The minutes of the meeting record that eight members, forming the majority, supported all nine resolutions, and therfore, they were approved. According to Standard by laws, Section 19, it is clearly stated that these nine resolutions must be conducted in accordance with the porper Council procedures, specifying the steps to be followed. However, when adopting these nine resolutions, certain procedures were not properly considered before executing them.

a. It was not written in the meeting minutes which members presented opinions for and against the motions.

b. The motions were not accepted unanimously.

c. The motion was passed by vote. However, the number of members who voted against the motion and who they were was not written in the meeting minutes.

d. The number of members who abstained from voting and who they were was not written in the meeting minutes.

e. The total number of members of the Mannar Urban Council is 16, The members who attended the meeting the meeting are 16 people. It is recorded that the above 09 resolutions were passed with the support of 08 majority members. Since 08 people supported, equal votes were received. Nothing about the chairman’s additional casting vote being cast would have been written in the minutes of the meeting, which states that the said resolutions were passed with the support of majority members.
