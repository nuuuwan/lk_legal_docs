Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational EducationAmendments

Competitive Examination for the recruitment of Graduates to the Sri Lanka Teachers’ Service for Sinhala, Tamil and English medium Teacher Vacancies in National and Provincial Schools of the Island - 2026

Notification published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2474/19 dated 02.02.2026 regarding the calling of applications for the aforesaid competitive examination;

1. Is hereby amended as follows.

• In Note 3,

There shall be no impediment for those who have completed the degree qualifications to apply in terms of this Gazette notification: graduates who were in the public service as at 10.02.2023 and remain in service to date, who were ineligible to apply under the Gazette notifications mentioned in Note 2 above as at 10.02.2023, but had not exceeded 40 years of age by 10.02.2023 and completed the degree qualifications after 10.02.2023 and before 30.06.2025; and those who were in the public service as at 10.02.2023 but have since resigned from said position with formal approval, having completed the degree qualifications prior to 30.06.2025.
