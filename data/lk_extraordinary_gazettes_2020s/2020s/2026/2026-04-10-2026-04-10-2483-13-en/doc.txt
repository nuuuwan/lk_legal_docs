TAKE OVER BASE HOSPITAL - PUTTALAM BY THE LINE MINISTRY OF HEALTH ANDMASS MEDIA FOR DEVELOPMENT AND UPGRADING

BASE Hospital - Puttalam, located in the Puttalam District, is currently administered by the North Western Provincial Council. It is one of the larger hospitals in the province. Considering the prevailing issues in the hospital's development, it has been proposed to take over the hospital under the Line Ministry of Health and Mass Media.

This is to inform that, in accordance with Cabinet Decision No. CP/26/0349/815/023 dated 03.03.2026, and with the concurrence of the Governor of the North Western Province, Base Hospital - Puttalam will be taken over by the Line Ministry of Health and Mass Media for the purpose of development and upgrading. This action is subject to the observations and recommendations of the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development and the Public Service Commission.

Dr. Anil Jasinghe, Secretary, Ministry of Health and Mass Media .

No. 385, “Suwasiripaya”, Rev. Baddegama Wimalawansa Thero Mawatha,Colombo 10.March 31, 2026.
