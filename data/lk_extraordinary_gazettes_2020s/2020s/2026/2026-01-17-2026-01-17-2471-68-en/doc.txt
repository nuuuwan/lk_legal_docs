NATIONAL TRANSPORT COMMISSION ACT, No. 37 OF 1991

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development under paragraphs (h) and (j) of section 44 of the National Transport Commission Act, No. 37 of 1991 read with sections 18, 24, 28 and 30 of the said Act.

Bimal Niroshan Rathnayaka, Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.

Colombo, 16th January, 2026.

Regulations

1. These regulations may be cited as the Transfer of Passenger Service Permits Regulations, No. 1 of 2026.

2. A holder of a valid passenger service permit (in these regulations referred to as the “transferor”) who seeks to transfer such permit to another person or entity shall –

ශ්‍රී ලංංකාා ප්‍රජාාතාාන්ත්‍රික සමාාජවාාදී ජනරජයේɏ ගැැසට් පත්‍රය

(a) ensure that he is the owner of the respective omnibus to whom the certificate of registration has been issued under section 9 of the Motor Traffic Act (Chapter 203);

(b) submit an application, for obtaining the prior approval of the Commission for the transfer of the passenger service permit, to the Chairman of the Commission in writing or electronically substantially in the form set out in Schedule I along with the required documentation specified in Schedule II and the application processing fee specified in item 1 of Schedule III;

(c) ensure that the relevant passenger service permit is devoid of any judicial proceeding, public complaint, institutional investigation, contract, agreement or any other legal obligation prior to submission of the application under paragraph (b);

(d) ensure that any fees, logbook fees, entrance fees, fines, loans or any other payments in respect of the passenger service permit have been duly settled without any deficit prior to submission of the application under paragraph (b);

(e) revoke or cancel any registered power of attorney, either submitted to the Commission or otherwise by such transferor;

(f) continue to operate the respective omnibus until the passenger service permit is granted by the Commission to the person to whom the transfer is to be effected (in these regulations referred to as the “transferee”); and

(g) not be entitled to claim ownership of the passenger service permit once the transfer of such permit is done.

3. Any person intending to become a transferee of a valid passenger service permit (in these regulations referred to as the “transferee”) shall, within one week of receiving possession of the respective omnibus and obtaining the certificate of registration under section 9 of the Motor Traffic Act (Chapter 203), make an application to the Chairman of the Commission in writing or electronically substantially in the form set out in Schedule IV along with the required documentation specified in Schedule V and the application processing fee specified in item 2 of Schedule III.

4. The transferee shall ensure that he has been maintaining a balance of two million rupees covering a period of six months immediately preceding the submission of the application referred to in regulation 3 and such sustained financial competence of the transferee shall be proved by a bank statement to that effect.

5. The Commission may, subject to the provisions of sections 18 and 24 of the Act, within two weeks of receiving the application referred to in regulation 3 or on such later date as may be notified by the Commission, grant or refuse to grant the passenger service permit to the transferee.

6. The transferee shall be entitled to obtain the passenger service permit, where-

(a) the passenger service permit is transferred to an individual or company; and

(b) the transfer to be effected on kinship or relationship,

on payment of the fees specified in items 3 and 4 of Schedule III respectively.

7. The transferee shall be entitled to operate the respective omnibus only on the route specified in the passenger service permit in compliance with the provisions of the Act and applicable regulations, and according to the terms and conditions of such permit and time tables for the journeys on the relevant route prepared by the Commission from time to time.

8. Where the total approved number of passenger service permits pertaining to a particular route amount to four or more than four, a transferee shall be entitled to hold only such number of passenger service permits which does not exceed twenty-five per centum of such total amount.

9. Where the transferee has been granted the passenger service permit by the Commission, the transferee shall be entitled to apply to the Commission, along with the fee specified in item 5 of Schedule III, to renew such permit in accordance with the provisions of section 30 of the Act.

10. The transferee shall not transfer the passenger service permit granted by the Commission to any person or entity until a minimum period of two years has lapsed from the date of such grant by the Commission.

11. For the purposes of these regulations –

“Act” means the National Transport Commission Act, No. 37 of 1991;

“Commission” means the National Transport Commission established under section 2 of the National Transport Commission Act, No. 37 of 1991;

“electronic” shall have the same meaning assigned to it by the Electronic Transactions Act, No. 19 of 2006;

“qualified auditor” means a qualified auditor defined under Article 154(8) of the Constitution of the Democratic

Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka; and

“Registrar General” means the Registrar General referred to in section 2 of the Powers of Attorney Ordinance (Chapter 122).

(a) “Normal Service” means a permitted omnibus service on approved routes, in which the carriage of seated and standing passengers is permitted.
