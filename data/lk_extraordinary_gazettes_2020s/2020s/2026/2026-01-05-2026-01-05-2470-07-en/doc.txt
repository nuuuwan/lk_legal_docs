Local Government Notifications

No. 2470/07 – MONDAY, JANUARY 05, 2026

PRADESHIYA SABHA - DERANIYAGALA

IT is hereby notified for the public information that it has been decided at the meeting No. 01(XXVII) of Pradeshiya Sabha held on 11th December 2025 that all the roads and thoroughfares set out in the following schedule should be declared as the roads and thoroughfares owned by Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha in terms of orders made by the series of “Orders on designing of plans of the roads and thoroughfares maintained by Pradeshiya Sabha’s of Sabaragamuwa Province and publication of notifications specifying the list of roads and thoroughfares’’ published by Extraordinary Gazette No. 2316/20 dated 24th of January 2023 by the Hon. Governor of Sabaragamuwa Province and in terms of the order by Section 24(1) of Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987.

It is hereby notified that, if it is the opinion of the party claiming the said land that any road or thoroughfare included in the said schedule passes through any land or a part of any land of that party, a case should be filed in the competent Court to prove his right to the said land before the expiry of a period of three months from the date of this notification.

It is further notified that all the roads and thoroughfares, against which a legal action had not been taken before the expiry of three months, mentioned in that schedule, shall be considered as roads and thoroughfares owned by Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

R.Indika Prasad Ranasinghe, Chairman, Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha.

At the Deraniyagala Pradeshiya Sabha Office,15th December, 2025.
