UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(l)(b) read with Section 25A

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 27(1)(b) read with Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of the Lanka Nippon BizTech Institute (Private) Limited a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 and bearing Registration Number PV 109910 and located at No. 278, High Level Road, Maharagama and at Sathara Building, 1/1/3, Mahamegawaththa Road, Maharagama, being recognized as a Degree Awarding Institute, do by this Order amend the Schedule II to the order made under the said Act, and published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2286/08 of June 27, 2022, by the addition immediately after entry “Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Software Engineering” appearing in Column I and its corresponding entry in Column II thereof, of the entries specified under Column I and II respectively of the Schedule II hereto, subject to the conditions specified in Schedule I hereto:-
