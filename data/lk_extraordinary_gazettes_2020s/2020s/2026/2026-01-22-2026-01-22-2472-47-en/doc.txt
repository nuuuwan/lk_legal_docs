NATIONAL CONSUMER PRICE INDEX COMPUTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF CENSUS AND STATISTICS

(Base : 2021 = 100) IT is hereby notified for general information that the National Consumer Price Index computed by the Department of Census and Statistics for the month of December 2025 was 210.5 (Two Hundred and Ten point Five).

K. M. D. S. D. Karunarathne, Director General of Census and Statistics.
