(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THECHAVAKACHCHERI PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of return member of Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of ceased to be member of the Party;

And whereas the Secretary of the Thamizh Makkal Koottani, the recognized political party to which Kamalathasan Rajivarnan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Nakan Balasuppiramaniyam to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Nakan Balasuppiramaniyam was elected as the member of the Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha. R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Chavakachcheri Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Jaffna,24th March of 2026.
