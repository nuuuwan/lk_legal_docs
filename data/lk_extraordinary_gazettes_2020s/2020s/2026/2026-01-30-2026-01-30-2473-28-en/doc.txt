(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GAMPAHA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Mudunkotuwage Jayasiri Gunawardhana the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number No. 8 - Pahala Yagoda of Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Jayasooriya Arachchige Prabhash Ranga Jayasooriya to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Jayasooriya Arachchige Prabhash Ranga Jayasooriya was elected as the member of the Ward Number No. 8 - Pahala Yagoda of Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Gampaha,30th of January, 2026.
