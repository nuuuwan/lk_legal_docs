The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE KULIYAPITIYA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of member of Kuliyapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of the resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Kumbal Gamayalage Malani Gunathilaka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward No. 21 Kalugamuwa of Kuliyapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Anhetti Gedara Sudharma Janaki Danapala to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of the Kuliyapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Anhetti Gedara Sudharma Janaki Danapala was elected as the member of the ward No. 21 Kalugamuwa of Kuliyapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. R. M. H. B. Rathnayaka, Returning Officer, Kuliyapitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,27th January, 2026.
