SRI LANKA ELECTRICITY ACT, No. 20 OF 2009

Order under Sub section (1) of the Section 46

BY virtue of the powers vested under Sub section (1) of the Section 46 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009 (as amended), the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka does hereby impose an annual levy in such amounts as are set out in Column II of the Schedule hereto, to be recovered from those licensees holding such licences as are specified in the corresponding entry in Column I of that schedule. The previous Gazette Notification published in the Extraordinary Gazette No. 2267/13 on Tuesday 15th February 2022, is hereby revoked.

Chairman, Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka.

Level 06,Bank of Ceylon Merchant Tower,No. 28, St. Michael’s Road,Colombo 03,04th March 2026.
