(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE GALIGAMUWA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Gligamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be a member of the party,

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the Sarvajana Balaya, the recognized political party to which Godayalage Lalith Rohana Pieris the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Gligamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Asura Pedige Ajith Rohana Kumara Karunarathne to fill such vacancy;

THEREFORE I, Karunathilaka Ralahamilage Wasundara Erandi Dissanayake, Returning Officer of the Gligamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Asura Pedige Ajith Rohana Kumara Karunarathne was selected as the member of the Gligamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha. K. R. Wasundara E. Dissanayake, Returning Officer, Gligamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Kegalle,29th of January, 2026.
