(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEYATINUWARA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Death;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Hapugoda Ranhawadi Gedara Chanmika Nilantha the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that ward Number 01 Kobbekaduwa of Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Ajith Priyantha Muthugala to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Hewage Dhanushka Naleen Sampath Fernando, Returning Officer of the Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ajith Priyantha Muthugala was elected as the member of the Ward Number 01 Kobbekaduwa of Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha. H. D. N. Sampath Fernando, Returning Officer, Yatinuwara Pradeshiya Sabha. District Election Office,Kandy,10th of March, 2026.
