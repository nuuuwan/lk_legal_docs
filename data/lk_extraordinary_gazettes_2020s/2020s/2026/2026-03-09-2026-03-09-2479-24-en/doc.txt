Description of the Land/Servitude to be acquired: An allotment in extent of 1.0140 Hectare of land called Egodahawatta, which depicted as Lot No. 01 in the Cadastral Map bearing No. 310429 situated at E 341 B Daluwela Grama Niladhari Division, Minor Division of Asgiri Udasiya Pattu in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Yatawatta, Matale District of Central Province.

Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Egodahawatta; Description: Acquisition of plot of land for Construction of Village

Secretariat at Daluwela GN division; Claimant: Herath Walawwe Senerath Bandara Daluwela, Daluwela, Doollewa; Extent : 0.0140 Hec. And hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before 20th March, 2026 the nature of their interests in the land, the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, the amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.
