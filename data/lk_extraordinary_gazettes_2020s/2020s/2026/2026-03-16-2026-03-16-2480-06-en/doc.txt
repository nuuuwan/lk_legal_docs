and

Sri Lanka Transport Board, No. 200, Kirula Road, Narahenpita.

STATEMENT OF MATTER IN DISPUTE

The matter in dispute between the aforesaid parties is –

Whether Mr. P. V. Bandula Padmasiri, who served as a Bus Driver at the Akuressa Depot of the Sri Lanka Transport Board has been subject to injustice by the non-payment of salaries from 23.08.2019 to 26.12.2019, the period during which his service was interdicted due to his refusal to perform long-distance duties in 2019, and if so, to what relief he is entitled.

H. M. D. N. K. WATALIYADDA, Commissioner General of Labour.

Dated at the Ofﬁce of the Commissioner General of Labour,Colombo, on this 09th day of February, 2026.
