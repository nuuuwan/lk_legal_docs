(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

NOTICE UNDER SECTION 10A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE

Colombo Municipal Council

I, Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer of the Colombo Municipal Council do hereby declare under Section 10A(1)(a) of the (Chapter 262) Local Authorities Elections Ordinance that Mrs. Mohamed Buhari Fathima Sohara of the Colombo Municipal Council has vacated her office of member of that Municipal Council as she has been ceased to be a member of the Sri Lanka Muslim Congress. Piumi Attygalle, Returning Officer, Colombo Municipal Council.

District Elections Office,Colombo City Election Office, 09th February, 2026.
