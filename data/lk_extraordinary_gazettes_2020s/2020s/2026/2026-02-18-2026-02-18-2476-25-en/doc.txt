08TH AMENDMENT

SERVICE MINUTE OF THE SRI LANKA PLANNING SERVICE

THE Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Planning Service published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1670/32 dated 10th September 2010 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as amended by the Gazettes extraordinary mentioned in Part I below is amended as stated in following Part II. Other matters and conditions stated therein remain unchanged. The amended provisions set out in Part II below will be in effect from 01.01.2026.

By order of the Public Service Commission,

W. H. M. M. C. K. Dayarathne, Secretary, Public Service Commission.

On 13th February 2026.

Part II

8. Efficiency Bar

“Level - I of the course on capacity building” mentioned under the Nature of the 3rd efficiency bar in Table 8.1, as amended by the 3rd Amendment to the Service Minute, shall be amended as “Level - III of the course on capacity building”. All other matters shall remain unchanged.

10. Promotion to Grades

10.1.1.1: The following qualification shall be newly included:(vii) Should have completed Level - I of the course on capacity building.

10.2.1.1: The following qualification shall be newly included:(vi) Should have completed Level - II of the course on capacity building.

12. II. Skills Development

12. II (b): The provisions relating to In-Service Training shall be substituted by the following provisions. All other matters shall remain unchanged.

(b) Courses on Capacity Building

i. Officers in Grade III should have completed Level- I of the course on capacity building prior to promotion to Grade II.

ii. Officers in Grade II should have completed Level - II of the course on capacity building prior to promotion to Grade I.
