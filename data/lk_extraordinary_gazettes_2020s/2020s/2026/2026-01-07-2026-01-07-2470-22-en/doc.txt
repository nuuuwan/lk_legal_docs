The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE DEHIOWITA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending for council meetings for a period of three months without permission;

And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Udawalage Deepika Priyadarshani the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Thelge Yenali Chamodi Nadeera Peris to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Kasthurisinghe Herath Mudiyanselage Sajith Priyankara Kasthurisinghe, returning officer of the Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Thelge Yenali Chamodi Nadeera Peris was selected as the member of the Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha. K. H. M. Sajith P. Kasthurisinghe, Returning Officer, Dehiowita Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kegalle,06th January, 2026.
