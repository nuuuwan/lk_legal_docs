(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEWATTALA - MABOLA URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Wattala - Mabola Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Jathika Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Wanigasooriya Arachchige Don Prasadini Surangi Jeewani Wanigasooriya the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Wattala - Mabola Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Malathi Indra Sudusinghe to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Ravindra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Wattala - Mabola Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Malathi Indra Sudusingha was elected as the member of the Wattala - Mabola Urban Council. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Wattala - Mabola Urban Council. District Elections Office,Gampaha,11th March, 2026.
