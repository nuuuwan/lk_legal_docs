THE LAND ACQUISITION ACT (CHAPTER 460) Notice under Section 7 Land Ministry's Reference No.: 4/3/15/2018/VE/294. Divisional Secretary Ref. No.: KMB/LAN/ACQUI/02/90. I do hereby give notice under Sub section (1) of Section 7 of the Land Acquisition Act, that the Government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which is required for a public purpose. Description of the land or servitude to be acquired : Allotments of land hereinunder described and depicted in Block No. 05 and Sheet No. 2 the Cadastral Map No. 820239, situated in the Grama Niladhari Division No. 330 of Kamburupitiya in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Kamburupitiya in Matara District, Southern Province. 1. Lot No. 01 Name of the Land: Ratalankawatta; Description: Garden, Cinnamon Cultivation of about 15 - 20 years, Mixed Crop Cultivation, 2 Jak fruit trees of around 20 - 25 year and 4 Jak fruit trees of around 5 - 10 years; Original claimant: Land Reform Commission, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07; Extent: 0.2317 Hectare.

2. Lot No. 02 Name of the Land: Ratalankawatta; Description: Law Jungle; Original claimant: Land Reform Commission, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07; Extent: 0.9826 Hectare. All persons interested in the aforesaid land/servitude are hereby required to appear personally, or by agents duly authorized in writing before me at the Divisional Secretariat, Kamburupitiya, on the 23rd day of March, 2026, at 10.00 a.m. and are hereby requested to notify me in writing (in duplicate) on or before the 16th day of March 2026, the nature of their interests in the aforesaid lands the particulars of their claims for compensation for the acquisition of the said land/servitude, amount of compensation and the details of the computation of such amount.
