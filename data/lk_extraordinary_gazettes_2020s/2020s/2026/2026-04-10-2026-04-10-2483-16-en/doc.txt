EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEMUTUR PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1) (b) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the Office of return member of Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of resign;

And whereas the Secretary of the National Congress, the recognized political party to which Muhammadhu Unas Muhammadhu Nimsath the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Jawaheer Mohamed Aski to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Selladhore Kaliyuganathan Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer of the Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Jawaheer Mohamed Aski was elected as the member of the Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha. S. K. Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer, Mutur Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Trincomalee,07th of April 2026.
