Amendment to Governor's Memorandum No.2026/01

Order under the Section 32(2) of Provincial Councils Act, No.42 of 1987 amended byProvincial Council's (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 1990 on delegation of powers of Eastern Provincial Public Service

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 32(1) of Provincial Councils Act, No.42 of 1987 as Amended by Provincial Councils (Amendment) Act, No.28 of 1990, I, Jayantha Lal Ratnasekera, the Governor of Eastern Province do by this order amend the Sub-section I of Paragraph 4(A) of Schedule 2 of Order made by me under the above headings published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2468/10 of December 23, 2025 with effect from 01.01.2026.

(1) Paragraph (4)(A) I of Schedule 2 is hereby amended by insertion of new phrase as “In case of Sri Lanka Educational Administrative Service, the power of disciplinary control shall be exercised by the Chief Secretary”,and substituted as follows;

“The powers of placements, transfers and preliminary investigation of officers belonging to the All-Island Services “(in case of Sri Lanka Educational Administrative Service, the power of disciplinary control shall be exercised by the Chief Secretary)”, as categorized under paragraph 01 of Appendix I annexed hereto as released or attached on the basis of secondment to the Provincial Public Service in terms of Public Administration Circular No. 56/89 dated 15.11.1989, as amended subsequently by circular No.56/89(i) dated 23.04.1993 and No.56/89(ii)
