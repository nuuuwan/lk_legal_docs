DRUG DEPENDANT PERSONS (TREATMENT AND REHABILITATION) ACT, No. 54 OF 2007

Order under Section 2

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by section 2 of the Drug Dependant Persons (Treatment and Rehabilitation) Act, No. 54 of 2007, I, K.M. Ananda Wijepala, Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs, on the recommendation of the National Dangerous Drug Control Board, do by this Order, designate the places specified in the Schedule hereto to be Treatment and Rehabilitation Centres for the purpose of providing care and rehabilitation to drug dependant persons.

K. M. Ananda Wijepala Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs.

Colombo,February 21, 2026.
