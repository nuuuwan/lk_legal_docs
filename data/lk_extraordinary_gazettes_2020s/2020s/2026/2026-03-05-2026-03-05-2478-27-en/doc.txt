Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998

(Section 12)

NOTICE No : 31/0511 MATALE DISTRICT

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 310063 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Ihala Erevula (Block 08, Sheet No. 05) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.E/446C - Ihala Erevula in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Dambulla in the District of Matale in the Province of Central Province should submit his claim to the undersigned before 5th of March, 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 310063 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office. Further information may be obtained from the Grama Niladhari, the Divisional Secretary, or the Commissioner of Land Title Settlement. P. C. D. SIGERA, Commissioner General of Land Title Settlement.
