(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEAMBALANGODA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Ambalangoda Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Leader of the Independent Group to which Dampelle Gamage Janaka Deepthi Kumara the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Ambalangoda Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such Independent Group and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Leader of the aforesaid Independent Group has communicated his decision to nominate Olagamage Upul Chanda Kumara to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Wewelwala Hewage Ravindra Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer of the Ambalangoda Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Olagamage Upul Chanda Kumara was elected as the member of the Ambalangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. W. H. R. Wijaya Kumara, Returning Officer, Ambalangoda Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Galle,04th March, 2026.
