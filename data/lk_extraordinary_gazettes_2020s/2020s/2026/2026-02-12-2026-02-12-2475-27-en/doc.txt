(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE VALIKAMAM NORTH PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1)(a) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of member of Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending for council meetings for a period of three months without permission;

And whereas the Secretary of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi, the recognized political party to which Kanthasamy Mayoorathan the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon the member of that Ward Number 19 Alavetty South-West of Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Raveenthiran Thanushiyan to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Raveenthiran Thanushiyan was elected as the member of the Ward Number 19 Alavetty South-West of Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha. R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Valikamam North Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Jaffna,11th of February, 2026.
