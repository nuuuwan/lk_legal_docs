STAMP DUTY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, No. 12 OF 2006

Order under Section 5

BY virtue of the powers vested in me under Section 5 of the Stamp Duty (Special Provisions) Act, No. 12 of 2006, I, Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, do by this Order, amend the Order made under that Section and published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1465/20 dated October 05, 2006 as last amended by the Order published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2312/73 dated December 31, 2022, by the addition of the following new item immediately after item 37 of the Schedule thereto.

“38. Any receipt given for payments made by the Government, to any person affected by a natural disaster.”

Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, Colombo 01,February 24, 2026.
