(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEALAWWA PRADESHIYA SABHAUNDER SECTION 66A(1)(b) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of a returned member of the Alawwa Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of not attending consecutine council meetings for a period of three months without permission;

And whereas the Secretary of Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized Political party to which Mrs. Senarathna Mudiyanselage Yasintha Sajeewanie Senarathna the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A (1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Alawwa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political Party has communicated the decision to nominate Mrs. Jayasundara Herath Mudiyanselage Lakshmi Sanjila Jayasundara to fill the said vacancy;

Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayaka, returning Officer of the Alawwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A (1) (b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mrs. Jayasundara Herath Mudiyanselage Lakshmi Sanjila Jayasundara was elected as the member of the Alawwa Pradeshiya Sabha.

R. M. H. B. Rathnayaka, Returning Officer, Alawwa Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Kurunegala,07th April, 2026.
