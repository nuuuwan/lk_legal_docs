THE UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 27(1) read with Section 21 and Section 139

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 27(1) of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, on the recommendation of the University Grants Commission, do by this Order, amend the Schedule to the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 1469/38, of November 03, 2006 (as amended from time to time) which specifies the Faculties and the Departments of the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, which is deemed by virtue of the provisions of Section 139 of the aforesaid Act, to be a University established under Section 21 of the said Act, by the substitution for the item “10. Department of Textile and Clothing Technology” appearing in Column II of the Schedule which corresponds to the item “Faculty of Engineering” appearing in Column I of the Schedule, (specified in the Order published in Gazette Extraordinary, No. 2037/18, of September 21, 2017) of the following entry:-

“10. Department of Textile and Apparel Engineering”

Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education.Colombo,26th February, 2026.
