PILGRIMAGES ORDINANCE

IT is hereby notified that the Annual Sri Maha Bodhiraja Procession of Sri Shasanalankararama Ancient Temple in the Grama Niladhari Division of Udugama Divisional Secretary's Division of Nagoda, District of Galle, Southern Province, will be held from 29th June 2026 to 12th July 2026 as per the regulations published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 1956/11 dated 01.03.2016 of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka in terms of the Section 2 of the Pilgrimages Ordinance (Chapter 175).

K. U. Chandralal, District Secretary/Government Agent, Administrative District, Galle. At the District Secretariat, Galle,23rd of February, 2026.
