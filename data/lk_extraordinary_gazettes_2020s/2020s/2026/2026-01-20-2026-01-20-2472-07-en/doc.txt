(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Notices under the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

NOTICE GIVEN UNDER SECTION 66 G OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS ORDINANCE AS AMENDED BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTIONS(AMENDMENT) ACT, NO. 22 OF 2012.

I, Lekam Mudiyanselage Pragnakeerthi Wijesundara Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province, hereby announce that the meeting for the purpose of electing the Chairman, for the Local Government named under the annexure here to situated in the Sabaragamuwa Province, will be convened on the date and at the time specified in the said annexure.

L. M. P. W. Bandara, Commissioner of Local Government, Sabaragamuwa Province. Department of Local Government,Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council,New Town,Ratnapura.20th January 2026.
