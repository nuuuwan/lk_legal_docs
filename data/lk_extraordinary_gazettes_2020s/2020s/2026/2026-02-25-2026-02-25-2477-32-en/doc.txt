Notices Under The Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

DEPARTMENT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT - EASTERN PROVINCE

Notice on summon meeting of under the Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012

BY virtue of power vested on me under Section 66(C) read with Section 66(G) of Local Authorities Elections Ordinance amended by Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, I, Adam Lebbe Mohamed Azmi, the Commissioner of Local Government of Eastern Province do hereby summon the re-calling meeting to fill the vacancy arose to the post of Chairman of the Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha, Ampara District, Eastern Province at 10.00 a.m. on 09.03.2026 at Ninthavur Pradeshiya Sabha Hall for the purpose of elect the new Chairman.

A. L. M. Azmi, Commissioner of Local Government, Eastern Province.

Department of Local Government,Eastern Province,Kanniya Road, Varothaya Nagar, Trincomalee,On this 25th day of February, 2026.
