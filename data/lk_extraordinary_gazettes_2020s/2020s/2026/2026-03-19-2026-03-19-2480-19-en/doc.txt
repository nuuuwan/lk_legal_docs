EXTRAORDINARYThe Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THEKATTANKUDY URBAN COUNCILUNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Kattankudy Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the National Front for Good Governance the recognized political party to which Abdul Latheef Muhammadu Sabeel the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Kattankudy Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Masooth Faizul Maha to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Kattankudy Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Masooth Faizul Maha was elected as the member of the Kattankudy Urban Council. Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer, Kattankudy Urban Council. District Elections Office,Batticaloa,12th March, 2026.
