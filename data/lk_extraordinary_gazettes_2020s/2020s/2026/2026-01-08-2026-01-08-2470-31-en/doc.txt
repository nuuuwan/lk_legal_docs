LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONS ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

Appointment in Accordance with the Provisions of Section 66C.(4) of the Local Government Institutes Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, as amended by the Local Government Institutes Elections (Amendment)Act, No. 22 of 2012

I hereby announce that the elected Officials for the posts of Chairman and Vice Chairman elected under Section 66C.(4) of the Local Government Institutes Elections Ordinance, Chapter 262, As amended by the Local Government Institutes Election (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012, in relation to Anamaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha in North Western Province are as specified in the schedule below.

H. M. Sanjeewanee Herath, Commissioner of Local Government, North Western Province. Department of Local Government,2nd Floor, Vidusara,Provincial Council Office Complex,Kurunegala,08th of January 2026.
