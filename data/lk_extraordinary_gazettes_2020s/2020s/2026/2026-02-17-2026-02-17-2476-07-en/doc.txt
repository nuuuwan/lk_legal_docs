(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE MANNAR URBAN COUNCIL UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Mannar Urban Council has fallen vacant by reason of being ceased to be a member of the party;

And whereas the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Labour Party the recognized political party to which Xavier Johns Polington the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Mannar Urban Council who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Jesuthasan Anton Sahayathasan Perera to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Kangatharan Mugunthan, Returning Officer of the Mannar Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Jesuthasan Anton Sahayathasan Perera was selected as the member of the Mannar Urban Council. K. Mugunthan, Returning Officer, Mannar Urban Council. District Elections Office,Mannar,13th of February, 2026.
