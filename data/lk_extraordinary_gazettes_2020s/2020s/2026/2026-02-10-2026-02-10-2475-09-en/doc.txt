UNIVERSITIES ACT, No. 16 OF 1978

Order under Section 25A

BY virtue of powers vested in me by Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978, I, Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education having obtained a report under Section 70C of the aforesaid Act, in respect of “Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology Lanka (Private) Limited”, a company incorporated in Sri Lanka under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007 bearing Registration Number PV 3052 and located at No. 388, Union Place, Colombo 02 and No. 85, Braybrook Place, Colombo 02, do by this Order subject to such conditions as specified in Schedule I hereto, recognize “Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology Lanka (Private) Limited” as a Degree Awarding Institute for the purpose of developing Higher Education therein, leading to the award of the Degrees specified in Column I of the Schedule II hereto, of which the abbreviation is set out in the corresponding entry in Column II of the Schedule II hereto:-
