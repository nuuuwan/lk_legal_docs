L.D.B 6/2022 COMPANIES ACT, No. 07 OF 2007

REGULATIONS made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development under section 527 of the Companies Act, No. 07 of 2007 read with sections 130A, 130C, 130D, 130I and 130I of the aforesaid Act.

Wasantha Samarasinghe, Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development.

Colombo,21st March, 2026.

Regulations

1. (l) These regulations may be cited as Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations, No. 01 of 2026.

(2) The provisions of these regulations shall come into operation on March 30, 2026.

2. (1) Every company incorporated or registered under the Companies Act, No. 7 of 2007, shall, at the time of its incorporation or registration, as the case may be, notify the Registrar of the details of its beneficial owners in Form BO 1 set out in Schedule I.

(2) Every company shall, within twenty working days of the issue of shares or the transfer of shares, as the case may be, notify the Registrar of the details of its beneficial owners in Form BO 2 or Form BO 3, respectively, set out in Schedule I.

3. Every company shall deliver to the Registrar, together with its annual return, the details of the beneficial ownership of the company in Form BO 4 set out in Schedule I.

4. Every company shall appoint a natural person as an authorised person under subsection (1) of section 130C of the Act and shall notify the Registrar of such appointment and any subsequent changes to the authorised person in Form BO 5 set out in Schedule I.

5. Any change in the location of the register of beneficial owners of a company shall be notified to the Registrar in Form BO 6 set out in Schedule I.

6. (1) Every company that has beneficial owners as at the date on which these regulations come into force shall -

(a) forward to the Registrar the details relating to the beneficial owners of the company; and

(b) verify, report and notify the Registrar of the details relating to the beneficial owners of the company,

in Form BO 7 set out in Schedule I.

(2) Every depository of a licensed stock exchange shall verify, report and notify the Registrar of the details relating to the beneficial owners of a company as at the date on which these regulations come into force in Form BO 7 set out in Schedule I.

7. Any change to the information relating to a beneficial owner, including a change of name, address, contact details, nationality, National Identity Card number, Tax Identification Number, or passport number, shall be notified to the Registrar within fourteen working days of such change through the Beneficial Ownership Change Module of the eROC system.

8. The fee payable to the Registrar in respect of the notification, delivery, verification, or reporting of the details of beneficial owners shall be paid in accordance with the relevant forms specified in Column I of Schedule II hereto, for the matters specified in the corresponding entries in Column II, at the rates specified in the corresponding entries in Column III of that Schedule.
