NOTICE No. : 12/0738 POLONNARUWA DISTRICT

It is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 120199 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Pimburaththewa (Block 02, Sheet No. 01) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.236 - Pimburaththawa East in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Dimbulagala in the District of Polonnaruwa in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 6th of April 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act.
