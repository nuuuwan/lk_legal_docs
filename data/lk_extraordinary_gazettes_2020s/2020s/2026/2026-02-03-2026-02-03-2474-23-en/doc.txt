(Published by Authority)PART IV (B) — LOCAL GOVERNMENT

FILLING OF A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF MEMBER OF THE BIYAGAMA PRADESHIYA SABHA UNDER SECTION 66A(1) OF THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES ELECTIONSORDINANCE (CHAPTER 262)

WHEREAS the office of return member of Biyagama Pradeshiya Sabha has fallen vacant by reason of Resignation;

And whereas the Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna, the recognized political party to which Ganepola Arachchige Anandasiri Chandrabandu Ganepola the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(l)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon returned member of Biyagama Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared selected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days;

And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Hettige Don Sunanda Gunasekara to fill such vacancy;

Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Biyagama Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Hettige Don Sunanda Gunasekara was selected as the member of the Biyagama Pradeshiya Sabha. W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Biyagama Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office,Gampaha,02nd of February, 2026.
