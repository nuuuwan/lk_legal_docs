NOTICE No : 11/1118 ANURADHAPURA DISTRICT

IT is hereby notified that any person having a claim to the ownership or possession or interest to one or more of the parcels of lands reflected in the Cadastral Map No. 110347 made by the Surveyor-General under Section 11 of the Registration of Title Act, No. 21 of 1998 relating to the village of Thammenna Elawaka (Block 11, Sheet No. 02) or any part thereof, situated within the Grama Niladhari Division of No.63 - Thammenna Elawaka in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Medawachchiya in the District of Anuradhapura in the Province of North Central should submit his claim to the undersigned before 6th of March 2026 In the event of failure to submit any claim before the above-mentioned date, action will be taken to hold an ex parte inquiry to determine title to the land and accordingly publish such determination in the Government Gazette in terms of Section 14 of the above Act. The Cadastral Map No. 110347 referred to above may be perused at the relevant Grama Niladhari Office, Divisional Secretariat, District Survey Office, Office of the Commissioner of Title Settlement, or the Surveyor - General’s office.
