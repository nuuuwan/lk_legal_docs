Description of the land to be acquired: An allotment of land, referred in the basic plan in Sheet No. 02, Block 02, Cadastral Map No. 421356, situated in Siyambalagasruppa village in 1488 - Siyambalagasruppa East Grama Niladhari Division in Udubaddawa Divisional Secretary's Division in Kurunegala District of North Western Province.
