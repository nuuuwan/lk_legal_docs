Land Acquisition Act (Chapter 460) Notification under Section 7 My No.: RUW/LAN/AQUTN/A2/57. Land Ministry's Ref No.: 4-3/9/2017/Z/WS/83. I hereby give notice under Sub-section (1) of Section 7 of Land Acquisition Act as amended by Land Acquisition (Amendment) Act No. 28 of 1964 that government intends to acquire the following land/servitude which are required for a public purpose.
