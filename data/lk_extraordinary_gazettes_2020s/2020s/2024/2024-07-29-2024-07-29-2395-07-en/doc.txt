(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

parliamentary elections Act, No. 01 of 1981

For the purpose of the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 01 of 1981 the Election Commission does hereby -

01. revise the symbols in the schedules published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2315/06, dated January 16, 2023; and,

02. approve for the purposes of elections prescribed in the Parliamentary Elections Act, No. 1 of 1981, the symbols set out in the Schedule (A) and Schedule (B) hereto.

R. M. A. L. Rathnayake, M. A. P. C. Perera, Chairman, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission.

Ameer Faaiz, A. Shanmuganathan, Prof. Lakshman Dissanayake, Member, Member, Member, Election Commission. Election Commission. Election Commission.

At Election Commission,Sarana Mawatha,Rajagiriya,on this 26th day of July, 2024.
