The Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

(Published by Authority)PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL

Government Notifications

CIVIL AVIATION Act, No. 14 of 2010

Order under Section 6

by virtue of the powers vested in me as the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation under Section 6 of the Civil Aviation Act, No. 14 of 2010, I, Nimal Siripala De Silva, do by this order amend the Order published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 1797/27 of February 15, 2013 as specified in the Schedule below.

Schedule

(1) in the heading and first paragraph of the Order in Sinhala language, by substitution for the words "Regulation" and "by the Regulation", of the words "Order" and "by the Order" respectively,

(2) By the repeal of the term and condition specified under No. 03 in the first Schedule of the said amended Order and the substitution therefor, of the following term and condition;

"3. The Statutory Service Provider shall maintain in good order all movable and immovable properties which it holds for and on behalf of the State for the development of the air transport industry and shall not grant such immovable property on lease to a third party without prior consent in writing from the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka."

Nimal Siripala De Silva, Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation.

Colombo,July 24, 2024.
