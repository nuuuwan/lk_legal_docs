[https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data_lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s/data/lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data_lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s/data/lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s)

An Extraordinary Gazette is an official government publication used to announce urgent laws, regulations, or public notices with immediate effect.

- [**46,821** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_legal_docs/tree/data_lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s/data/lk_extraordinary_gazettes_2020s) (**7.4 GB**), from **2020-01-01** to **2025-12-19**, scraped from [https://documents.gov.lk](https://documents.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (18%), **TXT** (18%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (18%)

- In **සිංහල**, **தமிழ்** & **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**
